Uglazbljene pjesme

Stihovi Ivana Benaka lako su se pjevali, pa ih je uglazbio niz skladatelja, a on je bio pridruženi član Hrvatskog društva skladatelja. Tri pjesme predstavljene ovdje izvedene su na velikim festivalima zabavne i tamburaške glazbe, a Šokica se na festivalu pjevala 2014.

Songs

Ivan Benak's lines were easy to sing, and a number of composers set them to music; he was an associate member of the Croatian Composers' Society. The three songs below were performed at major festivals of popular and tamburica music, and Šokica was sung at a festival in 2014.

Snimke su na YouTubeu i pripadaju svojim izvođačima, autorima i nakladnicima; ovdje su samo povezane. Tekstovi pjesama © nasljednici Ivana Benaka.

The recordings are hosted on YouTube and belong to their performers, composers and publishers; they are only linked here. Lyrics © the heirs of Ivan Benak.

Ne budi me, majko, rano

Glazba: Josip Ivanković · izvode: Ivo Pattiera i Zlatni dukati · Splitski festival, 1989.

Don't Wake Me Early, Mother

Music: Josip Ivanković · performed by Ivo Pattiera and Zlatni dukati · Split Festival, 1989

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Još se nisam naspavao.Kad mi draga u san dođe,za te snove sve bih dao.

Mother, do not wake me early,I am not yet done with sleeping.When my love comes in my dreaming,all I'd give those dreams for keeping.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Ne kidaj mi divne snove,kada zoveš moje ime,mislim da me draga zove.

Mother, do not wake me early,leave my lovely dreams unbroken:when you call my name, I fancyit's my darling who has spoken.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Najljepše je snivati u zoru.U snovima na rastanku,ljubim dragu na prozoru.

Mother, do not wake me early,sweetest are the dreams when day is starting;in my dreams I kiss my true loveat her window, at our parting.

Zelene su oči tvoje

Glazba: Renato Lukić · izvodi: Blanka Došen · Zlatne žice Slavonije, Požega, 1993.

Green Are Those Eyes of Yours

Music: Renato Lukić · performed by Blanka Došen · Zlatne žice Slavonije festival, Požega, 1993

Blanka Došen – Zelene su oči tvojeSnimka je na YouTubeu · hosted on YouTube: youtube.com/watch?v=gw96lYnYeQo

Zelene su oči tvojesve bogatstvo meni bile.Kao čašu rujnog vinamoje su ih usne pile.

Those green eyes of yours, my darling,were my riches and my treasure;like a glass of red wine, brimming,my lips drank them in with pleasure.

Zašto kažeš da me voliš,kada lažno grliš mene?I dok mučiš srce moje,moja mladost tužno vene.

Why then tell me that you love me,when you hold me, just pretending?While you torture me with heartache,all my youth in grief I'm spending.

Pijetli zoru najavljuju,a sokakom bagrem miri,dok ja sanjam usne tvoje,u grudima bol se širi.

Roosters crow to wake the morning,down the lane acacias blowing;while I'm dreaming of your kisses,in my breast the pain is growing.

Zašto kažeš da me voliš,kada lažno grliš mene?Zar ne vide moju ljubav,te zelene oči snene.

Why then tell me that you love me,when you hold me, just pretending?Can they not see how I love you,those green eyes of dreams unending?

Ne muči mi bolnu dušui ne stvaraj puste noći.Zar ne vide moju ljubav,te zelene lažne oči?

Do not wound my aching spirit,give me no more nights of crying.Can they not see how I love you,those green eyes, so false and lying?

Pod granama jorgovana

Glazba: Renato Lukić · izvodi: Nevenka Fiala · Zlatne žice Slavonije, Požega, 1994.

Beneath the Lilac Boughs

Music: Renato Lukić · performed by Nevenka Fiala · Zlatne žice Slavonije festival, Požega, 1994

U baščici tvojoj maloj,dok sam rosno cvijeće brao,ljubio ti lice bijelo,kosu tvoju milovao.

In your little garden, darling,as I picked the dewy flowers,I would kiss your fair face fondlyand caress your hair for hours.

Pod granama jorgovana,u zvjezdanoj tihoj noći,dok je ruža mirisala,ljubio sam tvoje oči.

Underneath the lilac branches,in the hush of starlit splendour,while the rose was breathing fragrance,your dear eyes I kissed so tender.

Na rastanku punom sreće,dok si ljubav meni dala,budilo se rosno cvijeće,zora nam se radovala.

At our joyful, sweet leave-taking,as your love you were bestowing,dewy flowers were awaking,and the dawn with joy was glowing.

Uto zvijezde izblijedješe,mjesec mudro zažmirio,pa se i on sa Danicomu ljubavni kutak skrio.

Then the stars grew pale and faded,and the wise old moon was winking;and he too, with his Danica,to a lovers' nook went slinking.

I druge uglazbljene pjesme. Renato Lukić uglazbio je i pjesmu Zar je sve to bila šala?, Dušan Topić dječju pjesmu Djeda Mraz, a prof. Želimir Sušić dječju pjesmu Jutarnji problemi (u drugom prijepisu naslovljenu Cipelice). Marija Cavor uglazbila je U mom naručju i Volio bih da sam vjetar, koje pjeva ženski zbor „Perunike“ iz Ivankova. Šokicu je uglazbio Antun Nikolić – Tuca, a na festivalu 2014. otpjevao Željko Witovsky.

Other songs. Renato Lukić also set Was It All Just a Joke? to music, Dušan Topić the children's poem Grandfather Frost, and prof. Želimir Sušić the children's poem Morning Troubles (titled Little Shoes in another copy). Marija Cavor set In My Arms and I Wish I Were the Wind, sung by the Perunike women's choir of Ivankovo. Šokica was set by Antun Nikolić – Tuca and sung by Željko Witovsky at a festival in 2014.

Pjesma Dječji vrtić Maza postala je himna valpovačkog vrtića „Maza“, Petrijevačkim sokacima himna općine Petrijevci, a Kad zaigra kolo na Čimenu himna Marijanaca.

Maza Kindergarten became the anthem of the Maza kindergarten in Valpovo, Through the Lanes of Petrijevci the anthem of the municipality of Petrijevci, and When the Kolo Starts on the Čimen the anthem of Marijanci.

Svoje je uglazbljene pjesme i sam brojao, i broj je s godinama rastao: 2007. navodi pet, 2010. sedam, a u posljednjem životopisu četrnaest.

He kept count of his songs himself, and the number grew over the years: five in 2007, seven in 2010, and fourteen in his last biography.
