Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Dragice!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Dragica!

Ja, Dragice, imam želju,da pročitaš misli svoje,što si meni napisalakoje krasi djelo moje.

Dragica, I have a wish:that you'd read out every lineof the thoughts you wrote for me,which adorn this work of mine.

Zato dođi meni, sada,subotnjega, ljetnog dana,a sa Tobom neka dođu:Marica i Julijana.

So come over to me, now,on a summer Saturday,and have with you, when you come,Marica and Julijana.

U tom pismu točno piše,da bi meni doći znala,a za napor Tvome trudu,iskreno Ti kažem HVALA!

In that letter it says plainlythat you'd come to me, you see;for your effort and your trouble,I say "Thank you!" sincerely.

Valpovo, 09. 07. 2010.

Valpovo, July 9, 2010

Translator's note. Dragica Aleksa of Berak had written words of praise for his book Stihom kroz život (2010); here he invites her to read them at the book's launch.
