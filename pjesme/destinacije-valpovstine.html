Destinacije Valpovštine

Homeland and native region

Destinations of Valpovština

Valpovština Info piše,na početku stoji jasno.Sva naselja Valpovštinekoja ovdje žive časno.

So Valpovština Info writes,at the start it's written clear:every place in Valpovštinaliving honourably here.

Na zapadu Valpovštine,na domaku Karašici,spojili se i poštuju,selo Gat i Veliškovci.

Westward in Valpovština,close beside the Karašica,joined together, in respect,lie Gat village and Veliškovci.

Pa krenimo sad na istok:desno stoje Tiborjanci,malo dalje Vinogradci,a lijevo su Kitišanci.

Let us head off eastward now:on the right stands Tiborjanci,a bit further, Vinogradci,and on the left, Kitišanci.

Bocanjevci i Gorica,ljudi žive tu u slozi,od njih dalje ceste nema,na njivu ih traktor vozi.

Bocanjevci and Gorica:here in harmony folk bide;there's no road beyond them, soto the fields by tractor they ride.

Bistrinčani godinamaza svetište Ane znaju,mnogi ljudi ovog krajaSvetoj Ani zavjet daju.

Bistrinci folk have known for yearsof the shrine to Saint Anne there;many people of this regionmake their vows to Saint Anne in prayer.

Od Gutmanskih slavnih dana,Belišće je radno bilo,industrijska to je zona,Valpovštinu zaposlilo.

Since the glorious Gutmann daysBelišće's worked on and on;giving Valpovština jobs,it's an industrial zone.

Harkanovci od davnina,"BELA SELA" časno slave.Stanovništvo toga sela,sretno sniva zore plave.

Harkanovci, since of old,keep "Bela sela" fine and true;all the people of that villagehappily dream of dawns of blue.

Ivanovci dično selo,Biskupa su časno dali.U Zelčinu često Miro,na KONJARI vatre pali.

Ivanovci, village proud,gave a Bishop, to its fame;in Zelčin, Miro oftenat Konjara lights the flame.

Marjančaci su u kutu,ali ništa im ne fali.Sami su na svome putu,selo kao Pariz Mali.

Marjančaci's in a corner,yet they lack for nothing there;on their own road, all alone —a Little Paris, I declare!

Divno mjesto Lad'mirevcii ŠOVO se tu rodio.SOS je ovdje selo,pa tko ne bi tu živio.

Lad'mirevci's a lovely place,and here Šovo was born, too;there's the SOS Village here —so who wouldn't live here? Who?

Šag i Nard su jako blizui to nije ništa novo.Ljudi bi se i zbližili,asfalt nema Kosatovo.

Šag and Nard are very near,and that's nothing new, you know;folk would grow still closer, butthere's no asphalt in Kosatovo.

Petr'jevačka "REPUBLIKA",napredna je uvijek bila.U naručje svoje, nježno,Satnicu je prigrlila.

The "Republic" of Petr'jevcialways was progressive, true;tenderly into its armsit has taken Satnica too.

Samatovci su postranii Bizovcu pripadaju.Njima ništa i ne fali,kao drugi sve imaju.

Samatovci's to one side,part of Bizovac, it's true;they're not lacking anything:they've got all the others do.

Selo Selci i Cerovac,poznati su još od prije.Tu je ŠOVO filma snimo',"VOLIO GA SOKOL NIJE".

Selci village and Cerovachave been known from long ago.Here it was that Šovo filmed"Sokol Loved Him Not", you know.

Brođanci se sportom bave,tu se skače preko grede.Olimpijsko to je seloi pečeni vol se jede.

Brođanci folk go in for sport —here they jump across the beam;it's an Olympic village, androast ox is the cuisine.

Habjanovci, Cret, Novaki,svatko živi sam za sebe.Da bi selo opstojalo,ponekad se rode bebe.

Habjanovci, Cret, Novaki:each lives for himself alone;so the village may live on,now and then a baby's born.

Bizovac je Aquapolisi tu sviću zlatne zore.Turizmom se ljudi bave,jer imaju svoje more.

Bizovac is Aquapolis,here the golden dawns break through;tourism's the people's trade,for they've got their own sea, too.

Valpovo je centar svega.Divni park i dvorac ima.I Kulturni centar novi,za potrebe građanima.

Valpovo's the centre of all:a fine park and castle stand;and a new Cultural Centre,for the townsfolk's needs, at hand.

Godinama tu je raslo,dvadeset i sedam sela,na njedrima Slavonije,Valpovština to je cijela.

Here, as years went by, there grewtwenty-seven villages, all told,on Slavonia's bosom: there'sValpovština, whole, to behold.

Valpovo, 23. 03. 2011.

Valpovo, March 23, 2011

Translator's note. A tongue-in-cheek tour of the 27 villages around Valpovo. "Šovo" is the actor Fabijan Šovagović (1932–2001), born in Ladimirevci, which also has an SOS Children's Village; the film is his "Sokol ga nije volio" (1988), quoted by the poet with the words turned round.
