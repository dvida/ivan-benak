Bole's First Book Launch

Šesnaestoga svibanja, ovog ljeta,Klokočevac u ljepoti svijeta.Promocija prava je krasota,divne knjige: „Kazaljke života“.

The sixteenth day of May, this year — and thereKlokočevac, in beauty past compare.The launch was lovely, as a launch should be,for the fine book "The Hands of Life", you see.

Sinoć stigoh kući malo kasno,s promocije jedne knjige časno,i pročitah par pjesama jasno,sve su pjesme napisane krasno.

Last night I got home just a little latefrom a book's launch, an honoured, worthy fête,and read a few poems through, clear and straight:and every poem in it was first-rate.

Književnost je ukrašena s time.Već polako i zorica sviće.Još me drže te predivne rime,od radosti popio sam piće.

Our literature's adorned with this, I'd say;already, slowly, dawn begins to break;those lovely rhymes still hold me in their sway;I've had a drink for joy, make no mistake!

Knjigu gledam, već su sitni sati,čitao bih, al ne mogu više.Ovu knjigu moram pročitati,jer predivne pjesme Bole piše.

I look at the book; the small hours are here;I'd read on, but I cannot any more.I have to read this whole book through, it's clear,for Bole writes such lovely poems, for sure.

Daj Muzama svoju ruku pruži,tebi, Bole, želim sretne dane.Pero spretno da papirom kruži,da deseta knjiga skoro svane.

Come, reach your hand out to the Muses, do!Bole, I wish you happy days, and mayyour nimble pen keep circling pages through,so that your tenth book soon may see the day.

Valpovo, 17. 05. 2014.

Valpovo, May 17, 2014

Translator's note. For the first launch of his fellow poet Bole's ninth book, Kazaljke života ("The Hands of Life", as in the hands of a clock), at Klokočevac on May 16, 2014.
