Prijatelji slavnog Komla grada

Occasional verse and dedications

Friends from the Famous Town of Komlo

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi dolasku vašem se veselim.

To all of you warm greetings I extend,and I rejoice at your arrival, friends.

Komla grada susjedne Mađarske,dobro došli, prijatelji mili,u Valpovu u našemu gradu,ugodno se sa nama družili.

From Komlo, town of neighbouring Hungary,dear friends, we bid you welcome, all of you,here in Valpovo, in this town of ours;and have a pleasant time with us here, too.

Što je lijepo neka dugo traje,obostrano družili se često,svu ljepotu to druženje daje,bilo naše ili vaše mjesto.

What's lovely, let it last for many a day;may we meet often, taking turns again;such friendship gives all beauty, in its way,be it your town or ours, it's all the same.

U svom gradu divne snove snilii najljepšu ljubav provodili,mirovinu još veću imali,da bi bolji standard uživali.

May lovely dreams be dreamt in your own town,and may the finest love be yours to own;and may your pensions still more generous grow,so that a better standard you may know.

Svi u zdravlju i veselju bili,uživajte veselo bez rada,mnogo ljeta penziju primali,iskrene su moje želje sada.

May all of you in health and gladness be,enjoy yourselves in cheer, no work to do,and draw your pensions many summers through —these are my heartfelt wishes now to you.

Valpovo, 22. 04. 2010.

Valpovo, April 22, 2010

Translator's note. Komló is a town in southern Hungary, partnered with Valpovo; the visitors were pensioners like himself.
