Poems for children

Daddy's Little Doll

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Imam jednu lutkicu,jedno malo đače,pa od škole do kućecupka kao pače.

I have a little dolly,a little schoolgirl, whocomes hopping home from schoolthe way that ducklings do.

Jednog dana plačućidođe meni kući:Oh, tatice, molim te,nemoj mene tući!

One day, all tears and sobbing,she came back home to me:Oh, Daddy dear, I beg you,don't you smack me, please!

Reci, zlato tatino,zašto plačeš tako?Jesi li se udarila?Boli li te jako?

Tell me, Daddy's treasure,why such tears, oh my?Did you go and bump yourself?Does it hurt, is that why?

Oh, pomozi, tatice!Tko da povijest znade!?Pa mi učiteljicajedinicu dade.

Oh, help me, Daddy, help!Who can know history?!And so my teacher wentand gave an F to me.

A ti uči, lutkice,i uzmi čitanku,pa ćeš uvijek učenjemrazveselit majku.

Then study, little dolly,and take your reader, do,and studying will alwaysmake Mummy glad of you.

Ti ćeš sutra u školipovijest dobro znati,pa će učiteljicapeticu ti dati.

Tomorrow, then, at schoolyou'll know your history through,and then your teacher surelywill give an A to you.

Translator's note. Croatian school marks run from 1, a fail, to 5, the top mark; here they are given as an F and an A.
