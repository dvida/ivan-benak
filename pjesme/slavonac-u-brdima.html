Domoljubne i zavičajne

Slavonac u brdima!

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

A Slavonian in the Hills!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Po brdima i klancima,kotlinama kao lonac.Hodam kao u lancima,jer rodom sam ja Slavonac.

Over hills and down through gorges,hollows deep as any pot,I go walking as in fetters —born Slavonian, that's my lot.

Zato pođoh ja na niže,silazeći sa planine.Odoh svojoj kući bližeu slavonske te ravnine.

So I headed down, and lower,coming off the mountainside,went back homeward, ever closerto Slavonia's plains so wide.

Svud je lijepo za trenutak.Da me pusta želja mine,ja poželih topli kutak,bijela kruha i slanine.

Every place is fine a while.So this longing would be through,all I wished was a warm corner,some white bread, and bacon too.

Valpovo, 1978.

Valpovo, 1978
