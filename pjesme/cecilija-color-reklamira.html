Humorous poems

Cecilija Advertises Color

Jučer svratih ja u MARKET COLOR.Cecilija na ulazu stoji,pogledam ju, a ona se smije,oh, ljepote, dragi ljudi moji.

Yesterday I dropped in at MARKET COLOR.There at the entrance Cecilija stands;I look at her, and she just smiles at me —oh, what a beauty, my good folk and friends!

Pozdravim ju i namignem malo,a ona se još jače nasmije,u pete mi odmah srce palo:ljepše žene od Cecike nije.

I greet her, and I give a little wink,and she smiles back more broadly than before;at once my heart dropped right down to my heels:no woman's lovelier than Cecika, I'm sure.

Od sada ću svaki dan u COLORda kupujem mada ništ' ne želim,i pozdravim dragu Ceciliju,nekoliko riječi da joj velim.

From now on, every day, to COLOR I'll goto shop, though there is nothing that I need,to say hello to my dear Cecilijaand speak a few words to her, yes indeed.

Svojim glasom da ljepotu stvara,divnom pjesmom da nam pruži raja:„Ne ritaj se mačketino stara,joj, kirvaja ispod šlingeraja!“

So that her voice would make such beauty ring,and with her lovely song bring heaven near:"Don't you kick, you old she-cat, you old thing —oh, kirvaja beneath the lace — oh dear!"

U COLORU sad kupujem stvari,koje nikad ja trebati neću,da dokažem da sam kupac starii da COLOR zbog nje ima sreću.

At COLOR now I buy up things galorethat I shall never need in any way,to prove I am a customer of yore,and that, thanks to her, COLOR's luck will stay.

Narod žuri, ništa ne govori,u tom trenu Cecilija sniva.Samo stoji i ništa ne zboridivna lutka kao da je živa!

The people hurry past, no word is heard;in that same moment Cecilija dreams.She only stands there, never says a word —a lovely doll — alive, or so it seems!

Valpovo, 01. 05. 2013.

Valpovo, May 1, 2013

Translator's note. Color is a shop. Cecilija, who greets him at its entrance, turns out in the last stanza to be a doll. The line she sings is from a folk song; the kirvaja is the village church fair.
