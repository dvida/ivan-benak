It's Not Easy for the Postman Either

Ni poštaru lako nije,dok ga žarko sunce grije.On veliku torbu vučei brojeve traži kuće.

Nor has the postman got it sweetwhile he's baking in the heat;he must lug a great big sack,hunting house numbers, there and back.

Često puta sanduk nema,a vani se kiša sprema,pa ni zvonce da se javi,kuda poštu da ostavi.

Often there's no letterbox,and a rainstorm's on its way,not a doorbell he can ring:where to leave the post, then, pray?

Kad telegram nosi Mati,onda mora dobro znati,kako će ga dati prije,jer pas reži kod kapije.

When a telegram's for Mata,he must know, and know it straight,how to hand it over fast,for a dog growls by the gate.

Nakon divnih noćnih snova,poželi se radost nova.Poštar nosi pismo snova,pa i to je radost nova.

After lovely dreams at nightone longs for a joy that's new;postman brings the dreamed-of letter —and that is a new joy, too.

Kad penziju nosi Ivi,tu nastaje tulum živi.Uz kavicu i rakiju,divne zgode ispričaju.

When he brings Ivo his pension,there's a lively party then;over coffee and rakijathey swap lovely tales again.

I na kraju što da radi,udovici Mari svrati.A ona ga vjerno prati,slatki cjelov će mu dati.

And in the end, what can he do?He drops in on widow Mara;faithfully she sees him offand gives him a sweet kiss, too.

Nekad sunce, nekad kiše,nekad torbu tegli više.Ni poštaru nije lako,to ne može biti svatko!

Sometimes sun, and sometimes rain,sometimes more to haul, it's true.Nor has the postman got it sweet —not just anyone would do!

Valpovo, 27. 10. 2012.

Valpovo, October 27, 2012
