Protestne i društvene

Ja mrzim!

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

I Hate!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja mrzim dvorcei ne volim kule,tvrđave i vilei sva zdanjabez ljudske topline.Mrzim kazamate,glad i nasilje,ratove i krvoproliće.Mrzim gramzljivostsvu bez pardonai slatku fasadupokvarene duše.Ja mrzim sjaj,ljudskog licemjerstva,uvijeno u fino ruho bontona.

I hate castlesand do not love towers,fortresses and villasand all the buildingswithout human warmth.I hate dungeons,hunger and violence,wars and bloodshed.I hate greed,all of it, without mercy,and the sweet façadeof a rotten soul.I hate the glitterof human hypocrisy,wrapped in the fine garb of etiquette.

Valpovo, 18.1.1975.

Valpovo, January 18, 1975
