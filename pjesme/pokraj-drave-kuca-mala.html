Radio burza

Pokraj Drave kuća mala

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Little House by the Drava

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Vikendicu prodajem kraj Drave,da dočekaš u njoj zore plave.

By the Drava I've a cottage up for sale,to greet the dawns of blue there without fail.

Na prirodnoj ljepoti smješteno,sve okolo lijepo ograđeno.

It's set in natural beauty, and all roundit's nicely fenced, a tidy plot of ground.

Površine od šesto kvadrata,za odmor je nakon teškog rada.

It covers some six hundred metres square,for resting after heavy labour there.

A to vam je prava vikend zona,vikendica na njoj od vagona.

And that's a proper weekend zone, it's true;the cottage is a railway wagon, too.

I bunar je pokraj vikendice,nakon snova da umiješ lice.

And by the cottage stands a well for you,to wash your face in when your dreams are through.

Pitka, hladna u njemu je voda,ukusna je kao TRAUBI SODA.

The water in it's cold and fit to drink,as tasty as a Traubi Soda, I think.

Tu su šljive koje dobro rode,za rakiju, eto ljudi zgode.

There are plum trees, too, that bear a lot of fruit —for rakija, folks, a chance that's sure to suit.

Orah, višnja što plodove daju,za kolače, to ženice znaju.

A walnut and sour cherry bear as well —for cakes, the wives know that, I needn't tell.

Poljski WC i šupa još priđe,molim kupce nek' dođu da vide.

An outdoor loo, a shed come with it, too —I ask the buyers: come and have a view.

Ljepota je k'o u bajnom dvoru,a cijena je sve po dogovoru.

It's lovely as a fairy palace hall;the price is by agreement, after all.

Zovite me kupci svakog dana,neka zvoni telefon mog stana.

So call me, buyers, call me every day,and let the phone in my flat ring away.

Valpovo, 09. 04. 2001.

Valpovo, April 9, 2001

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza. The cottage is a converted railway wagon; Traubi Soda was a popular grape soft drink.
