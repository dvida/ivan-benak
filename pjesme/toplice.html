The Spa!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Susreću se ovdje ljuditko zna sve od kuda?Pokraj ove tople Terme,tu u tome holu.Ovdje ih je sakupilaista sudba kruta.Svatko traži sebi pomoću svojemu bolu.Svi oni što voze kolica,u njima skrhano tijeloi oni koje vodei onima što pomaže štaka,svima je tijelo slabo,od bola satrveno,ali je duša mlada.Želja za zdravljemi životomsvima je tako jaka.I božicu Minervu molesa tisuću želja sada,ona jedina znade,koliko je ovdje jada.

People meet here —who knows from where?Beside these warm Baths,here in this hall.They have been gathered hereby one and the same harsh fate.Every one seeks helpin his own pain.All those who ride in wheelchairs,broken bodies in them,and those who must be led,and those a crutch helps along,all of them weak in body,ground down by pain,but young in soul.The wish for healthand for lifeis so strong in all of them.And to the goddess Minerva they praywith a thousand wishes today;she alone knowshow much sorrow is here.

Valpovo, 1977.

Valpovo, 1977

Translator's note. Minerva, whose Roman statue was found at the ancient spa of Aquae Iasae, is the emblem of Varaždinske Toplice; the poem is probably set there.
