The Flower Gatherer

Širio se miris pokošenog sijena,a zeleno polje i oku je milo.Na livadi sjedi divna, mlada žena,bere poljsko cvijeće i skuplja u krilo.

The scent of new-mown hay was spreading on the air,the field was green and sweet for any eye to see;upon the meadow sat a woman, young and fair,and picked wild flowers, gathering them on her knee.

Sjedila je žena kraj gorskog potokai vijenac je plela od šarenog cvijeća.Točila se radost iz njenog oka,a na njenom licu blistala je sreća.

The woman sat beside a little mountain brookand wove herself a wreath of flowers of every hue;the joy came pouring out of her in every look,and on her face her happiness was shining through.

Dok je tako sretna svoje snove snilai gledala milo biće koje voli,sa zapada zlatna rumen se razlila.Da je vječno tako, tiho Boga moli.

And while so happily she dreamed her gentle dreamsand gazed upon the dear one whom her heart adored,a golden glow spilled from the west in rosy streams;she prays in silence: "Let it last for ever, Lord."

I toga je časa najsretnija bila,svi trenuci sreće te predivne žene.Voljenom se biću u zagrljaj svila.Ostat će joj vječno drage uspomene.

That hour, of all her happy hours, was the most blessed,of all the joyful moments that the fair one knew;she nestled close against her dear beloved's breast;she'll keep those memories for ever, dear and true.

Ali vjetar sudbe pirne sa planine,k'o latice nježne raznese joj sreću.Još jedino čujem vapaj iz daljinei znam da je više nikad vidjet ne ću.

But then the wind of fate comes gusting from the hilland scatters all her joy, as tender petals fall;and only from afar a cry comes to me still,and I know I shall never see her more at all.

Valpovo, 03. 07. 1977.

Valpovo, July 3, 1977
