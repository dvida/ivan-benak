The Flower Gatherer

Širio se miris pokošenog sijena,a zeleno polje i oku je milo.Na livadi sjedi divna, mlada žena,bere poljsko cvijeće i skuplja u krilo.

The scent of new-mown hay was spreading on the air,the green field, too, was sweet for eyes to see;there in the meadow sat a woman, young and fair,picking wild flowers, gathering them on her knee.

Sjedila je žena kraj gorskog potokai vijenac je plela od šarenog cvijeća.Točila se radost iz njenog oka,a na njenom licu blistala je sreća.

The woman sat beside a mountain brookand wove a wreath of flowers of every hue.Joy was pouring from her every look,and on her face, bright happiness shone through.

Dok je tako sretna svoje snove snilai gledala milo biće koje voli,sa zapada zlatna rumen se razlila.Da je vječno tako, tiho Boga moli.

While, happy as she was, she dreamed her dreamsand gazed upon the dear one whom she loves,a golden glow spilled from the west in streams.She softly prays that it may last, to God above.

I toga je časa najsretnija bila,svi trenuci sreće te predivne žene.Voljenom se biću u zagrljaj svila.Ostat će joj vječno drage uspomene.

And at that hour she was happiest of all —all the happy moments that lovely woman knew.She nestled close into her dear beloved's arms;dear memories will stay with her all her life through.

Ali vjetar sudbe pirne sa planine,k'o latice nježne raznese joj sreću.Još jedino čujem vapaj iz daljinei znam da je više nikad vidjet ne ću.

But then the wind of fate gusts from the mountainsideand scatters all her happiness as tender petals fall.Now all that I can hear is a cry from far away,and I know that I shall never see her again at all.

Valpovo, 03. 07. 1977.

Valpovo, July 3, 1977
