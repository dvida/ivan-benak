Svim tetama iz vrtića

Occasional verse and dedications

To All the Aunties of the Kindergarten

Svim tetama iz vrtića „MAZA“što su ljubav nama djeci dali,riječ je mala da kažemo hvala,od srca im ovaj poklon mali.

To all the aunties, kindergarten "Maza",who gave their love to us, the children, true:though "thank you" is a little word to say,here, from our hearts, this little gift for you.

Posuta im bila cvjećem staza,žele djeca iz vrtića „MAZA“.

May flowers strew the path they walk upon,wish kindergarten "Maza's" little ones.

Translator's note. Croatian children call their kindergarten teachers "teta", auntie. Written in the children's voice for the teachers of the Maza kindergarten.
