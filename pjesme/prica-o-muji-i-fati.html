The Tale of Mujo and Fata

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U oskudno ovo dobaMujo Fatu oženio.Posla nema u domaji,težak im je život bio.

In these lean and needy daysMujo and his Fata wed;work at home was nowhere found,and a heavy life they led.

Ode Mujo u pečalbu.Otvorio zapad vrata.A kod kuće osta plačuć,ucviljena, lijepa Fata.

Mujo went abroad to work:the West had opened wide its gate.And at home, in tears, stayed Fata,lovely, grieving, left to wait.

Na baušteli kod Jericha,Mujo se je zaposlio,pisma, marke kući slaoi na Fatu je mislio.

At the Jericha building siteMujo found a job to do;letters, marks he sent back home,and he thought of Fata too.

Kratko vrijeme nakon toga,prvog sina rodi Fata.Blistala je sva od sreće,jer sretan će bit i tata.

Just a short while after that,Fata bore a first-born boy;she was shining with delight,for the dad would share her joy.

Al' samoća dušu truje,mladost traži želje svoje,a noći su puste, duge,ljepši život je u dvoje.

Loneliness, though, poisons souls,youth has longings of its own,and the nights are long and empty:life is sweeter not alone.

A Fata je mlada bila,imala je burne snove,tijelu ljubav priuštilai rađala sokolove.

Fata, young as young could be,had such dreams, such stormy ones;let her body's love run free,and gave birth to falcon sons.

I prođoše deset ljeta,kući Mujo odlučio,na baušteli u Švabiji,invalidsku zaradio.

Ten years passed, and Mujo reckonedhe'd go home again to stay:on a site in Švabijahe'd earned disability pay.

Stiže Mujo pred kućerak.Izletjela pred njeg' Fata.Lijepo djecu odgojila.Djeca viču: "Ide tata!"

Mujo reached their little house;Fata flew out to his side.She had raised the children nicely."Here comes Daddy!" children cried.

"Čija su to djeca Fato?Reci meni ženo mila.U Njemačku kad sam oš'o,kuća nam je prazna bila."

"Whose are all these children, Fata?Tell me, wife, my dearest one:when I left for Germany,of these children there was none."

Umiljato poče Fata:"Ovi dragi sokolovi,vjeruj Mujo svojoj Fati,sve su naši to sinovi."

Sweetly Fata then began:"These dear falcons, every one,Mujo, trust your Fata, dear,each of them is our own son."

"Reći ću ti sve po redu:U Švabiju kad si oš'o,nije bilo zatim dugo,rodio se tvoj sin Tošo."

"I will tell you all in order:when to Švabija you'd flown,it was not so long thereafterTošo came, your very own."

"Zatim jedne divne noći,došao si meni na san.Ostala sam, Mujo, trudnai na svijet je doš'o Hasan."

"Then, upon a lovely night,in a dream you came to me;I was left with child, my Mujo,and so Hasan came to be."

"Često sam te poželjela,i kad nisam mogla durat,Bog mi želje ispunio,kao s neba pade Murat."

"Often I would long for you,and when I could bear no more,God Himself fulfilled my wish:Murat dropped from heaven's door."

"A sjećaš se, dragi Mujo,kad mi dugo nisi pis'o?Mislila sam samo na tei zbog tog se rodi Iso."

"Mujo dear, do you rememberwhen for ages you'd not write?I kept thinking just of you,and so Iso saw the light."

Sluša Mujo mile riječi,kako Fata sve razlaže.Pa kad skonta to u glavi,misli, možda i ne laže.

Mujo hears her tender words,how she sets it out, and why;and once he has thought it through,thinks: perhaps she doesn't lie.

"Ih, još kada se moji rode,bit će djece kuća puna.Bar sa djecom ja ću biti,bogatiji od tajkuna."

"Ha! And when my own are born,kids will fill this house up soon;then at least in kids I'll bericher than any tycoon."

Valpovo, 03.03.2000.

Valpovo, March 3, 2000

Translator's note. Mujo and Fata are the stock couple of Bosnian jokes. Švabija ("Swabia") is the folk name for Germany, where Mujo goes as a guest worker; "falcons" is the folk word for fine sons.
Druga inačica: „Storija o Mati i Kati“, 2004. Another version: “The Story of Mata and Kata”, 2004

Prerada s imenima Mata i Kata.

A retelling with the names Mata and Kata.

U oskudno ovo doba,Mata Katu oženio.U domaji posla nema,težak im je život bio.

In these lean and needy daysMata and his Kata wed;work at home was nowhere found,and a heavy life they led.

U pečalbu ode Mata,otvorio zapad vrata.A kod kuće osta plačuć,ucviljena lijepa Kata.

Off went Mata, for, of late,the West had opened wide its gate;and at home, in tears, stayed Kata,lovely, grieving, left to wait.

"I will tell you all in order:when to Švabija you'd flown,it was not so long thereafterthat I bore Ilija, your own."

„A onda sam jedne noći,u snovima s tobom bila,ostala sam trudna, Mata,sina Peru sam rodila.“

"Then one night, when I was sleeping,in my dreams I was with you;I was left with child, dear Mata,and gave birth to Pero too."

„Često sam te poželjelanajgore su noći bilei kad nisam mogla durat,kao s neba pade Mile.“

"Often I would long for you,nights were hardest, I confess,and when I could bear no more,Mile dropped from heaven, no less."

„Da l' se sjećaš, dragi Mata,kad mi marke nisi slao?Mislila sam samo na te,Bog mi tada Đuku dao.“

"Mata dear, do you rememberwhen the marks stopped coming through?I kept thinking just of you,and God gave me Đuka too."

Mata hears her tender words,how she sets it out, and why;and once he has thought it through,thinks: perhaps she doesn't lie.

„Ih, još kada se moji rode,bit će djece kuća puna.Bar sa djecom ja ću biti,bogatiji od tajkuna.“

Valpovo, 16. 08. 2004.

Valpovo, August 16, 2004

Prerada na šokačkoj ikavici.

A retelling in the Šokac (ikavian) dialect.

U oskudno ovo vrime,Mata Katu oženio.U domaji posla nima,težak im je život bio.

In these lean and needy daysMata and his Kata wed;work at home was nowhere found,and a heavy life they led.

U pečalbu ode Mata,otvorio zapad vrata.Ali doma osta plačuć',ucviljena lipa Kata.

Off went Mata, for, of late,the West had opened wide its gate;and at home, in tears, stayed Kata,lovely, grieving, left to wait.

Na baušteli kod Jericha,Mata se je zaposlio,pisma, marke doma slaoi na Katu je mislio.

At the Jericha building siteMata found a job to do;letters, marks he sent back home,and he thought of Kata too.

Kratko vrime nakon toga,prvog sina rodi Kata.Blistala je sva od sriće,jer sritan će bit i tata.

Just a short while after that,Kata bore a first-born boy;she was shining with delight,for the dad would share her joy.

Al' samoća dušu truje,mladost traži želje svoje,a noći su puste, duge,lipši život je u dvoje.

Loneliness, though, poisons souls,youth has longings of its own,and the nights are long and empty:life is sweeter not alone.

A Kata je mlada bila,imala je burne snove,tilu ljubav priuštilai rađala sokolove.

Kata, young as young could be,had such dreams, such stormy ones;let her body's love run free,and gave birth to falcon sons.

I prođoše deset lita,doma Mata odlučio,na baušteli u Švabijiinvalidsku zaradio.

Ten years passed, and Mata reckonedhe'd go home again to stay:on a site in Švabijahe'd earned disability pay.

Mislio je da će s Katom,sriću, radost podiliti,lipu kuću sagraditi,mnogo dice izroditi.

He had thought that he and Katajoy and happiness would share,build a lovely house with care,and have children, lots to spare.

Al' sudba je drugo tila.I Kata je vridna bila.Dok je Mata arbajtovo,dica su kuću popunila.

But fate willed another way:Kata, too, worked hard each day.While Mata was off arbeiting,the house filled up with kids at play.

Pred kućerak stiže Mata,izletila pred njeg' Kata.Lipo dicu odgojila.Dica viču: "Ide tata!"

Mata reaches home one day;Kata flies out right away.She has raised the children nicely."Here comes Daddy!" children say.

"Čija su to dica Kato?Reci meni, ženo mila,u Njemačku kad sam oš'o,kuća nam je prazna bila."

"Whose are all these children, Kata?Tell me, wife, my dearest one:when I left for Germany,of these children there was none."

Umiljato poče Kata:"Ovi dragi sokolovi,viruj Mata svojoj Kati,sve su naši to sinovi."

Sweetly Kata then began:"These dear falcons, every one,Mata, trust your Kata, dear,each of them is our own son."

"Reći ću ti sve po redu:Kad si oš'o u Švabiju,kratko vrime nakon toga,rodila sam tvog Iliju."

"I will tell you all in order:when to Švabija you'd flown,just a short while after thatI bore Ilija, your own."

"A onda sam jedne noći,u snovima s tobom bila,ostala sam trudna, Mata,sina Peru sam rodila."

"Then one night, when I was sleeping,in my dreams I was with you;I was left with child, dear Mata,and gave birth to Pero too."

"Često sam te poželilanajgore su noći bilei kad nisam mogla durat,kao s neba pade Mile."

"Often I would long for you,nights were hardest, I confess,and when I could bear no more,Mile dropped from heaven, no less."

"Sićaš li se, dragi Mata,kad mi marke nisi slao?Mislila sam samo na te,Bog mi tada Đuku dao."

"Mata dear, do you rememberwhen the marks stopped coming through?I kept thinking just of you,and God gave me Đuka too."

Sluša Mata mile riči,kako Kata sve razlaže.Pa kad skonta to u glavi,misli, možda i ne laže.

Mata hears her tender words,how she sets it out, and why;and once he has thought it through,thinks: perhaps she doesn't lie.

"Ih, još kada se moji rode,bit će dice kuća puna.Bar sa dicom ja ću biti,bogatiji od tajkuna."

"Ha! And when my own are born,kids will fill this house up soon;then at least in kids I'll bericher than any tycoon."

Valpovo, 16. 08. 2004.

Valpovo, August 16, 2004

Translator's note. A retelling in Šokac dialect of his comic tale of a guest worker. Švabija ("Swabia") is the folk name for Germany, "arbeiting" echoes the German words the guest workers brought home, and "falcons" is the folk word for fine sons.
