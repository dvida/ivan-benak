Humorous poems

The Tale of Mujo and Fata

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U oskudno ovo dobaMujo Fatu oženio.Posla nema u domaji,težak im je život bio.

In these hard and hungry daysMujo took his Fata to wed.Work at home was nowhere found,and a heavy life they led.

Ode Mujo u pečalbu.Otvorio zapad vrata.A kod kuće osta plačuć,ucviljena, lijepa Fata.

Mujo left to work abroad,for the West had opened its door;and at home, in tears, stayed Fata,lovely Fata, grieving sore.

Na baušteli kod Jericha,Mujo se je zaposlio,pisma, marke kući slaoi na Fatu je mislio.

At the Jericha building siteMujo found some work to do;letters, marks he sent back home,and he thought of Fata too.

Kratko vrijeme nakon toga,prvog sina rodi Fata.Blistala je sva od sreće,jer sretan će bit i tata.

Just a little time thereafterFata bore their first-born boy;all aglow she was with gladness,for the dad would share the joy.

Al' samoća dušu truje,mladost traži želje svoje,a noći su puste, duge,ljepši život je u dvoje.

Loneliness will poison souls,youth has longings of its own,and the nights are long and empty:life is sweeter not alone.

A Fata je mlada bila,imala je burne snove,tijelu ljubav priuštilai rađala sokolove.

Fata, after all, was young,and she dreamed such stormy dreams;to her body love she granted,and bore falcon sons, it seems.

I prođoše deset ljeta,kući Mujo odlučio,na baušteli u Švabiji,invalidsku zaradio.

Ten years passed, and Mujo settledthat he'd go back home to stay:on a site in Švabijahe'd earned disability pay.

Stiže Mujo pred kućerak.Izletjela pred njeg' Fata.Lijepo djecu odgojila.Djeca viču: "Ide tata!"

Mujo reached the little house,Fata flew out to his side;she had raised the children nicely."Here comes Daddy!" children cried.

"Čija su to djeca Fato?Reci meni ženo mila.U Njemačku kad sam oš'o,kuća nam je prazna bila."

"Whose are all these children, Fata?Tell me, wife, my dearest one:when I left for Germany,in this house there wasn't one."

Umiljato poče Fata:"Ovi dragi sokolovi,vjeruj Mujo svojoj Fati,sve su naši to sinovi."

Sweetly Fata then began:"These dear falcons, one by one —Mujo, trust your Fata here —every one of them's our son."

"Reći ću ti sve po redu:U Švabiju kad si oš'o,nije bilo zatim dugo,rodio se tvoj sin Tošo."

"I will tell you all in order:when to Švabija you'd gone,it was not so long thereafterTošo came, your little son."

"Zatim jedne divne noći,došao si meni na san.Ostala sam, Mujo, trudnai na svijet je doš'o Hasan."

"Then one lovely night you camein a dream to me, my man;I was left with child, dear Mujo,and the world received Hasan."

"Često sam te poželjela,i kad nisam mogla durat,Bog mi želje ispunio,kao s neba pade Murat."

"Often I would long for you,and when I could bear it not,God fulfilled my wish, and thenout of heaven fell Murat."

"A sjećaš se, dragi Mujo,kad mi dugo nisi pis'o?Mislila sam samo na tei zbog tog se rodi Iso."

"Mujo dear, do you rememberwhen for ages you'd not write?I kept thinking just of you:that's how Iso saw the light."

Sluša Mujo mile riječi,kako Fata sve razlaže.Pa kad skonta to u glavi,misli, možda i ne laže.

Mujo hears her tender words,how she sets it out, and why;and when he has worked it through,thinks: perhaps she doesn't lie.

"Ih, još kada se moji rode,bit će djece kuća puna.Bar sa djecom ja ću biti,bogatiji od tajkuna."

"Ha! And once my own are born,there'll be children in every room;then at least in kids I'll bericher than any tycoon."

Valpovo, 03.03.2000.

Valpovo, March 3, 2000

Translator's note. Mujo and Fata are the stock couple of Bosnian jokes. Švabija ("Swabia") is the folk name for Germany, where Mujo goes as a guest worker; "falcons" is the folk word for fine sons.
