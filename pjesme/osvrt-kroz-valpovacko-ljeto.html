Osvrt kroz Valpovačko ljeto

Homeland and native region

A Tour of the Valpovo Summer

Divna smotra Valpovačkog Ljeta,od davnina u Valpovu cvjeta.Četrdeset četvrta je sada,ta ljepota Valpovačkog grada.

The Valpovo Summer's splendid showhas bloomed in Valpovo since long ago.Now the forty-fourth is here today:Valpovo town's beauty on display.

U četvrtak stiže program novi:koncert daju pjevački zborovi.Mnoga društva svoje slike slažu,pa i lovci trofeje izlažu.

Thursday brings a new programme in store:choirs give a concert, and there's more.Many clubs put pictures up on show,hunters set their trophies in a row.

Promocija "Tete Eve knjiga",pa izložba šlinganih ručnika,na izložbi mnoge žene sadanude robu od vezenog rada.

First the launch of "Auntie Eva's Book",then the eyelet towels: take a look!At the show, the many women thereoffer wares embroidered with great care.

U petak se pogledati mora:divna smotra dječjega folklora.Sedamnaesti godišnjak još teče,to bi bilo sve za ovo veče'.

Friday brings a show for every eye:lovely children's folklore, dancing by.That's the seventeenth year it has run,and with that the evening's programme's done.

Kad puhači trubom zasvirajui palicom cure žongliraju,ta ljepota na trgu se gleda,to je dio Valpovačkog Ljeta.

When the brass band plays its trumpets loud,and the girls twirl batons for the crowd,all that beauty on the square they view:that's part of the Valpovo Summer too.

I slikari svoj su obol dali,u subotu slike naslikali.Amaterska revija pjevača,rakijada da tijelo ojača.

And the painters, too, have done their share:Saturday they painted pictures there.There's an amateur singers' revue,and a rakija fest to strengthen you.

Osvanula i nedjelja sveta,oko dvorca sve divno izgleda,posvuda su šatre načičkane,raznovrsnom robom natrpane.

Holy Sunday dawns, so bright and fair,round the castle all looks lovely there;everywhere the tents are crammed in tight,stuffed with every kind of goods in sight.

Kad povorka prema dvorcu krene,na ulici: djeca, ljudi, žene,svi gledaju tu ljepotu snova:kako petnaest nastupa KUD-ova.

When the parade heads to the castle, thenout in the street: children, women, men;all are watching this, a dreamlike sight:fifteen KUDs perform with all their might.

Folkloraša iz Hrvatske cijele,mladež naša kako se vesele.Mnoge smotre u Valpovu gradu,konji kasom idu uz paradu.

Dancers come from all Croatia here,and our young are full of joy and cheer.Many shows in Valpovo are played,horses trot beside the grand parade.

Kad u dvorac stignu folklorašii sviraju kolo tamburašii šokica kad ugleda lolu,od miline vrisne u tom kolu.

When the dancers reach the castle, andkolo tunes ring from the tambura band,and a Šokica, when she spies her lad,in the kolo whoops, her heart so glad.

Da bi ovo Ljeto uljepšali,Zlatni vez su muziku nam dali.Kroz Valpovo svud veselje teče,nek' se pamti to čarobno veče'!

So this Summer might be finer still,Zlatni vez have played for us with skill.All through Valpovo flows pure delight:let us long remember this magic night!

Eto, to je Valpovačko Ljeto,kad se skupi Šokadija mlada,pa kad kolo šorom zavijori,a pod šatrom svakojakog slada.Tom veselju nigdje nema kraja,to je dio zemaljskoga raja.Slavit ćemo Valpovačko Ljeto,do godine četrdeset peto!

So, that's the Valpovo Summer here,when the young Šokadija gather round,when the kolo flutters down the lane,and in tents all kinds of treats abound.No end to this merriment, and soit's a part of paradise below.We will keep the Valpovo Summer heretill the forty-fifth one, come next year!

Valpovo, 6. 6. 2012.

Valpovo, June 6, 2012

Translator's note. Valpovačko ljeto ("Valpovo Summer") is the town's annual folklore festival, held around the castle; this poem walks through the programme of its 44th year. Šlingani ručnici are towels with eyelet embroidery; Zlatni vez ("Golden Embroidery") is a band.
