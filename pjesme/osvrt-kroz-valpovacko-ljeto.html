Domoljubne i zavičajne

Osvrt kroz Valpovačko ljeto

Homeland and native region

A Tour of the Valpovo Summer

Divna smotra Valpovačkog Ljeta,od davnina u Valpovu cvjeta.Četrdeset četvrta je sada,ta ljepota Valpovačkog grada.

The lovely show, the Valpovo Summer, herehas flowered in Valpovo for many a year.The forty-fourth one's come around today:the beauty of Valpovo town, I say.

U četvrtak stiže program novi:koncert daju pjevački zborovi.Mnoga društva svoje slike slažu,pa i lovci trofeje izlažu.

On Thursday there's a new programme in store:the choirs give a concert, and there's more.And many clubs put pictures up on show,and hunters set their trophies in a row.

Promocija "Tete Eve knjiga",pa izložba šlinganih ručnika,na izložbi mnoge žene sadanude robu od vezenog rada.

Then Auntie Eva's book launch, and on view,the eyelet-worked embroidered towels, too;and at the show, the many women therenow offer wares they've embroidered with care.

U petak se pogledati mora:divna smotra dječjega folklora.Sedamnaesti godišnjak još teče,to bi bilo sve za ovo veče'.

On Friday there's a thing that must be seen:the lovely children's folklore show, I mean.This is the seventeenth year it has run,and that's the programme for the evening done.

Kad puhači trubom zasvirajui palicom cure žongliraju,ta ljepota na trgu se gleda,to je dio Valpovačkog Ljeta.

When the brass players blow their trumpets loud,and girls twirl batons, juggling for the crowd,that lovely sight is watched upon the square:it's part of the Valpovo Summer's fare.

I slikari svoj su obol dali,u subotu slike naslikali.Amaterska revija pjevača,rakijada da tijelo ojača.

The painters, too, have offered up their share:on Saturday they painted pictures there.An amateur revue of singers' song,a rakija show to make the body strong.

Osvanula i nedjelja sveta,oko dvorca sve divno izgleda,posvuda su šatre načičkane,raznovrsnom robom natrpane.

Then holy Sunday dawned, so fine and fair,and round the castle, all looks lovely there.The tents are packed in tightly everywhere,crammed full with every kind of merchant's ware.

Kad povorka prema dvorcu krene,na ulici: djeca, ljudi, žene,svi gledaju tu ljepotu snova:kako petnaest nastupa KUD-ova.

When the procession heads for the castle, thenout in the street are children, women, men;all watch this dreamlike beauty come to be:fifteen KUDs perform for all to see.

Folkloraša iz Hrvatske cijele,mladež naša kako se vesele.Mnoge smotre u Valpovu gradu,konji kasom idu uz paradu.

Folk dancers come from all Croatia here,and how our young folk revel in the cheer!So many shows in Valpovo are played,and horses trot along beside the parade.

Kad u dvorac stignu folklorašii sviraju kolo tamburašii šokica kad ugleda lolu,od miline vrisne u tom kolu.

When the folk dancers reach the castle, andthe kolo's played by the tambura band,and when a Šokica spies her sweetheart near,she squeals for joy within that kolo here.

Da bi ovo Ljeto uljepšali,Zlatni vez su muziku nam dali.Kroz Valpovo svud veselje teče,nek' se pamti to čarobno veče'!

To make this Summer lovelier to hear,the Zlatni vez gave music to our ear.All Valpovo is flowing with delight —let it be long remembered, this magic night!

Eto, to je Valpovačko Ljeto,kad se skupi Šokadija mlada,pa kad kolo šorom zavijori,a pod šatrom svakojakog slada.Tom veselju nigdje nema kraja,to je dio zemaljskoga raja.Slavit ćemo Valpovačko Ljeto,do godine četrdeset peto!

So that's the Valpovo Summer: when they meet,the young Šokadija, gathered round,and when the kolo flutters down the street,and under tents all kinds of treats abound.And there's no end to all this merry cheer,it is a part of paradise, right here.We'll celebrate the Valpovo Summer, and thennext year, the forty-fifth, we'll meet again!

Valpovo, 6. 6. 2012.

Valpovo, June 6, 2012

Translator's note. Valpovačko ljeto ("Valpovo Summer") is the town's annual folklore festival, held around the castle; this poem walks through the programme of its 44th year. Šlingani ručnici are towels with eyelet embroidery; Zlatni vez ("Golden Embroidery") is a band.
