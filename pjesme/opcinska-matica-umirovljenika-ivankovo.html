Umirovljenički jadi

Općinska matica umirovljenika Ivankovo

Pensioners' woes

The Municipal Pensioners' Society of Ivankovo

POŠTOVANA GENERACIJO!

ESTEEMED GENERATION!

TOPLI POZDRAV VAM SVIM ŽELIMI SKUPŠTINI VAŠOJ SE VESELIM!

WARMEST GREETINGS, ALL OF YOU, I SEND,AND YOUR MEETING GLADDENS ME NO END!

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što, Željko, vaš predsjednik kažeja čestitam svima vama sada.

Now I hear the fine results you've made,your success, the course your work will steer;what your president, Željko, has conveyed:I congratulate you all right here.

I prošla je godinica ova,sve uspjehe od prošloga rada,u toku je i skupština nova,nek' uspjeha bude k'o do sada.

And this year of yours has gone its way,with the triumphs that your work has won;now the new assembly meets today:may success go on as it has done.

I skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ista sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

Let the meeting, at this very hour,back the Government that holds the reins,for with plenty of élan it willlead the club's work on to future gains.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For the deeds that you have carried outwith your labour in this passing year,I wish you still more success to come,won in the new year that's drawing near.

Vaše društvo u sastavu ima,zbor pjevački zvane "Perunike",pjesme poje diljem Slavonije,vašem društvu počasne su dike.

Your society counts in its ranksa singing choir, the "Perunike" by name;all across Slavonia they sing,and they are your honour, pride and fame.

Kada pjesma nježno dušu dira,da bi srcu toplu ljubav dala:Marija je, časna Cavor-Mira,"Perunike" društvo osnovala.

When a song, so gentle, brings love nearer,warming soul and heart with tender glow,it was Marija, honoured Cavor-Mira,who founded the "Perunike" choir we know.

U nastupu svojim toplim glasom,ni sirene nisu im na diku,zato svome mjestu Ivankovu,one tvore božanstvenu sliku.

In performance, with their voices warm,not the Sirens could with them compete;and so, for their own Ivankovo,they form a picture heavenly and sweet.

Pa je zato i meni do dike,moje pjesme poje "Perunike".Nek' vam bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas: pjesnik Benak Ivo!

And so I'm proud as well, and glad to seethe "Perunike" singing songs by me.May life be merry, lively, full of cheer:the poet Benak Ivo greets you here!

Valpovo, 29. 11. 2012.

Valpovo, November 29, 2012

Translator's note. Ivankovo is a village near Vinkovci. Perunike ("The Irises") is the village choir that sang his poems.
