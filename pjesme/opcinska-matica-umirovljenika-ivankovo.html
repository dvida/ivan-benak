Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Općinska matica umirovljenika Ivankovo

Occasional verse and dedications

The Municipal Pensioners' Society of Ivankovo

POŠTOVANA GENERACIJO!

HONOURED GENERATION!

TOPLI POZDRAV VAM SVIM ŽELIMI SKUPŠTINI VAŠOJ SE VESELIM!

WARM GREETINGS TO YOU ALL I WISH TO SEND,AND I REJOICE IN THE ASSEMBLY YOU ATTEND!

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što, Željko, vaš predsjednik kažeja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear of all the splendid results you've won,success, and guidelines for the work you do;and for what Željko, your president, has said,I now congratulate each one of you.

I prošla je godinica ova,sve uspjehe od prošloga rada,u toku je i skupština nova,nek' uspjeha bude k'o do sada.

And so this little year has passed away,with all the gains the past work had in store;the new assembly's under way today:may it be just as fruitful as before.

I skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ista sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And may the assembly at this hoursupport the present leadership, I say,for, full of élan as it is, it willlead the society on its future way.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the deeds that you have done, and allyour labour in the year that we've been through,I wish you more success, and may it fallto you in the new year that's coming, too.

Vaše društvo u sastavu ima,zbor pjevački zvane "Perunike",pjesme poje diljem Slavonije,vašem društvu počasne su dike.

Your society has, within its ranks,a singing choir, the "Perunike" by name;they sing their songs across Slavonia,your society's honoured pride and fame.

Kada pjesma nježno dušu dira,da bi srcu toplu ljubav dala:Marija je, časna Cavor-Mira,"Perunike" društvo osnovala.

When song so gently touches the soul, to bringa warm and tender love into the heart:it was Marija, honoured Cavor-Mira,who founded the "Perunike" from the start.

U nastupu svojim toplim glasom,ni sirene nisu im na diku,zato svome mjestu Ivankovu,one tvore božanstvenu sliku.

When they perform, with voices warm and clear,not even sirens could compare, it's true;and so, for their own Ivankovo, theycreate a picture that's divine to view.

Pa je zato i meni do dike,moje pjesme poje "Perunike".Nek' vam bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas: pjesnik Benak Ivo!

And so it gives me pride as well, I find,that the "Perunike" sing these poems of mine.May merry, lively times be yours to know:with greetings from the poet, Benak Ivo!

Valpovo, 29. 11. 2012.

Valpovo, November 29, 2012

Translator's note. Ivankovo is a village near Vinkovci. Perunike ("The Irises") is the village choir that sang his poems.
