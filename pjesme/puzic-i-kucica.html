The Little Snail and His House

Naprtio pužićbaš ovoga ljetakućicu na leđapa se poljem šeta.

The little snail, this summer,without further ado,put his house upon his backand strolls the meadow through.

Govorili mravikraj puževe kuće:"Sigurno je teškakad se tako vuče."

The ants were saying, standingnear the snail's house, so:"It surely must be heavy,the way he drags it slow."

Odgovara pužić:"Meni teška nije,ljeti imam hlada,a zimi me grije."

The little snail replies:"It's not heavy, not for me;in summer I have shade,in winter, warm I'll be."

Pitale ga glistešto se tako vuče:"Mogu kako hoću,kod svoje sam kuće!"

The earthworms asked him whyhe dragged it all about:"I can do as I please —this is my own house!"
