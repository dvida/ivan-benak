Humorous poems

The Merry Adventures of Mata the Driver

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Sviće zora. Ja na cesti.Preda mnom je put daleki.Da ubijem tu samoću,gledam ima l' putnik neki.

Dawn is breaking. On the road,a long way before me lies.Just to kill the loneliness,I keep watch for passers-by.

Motor radi, tiho prede.Zlatna zora zrake sije.Pokraj ceste žensko stoji,kosa joj na vjetru vije.

Engine humming, softly purring,golden dawn casts rays about;by the roadside stands a woman;in the wind her hair streams out.

Već žmigavac desni radi.Ne znam da l' je san il' java?Motor stao, ni da mrdne,a preda mnom cura plava.

Now the right-hand blinker's going.Is it dreaming, is it true?Engine's stopped, it will not budge,and a blonde girl's in my view.

Još se nisam ni snašao.U kabinu sjelo luče.Kao da smo stari znanci,veselo mi ona guče.

I had hardly caught my breath:in the cab there sat a dear;just as if we were old friends,she cooed merrily in my ear.

Ja namjestim retrovizor.Put krivuda lijevo – desno.Kad ugledah pogled njezin,meni oko srca tijesno.

I adjust the rear-view mirror;road bends left, and bends to right.When I caught the look she gave me,round my heart it all went tight.

Mislim, baš je zgodna mala.Gledam: glavu, noge, grudi.Čarobno se smiješi mačka,šofersku mi strast probudi.

"She's a cute one," so I'm thinking,eyeing head and legs, breasts too.How the kitten smiles, bewitching!And my trucker's passion grew.

Sunce prži. Motor grije.Skidati se poče dama.Više ne znam kuda vozim.Na oči mi pala tama.

Sun is scorching. Engine's hot.Now the lady starts undressing.Where I'm driving, I forgot;darkness falls across my eyes.

Zadrhtaše moje ruke.Lijevo – desno put krivuda.Tijelo moje ognjem gori.Skrenut moram, gledam kuda.

Then my hands began to tremble.Left and right the road wound round.All my body burned with fire;I must turn off: I look around.

Već je prvi mračak pao.Pokraj ceste divna šuma.U sjeni sam hrasta stao,da ne pukne prednja guma.

The first dusk had fallen now.By the road, a lovely wood.In an oak tree's shade I stopped,lest the front tyre burst for good.

Valpovo, 17. 04. 1983.

Valpovo, April 17, 1983

Translator's note. Benak was himself a professional driver.
