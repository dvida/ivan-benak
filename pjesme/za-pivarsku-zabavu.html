For the Brewers' Party

Nek' vam bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas iz Valpova Ivo.Osječko se pivo rado pije,u Hrvatskoj to je najstarije.Kad bi znali, dragi ljudi moji,kol'ko ljeta pivovara broji.Godine je sagrađena jedne;šest stotina devedeset sedme.To je bilo sedamnaestog vijeka,u Šamačkoj ulici Osijeka.Dvadesetak koraka uz Dravu,sagradio Šeper pivovaru.Nakon ručka kad je dobro jelo,pijem pivo crno ili bijelo.Al' kad auto ja vozim uživo,onda pijem samo BAJKER pivo.Za dobroga pivopiju važim,ŠEPEROVO pivo svuda tražim.Vjerujte mi, istina je živa,rado pijem ja ESEKER piva.Pa doživjeh sada sreću pravu,na pivarsku ja dođoh zabavu.I sa sobom vodim familiju,k'o pačići rado pivo piju.Došla mi je: kćerka, sin i žena,snaha moja, pa i sestra njena.Kako imam rodbinu veliku,vodim kuma i njegovu Milku.

May you be merry, lively, full of cheer:from Valpovo, Ivo sends his greetings here!Osječko is the beer folk drink with zest:Croatia's oldest, older than the rest.My dear good people, if you only knewhow many years this brewery's come through!The year that it was built? Well, here's the one:six hundred ninety-seven, then 'twas done.It was the seventeenth century, you know,in Šamačka Street in Osijek, long ago.Some twenty paces from the Drava, thereŠeper built the brewery, I declare.When after lunch I've had a hearty bite,I drink my beer, it may be dark or light.But when I'm at the wheel, live, as you see,then only BAJKER beer will do for me.I'm counted as a beer man, good and true,and everywhere ask for ŠEPEROVO brew.Believe me, friends, the living truth is here:I gladly drink my share of ESEKER beer.And now real joy has come my way, I say:I've come along to the brewers' party today!I've brought my family along with me:like little ducklings, they drink beer with glee.My daughter's come, my son, my wife beside,my daughter-in-law, her sister at her side.And since my family's so large a crew,I bring my kum, and bring his Milka too.

Uzoriti ja sam zet na diku,svuda nosim puničinu sliku.Znam kad mi je rodila se ženai ostala familija njena.I tko želi mojom Marom leći,al' ne mogu sada javno reći.Znam kada se teli stricu kravai tko s Katom komšinicom spava.Znam kad Joza ide snaši Juli,jer kroz moje dvorište projuri.Eto, sve sam lijepo razložio,samo da bih nagradu dobio.Sada znate, želja mi je živa,da osvojim koji sanduk piva.I neka nam tako svako veče,iz Osijeka slatko pivo teče.Mnogo sreće u Osijeku gradu,pivarcima u njihovom radu.Da nikada za štrajk ne bi znali,ni u krizu financijsku pali.Da im kasa bude uvijek puna,za blokadu ne znali računa.Konobari u brzome kasu,svome gazdi napunili kasu.Dosta para ostalo i njima,sretno večer i zabave svima.Svi prisutni, ovdje sretni bilii Osječko pivo u slast pili.Jer Osječko najbolje je pivo,to vam veli pjesnik Benak Ivo.

A model son-in-law, a source of pride,I take my wife's mum's picture far and wide.I know the day my wife was born, it's true,and when the rest of her family were, too.And who would like to bed my Mara, thoughI can't say that in public now, you know.I know when Uncle's cow will calve, and more:who sleeps with Kata, the neighbour next door.I know when Joza pays young Jula a call,because he rushes through my yard, that's all.So there, I've laid it all out, nice and right,just so the prize might come my way tonight.So now you know my wish, alive and keen:to win a crate or so of beer, I mean.And so, each evening, may it come to passthat sweet beer flows from Osijek to our glass.Good luck to Osijek, to that city fair,and to the brewers in their labour there.May strikes be something they will never know,and may no money crisis lay them low.May all their tills be always full, I pray,and frozen bank accounts stay far away.May waiters, trotting briskly, fill the tillup for their boss, and fill it with a will.And may there be enough left for them, too:a happy evening, fun for all of you!May all who've gathered here be happy, anddrink Osječko with relish, glass in hand.For Osječko is the finest beer, you know:so says the poet, Benak Ivo.

Valpovo, 12. 02. 1989.

Valpovo, February 12, 1989

Translator's note. Written for a party and prize contest of the Osijek brewery, founded in 1697 by Šeper; Osječko, Bajker, Šeperovo and Eseker are its beers. In Croatian the waiters' "trot" (kas) and the boss's "till" (kasa) rhyme as the same word, kasu.
