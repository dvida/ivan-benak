Love poems

Dawn Brings You Back into My Arms

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U mislima s tobom, zorom se budim,kada se zlatno sunašce rađa.U grudima, draga, svuda te nosim.Od ljubavnog žara postaješ mlađa.

In thoughts with you, I wake up with the dawn,as the small golden sun is being born;I carry you, my darling, in my breast —love's ardour makes you younger every morn.

Na krilima mašte negdje daleko,nas sretne odvozi ljubavna lađa.Sa usana tvojih poljupce skidam.Svakog si dana sve slađa i slađa.

On wings of fancy, somewhere far away,the boat of love bears us away, content.I pluck the kisses from your lips, and yougrow sweeter, sweeter, every day that's spent.

I onda opet na izvoru noći,zvijezdama kada se mjesec vraća.Naša se voljena stapaju srca,a slatki poljubac, poljupcem plaća.

And then again, at the wellspring of the night,when the moon returns along the starry ways,our loving hearts are melting into one,and one sweet kiss another kiss repays.

Mi se gubimo u oblaku sreće,noć mi je tada blažena i kraća,a zlatnim zracima veselu, sretnu,zora te meni u zagrljaj vraća.

We lose ourselves within a cloud of joy,my night is blissful then, and flies apace,and, glad and happy, on its golden rays,the dawn brings you back into my embrace.

Valpovo, 06. 07. 1983.

Valpovo, July 6, 1983
