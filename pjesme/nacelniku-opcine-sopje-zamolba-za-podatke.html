Načelniku Općine Sopje: zamolba za podatke

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Head of Sopje Municipality: A Request for Information

Oprostite gosp'on Jozo,što zamolbu ovu šaljem.Vjerujte mi, želja mi jeda ljepotu Sopju dajem.

Pardon me, dear Mister Jozo,that I send you this request;but believe me, it's my wish toshow our Sopje at its best.

Podatke mi pošaljiteda bih znao nešto više:stvarnu vrijednost našeg sela,sad malenkost moja piše.

Please send me the facts and figures,so I'll know a little moreof our village's true value:that is what I'm writing for.

Stanovnika kol'ko ima,od kad svoje ime nosi,sa čime se Sopje bavii sa čime se ponosi.

How many souls it numbers,since when it has borne its name,what our Sopje works and lives by,and what brings it pride and fame.

Napišite sve što treba,da bi Sopje proslavili.Dični ljudi onog kraja,za ljepotu Sopja znali.

Write down everything that's needed,so that Sopje we may praise,so the worthy folk of those partslearn of Sopje's lovely ways.

Ja Vam šaljem ovu molbu,da bi mogli Vi saznati,sve podatke našeg sela,što trebate meni dati.

I am sending this requestso that you may seek them out:all the facts about our villagethat I'm asking you about.

Želja mi je Načelnice,ovu pjesmu obdaniti.Jednog dana ja u Sopju,i javno ju obznaniti.

It's my wish, dear Mister Mayor,that this poem sees the light,and one day I'll stand in Sopjeand recite it, as is right.

Na trudu Vam puno hvalai neće Vam biti krivo.Najtoplije pozdravlja Vasiz Valpova pjesnik Ivo.

Many thanks for all your trouble,and you'll not regret it, no;sending you his warmest greetings:Ivo, poet of Valpovo.

Valpovo, 20. 11. 2010.

Valpovo, November 20, 2010

Translator's note. A načelnik is the elected head of a Croatian municipality, roughly its mayor. He did write the poem, "Nek' se slavi u Sopju veselje", in 2012.
