Occasional verse and dedications

The Poet's Wish

2011

JA VAM DJECO DONOSIM PROLJEĆETOPLO VRIJEME I MIRISNO CVIJEĆE!

To you, dear children, springtime's what I bring:warm weather, fragrant flowers blossoming!

Da Vam kažem, djeco mila,oduvjek mi želja bila,da u selo Sopje priđem,Vašu školu da obiđem.

Let me tell you, children dear,long I've wished that I'd come here,to your village, Sopje, soI could see your school, you know.

Na puno sam mjesta bio,mnogo škola pohodio,ali često ja sam snio,da bi Vašu posjetio.

Many places I have gone,many schools I've called upon,yet I often dreamed, it's true,that I'd come and visit you.

Ono što je pjesnik snio,sanak mu se ostvario.Sada mi je želja živa,da sa Vama čitam štiva.

What the poet used to dream,now his little dream's come true.Now my eager wish is this:to read verses here with you.

Pjesmice iz mojih knjiga,to će biti moja dika.Iskrena je moja želja,pružiti Vam sat veselja.

Little poems from my books:they will be my pride and joy.And my heartfelt wish is clear:to bring you an hour of cheer.

21. 03. 2011.

March 21, 2011

Translator's note. Written for his visit to the primary school in Sopje, his native village, on March 21, 2011, the first day of spring.
