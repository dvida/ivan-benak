Prigodne pjesme i posvete

„Atrij“ dom je kod Slišković Ane

Occasional verse and dedications

Atrij, a Home at Ana Slišković's

Darda ima dom za stare ljude,tu mnogima utočište nude.To je ATRIJ kod gospođe Ane,da ugodno tu provode dane.

In Darda there's a home where old folk stay,a refuge for so many, night and day.It's Atrij, Mrs Ana's, meant to bea place to spend one's days agreeably.

Svima vama što ste u tom domu,usamljeni u životu svomu.U ljepoti provodili dane,u ATRIJU kod gospođe Ane.

To all of you who live within this home,lonely in life, and living it alone:may all your days in beauty flow and runin Atrij, Mrs Ana's, every one.

Tuga vas je morila u tami,noć je tiha, vi kod kuće sami,ali ovdje blažene su noći,gospe Ane ATRIJ će pomoći.

In darkness, sorrow wore away at you;the night was still, at home alone, just you;but here the nights are blessed ones, it's true,and Mrs Ana's Atrij will help you through.

Sjetite se kad ste mladi bilii ljubavne snove dok ste snilii starost je ljepša kad se druži,to druženje ATRIJ će da pruži.

Remember when you still were young, and whenyou dreamed of love, the dreams you dreamed back then;old age is lovelier in company, too,and that is what Atrij will give to you.

I starački dovukli se jadi,kao nekad niste više mladi.Vi potrebe imate po noći,osoblje će ATRIJA pomoći.

And now the woes of age have crept your way;you are not young, as you were in your day.At night you have your needs, as old folk do,and Atrij's staff will be on hand for you.

Nakon snova kad otvoriš oči,sve što trebaš ATRIJ će pomoći.Dobar ručak i društvo te čeka,prije snova posteljica meka.

When, after dreams, you open up your eyes,whatever you may need, Atrij supplies.A good lunch and good company await,and, before dreams, a soft bed, neatly made.

Pa zar može nešto bolje biti,u snovima divne snove sniti?Vaša mladost u snove se vrati,to vam može samo ATRIJ dati.

So could there be a better thing than this:in dreams to dream such dreams of loveliness?Your youth returns in dreams to visit you,and that Atrij alone can give to you.

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2012.

Valpovo, March 28, 2012

Translator's note. Written for Atrij, a home for the elderly in Darda run by Ana Slišković.
