Come Into My Arms

Misli moje, voljena,sa željom k' tebi hrle,ruke moje htjele binježno da te grle.

My thoughts, my beloved,with longing rush to you,my arms would like to hold youtenderly and true.

Ti ne znaš patnju srca,ni boli mojih grudi,jer pogled oka tvogau meni čežnju budi.

You do not know my heart's torment,nor the pain my breast holds deep,for just a glance from your eyeswakes longing from its sleep.

Zato dođi, voljena,u nježni zagrljaj,da te ljubim, milujem,jer ja te volim znaj.

So come to me, beloved,into my arms so tender,to let me kiss, caress you,for I love you — remember.

Valpovo, 20. 08. 1982.

Valpovo, August 20, 1982
