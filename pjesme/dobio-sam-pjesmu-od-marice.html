Dobio sam pjesmu od Marice

Occasional verse and dedications

I've Had a Poem from Marica

Neki dan me poštar posjetioi predivno pismo mi donio,kad pogledam pečat Čađavice,znao jesam da je od Marice.Čitam pismo, čini mi se lično,da je malo na pjesmicu slično.Napisano u dobrome skladu,da je Mara u velikom radu:Krumpir vadi i pekmeza kuha,paradajza da je bolja juhai za svoje drage ukućane,pripremit će za zimnicu hrane.Poznato je, žene puno rade,u proljeće razno bilje sade.Dođe jesen, priroda se zlati,žena opet za zimnicu radi.Radi Mara i ugodno diše,pored posla i pjesmice piše.Do tog rada neka ti je stalo,divno kad je i zabave malo,neka bude to ponos i dika,to je želja svakoga pjesnika,pogotovo kad nastupa javno,za pjesnika to je djelo slavno.Ne bih više, toliko za sada,do sljedećeg tvog pismenog rada.Eto i ja u radu sam bio,u Baziju šljive sam kupioi sad gledam kako šljive zriju,čekam vrijeme da pečem rakiju.U Valpovu, desetoga rujna,dv'je tisuće i trinaestog ljeta.Poezija nek' ti bude bujna,piši Maro, dok ti mašta cvjeta.

The postman came to visit me one dayand brought a lovely letter on his way;I looked: the postmark said Čađavica,and so I knew it came from Marica.I read it, and it struck me, as I read,a little like a poem, it must be said.Composed in good accord, it told me howour Mara's very busy working now:she's digging spuds, she's cooking jam, and more,tomatoes, for a better soup, in store,and for the dear ones in her house she'll laysome food by for the winter, put away.It's common knowledge: women work a lot;in spring they plant all kinds in every plot.Then autumn comes, and nature turns to gold,and wives again put stores up for the cold.So Mara works, and breathes content and free,and, next to all her work, writes poetry.Keep caring for that work, as you should do;it's lovely when there's some amusement too.And may it be your pride and joy to own,the wish of every poet ever known,especially on stage, in public view:for poets, that's a glorious thing to do.I'll stop right here, that's all for now, my friend,until the next work that you write and send.I too have had my share of work, you see:I bought a load of plums in Bazije,and now I watch the plums grow ripe, untilit's time to make rakija in the still.In Valpovo, the tenth day of September,in the year two thousand and thirteen.Let your poetry run rich and lush, remember:write, Mara, while your fancy's blooming green.

Sve pjesmice posloži od slova,pozdravlja te Ivo iz Valpova!

Arrange your poems letter by letter, do;Ivo from Valpovo sends his best to you!

Valpovo

Valpovo

Translator's note. A verse reply to a letter-poem from Marica (Mara), a fellow poet from Čađavica. Rakija is the home-distilled fruit brandy of Slavonia, here made from plums.
