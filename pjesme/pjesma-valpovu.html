Pjesma Valpovu

Homeland and native region

A Song to Valpovo

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Tu mi žive preci od davnina.U pitomom kraju meni dragom,u ljepoti slavonskih ravnina,u Valpovu, u gradiću blagom.

Here my forebears have lived since ancient days,in the tranquil country I love so,in the beauty of Slavonia's plains,in Valpovo, the gentle town I know.

Nekad davno, šume te kitile,Slavonijo, ponos srca moga.U krošnjama ptice gnijezdo vile,nadaleko hrasta čuvenoga.

Long ago the forests used to deck you,O Slavonia, pride of this my heart;in the crowns of oak renowned afarbirds would build their nests in every part.

Nestale su šume i lugovi,naokolo polje se razvilo.Zaoraše rala i plugovi,u tom raju Valpovo se svilo.

Gone are all the forests and the groves,all around the open fields have grown;wooden ploughs and ploughshares turned the soil,in that paradise Valpovo made its home.

Od te šume što je nekad bila,svu ljepotu i bogatstva svoja,igrajući kolo šumska vila,nama dala krasna perivoja.

Of the forest that once used to stand,all its beauty and its riches there,the forest fairy, dancing in the kolo,gave to us: a lovely park to share.

Kad u parku ptice zapjevaju,nigdje nema tako lijepog kraja.Rodne njive plodove nam daju,to je dio zemaljskoga raja.

When the birds begin to sing in the park,nowhere is a land as fair as this;fertile fields give us their fruit in plenty —it's a part of earthly paradise.

Tek u ljetu kad sunce zažeže,zlatnog klasja puna je dolina.Sve je ravno dokle oko seže,to bogatstvo gledat je milina.

When the summer sun begins to blaze,golden ears fill all the valley plain;flat as far as ever eye can see —it's a joy to see such wealth of grain.

Dođe jesen i lišće uvene,dani kratki, a noć je velika.Kada mi se sklope oči snene,Valpovo je opet meni dika.

Autumn comes, the leaves begin to wither,short the days, the night is vast and wide;when my drowsy eyes at last are closing,Valpovo once more remains my pride.

Valpovo, 03.11.1974.

Valpovo, November 3, 1974
