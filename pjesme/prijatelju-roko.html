Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Prijatelju Roko!

Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend Roko!

Imam jednog prijatelja,Roko mu je ime.Kamerman je S T V-eaponosi se time.

I have got a friend of mine,Roko is his name.He's a cameraman at S T V,which he's proud to claim.

Ja mu dajem ovu knjigu,tu "ZEKINU SREĆU",trebat će mu da je čita,za obitelj veću.

I now give this book to him,this, my "BUNNY'S LUCK";he'll be needing it to readto his growing flock.

Čitaj djeci prije snova,da bi bolje snili,a zorica kada svane,da bi vedri bili.

Read it to the kids at bedtime,so they'll dream the better,and when little dawn is breaking,they'll wake up the brighter.

A zeko će u snovima,da ih brižno pazi.Nakon snova sinak će setatici da mazi.

And the bunny, in their dreaming,will watch over them with care;after dreams, his little sonwill cuddle up to Daddy there.

Eto, Roko, to su želje,što Ti pjesnik želi.Iz "ZEKINE SREĆE" čitaj,djecu razveseli.

There, Roko, these are the wishesthat the poet sends your way:read to them from "BUNNY'S LUCK",make the children glad today.

Poštovanom prijateljuRoku Gurdon, ugodnetrenutke: želi autor!

To my esteemed friendRoko Gurdon, pleasantmoments — wishes the author!

Valpovo, 22. 12. 2013.

Valpovo, December 22, 2013

Translator's note. Roko Gurdon was a cameraman at STV, Slavonska televizija (Slavonian Television). The book is Benak's children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck).
