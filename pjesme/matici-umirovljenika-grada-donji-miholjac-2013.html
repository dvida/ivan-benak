Umirovljenički jadi

Matici umirovljenika grada Donji Miholjac

2013.

Pensioners' woes

To the Pensioners' Union of the Town of Donji Miholjac

2013

TOPLI POZDRAV VAM SVIM ŽELIMI SKUPŠTINI VAŠOJ SE VESELIM!

To all of you warm greetings I extend,and your assembly gladdens me no end!

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što, Mira, predsjednica kažeja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear of all the fine results you've won,your good success, the course your work will take,and what your president, Mira, has said;to each of you my compliments I make.

I prošla je godinica ova,sve uspjehe od prošloga rada,u toku je i skupština nova,nek' uspjeha bude k'o do sada.

This little year of yours has also passed,with all the triumphs that your work has won;the new assembly's under way at last:may it succeed the way the old has done.

I skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ista sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And let the assembly, at this very hour,stand firmly by the Government in place,for it will lead, with plenty of élan,the club in all its work in coming days.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the deeds that you have carried outthrough all your work within this passing year,I wish you still more triumphs, and that youachieve them in the new year drawing near.

Vaše društvo u sastavu ima,zbor pjevački zvane "Miholjanke",koje vodi gospođa Stanišić,vašem društvu počasne su dike.

Your club includes within its companya singing choir, the "Miholjanke" by name,and Mrs Stanišić it is who leads;to your society they're pride and fame.

Kada pjesma nježno dušu dira,da bi srcu toplu ljubav dala:poštovana Ljerka Stanišić je,„Miholjanke“ časno uvježbala.

When songs so gently touch the very souland to the heart a love so warm impart,it's our respected Ljerka Stanišićwho trained the "Miholjanke" in their art.

U nastupu svojim toplim glasom,ni sirene nisu im na diku,zato svome dragome Miholjcu,one tvore božanstvenu sliku.

In their performance, with their voices warm,not even Sirens could with them compete;and so, for their beloved Miholjac,they form a picture heavenly and sweet.

Budite i dalje tako vrijedni,u radu će vama uspjeh doći,a zaštitnik vašega Miholjcabit će vama uvijek u pomoći.

Keep on being as hard-working as you are,and in your work success will come your way,and he who is your Miholjac's patron saintwill always be your helper, come what may.

Sve najbolje ja vam sada želim.Ne bilo vam sreći nikad kraja.A penziju primali što duže,Ne tražite brzo vrata raja!

I wish you all the best that there can be;may your good fortune never reach its end;and draw your pension long and happily,and don't rush off to heaven's gate, my friend!

Valpovo, 12. 07. 2013.

Valpovo, July 12, 2013

Translator's note. A second greeting (2013) to the same association. The "Government" is his joke name for the newly elected committee; the town's patron is Saint Michael, after whom Miholjac is named.
