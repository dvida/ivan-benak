Don't Blame Me

Ne krivi mene što te žarko ljubim,ni srce moje što za tebe bije.Bez ljubavi tvoje ja nadu gubim.Bez smijeha tvoga, draga, tužno mi je.

Don't blame me that I love you ardently,nor blame my heart for beating so for you;without your love, all hope is lost to me,without your laughter, darling, I am blue.

Ne krivi mene za te divne snove,što svakog dana ja snivam o tebi.Kad tebe nema, ćutim boli nove,sve patnje naše pripisujem sebi.

Don't blame me for those wondrous dreams I keepon dreaming every single day of you;when you're not here, I feel new pains run deep,and blame myself for all our suffering too.

Ne krivi mene za sve tanke niti,što nisu čvrsto ljubavlju vezane.Za našu patnju skrivila si i tii boli moje u srcu stezane.

Don't blame me for the slender threads, each onethat was not firmly bound by love and tied;you share the blame for all we've undergone,and for the pains my heart has clenched inside.

Ne krivi mene što te silno ljubimi ljubav žarku je u srcu nosim.Ne krivi mene što za tobom žudimi ljubav tvoju kao prosjak prosim.

Don't blame me that my love for you is strong,and in my heart a burning love I bear;don't blame me that for you I yearn and longand beg you for your love — a beggar's prayer.

Valpovo, 22. 07. 1974.

Valpovo, July 22, 1974
