Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Marija!

Marija Ćorić

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Marija!

Marija Ćorić

I stihovi ove knjige,od mene su iznjedreni,čitateljstvu namijenjeni:a Vama su poklonjeni.U čitanju neka knjiga,ugodno Vam da veselje,stvarajući ovu knjigu,to su bile moje želje.Da bi jutrom vedri bilii ugodne snove snili,nek' Vam bude knjiga ovauspavanka prije snova.

And the verses of this book,brought forth from my heart, and meantfor its readers, every one,and to you as gifts are sent.May the book, as you read through,give you pleasant joy and cheer;when I made this book, these wereall my wishes, most sincere.So that mornings find you brightand you dream sweet dreams at night,let this book, before you sleep,be your lullaby to keep.

Valpovo, 29. 10. 2012.

Valpovo, October 29, 2012

Translator's note. Written in a copy of Stihom kroz život for Marija Ćorić, a teacher at the Vladimir Nazor primary school in Komletinci.
