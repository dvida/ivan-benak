Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Dragutinu Gibičaru

Occasional verse and dedications

To Dragutin Gibičar

Pišem knjige od mladosti rane,ovaj puta tiskana je šesta.Iz Belišća pomoć mi je dalaTuristička Zajednica mjesta.

I've written books since youth, since long ago;and this time it's the sixth that's gone to press.From Belišće came help, as you must know:the Tourist Board of that same town, no less.

Svim ljudima iz te zajednice,najtoplije od srca im HVALA!A posebno Gibičaru Dragi,prijatelju što mi mladost dala.

To all the people in that Board, I say,my warmest, heartfelt THANKS to them are due!And most of all to Gibičar Drago,the friend that my young years gave me, it's true.

Još se sjećam prošlosti daleke,divnih dana koje smo proveli.U TEATRU čitao sam pjesme,družili se kao ljudi zreli.

I still remember that far-distant past,the lovely days that we together knew;there in the THEATRE I read my poems,and we kept company as grown men do.

Promocije pravio je za me,najsretniji ja sam tada bio.Da imadem dobrog prijatelja,oduvijek sam ja u duši snio.

He put on launches of my books for me;the happiest of men was I back then.To have a friend, a good one, all my own,was what my soul had dreamed, time and again.

I starački dovukli se dani,još nam srca ukrotila nije.Ja vjerujem naše prijateljstvoda će vjerno ostat kao prije.

And now the days of age have dragged along,and still they have not tamed our hearts, you see;I do believe our friendship will remainas faithful as it always used to be.

Valpovo, 11. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 11, 2010

Translator's note. A thank-you to Dragutin (Drago) Gibičar, president of the Belišće Tourist Board, which sponsored his sixth book, Stihom kroz život (2010).
