Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Za Miroslava Škoru

Occasional verse and dedications

For Miroslav Škoro

Topli pozdrav, Miroslave,od srca Vam sada šaljem,ovu moju knjigu malusa ljubavlju Vama dajem.

Warmest greetings, Miroslav,from my heart I send to you,and this little book of minewith my love I give you too.

Čestitam Vam gospon' Škoro,za „DRŽAVU, GRAD I SELO“.Mnogo takvih emisijavodili i dalje smjelo.

Congratulations, Mister Škoro,on your "State, Town and Village" show;may you boldly keep on hostingmany more, and never slow.

Uspjeha Vam puno želimu predivnom Vašem radu.Nakon Vaše emisijei za selo Sopje znadu.

Much success I wish you gladlyin the splendid work you do;since your programme went on air,people know of Sopje too.

Zato ova knjiga moja,što je meni sreću dala.Za čuvenost sela Sopjai ona Vam veli HVALA!

That is why this book of mine,which has brought me joy, it's true,for the fame of Sopje villagesays its own "Thank you!" to you.

Valpovo, 06.12.2010.

Valpovo, December 6, 2010

Translator's note. Sent with a copy of his book to the singer and TV host Miroslav Škoro, whose programme "Država, grad i selo" (State, Town and Village) had featured the village of Sopje.
