Umirovljenički jadi

Životna premija

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

Life's Bonus

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Opet prođe godinica cijelai penziću na pleča je sjela.Koliko će još moći da prođe,dok "ANĐELAK" po njega ne dođe?Da ga vodi u lovišta vječnai napusti ta zemaljska sretna.To se često pita kada legne,pa ga nešto oko srca stegne.Bojažljivo on zatvori očii pita se, što će sutra doći?Da mu smanji malo patnje ove,Bog mu dade mladenačke snove.Gle divote, svemogući Bože,iz kreveta on se dići možei nikakva boljka mu ne smeta,jer on ima tek trideset ljeta.Oh ljepote, majko, moja mila,Raširio ruke kao krila.Sve od sreće i od želje jake,on se vinu nebu pod oblake.Pa pogleda dolje u ravnicui ugleda Maju komšinicu.Ruke širi, milo mu se smije,hajde susjed, dođi mi što prije,u zagrljaj da te primi Maja,da ti pruži zemaljskoga raja.Tek što sleti u Majino kriloi uzvikne, janje moje milo!Neka vika prekine milinu,kad to poštar nosi mirovinu.Probudi se sav blažen u sreći,jer još jedan dan će mu proteći.Pa poštara na rakiju zove,da proslavi večerašnje snovei produži ove dane nove,uto baka na doručak zove.Taj trenutak penziću baš prija,to se zove ŽIVOTNA PREMIJA.Mili Bože, lijepi dan me čeka,barem da je ovako dovijeka.Stigla mu je penzijica puna,sad će igrat ulogu tajkuna.Da režije i račune plati,pa do druge da živi u nadi.

Another little year has slipped awayand on the pensioner's shoulders come to stay.How many more can pass him by — and whenwill the "LITTLE ANGEL" come to fetch him then,to lead him to the hunting grounds on highand leave the happy earthly ones behind?He often asks it as he goes to bed,and something clutches at his heart with dread.He timidly shuts both his eyes up tightand wonders what tomorrow brings to light.To ease a little all this pain of his,the Lord sends dreams of youth — and here it is:Oh, wonder, Lord Almighty, can it be?He's able to get out of bed, you see,no ache or ailment troubles him one bit,for he is only thirty — think of it!Oh, mother dear, the beauty of such things!He spreads his arms out wide, as though they're wings.From happiness and longing, strong and deep,he soars up to the clouds in one great leap.Then down upon the plain he looks, and — my! —his neighbour Maja there has caught his eye.She spreads her arms and sweetly smiles with glee:"Come, neighbour, hurry, come along to me,so Maja may receive you in her armsand give you earthly paradise's charms."No sooner does he land on Maja's kneeand cry, "My little lamb, so dear to me!",than shouting cuts his bliss off there and then:the postman's brought the pension round again.He wakes up blissful, full of happiness,for one more day's to pass for him, no less.He calls the postman in for rakija,to toast the dreams he dreamed the night before,and stretch these new days out a little more —when Granny calls to breakfast from the door.This moment is the pensioner's delight;they call that LIFE'S BONUS — and quite right.Dear Lord, a lovely day awaits me, see —if only it would stay so, endlessly!His little pension's here, paid out in full;now he can play the tycoon — what a thrill!Enough to pay the bills and charges due,then live in hope until the next comes through.

Valpovo, 20. 11. 2007.

Valpovo, November 20, 2007

Translator's note. A comic dream of an old pensioner, cut short by the postman bringing the monthly pension. "Premija" means a bonus or prize, and also an insurance premium.
