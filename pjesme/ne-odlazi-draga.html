Ne odlazi, draga

Love poems

Do Not Leave, My Darling

Ne odlazi, draga, ja te molim,ostani još ovu noć kraj mene.Bez ljubavi tvoje duša boli,moja mladost sada tužno vene.

Do not leave me, darling, I am begging,stay beside me one more night, my dear.When I lack your love my soul is aching,and my youth is sadly withering here.

Da utopim bol, patnju i tugu,u naručju slatkom, mila, tvome.Biti svaki trenutak sa tobom,to je slatki melem srcu mome.

Let me drown my pain, my grief and sorrowin your sweet embrace, my dear, my own.Just to be with you in every momentis the sweetest balm my heart has known.

Rastanak je s tobom, draga, tužan,samo čežnja meni boli stvara.Srce pati i čezne za tobom,a tuga mi bolne grudi para.

Sad it is, my darling, to be parted,longing only brings me pain, it's true;while my heart keeps suffering and pining,grief now tears my aching breast in two.

Bez tebe je vrijeme tako pusto,jer znaj, draga, ja te ludo volim!Za trenutak produži mi sreću,ne odlazi noćas ja te molim!

Time without you is so bleak and empty,for, my darling, madly I adore you!Stretch my happiness a moment longer,do not leave tonight, love, I implore you!

Valpovo, 18. 01. 1974.

Valpovo, January 18, 1974
