Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Šlinge

Occasional verse and dedications

Šlinge

Na sjeveru Slavonije,Koška svoja ljeta broji,Šokcima je naseljena,kao ukras tu postoji.

In the north of Slavonia,Koška counts its summers through;home to Šokci, there it standsas an ornament in view.

Iz Koške je Šišić Majaviše dama odabrala,zbor pjevački osnovala,časno ime "ŠLINGE" dala.

Maja Šišić, out of Koška,chose a number of its ladies,formed a singing choir with them,gave the honoured name, "ŠLINGE".

Divne dame toga krajasad pjevaju iz sve duše,u tom zboru skupile se,da si radost srcu pruže.

Lovely ladies of that regionnow they sing with all their might;in that choir they've come togetherso their hearts may take delight.

Kad predivne "ŠLINGE" poje,SIRENE im nisu ravne.Slavonijom nastupaju,postale su vrlo slavne.

When the lovely "ŠLINGE" sing,even SIRENS can't compare;through Slavonia they perform,they've grown famous everywhere.

"ŠLINGE" vodi dična Maja,družina je to vesela.Razne pjesme one pojei ponos su svoga sela.

Worthy Maja leads the "ŠLINGE" —such a merry, cheerful crowd!Songs of every kind they sing,and they make their village proud.

Valpovo, 11. 10. 2012.

Valpovo, October 11, 2012

Translator's note. Šlinge, the name of the women's choir of Koška, is the white cutwork embroidery on Slavonian folk costume.
