Radio burza

Imam žensku bundu za prodaju

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Lady's Fur Coat for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Imam bundu od pravoga nerca,da joj nema na daleko premca.Stigla zima i godina nova,za prodaju bundica je ova.Nije stara, al' ni nova nije,topla bunda k'o pećica grije.Divna bunda, njegovana, fina,mili Bože, gledat ju milina.I šubaru koja dobro grije,sašivenu od krzna nutrije.I još krzno polarne lisice,mjesto šala da nose ženice.U kompletu dajem i lisicu,možda netko kupi za ženicu.Kad ju žena stavi oko vrata,vrjednija je od samih dukata.Sve zajedno prodao bih ženi,koja bi se sad javila meni.Devet stoja eura nek' sprema,jeftinije ni u Poljskoj nema.Ja prodajem dobre, fine stvari,moj telefon još uvijek je stari.Molim kupce ozbiljne i prave,neka mi se na telefon jave.6 5 2 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,ako prodam bit ću vrlo sretan.

I have a coat of mink, the real thing, too —for miles around there's none as fine, it's true.The winter's here, the New Year's on its way,and so this little coat's for sale today.It isn't old — but new it isn't, quite;it warms you like a little stove, just right.A lovely coat, well kept, so fine and neat —dear Lord, just looking at it is a treat!A fur šubara too, that warms you through,sewn out of nutria — I'm selling that too.And arctic fox fur, too, a lovely piece,for little wives to wear as scarves, with ease.I'll add the fox into the set as well —perhaps some man will buy it for his belle.Once round her neck a lady puts it on,it's worth more than gold ducats, every one.I'd sell the lot together to the damewho calls me first — just ring and give your name!Nine hundred euros she should put aside —not even Poland's cheaper, far and wide.I sell fine goods, good things, and that's a fact;my phone's the same old number — still intact.I ask for serious buyers, genuine, fair:just ring me on the phone, I'm always there.Six-five-two — eight hundred twenty-one —and if I sell, I'll be a happy one!

Valpovo, 10. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 10, 2000

Translator's note. One of his verse classified ads for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. He really did read them on air, and the phone number was his own. "Poland" refers to the cheap Polish traders' markets of the 1990s.
