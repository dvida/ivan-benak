Ljubavne pjesme

Zašto majko!

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Love poems

Why, Mother!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Zašto si me, majko, uzdizala?Zašto si me, majko, njegovala?I sa svojim mlijekom zalijevala?Zašto si mi ovaj život dala?!Dok za moje boli nisi znala!!

Why, my mother, did you raise me so?Why, my mother, did you tend me so,and with your own milk water me to grow?Why did you give me this life here below?!While of my pains you did not know!!

Gdje su tvoje riječi pune sreće?Zašto sada lomiš moje cvijeće?Zašto daješ što mi srce neće?Da moj život bez ljubavi teče.Da me srce u grudima peče.

Where are your words of joy? Where are they, pray?Why do you break my flowers now this way?Why give me what my heart would turn away?So that my life flows loveless on its way.So that my heart burns in my breast each day.

Zašto moram svoju tugu kriti?Zašto moram nesretno ljubitii za drugim divne snove sniti?Zašto sretna ja ne mogu biti?Mladost svoju u ljubav uviti.

Why must I hide my grief so none can see?Why must I love, and love unhappily,and dream fair dreams of another, longingly?Why can I not a happy woman be,and wrap my youth in love, as it should be?

Propali su mog života snovi.Shvati, majko, želim život novi.Želim ljubit koga srce voli.Da mi patnje nestanu dozvoli.Skrušeno te moja duša moli!

The dreams of all my life have come apart.Please understand, mother: I want a new start.I want to love the one who has my heart.Allow it, let my suffering depart.Humbly my soul implores you, from the heart!

Valpovo, 26. 12. 1974.

Valpovo, December 26, 1974

Translator's note. A young woman's lament to her mother, pleading to be allowed to love the one her heart has chosen.
