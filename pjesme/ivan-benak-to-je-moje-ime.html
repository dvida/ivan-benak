Ivan Benak, That Is My Name

Od rođenja ponosim se njimeJa sam rođen u blizini DraveU dolini polja i livadaOkoline Podravske SlatineŠokice me porodila mladaTo je bilo trideset i trećeU mjesecu kad se žito želoDva'jst devetog a bio je petakPa sam svakom veselju početak

From my birth I've borne this name with pride.I was born close by the Drava's side,in a valley of fields and meadows wide,in the country round Podravska Slatina.A young Šokica gave birth to me;it was in nineteen thirty-three,in the month when they were reaping wheat,the twenty-ninth, and Friday was the day,and so with me all fun gets under way.

Translator's note. He was born on Friday, June 29, 1933, near Podravska Slatina. The verse appears to be the opening of a longer poem.
