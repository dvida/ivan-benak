Radio burza

Prodaja pilića

Radio market

Chicks for Sale

Pilići mali, tek su progledali,bez svoje majke oni su ostali.

Tiny chicks, their eyes have just now opened wide;they're left without their mother by their side.

Samo cvrkuću, milina neka,ne znaju kakva ih sudbina čeka.

They only cheep and chirp, a joy to hear;they don't know what fate waits for them, I fear.

Možda ih netko udomi za se,skoro badava dajem ih, zna se.

Perhaps somebody takes them home to keep —I'm all but giving them away, dirt cheap.

Zato sad javljam, da bi tko znao,za pet kunića, ja bi ih dao.

So now I'm telling you, so people know:for five small kunas, I will let them go.

Oni su zdravi, nemaju gripu,sve ih prodajem u jednom hipu.

They're healthy, and they haven't got the flu;I'm selling all of them in one go, too.

Neka se kupac sa cvrkutom javi,na moj telefon jedini i pravi.

Let buyers ring me up and chirp helloon my one proper telephone, you know.

Šest, pet, dva, osam, dva, jedan,da ih zbrinem, da ne budem sjetan.

Six, five, two, eight, two, one — ring through,so they're provided for, and I'm not blue.

Valpovo, 13. 11. 2000.

Valpovo, November 13, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza.
