Domoljubne i zavičajne

Bizovačke toplice

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

The Bizovac Spa

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Na istoku ravne Slavonije,Bizovac mi selo najmilije.Od Bizovca vode staze pravo,za Našice, Osijek i Valpovo.U Bizovcu sviću zlatne zorei izvire bizovačko more.Tu se čuje pjesma cura, snašai vesela svirka tamburaša.Za liječenje i zdravu masažu,Bizovačke toplice pomažu.Ta je voda puna morske soli,bolesniku smanji teške boli.Voda liječi sve teške traume,išijase i razne reume.Terapije i snažne masaže,slabo tijelo ponovno osnaže.Nerotkinje koje lijeka traže,neka dođu, tu voda pomaže.Da im budu lijepi, vedri dani,pomoći će mladi Bizovčani.Muškarci će naći lijeka jadu,kad poljube Bizovčanku mladu.U "TERMIJI" tko jednom prenoći,taj će opet u toplice doći.Da se divi slavonskoj ljepotii uživa u kupki i vodi.Tko poželi radost i veselje,u "TERMIJI" ostvarit će želje.

In level Slavonia's eastern partlies Bizovac, the village of my heart.From Bizovac the roads run straight, you know,to Našice, to Osijek, Valpovo.In Bizovac the golden dawns appear,and Bizovac's own sea springs up right here.Here you can hear the girls' and young wives' song,the merry tambura players play along.For treatment and a healthy massage, too,the Bizovac spa will help to see you through.The water's full of sea salt, and it willrelieve the heavy pains of those who're ill.The water heals all injuries severe,sciatica and rheumatism here.The therapies, the massages so strong,put strength back in weak bodies before long.And barren women, seeking for a cure,should come: the water helps them here, for sure.So that their days be lovely, bright and clear,the young men of Bizovac will help them here.Men, too, will find a cure for every acheonce a young Bizovac girl's kiss they take.Who spends a single night at Termia therewill come back to the spa again, I swear,to marvel at Slavonia's beauty trueand to enjoy the bath and water too.Whoever wishes joy and merrimentat Termia will find his heart's content.

Valpovo, 25.12.1992.

Valpovo, December 25, 1992

Translator's note. Bizovac, near Osijek, has hot springs of salty thermal water, "the Bizovac sea"; Termia is the spa hotel.
