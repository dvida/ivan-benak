Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Gospođi Ani Gillinger iz Ivankova

Occasional verse and dedications

To Mrs Ana Gillinger of Ivankovo

Gospa Ana, puno hvala,za poklone što ste dalii ja Vama od sveg srca,šaljem ovaj poklon mali.

Mrs Ana, thank you sofor the presents you have sent;now to you, with all my heart,this small gift of mine is meant.

Kad budete čaja pilišalica nek' kaže ova,da je Vama darovana,od pjesnika iz Valpova.

When you drink your tea, may thismug here tell you, so you know,that it was a gift to youfrom the poet of Valpovo.

Što će Vama dokazatina šalici moja slika,da Vam pružim kap veselja,posebna je meni dika.

What my picture on the mugwill be proving, as you see:giving you a drop of joyis a special pride to me.

Nek' Vam bude SRETAN BOŽIĆI GODINA ova NOVA,a najviše dobrog zdravlja:želi Ivo iz Valpova!

Have a very Merry Christmasand a happy New Year so,but above all, good health too:wishes Ivo of Valpovo!

Valpovo, 27. 11. 2013.

Valpovo, November 27, 2013

Translator's note. A thank-you and Christmas greeting sent with a mug bearing the poet's portrait.
