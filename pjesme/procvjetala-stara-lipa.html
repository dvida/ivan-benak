Poems for children

The Old Linden Is in Bloom

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Procvjetala stara lipau dvorištu naše kuće,braco, seka, baka, deka,brali su je, eto, jučer.

The old linden tree's in bloomin the yard beside our house;brother, sister, Gran and Grandpapicked its flowers just yesterday.

Godinama još će cvasti,mirisni nam dati čaj.Kad snijeg vije,čaj se pije,svima nam je tada raj.

Many years yet it will flower,give us fragrant tea to share.When snow's blowing,tea is flowing —heaven for us all, right there.

Životinjsko tu je carstvo,svatko u svom logu spava:zeko, pužić i stonoga,jedino što nema lava.

Here's an animal kingdom, too,each asleep in its own lair:bunny, snail and centipede —just the lion isn't there.
