Sveta tri kralja

Spiritual poems

The Three Kings

BALTAZAR

Baltazar

Baltazar se po imenu zovem,poštovan sam u narodu svojem.Čuo jesam da se kralj rodio.Iz daljine ja sam dohodio.Došao sam da mu se poklonimi bogate darove donosim.

My name is Baltazar, that's what I'm called;my people hold me honoured, one and all.I heard it told a newborn king had come;from far away I've journeyed, far from home.I've come to pay him homage and to bow,and bring rich gifts, which I shall give him now.

MELKIOR

Melkior

Melkior sam iz zemlje daleke,tamo imam zasluge velike.U mom carstvu mene sve veseli,imam svega što mi srce želi.Čuo jesam da se kralj rodio,zato sam ga sada posjetio.Da se kralju nebeskom poklonimi mnoge mu darove donosim.Ja mu nosim: tamjan, smirnu, zlato,neka raste i kraljuje slavno.

I'm Melkior, and I come from far away;great are my merits there, as people say.In my own kingdom all things make me glad;all that my heart desires, I've always had.I heard it told a king had come to earth,and so I've come to visit at his birth,that I may bow before the King of heavenand bring him many gifts, which shall be given.I bring him frankincense, and myrrh, and gold:may he grow up and reign, in glory untold.

GAŠPAR

Gašpar

Crn sam Gašpar, crna slova pišem.Iz Babilona u Betlehem stižem,da se kralju nebeskom poklonimi darove ja mu lijepe nosim.Sretan život ja mu želim javno,u svom carstvu da kraljuje slavno.

I'm Gašpar, black, and black the script I pen;from Babylon I come to Bethlehem,that I may bow before the King of heavenand bring him lovely gifts, which shall be given.Before you all I wish him happy days,and in his realm, a glorious reign always.

Valpovo, 12. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 12, 2012

Translator's note. Speaking parts for the three kings in the Epiphany custom of the star-singers, children dressed as the Magi who go from house to house. Baltazar, Melkior and Gašpar are the Croatian forms of Balthasar, Melchior and Caspar.
