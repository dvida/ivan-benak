Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani gospodine Franjo

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Mr Franjo

Na slici Vašoj puno hvala!Što mi Vaša ličnost dala!Ja ću sliku uramitii moj stančić ukrasiti.

For your painting, thanks from me,given by you personally!I will frame the picture, too,deck my little flat anew.

Zato moja knjiga mala,koju mi je mašta dala,što nas sudba upoznala,ona će Vam reći hvala!

So this little book of mine,fancy's gift to me, I say —since our fate has let us meet —it will thank you in its way!

Mladen Franjo Nikšići pjesnik su k'o dva druga.Kroz poznanstvo ovo naše,pjesnik knjigom vraća duga!

Mladen Franjo Nikšić andthe poet — like two friends, it's true;through this acquaintance that we share,with a book the poet pays his due!

Valpovo, 20. 04. 2014.

Valpovo, April 20, 2014

Translator's note. The painter Franjo Nikšić gave him a painting at the "Preprekovo proljeće" gathering in Novi Sad in April 2014, where he read four of his poems.
