Poems for children

I Love Nada

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Vjerujete mi, dečki,nisam više dijete.Ja volim Naduiz klupe pete!

Believe me, lads,I'm no kid now, I swear.I love Nada,fifth desk, over there!

Spojena srcaja zbog nje rišem,ljubavne pjesmeu teku pišem.

I draw for hertwo hearts joined tight,and in my notebooklove poems I write.

U oblake plaveželje mi lete,a srce drhtii pada u pete.

My wishes fly upto the clouds so blue;my heart is tremblingand sinks into my shoe.

A kada je nema,srce me boli.Često se pitam,je li me voli?

And when she's not there,my heart aches, it's true.I often wonder:does she love me too?

I zato mi vjerujte,nisam više dijete.Ja volim Naduiz klupe pete!

So believe me, then,I'm no kid now, I swear.I love Nada,fifth desk, over there!

Translator's note. Nada is a girl's name. Pete means both "fifth" (her desk) and "heels", where his heart sinks, as English hearts sink into their shoes.
