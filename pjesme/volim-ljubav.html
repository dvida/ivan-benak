Humorous poems

I Love Love

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010) · he titled the revised version “In Confidence”

Sa izvora slatkog,često med sam pio,sa usana ženskihi sretan sam bio.

Often, from a sweet spring,honey I would sip,and I was happy, drinkingfrom a woman's lip.

Volio sam ljubav,što bih to krio?Najljepše sam ženeiskreno ljubio.

I was fond of love:why should I conceal?The loveliest of womenI kissed with honest zeal.

Na trenutak samo,da vam kažem nešto:ženice sam bajneja ljubio vješto.

Just for a moment,let me tell you this:charming little wivesI knew just how to kiss.

Za života svogaljubio sam žene.A one su čestozavodile mene.

All my life I've beena kisser of women, see;and often they have beenthe ones seducing me.

Za sve grijehe mojene krivite mene.Ja griješio ne bih,al su htjele žene.

Put no blame on mefor my sins, I pray;I would never sin,but women had their way.

Mladost bješe kratkana ovome svijetu.Zato ljubljah ženeu najljepšem cvijetu.

Youth was all too briefin this world of ours;so I kissed the womenin their fairest flower.

Pa i kada umremneka svi znaju.I onda ću ljubitidjevice u raju.

And when I've passed away,let all know it's true:I'll be kissing maidensup in heaven too.

Valpovo, 21. 03. 2008.

Valpovo, March 21, 2008

Druga inačica: „Volim ljubav“ Another version: “I Love Love”

Ranija, kraća inačica.

An earlier, shorter version.

Volio sam ljubav,što bih to krio?Najljepše sam ženeu životu htio.

I have loved love,why hide it away?The loveliest womenI've wanted all my days.

Za života svogaljubio sam žene.A one su čestozavodile mene.

Throughout my lifeI kissed women, too,and quite often theyseduced me, it's true.

Mladost bješe kratkana ovome svijetu.Zato ljubljah ženeu najljepšem cvijetu.

Youth was all too shortin this world of ours;so I kissed the womenin their fullest flower.

Pa i kada umremneka svi znaju.I onda ću ljubitidjevice u raju.

And even when I'm dead,let everyone see:the maidens in heavenwill be kissed by me!
