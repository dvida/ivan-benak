Valpovačka Satnica

Occasional verse and dedications

Valpovačka Satnica

Na njedrima Panonske nizine,od davnina Satnica postoji,na domaku rijeke Karašice,mnoga ljeta sa ponosom broji.

Upon the bosom of the Pannonian Plain,has Satnica stood since ancient times, right here,close by the river Karašica's flow,and proudly counts its summers, year on year.

Agrarom se ovdje ljudi bave,veseli se narod toga kraja,sve praznike u svom selu slave,uživaju zemaljskoga raja.

Here people make their living from the land,the folk around these parts are glad and bright;they celebrate each feast day in their village,in earthly paradise they take delight.

Sve što treba u Satnici ima.Raznom robom trgovina puna.Kružni tok je za potrebe svimai birtija gdje se troši kuna.

In Satnica there's everything you need;the shop is full of goods of every kind;a roundabout that serves us all, indeed,and there's a pub where kunas go, you'll find.

Crkva sveta, gdje se mise služe,ponekada i svatove pravetu je škola, gdje se djeca druže,često puta i krštenje slave.

There is the holy church, where Mass is said,where sometimes, too, a wedding is prepared;and here's the school, where children meet as friends,and often, too, a christening feast is shared.

I još jedno časno društvo ima,dični ljudi od bivšega rada,to je društvo potrebno mnogima,da se druže u starosti sada.

And one more honoured club the village has:proud people who have worked for many a year;it is a club that many people need,to gather in their old age, now, right here.

U druženju matica je prvata udruga Satnici je dikanabavljaju zimnicu i drva,to je društvo umirovljenika.

In fellowship this club is first of all,the pride of Satnica, its glory too;they lay in winter stores and firewood:this is the pensioners' club, through and through.

Već je prošlo nekoliko ljeta,ti heroji minuloga radada ponovno izaberu Vladui skupštinu imaju baš sada.

A few more summers now have passed away,and these, the heroes of the work now past,are holding their assembly here todayto choose their "Government" again at last.

Valpovo, 27. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 27, 2013

Translator's note. Written for the annual assembly of the pensioners' association in the village of Valpovačka Satnica, on the Karašica river near Valpovo. The kuna was the Croatian currency until 2023. The "Government" is his joke for the association's board.
