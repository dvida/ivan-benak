Valpovačke poete

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Occasional verse and dedications

Valpovo's Poets

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Poštovani čitatelji,prigoda je evo sadada za ovu obljetnicukažem rezultate rada.

Honoured readers, here and nowthe occasion has come duefor this anniversary:I'll report our work to you.

U ovome divnom časuja vam dajem osvrt mali,kako bi se s „Poetama“u svom radu upoznali.

On this wonderful occasionI will give a short review,so you'll come to know "Poeta"and the work its members do.

Tisuću i devetstotedevedeset drugog ljeta,osmog dana listopadaosnovana je „Poeta“.

In the year of nineteen hundredninety-two, as you may see,on the eighth day of October,our "Poeta" came to be.

Ni novaca, a ni stana,tada nismo mi imali.Zato smo se iz potrebesa HKUD-om povjenčali.

Neither money nor a placeof our own did we possess;so, from need, we wed the HKUD,tied the knot, and said our "yes".

No, da krenem od početka:po osnutku iste zimena daskama pozornicezačulo se naše ime.

But I'll start from the beginning:that same winter, still brand-new,on the boards upon the stageour name rang out, clear and true.

U tih petnaest divnih ljetauspjehe smo mi nizalii na mnogo pozornicasvoje pjesme smo čitali.

Through those fifteen lovely summerswe strung successes, row on row,and upon so many stagesread our poems, high and low.

Ne samo na pozornici,već u eter mi smo slali,u knjigama, revijama,naše pjesme su čitali.

Not upon the stage alone,but on air we sent them too;and in books and magazinespeople read our poems through.

Devedeset trećeg ljeta,da bi za nas pisci znali,Šokačko smo sijelo tadasa HKUD-om osnovali.

In the year of ninety-three,so that writers, too, might knowof us, we with the HKUDfounded Šokačko sijelo.

„Poete“ su sa HKUD-om,dvadesetak sijela dali,da bi pisci SlavonijeValpovštinu upoznali.

"Poeta", with the HKUD,held some twenty sijela, soall the writers of Slavoniawould Valpovština come to know.

Ukratko ću samo reći:da bi bolje za nas znali,dvjesto deset po Hrvatskojnastupa smo mi imali.

I'll just say it briefly, then:so they'd know us better still,all across Croatia wegave our shows: two hundred ten.

Kad uzmemo u globalu,to je sve ovako bilo:Tisuću i sedamdesetpjesnika je nastupilo.

If we take it all in all,this is how it all turned out:one thousand and seventypoets have performed, no doubt.

Krenuo bih od Slatinei Miholjca do Baranje,Feričanci i Našice,od Osijeka do Županje.

I would start out from Slatina,Miholjac, to Baranja,Feričanci and Našice,from Osijek to Županja.

U Zagrebu na Jaranusvoje pjesme smo čitali,i Drenovce na istokus radošću smo upoznali.

In Zagreb, at the Jaran,we have read our poems through,and we gladly came to knowDrenovci, in the east, too.

Pa Vrbanju, Rešetare,Đeletovce pohađali,Brod, Orašje do Tolisekulturu smo promicali.

Then Vrbanja, Rešetari,and Đeletovci we saw;Brod, Orašje, to Tolisa,we spread culture more and more.

Obišli smo Slavonijui Vezove Đakovačke,Ivankovo i Semeljcei Jeseni Vinkovačke.

All Slavonia we have toured,Đakovo's Embroideries fair,Ivankovo and Semeljci,Vinkovci's Autumns: we were there.

Za druženje ovo divnoMuza nas je povezala,i HKUD-u iz Valpova,na pomoći puno hvala.

For this lovely fellowshipit's the Muse who bound us; so,to the HKUD from Valpovomany thanks for help we owe.

Dvadesetak divnih knjigaod „Poeta“ izatkano,i po cijelom ovom svijetusa ponosom razaslano.

Some twenty lovely books were spunby "Poeta", thread by thread,and throughout this whole wide worldsent with pride, and widely spread.

I kroz ovih petnaest ljetatri su zbirke iznjedrili,da bi ovo čovječanstvosa ljepotom podarili.

In these fifteen summers, too,three collections came to birth,so that they might give mankindgifts of beauty here on earth.

Dok „Poetska svitanja“ sudala radost i veselje,„10 godina Poeta“,ostvarenje davne želje.

While "Poetic Dawns" has givenjoy to us and glad delight,and "10 Years of Poeta"brought a long-held wish to light.

„Snovi stihom izvezeni“to je treća naša knjiga,od „Poeta“ iznjedrena,naš je ponos, naša dika.

"Dreams Embroidered Into Verse"is the third book we have penned,brought to life by our "Poeta":it's our pride and glory, friend.

Kad napiše svoju knjigu,pjesniku je svakom dikapa i onda, kad ga nema,ostala je časna slika.

When he's written his own book,every poet's proud, each one;even later, when he's gone,an honoured image lingers on.

I HKUD-u nek' je zato,ponos što smo im sekcija.I Valpovu našem gradu,jer nas znade sva nacija.

And so let the HKUD beproud to have us as its section,and our town of Valpovo too,since we're known throughout the nation.

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2007.

Valpovo, March 28, 2007

Translator's note. A verse history of the poets' society Poeta, founded in 1992 and a section of the Valpovo cultural society HKUD Valpovo 1905, written for its 15th anniversary. Đakovački vezovi (Đakovo Embroideries) and Vinkovačke jeseni (Vinkovci Autumns) are Slavonia's big folklore festivals; Šokačko sijelo is the festival the society co-founded.
