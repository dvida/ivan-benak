Domoljubne i zavičajne

Rođena gruda

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Native Soil

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ako ti se prohtje, u bijeli svijet pođi.Život bolne drame priređuje često.A kada se vratiš, na grudi mi dođi.Tu ćeš naći uvijek svoje toplo gnijezdo.

If you should wish it, go into the wide world — go!For life will often stage its dramas full of pain.But when you come back home, come to my breast; you knowthat here you'll always find your own warm nest again.

I ljubav i sreću svoga zavičaja,i rođenu majku što te silno voli.ništa nema ljepše od svojega krajajer tuđina često znade da zaboli.

And love and joy you'll find in your own native land,and your own mother, too, who loves you, deep and strong.There's nothing lovelier — you'll surely understand —than home, for foreign lands can often do you wrong.

Najljepše je uvijek na rođenoj grudii zemljica ova što te kruhom hrani.Tu si se rodio, tu živi i budi.Ne daj domovinu, s ljubavlju je brani.

The loveliest place is always one's own native ground,and this dear little earth that feeds you with its bread.Here you were born; here live, and here be found.Don't give your homeland up; with love, defend her well.

Valpovo, 15.01.1976.

Valpovo, January 15, 1976

Translator's note. The homeland herself speaks, to one of her children who goes abroad.
