A Lifetime of Pain

Trpio sam mnogo bola,na svom putu punom jada,Bog mi bio u pomoći,od djetinjstva sve do sada.

I have suffered so much painon my road so full of woe;God has always been my helper,from a child till now, I know.

Nekad tuga dušu mori,sudba nam za grijehe sudi,za pravdu se čovjek bori,od pomoći, Gospo, budi!

Sometimes sorrow wears the soul,fate will judge the wrongs we've done,man must struggle for what's just —Lady, help us, every one!

Ponekad se teško hodi,neravni su često puti,kroz život me sudba vodi.Ti uz mene, Gospo, budi!

Sometimes it is hard to walk,often rugged is the way;fate is leading me through life.Lady, stay by me, I pray!

Sa iskrenom željom, Gospo,došao sam sada k tebida sa srca teret bacimšto ga nosim ja u sebi.

With sincere desire, Our Lady,I have come to you today,that the weight I bear withinfrom my heart I'd cast away.

Ljudima si puno puta,u pomoći, Gospo, bila,iako si i ti gorke,na svom putu suze lila!

Many times you've been, Our Lady,help to people in their need,though you too along your journeyshed your bitter tears indeed!

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2010.

Valpovo, March 28, 2010

Translator's note. A prayer to Our Lady (Gospa), the Virgin Mary.
