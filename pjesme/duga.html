The Rainbow

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Jeste li vidjelina nebu dugu,onu divnušarenu prugu?

Have you ever seenthe rainbow in the sky,that lovely stripeof colours, way up high?

Kad kiša padai sunce sije,to oblak voduiz rijeke pije.

When rain is fallingand the sun's aglow,the cloud drinks waterfrom the river below.

A kada s kišomvjetar vije,tad´ nebo suzekroz oblak lije.

And when the windcomes blowing with the rain,then through the cloudthe sky's tears pour again.
