Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Učenicima Osnovne škole August Cesarac Ivankovo

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Pupils of August Cesarac Primary School, Ivankovo

Ovu knjigu pročitalii petice naslagali,da nikome krivo nije,po predmetu može dvije.

Read this book right through, and thenpile up top marks, row on row;so that no one feels left out,two per subject's fine, you know.

Kad iz škole vi dođetevi do bare prošetajte,sve do one barske vode,da vidite gdje su rode,da li žabe kolo vode?Ili bježe ispred rode.

When from school you come away,take a walk down to the pond,to the marshy water there,and see where the storks all stay:are the frogs all dancing kolo,or from storks they run away?

Poštovani dragi đaci,vaše škole svi prvaci.Bili sretni i veseli,pjesnik Ivo to vam želi!

Dear respected pupils, you,first-graders, and champions too:happy, cheerful, may you stay:that's poet Ivo's wish today!

Valpovo, 12. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 12, 2013

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting), hence the frogs and storks. A "petica", a five, is the top mark in Croatian schools. "Prvaci" means both first-graders and champions.
