Obiteljski kutak

Sretan ti rođendan, sine!

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Family corner

Happy Birthday, Son!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ostalo je iza tvojih leđa,četrdeset četiri proljeća.Nek' sve patnje ostanu za tobom,cvjetala ti grana puna cvijeća.

Behind your back, now left for good, there liefull forty-four springtimes, every one;may all your sufferings stay behind you there,and may your branch bloom full of flowers, son.

Od rođenja, sve do ovih dana,pratila te u životu sreća.Sve što želiš nek' ti se ostvari,želja mi je od svega najveća.

From birth right up until these present days,good fortune through your life has walked with you.May everything you wish for come to pass:that is my greatest wish of all, it's true.

Ne saznao nikada za tugu.Sve u sreći i bez bolnih rana,s najdražima svoje obitelji,proslavio mnogo rođendana.

May you not ever know what sorrow is;all happy, free of wounds and free of pain,among the dearest of your family,may you see many birthdays come again.

Radost svome pokoljenju dao,raj zemaljski podijelio s nama,sa njihove sreće, sreću brao,to ti želi: Tvoj tata i mama!

May you bring gladness to your children's line,and share with us an earthly heaven, son;from their good fortune may you gather yours —this is the wish of your own Dad and Mum!

Valpovo, 30. 04. 2000.

Valpovo, April 30, 2000

Translator's note. For his son's 44th birthday. The son had started at the Valpovo health centre as an ambulance driver at 25 and still worked in the Osijek-Baranja County emergency service.
