Prijatelju Jozi Šantaku

Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend Jozo Šantak

U ravnini Podravinerodila me moja mati,mnogo znanja u djetinjstvu,nije mogla meni dati.

In the Podravina lowlandit was there my mother bore me;much of learning, in my childhood,she could never lay before me.

Prohujale godinice,drugima sam mladost dao,upoznao mnogo svijeta,o Sopju sam malo znao.

The dear years went rushing by,to others I gave my youth;much of the world I came to know,little of Sopje, in truth.

Dogodi se neko čudo,slučaj me u Sopje vrati.Da bih Sopje opisao,nisam mog'o ništa znati.

Then some miracle occurred:chance brought me to Sopje again.But to write Sopje's description,I knew nothing, it was plain.

Kad upoznah Šantak Jozu,Podravinu on je znao.Sopjanskoga Doajena,što mi mnogo znanja dao.

When I met with Jozo Šantak,Podravina he knew well;Sopje's Doyen, who then gave meknowledge more than I could tell.

I tako je sve počelo,stih po stih sam pisat stao,da napišem ovu knjigu,Šantak mi je pomoć dao.

And that's how it all began:verse by verse I took to pen;so that I could write this book,Šantak helped me there and then.

Al u knjizi pokraj mogai njegovo ime stoji.Stvarajući ovu knjigu,pjesma se za pjesmom roji.

But beside my name, his ownin the book is standing too;as this book was being made,poem after poem came swarming through.

I tako se eto rodi,djelo koje Sopju znači.Ja ću opet sa Šantakom,vremena za drugu naći.

And so, there, a work was bornthat means much to Sopje's folk;I'll, with Šantak, once againfind the time for one more book.

Valpovo, 23. 01. 2013.

Valpovo, January 23, 2013

Translator's note. Jozo Šantak, the "doyen" of Sopje, helped Benak write the book Destinacije Općine Sopje; Sopje in the Podravina lowland was Benak's birthplace.
