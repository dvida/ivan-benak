Poštovana Anice!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Anica!

Ova je divna knjiga pisana tebi,bez Tvoje pomoći štampali je ne bi.Draga Anice, od srca Ti hvala,svoju si dobrotu za tu knjigu dala.STIHOVI KROZ ŽIVOT, divna knjiga nova,nek' Ti bude noću uspavanka snova.Da bi svake noći sretne snove snila,ova divna knjiga, uzorak Ti bila.Zato ovu knjigu čitaj prije snova,svježa, vedra ujutro bit ćeš kao nova.U životu, Anice, pratila Te sreća,životna Ti staza bila puna cvijeća.Prijateljstvo tvoje koje si mi dala,od sveg srca, Anice, na njemu Ti hvala!

This lovely book was written just for you;without your help, they'd never have seen it through.Dear Anica, I thank you from my heart:you gave this book your kindness from the start."Verses Through Life", a lovely book, and new:at night, let it be a lullaby of dreams for you.So you might dream sweet dreams each night, and solet this fine book the pattern for them show.So read this book before you dream, and thencome morning you'll be fresh and bright, like new again.and may good fortune, Anica, attend your ways,your path of life be full of flowers all your days.For all the friendship you have given, too,with all my heart, Anica, I say thanks to you!

Translator's note. A thank-you to Anica, who helped him print his book Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse), which he calls here Stihovi kroz život (Verses Through Life).
