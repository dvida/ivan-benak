Occasional verse and dedications

When Boredom Poisons Your Soul

Kada Ti dosada dušu truje,kada te mora iz snova budi,kada Te očaj u životu snađe,kada već misliš da tonu lađe,pročitaj pjesme iz knjige ove,pa će Ti radost ozariti snove.

When boredom is poisoning your soul,when nightmares wake you out of your sleep,when in your life despair has taken hold,when you think your ships are sinking deep,then read these poems from this book of mine,and joy will make your dreams begin to shine.

Translator's note. One of several short verses he wrote for inscribing gift copies of his books.
