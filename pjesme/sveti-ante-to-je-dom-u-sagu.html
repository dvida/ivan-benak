Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Sveti Ante to je dom u Šagu

Occasional verse and dedications

St Anthony's, the Home in Šag

I Šag ima dom za stare ljude,tu mnogima utočište nude.Ovaj dom je od gospođe Maje,svu ugodnost štićeniku daje.

Šag, too, has now a home where old folk stay,and many find a refuge here today.This home belongs to Mrs Maja, whereeach resident finds every comfort there.

SVETI ANTUN časno ime nosi,Jeger Maja njime se ponosi.Ovaj dom je Janji draga slika,mještanima i ponos i dika.

It bears the honoured name St Anthony,and Maja Jeger's proud of it, you see.To Janja this home is a picture dear,the pride and glory of the locals here.

Svima vama što ste u tom domu,usamljeni u životu svomu.Svu ljepotu ovaj dom vam daje,SVETI ANTE od gospođe Maje.

To all of you who live within this home,who in your lives were lonely and alone:this home gives you all loveliness, all cheer:St Anthony's, that Mrs Maja runs here.

Tuga vas je morila u tami,noć je tiha, vi kod kuće sami.Ali ovdje blažene su noći:SVETI ANTE vama će pomoći.

Sorrow would wear you down when dark had come,the night was still, and you alone at home.But here the nights are blessed through and through:St Anthony will be a help to you.

Sjetite se kad ste mladi bilii ljubavne snove dok ste snili.I starost je ljepša kad se druži,to druženje ANTE će da pruži.

Remember, now, when you were young, and whenyou used to dream your dreams of love back then.Old age is lovelier, too, with friends around,and at St Anthony's such friends are found.

I starački dovukli se jadi,kao nekad niste više mladi.Vi potrebe imate po noći,osoblje će ANTUNA pomoći.

The aches of age have crept up on you too;you're not as young as once you were, it's true.And should you have some needs during the night,St Anthony's staff will help you set things right.

Nakon snova kad otvoriš oči,sve što trebaš tu će ti pomoći.Dobar ručak i društvo te čeka,prije snova posteljica meka.

When after dreams you open up your eyes,here help with all you need is standing by.A good lunch and your friends are waiting here,before your dreams, a soft bed, too, is near.

Pa zar može nešto bolje biti,u noćima divne snove sniti.Vaša mladost u snove se vrati,to vam može SVETI ANTE dati!

Can anything be better, I ask you,than dreaming lovely dreams the whole night through?In dreams your youth comes back to you once more:and that is what St Anthony has in store!

Valpovo, 01. 09. 2012.

Valpovo, September 1, 2012

Translator's note. A care home for the elderly in Šag, named Sveti Antun (St Anthony; Ante is the familiar form) and run by Maja Jeger. The poem lets the home's name and the saint himself stand for each other.
