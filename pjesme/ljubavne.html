Pjesme

Ljubavne pjesme

„O tebi se moje / misli sada roje…“ Ljubavne pjesme pratile su ga od mladosti; iz njih su nastale i sve tri pjesme izvedene na festivalima.

Poems

Love poems

“My thoughts are all of you, / they swarm the whole night through…” Love poems were with him from his youth; all three of the songs performed at festivals grew out of them.
