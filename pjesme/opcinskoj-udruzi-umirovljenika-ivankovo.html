Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Općinskoj udruzi umirovljenika Ivankovo

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Municipal Pensioners' Association of Ivankovo

POŠTOVANA GENERACIJO

HONOURED GENERATION

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

Warm greetings to you all I wish to send,and I rejoice in the assembly you attend.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što Željko vaš predsjednik kaže,ja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear of all the splendid results you've won,success, and guidelines for the work you do;and for what Željko, your president, has said,I now congratulate each one of you.

Sve čestitke vašem predsjednikui ostalim ovdje prisutnima,plodonosni rad skupštini vašoj,ja iskreno želim sada svima.

Congratulations to your presidentand all the others who are present here;I wish your meeting fruitful work, and sendthis wish to all of you, and it's sincere.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the deeds that you have done, and allyour labour in the year that we've been through,I wish you more success, and may it fallto you in the new year that's coming, too.

A skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ona sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And may the assembly at this hoursupport the present leadership, I say,for, full of élan as it is, it willlead the society on its future way.

Da bi zdravo tijelo održali,redovito terme pohađali.Na godinu više se družili,s udrugama sportski takmičili.

So that you keep your bodies fit and hale,go to the thermal baths and never fail;next year, may you get together more,compete with other clubs in sport, what's more.

Podružnice što se s vama druže,divna sela oko vas što kruže:Retkovci, Vođinci i Prkovci,pa i društvo Stari Mikanovci.

The branches that keep company with you,the lovely villages around you, too:Retkovci, Vođinci and Prkovci,and Stari Mikanovci's society.

I pjevačko društvo vaše slavno,taj predivni zbor "PERUNIKA",sa uspjehom zajedno sa vama,Ivankovu posebna su dika.

And then your singing society of fame,that lovely choir, "PERUNIKA" by name,successful, side by side with all of you,are Ivankovo's special pride, it's true.

Svi u zdravlju i veselju bili.Uživajte veselo bez rada.Mnogo ljeta penziju primali,ne tražite svetog Petra vrata.Ostvarenje vaših divnih snova,želi: Pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova!

May all of you in health and gladness stay;enjoy yourselves, now work is put away.Draw pensions many years, and may you wait:don't go out looking for Saint Peter's gate!That all your lovely dreams come true, just so,wishes the poet Ivo from Valpovo!

Valpovo, 24. 11. 2011.

Valpovo, November 24, 2011

Translator's note. Ivankovo is a village near Vinkovci; Retkovci, Vođinci, Prkovci and Stari Mikanovci are the villages around it. Perunika ("The Iris") is the village choir.
