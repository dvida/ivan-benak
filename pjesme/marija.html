Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Marija!

Occasional verse and dedications

Marija!

Nek' Ti ova knjiga moja,pruži radost i veseljei dosadu da Ti skrati,to su, Maro, moje želje.

May this book of mine, I pray,bring you joy and happy cheer,and cut short your boredom, too:these are my wishes, Mara dear.

U postelji toploj, mekoj,čitaj štiva knjige ove,da Ti zora vedra svanei snivat ćeš divne snove.

In a bed that's warm and soft,read this book of mine right through,so the dawn will rise up bright,and sweet dreams will come to you.

Kad iznjedriš svoju knjigu,koja će Ti radost dati,Ti se sjeti prijatelja,svojom knjigom dug mu vrati.

When you bring your own book out,one that joy will bring to you,spare a thought then for your friend:with your book, pay back what's due.

Neka i on vedar bude,čitajući Tvoje štivo,iskreno Ti ovo želi:od sveg srca Benak Ivo!

May he, too, be of good cheer,reading all your writing through;so sincerely wishes you,with all his heart, Benak Ivo!

Tridesetog listopadaposvetu u knjigu stavljam,dv'je tisuće i trinaeste,Mariji se s knjigom javljam.

On the thirtieth of OctoberI inscribe this dedication,in the year two thousand thirteen,and with this book greet Marija.

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his book Stihom kroz život for Marija (Mara) Kenjerić, a poet from Čađavica.
