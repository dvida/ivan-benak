Radio burza

Orasi su za praznik u modi

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Walnuts Are in Fashion for the Holidays

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ide Božić i Nova godina,doček gosti prava je milina.

Christmas is coming, and the New Year's night;to welcome guests then is a pure delight.

O prazniku peku se kolači,za tu svrhu treba orah naći.

For holidays the cakes are baked with care,and walnuts must be found to put in there.

Orasima tortu filovanu,čokoladom slatkom premazanu.

A torte that's filled with walnuts through and through,and spread with sweetest chocolate on top, too.

Pa medine šape svima znanei oblatne fino rolovane.

And bear's-paw biscuits, known to all by name,and finely rolled-up wafers, just the same.

A gužvaru k'o noga debelu,orasima dobro napunjenu.

A gužvara as thick as a leg, you see,with walnuts filled as full as it can be.

Ništa bolje od kolača nema,nego kad se orasima sprema.

There is no better cake that one can bakethan one with walnuts — that's the cake to make!

Mnoge žene, dabome, to znaju,kakvu vrijednost orasi imaju.

And many women know it, sure enough:how much the walnut's worth — it's precious stuff.

Zato treba orah da se nađe,jer je dobar za svake kolače.

And so the walnut's something you must find:it's good for cakes of every sort and kind.

Kažem glasno, neka žene znaju,ja oraha imam za prodaju.

I say out loud, so all the wives know well:I've walnuts, and I've got them here to sell.

Jeftinije o prazniku važi,pet eura za kilu se traži.

For holidays, a cheaper price will stand:five euros for a kilo's my demand.

Brže žene, nemojte sustati,petsto kila brzo će nestati.

So hurry, ladies, don't fall back — be fast!Five hundred kilos isn't going to last.

Kolačića za blagdan nek' ima,da ugodno bude nama svima.

Let little cakes abound for the feast, and somay all of us enjoy it, high and low.

Bez ljuske su fino očišćeni,na telefon javite se meni.

They're finely cleaned, and every one shell-free;just use the telephone, get through to me.

6 5 2 osamsto dvadeset jedan,da orahe prodam ovaj tjedan.

6 5 2 eight hundred twenty-one,to sell the nuts before this week is done.

Valpovo, 11. 12. 2000.

Valpovo, December 11, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza. Bear's paws (medine šape) are walnut biscuits baked in paw-shaped moulds; gužvara is a rolled, crumpled pastry, here with walnut filling.
