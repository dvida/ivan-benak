Prijateljsko druženje

Occasional verse and dedications

A Friendly Get-Together

Dobro došli prijatelji Komla!Svima nama posebno je drago,naše društvo ovaj susret želi.Neka bude svakog ljeta tako.

Welcome, our friends from Komlo, one and all!We're all especially glad to have you here;our fellowship has longed to meet you so.May it be so with every passing year.

Do sada smo skupa drugovali,puno puta vaši gosti bili.Mi smo Komlo rado upoznalii Mađarsku često posjetili.

Till now good company we've always been,and many times as guests we've stayed with you;and Komlo, gladly, we have known and seen,and paid Hungary many visits too.

Članica ste slavne Europešto je ponos i posebna dika.Kad Hrvatska uđe u Europui nama će biti ista slika.

You're members now of famous Europe,a thing of pride and special glory too;and when Croatia comes to join Europe,the same will hold for us as holds for you.

I to će nas još više zbližiti,biti ćemo kao porodica.Slobodna će biti naša šetnja,neće biti na međi granica.

And that will bring us closer even more;we'll be as close as family and kin;and we'll be free to walk from side to side,no border at the line to fence us in.

A Forinte i Hrvatske Kune,u to vrijeme neće više biti.Da l' na radost ili našu tugu,Euro će ih obje zamijeniti.

And Forints, and Croatian Kunas too,will by that time have vanished and be gone;whether to our gladness or our sorrow,the Euro will replace them from then on.

Ali nama to sve nije važno,kao do sad skupa ćemo biti.U Europi zagrljeni snažno,mi ćemo se još više zbližiti!

But all of this is not what's on our mind;as up to now, together we will be.In Europe, in a strong embrace entwined,we'll grow still closer, both you and we!

Valpovo, 05. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 5, 2013

Translator's note. Written a few weeks before Croatia joined the European Union on July 1, 2013; Hungary had joined in 2004. Croatia later adopted the euro in 2023.
