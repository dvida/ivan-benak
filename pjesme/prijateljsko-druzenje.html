Occasional verse and dedications

A Friendly Get-Together

Dobro došli prijatelji Komla!Svima nama posebno je drago,naše društvo ovaj susret želi.Neka bude svakog ljeta tako.

Welcome, our friends from Komlo, one and all!We're all especially glad to have you here;our society was longing for this meeting.May it be so with every passing year.

Do sada smo skupa drugovali,puno puta vaši gosti bili.Mi smo Komlo rado upoznalii Mađarsku često posjetili.

Till now we've kept each other company,and many times we've been your guests, it's true;we came to know Komlo, and gladly so,and often visited Hungary too.

Članica ste slavne Europešto je ponos i posebna dika.Kad Hrvatska uđe u Europui nama će biti ista slika.

You're members of the famous Europe now,which is a pride and special glory too;and when Croatia joins Europe as well,the same will hold for us as holds for you.

I to će nas još više zbližiti,biti ćemo kao porodica.Slobodna će biti naša šetnja,neće biti na međi granica.

And that will bring us closer even more;we'll be just like a family, you'll see.We'll stroll across as freely as we please;there'll be no border on the boundary.

A Forinte i Hrvatske Kune,u to vrijeme neće više biti.Da l' na radost ili našu tugu,Euro će ih obje zamijeniti.

And Forints and Croatian Kunas, too,will, by that time, be gone and be no more.Whether to our joy or to our sorrow,the Euro will replace them both, for sure.

Ali nama to sve nije važno,kao do sad skupa ćemo biti.U Europi zagrljeni snažno,mi ćemo se još više zbližiti!

But none of this has any weight with us;as up to now, together we will be.In Europe, arm in arm, embracing close,we shall grow closer still, both you and we!

Valpovo, 05. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 5, 2013

Translator's note. Written a few weeks before Croatia joined the European Union on July 1, 2013; Hungary had joined in 2004. Croatia later adopted the euro in 2023.
