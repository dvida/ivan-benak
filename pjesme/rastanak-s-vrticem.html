Farewell to Kindergarten

Reći ću vam sadajednu malu priču:najljepše je namabilo u vrtiću.

Let me tell you alla little story, so:the loveliest place for uswas kindergarten, you know.

Svu nježnost i pažnjuovdje su nam dali,pa i prvu ljubavtu smo upoznali.

All their tenderness and carethey gave us here, it's true,and here we met our first love —yes, that happened too!

Sa vrtićem sada,mi se opraštamo,na rastanku tete,puno pozdravljamo.

We're saying our goodbyesto kindergarten here,and to our teachers, parting,send greetings, warm, sincere.

Što bi u tom času,naša srca znala?Svima u vrtićunajtoplije hvala!

What else at this momentcould our hearts know to say?To all in kindergarten,the warmest thanks today!
