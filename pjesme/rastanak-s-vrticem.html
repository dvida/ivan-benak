Poems for children

Farewell to Kindergarten

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Reći ću vam sadajednu malu priču:najljepše je namabilo u vrtiću.

Let me tell you nowa story short and sweet:our days in kindergartenwere the loveliest treat.

Svu nježnost i pažnjuovdje su nam dali,pa i prvu ljubavtu smo upoznali.

Their tenderness and carethey gave us all around,and here our very first lovewas also to be found!

Sa vrtićem sada,mi se opraštamo,na rastanku tete,puno pozdravljamo.

And now we say goodbyeto kindergarten here,and to our teachers, parting,we send greetings most sincere.

Što bi u tom času,naša srca znala?Svima u vrtićunajtoplije hvala!

What else, at this moment,could our hearts know to say?To all in kindergarten,the warmest thanks today!
