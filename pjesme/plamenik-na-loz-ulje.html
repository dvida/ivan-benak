Radio burza

Plamenik na lož ulje

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Heating-Oil Burner

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Na burzu se sada opet javljam,nisam prodo' pa oglas ponavljam.

Once more I'm on the Burza, here to say:unsold, I'm running the same ad today.

Na lož ulje grija' sam se prije,svake zime, eto sve do juče',a sada sam počeo sa plinom,prostorije grijat moje kuće.

I used to heat with heating oil before,each winter, right up until yesterday,but now I've switched, and started using gasto heat the rooms inside my house each day.

Na lož ulje osto' mi plamenikbeskorisno sad kod mene stoji,a u snazi dvadeset i osamefektivnih kilovata broji.

The oil burner is what I've got left over,it's standing useless here at home with me;it's got a power rating of twenty-eighteffective kilowatts, as you can see.

Može dalje stanove da grijeili možda sušaru montažnu.Plamenik je u odličnom stanju,on još ima energiju snažnu.

It could go on and heat apartments still,or a prefabricated drying-shed;the burner's in excellent condition, andits energy is strong still, as I said.

Tko ga kupi, kajati se neće,čuvene je Švedske marke EKO.Za sto pedeset eura bih dao,ako mi se sada javi netko.

Whoever buys it won't regret it, no —it's EKO, that famous Swedish brand, you know.For a hundred and fifty euros it is yours,if someone calls me now and says hello.

Još i pumpa što naftu pokrećei mišventil ide skupa s time,da iz tanka u plamenik teče,da ugodno bude preko zime.

And there's the pump as well that drives the oil,and then the mixing valve comes with it, see,to let it flow from tank into the burner,so winter-time is cosy as can be.

Moj telefon osto' je k'o prije;652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,želim kupcu, da se toplo grije.

My phone's the same as ever, as of old:652 – eight hundred twenty-one;I wish the buyer warmth against the cold.

Valpovo, 04. 12. 2000.

Valpovo, December 4, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza, read on air a second time because the burner had not sold.
