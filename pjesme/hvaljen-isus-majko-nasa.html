Spiritual poems

Praised Be Jesus, Our Mother

Hvaljen Isus Majko naša,što utjehu daješ svima.Dolazimo u pohode,već stotinjak ljeta ima.

Praised be Jesus, our dear Mother,you who comfort one and all.Now for nigh a hundred summerswe have come to pay our call.

Na istoku Slavonije,Prkovci su selo malo.Gospi Lurdskoj i svetištusvoj je obol selo dalo.

In the east of Slavonialies Prkovci, a village small.To Our Lady of Lourdes and her shrinethe village gave its offering.

Mnogi ljudi na svetištu,svetoj Gospi zavjet daju.Da l' zbog bola ili jada,kakav problem već imaju.

Many people at the shrinemake the holy Lady a vow,be it out of pain or sorrow,or what troubles they have now.

Tu na tome hodočašću,ispovijed su mnogi dali,primili su pričest svetu,dušu svoju okajali.

There upon the pilgrimagemany made confession then,took the Holy Communion,did their penance for their sin.

Po dolasku domu svome,danima bi raspravljali,Gospi su se povjerili,u srcu su sretni bili.

Once they'd come back home again,days on end they'd talk it through;they'd confided in Our Lady,and their hearts were happy too.

Valpovo, 10. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 10, 2010

Translator's note. "Praised be Jesus" (Hvaljen Isus) is the traditional Catholic greeting in Croatia. Written for the pilgrimage of the village of Prkovci to a shrine of Our Lady of Lourdes.
