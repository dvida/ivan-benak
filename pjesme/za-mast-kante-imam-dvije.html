Radio burza

Za mast kante imam dvije

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Two Cans for Lard

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dobio sam nešto kuna,ošla' vaga i furuna.

A few kuna came my way:scales and stove have gone away.

I još k tomu gume dvije,prodao sam malo prije.

And two tyres besides, what's more,I sold just a bit before.

Pomozi mi sveti Anteza prodaju imam kante.

Saint Anthony, lend a hand:I've cans for sale, and here I stand.

Ide zima, palo inje,u trendu je sad kolinje.

Winter's near, the hoarfrost's here,kolinje's in trend this year.

Zla sudbina gice vreba.Možda netko kante treba.

Evil fate now stalks the swine;someone may need cans of mine.

Nek me prati danas sreća,jedna manja, druga veća.

May good luck be at my side:one is small, and one is wide.

Vjerujte mi, nije šala,kantama je cijena pala.

Trust me, this is not a joke:cans have dropped in price, good folk!

Prazna kanta, nije puna,za stotinu dajem kuna.

Empty can, not full, and sofor a hundred kuna it'll go.

Da ne pričam sada dulje,još peć imam na lož ulje.

Not to keep you any more:I've an oil stove, too, in store.

Peć je dobra, kao nova,grije sobu sve do krova.

Good the stove, as good as new:heats the room, and the roof beams too!

Bila sklona mi fortuna,dajem nju za tristo kuna.

Fortune has been kind to me:three hundred kuna, it will be.

Nek' sretan mi bude dan,nazovite:652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan.

Luck be with me, day's begun!Give a ring:652 – eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 15. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 15, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. Lard from the winter pig-slaughter was stored in big tin cans; Saint Anthony is the saint Croatians pray to for help in need. The phone number was his own.
