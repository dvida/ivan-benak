Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Obitelji Klement!

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Klement Family!

Oduvijek sam ja rakijaš bio,na kirvaju rakiju sam pio.Tek ona mi od sveg srca štima,iz flašice koja gače ima.Prekrila je svoje divne grudi,slatka mašta u srcu se budi.Nek' vam bude veselo i živo,uslast pili: želi Čika Ivo!

A rakija man I've always been, it's true;at kirvaj time I drank my rakija too.And only one suits me with all my heart:from a little flask that wears her trousers smart.She's covered up her lovely bosom, too,and in the heart sweet fancies wake anew.So may your time be merry, lively, so:drink up with relish! wishes Čika Ivo!

Valpovo, 19. 10. 2014.

Valpovo, October 19, 2014

Translator's note. A label for a bottle of rakija dressed up in trousers, a present to his relatives for the kirvaj, the village saint's-day feast. Čika means uncle.
