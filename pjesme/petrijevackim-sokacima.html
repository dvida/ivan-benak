Domoljubne i zavičajne

Petrijevačkim sokacima

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Through the Lanes of Petrijevci

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Eh, da su mi konji vrancii fijaker novi,tada bi se ostvarilidavni moji snovi.

Oh, if I had raven horsesand a carriage new,then my dreams of long agowould at last come true.

Voz'o bih se kroz Petr'evcepo danu i mrakui ljubio cure, snašeu svakom sokaku.

I would drive through Petrijevciboth by day and night,kissing girls and young wives therein each lane in sight.

Krenuo bih Bregovitomsve do Veternice,gdje bih dragoj izljubio,njeno bijelo lice.

Down Bregovita I'd set offto Veternica's place,where I'd cover with my kissesmy love's fair white face.

Pa bih zatim Drenovicomu Lopovski sokak,a potom bih u Ciganski,dok ne padne mrak.

Then along Drenovicato Lopovski sokak go,and from there into Ciganskitill no daylight shows.

Prošao bih Kolođvaromčak u Novo selo,tu bi mene dočekalojanje moje bijelo.

I'd pass down Kolođvar and goright to Novo selo, whereI'd be greeted, waiting for me,by my own white lamb, right there.

Iz Mlinske bih ja polakou Bikovski sokak,došao bih svojoj dragojs večeri na konak.

From Mlinska, slow and easy,down Bikovski sokak way,I would come to my own darlingin the evening, there to stay.

Pa bih tada zapjevao,kroz naš sokak Stari,nek se znade da Ivetaide svojoj Mari.

And then I would start to singdown Stari sokak, loud and clear,so that all would know that Ivetagoes to Mara, his own dear.

I na kraju kada cijeluRepubliku prođem,kad izljubim cure, snaše,ja u Fenek dođem.

And at last, when I have drivenall the whole Republic round,when I've kissed the girls and young wives,then at Fenek I'll be found.

Valpovo, 10.07.2001.

Valpovo, July 10, 2001

Translator's note. Set to music by Goran Ivanović – Lac and Goran Đurković – Maš, arranged by Goran Rusan and sung by Boris Šili. The lanes named are the streets of Petrijevci, which locals call "the Republic"; Iveta is a pet form of his own name, Ivan.
