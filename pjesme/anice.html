Anice

Occasional verse and dedications

To Anica

Ovaj život kao rijeka tečei odnosi dio naših snova.Kap veselja od srca ti želim,tvojoj sreći i ja se veselim.

This life of ours flows onward like a riverand carries off a part of all our dreams.I wish you, from my heart, a drop of joy,and in your happiness I too rejoice.

Kao nekad nismo više mladii starački nek' nas minu jadi.Sretnih dana neka bude više,i veselja da se lakše diše.

We are not young now, as we used to be;may age's troubles pass by you and me.Let there be happy days, and many more,and joy, to breathe more lightly than before.

Nek' ti pruži sada knjiga ova,malo sreće, puno divnih snova.Svakog dana kad zora zarudi,sretnu, vedru, nek' te sunce budi.

And may this book now offer you, my friend,a little joy, and lovely dreams no end.Each day, as dawn is reddening the skies,may the sun wake you glad and bright to rise.

Nek ti bude veselo i živo,svoju knjigu daruje ti Ivo.

May all be merry for you, lively, so:his book is here, a gift to you from Ivo.

Valpovo, 05. 11. 2010.

Valpovo, November 5, 2010

Translator's note. Written for Anica Lovrenščak, a member of the Poeta society of poets in Valpovo since its founding.
