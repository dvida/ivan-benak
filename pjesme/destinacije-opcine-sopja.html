Domoljubne i zavičajne

Destinacije općine Sopja

Homeland and native region

Destinations of Sopje Municipality

Kada kreneš cestom Podravinekoja veže slavonske ravnice,prvom cestom što kraj Drave teče,od Suhopolja sve do Čađavice.

When you set out on the Podravina roadthat joins the level plains of Slavonia,the first road running by the Drava's side,from Suhopolje through to Čađavica.

Najvažnije tu je selo Sopje.Od davnina općina je bila.U ljepoti podravskoga krajadevet sela k sebi prigrlila.

The most important village here is Sopje,a municipality since days of old;amid the beauty of the Podravina landnine villages it gathers in its fold.

Na istoku, ali s lijeve stranePredrijevo Gornje tu se svilo,a na desno Šarovka je Nova,nekada je to Čomborje bilo.

To the east, but on the left-hand side,Gornje Predrijevo lies nestled in,and on the right there's Nova Šarovka,which once upon a time Čomborje had been.

Od Šarovke kad na zapad kreneš,Grabić selo, to vam nije novo.Tu je nekad i Ciganka bila,a sada se zove Josipovo.

When from Šarovka you head off to the west,there's Grabić village — nothing new, you know.And here there once was Ciganka as well,which nowadays is called Josipovo.

Kada jednom u Sopje navratiš,dobar narod, tu poštuju ljude.Sve što trebaš u Sopju ćeš naći.Gostoprimstvo dvije krčme nude.

And when you once drop in to Sopje, you'll findgood people here, who treat folk with respect.Whatever you may need, in Sopje it's there;two taverns offer welcome, as you'd expect.

Pa krenimo u Sopjansku Gredu,nogometno tu je igralište.KLUB "PODRAVAC" kad golove niže,navijači galame i vrište.

Now let us go on to Sopjanska Greda,where you will find the football pitch, and whenthe club "Podravac" scores goal after goal,the fans all yell and scream and holler then.

A Udruga "SPORTSKE REKREACIJE",mnogo ljeta već u Sopju traje,svome selu pružajuć' ljepotu,priređuje stare običaje.

The Association "Sports Recreation"has been in Sopje many summers through;it brings its village beauty, and it stagesthe old-time customs, as they used to do.

Kada stigne "DRAVSKO PRAMALJEĆE",tad MAJIPANE u Patošu sade.Na IVANJE svu ljepotu vatre,običaje svome selu rade.

And when the "Drava Springtime" comes around,they raise the majipane in Patoš then;at Ivanje, with all the bonfires' beauty,they keep their village customs up again.

KUD "PODRAVAC" u selu je dika,Slavonijom ponosno nastupa.Tamburaši i folklorna grupa,sve uspjehe oni čine skupa.

The KUD "Podravac" is the village's pride,and tours Slavonia proudly, far and wide;the tambura players and the dancing troupeachieve all their successes side by side.

Ritska Ada tu je nekad bila,pjesnika je ona iznjedrila.Još je ovdje, samo što se skriva,pjesnikovo još djetinjstvo sniva.

Here, long ago, was Ritska Ada's place;it brought the poet forth from its embrace.It's still here, only hiding from our view,and still the poet's childhood dreams there, too.

Sad krenimo na zapad polako:doći ćemo u selo Kapince.U "FIGARE" četrdeset šeste,naselio Tito Dalmatince.

Now slowly we'll head westward, and we'll comeinto the village of Kapinci, where,in "Figare", in the year of forty-six,Tito settled folk from Dalmatia there.

A Vaška je selo od davnina.Tri kirvaja u selu se slavi:VALENTINA, JAKOVA i ROKA,eto, to su običaji pravi!

And Vaška's been a village from of old;three kirvaj feasts the village celebrates:Saint Valentine's, Saint James's and Saint Roch's —now those are proper customs, those are great!

Novaki su odmah, iza Vaške,a desno je Đota i Zidina.Tu se čuje i rika jelena,mili Bože, pa to je milina!

Novaki's straight behind Vaška, and there,off to the right, lie Đota and Zidina.Here you can hear the stags bellowing, too —dear God, what a delight it is to hear!

Pustara je u Višnjici bila,nekad davno, prije mnogo ljeta.Neimari hotel izgradili,pa seoski turizam tu cvijeta.

There was a farmstead in Višnjica once,a long time back, so many years ago;the builders put a hotel up, and nowthe rural tourism here has come to grow.

Ljepše zgrade u tom kraju nema,stvorena je za razne namjene:Za sastanke i izlete raznei za svadbu kad se momci žene.

There is no finer building in those parts;it's made so it can serve all sorts of ends:for meetings and for outings of all kinds,and weddings, when the lads take wives, my friends.

Tu postoji i ergela konjai moderno izgrađene staje,a priroda dokle oko seže,turistima uživanja daje.

There is a stud farm here with horses, too,and stables newly built the modern way,and nature, far as ever eye can see,gives tourists their enjoyment every day.

Kad sve prođeš, tad u Španat dođeši Španat je naš djelokrug rada.Ta ljepota ove Podravine,eto, to je što Sopju pripada!

When you've gone through it all, you'll reach Španat,and Španat, too, lies in our working sphere.The beauty of this Podravina land —there, that is what belongs to Sopje, here!

Valpovo, 08. 09. 2011.

Valpovo, September 8, 2011

Translator's note. A tour of the villages of Sopje municipality, where the poet grew up (Ritska Ada). The majipane are maypoles; Ivanje is St John's Eve, with its bonfires; a kirvaj is a village's patron-saint feast; KUD "Podravac" is the village folk-culture society; in 1946, in the post-war resettlement, families from Dalmatia were settled at Kapinci.
