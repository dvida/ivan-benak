Domoljubne i zavičajne

Destinacije općine Sopja

Homeland and native region

Destinations of Sopje Municipality

Kada kreneš cestom Podravinekoja veže slavonske ravnice,prvom cestom što kraj Drave teče,od Suhopolja sve do Čađavice.

When you set out on the Podravina roadlinking all Slavonia's flatlands wide,from Suhopolje through to Čađavica,the first road that runs by Drava's side.

Najvažnije tu je selo Sopje.Od davnina općina je bila.U ljepoti podravskoga krajadevet sela k sebi prigrlila.

The most important village here is Sopje,a municipality since days of old;in the lovely Podravina countrynine villages it gathers in its fold.

Na istoku, ali s lijeve stranePredrijevo Gornje tu se svilo,a na desno Šarovka je Nova,nekada je to Čomborje bilo.

To the east, but on the left-hand side,Gornje Predrijevo is nestled in,and on the right lies Nova Šarovka,which in olden days Čomborje had been.

Od Šarovke kad na zapad kreneš,Grabić selo, to vam nije novo.Tu je nekad i Ciganka bila,a sada se zove Josipovo.

When from Šarovka you head off westward,there's Grabić village — nothing new, you know.Here there was Ciganka too, of old,which is called, these days, Josipovo.

Kada jednom u Sopje navratiš,dobar narod, tu poštuju ljude.Sve što trebaš u Sopju ćeš naći.Gostoprimstvo dvije krčme nude.

When one day you come to visit Sopje,the folk are good, and treat all people fair;all you need, in Sopje you will find it,hospitality two taverns share.

Pa krenimo u Sopjansku Gredu,nogometno tu je igralište.KLUB "PODRAVAC" kad golove niže,navijači galame i vrište.

Now let's go on to Sopjanska Greda:that is where the football pitch is laid out;when the club "Podravac" scores goal on goal,all the fans will yell and scream and shout.

A Udruga "SPORTSKE REKREACIJE",mnogo ljeta već u Sopju traje,svome selu pružajuć' ljepotu,priređuje stare običaje.

And the "Sports Recreation" Associationhas been part of Sopje's life for many a year;bringing beauty to its native village,and it stages the old customs, held so dear.

Kada stigne "DRAVSKO PRAMALJEĆE",tad MAJIPANE u Patošu sade.Na IVANJE svu ljepotu vatre,običaje svome selu rade.

When the "Drava Springtime" comes around,they plant majipane in Patoš then;on Ivanje, with the bonfires' beauty,they perform the village customs once again.

KUD "PODRAVAC" u selu je dika,Slavonijom ponosno nastupa.Tamburaši i folklorna grupa,sve uspjehe oni čine skupa.

KUD "Podravac" is the village's pride,proudly playing all Slavonia's lands;tambura players and the dancing bandswin their triumphs all by joining hands.

Ritska Ada tu je nekad bila,pjesnika je ona iznjedrila.Još je ovdje, samo što se skriva,pjesnikovo još djetinjstvo sniva.

Here, long ago, was Ritska Ada's place;it brought the poet forth from its embrace.It is still here, only hidden away,and the poet's childhood dreams there to this day.

Sad krenimo na zapad polako:doći ćemo u selo Kapince.U "FIGARE" četrdeset šeste,naselio Tito Dalmatince.

Now let's slowly head off to the west,and we'll come to Kapinci village, where,in "Figare", in the year forty-six,Tito settled folk from Dalmatia there.

A Vaška je selo od davnina.Tri kirvaja u selu se slavi:VALENTINA, JAKOVA i ROKA,eto, to su običaji pravi!

And Vaška's been a village from of old,with a kirvaj, not just one, but three:Saint Valentine's, Saint James's and Saint Roch's —there you are, real customs, as they should be!

Novaki su odmah, iza Vaške,a desno je Đota i Zidina.Tu se čuje i rika jelena,mili Bože, pa to je milina!

Straight past Vaška you will find Novaki,and Đota and Zidina to the right;here you'll hear the stags' deep roaring, too —oh, dear God, it's such a sweet delight!

Pustara je u Višnjici bila,nekad davno, prije mnogo ljeta.Neimari hotel izgradili,pa seoski turizam tu cvijeta.

In Višnjica there was once a farmstead,in the old days, many summers ago;builders came and put a hotel up,and here rural tourism came to grow.

Ljepše zgrade u tom kraju nema,stvorena je za razne namjene:Za sastanke i izlete raznei za svadbu kad se momci žene.

There's no finer building in those parts;it's built to serve all needs of village life:for meetings and for outings of all kinds,and for weddings, when a lad takes a wife.

Tu postoji i ergela konjai moderno izgrađene staje,a priroda dokle oko seže,turistima uživanja daje.

There's a stud farm here, with horses, too,and new stables built the modern way,and all nature, far as eye can see,gives the tourists pleasure every day.

Kad sve prođeš, tad u Španat dođeši Španat je naš djelokrug rada.Ta ljepota ove Podravine,eto, to je što Sopju pripada!

When you've gone through all, you'll reach Španat,and Španat, too, lies in our work and care;all this beauty of the Podravina,there, that is what's Sopje's rightful share!

Valpovo, 08. 09. 2011.

Valpovo, September 8, 2011

Translator's note. A tour of the villages of Sopje municipality, where the poet grew up (Ritska Ada). The majipane are maypoles; Ivanje is St John's Eve, with its bonfires; a kirvaj is a village's patron-saint feast; KUD "Podravac" is the village folk-culture society; in 1946, in the post-war resettlement, families from Dalmatia were settled at Kapinci.
