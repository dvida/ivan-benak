To a Girl from Drenovci

Oj, djevojko, ljepotice,iz povijesne Cvelferije.Da l' bi mogla noćas, djevo,da me tvoja ljubav grije.

Oh, young maiden, oh, you beautyfrom historic Cvelferija,could you let your love, dear maiden,keep me warm tonight, I wonder?

Došao sam iz daleka,do tih krasnih Drenovaca.Put me snaš'o u tuđini,a ja žedan poljubaca.

I have come from far awayto fair Drenovci, to you;far from home the road has caught me,thirsting for a kiss or two.

Želio bih noćas, draga,da ti ljubim bijelo licei s usana tvojih slatkih,pijem nektar, ljepotice.

I would like, my love, tonightjust to kiss your face so fair,and to drink, my lovely beauty,nectar from your sweet lips there.

Zato, dušo, dopusti mi,ostvarenje mojih snova.To bi bile samo skromne,želje Ive iz Valpova.

So, my darling, let it be,let my dreams come true, just so;these would only be the modestwishes of Ivo from Valpovo.

Valpovo, 12. 03. 2007.

Valpovo, March 12, 2007

Translator's note. Drenovci is a village in Cvelferija, the historic district of Sava-valley villages in Croatia's far east. Ivo, in the last line, is the poet himself.
