Domoljubne i zavičajne

Diljem Slavonije

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

All Across Slavonia

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja sam rođen u blizini Drave,u dolini polja i livada,okoline Podravske Slatine,Šokica me porodila mlada.

I was born close to the Drava river,in a vale of fields and meadows fair,round about Podravska Slatina,and a young Šokica bore me there.

Stasao sam kao jablan gordo,nema grada u kom nisam bio.Cure, snaše diljem Slavonije,kao bećar nježno sam ljubio.

Like a poplar I grew tall and proud,and there is no town that I have missed;girls and wives throughout Slavoniatenderly, as a bećar, I have kissed.

Kada krenem slavonskom ravnicom,u Or'ovcu kod Nade na prelo,pa kod Željke odoh u Miholjac,da obiđem „MIHOLJAČKO SIJELO“.

When I cross the plain of Slavonia,to Or'ovac, to Nada's prelo,then to Željka in Miholjac I go,there to see the "Miholjac Sijelo".

Eh što volim "VALPOVAČKO LJETO"!Kad se skupi Šokadija mlada,pa se kolo šorom zavijori,a pod šat'rom svakojaka slada.

Eh, how I love the "Valpovo Summer"!When young Šokadija gathers round,and the kolo whirls along the lane,and beneath the tent the treats abound.

Noću sjednem u park pokraj dvorca.Gledam mjesec Karašicu gazi,dok ja ljubim i milujem Katu,ona mi se umiljato mazi.

Nights I sit in the park by the castle,watch the moon on Karašica stride;while I'm kissing and caressing Kata,she cuddles sweetly, snug against my side.

Iz Đakova Andrejica mala,o njoj sanjam svake tihe noći,bećarsko mi srce otrovala,zato ću joj na „VEZOVE“ doći.

Little Andrejica, Đakovo's own,she's in my dreams each quiet night I sleep;she's poisoned my bećar heart to the bone,so at the "Vezovi" our tryst I'll keep.

Moj Osijeku grade pokraj Drave,u tebi sam divne snove snio,ljubeć' žarko jedne oči plave,s Valentinom ljubav provodio.

My Osijek, town where Drava's waters flow,in you I dreamed sweet dreams beyond compare,kissing ardently two blue eyes aglow,I shared my love with Valentina there.

Ej, Baranjo, tko te ne b' volio,tu sam često keriti se znao,sa Ilonkom uz zvuk violina,čardama sam noći poklanjao.

Hey, Baranja, who could help but love you?Here I'd often swagger, strut and play;with Ilonka, to the violins,to the čardas I gave my nights away.

"VINKOVAČKE JESENI" kad dođu,na Bosutu skupe se Šokice.S Verom idem u berbu jabuka,pjevajući u selo Borince.

When the "Vinkovci Autumns" come around,by the Bosut, all the Šokice throng;I go picking apples there with Vera,to Borinci village, singing a song.

Na istoku divne Slavonije,Vukovar se s Borovom srodioi na Dunav nježno oslonio.Vukicu sam u šajki ljubio.

In the east of lovely Slavonia,Vukovar and Borovo together resttenderly upon the Danube's breast;in a šajka Vukica I caressed.

Na svom putu odoh do Iloka,sve uz Dunav preko Šarengrada,da oberem vinograd sa Smiljom,da bećarskom srcu pružim slada.

On my journey I went down to Ilok,by the Danube, through Šarengrad, and therewith Smilja I would pick the vineyard clean,to treat my bećar heart to sweetest fare.

U Županji na "ŽUPANJSKOM SIJELU",ljubio sam jedne oči snene.U ČARDAKU na obali Save,srce mi je ostalo kod Tene.

In Županja, at the "Županja Sijelo",I kissed a pair of drowsy eyes, and oh,in the "Čardak", on the Sava's bank,my heart stayed back with Tena, loath to go.

Zatim krenuh za Babinu Gredu,iduć' cestom do Slavonskog Broda,da navratim do Bare i Klare,da se cure sjete moga roda.

Then I set off for Babina Greda,down the road to Slavonski Brod I came,to drop in on Bara and on Klara,so the girls recall my family name.

Kad Psunj prođem ja u Pakrac dođem,tu me čeka ljepotica Tanja.U Lipiku da osnažim tijelo,dočeka me i Sanja i BANJA.

When I pass Psunj and come to Pakrac town,there awaiting me is lovely Tanja;in Lipik, to give my body strength,I am met by Sanja and the Banja.

U Požegu na festival svratim,na festival pjesme Šokadije.Slušam zlatne žice tamburice,uz poljupce moje male Mice.

In Požega I call at the festival,the festival of Šokadija's song,and hear the tamburica's golden strings,along with kisses little Mica brings.

U Kutjevu zaustavim vrijeme,pijuć' vino nekoliko dana,uz tambure i Šokice mlade,volim život i to mi je mana.

In Kutjevo I make the time stand still,drinking wine as several days go by,with the tamburas and young Šokice;I love life, a fault I can't deny.

Na sir svratim kod Zdenke u Zdence,uz večeru da joj ljubim lice.U Daruvar kao svaki bećar,sa Anicom odoh u toplice.

I stop for cheese at Zdenka's, in Zdenci,and over supper there I kiss her face;to Daruvar, like every bećar does,I take Anica to the bathing-place.

Virov'ca je za mene bećara,sve najljepše što se može sniti.Tu mi Mara žarku ljubav dala,uvijek će mi u sjećanju biti.

Virov'ca, for a bećar like myself,is the loveliest thing that one could dream;here Mara gave me love that burned so bright,in my memory it will always gleam.

I na kraju moga putovanja,ja ponosno svratim u Našice.Ljubeć' žarko Mirjanicu malu,na izvoru rijeke Karašice.

And at the end of all my travellingI proudly call at Našice, to bringmy ardent kiss to little Mirjanicabeside the Karašica's very spring.

Obiš'o sam cijelu Slavoniju,sve gradove i šokačka sela.Naljubio i cura i snaša,sad me mazi u Valpovu Jela.

I've travelled all Slavonia over,its towns and Šokac villages I'd roam;I've kissed my fill of maids and young wives both,now Jela spoils me in Valpovo, at home.

Ponosan sam na svoje porijeklo,što me rodi Hrvatica mlada,ja sam sinak dične Slavonije,ja sam rodom iz svakoga grada.

I am proud of my roots and where I'm from,that a young Croatian woman gave me birth;I'm a son of glorious Slavonia,every town's my home town on this earth.

Valpovo, 16. 12. 1993.

Valpovo, December 16, 1993

Translator's note. A bećar's tour of Slavonia's towns and festivals, with a sweetheart in each. Baranja is the region across the Drava with Hungarian traditions (a čarda is a Hungarian-style roadside inn); a šajka is a small Danube boat; a čardak is an old frontier watchtower; the "golden strings" nod to Požega's festival, Zlatne žice Slavonije ("The Golden Strings of Slavonia"); and "banja" means a spa, rhyming with Tanja and Sanja.
