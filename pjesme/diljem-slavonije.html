Domoljubne i zavičajne

Diljem Slavonije

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

All Across Slavonia

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja sam rođen u blizini Drave,u dolini polja i livada,okoline Podravske Slatine,Šokica me porodila mlada.

I was born close by the Drava's banks,in the vale of the fields and meadows fair,in the country round Podravska Slatina;a young Šokica gave birth to me there.

Stasao sam kao jablan gordo,nema grada u kom nisam bio.Cure, snaše diljem Slavonije,kao bećar nježno sam ljubio.

I grew as proud and tall as a poplar tree;there is no town in which I haven't been.The girls and young wives all across SlavoniaI've kissed as a bećar, tenderly, each one.

Kada krenem slavonskom ravnicom,u Or'ovcu kod Nade na prelo,pa kod Željke odoh u Miholjac,da obiđem „MIHOLJAČKO SIJELO“.

When I set off across Slavonia's plain,to Or'ovac, to Nada's, for the prelo,then off to Željka in Miholjac I go,to visit the "Miholjac Sijelo".

Eh što volim "VALPOVAČKO LJETO"!Kad se skupi Šokadija mlada,pa se kolo šorom zavijori,a pod šat'rom svakojaka slada.

Eh, how I love the "Valpovo Summer", yes!When the young Šokadija gathers round,and the kolo whirls along the village lane,and under the tent all kinds of treats are found.

Noću sjednem u park pokraj dvorca.Gledam mjesec Karašicu gazi,dok ja ljubim i milujem Katu,ona mi se umiljato mazi.

At night I sit in the park beside the castle,watching the moon wade Karašica through,and while I'm kissing and caressing Kata,she cuddles up so sweetly to me, too.

Iz Đakova Andrejica mala,o njoj sanjam svake tihe noći,bećarsko mi srce otrovala,zato ću joj na „VEZOVE“ doći.

Little Andrejica from Đakovo:I dream of her through every quiet night;she's poisoned this bećar heart of mine,so to her at the "Vezovi" I'll go.

Moj Osijeku grade pokraj Drave,u tebi sam divne snove snio,ljubeć' žarko jedne oči plave,s Valentinom ljubav provodio.

My Osijek, my city by the Drava,in you I dreamed the loveliest of dreams,kissing a pair of blue eyes ardently,and spending all my love on Valentina.

Ej, Baranjo, tko te ne b' volio,tu sam često keriti se znao,sa Ilonkom uz zvuk violina,čardama sam noći poklanjao.

Hey, Baranja, who could help but love you?Here I'd go carousing, as I'd often do;with Ilonka, to the sound of violins,I gave my nights away to the čardas, too.

"VINKOVAČKE JESENI" kad dođu,na Bosutu skupe se Šokice.S Verom idem u berbu jabuka,pjevajući u selo Borince.

When the "Vinkovci Autumns" come around,the Šokice gather by the Bosut, see;with Vera I go picking apples, singingalong the way to Borinci, she and me.

Na istoku divne Slavonije,Vukovar se s Borovom srodioi na Dunav nježno oslonio.Vukicu sam u šajki ljubio.

In lovely Slavonia's east, Vukovarhas grown together with Borovo,and leans upon the Danube tenderly.I kissed Vukica in a šajka boat.

Na svom putu odoh do Iloka,sve uz Dunav preko Šarengrada,da oberem vinograd sa Smiljom,da bećarskom srcu pružim slada.

Upon my way I went on to Ilok,along the Danube, through Šarengrad, and thereto pick the vineyard's grapes with Smilja,to treat my bećar heart to sweetness fair.

U Županji na "ŽUPANJSKOM SIJELU",ljubio sam jedne oči snene.U ČARDAKU na obali Save,srce mi je ostalo kod Tene.

In Županja, at the "Županja Sijelo",I kissed a pair of drowsy eyes, and thenin the Čardak, on the bank of the Sava,I left my heart behind me with Tena.

Zatim krenuh za Babinu Gredu,iduć' cestom do Slavonskog Broda,da navratim do Bare i Klare,da se cure sjete moga roda.

Then I set off for Babina Greda,going along the road to Slavonski Brod,to drop in on Bara and on Klara,so that the girls would still recall my stock.

Kad Psunj prođem ja u Pakrac dođem,tu me čeka ljepotica Tanja.U Lipiku da osnažim tijelo,dočeka me i Sanja i BANJA.

When I pass Psunj, I come into Pakrac,where I am awaited by the lovely Tanja;in Lipik, to restore my body's vigour,I'm welcomed there by Sanja and the Banja.

U Požegu na festival svratim,na festival pjesme Šokadije.Slušam zlatne žice tamburice,uz poljupce moje male Mice.

To Požega I drop in for the festival,the festival of Šokadija's song;I hear the golden strings of the tamburica,with kisses from my little darling Mica.

U Kutjevu zaustavim vrijeme,pijuć' vino nekoliko dana,uz tambure i Šokice mlade,volim život i to mi je mana.

In Kutjevo I make the time stand still,and drink the wine for several days away,with tamburas and with the young Šokice;I love life — that's my failing, I must say.

Na sir svratim kod Zdenke u Zdence,uz večeru da joj ljubim lice.U Daruvar kao svaki bećar,sa Anicom odoh u toplice.

I stop for cheese at Zdenka's, in Zdenci,and over supper there I kiss her face;to Daruvar, as every bećar does,I go off with Anica to the spa.

Virov'ca je za mene bećara,sve najljepše što se može sniti.Tu mi Mara žarku ljubav dala,uvijek će mi u sjećanju biti.

Virov'ca is, for a bećar like me,the loveliest of all that one could dream;'twas here that Mara gave me burning love,and it will always stay in my memory.

I na kraju moga putovanja,ja ponosno svratim u Našice.Ljubeć' žarko Mirjanicu malu,na izvoru rijeke Karašice.

And at the end of all my travellingI proudly make my call at Našice,ardently kissing little Mirjanicabeside the spring of the Karašica.

Obiš'o sam cijelu Slavoniju,sve gradove i šokačka sela.Naljubio i cura i snaša,sad me mazi u Valpovu Jela.

I've travelled all around Slavonia,through all its towns and Šokac villages, too;I've kissed my fill of girls and of young wives;now Jela cuddles me in Valpovo, true.

Ponosan sam na svoje porijeklo,što me rodi Hrvatica mlada,ja sam sinak dične Slavonije,ja sam rodom iz svakoga grada.

I'm proud of my descent, of where I'm from,proud that a young Croatian woman bore me;I am a son of glorious Slavonia,and every town is my home town to me.

Valpovo, 16. 12. 1993.

Valpovo, December 16, 1993

Translator's note. A bećar's tour of Slavonia's towns and festivals, with a sweetheart in each. Baranja is the region across the Drava with Hungarian traditions (a čarda is a Hungarian-style roadside inn); a šajka is a small Danube boat; a čardak is an old frontier watchtower; the "golden strings" nod to Požega's festival, Zlatne žice Slavonije ("The Golden Strings of Slavonia"); and "banja" means a spa, rhyming with Tanja and Sanja.
