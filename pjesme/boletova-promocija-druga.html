Bole's Second Book Launch

Sada drugu promociju ima,tu ljepotu da dokaže svima.Skupili se ljudi Slavonije,da mu pruže radost promocije.

And now he has a second launch, to showthis beauty to us all, so all may know.The people of Slavonia came, to sharewith him the joy of launching his book there.

Kada, Bole, promociju slavi,onda su to događaji pravi.Ovaj puta Bole sretan bio,Našički je dvorac ukrasio.

When Bole has a launch to celebrate,then those are real events, and truly great.This time our Bole, happy as could be,adorned the Našice castle splendidly.

U pisanju pjesnik Bole cvjeta,ova knjiga već mu je deveta,a zove se: "KAZALJKE ŽIVOTA",za čitanje prava je divota.

The poet Bole flourishes in rhyme,and this book is already ninth in line;it's called "The Hands of Life", and reading itis pure delight, a marvel, every bit.

Daj Muzama svoju ruku pruži,Tebi, Bole, želim sretne dane,pero spretno da papirom kruži,da deseta knjiga skoro svane!

Come, reach your hand out to the Muses, do!Bole, I wish you happy days, and mayyour nimble pen keep circling pages through,so that your tenth book soon may see the day!

Piši, Bole, ovo krasno štivo:to Ti želi pjesnik Benak Ivo!

Keep writing, Bole, such fine reading, so:that is the wish of poet Benak Ivo!

Valpovo, 27. 05. 2014.

Valpovo, May 27, 2014

Translator's note. For the second launch of his fellow poet Bole's ninth book, Kazaljke života ("The Hands of Life", as in the hands of a clock), held in the castle at Našice.
