Kroz Valpovo teče Karašica

Love poems

The Karašica Flows Through Valpovo

Noć Mjesecom obasjana cijelai zvijezdama okićeno veče',ja i moja Valpovčanka Jela,Karašicu gledamo gdje teče.

The moon has lit the whole night through and through,the evening is adorned with every star;my Valpovo girl Jela and I, we two,watch Karašica flowing where we are.

Kad je lipa šorom mirisala,ljubav mi je Valpovčanka dala,mjesečina nas je obasjala,Karašica tiho proticala.

When linden blossom scented all the lane,my girl from Valpovo gave her love to me;the moonlight shone upon the two of us,and Karašica flowed by quietly.

Kada noćca na počinak zove,poljupcima sklopim njene očii ugodne poželim joj snove,jer uskoro i zora će doći.

When gentle night calls us to take our rest,with kisses I close softly both her eyes,and wish her pleasant dreams, the very best,for soon enough the dawn, too, will arise.

Kad jutarnji zapjevaju pijetli,zora nam je odnosila snovezagrljeni kad danak zasvijetli,poljupci nam daju snage nove.

When morning roosters start their crowing cries,the dawn would carry all our dreams away;embraced, as daylight glimmers in the skies,our kisses give new strength to face the day.

Valpovo, 29. 01. 2012.

Valpovo, January 29, 2012

Translator's note. The Karašica is the small river that runs through Valpovo; Jela is a Valpovčanka, a girl from Valpovo.
