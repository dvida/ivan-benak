Još sam budan

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Love poems

Still Awake

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Još sam budan, mila,u to kasno doba.O tebi se mojemisli sada roje.Već i ponoć tihokuca mi na prozor.Ja željan ljubavisnivam usne tvoje.

Still awake, my darling,at this late, silent hour.My thoughts are all of you,they swarm the whole night through.And midnight, soft and low,is tapping on the glass.Longing for love, I lieand dream your lips anew.

Još sam budan, mila,u te sitne sate.Svojom pjesmom pjetlinajavljuju zoru.Sa zvijezdama sjajnimsvoje želje snujem.Da sam barem cvijetak,na tvome prozoru.

Still awake, my darling,in these small hours of night.The roosters herald dawnwith crowing loud and shrill;beneath the shining starsI spin my dearest wishes:were I a little flowerupon your window sill!

U snovima, mila,ti bi osjetila,da te toga časau naručje želim.Pa bi davnu mojuispunila željui stavila menek sebi u postelju.

In dreams, my darling, youwould feel it, sure and true,that at this very hourI long to hold you tight.And then you would fulfilmy old wish, long unsaid,and take me, love, to liebeside you in your bed.

Valpovo, 1973.

Valpovo, 1973
