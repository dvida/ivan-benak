Love poems

Still Awake

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Još sam budan, mila,u to kasno doba.O tebi se mojemisli sada roje.Već i ponoć tihokuca mi na prozor.Ja željan ljubavisnivam usne tvoje.

I'm still awake, darling,at this hour so late;my thoughts about younow are swarming in flight.And already midnightsoftly knocks at my pane;and, longing for love,I dream your lips tonight.

Još sam budan, mila,u te sitne sate.Svojom pjesmom pjetlinajavljuju zoru.Sa zvijezdama sjajnimsvoje želje snujem.Da sam barem cvijetak,na tvome prozoru.

Still awake, my darling,in these small hours now.The roosters with singingannounce the new morning.With the bright shining starsI am weaving my wishes.If I were but a flower,your window adorning.

U snovima, mila,ti bi osjetila,da te toga časau naručje želim.Pa bi davnu mojuispunila željui stavila menek sebi u postelju.

In your dreams, my darling,you'd feel it and knowthat this very momentI want you in my arms.And then you'd fulfilthe wish I've long had,and take me beside youto lie in your bed.

Valpovo, 1973.

Valpovo, 1973
