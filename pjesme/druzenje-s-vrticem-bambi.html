Pjesme za djecu

Druženje s vrtićem „Bambi“

Poems for children

Get-Together with the Bambi Kindergarten

Desila se djecoJedna lijepa priča,Dobili smo gosteIz „Bambi“ vrtića.To je naša radost,Nećemo je kriti,Kod nas ćete lijepoDočekani biti.I tete su našeJako vrijedne bile,Poklone su za vasDjeco napravile.Ja se zato nadamI vi ćete znatiPjesmice lijepeNama otpjevati.Zato i vi namaPružite veselje.Eto to su djecoNaše drage želje.

Children, something lovelyhas happened here today:guests from Bambi kindergartenhave come along our way.This is our joy,we won't hide it, you see;with us you'll be welcomedas warmly as can be.And our aunties, too,have worked so hard, it's true;they have made some presents,children, just for you.And so I hope that youwill know a song or two,some pretty little songsto sing for us. Will you?So give us, in your turn,some gladness too, we pray.There, children, those areour dear wishes today.

Valpovo, 21. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 21, 2012

Translator's note. A greeting for the children of the Bambi kindergarten at a Christmas get-together in the Valpovo pensioners' home, December 2012.
