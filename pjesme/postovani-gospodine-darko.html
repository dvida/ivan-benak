Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani gospodine Darko!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Mr Darko!

Nek Vam ova knjiga bude, Darko,kao dokaz da vas vrlo cijenim,ovo naše divno prijateljstvo,neka traje, ja iskreno želim.

May this book, Darko, serve you as the proofof the esteem I hold you in, it's true;this lovely friendship that we two now share —may it endure: my honest wish for you.

Spojilo nas kolo na Ćimenu,što Marijance krasi još odavno,poznata je to smotra folklora,na Petrovo što se slavi javno.

We two were brought together by the koloupon the Ćimen, Marijanci's pride of old;it is a well-known festival of folklorethat on St Peter's Day for all is held.

Da bi ljudi za Marijance znali,iskrene su bile moje želje,pa sam javno objavio pjesmu:NA ĆIMENU ŠOKAČKO VESELJE!

So that the people might know Marijanci,my wishes were as honest as could be;and so I went and published, openly,a poem: ŠOKAC MERRIMENT ON THE ĆIMEN!

U toj knjizi na dvanaestoj strani,tu je pjesma svoju vrijednost dalai služi nam slika k'o reklama,zato će nam ona reći HVALA!

In that same book, on page twelve, there it stands:the poem there has proved its worth, it's true;the picture serves us as an advert, too,and that's why it will say to us THANK YOU!

Valpovo, 27. 05. 2014.

Valpovo, May 27, 2014

Translator's note. The Ćimen is the village green of Marijanci, where the folklore festival is held on St Peter's Day (Petrovo, 29 June). The poem he names is his "Šokac Merriment on the Čimen".
