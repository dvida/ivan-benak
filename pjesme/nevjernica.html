Love poems

Faithless One!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ugledah te sinoć s ljubavnikom tvojim,ozarena lica gdje mu piješ oči.Osvrnuh se gnjevno u toj strašnoj noći.Tužan pođoh dalje ja sa bolom svojim.

Last night I saw you with your lover at your side,your face aglow, the way you drank in his eyes;I turned in anger, in that dreadful night,and sadly I went on, my pain inside.

Ljubila si njemu žarko oči snene.Najsretnije biće on je tada bio,da si prije mene, on to nije snio.Ljubiš sada njega kao sinoć mene.

You kissed his drowsy eyes so ardently;the happiest of beings he was then;that you'd been mine before was far beyond his ken.You kiss him now as, just last night, you kissed me.

Sjećaš li se riječi kada si se klela?Da nikada nikog ljubit nećeš moći,da ti žarku svjetlost ne vidjele oči,ako budeš ikad drugog ljubit htjela!

Do you remember what you swore, the words you said?That you could never love another all your days,that may your eyes no longer see the burning raysif ever you should want to love someone instead!

Ljubavi za tebe više ne postoji.Poljupcima svojim širiš samo laži.Drugi je od mene sada tebi draži.U grudima mojim bolno srce stoji.

For you there is no longer any love to be;with all your kisses you spread nothing now but lies;another man is dearer to you now than I;and in my breast my heart stands still in agony.

Valpovo, 1976.
