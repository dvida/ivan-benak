Domoljubne i zavičajne

Podravino, diko moja

Homeland and native region

Podravina, My Pride

Poštovani ljudi Sopja,sumještani dragi moji,u Sopju sam i ja rođen,u zapisu tako stoji.

People of Sopje, whom I honour,fellow villagers so dear,I was born in Sopje, too:so it's written, plain and clear.

Ali vrijeme čini svoje,sudbina je tako htjela,odvela me u tuđinu,ostao sam bez svog sela.

But then time will have its way,fate had willed that it should be:led me off to foreign lands,and my village lost to me.

Godine su prolazile,ponovo se sudba javi,na trenutak da me vrati,u zavičaj, tu na Dravi.

Years went by, as years will do,then once more did fate draw near,for a moment to return mehome, beside the Drava, here.

Evo mene, ja se javljam,Podravino, mili kraju.Vraćam ti se u zagrljaj,moj predivni zavičaju.

Here I am, and here I greet you,Podravina, dear land of mine;I'm returning to your embrace,O my homeland, so divine.

Ja u Sopju nikog nemam,al' me srce k njemu vuče.Ostalo mi tu djetinjstvou sjećanju stare kuće.

I have no one now in Sopje,yet my heart is drawn there, too;there my childhood stayed behind,in the old house that I knew.

Da ozarim Sopju licekao pjesnik, ja ću znati.Svojim srcem, svojim perom,svu ljepotu ću mu dati.

How to light up Sopje's face,as a poet, I will know:with my heart and with my penall its beauty I'll bestow.

Učinit ću sve što mogudičnom Sopju, mome selu,kulturno ga uzdizatii pružiti mu ljubav cijelu.

I will do all that I canfor my village, Sopje, fair,raise it up in culture, andgive it all my love and care.

Još te volim, rodni kraju,meni treba ljubav tvoja.Eh, da mogu prošlost vratiti,Podravino, diko moja!

Still I love you, native land;and I need that love of thine.Ah, if I could bring the past back,Podravina, pride of mine!

Valpovo, 29. 08. 2011.

Valpovo, August 29, 2011

Translator's note. Addressed to the people of Sopje, the village in the Podravina (the Drava lowland) where he was born.
