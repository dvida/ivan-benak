Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend, the Musician!

Od pjesnika umjetniku,nek nam bude to na diku,što stvaramo našim radom,ovom svijetu ljepšu sliku.

From a poet to an artist:let it be our pride and gracethat we give, through all our labours,to this world a lovelier face.

Ja sam pjesnik od davnina.Pišem pjesme ja iz želje,Tomislave uglazbi ih:Čovječanstvu na veselje!

I'm a poet from of old,I write poems as I'm inclined;Tomislav, set them to music,for the joy of all mankind!

Ovu knjigu ja darujem:Tomislavu iz dragosti,nek' mu ona sama dade,uživanja i radosti.

This, my book, I give awayto Tomislav, from the heart;may the book itself bestowjoy and pleasure, for its part.

Tomislavu Cvetkovićuza ugodu i veselje,nek' mu knjiga radost pruži.Autorove to su želje!

To Tomislav Cvetković,for his pleasure and his cheer:may the book bring him delight —such are the author's wishes here!

Valpovo, 12.02.2014.

Valpovo, February 12, 2014

Translator's note. Written in a book given to the musician Tomislav Cvetković, asking him to set the poems to music.
