Sve se "Čaje" okupiše

Occasional verse and dedications

All of Čaje Gathered Here

Ivankovo pitomo i milo,Slavoniju divno okitilo.Poštuju se Šokci tu odavno,zato slave običaje javno.Da bi bilo slavlje punopravno,mnoga društva nastupaju javno.

Ivankovo, so gentle and so dear,has decked Slavonia with beauty here.The Šokci here have long been honoured, sothey celebrate their customs, all on show.So that the feast be whole, as feasts should go,societies perform, a public show.

I POTKOVE svoj su obol dali,Ivankovom kasom projahali,Kada topot kopita odjeknei prašina kad se šorom slegne.

"POTKOVE", too, have played their part with pride,and through Ivankovo at a trot they ride,when hoofbeats echo loudly all around,and on the lane the dust sinks to the ground.

Tad povorka kod Bošnjaka stane,"ČAJE" dođu do kino dvorane.Kada "ČAJE" dobošar najavi,da šokačka priredba se slavi.Petnaest ljeta "ČAJE" osnovane,zato slave te svečane dane.Drage su im sve ljepote ove,pogotovo kad vode svatove.

Then at Bošnjak's the parade comes to a stand,and "ČAJE" reach the cinema hall as planned.The crier beats his drum for "ČAJE"'s name:a Šokac celebration, he'll proclaim.Since "ČAJE"'s founding fifteen summers passed,and so these festive days are here at last.They cherish all this beauty, and the mostwhen leading wedding guests, a merry host.

Obrt rada od davnina znani,to će biti u športskoj dvorani.KUD "ŠUMARI" kolo zavijore,a "PANJEVI" sa pjesmom zaore.I pjevački muški zbor "ORNICE",što pjevaju pjesme povjesnice,svu ljepotu večeras će dati,Ivankovo pjesmom opjevati.

The age-old crafts, well known from long ago,will in the sports hall now put on their show.KUD "ŠUMARI" will whirl the kolo round,and "PANJEVI" will make their songs resound.The male-voice choir, the "ORNICE", as well,whose songs the tales of olden times retell,tonight will give the beauty that they bring,and make Ivankovo the song they sing.

Ivan Benak pjesnik iz Valpova,napisao puno divnih snova,tri je himne napisao Ivoi šest knjiga i nije mu krivo.Tri su moje knjige razne teme,tri za djecu tu nema dileme.Pjesama je šesnaest uglazbljeno,tamburicom javno izvedeno.Kad svirači zasviraju znano,NE BUDI ME, MAJKO, TAKO RANO,To mi vrati onu ljubav staru,pa zaigra srce u bećaru.

Ivan Benak, poet of Valpovo,has written many lovely dreams, you know;three anthems has our Ivo penned with pride,six books as well, and he is satisfied.Three of my books have varied themes throughout,and three are for children, there's no doubt.Sixteen of my poems are set to song,and played in public, tamburas strumming strong.When players strike that tune so widely known,"DON'T WAKE ME EARLY, MOTHER", all my own,it brings me back that old love from before,and then the bećar's heart will dance once more.

To predivno društvo "PERUNIKE",Ivankovu posebne su dike.Slavonijom često nastupajui publici svu ljepotu daju."PERUNIKE" priroda je dala,a Marija Cavor izabrala.

That lovely troupe, the "PERUNIKE", bright,are Ivankovo's special pride and delight.Across Slavonia often they appearand give the public all their beauty here.The "PERUNIKE" are nature's gift, so fair,and Marija Cavor chose them with such care.

"RAVNICA" je Subotice dika,u nastupu zvijezda povelika.Bunjevačko kad zaplešu kolo,cura vikne: dođi meni lolo!

"RAVNICA" is Subotica's pride and fame,on stage a star of truly great acclaim.And when the Bunjevac kolo starts to go,a girl cries out: "Come here to me, my beau!"

Vinkovčanke srednje stručne škole,podarit će publici što vole.U reviji modnoj okićene,kao lutke lijepo obučene.

Vinkovci's trade-school girls are coming too,to give the audience what it loves to view:all dressed up for a fashion show parade,like dolls, in lovely clothing all arrayed.

Paško Mali Barunčić je znao,i svatovski rekord održao.U svadbi je najsretniji bio:petsto puta mladu poljubio!

Paško Mali Barunčić knew the way,and set a wedding record on his day:at that wedding none was happier than he:he kissed the bride five hundred times, with glee!

Jozo Penić prvi put nastupa,pa mu srce od miline lupa.

Jozo Penić performs today, his first,his heart with sheer delight is fit to burst!

"IVAN GORAN KOVAČIĆ" je pravi,KUD što svoju obljetnicu slavi.Pedesetak godina postoje,još pedeset neka slavno broje.Kolo plešu, a tambure rade,pa im nisu ravne niti LADE.Za to slavlje ovom KUD-u HVALA,tu ljepotu Ksenija je dala.

The true KUD, "IVAN GORAN KOVAČIĆ", herenow marks its anniversary this year.For fifty years or so they've been around;may fifty more in glory yet be found.They dance the kolo, and the tamburas play;not even "LADO" outshines them any day.For this great feast, this KUD our THANKS has won:this beauty is what Ksenija has done.

Tu bih sada ovo završioi prisutne toplo pozdravio.Zbog ljepote koje ovdje ima:puno hvala dragim sponzorima!

And here I'd like to bring this to an end,and warmest greetings to all present send.For all the beauty we have here on show,to our dear sponsors many thanks we owe!

Ivankovo to je gradić mali,još mu samo naslov grada fali.

Ivankovo's a little town, that's plain;it's just the title "town" it's yet to gain.

Valpovo, 11. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 11, 2013

Translator's note. Written as the compère's verse for the 15th-anniversary show of the folk society "Čaje" in Ivankovo, introducing each group in turn. A KUD is an amateur folk-culture society; Lado is Croatia's national folk dance ensemble; perunika is the iris.
