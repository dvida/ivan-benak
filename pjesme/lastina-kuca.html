The Swallow's House

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Lastino gnijezdo maloveć mnogo ljeta broji,kao ukras kuće starepod strehom ponosno stoji.

Many summers now have passedfor the swallow's little nest;an adornment to the old house,proud beneath the eaves, at rest.

U svako pramaljećenama se laste jave.Gnijezdo kad brižno srede,jaja u njega stave.

Every year, in early spring,swallows come to us again;when with care they've fixed the nest,they lay eggs inside it then.

Kad prođe neko vrijemei stigne ljeto vruće,ptići veselo cvrče,jer žele van iz kuće.

When a little time has passedand hot summer comes about,little chicks chirp merrily,for they're longing to get out!

Maleni brzo rastu,dođe i konac ljeta.U jata se tada skupe –odoše na kraj svijeta!

Quickly grow the little ones,and the summer's end draws nigh;then they gather into flocks,off to the world's end they fly!
