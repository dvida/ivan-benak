The Swallow's House

Lastino gnijezdo maloveć mnogo ljeta broji,kao ukras kuće starepod strehom ponosno stoji.

Many summers now have passedfor the swallow's little nest;an adornment to the old house,proud beneath the eaves, it rests.

U svako pramaljećenama se laste jave.Gnijezdo kad brižno srede,jaja u njega stave.

Every single springtimethe swallows greet us again;once they've tidied the nest with care,they lay their eggs in it then.

Kad prođe neko vrijemei stigne ljeto vruće,ptići veselo cvrče,jer žele van iz kuće.

When a little time has passedand hot summer comes about,the nestlings chirp so merrily —they're longing to get out!

Maleni brzo rastu,dođe i konac ljeta.U jata se tada skupe –odoše na kraj svijeta!

The little ones grow quickly,and summer's end draws nigh;then into flocks they gather —off to the world's end they fly!
