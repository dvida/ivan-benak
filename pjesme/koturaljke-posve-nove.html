Radio burza

Koturaljke posve nove

Radio market

Roller Skates, Brand New

Ja se javljam u proljetne dane,koturaljke još nisu prodane.

I'm calling in again, now spring is here:the roller skates aren't sold yet, I fear.

Koturaljke, rolne, posve nove,beskorisno tu kod mene stoje.Mnoga djeca već snivaju snove,da imaju koturaljke svoje.

These roller skates, or rollerblades, brand new,stand here with me, and idle all the day;and many children dream, the whole night through,of skates to call their very own, and play.

Od trideset devet do četrdeset tri,njihov je broj baš za takvu nogu.Curice, dečki i svi odrasli,za sitnu lovu kupit ih mogu.

From size thirty-nine up to forty-three,they fit a foot like that one, snug and neat;young girls and lads and grown-ups, yes, all three,can buy them for small change, oh, what a treat!

Neka bude djeci sreća puna,koturaljke dajem za sto kuna.

May children's happiness be full and whole:a hundred kuna, and away they roll!

Neka bude ovaj oglas sretan, javite se na:6 5 2 osamsto dvadeset jedan.

And may this ad be lucky, the sale soon done; ring me on:6 5 2 eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 10. 02. 2007.

Valpovo, February 10, 2007

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad; the sizes are European shoe sizes.
