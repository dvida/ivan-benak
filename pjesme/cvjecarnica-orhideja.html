Cvjećarnica „Orhideja“

Occasional verse and dedications

The Orhideja Flower Shop

Svratio sam u cvjećarsku radnjukod Jelene u Valpovu gradu,vidio sam razne aranžmane,koje stvara u svojemu radu.

I stopped in at a florist's shop, the onethat Jelena keeps, in Valpovo; and thereI saw arrangements of all kinds, the sortshe fashions in her work with skill and care.

Blizu crkve smještena je radnja,cvjećarnica zvana "ORHIDEJA".Tko što treba neka ovdje svrati,Jela ima svakakvih ideja.

Close by the church you'll find this shop of hers,the flower shop they call the "Orhideja".Whoever needs a thing, let them drop in:Jela's got every sort of fine idea.

Vijenac plete od prirodnog cvijeća.Ispleteno i oku je drago.Orhideje, ruže, tulipani,osnovno je to njezino blago.

She weaves her wreaths from real, natural flowers,and when they're woven, to the eye they're dear;orchids and roses, tulips — these are allthe basic treasures that she's keeping here.

Izrađuje predivne bukete,što mladenke nose na vjenčanju,aranžmane divno izrađene,jer je majstor u svojemu radu.

She makes the loveliest bouquets, the onesthat brides will carry on their wedding day,arrangements beautifully made, becauseshe is a master at her work, I'd say.

U cvjećarni ima raznog cvijeća,priroda joj tu ljepotu dala,a predmete lijepo izrađene,svojim radom Jela uljepšala.

The flower shop has flowers of every kind,their beauty given them by nature's will;and pieces that are beautifully wroughtby Jela's work are made more lovely still.

Ne znaš što je ljepše u cvjećarni,da li cvijetak ili majstorica,možda buket koji krasi mladu,il' uskršnja cvjetna košarica.

You cannot tell what's lovelier in there:the little flower, or the mistress of the trade,perhaps the bouquet that adorns the bride,or a flowered Easter basket she has made.

Kad je vidiš to je odmah jasno,divna usta uvijek nasmijana,nježan pogled što ti dušu grije,ta ljepota od Boga joj dana.

The moment you see her, it's clear to you:her lovely mouth is always smiling bright,her gentle look will warm your very soul —that beauty God has given her outright.

I još jedna vrlina ju krasi:poznata je po dobroti Jela.Ako nemaš novaca kod sebe,drugi put ćeš platit njena djela.

And there's one virtue more that graces her:for kindness Jela is widely known;if you've no money on you, you can payanother time for work that she has done.

Valpovo, 02. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 2, 2010

Translator's note. Orhideja means "orchid". Jela is the short form of Jelena.
