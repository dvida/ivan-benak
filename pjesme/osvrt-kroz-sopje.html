Domoljubne i zavičajne

Osvrt kroz Sopje

Homeland and native region

A Look Around Sopje

Kada kreneš cestom Podravinekoja veže Slavonske ravnice,tu ljepotu i gledat je milo,eto, tu se Sopje nastanilo.

When you set out along the Podravina roadthat links the plains of Slavonia as one,its beauty is a pleasure to the eye:and there, you see, is where Sopje came to lie.

Od drugoga stoljeća, još davno,Sopje broji svoja ljeta slavno.Vrijedni ljudi sa svačim se bave,događaje važne oni slave.

Since far-off days, the second century,Sopje has counted its summers gloriously.Its people work at all things, diligent,and celebrate each notable event.

Dan općine četvrti je svibnja,Florijanu ljudi zavjet daju,da ne bude velikih požara,kao Patoš što Sopjani znaju.

Municipal Day falls on the fourth of May;the people vow to Florian, and praythat no great fires befall them, like Patoš,as Sopje's people know unto this day.

U proljeće MAIPANI se sade,to mladići znak curama daju,jer ujesen svatovi se prave,neka budu spremne za udaju.

In spring they plant the maypoles in the ground:the young men give the girls a sign this way,for in the autumn weddings will be held,so let them be prepared for their wedding day.

Kada Dravsko proljeće osvane,to je smotra narodnog folklora,KUD "Podravac" slavi svoje dane,tu ljepotu doživjet se mora.

When Dravsko proljeće has dawned again,the people's folklore gathers on display;KUD "Podravac" celebrates its days —that beauty is a thing you must see, I say.

VIDOVO se u ljepoti slavi,kirvaj Sopja što odavno traje.Skupljaju se prijatelji pravi,da proslave stare običaje.

Vidovo's celebrated, fine and fair,the church fair Sopje's held for many a year;true friends come gathering together thereto celebrate old customs, held so dear.

IVANJE se slavi još odavno,u ljepoti dobro odigranood Udruge "Sportske rekreacije"sa ljepotom Sopju odigrano.

St John's is celebrated as of old,performed so well, in beauty, every part,by the Sports Recreation Society,performed for Sopje with a lovely art.

Kad Marija Magdalena dođe,drugi kirvaj što u Sopju traje,pa kroz Sopje sva ljepota prođe,to crkvene slave običaje.

When Mary Magdalene's feast day comes round,the second church fair Sopje keeps each year,then all of Sopje's beauty passes through:the customs of the parish feast are here.

Sopjani se sa agrarom bave,rodne njive plodove im daju.Sve praznike u svom selu slave,zato žive u zemaljskom raju.

The Sopje people farm the land they till,their fertile fields give fruit of every kind;they celebrate each holiday in their village,and so they live in paradise, you'll find.

Nogometni klub "Podravac" slavni,od davnina svoja ljeta broji.U Sopjanskom kraju on je glavni.Četrdeset šeste još postoji.

The famous football club, "Podravac",has counted up its years since long ago.In Sopje's region it's the leading one;it's been around since forty-six, you know.

Dobrovoljno vatrogasno društvo,već stotinjak godina on niže,sve požare u Sopju i bliže,svojim radom ugasiti stiže.

The Volunteer Fire Brigade by now has strunga hundred years or so, now, in a line;and all the fires in Sopje and close byit reaches with its work to quench in time.

KUD "Podravac" u selu je dika,Slavonijom ponosno nastupa.Tamburaši i folklorna grupa,sve uspjehe oni čine skupa.Bruji žica i vrisne Šokica,pa odjekne Sopjanska ravnica.Ej, Sopjani, bili puni cvijeća,u svom radu pratila vas sreća.

KUD "Podravac" is its village's pride,performs with pride all through Slavonia wide;its tambura players and its dancing troupewin all their triumphs, working side by side.A string will hum, a Šokica will shout,and then the Sopje plain rings all about.Hey, Sopje folk, may you be full of flowers,and may good luck attend your working hours!

Valpovo, 23. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 23, 2013

Translator's note. A tour of his native village. St Florian, patron of firefighters, is honoured on its municipal day; Vidovo (St Vitus) and St Mary Magdalene are its two church fairs (kirvaj); Dravsko proljeće ("Drava Spring") is a folklore festival and a KUD a folk-arts society. Patoš is kept as written; its local meaning is unclear.
