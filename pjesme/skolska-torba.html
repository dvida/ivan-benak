Poems for children

The School Bag

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nije teška, vjerujte,svi je đaci nose,a znanja je prepunakao cvijeće rose.

It's not heavy, honestly,every pupil bears it, too,and it's brimming full of knowledgeas the flowers are with dew.

Školska torba zna baš sve:pjesmice i štiva,sve račune, zemljovid,i sve što se zbiva.

The school bag knows everything:little poems, reading texts,every sum and every map,all that happens, all the facts.

Ne treba se čuditi,ona mora znati,da bi mogla đacimato bogatstvo dati.

There's no need to feel surprised,for it has to know it all,so that it can give this treasureto the pupils, great and small.

S odličnim bi uspjehomprošla škola svaka,kad bi torba odgovordala mjesto đaka.

Every school would pass with honours,every single one,if the school bag gave the answersand the pupils none.

Translator's note. Performed by the children of the Maza kindergarten at the launch of Žablji sastanak on 28 October 2005. A 2009 credit for a musical setting was later withdrawn by the poet: the music was never written.
