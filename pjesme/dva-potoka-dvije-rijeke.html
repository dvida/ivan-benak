Domoljubne i zavičajne

Dva potoka, dvije rijeke

iz knjige Zekina sreća (1990.)

Homeland and native region

Two Streams, Two Rivers

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Pod Papukom usred šume,ima stari grad Ružica.Tu izvire mali potok,nazvali su ga Vučica.

Under Papuk, deep in woodland,stands the castle of Ružica;there a little stream springs up,and they named it the Vučica.

Kod Zvečeva drugi potok,raste, što je nama dika,pa kroz Voćin on prošeta,tu ga zovu i Voćinka.

Near Zvečevo, a second streamletgrows, and it's our pride and fame;then through Voćin it goes strolling,there Voćinka is its name.

Brzim tokom niz planinu,jedne jako hladne zime,u žurbi je mali potokizgubio svoje ime.

Rushing swiftly down the mountain,one hard winter, bitter cold,in its haste the little streamletlost the name it had of old.

U dolini smirio se.Da l' zbog hira ili vicastanovnici toga krajanazvaše ga Karašica.

In the valley it grew calm;whether as a whim or joke,Karašica it was namedby the local country folk.

Na svom putu u prirodi,svaka voda teče vodi,pa se tako sve po malo,iz potoka rijeka rodi.

Out in nature, on it flows;water on to water goes;and so, gently, bit by bit,from a stream a river grows.

I sad dvije rijeke teku,ravnim poljem krivudaju.Ukrasne su to tkanice,što nam krase Slavoniju.

And two rivers now are flowing,winding over level ground;they are festive, woven sashesthat adorn Slavonia round.

Stopiše se dvije rijekeu ljepoti žitnih snova,Karašica i Vučica,u dolini kraj Valpova.

The two rivers flow together,lovely as the dreams of wheat,Karašica and Vučica,in the vale at Valpovo's feet.

Dalje teče Karašica,da bi sebe Dravi dala,a Drava se kraj Aljmaša,sa Dunavom povjenčala.

Further flows the Karašica,yielding to the Drava's tide,and the Drava, by Aljmaš,is the Danube's wedded bride.

Valpovo, 1990.

Valpovo, 1990

Translator's note. A little geography lesson from his children's book: the Vučica and the Karašica rise in the Papuk hills and meet near Valpovo; the Karašica flows into the Drava, which meets the Danube at Aljmaš. The tkanica, the "sash" of stanza six, is the woven belt of Slavonian folk costume.
