The Beer Lover

Uvijek slušam Radio Valpovo,pa sve znadem što imade novo.

I always tune to Radio Valpovo,so all the latest news I get to know.

Jučer slušam reklamu uživo,da se nudi iz Osijeka pivo.

Just yesterday I heard a live-aired ad:there's beer from Osijek now to be had.

Pivopija ja sam uvijek bioi Osječko pivo u slast pio.

I've always been a beer man, through and through,and relished every glass of Osječko too.

Da dokažem, meni problem nije,imam flaša i sanduk od prije.

It's no great trouble proving that, what's more:I've bottles, and a crate from long before.

Za dobroga pivopiju važim,Šeperovo pivo uvijek tražim.

I'm counted as a beer man, good and true;I always ask for Šeperovo brew.

Vjerujte mi istina je živa,rado pijem ja Eseker piva.

Believe me, friends, the living truth is here:I gladly drink my share of Eseker beer.

Zato sanjam skoro svaku veče,kako pivo Karašicom teče.

And so I have, near every night, a dreamthat beer flows down the Karašica stream.

Mnogo sreće u Osijeku gradu,pivarcima u njihovom radu.

Good luck to Osijek, to that city fair,and to the brewers in their labour there.

Da nikada za štrajk ne bi znali,ni u krizu financijsku pali.

May strikes be something they will never know,and may no money crisis lay them low.

Da im kasa bude uvijek puna,za blokadu ne znali računa.

May all their tills be always full, I pray,and frozen bank accounts stay far away.

Valpovački stanovnici mili,dobro pivo iz Osijeka pili.

Dear people of Valpovo, drink your fillof good beer out of Osijek, as you will.

Nek' vam bude veselo i živo:pozdravlja vas iz Valpova Ivo!

May you be merry, lively, full of cheer:from Valpovo, Ivo sends his greetings here!

Valpovo, 14.12.2002.

Valpovo, December 14, 2002

Translator's note. Written for Radio Valpovština (he calls it Radio Valpovo) after an advert for Osijek beer. Osječko, Šeperovo and Eseker are beers of the Osijek brewery; the Karašica is the river that runs through Valpovo.
