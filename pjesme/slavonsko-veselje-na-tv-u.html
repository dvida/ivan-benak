Slavonsko veselje na TV-u

Occasional verse and dedications

Slavonian Merriment on TV

Poštovani, gospodine Gorane,voditelju SLAVONSKOG VESELJAi najljepše TV emisije.Slušati Vas to je moja želja.

Respected Mister Goran, this to you,host of SLAVONIAN MERRIMENT,and of TV's most lovely programme too:to listen to you is my heart's intent.

Jedva čekam petak i nedjelju,kada počnu emisije Vaše,pa da svoju ja ostvarim želju:slušajući divne tamburaše.

I scarce can wait for Friday and for Sunday,when your programmes start to come on air,so that my wish, at last, may be fulfilled:to hear the lovely tamburaši there.

A subotom slušam Ceciliju,sa opojnim glasom kad se javi.Tamburaši ispunjaju želje,razno slavlje tog se dana slavi.

On Saturdays I listen to Cecilija,when she comes on with her enchanting voice;the tamburaši play the wishes sent,and all who've feasts to celebrate rejoice.

Još da mi je samo malo sreće,da se Vašem priključim veselju,da dobijem CD tamburaša,da ostvarim svoju davnu želju.

If only I could have a little luck,to join your merriment and share its cheer,to get a CD of the tamburaši,and grant the wish I've held for many a year.

Pa nek' bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas pjesnik Benak Ivo!

So let it all be merry, lively, so:with greetings from the poet, Benak Ivo!

Valpovo, 03. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 3, 2012

Translator's note. Slavonsko veselje ("Slavonian Merriment") was a folk-music programme on television; Goran and Cecilija were its presenters.
