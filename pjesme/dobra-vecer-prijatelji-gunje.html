Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Dobra večer prijatelji Gunje!

Occasional verse and dedications

Good Evening, Friends of Gunja!

Mi smo došli iz daleka mjesta,Bistrinaca, kraj Belišće grada.Čuli jesmo da je kod vas fešta,pa smo zato došli k vama sada.

We've come to you from somewhere far away:Bistrinci, just outside Belišće town.We heard there was a party here today,and that is why we've come to you just now.

Došlo nas je četvero sve skupa.Vjerujemo da vaš poziv vrijedi.Da bi našu dužnost obavili,tri pričaju, četvrti sjedi.

There are four of us who've come along, all told;we trust your invitation's good and fair.To carry out our duty as we ought,three do the talking, and the fourth sits there.

Da vam kažem i naša imena,Budić Frano i Anušić Stevo,gospa Reza i još Benak Ivo.Nek' nam bude veselo i živo!

Now let me tell you all our names as well:Budić Frano and Anušić Stevo,and Madam Reza, and then Benak Ivo.And let it all be merry, here we go!

Eto, sve se tako lijepo slaže,neka nešto i predsjednik kaže!

There now, it all fits nicely, as you see:the president should speak as well as me!

Valpovo, 14. 07. 2009.

Valpovo, July 14, 2009

Translator's note. A greeting for a poetry evening in Gunja. The four guests are named surname first, as is usual in Croatian.
