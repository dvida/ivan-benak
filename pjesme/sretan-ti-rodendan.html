Happy Birthday to You

Sve najbolje ja ti želim,za rođendan, voljena, tvoj.Primi ovaj buket ruža,dragi, nježni taj poklon moj.

All the best I wish for you,my beloved, on your day.Please accept these roses, love —tender gift, my dear bouquet.

Nek ti dosta bude svega,a najviše divne sreće.Kroz tvoj život, mila moja,cvjetalo ti krasno cvijeće.

May you have enough of all,and, most of all, happy hours;all your life, my darling, maythere be blooming lovely flowers.

U životu sretna bilabez imalo tužne sjene.A najljepše kad ti bude,ti se onda sjeti mene.

May your life be happy, freeof the slightest shade of sorrow;when your days are at their fairest,then, my dear, remember me.

Znaj na tome svijetu imanetko tko te silno voli.Bez ljubavi tvoje, draga,u grudima nosi boli.

Know that somewhere in this worldthere is someone loves you so,who, without your love, my darling,in his breast bears pain and woe.

Valpovo, 02.03.1974.

Valpovo, March 2, 1974
