The Swallow

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Lasto, ptico milaproljeća vjesniče!Jesi li umornaod dugoga leta?

Swallow, bird so dear,herald of the spring!Are you tired nowfrom your lengthy flight?

Volim tebe, lasto!Ti mi nosiš srećei toplo proljeće.Puno divno cvijeta.

Swallow, I love you!You bring me good cheerand warm springtime here,flowers, many and bright.

Vraćaš mi se, lasto,sa svojim cvrkutom.Tvoje toplo gnijezdogledajuć' uživam.

Back to me you come, swallow,with your chirping sound;watching your warm nest,I feel joy anew.

Miris cvijeta širišrascvjetanim vrtom.U tvoj stari domja te rado primam.

Scent of flowers you spreadthrough the garden blooming round;into your old homeI gladly welcome you.

Kada dođe jesen,srce me zazebe.S odlaskom svojimstvaraš meni sjetu.

When the autumn comes,my heart feels a chill;as you fly away,you leave me sad and blue.

Išao bih s tobomu tople krajeve,ali krila nemamda letim po svijetu.

I would go with youto the lands where it's warm,but I have no wingsto fly the whole world through.
