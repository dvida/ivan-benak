Radio burza

Rasprodaja

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Clearance Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Evo mene, ja se javljam,u studiju sve pozdravljam.Oglase sam prije dao,preko burze sve prodao.Nema ljepše emisije,na radiju Valpovštine.Sve konkretno tu se vodi,snađu se u svakoj zgodi.Prodaja je tu posrijedi,daj šta daš, tu zakon vrijedi.Rasprodaju vršim sada,za sto kuna od komada.Uskoro će doći zima,dobra peć kod mene ima,za nju dajem garancije,na lož ulje ona grije.Da ne pričam sada dulje,tri bureta za lož ulje,ostalo mi da prodajem,za sto kuna sva tri dajem.Hajde, drage mušterije,javite se, tko će prije!Moj brzoglas bit će vrijedan.Nazovite na: šest, pet, dva,osamsto dvadeset jedan.

Here I am, I'm calling through,greetings to the studio crew!Once before I placed my ad,sold through the Burza all I had.No broadcast's lovelier to hearon Valpovština's radio here.Everything's done straight and true;they know what in each case to do.Selling is the thing, you see:"Pay what you like" — that's the decree.Clearance sale I'm holding now:a hundred kunas each — and how!Winter's coming very soon;I've a good stove — what a boon!I can guarantee it, too —it runs on heating oil, it's true.Not to spin this out too long:three oil barrels, good and strong,I have left to sell, and see,for a hundred kunas, all three!Come on, customers, my dears,call — who's first? — I'm all ears!My trusty phone will get things done.Call on: six, five, two,eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 09.10. 2000.

Valpovo, October 9, 2000

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza, the swap-and-sell show on the radio of Valpovština, the Valpovo district. For the telephone he uses brzoglas ("swift-voice"), an old purist Croatian word, in jest.
