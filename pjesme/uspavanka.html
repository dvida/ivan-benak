Pjesme za djecu

Uspavanka

Poems for children

Lullaby

Jednom sam biojako daleko,tamo gdje tečemed i mlijeko.

Once I went travellingfar, far away,where milk and honeyflow every day.

Plivao sam, djeco,preko devet gorai pješice prešaosva tri Crna mora.

Dear children, I swamnine mountains through,and walked on foot acrossthe three Black Seas too.

U toj čudnoj zemljisve je za badava,uz hranu i pićeposluga je prava.

In that strange landit all comes for free:with food and with drinkthere's real service, you see.

Mlijeka dobiješkod starog kokosa,od pekmeza budešumazan do nosa.

Milk you can getwhere the old coconut grows,and jam will smear youright up to your nose.

Naranče bereš,smokve i grožđe,banana samado tebe dođe.

You pick oranges,figs, and grapes too,and a banana comesby itself to you.

Zrele se kruškesame tebi nude,a kajsije sočneapetit bude.

Ripe pears come offeringthemselves to you,and juicy apricotswhet your appetite too.

Lubenice slatkei dinje žute,ako ih nećeš,one se ljute.

Sweet watermelonsand yellow melons too:if you don't want them,they're cross with you.

Na grani viseslatke čokolade,datulje medneusta ti slade.

From the branches hangchocolates so sweet,and honeyed datesgive your mouth a treat.

Tamo na travijagoda sjedi,ananas viče:jedi me, jedi!

There on the grasssits a strawberry, see,and a pineapple shouts:"Eat me, eat me!"

Umjesto lišćarastu bomboni,a zrakom letedječji baloni.

In place of leavesit's candies that grow,and children's balloonsthrough the air go.

Igračke raznečekaju tebe,plišane medei razne bebe.

Toys of all kindsare waiting for you,plush teddy bears,and baby dolls too.

Čeka te čarobnaljuljačka snova,u san te uljuljauspavanka ova.

A magical swingof dreams waits for you,and this lullaby rocks youto sleep, as lullabies do.

Translator's note. A nonsense land of plenty; swimming over mountains and walking across seas are the poet's deliberate reversals.
