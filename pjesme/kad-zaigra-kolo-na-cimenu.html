Domoljubne i zavičajne

Kad zaigra kolo na Čimenu

Homeland and native region

When the Kolo Starts on the Čimen

Kunišinci i selo Mar'janci,poštuju se ovdje Šokci naši,kod zvonika svetoga Antuna,skupljaju se mladi folkloraši.

Kunišinci, and Mar'janci village, too —our Šokci here are honoured and held dear;beside the belfry of Saint Anthonythe young folk dancers gather every year.

Kad povorka Kunišince prođe,tu se čuje pjesma cura, snaša,pa na Čimen kada svirkom dođe,to je smotra naših folkloraša.

When the procession through Kunišinci goes,the songs of girls and young wives fill the air;and when with music to the Čimen it flows,our dancers' festival is gathered there.

Općina je tu odavno bila,svete Gospe praznik odabrala.Sportske igre, pa i fišijada,svu ljepotu narodu je dala.

The municipality has long been here,and for its feast it chose Our Lady's day;the games of sport, the fish-stew contest too,have brought the people loveliness that day.

Mar'jančani ljudi ponositi,u svom seli KUD su osnovali:"SLAVONKA VILA" ga nazvali,na Čimenu kolo odigrali.

The people of Mar'janci, proud and fine,set up a village folk club, their own KUD,and named it "Slavonka Vila", as they should,and on the Čimen danced the kolo through.

Sve dok bude bilo Slavonije,skupljat će se Šokadija naša,igrat će se na Čimenu kolo,uz tambure dobrih tamburaša.

As long as there is still Slavonia,our Šokadija will gather, come what may;upon the Čimen they will dance the koloto tamburas the finest players play.

Na Čimenu kad zaigra kolo,tankovito kad vrisne šokicai kad cikne na tamburi žica,tada ječi slavonska ravnica.

When on the Čimen the kolo starts to spin,and high and thin a Šokica cries out,and when the tambura's string rings sharp and clear,then all Slavonia's plain resounds about.

Valpovo, 07. 03. 2012.

Valpovo, March 7, 2012

Translator's note. Set to music by Goran Ivanović – Lac and adopted as the anthem of Marijanci, where the folklore festival is danced on the Čimen, the village green. A KUD is a village folklore society; the fish-stew cook-off (fišijada) is a favourite Slavonian contest.
