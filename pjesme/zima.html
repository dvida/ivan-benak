Pjesme za djecu

Zima

Poems for children

Winter

Došla zima.Snijeg je pao,pa krovove zatrpao.Iz dimnjaka dim se vije,kraj pećice hladno nije.Sva se djecazdrava, čila,kraj ognjišta sakupilai miluju macu slatku,koja prede sve po taktu.Igraju se,skaču, vrište,jer ne mogu na dvorište!

Winter came.Down fell snow,burying the roofs below.From the chimney smoke curls high;by the stove no cold comes nigh.All the children,hale and bright,round the hearth have gathered tight,and are stroking kitty sweet,who purrs on and keeps the beat.There they play,jump, and shout,for they can't go running out!
