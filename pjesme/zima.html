Poems for children

Winter

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Došla zima.Snijeg je pao,pa krovove zatrpao.Iz dimnjaka dim se vije,kraj pećice hladno nije.Sva se djecazdrava, čila,kraj ognjišta sakupilai miluju macu slatku,koja prede sve po taktu.Igraju se,skaču, vrište,jer ne mogu na dvorište!

Winter came.Down fell snow,burying the roofs below.From the chimney smoke curls high;by the stove it's warm and dry.All the children,hale and bright,round the hearth have gathered tight,stroking Kitty, soft and sweet,who purrs on and keeps the beat.There they play,jump, and shout,for they can't go running out!
