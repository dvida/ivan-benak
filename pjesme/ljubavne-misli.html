Thoughts of Love

DUGO TE NISAMVIDIO, MILA.Želim te!

I HAVE NOT SEEN YOUFOR SO LONG, MY DARLING.I want you!

BEZ TEBE SPOROPROLAZE DANI.Trebam te!

WITHOUT YOU, THE DAYSGO BY SO SLOWLY.I need you!

BEZ TVOGA MILOVANJA,U DUGIM NOĆIMA.Sanjam te!

WITHOUT YOUR CARESSES,IN THE LONG NIGHTS.I dream of you!

I NA KRAJUŠTO DA TI KAŽEM?Volim te!

AND IN THE END,WHAT CAN I TELL YOU?I love you!
