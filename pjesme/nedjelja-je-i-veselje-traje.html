Nedjelja je i veselje traje

Occasional verse and dedications

It's Sunday, and the Merriment Goes On

Zabavno je SLAVONSKO VESELJE.Gledatelje kad Goran pozdravi,a slušatelj javi svoje želje,zatim Jeršek svoj šešir popravi.

SLAVONSKO VESELJE's a merry show:when Goran greets the viewers watching there,and listeners phone to let their wishes know,then Jeršek sets his hat right, with some care.

A subotom slušam Ceciliju,sa opojnim glasom kad se javi.Tamburaši ispunjaju želje,razno slavlje subotom se slavi.

On Saturdays I listen to Cecilijawhenever she comes on with her heady voice;the tamburica players grant requests,and Saturdays bring feasts where folk rejoice.

Cecilija kad pjeva k'o prije,o macanu što škaklja tabane.Njezin pogled ugodno me grije,a osmijehom uljepšava dane.

When Cecilija sings, as once she did,about the tomcat tickling someone's feet,her glance so pleasantly warms me right through,and with her smile she makes the days more sweet.

Džentlmeni kad pjevaju znano:ne budi me, majko, tako rano.To mi vrati onu ljubav staru,tad zaigra srce u BEĆARU!

When the Džentlmeni sing that well-known song,"Don't wake me, mother, quite so early", thenmy old love comes back to me, and before longthe heart inside the BEĆAR dances again!

Šokadijo mili zavičaju,najljepša si u zemaljskom raju.Svako selo ima tamburaša,lijepih cura i čarobnih snaša!

Šokadija, dear homeland of mine,in earthly paradise the fairest one!Each village has its tambura players fine,fair girls and charming young wives, every one!

Na TV-u divno žice ječe,tamburaši sviraju po želji.Gledatelja to je divna veče',na T V-u SLAVONSKO VESELJE!

On TV the strings ring out so fine,the tambura players play on request;for viewers it's an evening, so divine:on TV, SLAVONSKO VESELJE!

Valpovo, 08. 01. 2013.

Valpovo, January 8, 2013

Translator's note. Slavonsko veselje (Slavonian Merriment) was a tamburica request show on TV and radio; Goran, Jeršek and Cecilija were its presenters. The Džentlmeni are a tamburica band, and the song recalls his own "Don't Wake Me Early, Mother".
