Occasional verse and dedications

Perunike

Ivankovo divno mjestoukrasilo Slavoniju.Dični ljudi toga krajaljube svoju Šokadiju.

Ivankovo, lovely place,graced Slavonia far and near;worthy people of that regionhold their own Šokadija dear.

Kada pjesma dušu dira,da bi radost srcu dala.Marija je Cavor-Mira"PERUNIKE" osnovala.

So that songs in every erastir the soul and warm the heart,Marija Cavor-Miragave the PERUNIKE their start.

Drage moje "PERUNIKE",vi ste pjesme ispjevale.Svome mjestu Ivankovu,svu ljepotu Vi ste dale.

Oh, my dear PERUNIKE,you have sung your songs, and soto your own town, Ivankovo,every beauty you bestow.

Divna sloga među Vama,što pjevaju deset dama,PJEVAČKIM je ZBOROM zvanaod Marije izabrana.

What sweet concord in your throng,where ten ladies sing their song!SINGING CHOIR the name you bear,chosen by Marija's care.

Vi stvarate svojim radom,čovječanstvu divnu slikui zato ste Ivankovu,na posebnu čast i diku.

Through your work you are creatingfor mankind a lovely sight;that is why, for Ivankovo,you're an honour and delight.

Valpovo, 08. 10. 2012.

Valpovo, October 8, 2012

Translator's note. Written for the women's choir Perunike ("Irises") of the pensioners' association in Ivankovo, founded by Marija Cavor-Mira.
