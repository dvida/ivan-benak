Poems for children

A Castle Graces Our Valpovo

Valpovo je lijepi gradićpark i dvorac ima,tu ljepotu valpovačkunaš grad nudi svima.

Valpovo's a pretty townwith a park and castle too;all this beauty of Valpovoour town offers all of you.

Pa krenimo od vrtića,u dvorac polako,pred nama je vel'ka zgrada,a lijepa je jako.

Let's set off from kindergartento the castle, nice and slow;there before us stands a great house,very beautiful, you know.

Taj dvorac su sagradili,još odavno ljudi,a sada nam svu ljepotu,ovaj dvorac nudi.

People built this castle herevery long ago,and now all its lovelinessthis castle has to show.

Kad prođemo kroz kapiju,dvorište postoji,a pred nama stoji kula,mnogo ljeta broji.

When we pass in through the gatewaythere's a courtyard, as you'll find;there before us stands a tower,many summers left behind.

Tu je divna kapelica,utjehu nam nudi,a priredbu često puta,priređuju ljudi.

Here's a lovely little chapel,comfort it bestows,and quite often people hereput on public shows.

Pa krenimo sad u dvoracu muzej polako,što sve ima u muzeju,saznat ćemo lako.

Let us go into the castle,to the museum, nice and slow;all the things in the museumwe will learn with ease, you know.

Namještaja lijepog imaovdje je u vidu,pa i slika izrađenih,tu vise na zidu.

Lovely furniture is here,plain for everyone to view,and the paintings people madehang upon the walls here too.

Pogledajte tog jelenašto ponosno stojii zastava ova naša,sto godina broji.

Look at that stag standing there,see how proud it seems to be,and this flag of ours has counteda full hundred years, you see.

Vatrogasnog našeg društva,postave su prave,tu je i kip Florijanakojeg oni slave.

From our local fire brigadethere's a fine display right here,and there's Florian's statue too,the saint whom they revere.

Te sve stvari tu su bileod grofa Normana,on je nekad ovdje bio,prije mnogo dana.

All these things you see were onceCount Norman's very own;he was living here, long since,many days now gone.

Sve je ovo posložila:kustosica znana,a to vam je, draga djecogospođa Mirjana.

All of this was put in orderby a curator of fame,and that, my dear children, isMrs Mirjana by name.

Još bih nešto spomenuo,nek je znano svima,glazbena je ovo školai dječji zbor ima.

One more thing I'd like to mentionso that everyone's aware:this is also a music school,and a children's choir is there.

Eto, to je za sad, djeco,toliko da znate.Da sav dvorac pogledate,cijelu noć imate.

There, that's all for now, dear children,that's as much as you need know;you have all the night before youto see the castle high and low.

Valpovo, 22.01.2014.

Valpovo, January 22, 2014

Translator's note. A guided tour for kindergarten children of Valpovo's castle and museum. St Florian is the patron saint of firefighters.
