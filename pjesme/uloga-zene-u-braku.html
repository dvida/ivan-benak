Memories and reflections

A Wife's Role in Marriage

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kada muža glava zaboli,kada treba problem da riješi.Žena je tu da mu utjehu pružii da ga nježno tješi.

When the husband has a headache,when some problem he must address,the wife is there to give him comfortand soothe him with tenderness.

Kad muž zaspi i san se oduži,na posao kasni, avaj!On gunđa, viče, žena ga služi:cipele ženo, kaput mi daj!

When the husband's sleep runs long, and thenhe's late for work — oh no!He grumbles, shouts, she serves him again:"Wife, my shoes! My coat! Let's go!"

Žena treba muža da volii djecu nježno mazi.Za njene nitko ne pita boli:tko još na to da pazi!

The wife should love her husband trueand softly stroke each little one;but no one asks about her pain —who'd heed such things? Not anyone!

Svi sretni u kući treba da budu,to je uloga njena.I tisuću još problema drugih,treba da riješi žena.

All in the house must happy be —that is her role to play;a thousand other problems, too,the wife must clear away.

Sto poslova žena u kući ima,trči po cijeli dan.Šije, pere, kuha, sprema,dok je ne savlada san.

A hundred jobs she has at home,she's on the run all day;she sews, she washes, cooks and cleans,till sleep bears her away.

Kada na kraju u krevet dođe,da odmori umornu dušui tada treba nježna da bude,da pruži ljubav mužu.

When at the end she comes to bedto rest her weary soul,then, too, she must be tenderand give her husband love, her role.

Valpovo, 22. 02. 2009.

Valpovo, February 22, 2009
