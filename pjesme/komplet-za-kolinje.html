Radio burza

Komplet za kolinje

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Kolinje Kit

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ide zima i kolju se svinje,da l' imate alat za kolinje?Neka sada slušatelji znaju,takav komplet imam za prodaju.

Here winter comes, and pigs are slaughtered; say,have you got tools for kolinje today?So let the listeners know, now, far and nigh:I have just such a kit for you to buy.

Da opišem ovaj komplet prvo:Vješala su jasenovo drvo.Dvjesto dvadeset centi su visoka,metar i pol ona su široka,čvrsta, jaka ništa se ne klima,a klinova točno osam ima.Od bagrema fino tokarena,u prečku su dobro usađena.Kad se jednom tu gica zateče,ta u svinjac poželjeti neće.

First let me tell you what this kit's about:the gallows first: they're made of ash, throughout.They're two hundred and twenty centimetres talland a metre and a half wide, overall;they're firm and strong, not wobbly, not a bit,and it has eight pegs, exactly, fixed in it.Turned finely from acacia wood, they're made,and firmly in the crossbar they've been laid.When once a piggy finds herself up there,she won't be wishing for her sty, I swear.

Korito je od čvrstoga lima,metar i pol u dužinu ima,sedamdeset centi je široko,četrdeset i pet je duboko.Gica može u koritu lijepom,kupati se sa njuškom i repom.

The trough is made of solid metal sheet,a metre and a half long, all complete;across, it's seventy centimetres wide,and forty-five centimetres deep inside.The piggy in this lovely trough can takea bath with snout and tail, for goodness' sake!

Još postoji hakštok takozvani,stol za meso, svima dobro znani.Dvjesto trideset ima u dužinu,sedamdeset centi u širinu,a debljine on je od dva cola,prvoklasna čičkasta topola.

There's also what is called the hakštok here,a meat table, known to all, far and near.In length, two hundred thirty, that is true,in width it's seventy centimetres, too,in thickness, it's two inches, all the way —top-quality knotty poplar, I dare say.

Javno dajem ja ovo na znanje,uz to ide furuna za klanje.Posve nova, eto ljudi zgode,sa posudom za grijanje vode.Uz furunu i cijev dođe pride,da dim znade kud treba da ide.

I publicly announce to all, as well:a slaughter stove is part of what I sell.It's brand new — people, what a chance to take! —with a pot for heating water, make no mistake.And with the stove a pipe's thrown in, you know,so that the smoke knows where it ought to go.

Sve je novo, ničeg se ne stidi,kupac može sve dobro da vidi.Prvoklasna roba, to je fora,komplet dajem za šesto eura.U valuti kak' se dvoje slože,za eure i za kune može.

It's all brand new, there's nothing it need hide;the buyer can inspect it, every side.First-class goods, and that's the point, I'd say;six hundred euros takes the kit away.In any currency on which we two agree —in euros or in kuna, it can be.

Ovo dajem da pomognem gici,da se brzo nađe u pušnici.

I'm selling this to help the piggy, sointo the smokehouse quickly she will go.

Nazovite dok traje kolinje:na 6 5 2 - osam dva jedan,jer već čujem kako cvile svinje.

Call while the kolinje's on — here's my appeal:on 6 5 2 - eight two one,for I can hear the piggies already squeal.

Valpovo, 20. 11. 2000.

Valpovo, November 20, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. The gallows is the frame the slaughtered pig is hung from, and the trough is for scalding off the bristles. A hakštok (from German Hackstock) is the heavy chopping table used at a kolinje.
