Radio burza

Komplet za kolinje

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Kolinje Kit

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ide zima i kolju se svinje,da l' imate alat za kolinje?Neka sada slušatelji znaju,takav komplet imam za prodaju.

Winter is coming, pigs are killed again;have you the tools you need for kolinje, then?So let the listeners know it, far and near:I have a kit like that for sale right here.

Da opišem ovaj komplet prvo:Vješala su jasenovo drvo.Dvjesto dvadeset centi su visoka,metar i pol ona su široka,čvrsta, jaka ništa se ne klima,a klinova točno osam ima.Od bagrema fino tokarena,u prečku su dobro usađena.Kad se jednom tu gica zateče,ta u svinjac poželjeti neće.

First let me show you what the kit's about:the gallows are of ash wood, good and stout.Two hundred twenty centimetres tall,a metre and a half wide, overall;they're firm and strong, and wobble not a bit,exactly eight pegs have been fixed in it.Turned finely from acacia, they are made,and firmly in the crossbar they are laid.When once a piggy finds herself up there,she won't be longing for her sty, I swear.

Korito je od čvrstoga lima,metar i pol u dužinu ima,sedamdeset centi je široko,četrdeset i pet je duboko.Gica može u koritu lijepom,kupati se sa njuškom i repom.

The trough is made of solid metal sheet,a metre and a half in length, complete;across, it's seventy centimetres wide,and forty-five centimetres deep inside.The piggy in this lovely trough can takea bath with snout and tail, for goodness' sake!

Još postoji hakštok takozvani,stol za meso, svima dobro znani.Dvjesto trideset ima u dužinu,sedamdeset centi u širinu,a debljine on je od dva cola,prvoklasna čičkasta topola.

There's also what is called the hakštok here,the table for the meat, known far and near.Two hundred thirty centimetres long,and seventy in width, both firm and strong;it's two full inches thick, from base to top,of knotty poplar, the cream of the crop.

Javno dajem ja ovo na znanje,uz to ide furuna za klanje.Posve nova, eto ljudi zgode,sa posudom za grijanje vode.Uz furunu i cijev dođe pride,da dim znade kud treba da ide.

I publicly announce to all, as well:a slaughter stove is part of what I sell.Brand new! Now, people, there's a chance for you!It has a pot for heating water, too.A stovepipe's thrown in with the stove, and sothe smoke will know which way it ought to go.

Sve je novo, ničeg se ne stidi,kupac može sve dobro da vidi.Prvoklasna roba, to je fora,komplet dajem za šesto eura.U valuti kak' se dvoje slože,za eure i za kune može.

It's all brand new, there's nothing it need hide;the buyer can inspect it, every side.First-class goods, and here's the clever bit:six hundred euros buys the entire kit.Whatever currency we two agree,in euros or in kuna, it can be.

Ovo dajem da pomognem gici,da se brzo nađe u pušnici.

I'm selling this to help the piggy out,so she'll be in the smokehouse soon, no doubt.

Nazovite dok traje kolinje:na 6 5 2 - osam dva jedan,jer već čujem kako cvile svinje.

Call while the kolinje's on, here's my appeal,on 6 5 2 - eight two one,for I can hear the pigs already squeal.

Valpovo, 20. 11. 2000.

Valpovo, November 20, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. The gallows is the frame the slaughtered pig is hung from, and the trough is for scalding off the bristles. A hakštok (from German Hackstock) is the heavy chopping table used at a kolinje.
