Occasional verse and dedications

To Slavica

Ovaj život kao rijeka tečei odnosi dio naše sreće.Kap veselja od srca Ti želim,Tvojoj sreći i ja se veselim.

Just like a river, life flows on and on,and part of all our happiness is gone.I wish you, from my heart, a drop of cheer;I too rejoice in your good fortune here.

Kao nekad nismo više mladii starački nek' nas minu jadi,sretnih dana neka bude višei veselja da se lakše diše.

We are not young, the way we were before;may old age's woes pass by our door;may happy days be more, and more increase,and joy, so we may breathe with greater ease.

Nek Ti pruži sada knjiga ova,malo sreće, puno divnih snova,svakog dana kad zora zarudi,sretnu, vedru nek' te sunce budi!

May this, my book, now bring you something more:a little joy, and lovely dreams galore;and every day, as dawn grows red with light,may the sun wake you happy, cheerful, bright!

Valpovo, 18. 10. 2010.

Valpovo, October 18, 2010

Translator's note. Written in a copy of Stihom kroz život for his niece, Slavica Mijatović of Donji Bazije.
