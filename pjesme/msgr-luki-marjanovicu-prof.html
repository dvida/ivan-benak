Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Msgr. Luki Marjanoviću, prof.

Occasional verse and dedications

To Msgr. Luka Marjanović, Prof.

U Đakovu jednog gospodnjega ljeta,obišla se tada katedrala sveta.

In Đakovo, in a certain year of grace,we toured the holy cathedral of the place.

Pjesnici su bili gosti Đakovštine,slušali su tiho sveto predavanje.

The poets were the guests of the Đakovo lands,and quietly they listened to a sacred talk.

Ima puno stvari koje nisam znao,Monsinjoru Luki ja sam knjigu dao.

There were so many things I hadn't known;I gave Monsignor Luka a book of my own.

I prođoše zatim nekoliko dana,pa navrati poštar do mojega stana.

Then several days went past, and after thatthe postman came around to my own flat.

Donio mi pismo, divnog sadržaja,što mi pruži sreću zemaljskoga raja.

He brought a letter, wonderful to read,that gave me joy of earthly paradise indeed.

Ovo divno pismo, ideju mi spasi,u mom novom tisku, sad mi knjigu krasi.

This wondrous letter saved the thought I had;it graces my new book in print, I'm glad.

Msgr. Luki Marjanoviću, sada knjigu šaljem,od sveg srca ja mu novu knjigu dajem.

To Msgr. Luka Marjanović I send my book today,and give my new book to him with all my heart, I say.

JEDNOG DANA. U ĐAKOVUJA PODARIH KNJIGU SVOJUMsgr. LUKI MARJANOVIĆUAUTOR!

One day. In ĐakovoI gave my bookto Msgr. Luka Marjanović.The Author!

Valpovo, 18. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 18, 2010

Translator's note. Written into a copy of his book Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse). Đakovo, with its great cathedral, is the seat of the archdiocese of Đakovo-Osijek.
