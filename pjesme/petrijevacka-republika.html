Domoljubne i zavičajne

Petrijevačka republika

Homeland and native region

The Republic of Petrijevci

Na njedrima Panonske Mursele,Petrijevci se udobno smjestili.Karaševo nježno prigrlili,jer su oni tu oduvijek bili.

Upon the breast of Pannonian MurselaPetrijevci has comfortably settled down,and tenderly embraced Karaševo,for they have always been here, on this ground.

Na Sigetu nekad crkva bila,Nikoli je svetom posvećena.Prije mnogo ljeta sagrađena,pučanstvu je bila namijenjena.

On Siget, once, a church was standing there,to Saint Nicholas it was consecrated,built many summers back, and for the useof all the common folk was designated.

Tisuću sedamsto pedeset 4.,Petrijevčani crkvu sagradili,glavni oltar u crkvi su tada,Apostolu Petru posvetili.

In seventeen hundred fifty-four, it's true,the Petrijevci people built a church,and its high altar they devoted thento Peter, the Apostle, as was due.

Od devetsto devedeset treće,Petrijevačka Općina postoji.Tri tisuće sedamdeset ljudi,ona skupa sa Satnicom broji.

Since nineteen ninety-three, as we can see,there's been the Petrijevci Municipality;three thousand seventy people it has, all told,together with Satnica, in its fold.

Još odavno, u samom početku,Općina je roditeljski bila,pa je nježno u okrilje svojei Satnicu k sebi prigrlila.

From long ago, right at the very start,the Municipality played a parent's part,and tenderly took Satnica as wellunder its wing, and held it to its heart.

Petrijevčani sad ponosno slave,osamnaesto ljeto postojanja.Punoljetna Općina je sada,vidljivo je iz njenoga rada.

Now Petrijevci's people proudly fêtetheir eighteenth summer of existence here;the Municipality has come of age —and from its work that much is very clear.

Petrijevački centar sad izgleda,kao divni trg od Velegrada.Za ljepotu Republike naše,zaslužna je to Općinska Vlada.

The centre of Petrijevci looks todayjust like the handsome square of some great town;for all the beauty of our Republic here,the Municipal Government earns renown.

Petrijevce nova škola krasii dvorana športska suvremena.A Općini to je ponos sada,za potrebe što je izgrađena.

A new school graces Petrijevci now,and a sports hall that's modern, brand-new too;and the Municipality's proud of it,since it was built to serve our needs, it's true.

Umjetničku udrugu kulturei to blago Republika ima.Da "NIKOLA to je ŠUBIĆ ZRINSKI“,što u selu pruža radost svima.

A cultural and arts society —that treasure, too, is one the Republic owns:it's "Nikola Šubić Zrinski", as you know,that brings delight to all the village homes.

A "ŽETVENA SVEČANOST" kad dođe,kad se skupi Šokadija mlada.Kad na bini kolo zavijori,a pod šatrom svakojakog slada.

And when the "Harvest Festival" comes by,when the young Šokadija come to meet,when on the stage the kolo flutters high,and under tents there's every kind of treat.

Dobrovoljno vatrogasno društvosto dvadeset sedam ljeta broji.Žensku četu sebi pridodalii limenu glazbu osnovali.

The Volunteer Fire Brigade can counta hundred twenty-seven summers now;they've added a women's squad to their own crew,and founded a brass band of their own, too.

Zaštitnika svoga obnovili,svojim radom dom su izgradili,mnoge snažne vatre ugasilii sa ZRINSKIM veselja su dali.

They've had their patron saint restored like new,and with their labour built their firehouse too;they've put out many a fierce and mighty blaze,and with the Zrinski given joyful days.

"OMLADINAC" Republiku krasi,najbolji je u ovome kraju,pogotovo kad golove nižu,hrabri momci svoj posao znaju.

"Omladinac" adorns our Republic, too;it's the best in this whole region, I'd say,especially when they rack the goals up high —the brave lads know their job, and how to play.

Trgovina ovdje dosta imai robe je od njih svaka puna.Kupili bi ljudi sve i svašta,ali narod nema dosta kuna.

There are enough shops here, and every oneis full of goods — as full as full can be.The people would buy anything and all,but folk don't have the kunas, don't you see.

Bregovitu ulicu kad prođeš,i na zapad Republike dođeš.Desno stoji industrijska zona,a na lijevo?! Jadna, majko, moja!

When you have passed along Bregovita Street,and reached the Republic's western side, you'll meetthe industrial zone upon the right, you'll see,and on the left?! Oh, mother, woe is me!

Pa pređimo još i Karašicu,u „JELENGRAD“ na piće il' hranu,Sve što želiš „JELENGRAD“ ti nudi,bilo noću il' možda po danu.

Then let's cross over the Karašica, too,to "Jelengrad" for a drink or for a bite;whatever you want, "Jelengrad" offers you,whether it's in the daytime or at night.

Često idem u kafić "KOD LUKE".Kad "IN" prođem, u "PLITVICE" dođem.Još "HOLLYWOOD", zatim nema više.Pa na kavu odoh ja "KOD MIŠE".

I often drop into the café "Kod Luke";past "In", I come to "Plitvice" in turn;then "Hollywood" — and after that's the lot,so off for coffee to "Kod Miše" I trot.

Za sve ovo što sam napisao,nek' uspjeha bude k'o do sadai za procvat Republike naše:nek' se bira opet ista Vlada.

For all of this that I have written down,may there be as much success as there has been;and for our own Republic's flourishing,let the same Government be voted in!

Valpovo, 15. 04. 2011.

Valpovo, April 15, 2011

Translator's note. "The Republic" is the villagers' proud nickname for Petrijevci; the poem was written for the 18th anniversary of the municipality. Mursela was the Roman settlement nearby. The names in quotation marks are the village's societies, its football club, a restaurant and its cafés.
