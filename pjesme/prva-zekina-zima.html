Little Bunny's First Winter

Nešto čudno se događa,vjetar vije, sniježak prši,mali zeko šape gladi,a studen mu štipa uši.

Something strange is happening here:wind is blowing, snowflakes swirl,little bunny rubs his paws,and the frost nips at each ear.

Poželio zeko malimajku, da mu krevet grije,od snijega je polje bijelo,cijele noći spav'o nije.

Little bunny longed for Motherthere to warm his bed at night;all the field was white with snow,and he never slept till light.

Gledao je vjevericukako se u duplju svila,topao joj krevet bio,bujnim repom se pokrila.

He had watched the squirrel curlingin her hollow, snug and tight;warm her bed was, with a coverof her bushy tail, just right.

Majčinih se riječi sjeti:"Zimi ćeš u žbunje zaći,tamo će ti toplo biti,i lovac te neće naći."

Then his mother's words came back:"Go in winter to the thicket;there you'll stay warm through and through,and no hunter will find you."
