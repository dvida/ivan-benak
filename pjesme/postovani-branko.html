Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani Branko!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Branko!

Na njedrima SlavonijeIvankovo tu se sviloSložan narod toga mjestaO ljepoti sela snilo

On Slavonia's bosom, here,Ivankovo nestled down;and the close-knit people theredreamed of beauty for their town.

Ivankovo gradić maliU svom radu vi ste znaliSve što treba IvankovuSvojim radom vi ste dali

Ivankovo, little town:in your work you knew the way;all Ivankovo might needthrough your work you gave, I say.

Zbog ljepote vašeg radaTo će narod cijenit znatiZa dogradnju IvankovaPovjerenje će vam dati

For the beauty of your workpeople's praise will be your due;to build up Ivankovothey will place their trust in you.

Valpovo, 12. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 12, 2013

Translator's note. Branko Galić was the head of the municipality of Ivankovo.
