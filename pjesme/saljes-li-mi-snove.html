Do You Send Me Dreams

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U noći meni divne snove šalješi ljubav svoju koju rado primam.U snovima slatke poljupce mi daješ,u tihoj noći kada divno snivam.

At night you send me lovely dreams, and yousend me your love as well, which I am glad to keep;in dreams you give me your sweet kisses too,in the still night, while blissfully I sleep.

Od kuda bih znao da me željno ljubiškada ne bi meni svoje snove slala?U zagrljaj nježni da se meni nudiš,da bi meni svoje medne usne dala.

How would I know you love me yearningly,if you had never sent your dreams my way?That you would come to my embrace so tenderly,and offer me your honeyed lips, I say?

I dok srce moje tebe tiho zovena krilima mašte koju tebi šaljekada tebe ljubim, znam za slasti nove,o ljubavi našoj ja snivam i dalje.

And while my heart is softly calling youon wings of fancy, which to you it sends,when I kiss you, I know delights anew,and of our love I dream on without end.

A čim se probudim i otvorim oči,u zoru ranu kad se gase snovi,na tebe mislim da ćeš meni doći,i tako dani započinju novi.

And once I wake, and open up my eyes,at early dawn, as all my dreams go out,I think of you, and that you'll come to me,and that is how the new days come about.

Valpovo, 23.11.1974.

Valpovo, November 23, 1974
