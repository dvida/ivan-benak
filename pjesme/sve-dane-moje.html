All My Days

Sve dane moje radosti,tebi sam, draga, dao.Najljepšu snagu mladosti,no, ipak mi nije žao.

All the days of all my joyI have given, dear, to you,all the finest strength of youth —and yet I do not rue.

U svome hiru, draga,jednom barem shvati,više od svoga života,nisam ti mogao dati.

In your caprice, my darling,just once, understand me true:more than my own lifeI could not give to you.

Valpovo, 1975.

Valpovo, 1975
