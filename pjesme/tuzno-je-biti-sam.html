It's Sad to Be Alone

U smiraj noći,u praznoj sobi,tužan je svaki dan.Teško je moći,u takvoj noći,naći ljubavni san.

As the night draws in,in an empty room,sad is every day.Hard it is to win,on a night like this,dreams of love, I say.

Zato si nađi,životnog druga,nikad ne budi sam.Život je slađi,manja je tuga,vjeruj mi,ja to znam.

So go out and finda companion true,never be alone.Life's sweeter, you'll find,and less sorrow, too —trust me,this I know.

Valpovo, 10. 12. 1989.

Valpovo, December 10, 1989
