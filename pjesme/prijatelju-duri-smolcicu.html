Prijatelju Đuri Smolčiću

Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend Đuro Smolčić

Dragi prijatelju!HAJDE DA VOLIMO.DRUŠTVO VESELIH PTICA,cvrkute slušati,

My dear friend!COME, LET US LOVETHE COMPANY OF MERRY BIRDS,and listen to their chirping,

SLAVONIJOM, PJESMOM, KASOM,na vrancu jahati.Sa KAMELEONOM,slavan ćeš biti.

THROUGH SLAVONIA, WITH SONG, AT A TROT,ride a raven steed.And with CHAMELEONyou will be famous.

IN VINO VERITAS,veselja Ti dati.A po mjesečini,ZORU DOČEKATI.

IN VINO VERITAS,to bring you cheer.And in the moonlightTO GREET THE DAWN.

NA BRAZDI će istobiti kao lani.Eto, to su, Đuro,svi BOSUTSKI DANI.

ON THE FURROW, thingswill go last year's ways.There, Đuro, you have them:all the BOSUT DAYS.

Sve što si napisao,to se lijepo slaže.Sada hoće Ivo,da Ti nešto kaže.

All that you have writtenfits so well, it's true;and now Ivo wishesto say a word to you.

Piši, prijatelju,što Ti srce želi,pruži si veselja:to Ti Benak veli.

Write on, dear friend,all your heart holds dear;give yourself some joy —Benak says so here.

Iskoristi pero,sve dokle Ti piše,pa ćeš imat, Đuro,jednu pjesmu više.

Make good use of your penfor as long as it will go,and you'll have, Đuro,one more poem to show.

To sam napisao:Dvajst osmoga dana,jedne divne srijede,listopad je bio,dv'je tisuće pete.

This I wroteon the twenty-eighth day,one lovely Wednesday,in October it was,two thousand and five.

Translator's note. Đuro Smolčić was a fellow poet; the phrases in capitals are the titles of his books, woven into the verse.
