Love poems

Love Is Not Measured

Ne mogu ti reći koliko te volim,jer se ljubav, dušo, ne mjeri aršinom.Od ljubavi tvoje i tvog milovanja,kao da sam sasvim opijen sa vinom.

I cannot tell you, darling, just how much I love you,for love is not a thing a yardstick can define;from all your love for me and all of your caresses,it is as if I'm drunk, completely drunk, on wine.

Najljepše sam pjesme tebi poklonio,i najdraže snove ja sam s tobom snio.Sa usana tvojih sladak med sam pio.I najslađu ljubav s tobom provodio.

The loveliest songs I ever wrote, I gave to you;and with you dreamed the dearest dreams, as lovers do;sweet honey from your lips in long, deep draughts I drew,and spent with you the sweetest love I ever knew.

Nikad neću žalit' što sam te ljubio.Za tvoju sam ljubav tebi svoju dao.Da se opet rodim, opet bih tvoj bioi na grudi tvoje sav sretan bih pao.

I'll never feel regret that I have loved you so;for all your love I gave you mine, I gave it all;and were I born again, I'd still be yours, I know,upon your breast, in perfect happiness, I'd fall.

Valpovo, 23. 12. 1975.

Valpovo, December 23, 1975

Translator's note. The aršin was an old measure of length, used for cloth; the yardstick is the nearest English equivalent.
