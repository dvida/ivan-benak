Vrati mi snove!

Love poems

Give Me Back My Dreams!

Vrati mi se danas,da ne plačem sutra.Bez ljubavi tvojetužna su mi jutra.

Come back to me today,so I won't weep tomorrow.Without your love, my morningsare mornings full of sorrow.

A noći su hladne,oh, kako su duge!Jer na sebi tvoje,ne osjećam ruke.

And the nights are cold,oh, the nights are long!For I cannot feelyour arms where they belong.

Ni tvog milovanja,ni poljupce slatke.Kao u noćima,dok su bile kratke.

Nor your fond caresses,nor your kisses sweet,as it was on nightswhen the nights were fleet.

Ni pogleda nježnogšto mi snove budi.Ujutro, u zoru,kad sunce zarudi.

Nor your tender glancethat would wake my dreamsin the early dawn,when the red sun gleams.

Sad prekini patnjui vrati mi snove.Da živimo sretnonaše dane nove.

Now end my suffering,give back my dreams to me,so we'll spend our new daysas happy as can be.

Valpovo, 17. 02. 1975.

Valpovo, February 17, 1975
