Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Belišćanski dom „Svete Lucije“

Occasional verse and dedications

The St Lucy Home in Belišće

LUCIJA je svjetlonoša bila,mnoge bolne vidom obdarila,svojim darom pomoći je znala,mnogima je danju svjetlost dala.

Saint Lucy was a bearer of the light,and many in their pain she gave their sight;she knew to help, with gifts that were her own,and gave the light of day to many a one.

Ovaj DOM se sad časno ponosi,što LUCIJE SVETE ime nosi.Oni što su DOMU ime dali,LUCIJINE vrline su znali.

This Home may now with honour proudly claimto carry as its own Saint Lucy's name.And those who gave the Home its name had knownthe virtues that were Lucy's very own.

Svima vama što ste u tom DOMU,usamljeni u životu svomu,nek' vam teku u ljepoti dani,od LUCIJE SVETE obasjani.

To all of you who live within this Home,lonely in life, and living it alone,may all your days in beauty flow and run,lit up by Holy Lucy, every one.

Samoća vas mučila u tami,noć je tiha, vi kod kuće sami,tu u DOMU blažene su noći,jer vi znate, LUCA će pomoći.

In darkness, loneliness tormented you;the night was still, at home alone, just you;here in the Home the nights are blessed, it's true,for, as you know, Luca will help you through.

I starački dovukli se jadi,kao nekad niste više mladi,često puta bolno srce kuca,tko će pomoć, ako neće LUCA.

And now the woes of age have crept your way;you are not young, as you were in your day;your heart so often beats in pain and grief:who'll help, if Luca won't, and bring relief?

Nakon snova kad otvoriš oči,sve što trebaš LUCA će pomoći.Dobar ručak i društvo te čeka,prije snova posteljica meka.

When, after dreams, you open up your eyes,whatever you may need, Luca supplies.A good lunch and good company await,and, before dreams, a soft bed, neatly made.

Pa zar može nešto bolje biti,u noćima možeš snove sniti.Mladost vaša u snove se vrati,to vam može samo LUCA dati.

So could there be a better thing to find?At night you dream the dreams that come to mind.Your youth returns in dreams to visit you,and that Luca alone can give to you.

Valpovo, 15. 12. 2011.

Valpovo, December 15, 2011

Translator's note. Written for the Sveta Lucija (St Lucy) home for the elderly in Belišće. St Lucy is the patron saint of eyesight; Luca is the homely short form of Lucija.
