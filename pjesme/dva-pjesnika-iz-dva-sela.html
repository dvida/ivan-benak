Occasional verse and dedications

Two Poets from Two Villages

Divno selo kraj Bosuta,na istoku Slavonije:Rokovci – Andrijaševci,ljepših sela nigdje nije.

Lovely village by the Bosut,in Slavonia's eastern lands:Rokovci – Andrijaševci,finer village nowhere stands.

Na Bosutu most ih veže,složan narod ovog kraja,ovdje ljudi od davninauživaju divnog raja.

On the Bosut a bridge links them,folk in concord, in this land;here, since olden times, the peoplehave had paradise at hand.

U tom selu Šokačkome,imam dragog prijatelja.Piše pjesme, tiska knjige,on je pjesnik kao i ja.

In that Šokac village therelives a friend who's dear to me;he writes poems, prints his books:poet, just as I, is he.

A zove se Smolčić Đuro,znamo se iz davnih dana.Što pišemo pjesme, knjige,to je našoj duši hrana.

And his name is Smolčić Đuro;we go back a long, long way.Writing poems, writing books,is our souls' food every day.

Trinaest knjiga mi imamo,kad napišem iz njih slovai poredam naslovnice,kitica se stvori ova:

Thirteen books we have between us;when I write them, row by row,and I line the titles up,this is how the verse will go:

HAJDE DA VOLIMO,MALE SNOVE,NA BRAZDI,sa VALPOVAČKIM BEĆARCEM,i ZEKINOM SREĆOM,SLAVONIJOM PJESMOM KASOM,ZORU DOČEKATI,nek' ODJEKNE iz SRCA,ŽABLJI SASTANAK,uz DRUŠTVO VESELIH PTICA,in VINO VERITAS,STIHOM KROZ ŽIVOT,na kraju: KAMELEON!

Come, Let's Love,Little Dreams,On the Furrow,with The Valpovo Bećaracand with Bunny's Luck,Across Slavonia at a Song's Trot,To Greet the Dawn,let it Echo from the Heart,The Frogs' Meeting,with The Company of Merry Birds,In Vino Veritas,Through Life in Verse,and at the end: Chameleon!

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 20, 2010

Translator's note. Đuro Smolčić is a fellow poet from Rokovci-Andrijaševci, twin villages on the Bosut river. The last stanza strings together the titles of the two poets' thirteen books.
