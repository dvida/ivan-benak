Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani Tomislave!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Tomislav!

Sinoć svratih na pjesničko sijelo,gdje pjesnici predu svoje snove.Tu sam sreo novog prijatelja:Tomislav se on Šutalo zove.

Last night I dropped in at a poets' sijelo,where poets gather, spinning out their dreams;and there I met a friend, a new one: heis called Tomislav Šutalo, you see.

Toga trena dogovor je bio,međusobno da se knjiga daje.Neka bude draga uspomena,da ugodno prijateljstvo traje.

Right then and there, we came to an accordto give each other books, as friends would do.So let it be a dear remembrance, then,so that our pleasant friendship may stay true.

Kao pjesnik divne pjesme pišei knjigu je svoju odštampao,zato ovu ja posvetu pišem,kako bih mu svoju knjigu dao.

He writes such lovely poems as a poet,and he has had a book of his own made;and that is why I write this dedication,to give my book to him, as we had said.

Nedjelja je, desetoga travnja,dv'je tisuće i trinaesta zima.Prijatelju ja posvetu pišem,jer mi srce ovu želju ima.

It's Sunday, and the tenth of April, too,two thousand thirteen winters now gone by.I write this dedication for a friend,because my heart so wished it — that is why.

Valpovo, 10. 03. 2013.

Valpovo, March 10, 2013

Translator's note. The dedication is signed March 10, 2013, which was a Sunday, so the "April" in the verse is probably a slip.
