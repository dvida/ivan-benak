Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Za Društvo prijatelja starina

Occasional verse and dedications

For the Society of Friends of Antiquities

Topli pozdrav svima Vama želimi skupštini ovoj se veselim.

I send a warm greeting to all of you here,and this assembly fills me with good cheer.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,ovog časa što predsjednik kaže,ja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear the fine results you've had, as wellyour good success, the guidelines you've laid down,which at this hour the president will tell;and I congratulate you all around.

A skupština u ovome času,neka novu izabere Vladu,jer će ona sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And may this gathering, at this very hour,elect a "Government", a new one, too,for it, with lots of zest and lots of verve,will lead the club in all the work to do.

Dobar uspjeh novom predsjednikui odboru nek' se rada prima.Plodonosni rad skupštini našoj,ja iskreno želim sada svima.

Success to our new president, and maythe board get down to work, and do it well;and fruitful labour to our meeting: thisI wish you all sincerely, truth to tell.

Da bi naše društvo održali,na godinu aktivniji bili,LIJEPOM NAŠOM ugodno družilii KUTICE razne pohađali.

To keep our club alive and going strong,next year let's be more active than before,across Our Lovely Land let's meet as friends,and visit little nooks of every sort.

Prošlog ljeta mi smo se družili,u Zagrebu lijepo se proveli,u Križevci na proštenju bilii restoran „PARK“ smo posjetili.

Last summer we spent time as friends, together:in Zagreb we enjoyed a lovely day,we went to Križevci for the feast-day fair,and stopped at the "Park" restaurant on the way.

I još jedna postoji vrlina,što je ljetos naše društvo smoglo,da se sazna o našoj stariniMatici je hrvatskoj pomoglo.

And there's one other virtue we have shown,that our society achieved this year:it helped Matica hrvatska, so thatthe story of our heritage be known.

Za nas mladost više ne postoji,jer smo časno mi DRUŠTVO STARINA,trebali bi više se družiti,to bi bila baš prava milina.

For us there's no more youth, that's been and gone,for we're the honoured Society of Antiques;we ought to get together more, for thatwould be a real delight, the best of treats.

Svi u zdravlju i veselju bili.Uživajmo veselo bez rada.Mnogo ljeta skupa se družili,ne tražimo svetog Petra vrata.

In health and gladness may you all remain;let's merrily enjoy ourselves, work-free;and many summers may we meet again,and seek St Peter's gate? Not yet, not we!

Ostvarenje svih naših snova:želi pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova!

That all our dreams come true, just sowishes the poet Ivo of Valpovo!

Valpovo, 05.02.2014.

Valpovo, February 5, 2014

Translator's note. Greeting to the society's annual assembly. "Lijepa naša" (Our Lovely Land) is Croatia, after the first words of the national anthem. The joke in stanza 8: the members are old enough to be the antiques themselves. The "Government" is the society's new board.
