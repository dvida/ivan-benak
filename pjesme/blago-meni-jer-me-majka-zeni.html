Lucky Me, My Mother's Marrying Me Off

Kao momak u Sopju sam bio,ljepših cura, ja nisam vidio.Preko dana žito sam vršio,a navečer curice ljubio.

When I was just a lad in Sopje, see,no prettier girls were ever seen by me.By day I used to thresh the wheat, and thenat night I'd kiss the girls, and kiss again.

Jednog dana, majka veli meni:„Dragi, sine, hajde se oženi!Stigla jesen, uskoro će zima,a u Sopju lijepih cura ima.“

One day my mother said to me: "My son,my dear, come on and marry, get it done!The autumn's here, and soon the winter's due,and Sopje has its pretty girls, it's true."

„Iz Crkvenog Mara te pozdravlja,jedra cura, puca sva od zdravlja!Još je mlada, nema svoga dike,kud ćeš, sine, ti bolje prilike!“

"There's Mara from Crkveni says hello —a buxom lass, just bursting with health, you know!She's still young, and she has no beau as yet —my son, what better match could you hope to get!"

„Eto jučer, susrela me Janja,jedinica, dobrog je imanja.Divna cura iz Gračanskog kraja,voljela bih da mi bude snaja.“

"And Janja — yesterday I met her, see:an only child, with land and property.A lovely girl from out Gračanski way —I'd like her for my daughter-in-law someday."

„Magdalena iz Sopjanske Grede,kad te vidi kao maca prede.Njezin otac hoće te za zeta,da si kod njih, ništa mu ne smeta.“

"There's Magdalena, from Sopjanska Greda, too:she purrs just like a kitten, seeing you.Her father'd have you as his daughter's groom;he wouldn't mind you living in their home."

„Ja sam jučer, nakon duge stanke,navratila u Patoš kod Anke.Znaš kod one, koja nema muža?Kćerka Kata lijepa je k'o ruža.“

"And yesterday, the first time in an age,I dropped in down in Patoš, Anka's place.You know the one — no husband, heaven knows?Her daughter Kata's lovely as a rose."

Braziliju ja sam posjetio,sve do zore kod Klare sam bio.Nježno smo se noćas ljubovali,al' o braku nismo šaputali!

I paid a call on Brazilija too,and stayed with Klara till the night was through.Last night we loved each other tenderly,but whispered not a word of matrimony!

Iz Vlaškoga, divna Ruža mala,noćas mi je slatku ljubav dala.Moja majka za to nije znala,zato kažem, dragom Bogu hvala!

From Vlaški, lovely little Ruža — shelast night gave all her sweetest love to me.My mother never knew of it, and soI say: thank God above, she didn't know!

Iz Ritske je komšinica Jana,dolazila meni svakog dana;ni noću mi nije dala mira.Bježao bih glavom bez obzira!

My neighbour Jana, out of Ritska, cameto call on me each day, always the same;at night she'd give me no peace of mind —I'd flee headlong, and never look behind!

Cure, snaše, mnoge sam ljubio,al' se nisam mlađan oženio.Otišo' sam iz svojega sela,valjda tako sudbina je htjela.

I kissed a lot of girls and young wives too,but never married young — that much is true.I left my native village, went away;that's how my fate had willed it, I dare say.

Prošao sam Gračanski i Patoš,Braziliju, Vlaški i Crkveni,Ritsku Adu i Sopjansku Gredu.Išao bih opet sve po redu.

I made my rounds of Gračanski, Patoš,Brazilija, and Vlaški, and Crkveni,Ritska Ada and Sopjanska Greda too —and all in order, I'd go round anew!

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2011.

Valpovo, March 28, 2011

Translator's note. Crkveni, Gračanski, Sopjanska Greda, Patoš, Brazilija, Vlaški and Ritska Ada are hamlets and outlying parts of Sopje, the village where he grew up.
