Lucky Me, My Mother's Marrying Me Off

Kao momak u Sopju sam bio,ljepših cura, ja nisam vidio.Preko dana žito sam vršio,a navečer curice ljubio.

In Sopje, as a lad, I'll say outright:no prettier girls had ever met my sight.By day I threshed the wheat with all my might,and kissed the little girlies every night.

Jednog dana, majka veli meni:„Dragi, sine, hajde se oženi!Stigla jesen, uskoro će zima,a u Sopju lijepih cura ima.“

One day my mother said to me: "Dear son,it's time you took a wife, so get it done!The autumn's here, and winter's drawing near,and Sopje has its pretty girls, my dear."

„Iz Crkvenog Mara te pozdravlja,jedra cura, puca sva od zdravlja!Još je mlada, nema svoga dike,kud ćeš, sine, ti bolje prilike!“

"There's Mara from Crkveni sends her best:a buxom girl, with bursting health she's blessed!She's young still, and no fellow has she found;my son, there is no better match around!"

„Eto jučer, susrela me Janja,jedinica, dobrog je imanja.Divna cura iz Gračanskog kraja,voljela bih da mi bude snaja.“

"Just yesterday, young Janja came in view:an only child, with land and money too.A lovely girl from out Gračanski way;I'd love her for my daughter-in-law one day."

„Magdalena iz Sopjanske Grede,kad te vidi kao maca prede.Njezin otac hoće te za zeta,da si kod njih, ništa mu ne smeta.“

"From Sopjanska Greda, Magdalena, too:she purrs just like a kitten, seeing you.Her father'd have you for his son-in-law;if you moved in with them, he'd see no flaw."

„Ja sam jučer, nakon duge stanke,navratila u Patoš kod Anke.Znaš kod one, koja nema muža?Kćerka Kata lijepa je k'o ruža.“

"Just yesterday, though long I'd stayed away,I stopped at Anka's, down the Patoš way.You know, the one no husband ever chose?Her daughter Kata's lovely as a rose."

Braziliju ja sam posjetio,sve do zore kod Klare sam bio.Nježno smo se noćas ljubovali,al' o braku nismo šaputali!

I paid a call on Brazilija, too,and stayed with Klara till the dawn broke through.Last night we kissed so tenderly and slow,but whispered not of marriage: no, no, no!

Iz Vlaškoga, divna Ruža mala,noćas mi je slatku ljubav dala.Moja majka za to nije znala,zato kažem, dragom Bogu hvala!

From Vlaški, lovely little Ruža, shelast night gave all her sweetest love to me.My mother never knew of it, not she!And so I thank dear God on bended knee!

Iz Ritske je komšinica Jana,dolazila meni svakog dana;ni noću mi nije dala mira.Bježao bih glavom bez obzira!

My neighbour Jana, out of Ritska, cameto visit me each day, always the same;at night she'd give me no peace of mind —I'd flee headlong, and never look behind!

Cure, snaše, mnoge sam ljubio,al' se nisam mlađan oženio.Otišo' sam iz svojega sela,valjda tako sudbina je htjela.

Of girls and young wives, many I have kissed,but marriage, in my youth, I somehow missed.I left my village, went and moved away;I guess that fate would have it just that way.

Prošao sam Gračanski i Patoš,Braziliju, Vlaški i Crkveni,Ritsku Adu i Sopjansku Gredu.Išao bih opet sve po redu.

I made my rounds: Gračanski and Patoš,Brazilija, and Vlaški, and Crkveni,Ritska Ada and Sopjanska Greda, too;in order, I'd go round them all anew!

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2011.

Valpovo, March 28, 2011

Translator's note. Crkveni, Gračanski, Sopjanska Greda, Patoš, Brazilija, Vlaški and Ritska Ada are hamlets and outlying parts of Sopje, the village where he grew up.
