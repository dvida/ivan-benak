Once, Long Ago

Nekada davnoAmor je htioda slatku ljubavs tobom podijelim.O ljubavi našojsnove sam snio,jer tebe, draga,još uvijek želim.Sudba je htjelada odeš od mene.S drugim sadadijeliš svoje noći,a moje srceza tobom venei tople suzeliju moje oči.

Once, long ago,Amor had willedthat I should sharesweet love with you.With our lovemy dreams were filled,for I still want you,darling, I do.Fate had willedthat you'd leave one day;with another nowyou share your nights,and my heart for youis wasting away,and warm tearsflow from my eyes.

Valpovo, 15. 01. 1975.

Valpovo, January 15, 1975
