Vrtić „Bambi“

Poems for children

The "Bambi" Kindergarten

Vrtić „BAMBI“, to je,k'o srnino lane,lijepo djeca mala,tu provode dane.

"Bambi" kindergartenis like a baby deer;nicely, little childrenspend their days in here.

Veselo se igrajui tete ih maze,cijelog dana brižnoone na njih paze.

Happily they're playing,aunties hug them tight,and all day with carekeep them in their sight.

Kada papat pođu,mnoge teta hrani,tako djeci veselo,tu prolaze dani.

When they go for yum-yum,auntie feeds the small,and so merrily the dayspass here for them all.

Kad je igre dosta,onda spavat pođu,divne snove sanjaju,dok po njih ne dođu.

When they've played enough,off to nap they creep,dreaming lovely dreamstill they're fetched from sleep.

Tako opet sutra,kada zora svane,u „BAMBIKU“ dođuda provedu dane.

And again tomorrow,when the dawn breaks through,back to "Bambi" they cometo spend their days anew.

Valpovo, 28. 03. 2012.

Valpovo, March 28, 2012

Translator's note. The "aunties" (tete) are what Croatian children call their kindergarten teachers; "yum-yum" renders the baby-talk "papat", to eat.
