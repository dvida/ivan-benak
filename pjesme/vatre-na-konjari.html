Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Vatre na konjari

Occasional verse and dedications

Fires on the Horse Pasture

Nekada davno u ono staro doba,dok su konji bili prvoklasna roba.Svaku noć bi konje napasali ljudi,U zvjezdanoj noći, dok mjesec zarudi.

Long, long ago, back in that olden day,when horses were first-class goods, so to say,each night the people grazed their horses, ledout under stars, as the moon rose, glowing red.

Da si pruže radost, a dosadu skrate,za potrebe svoje ložili su vatre.Uz vatru se igrali običaji stariI zato se zovu: VATRE NA KONJARI!

To bring some joy and make the dull hours fly,they lit their fires, their own needs to supply.Around the fire old customs played, from days gone past:that's why they're called the Fires on the Horse Pasture!

Da je sve u redu vodili su pažnjui slaninu noću pekli su na ražnju.Da bi snove snili i veseli bili,Rakiju bi često iz čuture pili.

They took good care that everything went right,and roasted bacon on the spit at night.To dream their dreams and keep a cheerful mind,they'd often drink rakija from the flask, you'll find.

VATRE NA KONJARI to je igra stara,taj predivni projekt, što nam Miro stvara,u Zelčinu traje već desetak ljetai za njega znade polovica svijeta.

The Fires on the Horse Pasture: an old game, it's true;this lovely project, which Miro makes for us anew;in Zelčin it has run for ten years or so,and half the world has heard of it, you know.

Ovo časno djelo: VATRE NA KONJARI,davno izgubljeni običaji stari.Da probudi prošlost i pruži veselja,eto, to je skromna Varžićeva želja.

This worthy work, the Fires on the Horse Pasture:old customs, long since lost, from ages past.To wake old times, and bring us joy and cheer:that is, you see, the modest wish of Varžić here.

Valpovo, 23.07.2011

Valpovo, July 23, 2011

Translator's note. Vatre na konjari: in the old days men grazed the horses at night on the "konjara", the night pasture, and kept fires burning. Miro Varžić revived the custom as an event in Zelčin.
