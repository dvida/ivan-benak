Umirovljenički jadi

Poštarica u penziji

Pensioners' woes

The Retired Postwoman

Ja sam znao poštaricuiz mojega grada,mnogo ljeta pošti dalakao žena mlada.

I once knew a postwomanfrom my town, and shegave the post her many years,young as young could be.

Godine su prolazile,vrijeme brzo prođe.Da l' u tuzi ili sreći,mirovina dođe?

Years went by, as years will do,time goes by so fast.Was it sad or was it gladwhen her pension came at last?

Maštala je da će biti,život puno ljepši,sad kod kuće u samoći,unučad ju tješi.

She had dreamed that life would beso much lovelier;now at home and on her own,grandchildren comfort her.

Na tekućem u poštanskoj,mirovina mala.Da se vrati bivšem radu,ne zna što bi dala.

In her postal bank accountthere's a pension, small;to go back to her old jobshe would give her all.

Al' već druga mlada žena,sa poslom se sladi.Došla starost, bol se javlja,sretna što ne radi.

But a younger woman nowhas the job to savour;age has come, the aches begin —she's glad she's done with labour.

Valpovo, 24. 08. 2013.

Valpovo, August 24, 2013
