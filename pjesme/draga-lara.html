Draga Lara

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Lara

Kao ovaj zeko mali,imala baš uvijek sreće,po životnom tvome putucvjetalo ti divno cvijeće.Ja Ti dajem ovu knjiguda bi mogla svašta sniti,zato, Lara, brzo rastida ju možeš pročitati.Svud veselje nek' te pratii snivala divne snove,od srca ti, Laro, želi:mali Zeko knjige ove!

Like this little bunny, mayyour good fortune never fail;all along your path through lifemay fair flowers line your trail.Here's this book I give to youso you'll dream of all sorts of things;Lara, grow up quickly, do,so you'll read the tales it brings.May joy follow where you go,may your dreams be sweet and sunny;from his heart, dear Lara, sowishes this book's little Bunny!

Dragoj Lari Dubrovići autor to isto želi!

To dear Lara Dubrović:and the author wishes the same, too!

Valpovo, 16. 7. 2012.

Valpovo, July 16, 2012

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck), whose hero is the little Bunny. Lara is the daughter of the journalist Josip Dubrović; see also "Dear Tena", written for her sister the same day.
