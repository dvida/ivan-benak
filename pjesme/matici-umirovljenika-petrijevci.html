Umirovljenički jadi

Matici umirovljenika Petrijevci

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

To the Petrijevci Pensioners' Association

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

ŠTOVANA GENERACIJO!

ESTEEMED GENERATION!

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

Warmest greetings, all of you, I send,and your meeting gladdens me no end.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što, Zvonko, vaš predsjednik kaže,ja čestitam vama svima sada.

Now I hear the fine results you've made,your success, the course your work will steer;what your president, Zvonko, has conveyed:I congratulate you all right here.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For the deeds that you have carried outwith your labour in this passing year,I wish you still more success to come,won in the new year that's drawing near.

A skupština u ovome času,neka novu izabere Vladu,bila ona sa puno elanai uspjeha u budućem radu.

Let the meeting, at this very hour,choose a Government that's fresh and new;with élan in plenty may it be,with success in all it has to do.

I PJEVAČKO DRUŠTVO vaše slavno,koje vam je ponos, čast i dika,za uspjehe, zajedno sa vama,ponosna je vaša REPUBLIKA.

And your famed SINGING SOCIETY,which is your pride and honour, glory bright,for its triumphs, right along with you,your REPUBLIC's proud with all its might.

Ovog ljeta više se družili,S udrugama sportski takmičili.Mirovinu još veću imali,da bi bolji standard uživali.

This year may you more often meet and greet,and at sports with other clubs compete;may your pension be yet bigger still,so a better life your days may fill.

Sto godina živjeli i više,u ljubavi proživjeli vijek,služilo vas i zdravlje i sreća:ne krećite putićem za FENEK!

May you live a hundred years and more,live your lives in love from day to day;may good health and happiness serve you well:don't set out on FENEK's little way!

Ja vam želim ostvarenje snova:Pozdravlja vas, Ivo iz Valpova!

I wish you all your dreams come true; and so,greetings from Ivo of Valpovo!

Valpovo, 15. 05. 2008.

Valpovo, May 15, 2008

Napomena. Fenek je stari naziv za Ulicu Republike u Petrijevcima, koja vodi od župne crkve sv. Petra i Pavla do groblja. Ime potječe od posjeda Fenekalve na otoku između Drave i Karašice, čiji su se stanovnici preselili u Petrijevce.
Translator's note. A greeting to the annual assembly of the pensioners' association in Petrijevci, near Valpovo. He calls the association a “Republic” and its committee a “Government”. Fenek is the old name for Ulica Republike in Petrijevci, the street from the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul to the cemetery; it comes from Fenekalve, an old estate on an island between the Drava and the Karašica whose people moved to Petrijevci.
