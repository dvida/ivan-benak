Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Matici umirovljenika Petrijevci

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Petrijevci Pensioners' Association

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

ŠTOVANA GENERACIJO!

Esteemed Generation!

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

Warm greetings to you all I wish to send,and at your meeting I rejoice, my friends.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,to što, Zvonko, vaš predsjednik kaže,ja čestitam vama svima sada.

I hear of your results, so fine and bright,the good success, the course your work will steer,which Zvonko, your own president, reports;and I congratulate you all right here.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the things that you've accomplished here,by all your work within this passing year,I wish you even more success to come,achieved in the new year that's drawing near.

A skupština u ovome času,neka novu izabere Vladu,bila ona sa puno elanai uspjeha u budućem radu.

And may the meeting, at this present hour,elect a Government that's fresh and new,and may it have great energy, and meetsuccess in all the work it has to do.

I PJEVAČKO DRUŠTVO vaše slavno,koje vam je ponos, čast i dika,za uspjehe, zajedno sa vama,ponosna je vaša REPUBLIKA.

And your illustrious Singing Society,your pride, your honour, and your glory too:of its successes, right along with you,your whole Republic is as proud as can be.

Ovog ljeta više se družili,S udrugama sportski takmičili.Mirovinu još veću imali,da bi bolji standard uživali.

May you this year more often get together,compete with other clubs in sporting play;and may your pensions yet be larger still,so you enjoy a better life each day.

Sto godina živjeli i više,u ljubavi proživjeli vijek,služilo vas i zdravlje i sreća:ne krećite putićem za FENEK!

A hundred years and more, long may you live,and spend your whole life in love, day by day;may health and happiness be at your side:don't take the little path to Fenek, pray!

Ja vam želim ostvarenje snova:Pozdravlja vas, Ivo iz Valpova!

I wish that all your dreams come true, and soyou're greeted by Ivo from Valpovo!

Valpovo, 15. 05. 2008.

Valpovo, May 15, 2008

Translator's note. A greeting to the annual assembly of the pensioners' association in Petrijevci, near Valpovo. He calls the association a “Republic” and its committee a “Government”.
