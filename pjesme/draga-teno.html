Draga Teno

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Tena

Evo Tebi knjiga ova,nek' ju čita: tata, mama,svaku večer prije snova,da ne budeš nikad sama.U toj knjizi svašta ima:u vrtiću prava himnai cipele koje trče,dok se noću slatko hrče.I sastanak žabe vodeda se bore protiv rode,mlijeka daje bijela krava,a kokica jaja prava.Pa i maca kako spava,to je, Teno, priča prava.Ovu knjigu dajem Teni,za dug život, lijepi, sneni.Kad ju budeš pročitala,onda meni reci - hvala!Puno sreće život cijeli,to Ti autor, Teno, želi!

Here's this book for you to keep;let your dad and mum, each one,read it nightly before sleep,so you're never left alone.In this book there's lots to meet:a true kindergarten hymn,and the shoes that run, while wesweetly snore at night, asleep.And the frogs convene to talkhow to fight against the stork;milk comes from the cow so white,and the hen lays eggs just right.And how kitty sleeps as well:Tena, that's a tale to tell!Tena, here's this book from mefor a long life, fair and dreamy.When you've read it all the way,then "Thank you!" to me you'll say!Lots of luck your whole life through:Tena, the author wishes you!

Valpovo, 16. 7. 2012.

Valpovo, July 16, 2012

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting), whose poems it lists: the kindergarten anthem, the running shoes, the frogs' meeting, the cow, the hen and the cat. Tena is Lara Dubrović's sister.
