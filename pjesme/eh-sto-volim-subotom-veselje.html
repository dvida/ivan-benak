Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Eh, što volim subotom veselje!

Occasional verse and dedications

Oh, How I Love Saturday Merriment!

Predivno je subotnje veselje,ispunjava slušatelja želje.To je dio tamburaškog raja,što je ukras našeg zavičaja.

How lovely is our Saturday Merriment!It grants the wishes that the listeners sent.It is a piece of tamburica bliss;our native region's ornament is this.

Šokadijo slavna i velika,ti si našoj Slavoniji dika.Svako selo ima tamburašadivnih cura i čarobnih snaša.

O Šokadija, glorious and grand,you are the pride of our Slavonian land.Each village has tamburica players there,and lovely girls, and young wives charming, fair.

Ja subotom slušam Ceciliju,što narodu divni osmjeh šalje.Na telefon odgovore daje,glas miline emisijom traje.

On Saturdays I tune in to Cecilija,who sends the people such a lovely smile;she answers every caller on the phone,her voice of sweetness lasting all the while.

Kad zapjeva ljepotu nam stvara:„ne ritaj se mačketino stara,joj, kirvaja ispod šlingeraja!“Od SIRENA ljepše pjesme stvara.

When she sings out, what beauty fills the air:"Don't kick about, you great old cat, you there,oh, kirvaj underneath the lace petticoat!"she makes songs lovelier than Sirene's, I swear.

Što je lijepo neka dugo traje,mnogo ljeta nek' Cecilija vodi,to veselje slavonskoga žara,to je žena koja ima dara.

What's lovely, may it last and last, I pray;for many years may Cecilija hostthis merriment with all Slavonia's fire:she is a woman with a gift, I'd say.

Sačuvajmo SLAVONSKO VESELJE,ispunjava šokadiji želje.Uživajmo kada ječi žica:neka traje mnogo godinica!

Let's keep alive Slavonian merriment:it answers every wish Šokadija sent.Let us enjoy it when the strings ring clear:may it go on for many a happy year!

Valpovo, 20.01.2014.

Valpovo, January 20, 2014

Translator's note. Subotnje veselje ("Saturday Merriment") is a tamburica request show on local radio, hosted by Cecilija. The quoted lines are from a song; the kirvaj is the village church fair, and Sirene is a group.
