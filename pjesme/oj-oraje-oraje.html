Radio burza

Oj, oraje, oraje

Radio market

Oh, Walnut, Walnut Tree

Ako netko trebaod oraha drvo,neka mi se javi,na telefon prvo.

If anyone's needingsome walnut for wood,just call me up first,on the phone, as you should.

Šesto pedeset i dva,osamsto dvadeset jedan,to je moj telefon,a stalno je vrijedan.

Six hundred fifty-two,eight hundred twenty-one —that's my telephone,it always gets things done.

Ja bih orah daoda ga netko sruši,jer ustvari menion više ne služi.

I'd give the walnut treeto one who'll cut it down,for really, to me nowit's no use, I have found.

Orah dobro rodiima jezgru pravu,ali baba veli:ne za sijedu glavu.

The walnut tree bears well,good kernels, I'd say,but granny says: "Not meantfor a head that's grey."

Još mi baba kaže:e, moj djeda sijedi,ni oraha ljuska,više ti ne vrijedi.

And granny tells me too:"Eh, my grandad grey,not even a walnut shell'sof use to you today."

Valpovo, 29. 11. 2011.

Valpovo, November 29, 2011

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza: he is giving the walnut tree away to whoever will cut it down.
