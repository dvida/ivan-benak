Love poems

There Is Still Hope

Sjeti se prošlosti,mog anđele plavi.Divnih zagrljaja,poljubaca slatkih,ne samo u snima,već u zbilji, javi,sjeti se nježnostii časova kratkih.

Remember the past,my angel in blue,the wondrous embraces,the kisses so sweet,not only in dreamingbut waking and true;remember the tenderness,the moments so fleet.

Još postoji nadada mi snove budiš,još postoji nadada mi ljubav vratiš,još postoji nadada me nježno ljubiš.U ljubavi našoj,da me vjerno pratiš.

There's hope for us yetyou'll waken my dreams,there's hope for us yetyou'll give love back to me,there's hope for us yetyou'll tenderly kiss me,and in our love walkbeside me faithfully.

Vrati mi se, mila,da me sunce grije.Tijelo, srce, dušu,sve ću tebi dati.Pružiš li mi ljubav,kao nekad prije,ljubav tvoju, draga,ja ću cijenit znati.

Come back to me, darling,so the sun warms me through.My body, heart, soul —I will give all to you.If you give me your loveas you once used to do,your love, my dearest,I will cherish it true.

Da ti ljubav pružim,u zagrljaj dođi.Patnju duše moje,pokušaj skratiti.Nježna milovanja,u tihanoj noćii poljupce tvoje,duplo ću vratiti.

So I may give you love,come into my arms;the pain of my soultry to shorten, I pray.The tender caressesin the night's quiet calm,and all of your kissestwice over I'll repay.

Valpovo, 14. 09. 1982.

Valpovo, September 14, 1982
