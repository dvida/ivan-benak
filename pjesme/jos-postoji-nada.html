Love poems

There Is Still Hope

Sjeti se prošlosti,mog anđele plavi.Divnih zagrljaja,poljubaca slatkih,ne samo u snima,već u zbilji, javi,sjeti se nježnostii časova kratkih.

Remember the old days,my angel dressed in blue,the wondrous embraces,the kisses honey-sweet,not only in dreamingbut waking, real and true;recall the tender ways,the moments short and fleet.

Još postoji nadada mi snove budiš,još postoji nadada mi ljubav vratiš,još postoji nadada me nježno ljubiš.U ljubavi našoj,da me vjerno pratiš.

There's hope for us yetmy dreams you'll renew,there's hope for us yetyou'll love me once more,there's hope for us yetyou'll kiss me, soft and true,and, side by side in love,stay faithful as before.

Vrati mi se, mila,da me sunce grije.Tijelo, srce, dušu,sve ću tebi dati.Pružiš li mi ljubav,kao nekad prije,ljubav tvoju, draga,ja ću cijenit znati.

Come back to me, darling,and warm me like the sun.My body, heart and soul,all this I'll give to you.If you give me your lovethe way you once had done,your love, my darling dear,I'll treasure my life through.

Da ti ljubav pružim,u zagrljaj dođi.Patnju duše moje,pokušaj skratiti.Nježna milovanja,u tihanoj noćii poljupce tvoje,duplo ću vratiti.

So I may give you love,come hold me close and tight;the pain my soul has known,please try to take away.The tender caressesin the still, quiet night,and kisses of your own,twice over I'll repay.

Valpovo, 14. 09. 1982.

Valpovo, September 14, 1982
