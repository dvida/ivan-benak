Domoljubne i zavičajne

Da se ne zaboravi

Homeland and native region

Lest It Be Forgotten

Ja sam rođen u blizini Drave,u dolini Podravine ravne.U Patošu od Sopjanke prave,mlade snaše, od Šokice slavne.

I was born close by the Drava's banks,in Podravina's valley, flat and plain,in Patoš, to a Sopje woman true,a young wife, and a Šokica of fame.

Još se sjećam svojega djetinjstvai mladosti što je Sopjem prošla.Svu ljepotu srcem dajem Sopju,u vremenu kad je starost došla.

I still remember all my childhood yearsand youth that passed in Sopje, day by day.To Sopje, with my heart, I give all beauty,now in the time when old age comes my way.

Valpovo, 07. 01. 2013.

Valpovo, January 7, 2013

Translator's note. Patoš is a hamlet of Sopje, the poet's native village in Podravina, the Drava lowland.
