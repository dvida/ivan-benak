Hvala ti, draga!

Love poems

Thank You, Darling!

Hvala ti na ruži i poljupcu tvome.I nježnim snovima što sam noćas snio.I današnjem danu, što sam s tobom bio.

Thank you for the rose, and for your kiss,and for the tender dreams I dreamed last night,and for today, the day I spent there by your side.

I Amoru hvala za te nježne snove,što noćas sa tobom ja biti sam smio,i ljubav moju na tvom srcu grij'o.

And thanks to Amor, too, for all those tender dreams,that I was granted, last night, to be with you,and warm my love against your heart the whole night through.

Na pogledu tvome, velika ti hvala,što me cijelog dana, nježno, toplo grije,kao ono nekad u davnini prije.

For that glance of yours, the greatest thanks I owe,that warms me, tenderly and warmly, all day long,the way it did before, in days of long ago.

Još su oči tvoje, mila, pune čežnje,a riječi su tople, iskrene i nježne.Oh, ljubavi moja, na svemu ti hvala.

Still full of longing are your eyes, my dear,and your words are warm and tender and sincere.Oh, my love, for everything, my thanks to you.

Valpovo, 23. 09. 1973.

Valpovo, September 23, 1973
