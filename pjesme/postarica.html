The Post-Office Girl

Što je lijepo poštarica biti,divna glava za šalterom viri,iza vjeđa dragulji joj skriti,sa pogledom moju dušu širi.

What a lovely thing, to be a postal clerk!Such a lovely head peeps out behind the glass;underneath her lids a pair of jewels hide;with a glance she opens wide my soul at last.

Na šalteru kad pisamce prima,pogledam joj obraze rumenei pošiljku ona vješto snima,u mom gradu nema ljepše žene!

At the counter, when she takes my little letter,at her rosy cheeks I cannot help but stare,and she logs the parcel deftly in its turn —in my town no other woman can compare!

Ažurna je u svojemu radu,marku stavlja na maleno pismo.Gledam nježno poštaricu mladu,mislim, šteta što voljeni nismo.

She is diligent and quick at every task,on my little letter sets the stamp just so.Tenderly I watch the young girl at the desk,thinking: what a pity we're not sweethearts, though.

A ona mi moj pogled uzvrati.Divan osmijeh s anđeoskog lica,pa mi marku za pismo naplati.Baš je divno zvanje poštarica!

And she answers me by giving back my gaze,from her angel's face a lovely smile displayed;then she charges me the postage for my letter.What a lovely calling is the post-girl's trade!

Valpovo, 07. 11. 2012.

Valpovo, November 7, 2012
