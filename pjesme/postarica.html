The Post-Office Girl

Što je lijepo poštarica biti,divna glava za šalterom viri,iza vjeđa dragulji joj skriti,sa pogledom moju dušu širi.

What a lovely thing, to ply the post-girl's trade!a pretty head peeps from the counter, just inside;jewels hidden underneath her eyelids' shade,with a glance she opens up my soul so wide.

Na šalteru kad pisamce prima,pogledam joj obraze rumenei pošiljku ona vješto snima,u mom gradu nema ljepše žene!

At the counter, when my letter she will take,at her rosy cheeks I cannot help but stare,and she logs the parcel deftly, no mistake —in my town no other woman can compare!

Ažurna je u svojemu radu,marku stavlja na maleno pismo.Gledam nježno poštaricu mladu,mislim, šteta što voljeni nismo.

Diligent and prompt she is in all her work,on my little letter sets the stamp with care;tenderly I watch the pretty postal clerk,thinking: what a pity we are not a pair!

A ona mi moj pogled uzvrati.Divan osmijeh s anđeoskog lica,pa mi marku za pismo naplati.Baš je divno zvanje poštarica!

And she looks right back at me, the way I do,from her angel's face a lovely smile displayed,then she charges me the postage that is due.What a lovely calling is the post-girl's trade!

Valpovo, 07. 11. 2012.

Valpovo, November 7, 2012
