Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Kad ispuni televizor želje

Occasional verse and dedications

When the Television Grants Wishes

Ja pozdravljam voditelja Laca,koji vodi SLAVONSKO VESELJE,a sa njime gospodina ŽACA,slušati ih to su prave želje.

My greetings go to Laco, the presenterwho hosts Slavonsko veselje on air,and Mister Žac, who's there with him as well:to hear them is a true wish, I declare.

Predivne su ovo emisije,najljepše su Osječkoga kraja.A čarobni osmijeh Cecilije,to je dio zemaljskoga raja.

How wonderful these programmes are to see,the loveliest the Osijek region's known;and Cecilija's magic smile, for me,is part of earthly paradise, our own.

Svako mjesto tamburaša ima,Slavoniji ljepotu su dali,glazbom srca otvorili svima,Šokadiju svirkom uljepšali.

Each place has its own tamburica band;they gave Slavonia beauty with their art;with music they have opened every heartand made Šokadija a lovelier land.

Slavonijom kada petak svane,slušatelji telefonom jave,bilo ljetne ili zimske dane,to su za nas emisije prave.

When Friday dawns across Slavonia,the listeners phone in to join the show;on summer days, and on the winter days,these are the proper programmes, as we know.

Cecilija subotu ukrasi,i programu svu ljepotu daje,svaki žamor u kući se gasi,u grudima sva milina traje.

And Cecilija graces Saturday,she gives the programme all its loveliness;each murmur in the house then dies away,and in our breasts there lingers happiness.

A nedjeljom SLAVONSKO VESELJEtamburaši naše Slavonije,ispunit će svakom Šokcu željeneka ovo što duže potraje!

And then on Sundays, Slavonsko veselje,Slavonia's own tamburica men, we hear,will grant each Šokac every wish he has —long may it last, and last for many a year!

Valpovo, 16. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 16, 2013

Translator's note. Slavonsko veselje (“Slavonian Merriment”) is a tamburica music programme on Osijek television; Laco, Žac and Cecilija present its folk-music shows.
