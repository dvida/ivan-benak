Love poems

In Merry Company

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Jednom dok sam pioplješevičko vino.Desetak je ljetaono bilo staro,pijanstvo je meni,ovu hrabrost dalo.Priđoh jednoj ženii oslovim fino.

Once, while I was drinkingPlješevica wine,some ten summers oldit was, that wine of mine,and drunkenness, you see,gave me this daring.I went up to a ladyand spoke, polite and fine.

"Oprostite ima l'tu mjesta za mene?Ne samo za stolom,već u srcu vašem."I još sam ja htionešto da joj kažem,ali kakav poglednepoznate žene.

"Pardon me, is therea place here for me?Not just at your table,but within your heart."And I was going to startto tell her something more,but oh, the look I gotfrom that stranger, that lady!

"Izvolite sjesti,jedno mjesto ima,ali srce mojesad pripada drugom."Uto mi se grudiispuniše tugom.Rado bih njen bio,baš za inat svima.

"Please, do take a seat,there is one place, it's true,but this heart of minebelongs to another, you know."At that my breastwas filled up with woe.Gladly I'd be hers,just to spite them all.

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979
