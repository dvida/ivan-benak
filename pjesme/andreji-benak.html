Obiteljski kutak

Andreji Benak

Family corner

To Andreja Benak

Unuko Andreja,evo ti velim,tvome životu,sve dobro želim.

Granddaughter Andreja,here's what I tell:for all of your lifeI wish you well.

Imala uvijeksreće, veselja,to mi je, vjeruj,najveća želja.

May you always havehappiness and cheer:believe me, that ismy greatest wish, dear.

Voli te autorknjižice ove,to je tvoj djeda,Ivo se zove.

This booklet's author,who loves you, you see,is your own grandpa,and Ivo is he.

Valpovo, 27. 10. 2005.

Valpovo, October 27, 2005

Translator's note. Written in a copy of one of his books for his granddaughter.
