Homeland and native region

Šokac Customs

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Volim tamburicu kad joj žica cikne,u dušu me takne kada vodim kolo.Srce zatreperi kad mi dika vikne:u kijer mi dođi moj voljeni lolo!

I love the tamburica when its string rings out high,it touches my soul when I'm leading the kolo;my heart starts to quiver whenever my sweetheart calls:"Come into my chamber, my dearly loved fellow!"

Volim kad se ljubav sokacima vodi.Volim škripu đerma i seosku viku.Volim kada mjesec sa zvijezdama hodi,na toj mjesečini volim svoju diku.

I love it when lovers go walking the lanes,I love the well-sweep's creaking, the shouts down the street,I love when the moon strolls along with the stars,and there in that moonlight I love my own sweet.

Volim prela, sijela, šokačke divane,i maškare volim, kada se opreme.svu ljepotu volim u svatovske dane,divne običaje što žive kroz vrijeme.

I love the spinning bees, sijela, the Šokac yarns,I love the masqueraders, when dressed up they appear;I love all the beauty of wedding-feast days,the wonderful customs that live year on year.

Nema više konja da dvorištem njište,mjesto njih traktori sokacima bruje.Nema mladih snaša da u kolu vrište,sve je utihnulo, ništa se ne čuje.

There are no more horses to neigh in the yard,instead of them tractors go droning down lanes;no young wives are left in the kolo to shriek,all has fallen silent, and no sound remains.

Sada televizor zamjenjuje prela.Sokaci su pusti, ne igra se kolo,ni bećara nama da obiđu sijela.Ničeg više nema, moj voljeni lolo!

Now television has taken the spinning bees' place,the lanes are deserted, and none dance the kolo,no bećars are left to go round the sijela —there's nothing left now, my dearly loved fellow!

Valpovo, 09.04.1994.

Valpovo, April 9, 1994

Translator's note. Prela (spinning bees) and sijela were the village's winter evening gatherings; the maškare are carnival masqueraders; "lolo", here "dearly loved fellow", is a girl's word for her sweetheart.
