Ljubavne pjesme

Zanos

Love poems

Rapture

Znam da ćeš doćicjelov ćeš mi dati.U naručju momemilim ćeš me zvati.

I know that you will come;you'll give a kiss to me.Held in my arms, you'll call me"my darling", you will see.

Dok ti usne drhteod poljupca strasnog,tvoje oko blista,k'o sjaj neba jasnog.

While your lips are tremblingfrom a passionate kiss,your eye is shininglike a sky without mist.

Sad, dok čulno tonešti u ljubav sretnu,tijelo tvoje drhti,kao list na vjetru.

Now, as all your sensessink in happy love,your body is tremblinglike a leaf in the wind.

Sva rajska blaženstvatvoja duša sanja,a tijelo ti željnotoplog milovanja.

All the bliss of heavenyour soul is dreaming,and your body's longingfor warm caressing.

I poslije, dok seiz slatkog sna budiš,svoje tople usnemojem licu nudiš.

And later, as youfrom sweet sleep awake,your warm lips you offerfor my face to take.

Poljupcima vrelimprekrivaš mi tijelo,dok te nježno ljubimi šapućem sneno.

With kisses burning hotyou cover all my body,while I kiss you tenderlyand whisper, drowsy.

Oh, ljubavi moja!Slatka si i mila,kao majka čedu,kad raširi krila.

Oh, my love, my darling!Sweet you are, and mild,like a mother spreadingher wings above her child.

Valpovo, 20. 12. 1973.

Valpovo, December 20, 1973
