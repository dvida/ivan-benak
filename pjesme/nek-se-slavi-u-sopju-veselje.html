Domoljubne i zavičajne

Nek' se slavi u Sopju veselje

Homeland and native region

Let Sopje Celebrate and Make Merry

Podravino, domovino moja,selo Sopje u tebi se svilo.Općina je tu odavno bila,devet sela k sebi prigrlila.

O Podravina, homeland of mine,Sopje nestled in your gentle hold;the municipal seat's been here of old,took nine villages into its fold.

Dan općine četvrtog je svibnja.Florijanu ljudi zavjet daju,da ne bude velikih požara,kao Patoš što Sopjani znaju.

Fourth of May's the Municipal Day;people make to Florian their vowthat no great fire like Patoš strike again:Sopje folk remember it even now.

Dan općine u Višnjici slave,svi vijećnici, uz domjenak malii nagrade kad podjele prave,nek' se znade što su Sopju dali.

In Višnjica they celebrate the day,councillors, a small reception laid,hand out prizes in the proper way,so all know the gifts to Sopje made.

Sopjani se sa agrarom bave,rodne njive plodove im daju.Svu ljepotu u svom selu slave,zato žive u zemaljskom raju.

Sopje's people farm and till the land;fertile fields give fruit to those who sow;they rejoice in beauty close at hand,so they live in paradise below.

U proljeće MAJIPANI se sade,to mladići znak curama daju,jer ujesen svatovi se prave,neka budu spremne za udaju.

Springtime sees the maypoles in the ground:that's how lads tell girls what's on the way,for when autumn's weddings come around,let the girls be ready for that day.

Kada Dravsko proljeće osvane,to je smotra narodnog folklora.KUD "Podravac" slavi ove dane,tu ljepotu doživjet se mora.

When the Dravsko proljeće dawns again,it's the people's folklore on full show;KUD "Podravac" celebrates it then:that's a beauty you must come to know.

Iz Mađarske i KUD-ovi naši,Sopju hrle na Dravsko proljeće.Kad se skupe dični folkloraši,i povorka kroz Sopje poteče.

Hungary's and our own KUDs draw near,flocking into Sopje for the Spring;when the splendid dancers gather here,and through Sopje their parade they bring,

Tada kolo KUD "Podravac" vije,a Šokica glasno pocikuje,na tamburi snažno žice bruje,preko Luke Greda odjekuje.

then the kolo KUD "Podravac" winds,and a Šokica lets out her cry,on the tambura strings ring loud and high,over Luka, to Greda, the echoes fly.

VIDOVO se u ljepoti slavi,kirvaj Sopja što odavno traje.Skupljaju se prijatelji pravi,da proslave stare običaje.

Vidovo's a feast so fine and fair,Sopje's church fair, held since days of old;all the truest friends are gathered there,all the good old customs to uphold.

IVANJE se slavi još od prije,u ljepoti dobro odigrano:od Udruge "Sportske rekreacije",sa veseljem Sopju darovano.

St John's Day is honoured as before,finely staged, in beauty, every part,by the "Sports Recreation" club once more,given Sopje with a joyful heart.

Kad Marija Magdalena dođe,drugi kirvaj, što u Sopju traje,pa kroz Sopje sva ljepota prođe,to crkvene slave običaje.

When Mary Magdalene's day draws nigh,second church fair Sopje keeps each year,then through Sopje beauty passes by:customs of the church feast, held so dear.

Sve dok bude bilo Slavonije,skupljat će se Šokadija našai veselja bit će kao prije,uz tambure dobrih tamburaša.

For as long as Slavonia stays,our Šokadija will gather still,there'll be merriment as in old days,with tamburas played by men of skill.

Nogometni klub "Podravac" slavni,od davnina svoja ljeta broji.U sopjanskom kraju on je glavni,to službeno u zapisu stoji.

Football club "Podravac", far renowned,counts its summers back to long ago;first in all of Sopje's lands around,as the official records clearly show.

Dobrovoljno vatrogasno društvo,već stotinjak godinica niže,sve požare u Sopju i bliže,svojim radom ugasiti stiže.

And the Volunteer Fire Brigadecounts some hundred little years today;fires in Sopje, and not far away,with its work it quells without delay.

Ribolovno društvo "Šaran" zvani,sa vršama često ribu hvata,kad uhvati soma deset kila,tu se onda pravi fišijada.

And the fishing club they call "Šaran"often catches fish in traps, and whenit has caught a catfish, ten kilos,there's a fish-stew party there and then.

Tamburaši "Sopjanski Bećari",na tamburi kad ugode žicei kad prave u Sopju veselje,tada ječe sopjanske ulice.

"Sopjanski Bećari", tambura band,when on tamburas they tune the strings,and when Sopje's merriment they make,then each Sopje street resounds and rings.

Valpovo, 06. 09. 2012.

Valpovo, September 6, 2012

Translator's note. Sopje was his birthplace. St Florian, patron of firefighters, is honoured on its municipal day; Vidovo (St Vitus) and St Mary Magdalene are its two church fairs (kirvaj); Dravsko proljeće ("Drava Spring") is a folklore festival and a KUD a folk-arts society. Patoš was the Sopje street almost wholly destroyed by fire. Luka is the ground in Sopje where the football pitch lies (the name also means a riverside meadow), and Greda the neighbouring village of Sopjanska Greda: the music carries from one to the other.
Druga inačica: „Stihom kroz Sopje“, 2012. Another version: “Through Sopje in Verse”, 2012

Valpovo, September 18, 2012

Translator's note. A year of Sopje's feasts: St Florian's Day (4 May) is the municipality's day; the maypoles (majipani) are set up by the young men; Ivanje is St John's Day at midsummer; the church fair (kirvaj) of Mary Magdalene falls in July. Patoš is the street once destroyed by fire, and Šaran means "carp".
