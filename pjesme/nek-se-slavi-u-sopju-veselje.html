Domoljubne i zavičajne

Nek' se slavi u Sopju veselje

Homeland and native region

Let Sopje Celebrate and Make Merry

Podravino, domovino moja,selo Sopje u tebi se svilo.Općina je tu odavno bila,devet sela k sebi prigrlila.

O Podravina, homeland of my own,in you the village Sopje made its nest.Its municipality has long stood here,nine villages it's gathered to its breast.

Dan općine četvrtog je svibnja.Florijanu ljudi zavjet daju,da ne bude velikih požara,kao Patoš što Sopjani znaju.

Municipal Day falls on the fourth of May;the people vow to Florian, and praythat no great fires befall them, like Patoš,as Sopje's people know unto this day.

Dan općine u Višnjici slave,svi vijećnici, uz domjenak malii nagrade kad podjele prave,nek' se znade što su Sopju dali.

In Višnjica they celebrate the day,the councillors, with light refreshments laid,and when the proper awards are handed out,so all will know the gifts to Sopje made.

Sopjani se sa agrarom bave,rodne njive plodove im daju.Svu ljepotu u svom selu slave,zato žive u zemaljskom raju.

The Sopje people farm the land they till,their fertile fields give fruit of every kind;they celebrate the beauty of their village,and so they live in paradise, you'll find.

U proljeće MAJIPANI se sade,to mladići znak curama daju,jer ujesen svatovi se prave,neka budu spremne za udaju.

In spring they plant the maypoles in the ground:the young men give the girls a sign this way,for in the autumn weddings will be held,so let them be prepared for their wedding day.

Kada Dravsko proljeće osvane,to je smotra narodnog folklora.KUD "Podravac" slavi ove dane,tu ljepotu doživjet se mora.

When Dravsko proljeće has dawned again,the people's folklore gathers on display;KUD "Podravac" celebrates these days —that beauty is a thing you must see, I say.

Iz Mađarske i KUD-ovi naši,Sopju hrle na Dravsko proljeće.Kad se skupe dični folkloraši,i povorka kroz Sopje poteče.

From Hungary, and our own KUDs too,they flock to Sopje for the Spring once more;and when the splendid dancers gather there,through Sopje the procession starts to pour.

Tada kolo KUD "Podravac" vije,a Šokica glasno pocikuje,na tamburi snažno žice bruje,preko Luke Greda odjekuje.

Then KUD "Podravac" winds the kolo round,a Šokica lets out her whooping cries,the tambura strings are humming loud and strong,across Luka Greda the echo flies.

VIDOVO se u ljepoti slavi,kirvaj Sopja što odavno traje.Skupljaju se prijatelji pravi,da proslave stare običaje.

Vidovo's celebrated, fine and fair,the church fair Sopje's held for many a year;true friends come gathering together thereto celebrate old customs, held so dear.

IVANJE se slavi još od prije,u ljepoti dobro odigrano:od Udruge "Sportske rekreacije",sa veseljem Sopju darovano.

St John's is celebrated as of old,performed so well, in beauty, every part,by the Sports Recreation Society,bestowed on Sopje with a joyful heart.

Kad Marija Magdalena dođe,drugi kirvaj, što u Sopju traje,pa kroz Sopje sva ljepota prođe,to crkvene slave običaje.

When Mary Magdalene's feast day comes round,the second church fair Sopje keeps each year,then all of Sopje's beauty passes through:the customs of the parish feast are here.

Sve dok bude bilo Slavonije,skupljat će se Šokadija našai veselja bit će kao prije,uz tambure dobrih tamburaša.

As long as there's Slavonia on the earth,our Šokadija will come together still,and there'll be merriment as once there was,as good tambura players show their skill.

Nogometni klub "Podravac" slavni,od davnina svoja ljeta broji.U sopjanskom kraju on je glavni,to službeno u zapisu stoji.

The famous football club, "Podravac",has counted up its years since long ago.In Sopje's region it's the leading one —the official records say that it is so.

Dobrovoljno vatrogasno društvo,već stotinjak godinica niže,sve požare u Sopju i bliže,svojim radom ugasiti stiže.

The Volunteer Fire Brigade by now has strunga hundred little years, or near, in line;and all the fires in Sopje and close byit reaches with its work to quench in time.

Ribolovno društvo "Šaran" zvani,sa vršama često ribu hvata,kad uhvati soma deset kila,tu se onda pravi fišijada.

The fishing club that goes by "Šaran"'s namewith fish traps often catches fish; and whenit lands a catfish of some ten kilos,a fish-stew feast is thrown right there and then.

Tamburaši "Sopjanski Bećari",na tamburi kad ugode žicei kad prave u Sopju veselje,tada ječe sopjanske ulice.

The "Sopjanski Bećari", tambura band,when on their tamburas they tune the strings,and when they bring the merriment to Sopje,then every street of Sopje echoes and rings.

Valpovo, 06. 09. 2012.

Valpovo, September 6, 2012

Translator's note. Sopje was his birthplace. St Florian, patron of firefighters, is honoured on its municipal day; Vidovo (St Vitus) and St Mary Magdalene are its two church fairs (kirvaj); Dravsko proljeće ("Drava Spring") is a folklore festival and a KUD a folk-arts society. Patoš is kept as written; its local meaning is unclear.
