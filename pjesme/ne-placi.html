Don't Cry!

Ne prosipaj biser očinjega vida,jer me suza tvoja na obrazu peče.Plakao bih i ja, ne mogu od stida,zato su mi boli u grudima veće.

Do not spill the pearls that well up from your eyes,for the tear upon your cheek burns me through and through.I would weep as well, but shame won't let me cry,and so the pain within my breast is greater, too.

Nasmiješi se malo kad si pokraj mene.Da nam slatka ljubav kao rijeka teče.Zaboravi tugu i te bolne sjene.Neka bude više u životu sreće.

Smile a little bit whenever you're with me,so that our sweet love may flow just like a river.Forget the grief, those painful shadows, let them be.Let our life hold more of happiness than ever.

Kad nisi kraj mene, srce mi se kida.Nikada te nitko jače voljet neće.Ne prosipaj suze, biser očnog vida.Neka bude divno, za nas ovo veće.

When you're not beside me, my heart is torn inside,no one else will ever love you more than I do.Do not spill your tears, those pearls from your eyes,and let this evening be a wonder for us two.

Valpovo, 27. 01. 1974.

Valpovo, January 27, 1974
