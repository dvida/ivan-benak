Humorous poems

Between Wanting and Being Able

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

IVICA: Ja sam, Slava, iz daleka momak.Bi li mene primila na konak?Pa da skupa u tu noćcu svetumi pišemo koncert u krevetu.Da nam bude veselo i živo,to bi htio iz Valpova Ivo.

Ivica: Slava, I'm a lad from far away —would you take me in and let me stay?So that together, on that holy night,a concert in bed we two might write.So that it's merry and lively, you know,that's what he'd like, Ivo from Valpovo.

SLAVA: Evo tebi Slava odgovara:Lip si, Ivo, da ti nema para.A pošto sam ja ovako mlada,za taj koncert još imamo kada.Oj, Ivane, moje lipo janje,to ti veli Slava iz Vrbanje.

Slava: Here's Slava's answer to you, plain and clear:you're handsome, Ivo — no one is your peer.And since I am so young, as you can see,we've time yet for that concert, you and me.Oh, Ivan, my sweet lamb, this is the wordfrom Slava of Vrbanja you have heard.

IVICA: Poznato mi da si jako mlada,al' ja koncert pisao bih sada.Naša mladost najljepše je doba,a ljubav je zato da se proba.Da me minu mladenačke želje,hajde, Slava, pruži mi veselje.

Ivica: I know that you're still very young, it's true,but I would write the concert now with you.Our youth, it is the loveliest time of all,and love is there for trying, after all.So that my youthful longings may be through,come, Slava, give me joy, I beg of you!

SLAVA: Kad baš hoćeš dragi moj Iveto,ti navrati na babinje ljeto.Ali nemoj skakat prek' tarabe,da ne laju čuke, a ni babe.Tiho moraš kroz vratanca proći,koncert ćemo pisati u noći.Malo pazi ti na moje skute,da nas ljudi loše ne osude.Dođi, Ivo, kada ti se hoće,bit ću tvoje zabranjeno voće.

Slava: Since you're so keen on it, Iveto dear,drop by when the old wives' summer's here.But don't go leaping over the fence, I say,lest dogs and old wives both start barking away.You'll have to slip in quietly through the gate;we'll write our concert in the night, till late.Take care a little of my skirts, and seethat people don't speak ill of you and me.Come, Ivo, come whenever you feel so —I'll be your own forbidden fruit, you know.

IVICA: Da ti spasim i dušu i tijelo,sve ću grijehe preuzet smjelo.Ti pripremi rakiju i kavu,ljubit ću usta, grudi, glavu.I postelju ugrij, dušo, meku,da nam sati u koncertu teku.

Ivica: To save your soul and body, both, my own,I'll boldly take on every sin alone.Get out the rakija and coffee, then;I'll kiss your mouth, your breasts, your head again.And warm the soft bed, darling, for us two,so that our hours may flow in concert, too.

Gospođa Slavica Butorac iz Vrbanje, napisala je knjigu pod naslovom: „KONCERT U KREVETU“. 09. 11. 2003. bio sam na pjesničkom druženju u Drenovcima. Došla mi je ideja da napišem I. strofu ove pjesme, koju sam joj pročitao. 01.12.2003. gospođa Slavica dala mi je II. strofu kao odgovor. 12. 03. 2004. napisao sam ostale tri strofe i završio pjesmu.

Mrs Slavica Butorac of Vrbanja wrote a book titled "KONCERT U KREVETU" ("Concert in Bed"). On November 9, 2003, I was at a poets' gathering in Drenovci. I had the idea of writing the first stanza of this poem, which I read to her. On December 1, 2003, Mrs Slavica gave me the second stanza as a reply. On March 12, 2004, I wrote the remaining three stanzas and finished the poem.

Valpovo, 12. 03. 2004.

Valpovo, March 12, 2004

Translator's note. A playful exchange with the poet Slavica Butorac, whose book is called "Koncert u krevetu" (Concert in Bed); she wrote the second stanza herself. "Old wives' summer" (babinje ljeto) is the Croatian Indian summer.
