Obiteljski kutak

Sretan ti rođendan, Karla!

Family corner

Happy Birthday, Karla!

Draga moja Karličice,mila moja unučice,djeda tebi pjesme piše,jer te voli ponajviše!

My dear Karla, little girl,my sweet granddaughter, my pearl,Grandpa writes you poems to sing,for he loves you more than anything!

Svakog dana, svaku veče',imala baš uvijek sreće,nek' ti život divno tečei mirisno cvalo cvijeće!

Every day and every night,may good fortune hold you tight,may your life flow sweet and light,fragrant flowers blooming bright!

Rođendan ti žele sada:mama, tata, baka Kata!Od srca ti želju pruža:djeda Željko, baka Ruža!

Birthday wishes, here they come:Grandma Kata, Dad and Mum!Heartfelt wishes, sent to you:Grandpa Željko, Grandma Ruža too!

Još nas ima, to se zna:tu je Denis i Suzana,pa djed Ivo, baka Jela,njihova je ista želja!

There are more of us, it's true:here's Suzana, Denis too,Grandpa Ivo, Grandma Jela, theywish the very same today!

Poklone ti lijepe dajui Rođendan čestitaju!

Lovely gifts they give to youand wish Happy Birthday too!

DRAGOJ KARLI SEMIALJACSRETAN TREĆI ROĐENDAN,NAJDRAŽI JOJ SREĆU ŽELE,TO JE NJIHOV DRAGI SAN!

TO DEAR KARLA SEMIALJAC,HAPPY THIRD BIRTHDAY, HURRAY!THOSE WHO LOVE HER WISH HER JOY:THAT'S THEIR DEAREST DREAM TODAY!

Valpovo, 11. 05. 2013.

Valpovo, May 11, 2013
