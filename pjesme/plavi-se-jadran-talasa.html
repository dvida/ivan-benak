Protestne i društvene

Plavi se Jadran talasa

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

The Blue Adriatic Sways

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nekada se pjevat znalo:Plavi se Jadran talasa!Na toplome ljetnom suncu,radnička odmara se klasa!

In the old days people sang:"The blue Adriatic sways!"In the warm sun of the summer,the working class takes holidays!

Sa parama tog radnika,izgrađeni su hoteli,igrališta, divne plažei sve što ti srce želi.

With the money of that workerthe hotels were built, and more:playing fields and lovely beaches,all the heart is longing for.

Tko devize sobom nosi,Jadranom se plavim kiti,a naš radnik u snovima,može more samo sniti.

Those who carry foreign moneydeck themselves in Adriatic blue,while our worker, in his dreams,only dreams the sea — that's true.

Na TV-u sada gleda,po Jadranu stranac skita,kada će na more moći,u duši se radnik pita.

Now he watches on TVforeigners roam the Adriatic;"When will I get to the sea?"asks the worker, in his soul.

Ni penzići sad ne znaju,je li se još Jadran plavi?Eh, da mogu opet tamo,to bi bio život pravi.

Pensioners no longer knowif the Adriatic's blue.Oh, if they could go back there —that would be the life, it's true!

Ljetno sunce morem žeže.Hoteli su stalno puni,a na plaži dupe griju,samo stranci i tajkuni.

Summer sun scorches the sea,the hotels have no empty rooms,and warming bums upon the beachare only foreigners and tycoons.

Valpovo, 05. 07. 1985.

Valpovo, July 5, 1985

Translator's note. The opening quotes an old song about workers' seaside holidays; by the time of the poem the coast had become too dear for the people who built it.
