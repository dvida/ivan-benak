Occasional verse and dedications

To a Friend!

Da vam budu ljepši sati,a dosada da se skrati,da nestane dnevnih briga,evo Vama moja knjiga,pa ju lijepo pročitajte,veselja si srcu dajtei snivali divne snove,želi autor knjige ove.

So your hours may pass more sweetlyand your boredom end completely,so your daily cares may go,here's my book, which I bestow;read it through with care and leisure,give your heart some joy and pleasure,and may lovely dreams come true,as this book's author wishes you.

Slične pjesme: O. Š. Bartola Kašića, Zagreb, Za Osnovnu školu Vladimir Nazor, Komletinci
