Poštovani prijatelju Željko

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Friend Željko

Telefon je zazvonio,predivni se glas javio,imenom se predstavio,da bih znao tko je bio.

Once the telephone rang out;a delightful voice came through,introduced itself by nameso I'd know just who was who.

Oprostite gospon' Ivo,da bismo se upoznali,mislim da vam nije krivo,ako bi za mene znali.

Please excuse me, Mister Ivo,so that we can meet, you see —I believe you wouldn't mind, though,if you came to know of me.

I čovjek se predstavio,a meni je drago bilo.On mi pjesmu izvodioi zbog tog sam sretan bio.

And the man then told his name,and I was glad as glad could be;he had sung a song of mine,which brought happiness to me.

Witovsky se Željko zove,divno moju pjesmu poje,ostvario moje snove,hvala mu od strane moje.

Željko Witovsky's his name,and he sings my song so fine;he has made my dreams come true,so he has these thanks of mine.

Ova moja knjiga mila,što je meni radost dalai ona će sretna biti,kad mu bude rekla: hvala!

This dear book of mine, as well,that has given joy to me,will be happy, too, to tellhim its thanks most gratefully!

23. 05. 2014.

May 23, 2014
