Love poems

Dreams of Longing

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Želim da mi snovi teku,u noć tihu, toplu, meku.Znaš li što bih htio sniti?To da mi je s tobom biti.

How I wish my dreams would flowthrough the night, soft, warm and low.Do you know what I'd dream of?Just to be with you, my love.

Da me kosa tvoja bujnai ustašca slatka rujna,ljube, dušo, i miluju,dok na nebu zvijezde siju.

That your hair, so lush and thick,and your little lips, sweet, red,kiss me, darling, and caress,while the stars shine overhead.

Tako sanjam svake noći,kada ćeš mi, draga, doći,da ti ljubim snene oči,usred tihe, tamne noći.

So I dream each night of whenyou will come, my darling; thenI would kiss your drowsy eyesin the quiet, darkened night.

A kada se zora javii osvane danak plavi,da ti ljubim medna usta,to je moja želja pusta.

And when dawn is on its way,and there breaks the dear blue day,then to kiss your honeyed mouth —that's my idle wish, no doubt.

Valpovo, 17. 09. 1975.

Valpovo, September 17, 1975
