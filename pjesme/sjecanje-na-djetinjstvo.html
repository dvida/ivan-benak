Memories and reflections

Remembering My Childhood

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Djetinjstvo moje bolno mi prisjelo.Svakoga sam dana bos po trnju hodo.Na meni je bilo skrpljeno odijelo,dok sam tuđe krave po strnjaku vodo.

My childhood stayed with me, a bitter pain.Each day I walked across the thorns, feet bare;patched-up clothes were all I had to wear,while leading others' cows across the stubble plain.

I sad kad se sjetim srce me zazebe.Na svakom koraku, bijedu sam susreto.Nikada se nisam stidio sam sebe,jer poštenje meni bilo je presveto.

And even now, when I recall it, my heart runs cold.At every step, it was misery I'd see;but I was never once ashamed — not me —for honesty was the most sacred thing I'd hold.

Koru suhe prove svaki dan sam grizo.Da se može bolje, nisam mog'o znati,ipak nisam nikom u životu gmiz'o.Tako me je moja naučila mati.

Each day I'd gnaw a crust of dry cornbread;that one could live much better, I could not know;yet never in my life have I crawled to anyone —that's how my mother taught me, long ago.

Valpovo, 16. 03. 1976.

Valpovo, March 16, 1976
