Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Hvala gradu Valpovu

Occasional verse and dedications

Thank You, Town of Valpovo

„Tu mi žive predci od davnina,u pitomom kraju meni dragom,u ljepoti slavonskih ravnina,u Valpovu u gradiću blagom.“

“Here since the olden days my forebears dwell,within a gentle land I hold so dear,amid the beauty of Slavonia's plains,in Valpovo, the kindly town, right here.”

Hvala tebi moj voljeni grade,što si moja djela prepoznao.Ti si meni, svome građaninu,prijateljsku pomoć ukazao.

Thank you, my own beloved town, I say,for recognising all the works I've penned.To me, your citizen, you've gladly shownthe helping hand that's given by a friend.

Ja ti dajem svoje obećanjei ljepoti ja se tvojoj divim,voljet ću te dok mi snage trajei cijeniti sve dokle ja živim.

I give to you my promise, here and now,and at your beauty I will marvel still:I'll love you for as long as strength holds out,and prize you all my days, and always will.

Ja Leona Žulja mnogo cijenim,topli pozdrav od srca mu želim.Moja djela on je prepoznao,svu dobrotu srcem darovao.

Leon Žulj I hold in high esteem;warm greetings from my heart I send to him.He recognised the works I wrote, and hegave all his kindness from the heart to me.

Valpovo, 14. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 14, 2010

Translator's note. Written for his book Stihom kroz život (2010) to thank the town and its mayor, Leon Žulj, for their support. The first stanza is set in quotation marks in the original.
