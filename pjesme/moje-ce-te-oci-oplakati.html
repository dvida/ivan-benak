Love poems

My Eyes Will Weep for You!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ne provodi uzaludno mladost!Ne ljubi me, voljena djevojko!Život tužan, a rijetka je radost.Srce mi je kao pelin gorko.

Don't spend your youth in vain, don't waste it so!Don't love me, darling girl whom I adore!For life is sad, and joy is rare, you know.My heart is bitter wormwood to the core.

Zaboravi sve trenutke sreće,kad smo, mila, nekad sretni bilii poljupce i mirisno cvijeće,oko vrata dok smo ruke vili.

Forget each moment of the joy we knew,when, darling, we were happy long ago,the kisses, and the fragrant flowers too,when round each other's necks our arms would go.

Zaboravi ono naše mjesto,gdje smo nekad sreću rasipali,gdje smo ljubav provodili čestoi ljepotu danu darivali.

Forget about that place, that place of ours,where once we used to scatter joy away,where often we would pass our loving hoursand made a gift of beauty to the day.

Za svu sreću i tvoje poljupce.Za svu ljubav, koju si mi dala,za sve suze, moje radosnice,riječ je mala da ti kažem hvala.

For all the joy we had, and all your kisses,for all the love that you have given me,for all the tears, my tears of happiness,"thank you" is much too small a word from me.

Zato pođi, ja ti želim zbogom.Moje će te oči oplakati.U mislima ja ću biti s tobom.Što ja nisam, drugi će ti dati.

So go, then; I will wish you now farewell.My eyes will weep for you, as they must do.In all my thoughts I'll be with you as well.What I did not, another will give you.

Valpovo, 10. 02. 1975.

Valpovo, February 10, 1975
