Šokica in Song

Šokicu sam napisao davno,još je moja mladost divno cvala,s njom sam često nastupao javno,svu ljepotu Šokica mi dala.

I wrote my "Šokica" long, long ago,while my youth was blossoming so fine;with her I often played at many a show;all her loveliness the Šokica made mine.

Na festival glazbe Pitomače,da se pjeva stigla je Šokica.Vrjednije to se sada shvaćai prati ju sa tambure žica.

To the Pitomača music festivalŠokica has come, there to be sung;now its worth is better seen by all,and tambura strings go with her song.

Tu je pjesmu uglazbio Tuca,a Witovsky sad Šokicu pjeva.Od sreće mi srce snažno kuca,a milina u grudima sijeva.

Tuca is the one who set the song,and Witovsky's singing Šokica today;my heart for happiness is beating strong,and through my breast sweet lightning makes its way.

Valpovo, 07. 06. 2014.

Valpovo, June 7, 2014

Translator's note. "Šokica" was one of his early poems. Set to music by Tuca, it was sung by Witovsky at the music festival in Pitomača.
