Umirovljenički jadi

Predsjedništvu HSU-a Valpovo

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

To the Presidency of HSU Valpovo

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja vam želim sve najbolje,ostvarenje vaših snova,sve probleme koje ima,nek' skupština riješi ova.

I wish you the very best,may your dreams at last come true;every problem that you have,may this Assembly see it through.

A uspjehe predsjedništvui ostalim članovima,za uspješni rad skupštini,ja iskreno želim svima.

And success to the Presidencyand the other members too;to the Assembly, fruitful work:this I truly wish for you.

Čestitam za dosadašnji,dobar uspjeh vašeg rada.Još revnija neka bude,u svom radu nova Vlada.

I congratulate you onthe good work you've done so far.May the new Government be keenerstill at work than now they are.

Za uspjehe naše stranke,u toj prošloj godinici.Svaki ponos za taj uspjeh:Predsjednici je Pavici!

For our party's fine successesin the little year now through,all the pride for this achievementis President Pavica's due!

I prođoše trinaest ljeta,od osnutka ove stranke,osnivač je Jukić bio,što je stranku oformio.

Thirteen summers have gone bysince the party's founding day;Jukić, founder, was the oneby whom the party was begun.

Godine su prolazile,predsjednici se mijenjali.Abramić je tad Pavici,pred'o mandat predsjednici.

Years went by, as years will do,presidents would come and go;then Abramić, from the chair,made Pavica president there.

A Pavica više ljeta,našu stranku spretno vodi,što njezina ruka takne,sve procvjeta, dobro rodi.

And for summers now Pavicadeftly leads us, as we see:everything her hand has touchedblossoms, fruitful as can be.

U suradnju s HDZ-om,izbornu kampanju vodi.Ko' dogradonačelnici,vjerujte mi, to joj godi.

Joined with HDZ, she ledthe election campaign through;now, as deputy mayor,trust me, she enjoys it too.

Valpovo, 03. 07. 2009.

Valpovo, July 3, 2009

Translator's note. The HSU is the Croatian Pensioners' Party; this greeting was for the assembly of its Valpovo branch. The HDZ is the Croatian Democratic Union.
