Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Kad Kočije zasviraju

Occasional verse and dedications

When Kočije Strike Up

Tamburaši sad preplićužice koje zvuka daju.To KOČIJE svojim radom,slušateljstvu radost daju.

Now the tambura players weavestrings that ring out with their sound;it's Kočije, with their work,bringing joy to all around.

Ovu pjesmu od KOČIJAslušao bih rado i jakada složno zasviraju„TO JE MOJA SLAVONIJA“!

This one song by KočijeI would also love to hear,when in harmony they play“This Is My Slavonia”!

Kad Martinje kaže svoje,pravo vino je u čaši.A „KOČIJE“ tada poje:„DOBRO VINO TAMBURAŠI“!

When St Martin has his say,real wine's in the glass again;and Kočije sing out then:“Good Wine, You Tambura Men!”

Kad tambure šorom bruje,onu pjesmu rado želim.Od „KOČIJA“ kad se čuje:„DOŠLO VRIJEME DA SE ŽENIM!“

When tamburas hum down the lane,that's the song I long to hear,when from Kočije it rings clear:“Time Has Come for Me to Marry!”

Žice kada kažu svoje,svu ljepotu nama pruže,a „KOČIJE“ pjesmom poje:„DJECA SU NAM KAO RUŽE“!

When the strings have had their say,all their beauty they disclose,and Kočije sing it out:“Our Children Are Like Roses”!

Alaj volim pjesmu pravu,do koje mi puno stalo.„KOČIJE“ nam to pjevaju:„EJ ŽIVOTE STANI MALO“!

How I love a proper song,one that means so much to me!Kočije sing it for us:“Hey There, Life, Stand Still a While!”

A „KOČIJE“ kad svirajudivni program što imaju.Svojim radom Slavonijom,slušateljstvu radost daju.

And when Kočije play on,what a lovely show resounds!Through Slavonia, with their work,they bring joy to all around.

Valpovo, 11.11.2014.

Valpovo, November 11, 2014

Translator's note. Kočije (“Carriages”) is a tamburica band from Nard, near Valpovo; the quoted lines are titles of songs they play. St Martin's Day, 11 November, when the new wine is first drunk, is also the date of the poem.
