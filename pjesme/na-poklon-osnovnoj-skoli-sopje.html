Na poklon Osnovnoj školi Sopje

Occasional verse and dedications

A Gift to the Sopje Primary School

Našoj školi darujem tu knjigu,kao spomen mog djetinjstva znaci,kroz te pjesme ja ostavljam sebe,nek' je vide i budući đaci.

I give this book to our school to keep,a keepsake of my childhood, as a sign;in these poems here I leave myself behind:may future pupils see me in each line.

Vratimo se u prošlost polakoda vidimo gdje nam Sopje stoji.Kad se čulo za njegovo imei od kada svoja ljeta broji.

Let's slowly make our way back to the pastto see where our own Sopje has its place,when first its name was heard by anyone,and since when all its summers it can trace.

Na Podravskoj nizini kraj Drave,pružila se cesta usred polja,spojila je sela toga kraja:s Čađavicom pa do Suhopolja.

On the Podravina plain beside the Dravaa road stretched out across the open fields;it joined the villages of all that region,from Čađavica right to Suhopolje.

Na tom putu što poljem vijugasmjestila se neka sela veća.Sopje vam je ovdje od davnina,spominje se od drugog stoljeća.

Along that road that winds across the fieldssome of the larger villages were placed.Our Sopje has been here since ancient times:back to the second century it's traced.

Lete ljeta, sela se razvila.Sopjani su ljudi vrijedni bili.Tisuću osamsto dvadeset šesteu Sopju su školu sagradili.

The years flew by, and all the villages grew;and Sopje's people worked hard, through and through;in eighteen hundred and twenty-six, in Sopje,the people built themselves a school brand new.

Godine su slavno prolazile,sa djecom je škola bila jaka:čak stotinjak, pa nekad i više,u školi je znalo biti đaka.

The years went by in glory, one by one;with children, then, the school was going strong:a hundred, sometimes more than that, would bethe pupils in the school the whole year long.

Brzo prođe vrijeme našoj školi:sto šezdeset i pet ljeta brojii mnoge je mudre glave dala,u zapisu školskom to postoji!

How quickly time has passed for our school:a hundred sixty-five years it can claim,and many a wise head it has given too;the school's own chronicle records the same!

Još se sjećam svojega djetinjstva,kao dijete u Sopju sam bio.Četrdeset četvrte i pete,u Sopju sam školu završio.

I still remember well my childhood days:in Sopje, as a child, I lived and grew;in nineteen forty-four and forty-fivein Sopje I saw all my schooling through.

Svoj je obol stara škola dala,zato su ju ljudi odstranili.I devetsto devedeset prvemodernu su školu sagradili.

The old school gave its share, as it was due,and so the people took it down; and thenin nineteen hundred ninety-one they builta modern school in Sopje, fresh and new.

Sad redovno nova prima đake,a djeca su sretna i vesela.Sopjana je to ponos i dika,a škola je ukras našeg sela.

Now pupils come there daily to the new one,the children happy, merry on their way;it is the pride and joy of Sopje's people,our village's adornment to this day.

Volio bih da što više ljudipročitaju ovo moje štivo,jer je ovo za voljeno Sopje,napisao pjesnik, Benak Ivo.

I would be glad if many people readthis piece of writing that I've set down here,because for my beloved Sopje, thiswas written by the poet, Benak Ivo.

Valpovo, 20. 03. 2013.

Valpovo, March 20, 2013

Translator's note. Sopje, in the Podravina lowland along the Drava, was his native village. Croatians often give the surname first, as in his signature "Benak Ivo".
