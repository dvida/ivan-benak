Poems for children

The Forest King

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Što je to:u šumi živišumski kralj,ima djece milijun,sva mu djecakapu nose,a samo jenema on.

What is this:in the woods lives he,the forest king,with a million children, see;all his childrenwear a cap,only hehas none of that.

Znam da znate- svaka čast -to su, djeco,žir i hrast:svaki sin mukapu nosi,dok olujihrast prkosi.

I know you know— hats off, good folk —children, it'sthe acorn and the oak:every sonwears a cap, it's true,while the oakstands the storm right through.
