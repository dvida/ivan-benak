Do Not Leave Tonight

Ne odlazi, draga, molim te!Ostani noćas kraj mene,bez ljubavi tvoje, dušo,i moja mladost vene.

Do not leave, my darling, I beg you!Stay tonight with me, I pray;for without your love, my dear one,my youth, too, fades away.

Da utopim bol i tuguu naručju, mila, tvome.Svaki trenutak s tobom,melem je biću mome.

Let me drown my pain and sorrowin your arms, my dear, my own;every moment spent beside youis a balm my soul has known.

Rastanak uvijek je tužan.Samo mi boli stvara.Jer čeznem, draga, za tobom,tuga mi grudi para.

Parting always is so sad,bringing only pain and smart;for I long for you, my darling,sorrow tears my breast apart.

Bez tebe je tako pusto,jer znaj, draga, volim te!Produži sreću za trenutakne odlazi noćas, molim te!

Without you it's all so empty,for, my darling, I adore you!Stretch my joy a moment longer,do not leave tonight, I implore you!

Valpovo, 18. 01. 1974.

Valpovo, January 18, 1974
