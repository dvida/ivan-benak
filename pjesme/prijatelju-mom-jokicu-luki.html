Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend Luka Jokić

Prijatelju i cijenjeni Luka,za SOFIJU OZLOJAĐENU ŽENU,došlo vrijeme da Ti vratim duga.za njen život i sudbinu njenu.

My friend, my Luka, honoured and esteemed,for SOFIA, THE EMBITTERED WOMAN, nowthe time has come to pay you back my debt,for her whole life, her fate — and here is how.

Na predivnoj knjizi Tebi hvala!Ja za dobro, uvijek dobrim plaćam.Od djetinjstva priroda mi dala,za tu knjigu, svojom duga vraćam.

My thanks to you for such a lovely book!For good I always pay with good, it's true.What nature gave me since my childhood years,with my own book I pay my debt to you.

Kao nekad nismo više mladi,a druženja bilo nam što više,i starački nek' nas minu jadi,jer se lakše u životu diše.

We are no longer young, as once we were;so let us meet together all we may,and may the woes of old age pass us by,for life is breathed more easily that way.

Zato piši, a pisat ću i ja,ne bilo nam u životu krivo,jer nam ovaj život tako prija.i to Ti želi: Tvoj prijatelj Ivo!

So you write on, and I will write as well;may life not give us cause for grief or rue,because this life of ours so pleases us —and this is your friend Ivo's wish for you!

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 20, 2010

Translator's note. Luka Jokić was a poet from Đurđenovac; Benak gave him this book in return for Jokić's own, Sofija, ozlojađena žena (Sofia, the Embittered Woman).
