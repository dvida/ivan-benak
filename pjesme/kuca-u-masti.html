A House in My Imagination

U mašti gradim kuću.Samo za nas dvoje,da u njoj ostvarimsve divne snove moje.

In fancy I'm buildinga house for us two,where all my fine dreamsI can make come true.

U jednom malom vrtupunom mirisnog cvijeća,ja tebe želim, draga,jer ti si moja sreća.

In a little gardenwhere fragrant flowers grow,I want you, my darling,for you're my joy, you know.

Ujutro kad se budiši čuješ ptica poj,da budeš samo moja,a ja da budem tvoj.

When you wake in the morningand hear the birds' song,may you be mine onlyand I to you belong.

Valpovo, 14. 02. 1974.

Valpovo, February 14, 1974
