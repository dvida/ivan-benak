Love poems

I Dream of You

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja te sanjam svake tihe noćisvako veče kad se zvijezde pale.Na mom licu ćutim usne tvoje,oko vrata dvije ruke male.

I dream of you through every quiet night,each evening, when the stars are coming out;upon my face I feel your lips, and thenaround my neck two small arms wound about.

To poželjno tijelo usred tame,tvoja glava moje rame traži,dojke tvoje, što mi pogled mame.Usne šapću: ti si mi najdraži.

That body I desire, amid the dark,your head that seeks my shoulder, drawing near,your breasts, that lure my gaze and hold it fast;lips whisper: you're the one I hold most dear.

Ljubljah tvoje usplamtjele oči,privinuh te još jače na grudi.Bijasmo sretni te blažene noćii u zoru kad sunce zarudi.

I kissed your eyes, that were aflame and bright,and pressed you to my breast more tightly yet;how happy we both were that blissful night,and at the dawn, as the sun was glowing red.

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979
