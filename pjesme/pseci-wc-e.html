Humorous poems

The Doggy WC

Ulica je moja slavnanajljepša u gradu,s jedne strane red je kuća,a stoje u hladu.

This street of mine, so famous,is the loveliest in town;along one side the houses standin shade, all up and down.

S druge strane Karašicašto polako teče.Voda često bude mutnapa nosi i smeće.

On the other, Karašicaflowing, slow, its way;often, too, its water's muddy,and bears rubbish away.

Cijelog dana mnogi ljudivode svoje štencepa vam dođe ta ulicakao pseći WC-e.

All day long, so many peoplewalk their puppies here, you see,so this street of ours becomesjust a doggy WC.

Eh, da vidi, što bi reklaBarunica Dora.Svaki stanar ispred kućesokak čistit mora.

Ah, if Baroness Dorasaw it, what she'd say!Each must sweep the sokakby his house each day.

Valpovačku molim Vladu,da ne skidam svece,nek' ulici mojoj dadunaziv: PSEĆI WC-e.

So I beg Valpovo's council,lest I curse the saints, you see:let them give my street the name:THE DOGGY WC.

Valpovo, 17. 04. 2010.

Valpovo, April 17, 2010

Translator's note. His street in Valpovo is named after the composer Dora Pejačević (1885–1923), whom he calls "Baroness Dora"; the Karašica river runs along it.
