Memories and reflections

Gossip

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kakvo je to sad nastalo vrijeme?Svatko može na svakog da viče.Što ja radim, što se koga tiče.Neka svatko nosi svoje breme.

What's this time that has come upon us today?Anyone can shout at anyone.What I do — whose business? Surely none.Let each one bear his own burden, I say.

Ja ne pitam nikoga kud ide.Tko je kada s kim u krčmi pio,ni sa čijom ženom snove snioiako ga moje oči vide.

I don't ask a single soul where he may go,who was drinking with whom at the tavern, and when,nor with whose wife he has been dreaming dreams,though with my own eyes I see him, even so.

Ja vas molim i ljudi i žene,počistite ispred svoga praga,ne tražite sad okolo vraga,Ostavite vi na miru mene.

Men and women, this I beg of you:sweep before your own threshold, clean and clear,don't go hunting for the devil round here,and just leave me in peace — that's all you need do.

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979
