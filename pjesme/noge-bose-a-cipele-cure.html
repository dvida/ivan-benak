Humorous poems

Bare Feet and Leaky Shoes

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Pukla mi je cipela na peti,pa mi stalno rupa na pameti.Rupu gledam, mislim sad je zgoda,pa popravim dok ne uđe voda.

My shoe's split open at the heel, you'll find,and so a hole is always on my mind.I eye the hole and think: now is the dayto fix it, before water finds its way.

Jednog dana oko četir' sata,ja pokucam šusteru na vrata.Al' kad tamo prelijepa Helena,šije, para i klince udara.

One day, at four or so, I made my wayand knocked upon the cobbler's door; but lo,there was the lovely Helena instead,sewing, unpicking, tapping tacks away.

Kakva je to majstorica bila,divna, mila, kao bajna vila.Milo gleda, slatko mi se smije,zar da ona moju rupu šije?

And what a master craftswoman was she:lovely and sweet, a fairy, fair to see.She looks so fondly, smiles so sweet at me —and she's the one to sew my hole? Can't be!

Stigla zima i sniježak je pao,cipele sam na popravak dao.Snaša Kata u kijer me zove,ja ne mogu, jer sam bosih nogu.

Then winter came, the little snowflakes fell;I'd handed in my shoes to mend as well.Then Kata, the young wife, calls me to come round;I can't — my feet are bare upon the ground.

Čekaj Kato, u petak ću doći,dok mi budu gotove cipele.Onda ću se ja obuti moći,pa ću kidnut od ženice Jele.

Wait, Kata, I will come on Friday night,when I have got my shoes back, mended right;then I'll be able to put my shoes on, andslip off from Jela, my little wife, as planned.

Ti pripremi rakiju i kavui postelju ugrij, dušo, meku.Ljubit ću ti usta, grudi, glavu,nek nam sati u ljubavi teku.

Get out the rakija and coffee, then,and warm the soft bed, darling, for us two;I'll kiss your mouth, your breasts, your head again,so that our hours in love may flow right through.

A kad bude zora zakucala,ljubit ću ti na rastanku oči.Ej cipele, nosite me brzo,dok još ima na vidiku noći!

And when the dawn comes knocking at the door,I'll kiss your eyes as we two say goodbye.Hey, shoes of mine, now carry me off fast,while there is still some night left in the sky!

Valpovo, 27. 12. 1996.

Valpovo, December 27, 1996
