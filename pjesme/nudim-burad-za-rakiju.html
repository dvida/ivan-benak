Radio burza

Nudim burad za rakiju

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Rakija Barrels on Offer

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Došle godine i na moja vrata.Puno se toga iz života briše.Zato ja ovaj oglas sad dajem,jer mnoge stvari ne trebam više.

The years have come to knock at my door too;so much of life is wiped clean off the slate.That's why I'm putting out this ad of mine:I've no more need of many things of late.

Prodajem rakijsko, skoro novo,od sedamdeset litara burei jedno manje, mlađeg mu brata,od šesnaest litara, burad ne cure.

For rakija I'm selling, nearly new,a barrel, seventy litres, built just right,and one that's smaller, its young brother, too,of sixteen litres; both are watertight.

Ta su vam burad od slavonskog duda,a obruči kao da su novi.Tko mu veći sadržaj pije,taj blažen u sedmo nebo plovi.

These barrels are of Slavonian mulberry,the hoops as good as new — a lovely sight;and he who drinks the more of what's insidesails off in bliss to seventh heaven's height.

Penzija mala, loše se piše.Burad prodajem, treba mi lova,jer prije neg' dođu jesenje kiše,da popravim starog na kući krova.

My pension's small, the writing's on the wall.I'm selling barrels, cash is what I need,because, before the autumn rains can fall,I've got to fix the old roof, with all speed.

Ako mi sklona bude Fortuna,te bačvice fine ja ću dati,za tristo i četrdeset kuna,na telefon ovaj možete zvati.

If only I'm in favour with Fortuna,these fine small casks I'll give you for your ownfor just three hundred and forty kuna;and you can ring me on this telephone.

Ako me kupac nazove pravi.Na: 6 5 2 8 2 1na taj telefon neka se javi.

If a true buyer's out there, let him tryat: 6 5 2 8 2 1,and ring that phone to give me his reply.

Valpovo, 15. 04. 2005.

Valpovo, April 15, 2005

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. Mulberry wood is prized in Slavonia for rakija barrels; "seventh heaven" is the same idiom in Croatian.
