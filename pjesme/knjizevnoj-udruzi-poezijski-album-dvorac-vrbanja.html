Književnoj udruzi „Poezijski album“ dvorac Vrbanja

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Poezijski Album Literary Society, Vrbanja Manor

POŠTOVANI PJESNICI

Esteemed Poets

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

Warm greetings to you all I wish to send,and your assembly gladdens me no end.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,što Ljubica, tajnica sad kaže,ja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear of your results, so fine and good,the good success, the course your work will steer,which Ljubica, the secretary, reports;and I congratulate you all right here.

Sve čestitke Wojnowski Ljubici:"Sakupljene latice", od cvijećai još jedna: "Obala slobode",te su knjige vašem društvu sreća.

To Ljubica Wojnowski, all my praisefor “Gathered Petals”, plucked from flowers bright,and one more book, “The Shore of Freedom”, too:these books are your society's delight.

I sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the things that you've accomplished here,by all your work within this passing year,I wish you even more success to come,achieved in the new year that's drawing near.

Sve uspjehe što ste ostvarilii za djela članstvu zahvalili,rukovodstvo novo izabrali,skupštinu ste ovu održali.

You've told of all successes you have won,and thanked the members for the work they've done;new leadership you've chosen for the year,and held this general assembly here.

A skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,predsjednika neka izabere,da vas vodi u budućem radu.

And may this meeting, at this hour, stand bythe present Government, and back it too,and choose a president, who then will leadthe future work that lies ahead for you.

Topli pozdrav Petru Pejakiću.On je ovo društvo osnovao,sa uspjehom sve je odradio,svu ljepotu ovom društvu dao.

Warm greetings go to Petar Pejakić;it's he who founded it, right from the start,who carried everything to good success,and gave the group its beauty, every part.

Uskoro nam stiže Novo Ljeto,što nam nosi neka bude sretno!Svu godinu u veselju bili,svoje snove sretno ostvarili.

Soon the New Year comes to us once more;may it be happy, all it holds in store!May all the year be merry through and through,and may your dreams come happily true for you.

Put života bio vam što duži,vašoj sreći i zdravlju nek' služi.Cijelog ljeta nek' vam bude živo.To vam želi pjesnik Benak Ivo!

Long may the road of life before you run,and serve your health and happiness, each one;and may the whole year long be lively too:this, poet Benak Ivo wishes you!

Valpovo, 14. 12. 2011.

Valpovo, December 14, 2011

Translator's note. A greeting to the annual assembly of the literary society Poezijski album (“Poetry Album”); Ljubica Wojnowski was its secretary and Petar Pejakić its founder. He calls the society's committee its “Government”, and uses the old phrase Novo Ljeto for the New Year.
