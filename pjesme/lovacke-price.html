Hunters' Tales

Jednom se ribiču,zvanom brza ruka,u šumi na panjuuhvatila štuka.

Once a fishermanwhom they called Quick Handcaught a pike in the forest,on a stump, on dry land!

Na pašnjaku nekom- oh, tako mu groma!Upecao malogod sto kila soma!

On a pasture somewhere— by thunder, he swore! —he landed a small catfish,a hundred kilos, no more!

I još meni veli- to je bilo lani -da po Dravi plivajupečeni fazani.

And he tells me, too— that was last year, mind —that pheasants swim the Drava,the roasted kind!

Na potoku nekomkobac ribu lovi,pilićima dajeda ih bolje tovi.

By some brook, a hawkgoes fishing, they say,and feeds the chicks with fishto fatten them that way.

I lija se snašlarepom vodu tuca.Kada koka pije,da joj se ne štuca.

The fox found a trick:with her tail she stirs the drink,so when the hen sips it,she won't hiccup, I think.

Janješce je malo- oh, tako mu muka! -Papčićima svojimzagrlilo vuka!

A little lamb— oh, what a plight! —with its tiny hooveshugged the wolf tight!

A vuk mu se smiješii nježno mu tepa,miluje ga šapomod glave do repa.

And the wolf smiles at it,coos to it, so dear,strokes it with his pawfrom its head to its rear.

Kad sam došo kući,pričala mi djeca:iz vrapčjega gnijezdaizvukla su zeca.

When I got back home,the children told me, I swear:out of a sparrow's nestthey had pulled a hare!

Mislite li djecoda je bilo tako?Meni ove pričena lovačke liče!

Children, do you thinkit really happened so?To me these stories soundlike hunters' tales, you know!

Translator's note. In Croatian a "hunter's tale" is a tall tale, like an English "fish story". Quick Hand is the fisherman's nickname.
