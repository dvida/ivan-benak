Obiteljski kutak

Obitelji Semialjac!

Family corner

To the Semialjac Family!

PRIJO!Da ti budu ljepši sati,a dosada da se skrati.Dobro zdravlje Bog će dati,ja to želim priji Kati,pa druženja i veselja,autorova to je želja.

Prija!May your hours be sweeter spent,and your boredom shorter too;God will give good health to you:that's my wish for Prija Kata;friendship, too, and merriment:that's the author's wish, well meant.

ŽELJKICE!Kada budeš čedo svoje,u naručju milovala,kada ne znaš od dragosti,što bi Karli darovala,draga dužnost nek' je ova:svaku večer prije snova,iz ove knjige čitaj štiva,da ti Karla ljepše sniva.

Željkica!When you hold your child to you,in your arms caressing her,when you don't know, for sheer love,what to give to Karla too,let this be a duty dear:every night, when dreams are near,read the stories in this bookso that Karla's dreams are sweeter.

ZDRAVKO!U životu sretan bio,svoje snove ostvario,iskreno ti želi Ivo,što je pis'o ovo štivo!

Zdravko!May your life be happy too,and may all your dreams come true:Ivo wishes it, sincere,he who wrote the verses here!

Neka vam se sreća javlja,maloj Karli puno zdravlja.Svi u kući sretni bilii vesele snove snili.

May good fortune come to you,little Karla good health too.May all in your house be gladand the merriest dreams be had.

Ne bilo vam nikad krivo,to vam želi: Djeda Ivo!

May you never have to rue:this, Grandpa Ivo wishes you!

Valpovo, 24. 07. 2010.

Valpovo, July 24, 2010

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his book Stihom kroz život. A prija is the mother of one's child's spouse, a relationship Croatian names with its own word.
