Pjesme

Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Rijetko je komu poklonio knjigu bez posvete u stihu. Ovdje su pjesme koje je pisao prijateljima, pjesnicima, udrugama, zborovima i tamburašima, školama, vrtićima, trgovinama i obiteljima, za rođendane, obljetnice i zahvale.

Poems

Occasional verse and dedications

He rarely gave anyone a book without a dedication in verse. Here are the poems he wrote for friends, fellow poets, societies, choirs and tamburica bands, schools, kindergartens, shops and families: for birthdays, anniversaries and words of thanks.
