Umirovljenički jadi

Draga generacijo!

2013.

Pensioners' woes

My Dear Generation!

2013

Sretnu Novu svima vama želimPuno sreće, zdravlja i veseljaDragi moji prijatelji miliS najdražima u ljubavi bili.

A happy New Year to you all I send,much luck, good health, and joy without an end;dear friends of mine, so cherished and so true,live lovingly with those most dear to you.

Pratili vas samo sretni daniNek vam bude bolje nego laniMeđu sobom svi u bratstvu biliSvoje snove sretno ostvarili.

May only happy days be yours, and maythis year be better than the last, I say;among yourselves in brotherhood agree,and may your dreams come true most happily.

Bolji standard donio vam slavuJa vam želim mirovinu pravuA ne ovu jadnu crkavicuTrošili je cijelu godinicu.

May better standards bring you pride and fame;I wish you a real pension worth the name,not this poor pittance: one that you could spendall through the year, from start until the end.

Među ljudstvom neka sloga vladaNe bilo vam staračkoga jadaDa se lakše u životu dišeI ratova da ne bude više.

Among all folk may harmony hold sway,may sorrows of old age not come your way,that we may breathe more easily in life,and let there be no more of war and strife.

Prođe Božić i godina NovaProjurio i Valentin svetiA godine na pleća se nižuJer nam život kao strijela leti.

Now Christmas and the New Year have gone past,Saint Valentine has rushed on by as well;upon our shoulders years are piling up,for like an arrow life goes flying fast.

Svi u zdravlju sreću uživaliGodinicu u ljepoti sniliVedri dani veseli bez radaNe tražite Svetog Petra vrata.

May you in health have happiness, my friends,and dream in beauty till the whole year ends;bright merry days, with work to do no more —and don't go looking for Saint Peter's door.

Put života bio vam što dužiVašem biću neka sretno služiOva želja neka vas veseliTo vam pjesnik Benak Ivo želi.

May your life's road be long as long can be,and serve your being well and happily.This wish of mine, may it bring you good cheer;the poet Benak Ivo sends it here.

Valpovo, 22. 12. 2013.

Valpovo, December 22, 2013

Translator's note. New Year wishes for 2014 to the poet's own generation. "Saint Peter's door" is the gate of heaven: the wish is that they not hurry there.
