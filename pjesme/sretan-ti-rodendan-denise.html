Obiteljski kutak

Sretan ti rođendan Denise

Family corner

Happy Birthday, Denis

Dajemo ti daraovo malo para,to ti daje djedai voljena baka,da bi među namabila ljubav jaka.

We give you a gift,a little bit of dough;it comes from Grandpaand from your dear Grandma, sothat between us allour love may strongly grow.

Valpovo, 21. 09. 2006.

Valpovo, September 21, 2006
