Family corner

A Poem for My Son!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Živio sam s tobom sretno svoju mladost.Spavao si, sine, na mojim rukama.U igri sa tobom dijelio sam radost.Za bol tvoju ja sam bio na mukama.

I lived my youth with you in happiness.You slept, my son, within my arms, at rest.In playing with you, I would share your gladness;when you were hurt, I suffered, sore distressed.

Ti još i ne znaš, kako bi i znao,koliko te moje biće silno voli,kada budeš svoje čedo sinom zvaotek ćeš onda znati za strepnje i boli.

You do not know yet (how could you have known)how mightily my being loves you so;but when you call a son of yours your own,then only will you know the fear and woe.

Sa tobom je bilo mnogo sretnih dana,a još više strepnjom probdivenih noći.Zato nikad, sine, nikad ne dozvoli,da ranjenom dušom u grob moram poći.

With you there have been many happy days,and more still, nights kept sleepless in my fear.So never, son, oh never let it bethat with a wounded soul I'm carried to my bier.

Valpovo, 15. 06. 1974.

Valpovo, June 15, 1974
