Radio burza

Za prodaju imam naftaricu

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Oil Heater for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ovim putem objavljujem svima,da kod mene naftarica ima.Odostraga, sa strane i sprijeda,vjerujte mi, k'o nova izgleda.Ona ima rezervoar sa strane,da ga puniš u te zimske dane.Zapremine od deset litara,u njoj nafta polako izgara.Na lož ulje ona grije sobu,ako treba dat ću je na probu.A lož ulja na benzinskoj ima,nek' se grije sve dok traje zima.Ne daj Bože, da se tko smrzava,dok pećica u garaži spava.Ako sklona bude mi fortuna,nju ću dati za četir'sto kuna.Sa prijevozom i montažom priđe,snašicama koje se ne stide.Jeftinije ovo nećeš naći,vjeruj, brate, ni u samoj Mađi.Da se griješ u zimi sav sretan,nazovi na broj:652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan.

I hereby make it known to all of youthat I have got an oil heater for you.From back and side and front, from every view,believe me, it looks just as good as new.There is a fuel tank fitted at the side,to fill up when the winter days arrive.It holds ten litres — that's its capacity;the oil burns slowly in it, as you'll see.It heats the room on heating oil with ease;if need be, you can try it, if you please.The petrol station's got the oil you need,so stay warm all the winter long, indeed.Now God forbid that anyone should freezewhile in the garage this small stove sleeps at ease.If Fortune smiles and helps me, as she may,four hundred kuna is the price you'll pay.With transport and installation, too, I'll callon young wives who are not too shy at all.You'll never find it cheaper, brother, no —not even over in Hungary, I know.To keep you warm and happy, everyone,ring up the number:652 – eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 4.09.2000.

Valpovo, September 4, 2000

Translator's note. A verse ad for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. Hungary, just over the border, was where people went for cheap goods. The phone number was his own.
