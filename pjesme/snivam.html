Love poems

I Dream

Snivam, da se nas dvojezagrljeni, nježno ljubimo.Snivam, da se nas dvojesretni u snovima budimo.

I dream that the two of us, claspedin each other's arms, softly kiss;I dream that the two of us wakenin our dreams, in happy bliss.

A kada se raspline noći zlatna zora zarudi,tvog srca tihi kucaj,mene iz snova budi.

And when the night melts awayand the golden dawn glows red,then your heart, so softly beating,wakes me from the dreams I've had.

Tada uz zračak sunca,vrele mi usne nudiš.U strasnom naručju tvome,cijelim me bićem ljubiš.

Then, with a little ray of sun,you offer me your burning lips,and in your passionate embraceyou kiss me with all your being.

Valpovo, 03. 05. 1985.

Valpovo, May 3, 1985
