Radio burza

Televizor „Gorenje“ prodajem

Radio market

Gorenje TV for Sale

Televizor imam dobar,GORENJE je njemu ime,tri mjeseca on ne radi,ne služim se više njime.

I have got a good TV,GORENJE is its name, you see;three months now it hasn't worked,so it's no more use to me.

Ekran mu je srednje velik,sedamdeset centi ima,Sahranjen je ANALOGNI,pa mu nešto sad ne štima.

Its screen is of middle size,seventy centimetres wide;ANALOGUE's been laid to rest,so there's something wrong inside.

Zato nema slike više,samo sniježak pada.DIGITALNI aparatić,treba njemu sada.

So there's no more picture now,only snowflakes coming down;what it needs now is a littleDIGITAL box of its own.

Imam drugi televizor,novi, pravi DIGITALAC.Za ovoga sada tražim:skromni kunski MINIMALAC.

I've another television,new, a proper DIGITAL one.For this old one I am askingjust a modest kuna MINIMUM.

Ako netko želi dobar,televizor da si kupi,nek' na ovaj moj telefon,svakog dana on pristupi.

If there's anyone who'd likea good TV set to buy,on this telephone of mineany day let him apply.

Šesto pedeset i dva,osamsto dvadeset i jedan.To je moj telefon vrijedan.

Six hundred and fifty-two,eight hundred and twenty-one:that's my phone, a worthy one.

Ozbiljan je ovo oglas,ovo nije radi rime.Prodao bih televizor,stvarno ne znam što ću s njime.

This advert is meant in earnest,not for rhyming's sake, it's true.I would sell this television:what's a man with it to do?

Valpovo, 09. 10. 2010.

Valpovo, October 9, 2010

Translator's note. One of his verse ads for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio, written in 2010, when Croatia switched off analogue television. The phone number was his own.
