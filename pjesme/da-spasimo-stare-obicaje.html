Domoljubne i zavičajne

Da spasimo stare običaje

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Let Us Save the Old Customs

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nekada se kosom zrelo žito želo,sa srpom kupilo, u snoplje vezalo,u krstine slagalo i kući vozilo,potom vršalicom žito ovršilo.

Once they would mow the ripened wheat with scythes,and gather it with sickles, bind the sheaves,stack them in shocks, and cart them home by wain,and then the thresher threshed out all the grain.

Ujesen bi ljudi kukuruze brali,uz vino i pjesmu doma komušali.Skupila se moba, veseli su bili,a dečki bi cure doma otpratili.

In autumn people used to pick the maizeand husk it, back at home, with wine and song;a moba gathered, merry, one and all,and then the lads would walk the girls back home.

Kada zima dođe, bilo je veselo,skupljale se cure s večeri na prelo.Bećari bi došli curama na sijelo,veselo je zimi bilo svako selo.

When winter came, it was a merry season:of evenings girls would gather at the prelo,the bećars came to join them at the sijelo,and every village, winter-long, was merry.

S tehnikom su došla moderna vremena.Kombajn kosi, vrši i u silos sprema,kukuruz ovrši komušanja nema,televizor sada zamjenjuje sijela.

Now with machinery came modern days:the combine mows and threshes, fills the silo,it shells the corn, and there's no husking now,and television takes the place of sijela.

Sjećamo se često starih običaja,pa njima u spomen podižemo slavu,tradiciju časnu vjekovima znanu,da spasimo prošlost vječnom zaboravu.

We often call to mind the customs of oldand raise their glory high in memory,a noble tradition known for centuries,to save the past from endless oblivion's hold.

Valpovo, 20.07.2008.

Valpovo, July 20, 2008

Translator's note. A moba was a communal work party, where neighbours helped each other with the harvest or the maize husking; the prelo was a winter evening gathering where girls spun and the young men came calling.
