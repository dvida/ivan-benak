Love poems

Now We Are Strangers!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Zar sam izgubio čulo vida očnog,kad te više nigdje ja sresti ne mogu?Ili povjerenje, Zeusa, boga moćnog?Ili sam odigr'o ja svoju ulogu?

Have I lost the sense of sight my eyes once knew,that nowhere now can I meet you face to face?Or the trust of Zeus, the mighty god, lost too?Or have I played my role, and lost my place?

Možda si nestala sa ovoga svijeta?Na oblaku sreće negdje gore letiš.Šetaš se po vrtu svemirskoga cvijeta,a mene se, draga, ne možeš da sjetiš!

Could it be you've vanished from this world of ours?On a cloud of happiness you fly somewhere above,strolling through the garden of the cosmic flowers,and me you can't remember any more, my love!

Ili su ti bozi dali moćna krila,pa prelijećeš mjesta gdje smo sretni bili.Ili si postala bajna, gorska vila?Il' poljupci moji nisu više mili?

Or did the gods bestow on you such mighty wingsthat you fly over places where we once were glad?Or have you now become a lovely mountain fairy?Or have my kisses lost the dearness that they had?

Tvoje su mi riječi sada prazna jeka,uz poljupce slatke kad si šaputala.Osjećam tu patnju sada iz dalekaiako si sebe meni poklanjala.

Your words are nothing but an empty echo now,those words you whispered with sweet kisses in my ear.I feel this suffering now as from afar, somehow,although you gave yourself to me, my dear.

Ne, ti nisi, draga, svemoguće biće.Iz ljubavnih snova sa drugim se budiši dok moje srce sada bolno kliče,usne svoje drugom uz drag osmijeh nudiš.

No, darling, you are no almighty being, no:from dreams of love you wake beside someone new,and while my heart cries out in pain and suffers so,you offer him your lips, and a sweet smile too.

Unaprijed sam znao kraj toj našoj srećii te crne noći da nisi ni došla,ja bih opet znao što ćeš meni reći,jer je naša ljubav za tebe već prošla.

I knew ahead of time how our happiness would end,and even had you never come that pitch-black night,I would have known what you would tell me in the end,because, for you, our love was over, finished quite.

Zar da kunem sudbu što sam te ljubio?Meni ova patnja bolnu dušu para.Na grudima tvojim ja sam snove snio.A sada smo stranci, to mi boli stvara.

Shall I then curse my fate for having loved you so?This anguish tears my aching soul in two.Upon your breast I dreamed my dreams, not long ago;and now we're strangers — that hurts me through and through.

Valpovo, 18. 10. 1974.

Valpovo, October 18, 1974

Translator's note. Zeus, the mighty god, is the king of the Greek gods; the "mountain fairy" is the vila of South Slavic folk song.
