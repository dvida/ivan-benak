Poštovani gospodine Bojane

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Mr Bojan

Sretnu Novu sada vama želim,puno sreće, zdravlja i veselja.Dv'je tisuće četrnaesto ljeto,nek Vam bude uspješno i sretno!

A Happy New Year now I wish to you,much luck and health and joy the whole year through.The year two thousand fourteen — may it besuccessful for you, happy, trouble-free!

Pratili Vas samo sretni dani,nek Vam bude bolje nego lani.Na svom radu divno se družili,svoje snove sretno ostvarili!

May only happy days now follow you,and may this year beat last year's, through and through.At work may you get on so splendidly,and see your dreams come true most happily!

Vi u zdravlju sreću uživali,s najdražima u ljepoti snili,cijelo ljeto u veselju bilii ugodne dane provodili.

In health may you enjoy your happiness,with those most dear, dream on in loveliness;may you be full of gladness all year round,and may your days in pleasantness abound.

Put života bio Vam što duži,Vašem biću neka sretno služi,Ovu želju sretno proživjeli:to Vam pjesnik Benak Ivo želi!

May your life's road be long as it can be,and may it serve you well and happily.Live out this wish in happiness, and knowit is the wish of poet Benak Ivo!

U Valpovu dva'jst osmog prosinca,dv'je tisuće trina'jstoga ljeta.Sa čestitkom ovom ja Vam želim:punim zdravljem da Vam život cvjeta!

In Valpovo, December twenty-eight,the year two thousand thirteen, is the date.With these good wishes, this is what I say:may your life bloom in full health every day!
