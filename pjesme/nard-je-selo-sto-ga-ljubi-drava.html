Domoljubne i zavičajne

Nard je selo što ga ljubi Drava

Homeland and native region

Nard Is the Village the Drava Kisses

Na istoku naše Valpovštine,al' od Šaga kratke je daljine.Divno mjesto tu postoji sada,to je selo sadašnjega Narda.

Out to the east of our Valpovo land,though from Šag it's a short way, close at hand,a lovely place is standing there today:it is the present village, Nard, I say.

Dok je Drava svojim tokom tekla,u potaji ona nam je rekla:da protječe kraj dva mala sela,mjesto Midlak pa i Nova Skela.

The Drava, flowing onward in her bed,in secret told us this, and this she said:that she flowed past two villages, both small:Midlak and Nova Skela, as they're called.

Od Turaka ljudi su bježali,sa tugom su mjesta napuštali.Osamnaestog vijeka se vratili,novo selo NARD su izgradili.

The people fled before the Turks; they wentand left their homes in sorrow and lament.In the seventeen-hundreds they came home,and built the new village, Nard, to be their own.

Da bi ljudi ovdje opstojali,sa ribarstvom oni se bavili,a kasnije sa osobnim darom,bavili se ljudi sa agrarom.

To make a living here and stay alive,by fishing, first, the people would survive;and later, with a gift that was their own,the people turned to farming, it is known.

I crkvu su sagradili, znajte,tisuću osamsto i petnaeste.Svetoj Kati oltar časno dali,da bi svoju vjeru održali.

They built a church as well, and you should know:in eighteen hundred fifteen it was so.They gave Saint Catherine the altar, with all grace,so that their faith might always keep its place.

I djeca se namnožila, znajte,tisuću devetsto i četrnaeste,osnovnu su školu sagradili,maloj djeci da bi znanje dali.

And children multiplied, now you should know:in nineteen hundred fourteen it was so;they built the primary school in that same year,to give the little children knowledge here.

Idu dani i vrijeme se budi,tu su bili mnogi časni ljudi.Okolo su tvornice nicale,bolji život narodu su dale.

The days go by, and time awakes anew;and many honoured people lived here, too.And all around, new factories would grow,and gave the people better lives to know.

Petsto deset stanovnika ima.Nema posla, pa je teško svima.Šest udruga u selu postoji,vrijedno rade svi uloge svoje.

Five hundred ten inhabitants, all told.No work, and so it's hard for young and old.The village has six societies, and theywork hard, each playing its part in its own way.

Al' Narčani napredni su ljudi,svome selu svo bogatstvo nudi.U kulturi i sportu su jaki,sa tim radom tu se bavi svaki.

But people of Nard are folk who make progress:they give their village all that they possess.In culture and in sport they're strong and true,and every one of them takes part there, too.

NK "DRAVA" kada igra javno,svojom igrom izdiže se slavno.Sa uspjehom igre se ponosi,svome selu poštovanje nosi.

When NK "Drava" plays a public game,its playing lifts it high to glorious fame.It takes a pride in playing with success,and brings its village honour, nothing less.

DVD je osnovan odavno,ponekada on nastupa javno.Stolni tenis klub je časni "DRAVA",svome selu često bude slava.

The fire brigade was founded long ago;sometimes in public it will make a show.The table tennis club, "Drava", of good name,will often bring its village pride and fame.

Udruga je ribolovna "ŠARAN",u pecanju on je uvijek slavan.A lovačko društvo "PREPELICA",često puta ulovi i zeca.

There is the anglers' club, "Šaran" by name,at fishing it has always won its fame.And then the hunters' club, "Prepelica", they say,will often bag a hare, too, on the way.

KUD "KOLO" je Nardu društvo slavnopo Hrvatskoj nastupaju javno.Kolo plešu divni folkloraši,a prate ih slavni tamburaši.

KUD "Kolo" is Nard's famous troupe; they standon stages all throughout Croatia's land.The splendid folk dancers dance the kolo round,and famous tambura players lend their sound.

U Nardu je mnogo tamburaša,divnih cura i čarobnih snaša.Kad Cvetković divnu glazbu svira,ta ljepota u srce me dira.

In Nard there are tambura players by the score,and lovely girls, enchanting young wives, what's more.When Cvetković plays music, sweet and fine,that beauty touches deep this heart of mine.

"KOČIJE" su snove ostvarili,svog života slavu ovjenčali,slušateljstvu ljepotu su dali,svoje mjesto svijetu prikazali.

"Kočije" saw their dreams come true, and theyhave crowned their lives with glory in their way;they gave their listeners beauty, and they've showntheir village to the world, and made it known.

Na tamburi kada cikne žica,odjekuje Slavonska ravnica.Pa i kirvaj uskoro će doći,sad svirajte, vi "ČUVARI NOĆI"!

When on the tambura a string rings high,the plain of Slavonia echoes in reply.The church fair, too, will soon be on its way —so now strike up, "Čuvari noći", play!

Eh, da nam je malo više para,pristanište sportsko da se stvara.Na području Labov rijeke Drave:to bi onda bile sreće prave.

Eh, if we had some money, just a little more,a sports marina might be built on shore,on Labov's stretch, beside the Drava's flow —then that would be true happiness, I know.

I za Nard bi svo pučanstvo znalo,Narčanima srce bi procvalo.Nek' vas ovaj problem razveseli:od srca vam pjesnik Ivo želi!

Then all the people would know Nard, and sothe hearts of Nard's own folk would bloom and grow.So may this problem bring you joy and cheer:the poet Ivo wishes, heart sincere!

Translator's note. A KUD is a village folk-arts society, NK a football club and DVD a volunteer fire brigade. Šaran and Prepelica mean "Carp" and "Quail"; Kočije ("Carriages") and Čuvari noći ("Night Watchmen") are local bands.
