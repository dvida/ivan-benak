Domoljubne i zavičajne

Moj voljeni grade

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

My Beloved Town

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Oj, Valpovo, moj voljeni grade,ti mi rasteš kao vita jela,na njedrima divne Slavonije,ljepota me tvoja zanijela.

O Valpovo, my own beloved town,like a slender fir you're growing tallon the breast of fair Slavonia;and your beauty holds me in its thrall.

Nekada su tu kućerci bili,ušoreni, slamom pokriveni.Sad po tebi naselja se grade,k'o gradovi skladno ustrojeni.

Once there were small cottages in rows,lined along the lane, and thatched with straw;now whole neighbourhoods are built in you,laid out like towns in order, without flaw.

Na tvom trgu punom uspomena,izgrađena jedna nova zgrada,izdiže se i ljepotom blista,što ti daje status novog grada.

On your square, so full of memories,a new building's risen, fresh and new;it stands tall and shines out with its beauty,and it lends a new town's rank to you.

Valpovo je centar Valpovštine.Od davnina park i dvorac imai kulturni centar posve novi,za potrebe svojim građanima.

Valpovo's the heart of Valpovština;it has had its park and castle long,and a cultural centre, all brand new,for the needs of all who here belong.

I dvoranu sportsku u Valpovu,izgradiše neimari rada,na veselje mnogobrojnih đaka,što je ponos Valpovačkog grada.

And a sports hall in Valpovo, too,master builders raised it side by side,to the joy of pupils by the score,and it is Valpovo town's own pride.

Samo rasti moj voljeni grade,svježila te rosa s Karašicecvjetalo ti sokacima cvijećei kitilo tvoje bijelo lice.

Grow on, grow on, my own beloved town,freshened by the Karašica's dew;may the flowers blossom in your lanesand deck your white face with bloom anew.

Poj slavuja budio te zorom,sa platana tvoga perivoja,odjek zvona sa crkvenog tornja,donio ti san noćnog spokoja.

May the nightingale's song wake you at dawnfrom the plane trees standing in your park,and the church bells' echo from the towerbring you peaceful sleep when night grows dark.

Sokacima i okolo dvorca,orio se smijeh, radost i pjesma.Žiteljima mog voljenog grada,neka bude to životna česma.

Through the lanes and all around the castlemay there ring out laughter, joy and song;to the people of my own dear townmay it be life's fountain, flowing strong.

Valpovo, 15.01.1994.

Valpovo, January 15, 1994

Translator's note. An ode to Valpovo, the town where he lived, with its castle and park; the Karašica is the river that flows through it, and Valpovština is the Valpovo district.
