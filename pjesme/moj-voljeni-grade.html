Domoljubne i zavičajne

Moj voljeni grade

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

My Beloved Town

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Oj, Valpovo, moj voljeni grade,ti mi rasteš kao vita jela,na njedrima divne Slavonije,ljepota me tvoja zanijela.

O Valpovo, my own beloved town,you grow for me just like a slender fir,upon the breast of fair Slavonia;your beauty sets my very heart astir.

Nekada su tu kućerci bili,ušoreni, slamom pokriveni.Sad po tebi naselja se grade,k'o gradovi skladno ustrojeni.

Once, little cottages stood here in rows,all lined along the street, and thatched with straw;now whole new neighbourhoods are built in you,laid out like towns, well ordered, without flaw.

Na tvom trgu punom uspomena,izgrađena jedna nova zgrada,izdiže se i ljepotom blista,što ti daje status novog grada.

Upon your square, so full of memories,a building now has risen, fresh and new;it rises up and shines out with its beauty,and lends a new town's status now to you.

Valpovo je centar Valpovštine.Od davnina park i dvorac imai kulturni centar posve novi,za potrebe svojim građanima.

Valpovo is the heart of Valpovština;it's had its park and castle from of old,and now a cultural centre, quite brand new,to serve the needs of all its citizens.

I dvoranu sportsku u Valpovu,izgradiše neimari rada,na veselje mnogobrojnih đaka,što je ponos Valpovačkog grada.

And in Valpovo, too, a sports hall stands,raised by the master builders' working hands,to the delight of pupils by the score,and it is Valpovo town's pride and joy.

Samo rasti moj voljeni grade,svježila te rosa s Karašicecvjetalo ti sokacima cvijećei kitilo tvoje bijelo lice.

Grow on, grow on, my own beloved town,may dew from Karašica freshen you,may flowers blossom all along your lanes,and deck your white face with their bloom anew.

Poj slavuja budio te zorom,sa platana tvoga perivoja,odjek zvona sa crkvenog tornja,donio ti san noćnog spokoja.

May nightingales' song wake you at the dawnfrom all the plane trees standing in your park,and may the church bells' echo from the towerbring you the peace of sleep when it grows dark.

Sokacima i okolo dvorca,orio se smijeh, radost i pjesma.Žiteljima mog voljenog grada,neka bude to životna česma.

Along the lanes and all around the castlemay laughter, joy and song ring loud and long;and to the people of my own dear townmay it be life's own fountain, flowing strong.

Valpovo, 15.01.1994.

Valpovo, January 15, 1994

Translator's note. An ode to Valpovo, the town where he lived, with its castle and park; the Karašica is the river that flows through it, and Valpovština is the Valpovo district.
