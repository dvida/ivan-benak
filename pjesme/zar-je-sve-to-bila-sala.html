Love poems

Was It All Just a Joke?

set to music

Još uvijek ja želim tebe.Zar mi nisi reći znala,da ljubavi više nema,da si ljubav drugom dala?

Even now I still want you.Could you not have told me truethat the love is there no more,that your love went to someone new?

Sve poljupce slatke tvoje,kako da ja shvatim sada,svu ljepotu naše srećei časove pune slada?

All those sweet kisses of yours,how am I to read them right,all the beauty of our blissand the hours of delight?

Sjećaš li se one suzešto ti je iz oka pala?Zar su to sve bile laži,zar je sve to bila šala?

Do you still recall that tearthat from out your eye once fell?Was it all a lie, then, all,was it all a joke as well?

Valpovo, 14. 06. 1975.

Valpovo, June 14, 1975

Translator's note. Set to music by Renato Lukić, who also composed "Green Are Those Eyes of Yours" and "Beneath the Lilac Boughs".
