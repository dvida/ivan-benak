Ljubavne pjesme

Duh crnoga Sama

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Love poems

The Ghost of Black Sam

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ispred kina stojim i gledam reklame.Pitala si tiho, prilazeći skromno,imate li možda jednu kartu za me?Dajući ti kartu, pogledah te pomno.

Outside the cinema I stand and look at posters there;you asked me quietly, coming shyly into view:"Have you perhaps a ticket for me you could spare?"And, giving you the ticket, I looked you through and through.

Gledah te u oči tada za trenutak,bile su ti sjetne, tople, tako snene.Mišljah da bi možda našla neki kutak,u srdašcu svome, bar mali za mene.

I looked into your eyes then, for a moment's space;they were so wistful, warm, as dreamy as could be.I thought that maybe you would find some little place,a corner in your heart, however small, for me.

Kako su na platnu promicale sjenei ljubav se tako rađala međ' nama.Gledala si krišom vatrene mi zjene,a film se zvao "Duh crnoga Sama."

And as across the screen the shadows flickered by,little by little, love between the two of us began;you kept on stealing glances at my fiery eyes,and the film was called "The Ghost of Black Sam."

Drhtalo mi srce kao da ga zebe.Ljubav nam je dao "Duh crnoga Sama."Ja sam htio biti tu večer kraj tebe,zato pođoh s tobom da ne budeš sama.

My heart was trembling as if shivering from the cold.Our love was given us by "The Ghost of Black Sam."That evening I had wanted to be near you, truth be told,and so I went with you, so you would not be alone.

Valpovo, 1978.

Valpovo, 1978

Translator's note. The film was shown under the Croatian title "Duh crnoga Sama", which rhymes in the original with "sama" (alone), the poem's last word.
