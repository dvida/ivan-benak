Monitor

Radio market

A Monitor

Ja monitor nudim svima,tko potrebu za njeg' ima.

I've a monitor to sparefor whoever needs one there.

Istina je, ljudi moji,monitor je taj u boji.

Here's the truth, my people dear:it's a colour one, that's clear.

On čak petnaest inča ima,za kompjuter svaki štima.

It's got fifteen inches — true! —and it suits each computer, too.

Morate još nešto znati,ja ne tražim da se plati.

One more thing you'll want to know:you don't pay, I tell you so.

Istina je ovo, ljudi,monitor se đabe nudi.

This is truly so, dear folk:it's for nothing — it's no joke.

Trenutak je ovo pravi,tko ga treba nek' se javi.

Now's the moment, don't delay:if you need it, call, I say.

Na telefon ovaj tjedan:652 – osamsto dvadeset jedan.

Call this week, the phone's the one:652 – eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 23. 12. 2010.

Valpovo, December 23, 2010

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad, giving the monitor away for nothing.
