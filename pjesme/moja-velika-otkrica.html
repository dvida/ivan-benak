Poems for children

My Great Discoveries

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Kad sam bio maličinio sam čuda.Gdje god nije trebalobilo me je svuda.

When I was a small boy,I worked wonders all around;wherever I shouldn't be,there I could be found!

Našoj sam maciošišao brke.Kad je majka vidjelaAl' je bilo frke!

I clipped the whiskersoff our kitty cat;and when Mother saw it,oh, what a fuss at that!

Jednoj sam vraniskresao krilada bi što bržau letu bila.

I cut back the wingsof a crow one day,so she'd be fasterwhen she flew away!

Žabi sam htiopotkovu datida bi bolje moglau vodu skakati.

I wanted to shoea frog like a horse,so she'd jump in the waterbetter, of course!

Htio sam svijetusve najbolje dati,al´ me nije mojarazumjela mati.

I wanted to givethe world all that's good,but my own mothernever understood.

Zato bi me čestoza ušesa vuklai velikom šibompo tuku istukla.

So she'd often drag meby my ears, and thenwith a great big switchshe'd tan my hide again.
