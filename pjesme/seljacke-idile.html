Domoljubne i zavičajne

Seljačke idile

Homeland and native region

Village Idylls

Sviće zora i sunašce viri,bez oblaka nebo čisto, plavo,kroz dolinu lahor tiho piri,za sjetvu je danas vrijeme pravo.

Dawn is breaking, and the sun peeps through,not a cloud — the sky is clean and blue,softly through the valley breathes the breeze,and the day is right for sowing, too.

Uranio Šokac u svitanje,ormu sprema i dorate preže.Spremio se, ide na oranje,taj ga nagon od djetinjstva veže.

Early up at daybreak is the Šokac,sets the harness, hitches up the bays;now he's ready, off to do the ploughing,drawn by instinct since his boyhood days.

Ženica mu otvara kapiju,poljubac mu od dragosti šalje.Sprema ručak i hladi rakiju,o ljubavi sretna sniva dalje.

His dear wife swings open wide the gate,fondly blows a kiss to speed his way;cooks the lunch and cools the rakija,happy, dreams of love on through the day.

Dobar ručak, rakija se pije,žena služi, od sreće se smije.Nakon ručka, polje će da sije,od te sloge ništa ljepše nije.

Hearty lunch; the rakija flows free,wife serves, and laughs aloud for joy and glee;after lunch he'll sow the field, you see —nothing's lovelier than such harmony.

Već i mjesec fenjer zapalio,a zvijezde su nebo okitile.Njih se dvoje u zagrljaj svilo:Eto to su: SELJAČKE IDILE!

Now the moon has lit his lantern too,and the sky is decked with every star;and the pair are twined in one embrace:Village Idylls — that is what they are!

Valpovo, 20.07.2014.

Valpovo, July 20, 2014
