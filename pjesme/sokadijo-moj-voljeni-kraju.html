Domoljubne i zavičajne

Šokadijo moj voljeni kraju

Homeland and native region

Šokadija, My Beloved Land

Slavonijo, mili zavičaju,u tebi je živjet k'o u raju,najljepša si kada zora rudii Šokica poljupcima budi.

Slavonia, my homeland, dear and true,it's paradise on earth to live in you;you're loveliest when rosy dawn will breakand with her kisses Šokica bids me wake.

Kad ujutro idem na oranje,poljupcima isprati me janje.U povratku rumenoga lica,u zagrljaj čeka me Šokica.

When I go out to plough at morning's start,my little lamb with kisses sees me part;and on my way back home, with rosy face,my Šokica awaits me with an embrace.

A navečer u našem sokaku,tu se kolo vijori u mraku,na tamburi tada bruji žica,u tom kolu ljubi me Šokica.

And in the evening, in our village lane,the kolo whirls in darkness once again;the tambura then hums with every string,and Šokica kisses me in the kolo's ring.

Šokadijo moj voljeni kraju,rodne njive plodove ti daju.Ravnicu ti sela okitila,najljepša si meni uvijek bila.

Šokadija, my own beloved land,your fertile fields yield fruit on every hand;with villages your plain is decked and dressed,and you to me were always loveliest.

Volim tebe mili zavičaju,jer sam rođen u tvojemu kraju.Da se rodim opet bi tvoj bioi šokicu dragu oženio.

I love you, native land so dear to me,for in your region I was born, you see;were I reborn, I'd still be yours, and thenI'd wed a dear Šokica once again.

Slavonijo moj mirisni cvijetu,najljepša si na čitavom svijetu.Nikada te neću nikom dati,vječito ću tebe svojom zvati.

Slavonia, my fragrant flower, youare loveliest the whole wide world through;I'll never give you up to anyone —forever I shall call you mine alone.

Valpovo, 07.03.2014.

Valpovo, March 7, 2014
