Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Čuvarima noći

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Guardians of the Night

Ove pjesme u posjed će doći,sviračima: "ČUVARIMA NOĆI"U svom radu da budu na broju,da sviraju i uglazbe koju.Ta milina da se rado čujei tambure nek' ugodno bruje.Nek' Dominik orkestar povede,a Nikola sa BASPRIMOM predei Vanja će divno da ih prati,Marko KONTROM ljepotu će dati,Andrej BASOM uljepšat će zvuka,a ljepotu GLASOM dat će Luka.

These songs of mine will soon belong, outright,to the musicians, "GUARDIANS OF THE NIGHT".May all of them be present when they play,and set to music one of these some day.May such sweet music gladly reach the ear,and may the tamburas hum warm and clear.Let Dominik lead the orchestra along,and Nikola purr the BASPRIM's gentle song,and Vanja's lovely backing will combinewith Marko's KONTRA, making beauty shine;with BASS, Andrej will make the sound more sweet,and Luka's VOICE will make its charm complete.

Ništa ljepše od tambure nema,pogotovo kad se svadba sprema.Tamburaši tiho će svirati,kad će svati mladu darivati.A kasnije, kad kum vodi koloi Šokica vrisne: dođi Lolo!Na tamburi kada cikne žica,odjekuje Slavonska ravnica.Pa i KIRVAJ uskoro će doći:sad svirajte vi "ČUVARI NOĆI!"

There's nothing lovelier than the tambura's play,especially when a wedding's on the way.The players then will softly play besidethe wedding guests who bring gifts to the bride.And later, when the kum leads out the kolo,and a Šokica cries out: "Come here, Lolo!",when on the tambura a string squeals high,the Slavonian plain rings out in reply.And soon the KIRVAJ too will be in sight:so play now, you, the "GUARDIANS OF THE NIGHT"!

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2014.

Valpovo, June 20, 2014

Translator's note. Čuvari noći ("Guardians of the Night") was a young tamburica band. The basprim, kontra and bass are instruments of the tambura orchestra; the kum is the wedding's best man, and lolo is what a girl calls her sweetheart.
