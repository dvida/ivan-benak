Radio burza

Nudim kazan što rakiju peče

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Rakija Still for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Prodah salaš i voljenu njivu,sa njom voćnjak i valpovku šljivu.Sad mi kazan u pecari spava,prod'o bih ga, istina je prava.

I sold my farmstead and my much-loved ground,the orchard too, with Valpovka plums around.My still is sleeping in the still-house now;I'd sell it, that's the honest truth, I vow.

Kazan vam je od kovanog bakra,sto litara zapremine ima,a furuna lijepo rolovana,napravljena od paljenog lima.

The pot is made of hammered copper, anda hundred litres' volume it will hold;the firebox is beautifully made,of fire-blackened sheet iron, finely rolled.

Kao torzo neke divne žene,kapak mu je oblikovan tako.Cijev savita tri metra je duga,paru vodi do stabarke lako.

Just like the torso of a lovely woman,its lid is shaped, with curves designed to please;the bent pipe, full three metres long, will leadthe steam into the cooling vat with ease.

Stabarka je od jednog kubika,zapremine to je valjda jasno,a čobanja dvadeset litara,hrastovo je drvo prvoklasno.

The cooling vat's a cubic metre in all;its volume, I should think, is plain and clear;the catching tub holds twenty litres, madeof first-class oak: you'll find no better here.

Spirala je dvadeset metara,kao prsten lijepo izrađena,sedam puta stabarkom vijuga,pa rakija curi ohlađena.

The spiral coil is twenty metres long,as fine as any ring, and neatly tooled;it winds around the vat full seven times,and then the rakija trickles out, well cooled.

On tisuću eura sad košta.Sve je novo, tu vam nema varke.Za sve ovo garanciju dajem:od čobanje, kotla, do stabarke.

A thousand euros is its price today.It's all brand new — no tricks, I tell you flat.I give my guarantee for all of it:the catching tub, the pot, the cooling vat.

Ako netko sve u kešu plati,ja ću njemu jeftinije dati.Kod pogodbe kak' se dvoje slože,nije važno i za kune može.

If someone pays in cash for the whole lot,I'll sell it cheaper to him on the spot.The bargain's as the two of us agree,and kuna's fine — it's all the same to me.

Javite se na ovaj telefon:652 osamsto dvadeset jedan,da obavim taj posao vrijedan.Nek' se peče u kazanu šljiva,pozdravlja vas iz Valpova Iva.

Just call me on this telephone of mine:652 eight hundred twenty-one,and let this worthy bit of trade be done.So let the plums be distilled in the still —from Valpovo, Iva greets you, with good will.

Valpovo, 10. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 10, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. One of his verse ads for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. Valpovka is the local Valpovo plum. He signs himself "Iva" instead of Ivo, to rhyme with šljiva (plum).
