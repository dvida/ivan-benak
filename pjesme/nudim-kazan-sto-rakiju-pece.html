Radio burza

Nudim kazan što rakiju peče

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Rakija Still for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Prodah salaš i voljenu njivu,sa njom voćnjak i valpovku šljivu.Sad mi kazan u pecari spava,prod'o bih ga, istina je prava.

I sold my farmstead and the field I prized,the orchard and the Valpovka plums likewise.My still now sleeps inside the still-house, soI'd sell it — that's the honest truth, you know.

Kazan vam je od kovanog bakra,sto litara zapremine ima,a furuna lijepo rolovana,napravljena od paljenog lima.

The pot is made of hammered copper, anda hundred litres' volume it will hold;the firebox, of blackened iron plate,is fashioned well and beautifully rolled.

Kao torzo neke divne žene,kapak mu je oblikovan tako.Cijev savita tri metra je duga,paru vodi do stabarke lako.

Shaped like the torso of some lovely woman,its lid is made that way, as you can see.The bent pipe, three whole metres long, will leadthe steam into the cooling vat with ease.

Stabarka je od jednog kubika,zapremine to je valjda jasno,a čobanja dvadeset litara,hrastovo je drvo prvoklasno.

The cooling vat's one cubic metre — there,its volume, I should think, is plain and clear;the catching tub holds twenty litres, madeof first-class oak — you'll find no better here.

Spirala je dvadeset metara,kao prsten lijepo izrađena,sedam puta stabarkom vijuga,pa rakija curi ohlađena.

The coil runs twenty metres, and is madeas lovely as a ring, with skill and care;it winds around the vat full seven times,and then the rakija drips out, cooled, from there.

On tisuću eura sad košta.Sve je novo, tu vam nema varke.Za sve ovo garanciju dajem:od čobanje, kotla, do stabarke.

A thousand euros is its price today.It's all brand new — you won't be tricked, that's that.I give my guarantee for everything:the catching tub, the pot, the cooling vat.

Ako netko sve u kešu plati,ja ću njemu jeftinije dati.Kod pogodbe kak' se dvoje slože,nije važno i za kune može.

If someone pays the whole lot cash in hand,I'll sell it cheaper, you must understand.The bargain's as the two of us agree;in kuna, too — it's all the same to me.

Javite se na ovaj telefon:652 osamsto dvadeset jedan,da obavim taj posao vrijedan.Nek' se peče u kazanu šljiva,pozdravlja vas iz Valpova Iva.

Just call me on this telephone of mine:652 eight hundred twenty-one,so this most worthy deal of mine gets done.So let the plums be distilled in the still —from Valpovo, Iva greets you, with good will.

Valpovo, 10. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 10, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. One of his verse ads for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. Valpovka is the local Valpovo plum. He signs himself "Iva" instead of Ivo, to rhyme with šljiva (plum).
