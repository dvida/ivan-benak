Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Tamari Španić, učiteljici iz Sopja

Occasional verse and dedications

To Tamara Španić, a Teacher from Sopje

POŠTOVANA TAMARA!

Dear Tamara!

Za Vašu pažnju koju ste mi dalii sa djecom što ste odigrali.Ja vjerujem da će knjiga ova,reći HVALA sve do zadnjeg slova!

For the attention you have kindly paid,and for the play the children with you played,I trust this book will tell you, through and through,"Thank you!" down to its final letter, too!

I stihovi ove knjige,od mene su iznjedreni,čitateljstvu namijenjeni,a Vama su podareni.

All the verses of this bookwere brought forth by me, it's true,meant for everyone to read,and are given now to you.

Da bi jutrom vedri bilii vesele snove snili,nek' Vam bude knjiga ova,uspavanka prije snova.

So your mornings may be bright,and your dreams be glad at night,let this book become for youa lullaby when dreams are due.

Što bi moja duša znala,već iskreno reći HVALA!

What else would my soul know to dobut say, with all sincerity, "Thank you!"

Valpovo, 23. 03. 2011.

Valpovo, March 23, 2011

Translator's note. Written in a copy of one of his books for the teacher who staged his work with her pupils.
