Happy Childhood

Ivankova to je vrtić,on se, djeco, tako zove.Tu ujutro djeca dođui snivaju divne snove.Igraju se i pjevaju,tu na djecu tete paze,djeci pjesme tu čitajui tete ih divno maze.U igri im nema kraja,to je dio pravog raja.Onda dođe po njih mama,ni kod kuće nisu sama.Na spavanje idu noći,sutra će u vrtić doći,da slušaju MALOG ZEKUkoji će im sve pomoći.

Ivankovo's kindergarten —children, that's its name, just so;here each morning children comeand dream lovely dreams, you know.Here they play and here they sing,and the aunties mind them there;here they read the children rhymes,and they cuddle them with care.There's no end to all their play,it's a piece of heaven, I'd say.Then Mum comes to take them home;there, too, they're never alone.At night-time they go to sleep,back tomorrow they will cometo listen to LITTLE BUNNY,who will help them, every one.

Valpovo, 12. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 12, 2013

Translator's note. Radosno djetinjstvo ("Happy Childhood") is the kindergarten in Ivankovo; the "aunties" (tete) are what Croatian children call their kindergarten teachers.
