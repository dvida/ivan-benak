Spiritual poems

Lord, Shall I Too to Heaven Go?

text by another author, kept among the poet's papers

Hoću li, Gospode, i ja u raj?Želim, al' sve se bojim.Jer tamo će biti slava i sjaj,A ja na niskom stojim!

Lord, shall I too to heaven go?I long to, yet I fear.For there will be glory and splendour's glow,and I stand lowly here!

Već ako imaš gdjekoji kutić,I to je mnogo za me.Stisnut ću tamo se,suhi k'o prutić,Usko je moje rame.

But if You have some corner, not too big,even that's plenty for me.I'll squeeze myself in there,dry as a twig:my shoulder is narrow, you see.

Nikomu neću praviti sjeneBez buke bi to bilo.Gospode, ne zaboravi mene,Ako to ti je milo.

On no one at all will my shadow fall,it would all be without noise.Lord, do not forget me, after all,if that should be Your choice.

Ako Ti nije milo prisućeTakova odrpanca,Pusti, da bar na vratima kućeČekam kog Tvojeg znanca.

If You'd not care to see here, Lord,so ragged a wretch as I,then let me at least by Your house's doorwait for some friend of Yours nearby,

Da ga zamolim,da se za me moli.Ako se ni to ne da,Ja ću svejedno čekati doli,Već i poradi reda!

so I might ask himto say a prayer for me.And if even that is not granted,I'll wait down below all the same,if only for order's sake!

Pa kada uđu, kojim je dato,I mjesta biti neće:Nebo je malo, al' ništa zato...Srce je Tvoje veće...

And when all go in to whom it was given,and no place is left to fill:Heaven is small, but never mind...Your heart is greater still...
