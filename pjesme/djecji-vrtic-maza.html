Poems for children

Maza Kindergarten

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010) · set to music

U parku kraj dvorcapružila se staza,tu je dječji vrtić,a zove se „MAZA“.

In the castle parka pathway stretches far;there's a kindergarten,called "MAZA"! There you are.

U tom su vrtićuValpovčani mali,tete djecu učeda bi svašta znali.

In that kindergartenlittle Valpovo folk stay;the aunties teach the childrenall sorts of things each day.

Skupile se jutrossve mamine maze,tu se lijepo igraju,na njih tete paze.

All their mummies' petshave gathered here today;the aunties watch over themas they sweetly play.

Igri i veseljuvrijeme brzo prođe,oko dva – tri satapo njih mama dođe.

Time flies by so quicklywith games and fun all round;at two or three o'clockMum comes, and home they're bound.

Translator's note. The kindergarten's anthem, set to music by Željka Pavošević and sung by its children. Maza means "pet" or "darling", and the kindergarten stands in the park by Valpovo's castle.
