Zlatku Rođaku – Babi

Occasional verse and dedications

To Zlatko Rođak – Baba

MARIJADUŠA OTVORENA SRCUKoliko sam želioimati dio tebe,imati neštoispod zvijezda.

MarijaA Soul Open to the HeartHow I have longedto have a part of you,to have somethingbeneath the stars.

ČAROLIJA TRENUTKAKruže mi mislitražeći spas.NA KRILIMA LJUBAVINe znam kamo da krenem,svijet mi okreće leđa.ŠETNJA U OBLACIMAA ja se borim.MARIJABorim s oblacima.

The Magic of a MomentMy thoughts are circling,seeking salvation.On the Wings of LoveI don't know where to go,the world turns its back on me.A Walk in the CloudsAnd I am fighting.MarijaFighting with the clouds.

Do godine, dragi Rođo, u pisanju vrijedan bio,nek' Ti budu sklone Muze, novu knjigu iznjedrio.

Till next year, dear Rođo, may you diligently write;may the Muses smile on you, and a new book see the light!

Valpovo, 16. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 16, 2010

Translator's note. A collage for his fellow poet Zlatko Rođak, nicknamed Baba: the headings are the titles of Rođak's books, each followed by lines from it; only the closing couplet is Benak's own.
