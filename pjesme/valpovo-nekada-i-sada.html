Domoljubne i zavičajne

Valpovo nekada i sada

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Valpovo, Then and Now

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kroz Valpovo teče Karašica,svojim tokom na dva dijela dijeli.Na KURĐANE i na ČAKANČANE,tako nama stari zapis veli.

Through Valpovo the Karašica flows,dividing it in two along its way,into KURĐANI and ČAKANČANI,so an old record tells us to this day.

Naseljeni u blizini dvorca,s desne strane ČAKANCI su bili,a kraj crkve na obali lijevoj,KURĐANI se udobno smjestili.

Settled close beside the castle walls,on the right bank the ČAKANCI would dwell;by the church, upon the left bank's shore,KURĐANI made their homes, and settled well.

Nekad davno u stara vremena,među njima sloge nije bilo.Tko zna otkad ovo stanje traje,o jedinstvu nije se ni snilo.

Once, long ago, in old and distant times,between the two no concord held its sway;who knows how long the state of things went on,and none dreamed unity would come one day.

Ne daj Bože da u noćno dobaneki momak pređe Karašicu,bilo koji od ova dva roda,da obiđe svoju vjerenicu!

God forbid that, in the dead of night,some lad across the Karašica went,whichever of the two clans he was from,to see his own betrothed, on courting bent!

Jedan mostić sagrađen kraj dvorca,što godi broji od starina,da bi mogli kroz Valpovo proći,plaćala se ovdje maltarina.

Beside the castle, a small bridge was built,that counted up its years from days untold;and so that folk through Valpovo could pass,the toll was paid here by the young and old.

A sada je sasvim nešto drugo.Vrijedni narod svoje njive radi.Grad se diže u svojoj ljepotii Valpovo iz osnova gradi.

But now things are entirely otherwise:the working people till their fields and plough;the town is rising in its loveliness,Valpovo's rebuilt from the ground up now.

Sad se više ne dijeli na dvoje,bratska ljubav ovaj narod veže.Šest mostova što se gordo dižu,Ta dva kraja u cjelinu steže.

It isn't split in two now, as before:a brother's love has bound its people fast;six bridges, rising proudly as they stand,draw those two parts into one whole at last.

Nema više onih starih svađa,teku u šali reći bi se znalo:I ljubav je zabranjena bila,davno bilo, sad se spominjalo.

Those quarrels of the old days are no more;it's only as a joke you'd hear folk saythat even love was once forbidden here:long gone, and only spoken of today.

Valpovo, 06.12.1989.

Valpovo, December 6, 1989

Translator's note. The Karašica stream divides Valpovo; the Kurđani and the Čakanci (Čakančani) were the old rival communities of its two banks.
