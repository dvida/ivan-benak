Domoljubne i zavičajne

Valpovo nekada i sada

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Valpovo, Then and Now

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kroz Valpovo teče Karašica,svojim tokom na dva dijela dijeli.Na KURĐANE i na ČAKANČANE,tako nama stari zapis veli.

Through Valpovo the Karašica flows,dividing it in two parts on its way,into Kurđani and Čakančani,so an old record tells us to this day.

Naseljeni u blizini dvorca,s desne strane ČAKANCI su bili,a kraj crkve na obali lijevoj,KURĐANI se udobno smjestili.

Close to the castle, on the right-hand side,the Čakanci had settled down to stay;and by the church, upon the left-hand shore,the Kurđani lived snugly, so they say.

Nekad davno u stara vremena,među njima sloge nije bilo.Tko zna otkad ovo stanje traje,o jedinstvu nije se ni snilo.

Once long ago, back in the olden days,between them there was never harmony;who knows how long this state of things had lasted —and no one even dreamed of unity.

Ne daj Bože da u noćno dobaneki momak pređe Karašicu,bilo koji od ova dva roda,da obiđe svoju vjerenicu!

God forbid that, in the dead of night,some young man were to cross the Karašica —from either clan, the left side or the right —to pay a visit to his own betrothed!

Jedan mostić sagrađen kraj dvorca,što godi broji od starina,da bi mogli kroz Valpovo proći,plaćala se ovdje maltarina.

Beside the castle, a small bridge was made,that counted up its years from days of old;and so that folk through Valpovo could go,here at this spot a toll had to be paid.

A sada je sasvim nešto drugo.Vrijedni narod svoje njive radi.Grad se diže u svojoj ljepotii Valpovo iz osnova gradi.

But now things are entirely otherwise:the working people till their fields and plough;the town is rising in its loveliness,and Valpovo's rebuilt from the ground up now.

Sad se više ne dijeli na dvoje,bratska ljubav ovaj narod veže.Šest mostova što se gordo dižu,Ta dva kraja u cjelinu steže.

It isn't split in two now, as before:a brother's love has bound its people fast;six bridges, proudly rising, shore to shore,draw those two parts into one whole at last.

Nema više onih starih svađa,teku u šali reći bi se znalo:I ljubav je zabranjena bila,davno bilo, sad se spominjalo.

Those quarrels of the old days are no more;it's only as a joke you'd hear it toldthat even love was once forbidden here —long past, and only now and then retold.

Valpovo, 06.12.1989.

Valpovo, December 6, 1989

Translator's note. The Karašica stream divides Valpovo; the Kurđani and the Čakanci (Čakančani) were the old rival communities of its two banks.
