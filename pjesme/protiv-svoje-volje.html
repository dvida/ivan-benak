Humorous poems

Against My Will

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kad sam bio u trbuhu majke,nikada mi nije bilo bolje.Mene ništa ni pitali nisu,rodiše me protiv moje volje.

When I was lying in my mother's womb,I never had it better; and yet, still,nobody even asked me what I wished:they brought me forth, and quite against my will.

Hranili me po vlastitoj želji,bacali me i gore i dolje,igrali se kao mačka s mišem,opet kažem: protiv moje volje.

They fed me when they wished, the way they'd choose,they tossed me up and down, as parents will,they played with me as cats play with a mouse:I say again, against my very will.

Kad sam stao na vlastite noge,mislio sam da će biti bolje.No, do škole opet svakog danatjerali me, protiv moje volje.

When I could stand at last on my own feet,I thought that things would get much better. Still,they packed me off to school, day after day,and every time, again against my will.

Došla mladost i sa njome ljubav,pa zar može nešto biti bolje?Tek što krenuh bećarskim životom,ja u vojsku, protiv svoje volje.

Then youth arrived, and love along with it:could anything be better, sweeter still?I'd barely started on the bećar's life:the army took me, quite against my will.

Lijepu mladost armiji sam dao,vratili me kad se želo polje.Još se nisam naljubio cura,ožene me, i bez moje volje.

I gave my lovely youth to army life;they sent me home when reapers cut the field.I hadn't loved the girls yet to my fill:they married me, and that without my will.

Prši sniježak u noćima hladnim,žena grije od furune bolje.Ljubljah strasno u noćima dugim,došla djeca, i bez moje volje.

On chilly nights the snow would flurry down;a wife warms better than the stove. I'd fillthe long nights up with ardent love, and thenthe children came, and that without my will.

Da bi kuća bila puna svega,mojoj djeci da bi bilo bolje,morao sam k'o magare vući,vjerujte mi - protiv svoje volje.

So that our house be full of everythingand that my children's lot be better still,I had to pull the load, just like a donkey:believe me, friends, against my very will.

Često žena, pa i majka njena,jezicima bodu kao zolje.Ja bih rado podviknuo glasno,ali šutim, protiv svoje volje.

My wife, and her own mother too, will oftensting with their tongues like wasps, and never still;I'd gladly give a good loud shout at them,but I keep quiet, against my very will.

Godine su učinile svoje,kičma mi se savila na dolje.Od života osta uspomena,sve je prošla, protiv moje volje.

The years have done their work, as years will do;my spine has bent, and bends down lower still.Of life there's nothing left but memory:it all went by, and quite against my will.

I starost je polako već stigla,barem da je zdravlje malo bolje.Sada čekam na rajska blaženstva,vjerujte mi, protiv svoje volje.

And now old age has slowly crept on in:if only I were not so often ill!And now I wait for heaven's blessed joys,believe me, friends, against my very will.

Valpovo, 15.12.1990.

Valpovo, December 15, 1990
