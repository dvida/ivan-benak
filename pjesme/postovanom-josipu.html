Poštovanom Josipu!

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Esteemed Josip!

Imam jednog znanca od davnina,dobar čovjek pun ljudskih vrlina.Fišter Josip, majstor svog zanata,puno radi sa malo alata.Nema toga što on ne bi znao,tu vrlinu sam Bog mu je dao.Dobar majstor sa puno zanatai vrijedan je ponos svoga rada.Ako trebam nešto popraviti,vjerujte mi, Fištera ću zvati.

There is a man I've known from days of old,a good man, full of virtues manifold.Fišter Josip, a master of his trade,does lots of work with few tools to his aid.There's nothing he would not know how to do;this virtue God Himself gave him, it's true.A good craftsman, with skills of many a kind,hard-working — pride of all his work, you'll find.If I need anything repaired, I say,believe me, it's Fišter I'll call straight away.

Valpovo, 14. 07. 2013.

Valpovo, July 14, 2013
