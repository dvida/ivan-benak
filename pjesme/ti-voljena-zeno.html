Love poems

You, Beloved Woman

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dok si me u krilunježno milovala,gledao sam sretanvatrene ti oči.Taj divan trenutaki to milo liceu snovima vidimskoro svake noći.

While you held me in your lapand tenderly caressed me,happily I gazedinto your fiery eyes.That wonderful momentand that dear facein my dreams I seealmost every night.

Ona mala klupa,kad sam cjelov krao!To mirisno sijeno,gdje si ljubav dalai zvjezdice sjajne,kad sam ljubav dao!...Naš su nijemi svjedok.

That little bench,where I stole a kiss!That fragrant hay,where you gave your love,and the shining stars,when I gave my love!...Are our silent witnesses.

Ti voljena ženo!

You, beloved woman!

Valpovo, 1981.

Valpovo, 1981
