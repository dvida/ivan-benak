Love poems

What Do I Need It For

Gledao sam njenu bračnu idilu:voljeni joj glavu držaše u krilui nejačad onu brbljavu i milu,tada srce svoje osjetih u bilu.

I saw the married idyll of her family:her dear one held her head upon his knee,the little children, chattering, sweet to see —and then I felt my heart start pounding inside me.

Uspomene naše iz prošlosti budim.Zbog ljubavi njene, zar moram da ludim?Ja joj ljubav svoju još i dalje nudim,i srcu se svome sve to više čudim.

I wake the memories of our distant past.For love of her, must I go mad at last?I offer her my love still, holding fast,and at my own heart I'm more and more aghast.

Kako ljubav njenu, sada da odolimi svu patnju moju kako da prebolim,zar da mutim sreću, onome kog volim.Oh, pomozi, bože, iskreno te molim.

How can I now withstand her love, I say,and how get over all my suffering someday?Must I disturb the joy of her I love, that way?Oh, help me, God, with all my heart I pray.

I dok ljubav njena moje srce vreba,razum mi govori što mi sve to treba.Dotle sreću svoju ja bacam sa neba,zar da takav život živim do pogreba.

And while her love lies waiting for my heart, close by,my reason asks me: what do I need it for, and why?Meanwhile I fling my happiness down from the sky —must I live such a life until the day I die?

Valpovo, 1980.

Valpovo, 1980

Translator's note. A poem of unrequited love for a happily married woman; every stanza runs on a single rhyme.
