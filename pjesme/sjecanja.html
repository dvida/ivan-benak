Love poems

Memories

Sjećaš li se te večeri?Kroz prozor je mjesec sjao.Mrsio sam kosu tvoju,kroz poljupce ime zvao.

Do you still recall that evening?Through the window moonlight came.I was tousling your hair,through our kisses called your name.

Te noći smo sretni bili,u igri k'o djeca mala.Ljubljah tvoje snene ti oči,na nebu je zvijezda sjala.

We were happy on that night,playing like small children do;I was kissing your sleepy eyes,in the sky a star shone through.

Uz poljupce slatke tvoje,duša topi se od sladada rastanka nikad nemaljubavnog tog teškog jada.

With those sweetest kisses of yoursmy soul melted, sweet, as thoughthere would never be a parting,never love's hard, heavy woe.

Željela si tada dragada nikada ne svane dan,da nam ljubav srca veže,da vječito traje taj san.

Then, my darling, you were wishingthat the day would never dawn,that our love would bind our hearts,that the dream go on and on.

Valpovo, 25.07.1973.

Valpovo, July 25, 1973
