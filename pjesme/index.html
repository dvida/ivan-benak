Pjesme

Pjesme su razvrstane po temama. Prve kategorije slijede poglavlja njegove posljednje knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.). Mnoge prigodne pjesme i posvete pisao je za prijatelje, udruge, škole i obitelji, često izravno u knjigu koju je poklanjao. Uz svaku pjesmu naveden je, gdje je poznat, datum i mjesto kako ih je sam zapisao.

Poems

The poems are arranged by theme. The first categories follow the chapters of his last book, Through Life in Verse (2010). Many of the occasional poems and dedications were written for friends, societies, schools and families, often straight into a book he was giving as a present. Where it is known, each poem carries the date and place as he wrote them.

Ljubavne pjesme (122)

Čežnja, zore, rastanci i poljupci pod jorgovanom.

Love poems (122)

Longing, dawns, partings, and kisses beneath the lilac.

Slavonija, Šokadija, Sopje, Valpovo, Drava i Karašica.

Homeland and native region (53)

Slavonia, Šokadija, Sopje, Valpovo, the Drava and the Karašica.

Pjesme za djecu (83)

Mače, zeko, pile i glista, i sve što djeca vole.

Poems for children (83)

Kittens, bunnies, chicks and worms, and everything children love.

Obiteljski kutak (25)

Za ženu, majku, djecu i unuke.

Family corner (25)

For his wife, his mother, his children and grandchildren.

Bećarci, zgode, nezgode i vesela društva.

Humorous poems (31)

Bećarac verses, mishaps, merry company.

Radio burza (51)

Šaljivi oglasi u stihu za radijsku emisiju.

Radio market (51)

Comic classified ads in verse, written for the radio.

Protestne i društvene (13)

Rat, nepravda, siromaštvo i gorke istine.

Protest and society (13)

War, injustice, poverty and bitter truths.

Umirovljenički jadi (26)

Mirovina, starost i društva umirovljenika, s osmijehom.

Pensioners' woes (26)

Pensions, old age and pensioners' clubs, with a smile.

Sjećanja i razmišljanja (20)

Djetinjstvo, starost, vrijeme i sama poezija.

Memories and reflections (20)

Childhood, old age, time, and poetry itself.

Duhovne pjesme (5)

Molitve, sveci i Božić.

Spiritual poems (5)

Prayers, saints and Christmas.

Za prijatelje, udruge, škole, obitelji i obljetnice.

Occasional verse and dedications (171)

For friends, societies, schools, families and anniversaries.
