Pjesme

Pjesme su razvrstane po temama. Prve kategorije slijede poglavlja njegove posljednje knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.). Mnoge prigodne pjesme i posvete pisao je za prijatelje, udruge, škole i obitelji, često izravno u knjigu koju je poklanjao. Uz svaku pjesmu naveden je, gdje je poznat, datum i mjesto kako ih je sam zapisao.

Poems

The poems are arranged by theme. The first categories follow the chapters of his last book, Through Life in Verse (2010). Many of the occasional poems and dedications were written for friends, societies, schools and families, often straight into a book he was giving as a present. Where it is known, each poem carries the date and place as he wrote them.

Dođi na moje grudi

loving

Come and Lie Upon My Breast

Kada se jutrom ranom budiši zlatna zora zarudi,da ljepši nam bude dan,dođi na moje grudi!

When you wake in early morningand the dawn in gold is dressed,so our day may be more lovely,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada umoran dođem kućii duša za domom žudi,da nađem radost srcu svome,dođi na moje grudi!

When I come home tired and wearyand my soul yearns for its nest,so my heart may find its gladness,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada prođe cvijet mladostii starost polako se nudi,da nađeš ljubav kao žena,dođi na moje grudi!

When the flower of youth has fadedand old age steals in, a guest,so you'll find love as a woman,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada djeca odrastu i odui život te izmuči kruti,da svome bolu utjehu nađeš,dođi na moje grudi!

When the children grow and leave usand harsh life has you distressed,so your pain may find its comfort,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada dođu starački danii mara te u snu budi,da odmoriš staru, umornu dušu,dođi na moje grudi!

When the days of age are comingand a nightmare breaks your rest,so your old and weary soul may find repose,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada dođe ono vrijemei sudba za prošlost sudi,da toplu suzu iz oka pustiš,dođi na moje grudi!

When at last that time is comingand fate puts our past to the test,so you may shed a warm tear from your eye,come and lie upon my breast!

Why Is Man Not Like a Child

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteu čistoći svoje duše?Zašto neki kad odrastusve vrline ljudske ruše?

Why is man not like a childin the pureness of his heart?Why do some, when they are grown,tear man's virtues all apart?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto koru kruha među sobom dijeli?Zašto mnogi zemaljsko blagosamo za sebe želi?

Why is man not like a childthat will share its crust of bread with all its friends?Why do many want earth's richesonly for their selfish ends?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteu poštenju, marljivosti?Zašto su pojedinci zvijeriu otimanju i gramzljivosti?

Why is man not like a childin his honesty and toil?Why are some like beasts of prey,grasping, snatching, greedy for the spoil?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto mu se pogled kupa u rosi?Zašto svojim postupcimabol, bijedu i jad nanosi?

Why is man not like a childwhose gaze bathes in dewdrops, fresh and clear?Why, by all he does, must hebring pain, want and sorrow here?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteŠto osmjehom proljeće nosi?Zašto prljavim ratovimatuđe živote gnječi i kosi?

Why is man not like a childthat brings springtime with its smile?Why does he crush the lives of other men,scything them in dirty wars so vile?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto se mora držati bontona?Zašto čovjek želi prestižu oružju od atoma?

Why is man not like a childthat must mind its manners, be polite?Why does man seek his prestigein atomic weapons' might?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto ne gleda porijeklo ni rasuZašto mnogi kad odrastumeđu ljudstvom prave klasu?

Why is man not like a childthat pays no heed to someone's birth or race?Why do many, once they're grown,rank mankind by class and place?

Protiv svoje volje

poignant

Against My Will

Kad sam bio u trbuhu majke,nikada mi nije bilo bolje.Mene ništa ni pitali nisu,rodiše me protiv moje volje.

When I was lying in my mother's womb,I never had it better there. Until,without so much as asking what I wished,they bore me into life against my will.

Hranili me po vlastitoj želji,bacali me i gore i dolje,igrali se kao mačka s mišem,opet kažem: protiv moje volje.

They fed me as and when it pleased them best,they bounced me up and down with all their skill,they played with me as cats play with a mouse:and once again, I say, against my will.

Kad sam stao na vlastite noge,mislio sam da će biti bolje.No, do škole opet svakog danatjerali me, protiv moje volje.

When I could stand at last on my own feet,I thought that things would now improve — until,to school again, and every single day,they chased me off, and all against my will.

Došla mladost i sa njome ljubav,pa zar može nešto biti bolje?Tek što krenuh bećarskim životom,ja u vojsku, protiv svoje volje.

Then youth arrived, and love along with it:could anything be better? What a thrill!I'd barely started on the bećar's life:the army called me up against my will.

Lijepu mladost armiji sam dao,vratili me kad se želo polje.Još se nisam naljubio cura,ožene me, i bez moje volje.

I gave my lovely youth to army life;they sent me home at harvest time; and stillI hadn't had my fill of courting girls:they married me, and that without my will.

Prši sniježak u noćima hladnim,žena grije od furune bolje.Ljubljah strasno u noćima dugim,došla djeca, i bez moje volje.

On chilly nights the snow would flurry down;a wife beats any stove against the chill.I loved with passion through the long, long nights:the children came, and that without my will.

Da bi kuća bila puna svega,mojoj djeci da bi bilo bolje,morao sam k'o magare vući,vjerujte mi - protiv svoje volje.

So that our house be full of everything,and that my children's lot be better still,just like a donkey I would pull and drag:believe me, friends, against my very will.

Često žena, pa i majka njena,jezicima bodu kao zolje.Ja bih rado podviknuo glasno,ali šutim, protiv svoje volje.

My wife, her mother too, will often stingme with their tongues, like wasps, so sharp and shrill;I'd gladly give a good loud shout at them,but hold my tongue, although against my will.

Godine su učinile svoje,kičma mi se savila na dolje.Od života osta uspomena,sve je prošla, protiv moje volje.

The years have done their work, as years must do;my spine is bent, and bends down lower still.Of life there's nothing left but memory:it all went by, and quite against my will.

I starost je polako već stigla,barem da je zdravlje malo bolje.Sada čekam na rajska blaženstva,vjerujte mi, protiv svoje volje.

Old age has slowly come to me as well;if only I were not so often ill!Now I await the blessed joys of heaven —believe me, friends, against my very will.

Usliši me, Bože

questioning

Hear Me, O God

Kako ja puno već ljeta brojim,u fazama raznim životom svojim.Zato se Bogu obraćam čestoda meni u raju nađe mjesto.

Of summers I have counted many a score,through all the stages life has held in store;and so I often turn to God in prayerthat He may find a place for me up there.

Hoću li, Bože, i ja u raj?To iskreno želim, ali se bojim.U raju će biti slava i sjaj,a ja Ti na lošem glasu stojim.

Shall I too, O God, to heaven go?I truly wish it, yet I live in fear.In heaven there'll be glory, splendour's glow,and my good name with You is poor down here.

Možda ima kakav skromni kutići to je, Bože, dovoljno za me.Stisnut ću se kao suhi prutić,usko je moje staračko rame.

Perhaps there is some corner, not too big,and that, O God, would be enough for me.I'll squeeze in there, as thin as a dry twig:my aged shoulder's narrow as can be.

Nikome neću pravit probleme,sve bi to, Bože, bez pompe bilo.Ja Te molim, ne zaboravi me.Biti u raju svakom je milo.

I'll make no trouble, cause no one distress,and all, O God, would pass without a show.I beg You, don't forget me, nonetheless,for heaven's dear to everyone below.

Ako Ti nije do mene stalo,tako starog, bijednog odrpanca.Pusti barem da pričekam malo,onog jadnog moga starog znanca.

If You don't care for me, if that's the case,so old a ragged wretch, so poor as I,then let me wait at least a little spacefor my poor old acquaintance to come by,

Da mu kažem nek' se za me moli,da bi u raj bio primljen boljei dalje ću čekati bez boli,makar bilo i bez moje volje.

to tell him that for me he ought to pray,so heaven might receive me better still,and I'll wait on without a pang, and stay,though it should even be against my will.

Pa kad uđu kojima je dato,znam da ću i ja imati srećei meni ćeš mjesta dati zato,jer srce je Tvoje od neba veće!

And when they've entered, those You've granted grace,I know that I'll be lucky by and by,and You will give me, too, a little place,because Your heart is bigger than the sky!

Pjesma sinu!

loving

A Poem for My Son!

Živio sam s tobom sretno svoju mladost.Spavao si, sine, na mojim rukama.U igri sa tobom dijelio sam radost.Za bol tvoju ja sam bio na mukama.

I lived my youth with you, in happiness, my boy.You slept, my son, so often in my arms at rest;in playing with you, I would share in all your joy;and when you were in pain, I suffered, sore distressed.

Ti još i ne znaš, kako bi i znao,koliko te moje biće silno voli,kada budeš svoje čedo sinom zvaotek ćeš onda znati za strepnje i boli.

You do not know it yet (and how could you have known)how mightily my whole being loves you so;but when you call a son of yours your very own,then only will you know the dread, the pain, the woe.

Sa tobom je bilo mnogo sretnih dana,a još više strepnjom probdivenih noći.Zato nikad, sine, nikad ne dozvoli,da ranjenom dušom u grob moram poći.

With you, my son, there have been many happy days,and more, the nights I lay awake in dread and fear.So never, son, oh never, never let it bethat with a wounded soul I'm carried to my bier.

Zov domovine

homeland

The Call of the Homeland

Rođena ti gruda sreću, ljubav daje,svako drvo, kamen, dječje radovanje.To ti tuđa zemlja nikad neće dati.Maćeha se majkom nikad neće zvati.

The soil where you were born gives you both love and joy,each tree, each stone, and all the gladness of a boy;no foreign land will ever give you this, no other:a stepmother will never bear the name of mother.

Kada si daleko, nostalgija zove.Iz dna duše čut ćeš poziv zemlje ove.Na njedarca svoja k'o voljena žena,radosno te prima, to je dužnost njena.

Whenever you're far off, nostalgia calls you near;from deep within your soul, this land's own call rings clear.Upon her own dear breast, just like a well-loved wife,she gladly takes you in: that is her part in life.

U tuđoj se zemlji gorka sjeta krije.Jednom ćeš se vratit, ako nikad prije,ono u starost, kad mladost uvene,da posiješ kosti u utrobe njene.

In foreign lands a bitter sorrow lies in wait;one day you will come back, if not before, then late:in old age, when your youth has withered all away,to sow your bones within her womb, and there to stay.

Zato svoju zemlju i ljubi i voli,najdražom je zovi, vrijeđat ne dozvoli.Ona ti sve prašta, k'o rođena mati.Tu rođenu grudu treba čuvat znati.

So love your land, and cherish her, and hold her dear;call her the dearest one, let no insult come near.She forgives all, just like the mother of your birth;you must know how to keep and guard your native earth.

San o sreći

poignant

A Dream of Happiness

Budila me moja majka,svako jutro zorimicei suzama svojim toplim,umivala moje lice.

My own mother used to wake meevery morning, at dawn's first trace,and with her own tears, so warm,she would wash my little face.

Od nepravde i od bola,cijepala joj tuga grudi,dok očuhov sinak spava,mene majka u mrak budi.

From injustice and from paingrief would cause her heart to break;while my stepfather's son slept,in the dark she'd make me wake.

Da bi moje noge bosetresle hladnu rosu s trave,oko suzom zamagljeno,gledalo gdje pasu krave.

So that my bare feet would shakethe cold dew from grassy ground,and my eye, all dimmed with tears,watch where cows grazed all around.

Dotle veći i stariji,od očuha sinak mio,u postelji toploj, mekoj,blažene je snove snio.

Meanwhile he, the older, bigger,my stepfather's darling boy,in a bed so warm and soft,dreamed his dreams of bliss and joy.

A ja malen još nejačak,daleki sam snio sanak.U mašti sam sretan bio,a na javi plaćo danak.

And I, little, weak, each daydreamed a dream of far away.In my fancies I was happy,but awake, I had to pay.

Majka

loving

Mother

Kada dijete noću plače,iz snova kada se budi,da čedu svome utjehu nađe,majka ga svija na grudi.

When a child cries in the night,waking from its dreams in fear,to find comfort for her little one,mother holds it to her bosom near.

Kad čuje bilo srca njenog,toplinu osjeti majke,ona je njemu božica sreće,ljepša od svake bajke.

When it hears her heart's soft beatingand its mother's warmth is there,she's the goddess of its joy and gladness,than all fairy tales more fair.

Kad padne i bolno plače,tad majci brže hita.Ona mu nježno suze brišei poljupcem rane vida.

When it falls and cries in pain,to its mother fast it goes;tenderly she wipes away its tearsand with kisses heals its woes.

Za svaku bolest čeda svoga,majčino srce pati.Svoje bi oči za njega dala.To može samo mati.

For each illness of her little onea mother's heart aches through and through.She would give her very eyes for it:that, a mother alone can do.

Love and Pain

Ljubav je davanje.Ljubav je primanje.I što više daješ,to duže traje.No, teško je trpjetinepravedne boli,a još su teže,kad ih zadajekoga srce voli.

Love is giving.Love is receiving.And the more you give,the longer it will stay.But it's hard to sufferpains that are not due,and harder stillwhen they come your wayfrom one your heart loves true.

The Old Man

Koliki je jad i bijeda,preko mojih prošlo leđa?Kosa mi je sada sijeda,a bila je crno-smeđa.

How much misery and carehave these shoulders come to know?Grey now, faded, is my hair,once dark brown, so long ago.

Udovi mi bolno hodei to krhko nose tijelo.Slabe oči što me vode,a još bi se živjet htjelo.

Painfully my limbs still go,bearing this frail frame along;weak the eyes that guide me so,yet the wish to live is strong.

Vrati bože, osmjeh, radost,svakom stvoru da se divim.Slatku ljubav, sretnu mladostjoš jedanput da proživim.

Give me back, O God, my smileand the joy to marvel still;sweetest love and youth, a whilelet me live them, if you will.

Suha kora prove

wisdom

The Dry Crust of Cornbread

Što da pričam siromaštvo,tugu svoju, jad i bijedu.Dok sam koru prove griz'o,gled'o sam gdje meso jedu.

What's to tell of poverty,sorrow, grief, and woe complete?While I gnawed the cornbread crust,I would watch them eating meat.

Bijelog kruha svježi miris,kod drugih se širit stao,dok sam koru prove griz'ood bola sam zaplakao.

Fresh white bread, its fragrant smell,from the others drifted near;while I gnawed the cornbread crust,in my pain I shed a tear.

U snovima bijelog kruha,svježu krišku ja sam snio,a na javi koru provesuzama sam natopio.

In my dreams I used to dreamof a fresh white slice of bread,but awake, the cornbread crustI would soak with tears instead.

Kao dijete ja ne shvatihmeđu ljudstvom što se stvara,da ne grize koru proveonaj koji ima para.

As a child I did not seehow the ways of men unfold:that the cornbread crust is nevergnawed by those who have the gold.

Kad ugledah nepoštenje,kod bogatih strvinara,i ta suha kora provevrijedila je meni para.

When I saw the crookednessof the rich, the vulture kind,even that dry cornbread crustwas worth money, to my mind.

A kad shvatih tu razlikumeđu ljudstvom što se rađa.I ta suha kora provebila mi je tada slađa.

And when I grasped the difference,grown between the rich and poor,even that dry cornbread crusttasted sweeter than before.

loving

Don't Wake Me Early, Mother

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Još se nisam naspavao.Kad mi draga u san dođe,za te snove sve bih dao.

Mother, do not wake me early,I am not yet done with sleeping.When my love comes in my dreaming,all I'd give those dreams for keeping.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Ne kidaj mi divne snove,kada zoveš moje ime,mislim da me draga zove.

Mother, do not wake me early,leave my lovely dreams unbroken:when you call my name, I fancyit's my darling who has spoken.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Najljepše je snivati u zoru.U snovima na rastanku,ljubim dragu na prozoru.

Mother, do not wake me early,sweetest are the dreams when day is starting;in my dreams I kiss my true loveat her window, at our parting.

Come, My Beloved

Dođi, voljena,kada sam sretan,da radost svojus tobom podijelim.

Come, my beloved,when I am happy,so that my gladnessI may share with you.

Dođi, draga,u naručaj meni,osmijehu tvomeda se veselim.

Come, my darling,come into my arms,so that your sweet smilemay gladden me too.

Dođi i ondakada sam tužan,srce te mojei onda želi.

Come to me alsowhen I am grieving,for this heart of minelongs to have you near.

Sorrow, too, is lighterwhen carried by two,if someone shares itwith a heart sincere.

poignant

The Soldier's Mother

Muško sam čedo rodila,njegovala ga i mazila.Nad njim bdila i strepila,u igri njegovoj,uz osmijeh njegovsretna sam bila.

I bore a son and held him tight,I nursed and cuddled my delight,kept watch and trembled through the night;in his every game,in his every smile,my heart was light.
