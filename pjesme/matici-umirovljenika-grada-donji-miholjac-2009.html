Umirovljenički jadi

Matici umirovljenika grada Donji Miholjac

2009. · iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

To the Pensioners' Union of the Town of Donji Miholjac

2009 · from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Topli pozdrav želi vama,ostvarenje vaših snovai uspješni rad skupštine:pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova.

Warmest greetings he sends you,may your dreams come true, and somay your meeting prosper well:Ivo, poet of Valpovo.

Sve uspjehe predsjedništvui ostalim prisutnima,plodonosni rad skupštine,ja iskreno želim svima.

All success to your committeeand the others present here;fruitful labour to the meeting:this I wish you all, sincere.

Za uspješni rad udruge,u toj prošloj godinici.Svaki ponos, za taj uspjeh:Časnoj, Miri, predsjednici.

For the year that's now gone through,for the club's successful run,honoured Mira, president,all the pride is yours: well done!

Ja vam želim kroz godinu,financijsko dobro stanje.Sponzori nek' kasu pune,izdataka nek' je manje.

All year long I wish you, too,a financial state that's sound;may your sponsors fill the till,and expenses be cut down.

Nek' izleta kolektivnih,u godini mnogo bilo.Divne šetnje po prirodi,dušu bi vam razgalilo.

May there be, throughout the year,many outings for you all;lovely walks in nature, too,would bring gladness to your soul.

Mirovina dosadašnja,nek' vam bude malo veća.Ak' platite sve namete,to se zove, prava sreća.

May the pension that you getgrow a little bigger, too;if you pay off all the charges,that's good fortune through and through!

Radi vašeg dobrog zdravlja,pohađali vi toplice,zdravo tijelo osnažili,barem svake godinice.

For the sake of your good healthmay you take the thermal cure,strengthen your sound bodies thereonce a year at least, for sure.

Sve najbolje ja vam želim.Ne bilo vam sreći kraja.I penziju dugoročnu:Ne tražite vrata raja!

All the best I wish you now,may your joy be without end,and your pension last and last:don't seek heaven's gate, my friend!

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2009.

Valpovo, June 20, 2009

Translator's note. A greeting to the assembly of the pensioners' association. "Don't seek heaven's gate" is his standing wish that his fellow pensioners not hurry into the next world.
