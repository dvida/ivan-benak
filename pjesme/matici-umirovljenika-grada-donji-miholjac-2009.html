Umirovljenički jadi

Matici umirovljenika grada Donji Miholjac

2009. · iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

To the Pensioners' Union of the Town of Donji Miholjac

2009 · from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Topli pozdrav želi vama,ostvarenje vaših snovai uspješni rad skupštine:pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova.

Warmest greetings he sends you,dreams fulfilled, and may it gowell with your assembly's work:Ivo, poet of Valpovo.

Sve uspjehe predsjedništvui ostalim prisutnima,plodonosni rad skupštine,ja iskreno želim svima.

All success to your committeeand the others present here;fruitful labour to the meeting:this I wish to all, sincere.

Za uspješni rad udruge,u toj prošloj godinici.Svaki ponos, za taj uspjeh:Časnoj, Miri, predsjednici.

For the club's successful workin the year that you have spent,all the pride for that success:honoured Mira, president!

Ja vam želim kroz godinu,financijsko dobro stanje.Sponzori nek' kasu pune,izdataka nek' je manje.

All year long I wish you allfinances in good repair;may your sponsors fill the till,and may costs be few and rare.

Nek' izleta kolektivnih,u godini mnogo bilo.Divne šetnje po prirodi,dušu bi vam razgalilo.

Many outings for the clubmay there be throughout the year;lovely walks in nature, too,would fill up your souls with cheer.

Mirovina dosadašnja,nek' vam bude malo veća.Ak' platite sve namete,to se zove, prava sreća.

May the pension that you've gotgrow a bit, and not stay stuck;if you pay off every charge,that is what you call real luck!

Radi vašeg dobrog zdravlja,pohađali vi toplice,zdravo tijelo osnažili,barem svake godinice.

For the sake of your good health,may you go and take the cure,build your healthy bodies' strengthonce a year at least, for sure.

Sve najbolje ja vam želim.Ne bilo vam sreći kraja.I penziju dugoročnu:Ne tražite vrata raja!

All the best I wish you, friends,joy unending, ever great,and a long-term pension, too:don't go looking for heaven's gate!

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2009.

Valpovo, June 20, 2009

Translator's note. A greeting to the assembly of the pensioners' association. "Don't go looking for heaven's gate" is his standing wish that his fellow pensioners not hurry into the next world.
