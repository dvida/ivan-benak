Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Za Marijance, za smotru folklora

Occasional verse and dedications

For Marijanci, for the Folklore Festival

POŠTOVANE DAME I GOSPODO!

Honoured ladies and gentlemen!

Sve najbolje ja Vam sada želim,Vašem slavlju i ja se veselim!

All the very best I wish you here,and I share your celebration's cheer!

MARIJANCI DIKA SLAVONIJE

Marijanci, Pride of Slavonia

To je pjesma što Matea kaže,učenica dične škole Vaše.

That's the poem that Matea says,pupil of your school so worthy of praise.

Marjanačka smotra je folklora,sva ljepota prikazat se mora.

This is Marijanci's folklore day;all its beauty must be on display.

Ovo slavlje, da bi uljepšali,moju pjesmu sad su pročitali.

To make this celebration still more fine,they have read aloud this poem of mine.

Tu je pjesmu Alšić pročitaoi za javnost odobrenje dao.

Alšić was the one who read it throughand approved it for the public too.

Marijance da bih opisao:Vaš učitelj podatke mi dao.

To describe Marijanci as I ought,your own teacher gave the facts I sought.

Zato kažem, Stanko, puno hvala,što je pjesma svjetlost ugledala!

So, dear Stanko, many thanks I saythat the poem saw the light of day!

Da biste me bolje upoznalii o meni nešto više znali.

So that you may get to know me betterand learn more about me, to the letter,

Reći ću Vam sada ime mojei podatke časne dati svoje.

now my name I'll gladly tell you true,and my honest details give you too.

Ivan Benak, to je moje ime,ja sam pjesnik koji piše rime.

Ivan Benak — that's my name, you see,and a poet writing rhymes, that's me.

Mnoge pjesme ja sam rimovaoi šest knjiga svijetu darovao.

Many poems I have set in rhymeand given the world six books in my time.

Tri su knjige moje razne teme,tri za djecu tu nema dileme.

Three books of mine have themes of every kind,three are for children — that's clear, you'll find.

Pjesama je osam uglazbljeno,tamburicom divno izvezeno.

Eight of my poems now are set to airs,embroidered fine by tamburica players.

Eto, ljudi, to sam ja uživo;iz Valpova pjesnik Benak Ivo.

There, good people, that's me live, on show:Benak Ivo, poet from Valpovo.

Valpovo, 28. 05. 2011.

Valpovo, May 28, 2011

Translator's note. His verse greeting to the folklore festival of the Marijanci primary school, where the pupil Matea recited his poem "Marijanci, Pride of Slavonia"; Stanko Alšić was her teacher.
