Obiteljski kutak

Unuci Andreji

Family corner

To My Granddaughter Andreja

Andrejice unučice,kako vališ prave novce,Božićnjak je dobar bio,pa ti para ostavio.

Andreja, granddaughter dear,you're worth real money, so here:Father Christmas has been kindand left some cash for you behind.

Pratila te sreća svaka,to ti žele djed i baka!

May all fortune come your way:so your Grandpa and Grandma say!

Valpovo, 24. 12. 2002.

Valpovo, December 24, 2002

Translator's note. A verse to go with a Christmas gift of money. Božićnjak, the Christmas gift-bringer, is rendered as Father Christmas.
