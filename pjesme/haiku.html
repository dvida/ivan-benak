Haiku

PROLJEĆE:Zima dala pete,topola baca perje,klepeću rode.

Spring:Winter's taken to its heels,the poplar tosses feathers,the storks clatter.

OLUJA:Oblaci plaču,zvijezda vara mjeseca,gromovi ječe.

Storm:The clouds are weeping,a star cheats on the moon,the thunders groan.

SVITANJE:Mjesec se gasi,pijetao kljuca zoru,miriše jutro.

Dawn:The moon is going out,a rooster pecks at the dawn,the morning's fragrant.

AEROBIK:Poludjela perava,ne da više mlika,legla pod avion,hoće aero bika!

Aerobics:The cow has gone quite mad,she won't give milk at all,she's lain beneath a plane —she wants an aero-bull!

Valpovo, 1998.

Valpovo, 1998

Translator's note. Three haiku and a joke quatrain; in the last, "aerobik" (aerobics) is heard as "aero bik", an "aero bull".
