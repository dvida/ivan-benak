Pjesme za djecu

Ide Božić i sveti Nikola i poklona nosi puna kola

Poems for children

Here Come Christmas and Saint Nicholas with a Cartful of Gifts

Kad sam bio tako mali,sad istinu da bi znali.To je bilo, djeco, lani,tad su bili sretni dani.

When I was so very small(here's the truth, I'll tell you all),that was, children, just last year:happy days they were, my dears.

Na Badnjak smo sretni bilii božićni bor kitili.Stavljali smo sve ukrase,još jabuke i orase.

Christmas Eve, how glad were we,trimming up the Christmas tree!All the baubles, every one,apples, walnuts too, we hung.

Pa od tate i od mame,dobili smo puno slamei na slami se valjali,preko noći i spavali.

Then from Mummy and from Dadlots of straw we children had;on the straw we rolled aroundand slept all night, safe and sound.

U božićnoj tihoj noći,Božićnjak je znao doći.Dok sam sladak sanak snio,poklone mi ostavio.

Christmas night was hushed and stillwhen Božićnjak came, as he will.While I dreamed my sweet dreams, heleft some presents there for me.

Kad božićno jutro svane,pod borove gledam grane.Zato što sam dobar bio,lijepi poklon sam dobio.

When the Christmas dawn came round,'neath the boughs I looked, and found,since I had been good, you see,a lovely present there for me.

Neka vam je to na znanjei sada je takvo stanje,jer Božićnjak imai poklone dijeli svima.

Let me tell you, you should know:even now it's still just so,for Božićnjak is real, it's true,and has presents for all of you.

Valpovo, 08.02.2014.

Valpovo, February 8, 2014

Translator's note. On Christmas Eve Slavonian families spread straw on the floor, and the children's presents were brought by Božićnjak, the "Christmas man". The Croatian title rhymes.
