Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Mi slavimo Valpovačko ljeto

Occasional verse and dedications

We Celebrate the Valpovo Summer

Valpovo je pitomo i milo,Slavoniju divno okitilo.Počinje nam VALPOVAČKO LJETO,ovaj puta četrdeset peto.Dvaj'st trećega svečano će biti,HNK će LJETO otvoriti.Da bi slavlje bilo punopravno,mnoga društva nastupaju javno.

The town of Valpovo is gentle, dear;it decks Slavonia with beauty here.Our VALPOVO SUMMER now begins its run:this time the festival's the forty-fifth one.The twenty-third will be the grand affair,the HNK will start the SUMMER there.So that the celebration's full and true,so many clubs and groups perform there too.

Dva'jst šestog se izložba otvara,za sve one što imaju dara.I književnost ovaj put je jaka,promocija časnog godišnjaka.Pa još jedna promocija LJETAi dvadeset godina "Poeta".

The twenty-sixth, the exhibition's shownfor all of those with talents of their own.And literature this time is going strong:the worthy yearbook's launch will come along.And then another launch, the SUMMER's own,and twenty years that "Poeta" has known.

DVD-a orkestar puhača,mažoretska udruga najmlađa.Dani meda fini ukus slada,pa okrepa kulen, rakijada.Mališani kad folklor igraju,tu ljepotu samo djeca znaju.Zborovi će koncert održati,četir' zbora ljepotu će dati.

The firemen's brass band, the DVD's,the majorettes, the youngest club of these.The Honey Days, with malt's fine taste to try,then kulen and the rakija fair close by.When little ones in folk dance take their part,that beauty only children know by heart.The choirs will give a concert for us all:four choirs will bring their beauty to the hall.

Nedjelja je i veselje traje,vašar ljeta u parku se daje.EKO-ETNO tu će sajam biti,po starinski jelo pripremiti.Mnogobrojni šatori postoje,svatko nudi te ljepote svoje.Raznih stvari u šatoru ima,za dječicu, pa i odraslima.

It's Sunday, and the merriment's still here,the summer fair is in the park this year.An ECO-ETHNO fair will be on show,with food prepared the way of long ago.And there are many tents set up around,where each one's lovely wares are to be found.All sorts of things inside the tents you'll findfor little ones and grown-ups of each kind.

Nedjelja je, sedamnaest je sati,ulicama konji će jahati.Zapregom su javno prikazali,kako su se svatovi vozili.

It's Sunday, five o'clock, and down the waythe horsemen ride along the streets today.With carriages they showed for all to viewhow wedding parties used to travel through.

Tada krene povorka folklora,u ljepoti svih šesnaest KUD-ova,sve uz svirku mladih tamburaša,to je slavna tradicija naša.

Then comes the folklore pageant on its way,all sixteen KUDs in beautiful array,to music from young tamburica bands:this is the glorious custom of our lands.

Prije dvorca kolo zavijori,ta ljepota o smotri govori,publici će pružiti veselja,gledatelja to je stvarna želja.

Before the castle, then, the kolo sways;that beauty speaks the festival's own praise.It gives the audience its fill of cheer:that's what the watchers truly wish for here.

Zatim pođu u dvorac polako,kolo plešu i milo i lako,da pokažu svoje časno znanje,to predivno folklorno igranje.

Then slowly to the castle yard they go,and dance the kolo sweetly, to and fro,to show the worthy skill they have in hand,that splendid folklore dancing, fine and grand.

Nakon smotre topli pozdrav žele,tom nastupu oni se vesele.Mnoga mjesta moraju još proći,na LJETO će u Valpovo doći.

After the show, warm greetings they convey;they're glad of their performance here today.They've many towns and places to go through,but come to Valpovo for SUMMER too.

A na Trgu kralja Tomislava"Jovalius bend" je iz Valpova,da nam pruže svu ljepotu sada,svirat će nam "Kočije" iz Narda.

And on the Square of Tomislav the Kingthe "Jovalius band" from Valpovo will sing;to give us all their beauty, now and here,"Kočije" from Nard will play for us to hear.

Slavit ćemo VALPOVAČKO LJETO,u Europi četrdeset šesto.Nek' je časna svaka smotra ova,što je ponos to grada Valpova.

We'll celebrate the VALPOVO SUMMER here,in Europe, as the forty-sixth this year.May every festival be honoured so,the pride of our own town of Valpovo.

U Europu ulazimo sada,to je dika našega naroda,zato društvo KATANČIĆA slavno,pjeva odu zahvalnicu javno.

Now into Europe we have made our way,it is our nation's pride and joy today;the KATANČIĆ society, so proud,sings out its ode of thanks to all aloud.

Valpovo, 18. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 18, 2013

Translator's note. Valpovačko ljeto (Valpovo Summer) is the town's festival. HNK is the Croatian National Theatre in Osijek, DVD the volunteer fire brigade, a KUD a folk-dance ensemble, kulen a spicy Slavonian sausage, and the Katančić society is named after the Valpovo-born poet Matija Petar Katančić. Croatia joined the EU on July 1, 2013.
