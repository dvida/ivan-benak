Give Me Back My Kisses

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Jednom u nekomkafiću smo bili.Rekla si!Ni osmijeha mi ne daš,cicija si, mili.Reci, voljena!Kada sam ti slatkuljubav uskratio?Zar ti nisam nježnamilovanja dao?Zar ti nisam poljubac,poljupcem platio?Za tople pogledeljubit sam te znao.Za svaki tvoj poljubac,po dva sam ti dao.Reci!Zašto šutiš,zar me ne čuješ?Tisuću poljubaca,ti meni, duguješ.

Once, at some café,the two of us sat.You said!You won't even spare me a smile,you skinflint, my dear.Tell me, my beloved!When did I ever keepsweet love from you?Did I not give youtender caresses?Did I not repayeach kiss with a kiss?For your warm glancesI would kiss you, it's true.For every kiss of yoursI gave you two.Tell me!Why so silent,can't you hear me?A thousand kissesis what you owe me.

Valpovo, 02. 01. 1985.

Valpovo, January 2, 1985
