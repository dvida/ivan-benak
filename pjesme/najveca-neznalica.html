The Greatest Know-Nothing

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Tko najmanje na tom svijetu znade,to je šofer kada zbraja jade.Dok umoran u kabini drijema,šofer ne zna što se njemu sprema.Ne zna kuda u zoru će poći,niti gdje će spavat ove noći.Ne zna gdje će policajca sresti,niti što će u motelu jesti.Dok udobno kupatilo sniva,u motelskom WC-u se miva.Direktora ne može da shvaća,nikad ne zna kolika mu plaća.Da l' će kola popraviti moći,i kada će svojoj kući doći.Često ne zna kakvu robu vozi,Da l' sa ženom još živi u slozi.Dok na cesti svoju mladost gubi,šofer ne zna tko mu ženu ljubi.Najveća je šofer neznalica,dok ga ljubi autostoperica.Što će dobit on nema u vidu,možda lues, a možda i sidu.Još tisuću on ne zna sitnica,eto, to je šofer neznalica.

Who in this world knows least of anyone?The driver, counting troubles one by one.While dozing in his cab, tired to the core,the driver doesn't know what lies in store.He doesn't know where he'll set off at dawn,nor where he'll sleep tonight when day is gone.He doesn't know where he will meet a cop,nor what he'll have to eat at the motel stop.While dreaming of a cosy bathroom, hewashes himself in the motel's WC.He can't make head or tail of his boss's ways,and never knows how much his job will pay.He can't tell if he'll manage to repairhis truck, or when he'll get back home from there.Often he doesn't know what freight he's got,or whether with his wife it's peace or not.While on the road he wastes his youthful life,the driver doesn't know who's kissing his wife.The driver's the biggest know-nothing there iswhile some girl hitchhiker's giving him a kiss.What he might catch, he's never stopped to guess:perhaps the pox — perhaps AIDS, no less.He doesn't know a thousand trifles more;and that's the driver, know-nothing to the core!

Valpovo, 10.01.1988.

Valpovo, January 10, 1988

Translator's note. He himself worked for years as a driver.
