Love poems

Requiem for Love

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Zar je bilo dosta sretnoga života?Zar me nikad više poljubiti neće?Ona koju volim, što mi nosi sreće.

Was that enough, then, of a life of happiness?Will she never, never give me one more kiss —she whom I love, who brings me all my bliss?

Zar je ljubav uvijek pomiješana plačem?Kao koktel sreće, a najviše bola.Zar se u ljubavi uvijek patit mora?

Is love forever mingled with our weeping?A cocktail made of joy, but mostly made of pain.Must one who loves keep suffering, again and again?

Za nagradu meni, što sam suze briso',procvjetat će rupčić, k'o mirisna ruža,jer je u njem sjeme, njena topla suza.

As my reward for all the tears that I have dried,my handkerchief will blossom like a fragrant rose,for her warm tear's the seed in it, from which it grows.

Zašto uvijek pišem na kraju života?A preskačem sreću onih prvih dana,dok još srce nije bilo puno rana?

Why do I always write when life is at its end,and skip the happiness of those first days, the start,when wounds had not yet filled up all my heart?

Zato moja pjesma svaka tugu lije,kao ova sada, kojoj suze brišem.Dok u agoniji osmijeh tugu krije,ja ljubavi svojoj sad opijelo pišem.

That's why each poem of mine pours out its grief,like this one now, whose tears I'm wiping as I write;while in its agony a smile hides grief beneath,I'm writing for my love its funeral rite.

Valpovo, 01. 03. 1974.

Valpovo, March 1, 1974
