Occasional verse and dedications

10 Years of Poeta

Nek' Ti budu sklone Muze,poezija nek' Ti cvjeta,s radošću Ti knjigu dajem:"10 GODINA POETA".Osnovao sam POETEi nije mi nikad krivo,družila se Ti sa nama,to Ti želi - Benak Ivo.

May the Muses smile upon you,may your poetry bloom anew;"10 Years of Poeta" — gladlyI present this book to you.It was I who founded Poeta,and I've never rued it, no;may you keep us company:that's the wish of Benak Ivo.

Translator's note. A verse inscription written in a copy of the anthology 10 godina Poeta ("10 Years of Poeta"), which marked the tenth year of Poeta, the society of poets in Valpovo that he founded.
