Kravica i koka mala

Poems for children

The Little Cow and the Little Hen

Kravica i koka malasvašta su nam, djeco, dala,za ta svoja dobra djela,baš ni jedna nije znala.

Little cow and little hengave us, children, much; but then,of these good deeds they had doneneither knew it — no, not one!

Vi istinu znate pravu:kako koka pase travu.Za kravicu drugo važi:po dvorištu zrnje traži.

You all know the real truth, then:grass is grazed by the little hen.For the cow, it's different again:round the yard she hunts for grain.

Zato ću vam sada, djeco,ispričati prave priče:kako koka mlijeko daje,krava leže nam piliće.

So I'll tell you, children, now,the real stories, as is due:how the hen gives milk, and howthe cow hatches chicks for you.

Vi kažite meni, djeco,da ta priča nije tako.Po vašem bi ona sada,bila posve naopako.

Children, you tell me it's not so,that's not how the tale is said;by your telling, it would goupside down, heels over head.

To znači da naša krava,svakog dana nese jaja,a kokica ona prava,nama male telce daje.

That means our cow, every day,lays her eggs, just as you say,and the hen, the real one, too,gives small calves to me and you.

Shvatio sam, djeco, sada,onu pravu vašu priču:krava se i koka svađe,pa jedna na drugu viču.

Now I understand it, children,that true story that you tell:cow and hen have had a quarrel,at each other how they yell!

Kravica je naša Bjelka,cijeli dan na paši bila,a navečer slatkog mlijeka,dječici je podarila.

Our little cow called Bjelkaspent all day out in the field,and at evening, milk so sweetfor the children she would yield.

Nato krava veli važno:„moje mlijeko nije lažno,zato molim, da se zna,njega vole djeca sva.“

Then the cow said, with an air:"My good milk's not fake, I swear,so let everybody know:all the children love it so."

A koka će nato snažno:„znam da mlijeko nije lažno,al' da život duže traje,ja darujem svoje jaje.“

Then the hen said, loud and clear:"I know milk's no fake, my dear,but to make life last, I say,I will give my egg away."

Sada znaju pravu priču,samo šute i ne viču.Pa i one dobro znaju,da ljudima sve trebaju.

Now they know the story true,and they're quiet — shouting's through;and they, too, know very wellpeople need them, truth to tell.

Valpovo, 14.01.2014.

Valpovo, January 14, 2014

Translator's note. A topsy-turvy game with the children, in which he folds in his earlier poem The Cow and the Hen. Bjelka ("Whitey") is a common name for a white cow.
