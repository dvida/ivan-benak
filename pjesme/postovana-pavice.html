Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Pavice

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Pavica

Posložio sam natpiseod naslova Tvojih knjiga,da bi postala ova pjesma:

I have put together headingsfrom the titles of your books,so that this poem would come to be:

PJEVAM TEBI SLAVONIJO!DUŠA MOJA OTVORILA VRATA!SLAVONIJA U ZJENICI OKA!Oduvijek si dio srca moga.

I SING TO YOU, SLAVONIA!MY SOUL HAS OPENED ITS DOOR!SLAVONIA, APPLE OF MY EYE!You've always been a part of my heart.

Za književnost svoj si obol dala,Slavoniju dragu opjevala,mnoge pjesme njoj si isčipkala,i tri knjige u javnost poslala.

To literature you gave your mite, and thenyou sang of dear Slavonia with your pen;and many poems for her you worked like lace,and sent three books out to the public's gaze.

Najsretnija u životu bilai kroz život divne snove snila.Puno svojih knjiga iznjedrila,čitateljstvu srcem podarila.

May you be happiest your whole life through,and dream through life the loveliest dreams, too;may you bring forth your many books, and impartthem to your readers, given from the heart.

Lacičima svoju mladost dala,s najdražima sreću uživala,svoje pjesme tu si rimovala,da bi Tebe Slavonija znala.

To Lacići you gave your youth, and sharedyour happiness with those for whom you cared;and there you set your poems into rhyme,so that Slavonia would know you in time.

Mnogo ljeta s nama se družila,u druženju pjesmice čitala,svoje knjige često predstavljala,prijatelje drage pogostila.

For many years you've kept us company,and in our gatherings read your poetry;and often you presented books you'd penned,and hosted at your table each dear friend.

Te navike i dalje ostvari,a najdraže sa stihom podari.Svoju radost s drugima podijeli,od srca Ti Benak Ivo želi.

Keep up these habits, carry on, and stillgive verses to your dearest, as you will;and share your joy with others. For my part,I, Benak Ivo, wish you this from the heart.

Valpovo, 29. 05. 2010.

Valpovo, May 29, 2010

Translator's note. Written for the launch of Pavica Kusturić's book Pjevam tebi, Slavonijo (I Sing to You, Slavonia). The second stanza is made from the titles of her books. Lacići is her village.
