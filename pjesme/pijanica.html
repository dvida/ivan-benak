The Drunkard

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Slušam violinukako bolno jeca.Ja pijan teturami skidam joj sveca.Što mi srce para,dira stare rane?Dok mi vrijeme tihoodbrojava dane.Sada nemam snageda pogledam smjelo.U moj bolni životni u sunce vrelo.Žena me je ostavila,sve zbog rujna vina,sa sobom je sve odnijelai voljenog sina.Više nemam žene,da me doma čeka.Nemam ni ognjišta,da me toplo grije,ni postelje nemam,gdje se noću leži.I zato ja pijem,jer me piće tješi.

I hear the violin,its painful, sobbing cry.I stagger, drunk, and damnher saints up there on high.What's tearing at my heartand stirs old wounds long past,while time so quietlycounts down the days I'll last?Now I've no strength leftto cast a fearless eyeupon my painful lifeor on the hot sun high.My wife walked out on me,all for the ruby wine;she took it all with her,and that dear son of mine.I've no wife any moreto wait for me at home.I've not even a hearthto warm me through and through,nor bed, where one lies downat night to take one's rest.And that is why I drink:the drink consoles me best.

Valpovo, 06. 04. 1975.

Valpovo, April 6, 1975

Translator's note. "Taking down someone's saints" is a Croatian way of cursing; here it is rendered as "damn her saints".
