The Last Kiss

Srušila se zemlja jedne crne noći.Pod nogama mojim kad si rekla zbogom,i da nikad više ti mi nećeš doći.Zar, zaista, više neću biti s tobom?

The earth gave way beneath my feet one dark nightwhen you bade me goodbye, and told me toothat you would never come again into my sight.Can it be true that I'll no longer be with you?

One bolne noći, ti nisi ni znala.U naručju mome milim si me zvala.I posljednji cjelov kada si mi dala:iz tužnoga oka suza ti je pala.

That painful night, you didn't even know;you called me "dear" as in my arms you lay,and when you gave the final kiss to me,out of your mournful eye a tear fell away.

Oh, kako je sada tiho oko mene:ni poj ptica više ne čujem k'o prije.Od ljubavi naše ostale su sjene!U srcu mi čemer k'o u kotlu vrije.

Oh, how quiet it has grown around me now:I cannot hear the birdsong as I used to do.Of all our love, just shadows now remain!My heart's a cauldron where the bitter sorrows brew.

Kroz moj bolni život ti si prošetala.Poljupcima vrelim rane si vidala.Milujući tebe sreća mi je sjala,sada sa mog neba zvijezda je nestala.

Through my life of pain you strolled along your way;with burning kisses you would heal my every wound;caressing you, my happiness shone every day;now from my sky a star has vanished, gone away.

Valpovo, 18. 10. 1974.

Valpovo, October 18, 1974
