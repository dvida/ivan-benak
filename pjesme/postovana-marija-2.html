Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Marija!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Marija!

Onaj pozdrav koji ste mi dali,na skupštini umirovljenika,ostat će mi kao uspomena,draga mi je to bila prilika.

The greeting that you gave to me that dayat the pensioners' assembly, where we met,will stay with me, a keepsake; and I'd say,a dear occasion, one I won't forget.

Prijateljski osmijeh s Vašeg lica,toga časa bila mi je dika.Slušajući moje recitale,Vi cijenite mene k'o pjesnika.

The friendly smile I saw upon your facewas, in that hour, a source of pride to me;and listening to me as I recite,you value me as poet, I can see.

Poštovana gospođo Marija,to je bila jedna radost mala,ta ljepota knjigu Vam je dala,koja će Vam sama reći: HVALA!

Respected Madam Marija, it's true,that was a little joy, a small delight;that beauty now has given this book to you,which by itself will say "Thank you!", as is right.

Valpovo, 27. 05. 2013.

Valpovo, May 27, 2013

Translator's note. Written in a book for Marija Sužnjević, president of the county pensioners' association, to thank her for her greeting at the pensioners' assembly.
