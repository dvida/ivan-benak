Occasional verse and dedications

A Review

Lipanjskoga toplog dana,umilni glas jedne žene,nježno me je zamolio,da radove vidim njene.

On a warm June day, a woman,with a gentle, winning tone,asked me tenderly to lookat some poems of her own.

I prođoše dva - tri dana,radove sam pregledaoi na opće zadovoljstvo,mišljenje sam svoje dao.

Two or three days went their way;I looked through the works she'd penned,and, to everyone's content,gave my verdict in the end.

"Pišete u dobrom stilu,za pjesmu je važna rimai određen broj slogova,a to Vaša svaka ima".

"You write in a pleasing style;for a poem, rhyme's the key,and a fixed count of syllables —each of yours has these, I see."

Nastavite samo takoi neće Vam biti krivo.Knjigu ćete napisati,garantira pjesnik Ivo!

Just keep going as you are,and you won't regret it, no.You are going to write a book:poet Ivo guarantees it so!

Kao pjesnik sada želim,da mi bude časno lice.Tu posvetu sada pišemMariji iz Čađavice!

As a poet, now I wishthat I keep an honest face;so I write this dedicationto Marija of Čađavica!

Valpovo, 24. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 24, 2013

Translator's note. A verse "review" of the poems of Marija from Čađavica, written as a dedication.
