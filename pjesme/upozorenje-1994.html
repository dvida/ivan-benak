Humorous poems

A Warning

1994 · from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Oduvijek si lopov bio,ne stidiš se svog zanata.Hajde, huljo, uzmi alati razvali ova vrata.

You have always been a thief,never shamed by what you do.Come on, scoundrel, take your tools:smash this door, I'm telling you!

Kad učiniš to nedjelo,u tuđi ćeš posjed zaći,razočarat ćeš se jako,jer što tražiš, nećeš naći.

When you've done this wicked deed,stepped inside another's place,you'll be sorely disappointed:what you're after? Not a trace.

U toj zgradi ništa nema,što bi tebi koristilo.A motiku za kopanje,nikad nisi ni volio.

In this building there is nothingthat could be of use to you.And a hoe to dig with? Thatyou have never liked, it's true.

Valpovo, 21. 05. 1994.

Valpovo, May 21, 1994

Translator's note. After several break-ins he posted this poem on the door of his weekend cottage.
