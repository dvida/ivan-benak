Radio burza

Prodaja salonit ploča

Radio market

Salonit Roof Sheets for Sale

Jučer skidoh ja ploče sa kuće,ugodne su fine, još su vruće.Sedam grba svaka ploča ima,razmak grba šesnaest je centima.Moram reći sada ovo svima,dimenziju ploča ovu ima:Jedan metar ploče su širine,a jedan i dvadeset dužine.Svaka zgrada takvu ploču prima,zato ploča gratis šaraf ima.Još ni jedna napuknuta nije,cijeli metar kvadratni pokrije.I još jedna sitnica tu važi,deset kuna za ploču se traži.A telefon ost'o je k'o prije,sve do sada mijenjao se nije.Javite se ljudi ovaj tjedan,na: šesto pedeset i dva,osamsto dvadeset jedan.

I took the sheets off my house yesterday;they're nice and fine — still hot, I'd have to say.Each sheet of them has seven ridges, andsixteen centimetres apart they stand.And now I have to tell you all as wellwhat size these sheets are — here is what I'll tell:one metre is the width of every sheet,and one metre twenty long, complete.Each building takes a sheet like this, and soeach sheet comes with a free screw, you know.Not one of them has any crack in it;each covers a whole square metre, every bit.And one more little detail I should say:ten kunas for a sheet is what you pay.My telephone has stayed the same as before;it hasn't changed at all till now, what's more.So give me a call this week, folks, every one,on: six hundred fifty-two,eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 14. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 14, 2010

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza. Salonit was a well-known brand of corrugated asbestos-cement roofing sheets.
