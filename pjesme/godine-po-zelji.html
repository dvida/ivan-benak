Umirovljenički jadi

Godine po želji

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

Years to Order

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kao u stara dobra vremena,dok je još Bog hodao po zemlji.Sa Olimpa Eros dobrostivo veli:Zbor penzioneri, mladost se dijeli.Koliko tko godina sada želi?

It was as in the good old days, back whenthe Lord still walked upon the earth with men.From high Olympus, Eros, kind, would say:"Assemble, pensioners! Youth's given away!How many years does each one want today?"

Prva se javi baka Mara:"Ja jesam odavno, ali nisam stara.Molim te, Erose, trideset mi smanji,da prkosim svojoj priji Janji."

Then Granny Mara spoke up first of all:"I've been around for ages — but old? Not at all!Please, Eros, take off thirty years from me,to spite my in-law Janja — let her see!"

Na to će djeda od osamdeset:"Hajde, Erose, skini pedeset,da mogu opet u svome stanukao nekada obljubiti malu Anu."

Then Grandpa, who was eighty, had his say:"Come on, Eros, take fifty years away,so in my flat, the way I did back then,I might make love to little Ana again."

"Ova mi starost strašno smeta,"strogo će bivši direktor reći."Skidaj, Erose, trideset ljeta,sa sekretaricom svojom želim leći!"

"This old age of mine annoys me no end,"the former director sternly said."Eros, take thirty summers off, my friend —I want to take my secretary to bed!"

Onda se javi raspuštenica Kata,bivša radnica starog zanata.

Then Kata spoke, the divorcée, in turn —the old trade was the way she used to earn.

"Mušterije moje više me ne šljive,za sve su ovo godine krive.Za trideset ljeta ja bih sve dala,da opet procvjetam k'o bijela lala."

"My customers don't give a fig for me,and all the blame lies on my age, you see.For thirty summers I'd give all I own,to bloom again, a tulip white, full-blown."

Tako se molba za molbom nižena kraju stari Kazanova stiže.

So plea on plea went stringing down the line,till last came Casanova, past his prime.

"Skini mi toliko mjesečevih mijenakoliko sam ljubio u životu žena.Tada bi, Erose, sve lijepe ženekao mladića opet ljubile mene."

"Take off as many moons from me, I pray,as all the women I have loved in my day.Then, Eros, all the beauties, I'm quite sure,would kiss me as a young man, like before."

Vidi Bog ljubavi o čemu se radi,da starcima malo patnju osladi,on pusti glazbu, a vino potečei ljubav se vodila čitavu veče.

The god of love saw what it was about,and so, to sweeten old folks' pain somewhat,he let the music play, the wine flow free,and love was made all evening, merrily.

U to sa istoka zlatna zora sinei staračke snove u maglu raspline.

Then from the east the golden dawn shone clearand made the old folks' dreams in mist disappear.

Valpovo, 13. 10. 1997.

Valpovo, October 13, 1997

Translator's note. A humorous tale. A "prija" (here "in-law") is the mother of one's son- or daughter-in-law, a traditional rival; "the old trade" hints at the oldest profession.
