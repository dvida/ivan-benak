Domoljubne i zavičajne

Marijanci dika Slavonije

Homeland and native region

Marijanci, Pride of Slavonia

U istočnoj hrvatskoj ravnici,povijesni nam kazuju podaci.U podravskom dijelu Slavonije,smjestilo se selo Marijanci.

Upon the plain of eastern Croatia,as history's own records let us see,within Slavonia's lands along the Dravathere lies the village of Marijanci.

Na njedrima Panonije ravne,agrarom se ovdje ljudi bave.Povezani sa tri divne rijeke:Karašice, Vučice i Drave.

Upon the breast of level Pannoniathe people here are farmers, through and through,and they are linked with three delightful rivers:the Karašica, Vučica, Drava too.

Bila crkva u trinaestom vijeku,od Marije Djeve zaštićena,osamnaestog vijeka obnovljena,svetom Petru i Pavlu posvećena.

There stood a church in the thirteenth century,protected by the Virgin Mary's care,and in the eighteenth century restored,to Saints Peter and Paul was hallowed there.

Marijanci školu izgradili.Obitelj je nekad bila jaka.Sto trideset, a nekad i više,u školi je znalo biti đaka.

The Marijanci people built a school;the families were strong in days of yore;the school would count a hundred thirty pupilswithin its walls at times, and even more.

Od devetsto devedeset treće,Marijanačka Općina postoji.Dvije tisuće sedamsto dvadeset,ona svojih stanovnika broji.

Since nineteen ninety-three there has existedthe Municipality of Marijanci;two thousand seven hundred twenty peopleit counts as its inhabitants today.

Općina je tu i prije bila,već odavno, dosta ljeta ima.Šest je sela k sebi prigrlila,a na ponos svojim seljanima.

The township had been here in days before,long, long ago; it's many summers old.Six villages it gathered to its breast,and to its people's pride, held in its fold.

A devetsto dvadeset i sedme,knjižnicu su ljudi osnovali.Za kulturu svoj obol su dali,„SLAVONIJA VILA“ je nazvali.

And then in nineteen hundred twenty-seventhe people here a library had made;their offering to culture thus was paid,and "Slavonija Vila" was its name.

Dvije tisuće i četvrtog ljeta,dični ljudi KUD su osnovali,„SLAVONIJA VILA“ ga nazvali,na Ćimenu kolo odigrali.

In two thousand and four, that very year,fine people founded here their village KUD,and "Slavonija Vila" named it, as they should,and on the Ćimen danced the kolo through.

Na Ćimenu kad zaigra kolo,tankovito kad vrisne šokica,i kad cikne na tamburi žica,tada ječi slavonska ravnica.

When on the Ćimen the kolo starts to spin,and high and thin a Šokica cries out,and when the tambura's string rings sharp and clear,then all Slavonia's plain resounds about.

Tisuću devetsto dvadeset osme,ravnatelj iz škole Marijanci,osnovo' je vatrogasno društvo,u svom radu to su pravi znalci.

In nineteen hundred twenty-eight, the headwho ran the school at Marijanci thenset up the fire brigade, and they are, still,in all their work true masters, skilful men.

Sa ponosom u svom postojanju,osamdeset ljeta proslavili.Koliko su požara gasili?To bi samo oni reći znali.

With pride in all the years of their existencethey've marked their eightieth year, and marked it well.How many fires have they put out? That's somethingthat only they themselves could ever tell.

Marijanci ambulantu imai doktora da pomogne svima.Dječji vrtić „PINOKIO“ zvani,u tom kraju svima dobro znani.

In Marijanci there's a clinic, anda doctor, too, to give us all a hand;a kindergarten named "Pinokio",which everyone around here's come to know.

Marijanačka pošta gordo stoji,već godina ona puno ima,ponosna je u svojemu radu,jer usluge pruža seljanima.

The Marijanci post office proudly stands,already it has seen a lot of years;it's proud of all the work it does, becauseit serves the needs of all the villagers.

Trgovina ovdje ima dostai robe je od njih svaka puna.Kupili bi ljudi sve i svašta,ali narod nema dosta kuna.

Of shops, there are a good few here, it's true,and each of them is stocked with goods galore;the people would buy all sorts, great and small,but kunas are too few to buy much more.

Nogometno društvo selo ima,to je „HAJDUK“, na diku je svima,najbolji je u ovome kraju,pa za njega mnoga sela znaju.

The village has a football club, as well,"Hajduk", the pride of all, as you can tell;it is the best in all this countryside,and many villages know it, far and wide.

„MATAKOVO“ čuvena je farma,obrađuje njive, oranice:Bobanjica, Grilje i Sovica,radi mlijeka koje vole djeca.

"Matakovo" is a famous farm, and thereit works the fields and ploughlands, far and near:Bobanjica, Grilje and Sovica —for milk that all the children hold so dear.

„MARJANČANKA“ ima peradarstvo,hrani koke koje nesu jaja.Pa uz mlijeko i kokino jaje,taj nam život puno duže traje.

And "Marjančanka" keeps a poultry farm,and feeds the hens that lay their eggs each day;so, with the milk, and with a hen's fresh egg,our lives last longer by a good long way.

Za sad tol'ko, već bi bilo dosta,nekoliko još birtija osta'.U Marijance na kirvaj otiđi,gostione sve redom obiđi.

That's it for now, I think that's quite enough —a few more taverns still are left, it's true;to Marijanci, for the church fair, go,and visit every inn, the whole way through.

Valpovo, 16. 04. 2011.

Valpovo, April 16, 2011

Translator's note. A chronicle of the village of Marijanci, from its medieval church to its farms and taverns. The Ćimen is the green where its folklore festival is danced, a KUD is a village folklore society, the church fair (kirvaj) is the village feast on its patron's day, and the kuna was the Croatian currency.
