Osječka je najbolja pivara

Occasional verse and dedications

Osijek's Is the Finest Brewery

Nek' Vam bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas pjesnik Benak Ivo.Često gledam Slavonsko veselje,tamburaši ispunjaju želje.Goran vodi priredbu uživo,pokrovitelj Osječko je pivo.Podmiruju obaveze svoje,a pred Lacom dvije boce stoje.Osječkoga što se rado pije,u Hrvatskoj to je najstarije.Da Vam kažem nešto o pivari:od davnina temelji joj stari.Kad bi znali, dragi ljudi moji,kol'ko ljeta pivovara broji?Godine je sagrađena jedne:šest stotina devedeset sedme.To je bilo sedamnaestog vijeka,u ŠAMAČKOJ ulici Osijeka.Dvadesetak koraka uz Dravu,sagradio ŠEPER pivovaru.Nakon ručka kad je dobro jelo,rado pijem ja Osječko bijelo.Al' kad auto ja vozim uživo,onda pijem samo BAJKER pivo.Pa i Radler tada rado pijem,to su piva koja piti smijem.Za dobroga pivopiju važim,ŠEPEROVO pivo svuda tražim.Vjerujte mi, istina je živa,rado pijem ja ESEKER piva.Mnogo sreće u Osijeku gradu,pivarcima u njihovom radu.Da nikada za štrajk ne bi znali,ni u krizu financijsku pali.Da im kasa bude uvijek puna,za blokadu ne znali računa.Nek' radnici u pivari radei redovno da primaju plaće.Eto, sve sam lijepo objasnio,samo da bih nagradu dobio.Sada znate želja mi je živa,da osvojim koju bocu piva.Svi prisutni ovdje sretni bilii Osječko pivo u slast pili.Jer Osječko priprema se pivo,to vam jasno veli pjesnik Ivo:u kristalnoj našoj čistoj vodi,zato svakom pivopiji godi.Sada idem vrlo dobre volje,u pivaru na Zeleno Polje,da se javim gospođi Boženiza nagradu da uruči meni.

May merry, lively times be yours, and soyou're greeted by the poet Benak Ivo.I often watch Slavonsko veselje's show;the tambura players grant requests, you know.Goran presents the programme, live on air;Osječko beer's the sponsor of the affair.Their obligations they fulfil, all right,and there in front of Laco, two bottles in sight.Osječko, which folks love to drink, is, well,the oldest in Croatia, I can tell.Let me tell you something of the brewery here:its old foundations go back many a year.Now, would you know, my dear good people, pray,how many years the brewery counts today?In one year it was built, I'll tell you when:the year six hundred ninety-seven, then.That was the seventeenth century, you see,in ŠAMAČKA street in Osijek city.Some twenty paces from the Drava's shore,and there ŠEPER built the brewery of yore.When after lunch I've had a hearty bite,I like to drink Osječko, the white.But when I'm driving my car out and about,I drink just BAJKER beer, without a doubt.And Radler, too, I like to drink, you see:those are the beers that are allowed to me.I pass as quite a decent beer man, too;I look for ŠEPER's beer the whole world through.Believe me, it's as true as true can be:ESEKER beer's a favourite for me.Much luck in Osijek to all the crewof brewers, in the work they have to do.May they not ever know a strike, nor fallinto financial crisis, not at all.May their cash box be always full, and maythey never see their accounts blocked one day.And may the workers in the brewery stayat work, and regularly get their pay.There now, I have explained it all, you see,just so a prize might be awarded me.So now you know my wish, alive and clear:to win myself a bottle or two of beer.May all who're present here be full of cheerand drink with relish their Osječko beer.Because Osječko beer, I'll tell you so,the poet Ivo says it plain, you know,is brewed in our clear water, crystal-pure,and so it suits each beer man, to be sure.And now in fine good humour off I goto the brewery at Zeleno Polje, soI can report to Mrs Božena, and seeif she will hand the prize across to me.

Valpovo, 20. 06. 2012.

Valpovo, June 20, 2012

Translator's note. A verse entry sent to the tamburica TV show Slavonsko veselje, sponsored by Osječko beer, in hope of winning a few bottles. Šeper founded the Osijek brewery in 1697; Eseker (from Essek, the old German name of Osijek) is one of its beers, and Zeleno Polje is where the brewery stands today.
