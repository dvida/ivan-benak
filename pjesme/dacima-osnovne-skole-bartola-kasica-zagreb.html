Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Đacima Osnovne škole Bartola Kašića, Zagreb

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Pupils of Bartol Kašić Primary School, Zagreb

Jednog dana usred bareSastale se mnoge žabe,Da se bore protiv rode,Usred ove barske vode.

One day, in the middle of a bog,there assembled many a frogto stand up against the stork,right amid the marshy murk.

Ja vas molim pročitajte,Što u bari žabe rade.Sastanak su održaleDa se riješe rode mlade.

Please, I beg you, read it through:what the marsh frogs get up to.And they held a meeting, sothat the young stork had to go!

Ak' vam ova knjiga mojaZadovoljstva pruži maloI meni bi, vjerujte mi,Toga časa radost dalo!

If this book of mine should bringyou some pleasure, just a touch,then, believe me, at that momentit would please me very much!

Poštovani, dragi đaci,Divne škole svi prvaci,Dobar uspjeh i veseljeRoditelji vam a žele!

Honoured pupils, children dear,first-years of a fine school here,good success and gladness tooare your parents' wish for you!

Valpovo, 10. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 10, 2013

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Žablji sastanak ("The Frogs' Meeting").
