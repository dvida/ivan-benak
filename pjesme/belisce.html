Homeland and native region

Belišće

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Prostrla se tvornica kraj Drave,u dolini Slavonije ravne.Tu se razni proizvodi prave.Belišće je mjesto pokraj Drave.

Along the Drava spreads a factory,within the vale of flat Slavonia,and here all kinds of products come to be.Belišće is the town beside the Drava.

Vrijedne ruke, tu su mudre glave,kao pčele svi marljivo radei svoj uspjeh svi jednako slave.Belišće je mjesto pokraj Drave.

Hard-working hands are here, and clever brains;like bees, they all work busily away,and all alike they celebrate their gains.Belišće is the town beside the Drava.

U naselju domovi se prave,kao gljive što na kiši niču,tu su cure garave i plave.Belišće je mjesto pokraj Drave.

New houses in the settlement appearlike mushrooms springing up amid the rain;the girls are dark of hair, and some are fair.Belišće is the town beside the Drava.

Jednom ljubljah djevojku kraj Drave.Pa i sada kada se tog sjetim,preda mnom su njene oči plave.Oj, Belišće mjesto pokraj Drave!

Once by the Drava I loved a maiden true;and even now, when it comes back to me,her eyes are there before me, eyes of blue.Oh, Belišće, the town beside the Drava!

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979

Translator's note. Belišće is an industrial town on the Drava that grew up around the Gutmann timber works.
