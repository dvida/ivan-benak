Protestne i društvene

Dug dobili bez para ostali

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

Debt Repaid, and Still No Money

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ovo djelo nastalo je tada,kad je stigla Sanadera Vlada.Na TV-u mi gledamo sadaiz sveg glasa viče naša Vlada.Dio duga da bi nama dala,za izbore što je obećala.Ali sporo vrši obećanje,valjda čeka da nas bude manje.Jer za onog tko vječno zadrijema,po zakonu mirovine nema.Ovaj problem Vladi baš ne smeta,riješit će ga za desetak ljeta.Financijski ministar će glasno:para nema, to je valjda jasno.Da podmire mirovinske daće,ne bi Vlada imala za plaće.Na to Vlada veli malo tiše,rješenja će svima da napiše.Da dug vrati penzićima sada,dva modela nudi naša Vlada.Prvi model, toliko da znate,na dva ljeta, na četiri rate.Al' da ne bi postali tajkuni,polovicom svoju kasu puni.

This poem came to be at that same hourwhen Sanader's Government came into power.Now on TV we sit and watch each nightour Government shout out with all its mightthat part of what is owed it would repay,as it had promised on election day.But it is slow to keep the promise made —perhaps it's waiting till our numbers fade.For he who falls asleep forevermoregets, by the law, no pension anymore.This problem doesn't bother them one bit:in ten years' time or so, they'll settle it.The Finance Minister gives out a shout:"There is no money — that is clear, no doubt."Were they to pay the pension dues, they say,the Government would have no wage to pay.At that the Government, in quieter tone,says it'll write solutions for everyone.To pay back pensioners the debt they're due,our Government is offering models — two.The first, just so you know the way it's laid:in two years, and four instalments, it's paid.But so we don't become tycoons, you know,it fills its coffers with half of the dough.

Puno para za sad ne bi dala,stara srca ne bi izdržala.Misli Vlada na zdravstveno stanjei riješit će svoje obećanje.Drugi model, ovako izgleda:sve se vraća tek za deset ljeta.Dv'je godine imaš poček sada,da kamatu još pobere Vlada.Sve dugove starcima da vrate,zato Vlada dug vraća na rate.Blago nama, tu nema što reći,dugovi će na kapaljku teći.Sve je jasno, nema smicalice,starcima će dati crkavice.Da se ne bi možda tko žaliosvetom Petru kad bi doselio.Eto što se penzićima sprema,dug je vraćen, ali para nema.Dok u Vladi moduse nalaze,u blaženstva penzići odlaze.Radili smo i zato smo krivi,blago onom tko sve to preživi.Još jedino sada starce tješi,dragi Bogo sve će to da riješi.

It wouldn't give us too much cash as yet —old hearts could never stand it, don't forget.The Government is mindful of our health,and it will sort its promise out itself.The second model looks like this, instead:it all comes back in ten years' time, it's said.You've two years' grace to start with, so that stillthe Government can gather interest at will.To pay the old folk back what they are due,so the Government pays in instalments too.Oh, lucky us! There's nothing more to say:the debts will trickle, drop by drop, our way.It's all quite clear, no trick or sleight of hand:the old folk will get crumbs, you understand,so nobody, perhaps, will lodge a pleawith Saint Peter once he's moved in, you see.That's what the pensioners have got in store:the debt is paid — of cash, there is no more.While in the Government they seek the ways,the pensioners pass on to blissful days.We worked, and so we are the guilty ones;and lucky he who lives till it's all done.The only thing that comforts old folk now:dear God will sort it all out, anyhow.

Valpovo, 30.07.2004.

Valpovo, July 30, 2004

Translator's note. Satire on the 2004 plan of Ivo Sanader's government to repay pensioners, in instalments, the pension money withheld from them in the 1990s.
