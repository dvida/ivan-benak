Tamburaško subotnje veselje

Occasional verse and dedications

Saturday Tambura Merriment

JA POZDRAVLJAM LJUDE HRV-AŠTO PRUŽAJU SLUŠATELJU ŽELJE

I send my greetings to the HRV crew,who make the listeners' wishes all come true.

Šest pet jedan petsto devedeset,narod zove telefoni brujeemisija počinje u desetTomislava nježni glas se čuje.

Six five one, five hundred ninety:people call, the phones are buzzing too;at ten o'clock the programme starts its run,and Tomislav's gentle voice is coming through.

U subotu dan je običajapo želji se emisija dajeu tom času ljepota se zbrajasve do dvanaest emisija traje.

On Saturday it is the custom, sothe programme's made by listeners' request;in that hour all the beauty's added up,till twelve o'clock it runs, without a rest.

To je za nas emisija pravada ostvariš svoje drage željekroz čestitke i voljene pjesmeSvome srcu da pružiš veselje.

For us, this is the programme that is right,to make your dearest wishes all come true,through greetings and the songs that you hold dear,to give your heart some joy and gladness, too.

Subotom se i svatovi slavepa ljepota sokacima tečeovog časa prigoda je pravada poželiš mladencima sreće.

On Saturdays the weddings, too, are held,and beauty flows along the sokaks here;this very hour's the right occasion, then,to wish the newlyweds good luck and cheer.

Uto baka tihim glasom zoveTomislav se umiljato javipuno sreće svom unuku želijer za nju je to užitak pravi.

Just then a grandma calls, her voice is low,and Tomislav picks up, so warm and sweet;she wishes her dear grandson lots of luck,since that, for her, is pleasure most complete.

A djeda je danas sretan bio,pa mu srce od radosti tučedragi unuk što se tek rodiosluša pjesmu bakino unuče.

And Grandpa, too, was happy there today,his heart is pounding with delight and cheer:their dear new grandson, only just now born,hears the song as well, Grandma's little dear.

Šezdesetu obljetnicu brakakum Iveta sa pjesmom im želikumovima iz starog sokakazajedno su sretno ostarili.

For their sixtieth wedding anniversarykum Iveta sends his wishes in a songto his kumovi from the old sokak,who've grown old happily, their whole lives long.

Dva mladića sa radom i znanjempredivnu su radost odigraliposebnu im ja čestitku dajemSvojim radom ljepotu su dali.

Two young men, with their work and their know-how,have played a wondrous joy for us today;my special compliments to them I pay:their work has given beauty to us now.

Divne pjesme, a tambure ječe,emisija što ugodno traje,a za mene ako bude srećeodsvirajte šokačke običaje!

The songs are lovely, and the tamburas ring,the programme runs so pleasantly and long;and as for me, if luck should come my way,play me the Šokac customs in a song!

To je bila emisija prava,nek' im bude veselo i živošto su dali ljudi HRV-apozdravlja ih pjesnik Benak Ivo.

That was a proper programme, through and through;may it be merry for them, full of go;for all the people of HRV gave, too,they're greeted by the poet, Benak Ivo.

Valpovo, 03. 05. 2014.

Valpovo, May 3, 2014

Translator's note. For the Saturday request show of HRV (Radio Valpovo), hosted by Tomislav: listeners phone in greetings and song requests. A kum is a godfather or wedding sponsor, a lifelong bond between families; kumovi is the plural.
