Domoljubne i zavičajne

Belišćanski pijac

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

The Belišće Market

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Belišćanski pijac staripotopila Drava,pa je zato načelnikazaboljela glava.

Belišće's old market-placedrowned when the Drava rose,and the mayor got a headachefrom these watery woes.

Da se gradi pijac novitreba puno para,al' načelnik, vjerujte mi,za to ima dara.

Building a new market takesmoney by the ton,but for that the mayor, trust me,is a gifted one.

Stručnjake je sakupiou Belišću gradu.Materijal nabavili,prionuli radu.

He brought experts all togetherhere in Belišće town;they got in the materialsand to work they buckled down.

Temelji su preko zimepostavljeni bili,u proljeće neimaripijac sagradili.

Over winter the foundationshad been laid in place;in the spring the master buildersraised the market space.

I tako su ostvareninačelnika snovi,a Belišće sada krasidivni pijac novi.

That is how the mayor's dreamsall at last came true,and now Belišće is gracedby a market, fine and new.

Dv'je tisuće petog ljetaovo se desilo,dvanaestog je maja zdanjeotvoreno bilo.

In the year two thousand fivethis is what was done:on the twelfth of May the buildingopened to everyone.

Tom prigodom skuhao jeDelač vrlo vješto,fin čobanac, u kotliću,od litara šesto.

For the opening Delač cooked,with the greatest skill,fine čobanac in the cauldron,six hundred litres' fill.

U Belišću sad je pijackakvog nećeš naći,u tri naše županijeni u samoj Mađi!

Now Belišće has a marketlike you won't find elsewhere,not in all of our three counties,nor in Hungary, I swear!

Valpovo, 12. 05. 2005.

Valpovo, May 12, 2005

Translator's note. Written for the opening of Belišće's new market on May 12, 2005, after the Drava had flooded the old one. Delač and his six-hundred-litre cauldron also appear in "The Little Cauldron of Belišće"; "Mađa" is a colloquial name for Hungary.
