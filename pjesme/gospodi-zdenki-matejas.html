Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Gospođi Zdenki Matejaš

Occasional verse and dedications

To Mrs Zdenka Matejaš

Poštovana gospa Zdenka!Ja Vas prije nisam znaoi prođoše deset danaZekinu Vam Sreću dao.

Most esteemed, dear Madam Zdenka!I had never known you, true,and it's ten days since the dayBunny's Luck I gave to you.

Upoznah divnu ženu,njezino sam zvanje htio.Stvarajući ovu knjigu,djeci sam ju namijenio.

A fine woman then I met:hers the calling that I sought;as I made this book, it wasfor the children that I wrote.

Sada šaljem drugu knjigu:SASTANAK se ŽABA zove.Siguran sam da će djeci,knjiga dati divne snove.

Now I send a second book:it is called The Frogs' Meeting.I am sure this book will givechildren lovely dreams to keep.

Sastale se mnoge žabe,usred ove barske vode,iz sveg glasa kreketale,da se bore protiv rode.

Many frogs have met to talkin the middle of the marsh,croaking loud with all their mighthow to fight against the stork.

Zato knjigu ovu, Zdenka,morat ćete proučiti,da bi mogli Vi dječici,žablji problem objasniti.

That's why, Zdenka, you will needto study this book and learn,so that to the little onesyou can explain the frogs' concern.

Sedamnaestog ovo slovim,VELJAČA se mjesec piše,dvije tisuće i trinaeste,jedna knjiga Zdenki više.

On the seventeenth I write it,February's written down,in two thousand and thirteen:one book more for Zdenka now.

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting), ten days after he had given her Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck).
