Poems for children

The Lost Shoe

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Izgubih svojucipelu desnu,ali ja ne znams noge koje.

I've lost my shoe,my right one, oh,but from which footI just don't know.

Molim nalaznikada sam provjeri,jer stonoge svojenoge ne broje.

Will the finder pleasecheck it for me, do?For centipedes nevercount their feet, it's true.

Bosonoga stonoga

Barefoot Centipede

Translator's note. Stonoga, the centipede, is literally a "hundred-legs"; the signature rhymes in Croatian.
