Obitelji Kopecki

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Kopecki Family

Da Vam budu ljepši satii dosada da se skrati,da nestane dnevnih briga,evo Vama moja knjiga,pa ju lijepo pročitajte,veselja si srcu dajte.ŽABLJI SASTANAK održali,STIHOM KROZ ŽIVOT prošetali.Hvala Vam za Vašu knjigu,koju ste Vi napisali,i POVRATAK SREĆI je nazvalii za poklon meni dali.S Vašom knjigom sretni bilii predivne snove snili.To Vam želi sve bez šaleautor ove knjige male.Bili sretni i veseli:Dragici i Franji želi!

So your hours may brighter runand your boredom soon be done,so your daily cares may go,here's my book for you, and soread it nicely, read it through,give your hearts some gladness too.May you hold THE FROGS' MEETING, too,stroll THROUGH LIFE IN VERSE right through.Thank you for your book, which youwrote together, the two of you,named it RETURN TO HAPPINESS,gave it me as a present, too.With your book may you be gladand dream the loveliest dreams you've had.This, and it's no joke at all,wishes the author of this booklet small.Happy, merry may you go:his wish to Dragica and Franjo!

Valpovo, 29. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 29, 2010

Translator's note. Written in his book Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse); he weaves in the titles of his books Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting) and Stihom kroz život, and of the Kopeckis' own book, Povratak sreći (Return to Happiness).
