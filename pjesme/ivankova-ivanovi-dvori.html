Ivankova "Ivanovi dvori"

Occasional verse and dedications

Ivankovo's Ivanovi Dvori

Na istoku ravne SlavonijeIvankovo tu stoljeća broji.Bogato je to Šokačko selo,sa ponosom u ljepoti stoji.

Out in the east of level SlavoniaIvankovo counts centuries gone by.It is a wealthy Šokac village, onethat in its beauty stands with head held high.

Kao gradić sve uvjete ima,na tisuće broji stanovnika.A restoran "IVANOVI DVORI",posebna su Ivankovu dika.

Just like a little town, it has it all,with many thousand people living there,and then the restaurant, IVANOVI DVORI,is Ivankovo's pride beyond compare.

Ako ideš u selo Retkovce,Ivankova kad prođeš središte,prije pruge u lijevom sokaku,nogometno tu je igralište.

Should you be heading for Retkovci village,once through Ivankovo's centre you have passed,before the railway, down the left-hand lane,you'll come upon the football ground at last.

Lijep restoran IVANOVI DVORI,GRAC ulica dvadeset tri broji,smješteno je blizu stadiona,uspjeha mu u radu postoji.

The fine IVANOVI DVORI restauranton GRAC Street stands at number twenty-three;close by the stadium it has its place,and in its work success is plain to see.

Sama zgrada k'o dvorac izgleda,kao da je građen u ANTICI.Interijer ovog restorana,predivan je baš kao na slici.

The building on its own looks like a castle,as if it had been built in ANCIENT days;and this fine restaurant's interioris lovely as a picture to the gaze.

Njegov vlasnik Buranji je Marko,on je vijećnik općinskoga tima.Sve usluge gostima će dati,bilo ljeto ili jaka zima.

Its owner is Marko Buranji, and hesits on the municipal council, too;he'll offer every service to his guests,in summer or when bitter winter's due.

Za svatove i druženja raznai do petsto gostiju on prima.Da bi gosti zadovoljni bili,on bogati svoj restoran ima.

For wedding parties, gatherings of all kinds,up to five hundred guests he'll welcome in;and so that every guest goes home content,his restaurant is richly stocked within.

Šef kuhinje Buranji je Maja.U meniju što ti srce želi.Gostima će servirati hranu,kakvu nisu ni kraljevi jeli.

The kitchen's head is Maja Buranji;the menu holds all that your heart could wish.She'll set before her guests a meal so grandthat not a king has tasted such a dish.

Često puta tu se svadba slaviil' sastanak ima družba nova,što nam daju IVANOVI DVORI,to je ponos mjesta Ivankova.

Here weddings are quite often celebrated,or some new company will meet here, too;and all that IVANOVI DVORI give usis Ivankovo's pride, through and through.

Translator's note. Ivanovi Dvori (“Ivan's Manor”) is a restaurant in Ivankovo, a large village in eastern Slavonia.
