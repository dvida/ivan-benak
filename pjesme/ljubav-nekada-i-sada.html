Humorous poems

Love Then and Now

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

LJUBAV NEKADA

Love Then

Bilo jednom davnoprošla vječnost cijela.Na grani se mladostidva goluba srela.

Once, long, long ago,an age since passed away,on the branch of youthtwo doves met one day.

On joj priča bajke,a ona mu guče: –"Ne mogu bez tebemoj mili mangupće."

He tells her fairy tales,and she coos, it's true: –"I can't live without you,my dear little rogue, you!"

"Sunašce moje,slatko moje lućesve najdraže moje!"Ona njemu guče. –

"You, my little sunshine,my sweet little doll,my dearest, my all!"So she coos to him. –

Uz poljupce slatke,on joj nježno tepa:"Anđelčiću mili,maco moj lijepa!"

Amid his sweet kisseshe babbles: "My pretty,my dear little angel,my own little kitty!"

Ali ta idilatrajala je kratko,sve je brzo prošlo,što je bilo slatko.

But that happy idylldidn't last for long;all that had been sweetvery soon was gone.

On se sada sjećaprošlosti daleke,nekadašnje ženice:tihe, skromne, meke.

Now he calls to mindthe far-off days gone by,the little wife he had:so modest, soft and shy.

LJUBAV SADA

Love Now

Sada žena krešei Boga mu skida.Nema dostojanstva,etike ni stida.

Now the wife lets flyand curses him to hell;no dignity, no ethics,and no shame as well.

Sada više ne kaže:"Oh, mužiću zdravo!"Već u bradu promrsi:"Odnio te đavo!"

She no longer says,"Oh, hubby, hello!"But mutters under her breath:"To the devil you go!"

Kada kući dolazimisli što će reći.Već sa vrata na njega,kao kvočka kreči.

When he's coming home,he wonders how she'll speak;right from the door, at him,like a broody hen, she'll shriek.

Kada bi minutus posla zakasnio."Zar se sada dolazi?Gdje si do sad bio?"

If he's a minute latefrom work, oh, then she'll cry:"Is this a time to come?Where have you been, and why?"

A kada ga ugleda,pogleda ga mrko.Od pogleda njezinog,već je na pol crko.

And when she lays eyes on him,she gives him a grim glare;and from that look of hershe's half dead then and there.

I to ga sve podsjetina brak starog strica.Postao je papučar,a žena vještica.

And all this brings to mindhis old uncle's married life:he's turned a henpecked man,and a witch, his wife.

Valpovo, 1976.

Valpovo, 1976
