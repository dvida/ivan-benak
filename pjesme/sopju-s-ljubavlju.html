Domoljubne i zavičajne

Sopju s ljubavlju

Homeland and native region

To Sopje, With Love

Podravino, mili zavičaju,selo Sopje u tebi se svilo.Nema ljepšeg od tvojega kraja,uvijek mi je tu najbolje bilo.

Podravina, my dear native land,the village Sopje nestles in your breast;there's nothing lovelier than your countryside,and here is where I've always felt the best.

Kada Dravsko proljeće osvane,to je smotra narodnog folklora.KUD "Podravac" slavi svoje dane,tu ljepotu doživjet se mora.

When the day of Dravsko proljeće dawns,that's the folk festival, the folklore show;KUD "Podravac" celebrates its days —that beauty's something you must feel and know.

Iz Mađarske i KUD-ovi naši,Sopju hrle na Dravsko proljeće.Kad se skupe dični folkloraši,i povorka kroz Sopje proteče.

From Hungary, and from our own KUDs too,to Sopje, for Dravsko proljeće, they throng;and when the proud folk dancers all have gathered,through Sopje the procession flows along.

Tada kolo KUD „Podravac“ vije,a Šokica glasno pocikuje,na tamburi snažno žice bruje,preko Luke Greda odjekuje.

Then KUD "Podravac" whirls the kolo round,the Šokica's loud whooping cries resound,the tambura strings hum with a mighty sound,and over Luka, Greda echoes round.

VIDOVO se u ljepoti slavi,kirvaj Sopja što odavno traje.Skupljaju se prijatelji pravi,da proslave stare običaje.

Vidovo is feasted in its splendour,the church fair Sopje's kept since long ago;and true friends come together, one and all,to celebrate the old ways that they know.

Sopjani se sa agrarom bave,rodne njive plodove im daju.Sve praznike u svom selu slave,zato žive u zemaljskom raju.

The folk of Sopje live off farming land,their fertile fields give harvests year by year;they keep all holidays in their own village,and so they live in earthly Eden here.

Sve dok bude bilo Slavonijebit će uvijek Šokačkoga žara.Igrat će se kod općine kolo,uz tambure „Sopjanskih bećara“.

As long as there's Slavonia, there'll bethe Šokac fire and zeal for evermore;the kolo will be danced by the town hallto tamburas of the "Sopjanski bećari".

Valpovo, 18. 09. 2012.

Valpovo, September 18, 2012

Translator's note. Dravsko proljeće ("Drava Spring") is Sopje's folklore festival; Vidovo, St Vitus' Day, is the village church fair (kirvaj); Sopjanski bećari is the local tamburica band.
