Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana gospođo Ana!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Madam Ana!

Sretnu Novu sada Vama želim,puno sreće, zdravlja i veselja!Dv'je tisuće četrnaesto ljeto,nek Vam bude uspješno i sretno!

A happy New Year now I wish to you,with lots of luck and health and gladness too!Two thousand fourteen: may that year of yoursbe prosperous and happy all the way through!

Pratili Vas samo sretni dani,nek' Vam bude bolje nego lani.Među sobom divno se družili,svoje snove sretno ostvarili.

May only happy days be yours to know,and fare much better than a year ago.May you have lovely times in company,and see your dreams fulfilled most happily.

Svi u zdravlju sreću uživali,s najdražima u ljepoti snili,cijelo ljeto u veselju bilii ugodne dane provodili.

In health may all of you enjoy good cheer,and dream in beauty with the ones most dear,and may you all be joyful the year through,and spend your days in pleasant fashion too.

Bolji standard donio Vam slavu,ja Vam želim mirovinu pravu,primali je ozarenog lica,trošili je puno godinica.

May better living standards bring you fame,and I wish you a pension worth the name;and may you draw it with a beaming smile,and go on spending it a long, long while.

Put života bio Vam što duži,Vašem biću neka sretno služi.Ovu želju sretno proživjeli:to Vam pjesnik Benak Ivo želi!

And may your path through life be long, and mayit serve your being happily, I pray;and may you live this wish out through and through:that's what the poet Benak Ivo wishes you!

U Valpovu dva'jst osmoga prosinca,dv'je tisuće trinaestoga ljeta.Sa čestitkom ovom ja Vam želim:punim zdravljem da Vam život cvjeta!

In Valpovo, December twenty-eight,two thousand thirteen: that's the year and date;and with this greeting card, I wish you, too:may life, in fullest health, just bloom for you!

Translator's note. A New Year's greeting for 2014.
