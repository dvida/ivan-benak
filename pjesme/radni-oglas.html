Radio burza

Radni oglas

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Job Ad

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Momentalno u krizu sam pao,preko burze sve sam rasprodao.

I'm in a crisis at the moment, friends:I've sold the lot on the Burza — there it ends.

Više ništa za prodaju nemam,zato sada ovaj oglas spremam.

I haven't anything to sell, and soI'm putting out this ad for you to know.

Kao firma koja što prodaje,moja ličnost sada oglas daje.

Just like some firm that sells its wares, I say,I place this ad in person here today.

Tko što ima, neka mi se javi,da napišem za vas oglas pravi.

Whoever's got some things, give me a ring;I'll write a proper ad for anything.

Za predmete, dragi ljudi znajte,sve podatke opširno mi dajte.

For all your items, dear folk, understand,give me the details, all of them to hand.

Vjerujte mi, nije loše znati,ja ću oglas rimovani dati.

Believe me, it's not bad for you to know:I'll write your ad in rhyming verse, just so.

Taj se oglas vrlo rado sluša,njega prima svaka nježna duša.

An ad like that is loved by all who hear,and every gentle soul will hold it dear.

Prodat ću vam sve iz kuće živo,to vam jamči iz Valpova Ivo.

I'll sell off everything your house has got;Ivo from Valpovo guarantees the lot.

Sijeno, slamu, ljutu šljivovicu,stari traktor, pa i prikolicu.

Hay, straw, and slivovitz, the fiery kind,an old tractor, and the trailer behind.

Telad, krave, svinje, pa i kozei nerasta PIJATRENSKE loze.

Calves, cows, and pigs, and goats as well, what's more,and one of Piétrain stock, a breeding boar.

Pa i onu automatsku spravu,koja muze u štalici kravu.

That automatic gadget, too, the kindthat milks the cow inside the shed, you'll find.

Stare gume, sa felgama skupa,pa i hondu, važno da ne lupa.

Old tyres, together with their rims, as well,a Honda too — if it runs smooth, I'll sell.

Malo janje, oh, tako mu sreće,pa i kazan što rakiju peče.

A little lamb — oh, bless its lucky star —and a still for making rakija, there you are.

Ružmarine i mirisno cvijeće,stari auto, važno da se kreće.

Rosemary and sweet-smelling flowers, too,an old car — as long as it moves, it'll do.

I kukuruz, makar je od lanei piliće dobro očupane.

And maize, although it's last year's, never mind,and chickens, nicely plucked, the proper kind.

Veš mašinu i kuću sa Drave,dobar hladnjak i rol šue prave.

A washer, and the house by the Drava's side,a good fridge, and real roller blinds beside.

Stol, stolice, krevet i madrace,sam da nisu sa najlon pijace.

A table, chairs, a bed, and mattresses, too —just not from the nylon market, that won't do.

Prodat ću vam sve kućanske sprave,važno da su kvalitetne prave.

I'll sell for you all household gadgets, too,as long as they're good quality and true.

Da prodamo višak vaših stvari,javite se na telefon stari.

To sell the surplus of the things you own,just call me on my same old telephone.

6 5 2 osamsto dvadeset jedan,neka bude sreće ovaj tjedan.

6 5 2 eight hundred twenty-one —may luck be with us till this week is done.

Valpovo, 13. 02. 2007.

Valpovo, February 13, 2007

Translator's note. He offers his services to listeners of Radio burza: send him the details, and he will write your ad in rhyme. Piétrain is a Belgian breed of pig; the "nylon market" was the name for the flea markets of cheap imported goods.
