Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Elviri Bočkaj, učiteljici iz Sopja

Occasional verse and dedications

To Elvira Bočkaj, Teacher from Sopje

Poštovana Elvira!

Esteemed Elvira!

Od pedeset prvog ljeta,ja u Sopju nisam bio,ali sada sam u školidivnu radost doživio.

Since the year of fifty-oneI had not set foot in Sopje;but now, at the school, I foundjoy as wondrous as could be.

Lijepo ste me dočekalii ljepotu srcu dali,zato moja knjiga malamjesto mene kaže HVALA!

What a welcome you prepared!You gave beauty to my heart;so this little book of minesays its "Thank you!" for my part.

Knjigu ovu pročitali,ljepotu si darovali.Bez dosade i bez tremeugodno Vam bilo vrijeme.

Read this book, and give yourselfbeauty as a gift, I say;with no boredom and no nerves,may you while the hours away.

U čitanju nek' Vam knjigapruži radost i veselje.Stvarajući ovu knjiguto su bile moje želje.

In the reading, may this bookbring you joy and merriment;as I wrote this book, that wasall my wish and my intent.

Bili sretni i veseli,od srca Vam autor želi!

May you be happy and merry too:the author's heartfelt wish for you!

Valpovo, 23. 03. 2011.

Valpovo, March 23, 2011

Translator's note. A thank-you written in a copy of Stihom kroz život after he visited the school in Sopje, his native village, which he had not seen since 1951.
