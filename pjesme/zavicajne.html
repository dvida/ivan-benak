Pjesme

Domoljubne i zavičajne

„Nigdje nema ljepšeg kraja / nego što je Slavonija.“ Pjesme o domovini, rodnom Sopju, Valpovu, selima, sokacima i starim običajima.

Poems

Homeland and native region

“There is not a land more lovely / than Slavonia, anywhere.” Poems about his homeland, his native Sopje, Valpovo, the villages, the village lanes and the old customs.
