Domoljubne i zavičajne

Bistrinci

Homeland and native region

Bistrinci

U stoljeću trinaestome,spominje se selo malo,na obali lijevoj Drave,seoce se Dnina zvalo.

In the thirteenth centurya small village is recordedon the left bank of the Drava;Dnina was the hamlet's name.

Zbog poplava i nezgoda,u šesnaestom bilo vijeku,da bi život sačuvali,prešli ljudi Dravu rijeku.

Due to floods and troubles sore,in the sixteenth century,so that they might save their lives,they crossed the Drava, shore to shore.

Na obali desnoj Drave,malo selo su izgradili,četrdeset bilo kuća,Bistrinci mu ime dali.

On the right bank of the Dravathey put up a village small;there were forty houses in it,and Bistrinci it was called.

Ribolovom i agraromljudi se u selu bave,uvjeti su bili dobri,jer stanuju pokraj Drave.

Fishing, farming, is the tradethat the village people ply;the conditions there were good,for the Drava's flowing by.

Osamnaestog prošlog vijeka,Dravom slika doplovila,spominje se sveta Ana,narodu je svetost bila.

In the eighteenth centurydown the Drava, floating, camean image bearing Saint Anne's name,and the people held it holy.

Bistrinčani plemeniti,u selu su vrijedni bilii svetište svetoj Ani,pokraj Drave napravili.

Bistrinci's noble peoplein their village worked with care,and a shrine to holy Annethey built by the Drava there.

Osamsto i trinaeste,bogomolju su gradilii svetome Stjepanu suoltar časno posvetili.

In the year of eighteen-thirteenthey put up a house of prayer,and with honour to Saint Stephenconsecrated the altar there.

Bistrinčani vrijedni ljudi,DVD su osnovali,već sto deset godinicasvome selu obol dali.

Bistrinci's hard-working peoplefounded the DVD brigade;for a hundred and ten years nowthey have given their village aid.

Selo se je razvijaloi ljudi su vrijedni bili,djeca selo popunila,pa su školu izgradili.

And the village grew apace,and its people toiled away;children filled the village up,so they built a school one day.

I tisuću devetsto osmeškola bila otvorena,kultura je bila časno,Bistrincima podarena.

And in nineteen-oh-eight the schoolopened its doors, and therebyculture, with all honour, wasgranted to Bistrinci.

Godine su prolazile,industriji nek' je hvala.Tvornica je Gutmanova,Bistrincima puno dala.

And the years went rolling by —let us thank the industry!Gutmann's factory it wasgave Bistrinci plenty.

Razvilo se ovo selo,ima dosta stanovnika,spojilo se sa Belišćem,za Bistrince to je dika.

And the village grew and spread,it has people, quite a few;now it's joined up with Belišće —pride for Bistrinci, that's true.

Uz ribičko društvo slavno,nogometni klub postoji:"PODRAVAC" mu ime dali,u zapisu tako stoji.

Next to the famous angling clubthere's a football club as well:"Podravac" is the name they gave it,so the written records tell.

Predivno je ovo selo,o napretku ljudi sniliBistrinci su sa ljepotom,Slavoniju okitili!

Lovely, lovely is this village,people dreamed of progress here;with its beauty, Bistrincihas adorned Slavonia dear!

Valpovo, 17. 10. 2013.

Valpovo, October 17, 2013

Translator's note. Bistrinci, now joined to Belišće, is known for its shrine of St Anne by the Drava. The DVD is the village's volunteer fire brigade, and the Gutmann factory is the Belišće timber works.
