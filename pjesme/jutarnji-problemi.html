Poems for children

Morning Troubles

Mala djeca s cipelamaimaju problema,misle da je desna,a ono je lijeva.

Little ones have troublewith their shoes, it's true:they think it's the right one,but it's the left shoe.

Djeca nisu krivaza ove probleme,cipele su krive –tu nema dileme.

The children aren't to blamefor troubles of this kind;the shoes are the culprits —no doubt in my mind.

Dok djeca spavaju,cipelice ustaju,po dvorištu trčei loptu šutaju.

While the children sleep,the shoes get out of bed,race around the yardand play football instead.

A kada se umorei kad zora svane,cipelica lijevamjesto desne stane.

And when they're worn outand the dawn is bright,the little left shoestands in place of the right.

Da su one krive,vidite i sami,jer su se pomješalebaš noćas u tami.

That it's them to blame,you can see, all right:they got themselves mixed upin the dark last night.

Eto, sada znate,djeci nije lakošto im noge malestoje naopako.

So now you knowkids have a hard time, too,for their little feet standback to front in each shoe!

Translator's note. Another copy, titled Cipelice ("Little Shoes"), says the poem was set to music by prof. Želimir Sušić.
