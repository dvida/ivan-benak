Radio burza

Nudim finu šljivovicu

Radio market

Fine Plum Brandy on Offer

Osim dobre šljivovice,imam ja i komovice.A stariji ljudi kažu,da je dobra za masažu.

Apart from good plum brandy, see,I've pomace brandy, too, with me.And the older people sayit's good for massage, anyway.

Ja vjerujem da je tako,bez rakije nije lako,u to sam se uvjerio,kad me stomak zabolio.

I believe that it is so —without rakija, it's hard, you know;I became convinced of itwhen my stomach ached a bit.

Najviše se ona voli,kada nekog zub zaboli,bilo koji, nema veze,makar onaj iz proteze.

It is loved the most, I've found,when a toothache comes around;any tooth at all will do,even one from dentures, too.

Još ako je prepečena,tek onda je korist njena.Zato neka ljudi znaju,takvu imam za prodaju.

Even more if twice distilled —then its benefit's fulfilled.So let all the people hear:I have that kind for sale here.

Imam dobru rakijicu,prepečenu šljivovicu.Vjerujte mi, takvu imami narudžbe skromne primam.

I've got rakija, good and fine,twice-distilled plum brandy — mine.Trust me, I have got that kind,and small orders I don't mind.

Može litra ili deset,možda petnaest il' pedeset.A moje su skromne cijene,eto, to je sad od mene.

It can be a litre, or ten,fifteen, maybe fifty, then;and my prices are but small —that's from me, and that is all.

Javite se na telefon,svakog dana cijeli tjedan:Na šesto pedes't i dvaosamsto dvadeset jedan.

Ring me on the telephone,every day, till the week is done:at six hundred fifty-two,eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 26. 08. 2008.

Valpovo, August 26, 2008

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. Šljivovica is plum rakija; komovica is rakija distilled from grape pomace, the skins left after pressing wine.
