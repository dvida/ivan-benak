Love poems

You Ask Me

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ti me pitaš zašto višeja ne pišem svoje pjesme?Zašto rano, zorimicesjeta krije moje lice?

You ask me why I no longerwrite my poems as before?Why at early break of daydoes sadness hide my face away?

Da me bilo tvoga srca,kao zvono iz sna budi.Pa da ljubim cijele noćiumiljate tvoje oči.

If your heartbeat, like a bell,woke me from my sleep, and thenI could kiss all through the nightsyour endearing, winsome eyes;

I još da se tvoje rukeoko moga sviju vrata,tek bi tada sunce sreće,obasjalo moje cvijeće.

and if, too, your arms would cometwining all around my neck,only then would fortune's sunshine on my flowers, every one.

Još da mogu da poljubim,tvoja medna usta mala.Vjeruj, dušo, da bi tadamoja pjesma sama cvala.

If, besides, I could but kissyour small mouth, so honey-sweet,trust me, darling, then my poemof itself would bloom, complete.

Valpovo, 1976.
