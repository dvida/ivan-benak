Radio burza

Krevet snova

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

The Bed of Dreams

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja polukauč sad prodajem,kažem svima sada javno.Evo opet ja se javljam,kao ono, nekad davno.

Now a sofa bed I'm selling,and I let the whole world know.Here I am again, calling in,just like once, so long ago.

On vam služi kao trosjedi razvuć' se lijepo može,pa je onda kao krevet,oh, divote, mili Bože.

As a three-seater it serves you,and it pulls out, nice and neat;then it's just the same as a bed —oh, dear Lord, oh, what a treat!

Nedavno je on presvučeni federe ima nove.Sad bi mogli i mladenci,da snivaju na njem snove.

Not long since, it's been recovered,and the springs in it are new.Even newlyweds could nowdream their dreams upon it, too.

Godinama ja sam takoi nije mi bilo krivo.Možda bi sad netko drugi,lijepe snove na njem sniv'o.

Many years I dreamed there too,and I never once complained;now perhaps some other personcould dream sweet dreams there again.

Za vrijednost me vi pitate,to bi htjeli sada znati?Za kuniće il' za eure,ja ga želim sad prodati.

And you ask me what it's worth —that's what you'd now like to know?For some kuna or for euros,I would like to let it go.

Samu cijenu polukauča,ja vam neću sada kriti,osam stoja naših kuna,takva će mu ona biti.

And the price of the sofa bedI will not conceal from you:eight hundred of our own kuna:that is what it will come to.

I telefon moj je stari,mijenjao se do sad nije:šest, pet, dva, osam, dva, jedan.Nazovite sad što prije!

And my phone's the same old number,hasn't changed until today:six, five, two, eight, two, one.Call me now, without delay!

Valpovo, 16.05.2004.

Valpovo, May 16, 2004
