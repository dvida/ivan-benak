Udomiteljska obitelj Belišće, Albert Anice

Occasional verse and dedications

Anica Albert's Foster Family, Belišće

POŠTOVANA ANICE!

Dear Anica!

Ova moja knjiga, pisana je Tebi,bez Tvoje pomoći tiskali je ne bi.Draga, Anice, od srca Ti hvala,svoju si dobrotu za tu knjigu dala.STIHOM KROZ ŽIVOT, divna knjiga nova,nek' Ti bude noću uspavanka snova.

This book of mine I've written, dear, for you:without your help we'd not have printed it, it's true.Dear Anica, from my heart, I thank you sofor all the kindness you gave this book, you know.Through Life in Verse, a lovely book and new:may it be a lullaby of dreams at night for you.

Da bi noću, Anice, divne snove snila,a ujutro svježa, puna snage bila.I za divnu tortu što si meni dala,na promociji knjige, od srca Ti hvala.Na mnogo promocija, ja sam često bio,ali malo torti da je tko dobio.U životu, Anice, pratila te sreća,životna Ti staza bila puna cvijeća.Ovo prijateljstvo koje si mi dala,od sveg srca Tebi, najtoplije HVALA!

So you may dream sweet dreams at night, Anica dear,and wake up fresh and full of strength when morning's here.And for the lovely cake you gave me, too,at the book launch, my heartfelt thanks to you.I've often been at launches, many a one,but cakes for anyone were few or none.In life, Anica, may good luck go with you,your path through life be strewn with flowers too.For the friendship you have given me, I saywith all my heart: my warmest thanks today!

Valpovo, 14. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 14, 2010

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his sixth book, Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse), for Anica Albert, who helped get it printed and brought him a cake at its launch.
