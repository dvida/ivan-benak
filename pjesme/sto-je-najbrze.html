Humorous poems

What Is Fastest?

Jednog dana nastavnica,zadaću u školi dade:Najbrže na svijetu što je,da li netko od vas znade?

One day, in school, the teacherset the class a task to do:"What's the fastest thing on earth?Does anybody know? Do you?"

"Kad moj tata u lov ide",važno reče mali Neca,"iz lovačke puške metak,brži je od samog zeca."

"When my dad goes out to hunt,"said young Neca, with an air,"why, the bullet from his rifleflies much faster than the hare."

Stevica se na to javi:"Svemirski je brod najbrži,on vam zvučni zid razbija,njega mlazni pogon drži."

Stevica chimed in at that:"Spaceships are the fastest; theysmash right through the sound barrier,jet-propelled along the way."

"Najbrže su, djeco, misli,one se baš svega sjete."Mudro reče nastavnica:"Misli među zvijezde lete."

"Fastest, children, are our thoughts;they can think of everything,"wisely then the teacher said."Thoughts among the stars take wing."

"Ne slažem se", veli Ivo,"to ću vama dokazati.Roditelji moji sinoć,počeli su se maziti."

"I disagree," says Ivo,"and I'll prove it, wait and see.Just last night, my mum and dadstarted cuddling, cosily."

Najvažnije kad je bilo,doživljaj se ovaj zbio:"Svršio sam", reče tata,"prije nego sam mislio."

When things reached the crucial spot,here's the moment that I caught:"I have come," my daddy said,"even quicker than I thought!"

Valpovo, 27. 06. 2004.

Valpovo, June 27, 2004

Translator's note. The punchline turns on svršiti, which means both "to finish" and "to climax": Dad was quicker than thought itself.
