Poems for children

The Cow and the Hen

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kravica je naša Bjelkacijeli dan na paši bila,a navečer slatkog mlijekadječici je podarila.

Our little cow called Bjelkaspent all day out in the field,and at evening, milk so sweetfor the children she would yield.

Potom reče krava važno:„Moje mlijeko nije lažno,zato, molim da se zna,njega vole djeca sva.“

Then the cow said, with an air:"My good milk's not fake, I swear,so let everybody know:all the children love it so."

A na to će koka snažno:„Ja znam da je mlijeko važno,al' da život duže traje,ja darujem svoje jaje!“

Then the hen said, loud and clear:"I know milk's important, dear,but to make life last, I say,I will give my egg away!"

Translator's note. Bjelka ("Whitey") is a common name for a white cow.
