Pod krošnjom kestena

Poems for children

Under the Chestnut's Crown

Kesten, to je krasno drvo.Reći ću vam sada svima,bilo divlje il' pitomo,njega dvije vrste ima.

The chestnut is a lovely tree.Now I'll tell you all, it's true:be it wild or be it tame,of this tree the kinds are two.

Pitomi se kesten jede,bilo kuhan ili pečen.Divljeg treba industrija,i oboje služe nečem'.

The sweet chestnut you can eat,either boiled or roasted, too.Industry has need of wild ones:both have useful work to do.

Kesten vam je u prirodi,kao svako drugo drvo.Reći ću vam sada, djeco:kao deblo dođe prvo.

Out in nature stands the chestnutjust like any other tree.Now I'll tell you, children, this:first the trunk comes, as you see.

To je njemu kao tijeloiz kojega žilje klije.Još na njemu rastu grane,što ih snažni vjetar vije.

That's its body, you might say,from which all its roots will sprout;and on it the branches growthat strong winds toss all about.

Na granama lista lišće,što ga nježni lahor njiše,a to su mu, dijete, pluća,ono kao i ti diše.

On the branches leaves come out,which the gentle breeze blows through,and these, my child, are its lungs:it breathes just the way you do.

Kad proljetno sunce grane,svako drvo tad procvjetai opojni miris širi,što je ukras ovog svijeta.

When the sun of spring shines down,every tree bursts into flowerand spreads heady scent aroundthat adorns this world of ours.

Tada pčele cvijet oplode,iza cvijeta plod urodi,a s jeseni, kad sazori,najljepše je u prirodi.

Bees then pollinate the blossom,after blossom, fruit will grow,and in autumn, when it's ripened,nature has no finer show.

Valpovo, 21. 10. 2010.

Valpovo, October 21, 2010
