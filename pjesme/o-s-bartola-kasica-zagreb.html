Prigodne pjesme i posvete

O. Š. Bartola Kašića, Zagreb

Occasional verse and dedications

Bartol Kašić Primary School, Zagreb

Draga djeco, dragi đaci,škole KAŠIĆA prvaci.Da vam budu ljepši sati,a dosada da se skrati,da nestane vaših briga,evo vama moja knjiga,pa ju lijepo pročitajte,veselja si srcu dajte.U čitanju nek' vam knjigapruži radost i veselje,stvarajući ovu knjiguto su bile moje želje.Bili sretni i veselii snivali divne snove,od sveg srca vama želimali zeko knjige ove.Svi u školi sretni bilii petice ostvarili.Ne bilo vam nikad krivo,to vam želi pjesnik Ivo.

Dearest children, pupils dear,KAŠIĆ school's first graders here:so your hours may brighter run,and your boredom soon be done,so your worries fly away,here's my book for you today;read it nicely, read it through,give your hearts some gladness too.As you read, I hope this bookbrings you joy and merriment;when I made it, that was justwhat I wished for, what I meant.May you all be glad and bright,dreaming lovely dreams at night;with his whole heart wishes youthis book's little bunny, too.May you all do well at schooland get straight A's, as a rule!Never sorry, never blue:Poet Ivo wishes you.

Valpovo, 10. 04. 2013.

Valpovo, April 10, 2013

Translator's note. Written in a copy of Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck), whose hero is the little bunny. Bartol Kašić (1575–1650) wrote the first grammar of Croatian.
