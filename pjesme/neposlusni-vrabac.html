Poems for children

The Disobedient Sparrow

from the books Bunny's Luck (1990) and Through Life in Verse (2010)

Pod strehom vrapčje gnijezdo,u njemu malena jaja.Kad prođe neko vrijemetu vam je prava graja.

Under the eaves, a sparrow's nest,and tiny eggs within;and when some time has passed,you'll hear a proper din!

Roditelji mlade uče,brižne im savjete dajuda budu dobri i mirnii da što više znaju.

The parents teach the young ones,give careful counsel, sothey will be good and quietand learn all they can know.

Jedan ih slušao nije,on je „sve bolje znao.“U gnijezdu se koprcao,pa je u baru pao.

One of them wouldn't listen,he "knew it all, and best."He fell into a puddlefrom squirming in the nest.

U blatu je cvrkutao,prljav od kljuna do buta.Kad ga strašni mačor zgrabi,istoga trena proguta.

He chirped there in the mud,all filthy, beak to toe;the fearsome tomcat seized himand gulped him in one go.

U ovoj priči imanešto vrjednije od zlata:nije vam svatko prijateljtko vas vadi iz blata.

In this story there is somethingmore precious than pure gold:not all who pull you from the mudare friends of yours, all told.

Kad netko visoko letii uživa lude slasti,tko je drzak i zločest,taj može nisko pasti.

When someone flies up highand revels in wild delight,if he's cheeky and naughty,he can fall from that height.

Neka vam ovo, djeco,bude savjet mali:Uvijek slušajte starije,da biste što više znali!

Let this be, dear children,a small piece of advice:always listen to your elders,and you will grow up wise!
