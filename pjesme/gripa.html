The Flu

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Mali je šarangripu dobio.Nos mu curii boli ga glava.Bos je na kišii mrazu bioi sada u mokrojpostelji spava.

The little carphas caught the flu.His nose is running,he's got an aching head.He was barefoot in the rainand frost right through,and now he's sleepingin a soggy bed.

Brižno su zvalimama i tata:„Dođi brzo,svemogući some!“Som:„Hitno ga moramizdvojit iz jatai liječiti gau trbuhu svome.“

Anxiously they called,his mummy and his dad:"Come quickly,almighty catfish, come!"Catfish:"I must urgentlyisolate the ladfrom the shoal, and treat himhere in my own tum."
