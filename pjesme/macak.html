Humorous poems

The Tomcat

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Eh, da mogu mačak biti,makar i crn bio.Rado bih se mladoj ženi,ja u krilu svio.

Ah, if I could be a tomcat,even if a black one, still,gladly in a young wife's lapI'd curl up, if she will.

Poznato je, mačori suumiljate ćudi.Nježno bih se ja mazio,uz njezine grudi.

It is known that tomcats havesuch a sweet and winning way;tenderly I'd nuzzle closeto her breasts, and there I'd stay.

Noću bih se uvukao,sred njene postelje.Tada bih joj ispuniosve ljubavne želje.

In the night I'd creep right into the middle of her bed,and fulfil for her each wishlove could put into her head.

Svu ljepotu ove noći,toj bih ženi dao.Šapicom bih tijelo njenonježno milovao.

I would give that woman allof the beauty of the night;with my paw I'd stroke her bodytenderly, with touches light.

U tom času ja bih preovješto sa vretenom.Sve što trebam, našao bih,u krilašcu njenom.

At that moment I would purr,skilful as a spindle's spin;everything I need I'd findin the little lap I'm in.

Nakon nježnog milovanjai ljubavi strasne,opet bih u krilu njenom,sniv'o snove krasne.

After all the tender strokesand the passion of love's heat,once again, curled in her lap,I would dream dreams fine and sweet.

Eh, da mi je mačak biti!

Ah, if only I could be a tomcat!

Valpovo, 22. 11. 1992.

Valpovo, November 22, 1992

Translator's note. "Presti" means both to purr and to spin yarn, hence the spindle.
