Radio burza

Sa felgama gume dvije

Radio market

Two Tyres, with Rims

Javljam svima neka znaju,imam gume za prodaju.

Hear me, all, and let it be known:I've got tyres to sell, my own.

Dijagonalne gume dvije,165 x trinaest dimenzije.

Here are two cross-ply tyres, and thesemeasure 165 x thirteen, please.

Gaze kamen, blato, vodu,to su gume za LADU i ŠKODU.

Stone and mud and water, they won't mind —tyres for Lada, Škoda, just that kind.

Sa felgama i sa zrakom,za sto kuna dat ću svakom.

Rims and air included, too:a hundred kunas, sold to you.

Tko poželi gume moje,ja podatke dajem svoje.

Whoever wants these tyres of mine,here are my details, line by line.

Ivan Benak mi je ime,pjesnik koji piše rime.

Ivan Benak — that is me,poet writing rhymes, you see.

Ulica se pisat mora,Pejačević slavna Dora.

Then the street you must write down:Dora Pejačević, of renown.

Ako netko me potraži,moje kuće broj pet važi.

Should somebody come to seek me,number five is where you'll meet me.

Još dodajte mjesto moje,znajte slavno Valpovo je.

Add my town — you ought to knowit's the famous Valpovo.

Moj telefon stalno vrijedan:šest, pet, dva, osam, dva, jedan.

My phone, hard-working, never done:six, five, two, eight, two, one.

Valpovo, 07.09.2000.

Valpovo, September 7, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza, with his real name and address. His street was named after Dora Pejačević (1885–1923), the celebrated Croatian composer.
