The Poet's Wish

1983

Jednom kada odemu rajska blaženstvaponekada dođi,moj grob obiđi.Karanfilom bijelimhumak mi podari.Uzdahni i šapni:Tu počiva dragi,moj prijatelj, stari.

Some day, when I've goneto the bliss of heaven,now and then, come round,visit where I lie.With a white carnationgrace my little mound.Sigh, and whisper softly:Here my dear one rests,my friend of old.

Valpovo, 25. 08. 1983.

Valpovo, August 25, 1983
