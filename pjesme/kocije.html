Occasional verse and dedications

Kočije

Na TV-u skoro svaki tjedan,časno vokal divno pjesme poje.Tamburaški zbor: "KOČIJE" gledam,daruju nam te uspjehe svoje.

Almost every week on the TV screentheir singer sings the songs, so fine and true;the tambura band Kočije can be seen,who share with us their own successes too.

Tamburaši iz Narda "KOČIJE",svojim radom ostvaruju želje.Da li kirvaj ili svadbe čije,slušateljstvu pružaju veselje.

The tambura men from Nard, Kočije,make wishes all come true with what they do;be it a kirvaj or somebody's wedding,they bring their listeners joy the whole way through.

Cecilija kad im nježno kaže,da izvedu tad "UNUČE" svoje.Od te pjesme ništa nema draže,zatreperi nježno srce moje.

When Cecilija sweetly bids them playtheir own “Unuče” then, upon the show,than that song nothing's dearer, I must say:my tender heart begins to tremble so.

Svojim glasom snove ostvarili,svog života slavu ovjenčali.Slušateljstvu ljepotu ste dali,Valpovštinu svijetu prikazali.

Their voices have made all their dreams come true,and crowned their lives with glory, as is due;you've given loveliness to all who've heard,and shown Valpovština to all the world.

Valpovo, 30. 08. 2014.

Valpovo, August 30, 2014

Translator's note. Kočije (“Carriages”) is a tamburica band from Nard, near Valpovo. Cecilija presented the folk-music programme on Osijek television; Unuče (“Grandchild”) is one of the poet's songs.
