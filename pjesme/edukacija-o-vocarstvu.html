A Lesson in Fruit-Growing

Četrnaestoga datuma aprila,Skupila se kod Dragana moba,Edukativna to je grupa bila,Da se voće obrezati proba.

Upon the fourteenth day of April, wegathered at Dragan's, a work-party crew;an educational group it was to be,to have a go at pruning fruit trees too.

Gospon' Franci držao je slovo,O proljetnoj rezidbi voćakaI što ima u voćarstvu novo,Slušala ga pomno grupa đaka.

Mister Franci took the floor and shared his viewson springtime pruning, how it should be done,and what in fruit-growing was the latest news;his pupils listened closely, every one.

Na praksu je on prešao tada,Jednu voćku obrezo' je jako.Grane lete, oh, tako mu jada,Nisam znao da se može tako.

Then he moved on to practice, straight away,and cut one fruit tree back, and cut it hard.The branches flew! Oh, dear me, what dismay!I'd no idea that pruning went so far.

Da se reže procvjetalo voće,Mi do sada zbilja nismo znali,Valjda zato bolje rodit hoće,Tom rezidbom poticaj smo dali.

That trees in blossom may be pruned as wellwe truly never knew until today;perhaps they'll bear the better, who can tell:we've spurred them on by pruning in that way.

Kod rezidbe „čepovi“ su važni,Da porastu grane do godineI plodovi da bi bili snažni,Da se duša topi od miline.

In pruning, it's the "stubs" that are the key,so that the branches grow by the next year,and that the fruit be strong as it can be,to make your soul melt with delight and cheer.

Sve savjete mi smo prihvatili,Predavanjem zadovoljni bili,Jabukovu rakiju kušali,O voćarstvu novo naučili.

We took in all the tips he had to tell,and were well pleased with what the lecture brought;we tasted apple rakija as well,and something new in fruit-growing were taught.

Valpovo, 15. 04. 2004.

Valpovo, April 15, 2004

Translator's note. A moba is a neighbourly work-party; "gospon" is the Slavonian way of saying "Mister". Rakija is fruit brandy.
