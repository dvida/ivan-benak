The Poets' Society "Sveti Mihovil"

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Cvelferijo, odavno si znana,ljepota ti srcem ispisana,tvoji preci o ljepoti snili,centar svega Drenovci su bili.

O Cvelferija, known since long ago,your beauty written by the heart, I know;your forebears used to dream of loveliness,and Drenovci was the centre, nothing less.

Tu poštuju stare običajepa se puno za kulturu daje.Pjesnika je ovdje želja bilaosnovati društvo "MIHOVILA".

They honour the old customs here, and sofor culture much is given, as you know.It was the poets' wish, on this same ground,the "Mihovil" society to found.

Dvadeset i sedmog listopadadv'je tisuće četvrtoga ljetau Drenovci skupio je Andrić,jedno društvo ovdašnjih poeta.

On October's twenty-seventh day,two thousand four, that was the very year,in Drenovci, then, Andrić gathered rounda company of local poets here.

Po zakonu klub su osnovalii svoj obol za kulturu dali,izabrali odbor i tajnika,a Iliju tad za predsjednika.

They founded the club by law, the proper way,and gave their mite to culture, too, that day;they chose a board, a secretary, thenthey made Ilija president of them.

U svom radu sve rekorde ruše,knjigu pišu "DAR PJESNIČKE DUŠE".Već i druga u tisku je sada,vrijedno radi ta Udruga mlada.

They're breaking every record as they go:they write a book, "Gift of a Poet's Soul".A second book is at the printer's now;this young Society works hard, and how!

Za to društvo, da bi drugi znali,dv'je uskršnje zabave su dali,Slavonijom cijelom putovali,svoje pjesme ponosno čitali.

So that the rest might come to know their name,they held two Easter parties to that aim,they travelled all Slavonia far and wide,and read aloud their poems there with pride.

Istog ljeta još izložba slikai književno druženje pjesnika,zatim složno i marljivo rade,da izgrade stazu "Dobre nade".

That year there was an art exhibit, too,a literary poets' rendezvous;then all in harmony they work awayto build the path of "Good Hope", day by day.

Translator's note. Sveti Mihovil is Saint Michael. Cvelferija is the region along the Sava in the far east of Croatia, and Drenovci is its main village. The society was founded by Ilija Andrić, and its anthology is Dar pjesničke duše (Gift of a Poet's Soul).
