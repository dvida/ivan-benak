Domoljubne i zavičajne

Sopje, selo moga djetinjstva

Homeland and native region

Sopje, Village of My Childhood

Na sjeveru Slavonijeu zapisu starom stoji:od stoljeća već drugogaSopje svoja ljeta broji.

In the north of Slavoniaan old record makes it clear:since the second centurySopje's counted every year.

To predivno selo mojesmjestilo se blizu Drave,na njedrima Panonijeagrarom se ljudi bave.

This, my lovely village, liesnestled by the Drava's flow;on Pannonia's breast, its peoplework the farmland, reap and sow.

Turski grad je staro zdanje,kao spomen gordo stoji,od stoljeća trinaestogai on svoja ljeta broji.

The old Turkish fort, a relic,stands, a proud memorial, here;since the thirteenth centuryit, too, has counted every year.

U mom selu od davninaopćina se tu smjestila.Devet sela oko sebeponosno je prigrlila.

In my village, since of old,has the Council found its place;nine surrounding villagesit holds proudly in embrace.

Godine su prolazile,ljudi Sopja vrijedni bili,u stoljeću osamnaestom,bogomolju sagradili.

Years went by, and Sopje's people,diligent, worked hard and true;in the eighteenth centuryraised a house of worship new.

Mariji su Magdaleni,oltar crkve posvetilii Vidu su oltar daliproštenjem ga proslavili.

To Mary Magdalene thenwas the altar consecrated;to St Vitus, too, an altar,with a feast day celebrated.

Postoji u selu poštaveć stotinjak ljeta ima,za potrebe toga krajaona služi seljanima.Selo malo al' ponosno,nova škola služi njima.Dok u staroj bivši učo,dijelio je packe svima.

There's a post office in the village,nigh a hundred years it's spanned;for the needs of all the regionit serves people of the land.Small the village, but it's proud,and a new school's close at hand;in the old one, our old teacherrapped the palm of every hand.

Društveni i vatrogasnidom kulture Sopje ima,ambulantu i doktoraza potrebe Sopjanima.

Sopje has a hall for culture,for the village and fire crew;and a clinic, and a doctor,for the Sopje people, too.

Dvadesetog ovog vijekanesreća se dogodila:ulica je zvana Patošskoro cijela izgorila.

In the twentieth centurya disaster came to pass:Patoš, as the street was called,almost all burned down, alas.

Sopjani su složni ljudi,Florijanu zavjet dali.I tisuću devetsto šestespomenik mu sagradili.

Sopje's people, all united,to St Florian vowed and prayed,and in nineteen hundred and sixa monument to him they made.

Tisuću i devetstote,trinaestoga bivšeg ljeta,u Sopju je osnovana,vatrogasna prva četa.

Nineteen hundred and thirteen:in that long-departed year,in our Sopje they first foundeda brigade of firemen here.

Još se sjećam svog djetinjstva,divna nošnja se nosila,što je bila ukras selu,folkloraše okitila.

I recall my childhood still:lovely costumes then were worn,the adornment of the village,and the dancers they'd adorn.

KUD „Podravac“ Sopje krasi,kolo pletu cure, snaše,Slavonijom nastupaju,imaju i tamburaše.

KUD "Podravac" graces Sopje,girls and wives dance kolo round;they perform across Slavonia,with their own tamburica sound.

Moderno je došlo vrijeme,ljudi voze asfaltima.Sinula je Sopjem strujavodovodni sustav štima.

Modern times have come at last,people drive where asphalt goes;power lights up Sopje now,and the water system flows.

Trgovine dvije imai robe je svaka puna,kupili bi ljudi svaštaal' nemaju dosta kuna.

There are two shops in the village,each is full of goods to spare;people would buy anything,but the kunas just aren't there.

Ni riđana više nema,rzaji se sad ne čuju,jer su ukras u Višnjici,turisti ih posjećuju.

Chestnut horses are no more,not a neigh is heard today,for they're Višnjica's attraction,and the tourists come their way.

Nogometni klub Podravac,najbolji je, to svi znaju.Četrdeset šestog ljeta,osnovan je u tom kraju.

Podravac, the football club,is the best, as all can tell;back in nineteen forty-six,it was founded here as well.

Dvije krčme selo krasi:PLAVO ZVONO krčma LUKA.Nakon meča tu se dijeli,da l' pohvala il' poduka.

Two fine taverns grace the village:PLAVO ZVONO, LUKA too;after every match, they sharepraise, or lessons someone's due.

Eh, da nam je Mišin Marko,kao nekad da nastupa.S problemima našeg sela,da po cesti rupe krpa!

Eh, if only Mišin Markocould turn out as once before,and, to solve our village troubles,patch the potholes by the score!

Valpovo, 26. 11. 2010.

Valpovo, November 26, 2010

Translator's note. A rhymed chronicle of his native village near the Drava. Patoš was a Sopje street almost wholly destroyed by fire; St Florian is the patron saint of firefighters; the kuna was Croatia's currency until 2023; Mišin Marko was the old road-mender.
