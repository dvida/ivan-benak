Homeland and native region

Kitišanci

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kitišanci selo malo,na kartama ne postoji,tek stotinjak kuća ima,al' godina puno broji.

Kitišanci, little village,on the maps does not appear;just a hundred houses, roughly,but it's counted many a year.

Tisućutristo devedeset šeste,vladali su Mađaroni.Spominje se ovdje pusta,po imenu Kustahoni.

In one thousandthree hundred and ninety-six,Magyar lords held power here;a deserted hamlet's mentioned,Kustahoni, so we hear.

Stoljeća su prolazila.Povijesni nam kažu znanci,tisućuosamsto šezdeset osme,stoji ime Kitišanci.

Centuries went passing by;learned historians tell us plain:in one thousandeight hundred and sixty-eight,Kitišanci is the name.

Doselili sa svih strana,izgradili kuće ljudi,udobno se tu smjestili,pošten narod, mirne ćudi.

From all sides the people came here,built their houses, one and all,settled in, at ease and snugly —honest folk, of temper calm.

Selo malo, al' ponosno,svi u njemu vrijedno rade.Odlučili dični ljudi,bogomolju da sagrade.

Small the village, but a proud one,all who live there toil and strive;and its worthy folk decidedthat a house of prayer should rise.

Tisućudevetsto osamdeset šeste,na šestotom rođendanu,rekoše da oltar bude,u čast svetome Ivanu.

In one thousandnine hundred and eighty-six,on its six-hundredth birthday,they declared an altar shouldrise in honour of Saint John.

U Hrvatskoj rat još traje,topovska se rika čuje.Složan narod ovog sela,materijal naručuje.

War in Croatia still is raging,cannon thunder can be heard.One in heart, the village peoplesend their orders for supplies.

Tisućudevetsto devedeset druge,temelji su postavljeni.Svetom misom zahvalnicom,radovi su posvećeni.

In one thousandnine hundred and ninety-twothe foundations then were laid;with a thanksgiving Holy Massblessings on the work were prayed.

Tek godina i pol minei odjeknu zvona jeka.Ovo zdanje bogom dano,sa radošću narod čeka.

Scarce a year and a half went by,and the bells rang, echoing;this, a house bestowed by God,folk awaited, all rejoicing.

Tisućudevetsto devedeset treće,na dvanaestoga prosinca.Misom svetog blagoslova,posvećena je crkvica.

In one thousandnine hundred and ninety-three,on the twelfth day of December,with a holy Mass of blessingwas the little church made holy.

Oh, posveti, Bože ruke,koje su ti hram dizali,da bi naša pokoljenja,za blagoslov božji znali.

Sanctify, O Lord, the handsthat have raised this house to you,so our generations aftermay know of God's blessing too.

I neka je puno hvala,svim ljudima ovog kraja,koji su svoj obol dali,za taj kutak svetog raja.

And may many thanks be givento the folk of this countrysidewho have offered up their mitefor this holy nook of paradise.

Valpovo, 11. 12. 1993.

Valpovo, December 11, 1993

Translator's note. The history of a small village and of its church of St John, which was built during the Croatian War of Independence (1991–95) and consecrated on December 12, 1993.
