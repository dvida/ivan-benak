Domoljubne i zavičajne

Podravino moj voljeni kraju

Homeland and native region

Podravina, My Beloved Land

Podravino domovino mila,ti si vječno Dravi bedem bila,mnoga mjesta na njedrima svilai Sopje si nježno prigrlila.

O Podravina, dear homeland, youhave always stood, the Drava's bulwark true;so many villages nestle at your breast,and Sopje you have tenderly embraced.

Nitko ne zna od kada postojiš,vječno ovdje ti ponosno stojiš,kao čedo svoje Sopje voliš,ponosna si, nikog se ne bojiš.

How long you've been, not one of us can say;you stand here proudly, always, and you stay;you love your Sopje as your very own child;you're proud, and fear no one in any way.

I svi tvoji običaji slavni,od davnina narodu su znani.Tvoji ljudi zovu se Hrvati,Sopje će ti svoju ljubav dati.

And all your customs, famous far and wide,the people long have known on every side;your people bear the name of Croats, andand Sopje will give you its love with pride.

Narodu si i brižna i milai Sopju si uvijek draga bila,a sebe si Slavoniji dala,što ti svaki Hrvat kaže HVALA!

You're caring toward your people, and so dear,and you were always loved by Sopje here;and to Slavonia you gave yourself, too:for that each Croat says a THANK YOU to you!

Valpovo, 16. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 16, 2013
