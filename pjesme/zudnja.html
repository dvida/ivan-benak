Yearning

Ako mene nekada sretneš,pokraj mene tiho prođi,pogled nemoj ti da smetneššapatom zvat ću te: dođi!

If you ever chance to meet me,pass me by, go quietly;do not turn your eyes away:whispering, I'll call, "Come to me!"

Ne mogu ti reći zbogom,ništa neka te ne čudi.Misleći na razlaz s tobom,očaj se u meni budi.

I can't say to you goodbye;let nothing make you wonder, though:thinking of our parting, Ifeel despair within me grow.

Ja od tebe sad odlazims mnogo bola pa i šutnje:novom stazom sada gazim,sad ostaju ipak žudnje.

Now from you I go awaywith much pain, and silence, too;on a new path I make my way,yet the yearnings stay, it's true.

Valpovo, 29. 11. 1975.

Valpovo, November 29, 1975
