The Owl

Sova hučena vrh kuće,gleda mišakako šišaniz dvorišteu vrtište.Ali jao,u čas zao!Sleti sovas vrha krova.Bocnu kljunommalog miša,na dva putaga proguta.

The owl is hootingon top of the house,her eyes on the scootingof a little mouseacross the yardto the garden bed.But oh, how hard,what an hour of dread!Down from the roofthe owl swoops now,pecks with her beakthe little mouse, and how!In two gulps, so,down he'll go.
