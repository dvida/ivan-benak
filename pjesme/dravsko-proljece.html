Domoljubne i zavičajne

Dravsko proljeće

Homeland and native region

Drava Spring

„VEZOVI“ su Đakovački,a „JESENI“ Vinkovačke,Sopjani su blizu Drave,pa „PROLJEĆE DRAVSKO“ slave.

Đakovo has its "Embroidery",Vinkovci, its "Autumn" days;Sopje folk live by the Drava,so their "Drava Spring" they praise.

Tridesetog travnja Sopjespretno veze kolo sreće,jer Sopjani toga danaslave „DRAVSKO PRAMALJEĆE“.

On the thirtieth of AprilSopje deftly weaves joy's ring,since upon that day its peoplecelebrate the "Drava Spring".

Za druženje ovo slavnosvi KUD-ovi dobro znaju,skupljaju se toga dana,da ljepotu Sopju daju.

Of this famous get-togetherevery folk group's well aware;on that day they all assemble,bringing Sopje beauty there.

Dolaze već godinamaSlavoniju da proslave.Iz Mađarske dični ljudi,lađom plove preko Drave.

Year on year they have been comingSlavonia's praises to declare;worthy folk from Hungarysail across the Drava there.

Dočeka ih KUD „PODRAVAC“,od sreće se bratski grle,pa kad kolo odigraju,veselo u Sopje hrle.

There KUD "Podravac" receives them,hug like brothers, glad to meet;then, the kolo danced, they gailythrong to Sopje's village street.

Mnoga društva iz Mađarske,svoja kola su igrali:Barč, Martinci, Pečuh, Starin,ljepotu su Sopju dali.

Many groups from Hungarydanced their kolos in that place:Barč, Martinci, Pečuh, Starin,lending Sopje beauty's grace.

U jesen će KUD „PODRAVAC“,Mađarskoj posjet vratiti,svu ljepotu našeg kraja,Mađarima on će dati.

In the autumn KUD "Podravac"will repay the Hungarians' call,and our region's every beautyit will carry to them all.

Kad se skupe svi KUD-ovii povorka Sopjem prođe,sva ljepota te priredbedo vrhunca slave dođe.

When the folk groups all assembleand the march through Sopje goes,all the splendour of the showto its peak of glory grows.

U tom času žica cikne,zaigraše cure snaše,s igrom su razigralimnogobrojne folkloraše.

At that moment strings ring out,girls and young wives take the floor,and their dancing sets a-dancingfolk performers by the score.

Kad se kolo zavijori,noge pletu, sve se praši.Tankovito cura vrisne,običaji to su naši.

When the kolo swirls and flutters,feet are weaving, dust flies high;shrill and thin a girl cries out:these are customs we live by.

I, tako se razmjenjujuslavna društva ovog kraja.S ljepotom nošnja krasiovaj dio našeg raja.

And in turn they come, the famoustroupes of all this countryside;with their beauty, costumes deckthis our piece of paradise.

I priredba sve polakoprimiče se svome kraju.Na redu su tamburaši,da pokažu sve što znaju.

Now the festival, so slowly,draws on nearer to its end;next in line, the tambura playersshow the skill that they command.

Već i polnoć tiho kuca,a veselje Sopjem teče.Opet ćemo do godineslaviti „DRAVSKO to PROLJEĆE“.

Softly midnight's knocking now,yet through Sopje joy flows free;we'll be back next year to hailthat same "Drava Spring", you'll see.

Valpovo, 25. 06. 2011.

Valpovo, June 25, 2011

Translator's note. Drava Spring (Dravsko proljeće) is the folklore festival held in Sopje every 30 April, named in the manner of Slavonia's two great festivals, the Đakovo Embroidery and the Vinkovci Autumn. A KUD is a village folklore society. Barč, Martinci, Pečuh and Starin are the Croatian names of Barcs, Felsőszentmárton, Pécs and Drávasztára in Hungary.
