Umirovljenički jadi

Članovima Udruge matice umirovljenika D. Miholjca

Pensioners' woes

To the Members of the Pensioners' Association of D. Miholjac

Dragi moji prijatelji,časno društvo Miholjčani.ja vam želim dobro zdravlje,bilo bolje nego lani.

Dearest friends, so dear to me,honoured Miholjac company,I wish you good health, and more:better than the year before.

Topli pozdrav Vama želi,ostvarenje Vaših snovai uspješni rad skupštine,pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova.

Warmest greetings he sends you,may your dreams come true, and somay your meeting prosper well:Ivo, poet of Valpovo.

Sve čestitke idu vama,u protekloj godiniciza uspješan rad udruge,časnoj Miri predsjednici.

All congratulations goto you all, the past year through,for the club's successful work —honoured Mira, president, too.

Aktivan je bio do sad,svaki uspjeh vašeg rada,još revnija neka bude,u svom radu nova Vlada.

Every triumph of your workhas been lively till today;may the new Government bekeener still in every way.

U proteklom svome mandatu,marljivo ste se trudili,takmičenja sa društvima,dobre rezultate dali.

All throughout your recent termyou have laboured hard and true;in the contests with other clubsgood results you've brought home, too.

A PJEVAČKI ZBOR vaš slavno,vaš je ponos, vaša dika,sa opojnim svojim glasom,oni su vam časna slika.

And your singing choir, renowned,is your pride, your glory bright;with their captivating voicesthey present an honoured sight.

Bili sretni i veseli,zdravo tijelo održali,a penziju vašu dragu,mnogo ljeta uživali.

May you all be glad and cheerful,keep your bodies sound and strong,and enjoy your precious pensionmany summers, well and long.

U životu vašem dragom,ne bilo vam nikad krivo,ne tražite vrata raja,to vam želi Benak Ivo!

All your dear life long, may younever have a thing to rue;don't go seeking heaven's gate —Benak Ivo wishes you!

Valpovo, 03. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 3, 2010

Translator's note. A greeting to the association's assembly in Donji Miholjac. He jokingly calls the newly elected committee the "Government" (Vlada); "don't go seeking heaven's gate" is his standing wish that his fellow pensioners not hurry into the next world.
