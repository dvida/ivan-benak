A Look at Love

Kad Šokica poljubi svog dikui prkosna kad istakne njedra,diki pruži ljubav u šljiviku,u posijelu obraza je vedra.

When a Šokica kisses her sweetheartand pertly thrusts her bosom into view,she gives him love within the plum orchard;then at the sijelo her face is cheerful, too.

Vjetar nježno kad miluje grudii pronađe one skrite sjene,svojoj dragoj kada ljubav pruži,sa poljupcem sklopi oči njene.

When the wind caresses breasts so gentlyand finds those hidden shadows there,when he gives his darling all his love,with a kiss he shuts her eyes with care.

Kada mjesec Danicu pronađe,pa za oblak svojom zvijezdom zađe,u ljubavi svoj fenjer ugasi,pa zar može nešto biti slađe.

When the moon finds Danica on his wayand with his star behind a cloud slips away,in love he puts his lantern out, and then —could anything be sweeter, pray?

Sretan mačor u krevetu prede,sve što treba u krilašcu nađe,tu su snovi koji zlata vrijede,ništa nema od ljubavi slađe.

A happy tomcat purrs upon the bed,in a little lap he finds all that he needs;here are the dreams worth their weight in gold,and nothing's sweeter than love, indeed.

Valpovo, 07. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 7, 2012

Translator's note. Danica is the Morning Star (Venus), in folk song the moon's beloved.
