Pjesme za djecu

Tri majstora

Poems for children

Three Craftsmen

Sastala se tri majstora,svaki hvali djelo svoje.Među sobom divanilinajvažniji od njih tko je.

Once three craftsmen came together,each one praised the work he'd done,and they chatted, weighing upwho was the most important one.

„Na prvome mjestu ja sam!“,šeštrdžija veli glasno.„Ja ljudima kape pravimda ne kisnu – to je jasno.“

"I come first of all!" the hattersays out loud, and makes it plain:"I make caps for all the people,so they won't get wet in rain."

Postolar će na to reći:„Da ne gaze ljudi rosu,ja cipele svima pravimi obuvam nogu bosu.“

Says the cobbler then in turn:"So folk needn't tread the dew,I make shoes for everyone,and bare feet, I shoe them too."

„Ja sam važan!“ veli krojač,„kod mene se ruho šije.Pravim kaput, prsluk, hlače,da ljudima zima nije.“

"I'm important!" says the tailor,"clothes are sewn by me, you see:coats and waistcoats, trousers too,so that folk from cold are free."

Ja zaista ne znam rećišto je za nas najvažnije,ali blato, kiša, studen –to je za nas najstrašnije.

I, in truth, can't really saywhich of these we need the most,but the mud, the rain, the cold —these we fear above the rest.

I majstori tad će znati:ne vrijedi se prepirati.Ta vidjet će oni lakoda su vrijedni podjednako.

And the craftsmen then will seethat it's pointless to disagree,for they'll find out easilythey're of equal worth, all three.
