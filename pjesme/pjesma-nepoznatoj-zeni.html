A Poem for an Unknown Woman!

Ovu pjesmu pišem jednoj ženi,koju prije nikad nisam znao.Njezin život meni je u sjeni,jer je do sad nisam upoznao.

I write this poem for a certain womanwhom I had never known in any way.Her life, to me, lies hidden in the shadow,for I had never met her till today.

OVO MI JE ISPRIČALA ŽENA!Težak život dok je bila mala.Prošla mladost, kao tužna sjena,u šesnaestoj već se je udala.

This is the story that the woman told!A hard life from the time that she was small.Her youth went by her like a mournful shade;at sixteen she was married, just a maid.

Kad je mladost u ljepoti cvala,prošla ljeta k'o da su u mraku,troje djece Mirjana je dala,a brak njezin lebdio u zraku.

When youth was blooming in its beauty's prime,the summers passed, it seemed, in darkness there;Mirjana bore three children in that time,her marriage hanging, floating in the air.

Još je bila u najljepšem cvijetu,teška bolest Mirjanu je našla.Rasula se dječica po svijetui duševna bol ju tada snašla.

While she was still in fullest flower and bloom,a grave disease sought out Mirjana, found her;the little ones went scattered through the world,and anguish of the soul then closed around her.

Bolni dani teško joj prolaze,nemoćna je u svome hodanju.Strašne boli i mužu dolaze,težak život u bračnome stanju.

Her painful days drag by, so hard to bear;she's feeble now, and helpless in her gait.And dreadful pains her husband too must share:a heavy life within the married state.

Ona ima šezdeset pet ljeta,a njemu je sedamdeset troje.Bivša mladost više im ne cvijeta,obadvoje pate boli svoje.

Her summers now have come to sixty-five,and his have reached to seventy-and-three;their bygone youth no longer blooms for them;both suffer their own pains, both he and she.

Svakog dana bol se s njima druži,teškom mukom Mirjana se kreće.Ružica im kćerka radost pruži,u toj patnji imaju i sreće.

Each day the pain keeps company with them;Mirjana moves about with toil and strain.Their daughter Ružica brings them some joy,and happiness, in suffering, remains.

Valpovo, 15. 07. 2014.

Valpovo, July 15, 2014

Translator's note. He wrote down the life of Mirjana, a woman in poor health, as she told it to him.
