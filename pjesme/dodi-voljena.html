Love poems

Come, My Beloved

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dođi, voljena,kada sam sretan,da radost svojus tobom podijelim.

Come, my beloved,when I am happy,so that my gladnessI may share with you.

Dođi, draga,u naručaj meni,osmijehu tvomeda se veselim.

Come, my darling,come into my arms,so that your sweet smilemay gladden me too.

Dođi i ondakada sam tužan,srce te mojei onda želi.

Come to me alsowhen I am grieving,for this heart of minelongs to have you near.

I tugu je lakšenositi udvoje,kad ju se s nekimiskreno dijeli.

Sorrow, too, is lighterwhen carried by two,if someone shares itwith a heart sincere.

Valpovo, 1976.

Valpovo, 1976
