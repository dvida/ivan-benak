Love poems

Come, My Beloved

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dođi, voljena,kada sam sretan,da radost svojus tobom podijelim.

Come, my beloved,when I am glad,so that my joyI may share with you.

Dođi, draga,u naručaj meni,osmijehu tvomeda se veselim.

Come, my darling,into my arms,so that your smilemay gladden me too.

Dođi i ondakada sam tužan,srce te mojei onda želi.

Come also then,when I am sad —this heart of minewants you then, too.

I tugu je lakšenositi udvoje,kad ju se s nekimiskreno dijeli.

Sorrow, too, is lighterwhen carried by two,when shared with anothersincerely and true.

Valpovo, 1976.
