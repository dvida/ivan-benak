Radio burza

Rasprodaja drva

Radio market

Firewood Clearance

Dobra drva ja prodajem svima,trebat će vam, jer dolazi zima.

Good firewood I'm selling, for all to buy;you'll need it, for the winter's drawing nigh.

Uzimajte drva sad što prije,dok još zima nastupila nije.

So get your wood as early as you may,while winter still has not come round to stay.

Drva dajem kako tko poželi,bukvu, jasen može i grab bijeli.

I'll sell you any wood that you may crave:beech, ash, or else white hornbeam you can have.

Suha drva sva su izrezanai sjekirom lijepo iscijepana.

The wood is dry and sawn, and all of itis nicely split — I took the axe to it.

Jeftina su u ovoj sezoni,jeftinija neg' u Arizoni.

This season, it's as cheap as it can go —it's cheaper than in Arizona, you know!

Tristo kuna cijena je od juče,sa prijevozom sve do vaše kuće.

Three hundred kunas, as of yesterday,with transport to your very house, I say.

Još dodajte neku crkavicu,složit ću ih sve u drvarnicu.

Add something small — a trifle, just a fee —I'll stack it in the woodshed; leave it to me.

I narudžbe primam čak što više,moj telefon sve će da zapiše.

And orders I'll accept, the more the better;my phone will write them down, to the letter.

652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,nazovite, pa ću biti vrijedan.

652 – eight hundred twenty-one:call me, and I'll be busy getting it done.

Valpovo, 09.10. 2000.

Valpovo, October 9, 2000

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza.
