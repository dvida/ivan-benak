Radio burza

Rasprodaja drva

Radio market

Firewood Clearance

Dobra drva ja prodajem svima,trebat će vam, jer dolazi zima.

I'm selling good dry firewood to all:you'll need it, for the winter comes to call.

Uzimajte drva sad što prije,dok još zima nastupila nije.

So get your wood as early as you may,before the winter comes along to stay.

Drva dajem kako tko poželi,bukvu, jasen može i grab bijeli.

Whatever wood you'd like, you'll have it here:beech, ash, or else white hornbeam, never fear.

Suha drva sva su izrezanai sjekirom lijepo iscijepana.

The wood is dry, it's all been sawn to size,and split up nicely with the axe likewise.

Jeftina su u ovoj sezoni,jeftinija neg' u Arizoni.

This season it's as cheap as it can be,cheaper than Arizona, you'll agree!

Tristo kuna cijena je od juče,sa prijevozom sve do vaše kuće.

Three hundred kuna, since just yesterday,delivered to your door, no extra pay.

Još dodajte neku crkavicu,složit ću ih sve u drvarnicu.

Throw in a little something, just a mite,I'll stack it in your woodshed, neat and tight.

I narudžbe primam čak što više,moj telefon sve će da zapiše.

I'll take your orders, and the more the better;my phone will write them down, to the letter.

652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,nazovite, pa ću biti vrijedan.

652 – eight hundred twenty-one:just call, and I'll be quick to get it done.

Valpovo, 09.10. 2000.

Valpovo, October 9, 2000

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza. Arizona was a local shop.
