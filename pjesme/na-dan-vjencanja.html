Family corner

On Your Wedding Day!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U životu mome sve si bio meni,za tebe bih dao obje svoje ruke.Od ovoga trena ti pripadaš ženi,ja ti želim sreću, bez bola i muke.

You were my everything throughout my life;for you I'd give away both hands, it's true.From this moment on you belong to your wife;I wish you joy, with no pain nor grief for you.

U ovome času ti ćeš gnijezdo sviti.Uskoro ćeš i ti svoje čedo zvati.Voli svoje bližnje i voljen ćeš biti.To ti želi: sestra, tvoj otac i mati.

This is the hour in which you'll build your nest;soon you as well will call a child your own.Love those close to you, and you'll be loved no less.This wish is from your sister, father, mother, every one.

Valpovo, 28. 07. 1979.

Valpovo, July 28, 1979

Translator's note. Written for his son's wedding.
