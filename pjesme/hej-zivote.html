Memories and reflections

Hey There, Life

Hej živote, malo stani!Sretna zoro, daj osvani!Radosnice, suzo, kani!Da nam budu ljepši dani.Dobro zdravlje, sreće puno,povrh svega, zlatno runo!

Hey there, life, slow down, stand still!Happy dawn, rise up — you will!Tears of joy, come fall your fill,so our days grow brighter still.Health and happiness in store,and the Golden Fleece, what's more!

Svim ljudima ovog svijeta,da im grana mira cvijeta!Ljudski razum da ne gube,da se kao braća ljube!Sretnu ljubav i veselje!Iskrene su moje želje.

To all people of this world:may the branch of peace unfurl!May they keep their human mind,love like brothers, true and kind.Happy love and merriment —these, my wishes, truly meant.

Valpovo, 24. 05. 1993.

Valpovo, May 24, 1993
