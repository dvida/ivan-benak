Love poems

The Castaway

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kao brodolomac na olujnom morusrce mi je bolno, oduzete ruke.Nemam čuna, vesla, ni vodilju zoru,da ja sretno stignem do ljubavne luke.

Like a shipwrecked sailor on a stormy sea,my heart is aching and my arms have lost their strength;no boat, no oar, no guiding dawn is left to meto bring me safely into love's harbour at length.

Sudbina je htjela da mi ruke pružiš,onoga trenutka, kada tonu lađe.Da sam opet voljen i da žarko ljubimod toga saznanja ništa nije slađe.

Fate willed that you should reach your hands out to mein that very moment when the ships go down.That I am loved once more, and ardently in love —no sweeter knowledge than this could be found.

Dala si mi sebe i sreću i radost,a moje patnje bile su preduge.Probudila ljubav i zaspalu mladost,za jedan pogled pun čežnje i tuge.

You gave yourself to me, and happiness and joy,when all my sufferings had lasted far too long;you woke my love, and woke my youth that lay asleep,with one glance, full of sorrow and of longing.

Ja opet živim, gledam žarko suncei jutarnju rosu kako trava pije.I dalje moje spavalo bi srce,da ga tvoja ljubav probudila nije.

I live again; I see the blazing sun above,and how the grass drinks in the morning dew so deep;and still this heart of mine would lie there fast asleepif it had never been awakened by your love.

Da ti kažem hvala, ne, ja ipak ne ću,ja ti dajem srce što za tobom žudi.Ti si zaslužila radost još i većui poljupce slatke kada zora rudi.

To tell you "thank you"? No, I won't, after all;I give you, rather, my heart that longs for you.You have deserved a joy still greater, and allthe sweetest kisses when the red dawn's breaking through.

Valpovo, 06. 04. 1975.

Valpovo, April 6, 1975
