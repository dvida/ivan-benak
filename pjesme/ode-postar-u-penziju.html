Umirovljenički jadi

Ode poštar u penziju

Pensioners' woes

The Postman Retires

Imao sam prijatelja,u pošti je on radio.Dobar čovjek, vrijedan bio,tešku torbu je nosio.

Once I had a friend of mine,at the post office he worked:a good man, hard-working, too;heavy bag he never shirked.

Kada dođe mojoj kući,posebno mi drago bilo,još kad bi mi pismo dao,meni oko srca milo.

When he came up to my house,it would make me very glad;more so when he brought a letter —how it warmed the heart I had!

Sada ide u penziju,vjerujte mi, drago mi je,život će mu lakši biti,neće teglit kao prije.

Now he's off to his retirement,and believe me, I am glad;life will now be easier for him,he won't haul the load he had.

Neće tražit ni broj kuća,niti slušat lavež pasa,neće tražit pravo mjesto,da pošiljki nađe spasa.

No more hunting for house numbers,nor hear dogs that bark and bay,nor go searching for the placewhere a parcel's safe to stay.

Mirovinu koju primaš,nije važno kolika je,već je bitno, prijatelju,da što duže ona traje.

And the pension you'll be drawing —how much it is, never mind;what's important, friend of mine, isthat it lasts a long, long time.

Valpovo, 24. 08. 2013.

Valpovo, August 24, 2013
