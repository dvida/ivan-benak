Domoljubne i zavičajne

Domovino moja

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

My Homeland

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Slavonijo, zemljo ponosita,malo ravna, malo bregovita,zlatnim klasjem ti si okićenai sa rujnim vinom zalivena.

Slavonia, O proud land of my birth,part level and part hilly is your earth,adorned with golden ears of wheat so fine,and watered well with ruby-coloured wine.

Slavonijo, ljepotice moja,nikada te neću nikom dati,svaki dio te ponosne zemlje,ja ću voljet i čuvati znati!

Slavonia, my beauty, all my own,I'll never give you up to anyone;each part of this proud land I'll love, and Iwill know how best to guard it, every one!

Zemljo moja, srcu najmilija,ja sam tebi dao zavjet sveti:Ako treba, život ću ti dati,tu sam rođen, tu ću i umrijeti!

My land, the dearest to my heart of all,to you I made a sacred vow, that I,if need be, would give up my life for you:here I was born, and here I'll also die!

Zato, mila, iskreno te molim,za svu ljubav koju sam ti dao,podari mi jedan kutak sebe,da bi vječno s tobom drugovao.

So, darling, I most earnestly implore,for all the love I've given you, one thing:bestow on me a little nook of you,that I may keep you company evermore.

I kad umrem ti ćeš dalje cvastii majčinski širit krila svoja.Dok je tebe, Šokadijo, mila,bit će dičnih ljudi i heroja!

And when I die, you'll blossom on and on,and spread your wings, as mothers always do;as long as you are here, dear Šokadija,there will be worthy people, heroes too!

Gospo sveta, čuvaj mi Hrvatsku,tu najdražu djedovinu moju,zato sam ti dao zavjet sveti,da sačuvam Šokadiju svoju.

O holy Lady, keep Croatia safe,my dearest land, my fathers' legacy;and so to you I made a sacred vowto guard my Šokadija faithfully.

Valpovo, 07.02.1997.

Valpovo, February 7, 1997
