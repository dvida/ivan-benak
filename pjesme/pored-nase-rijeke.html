Love poems

Beside Our River

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Od kada se nismo vidjeli nas dvoje?Možda te sad više prepoznao ne bi'?Godine su duge učinile svoje,a nekada mio bio sam ja tebi.

How long since last we saw each other, we two?Would I still know you now, if we should meet again?The long years that have passed have done what years will do,and once I used to be so dear to you, back then.

U sjećanju mome još je blijeda slika,onih dugih šetnji pored naše rijeke,slatkih poljubaca kraj gustih vrbika,divnih uspomena, prošlosti daleke.

In memory there still remains a faded viewof the long walks we took along our river's shore,of the sweet kisses where the willows thickly grew,of lovely memories, the distant days of yore.

Tko zna gdje si sada i čija si žena!Daleko od mene savila si gnijezdo.Ona divna prošlost sad je uspomenai sva ona mjesta gdje smo bili često.

Who knows where you are now, and whose wife you may be!Far off from me, somewhere, you've built your nest, your home.That lovely past is now a memory to me,and all those places where so often we would roam.

Sjetim se tebe kad prođem kraj rijeke,gledajući dvoje gdje se sretno ljube.Kao da mi šapću iz daljina jeke:Volim te, mili! A riječi se gube.

I think of you whenever by the river I strolland see two lovers kissing happily nearby;as though the distant echoes whispered to my soul:"I love you, dear!" — and then the words just fade and die.

Valpovo, 12. 03. 1976.

Valpovo, March 12, 1976
