Occasional verse and dedications

The Poet Bole Writes Many Books

Imam jednog dragog prijatelja,mnogi ljudi iskreno ga vole,a Božidar Golik on se zove,od miline njega zovu Bole.

I have a friend, and he is very dear;a lot of people love him, truly, wholly.Božidar Golik is his name, but here,for fondness' sake, they call him simply Bole.

Bole vam je pjesnik onaj pravi,mnoge pjesme on je rimovao,mnogo ljudi znaju ga na javi,devet knjiga do sad darovao.

Now Bole is a poet, the real kind;so many poems has he put in rhyme;a lot of people know him in the flesh;nine books he's gifted to us by this time.

Da vam kažem kako Bole diše,razne teme on je pisat znao.Ima knjiga koje i sad piše,naslove bih knjiga napisao.

Let me just tell you what our Bole's like:on many different themes he knew to write.Some books he's working on right now as well;the titles of his books I'll now recite.

U početku, bilo je to davno,ljubavna je koju ljudi vole.Po imenu: "Radosti života",prvu knjigu napisao Bole.

At the beginning (it was long ago),a book of love, which people cherish truly:"The Joys of Life", that was the title, sothe first book of them all was penned by Bole.

Nije prošla ni godina dana,druga knjiga: "Stazama zavičaja"Ta je knjiga bila vrlo znana,piše radost ljubavnoga raja.

Not even one whole year had passed, and herethe second book: "Along the Paths of Home".That book was widely known, both far and near;it tells the joy of love's own paradise.

Naslov knjige "Srce u plamenu"dv'je tisuće u devetom ljetu,napisao Bole knjigu znanu,tužnu knjigu o bolu i ratu.

"A Heart in Flames": that is the book's own name;in two thousand and nine, the year it came,our Bole wrote this well-known book of his,a sad book, all of pain and all of war.

Uplovio već je u desetu:"Pjesme za djecu", piše nove šeme,one su vam najbolje na svijetu:"Mali snovi" na istu su temu.

He's sailed on now into the tenth, and here's"Poems for Children", in a brand-new scheme;of all the world, the best of all are these;and "Little Dreams" is on the selfsame theme.

Eto stiže već do pete knjige:"Slavonija u paklu i raju"!Te su pjesme pisane od brige,o našem se radi zavičaju.

And now he comes already to book five:"Slavonia in Hell and Paradise"!These poems from worry and from care derive:it is our homeland that they're all about.

Moj prijatelj piše i romane,zove se: "Suze na putu nade"!Sa olovkom ispunjava dane,o pisanju sve što treba znade.

My friend, as well, writes novels, it is true;one's called "Tears on the Road of Hope"! And sohe fills his days with pencil, through and through;he knows all that a writer needs to know.

Sedma nosi naziv: "Putne staze"!Svaka pjesma neku tugu daje,pa ti suze bolom oči kvase,čitatelju ona tužno traje.

The seventh one is titled "Paths of Travel"!In every poem there's some grief to find,so tears of pain come welling in your eyes,and sadly lingers in the reader's mind.

Deveta je: "Kazaljka života"!Tu je knjigu ljubav okitila,čitati ju prava je divota,jer ljubav je svakom biću mila.

The ninth: "The Hand upon the Clock of Life"!This book with love has been adorned, and soto read it is a true delight, for loveis dear to every creature here below.

Eto, to je moj prijatelj Bole,knjige piše, ne znaš im ni broja,te sve knjige čitatelji vole,a posebno, on je dika moja.

So there you have him: that's my friend, our Bole;he writes his books, their number none can tell,and all these books the readers love so fully,and most of all, he is my pride as well.

Translator's note. A tribute to his friend, the Slavonian poet Božidar-Bole Golik, built from the titles of Golik's books, given here in English. The numbering of the books is the poet's own.
