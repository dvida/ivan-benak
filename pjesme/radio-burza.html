Radio burza

Za emisiju Radio burza na lokalnom radiju pisao je oglase u stihu: prodaje bundu, bure za rakiju, televizor u boji, kazan i kukuruz. Telefonski broj na kraju bio je stvarno njegov.

Poems

Radio market

For Radio burza, the buy-and-sell show on the local radio, he wrote his classified ads in verse: a fur coat, a brandy barrel, a colour television, a still, a load of maize. The phone number at the end was really his.
