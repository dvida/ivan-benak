Dragoj rodbini obitelji Klement

Occasional verse and dedications

To My Dear Relatives, the Klement Family

Klement Željku i Emicii njihovoj dragoj djeci.Nek' im bude knjiga ova,uspavanka prije snova.Da bi jutrom svježi bilii duhana posadili.A s jeseni kad sazori,sa rabotom duhan bralii državi urod dali,dobru lovu zaradili,preko zime odmarali,od svog rada uživali.Pa kad dođe Terezija,nek' veselo KIRVAJ prija,na svu radost i veselje,eto, to su moje želje!I snivali divne snove,želi autor knjige ove!

To Željko Klement, Emica,and their children, dear to me:may this book be theirs to keep,a lullaby before they sleep.So they'd rise up fresh and clearand plant tobacco for the year.And in autumn, when it's ripe,pick tobacco, hard at work,hand the state the crop they've grown,earn a heap of dough, their own,rest up all the winter through,and enjoy their labour's due.When Terezija comes around,may their kirvaj merry be,full of joy and merriment:there, those are my wishes, see!And sweet dreams the whole night through:this book's author wishes, too!

Valpovo, 18. 10. 2010.

Valpovo, October 18, 2010

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his book Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse) for relatives who grow tobacco, which is sold to the state buyer. St Teresa's Day is their village kirvaj, the church fair held on the parish saint's feast.
