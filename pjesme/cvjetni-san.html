A Dream of Flowers

Kad malenim selom prođemželim tebe vidjet tamo,na prozoru tvoje kućeto moja je želja samo.

When I pass the little villageI would like to see you so,at the window of your house —that's the only wish I know.

I sada još uvijek želim,da vidim na njemu cvijećasa usana njenih osmijehza mene bi bila sreća.

Even now I still keep wishingthere'd be flowers there to see,and a smile upon her lips —that would be such joy for me.

Njen prozor je sada prazanni cvijeta na njemu nema,kapak te stare roletneusamljen na njemu drijema.

Now her window's standing empty,not a flower, not a one;and the shutter of the old blinddozes on it all alone.

Sve je tako brzo prošlo,kao vjetrom odnesen dan,i cvijeće sa tog prozoraza mene je ostao san.

All of it has passed so quickly,like a day the wind has swept,and the flowers at that windowonly in my dreams are kept.

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979
