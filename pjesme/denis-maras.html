Occasional verse and dedications

Denis Maras

Denis Maras, to je dično ime.Jednog dana upoznah se s njime.Priđoh k njemu, nisam ga ni znao,kroz par riječi sam ga upoznao.Rekoh njemu, da ja pišem knjigei da imam financijske brige.Da li bi mi donaciju dao?Bez riječi je Denis pomogao.I prođoše nekoliko dana,knjiga mi je štampana bez mana.Ja sam svoje ostvario snove,STIHOM KROZ ŽIVOT knjiga se zove.A Denisu najtoplije hvala,u posjed mu ova knjiga mala!

Denis Maras — now there's a worthy name.One day I met him; this is how it came:I went to him, a man I never knew,and got to know him in a word or two.I told him that it's books I write, and howI'm troubled by my money worries now.Would he donate to me? I had to ask;without a word, Denis took up the task.A few days went by, and my book was there,printed without a flaw, with every care.And so I'd made my dreams come true, my own:"Through Life in Verse": by that name it is known.And to Denis, my warmest thanks are due:into his keeping goes this small book, too!

Valpovo, 17. 06. 2010

Valpovo, June 17, 2010

Translator's note. A thank-you to the sponsor who paid for the printing of his sixth book, Stihom kroz život ("Through Life in Verse", 2010).
