Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Za Osnovnu školu Vladimir Nazor, Komletinci

Occasional verse and dedications

For Vladimir Nazor Primary School, Komletinci

Draga djeco, dragi đaci,da vam budu ljepši sati,a dosada da se skrati,da ne stane dnevnih briga,evo vama moja knjiga,pa ju lijepo pročitajte,veselja si srcu dajte.U životu sretni bilii petice ostvarili.

Dearest children, pupils dear,so your hours may brighter be,and your boredom shorter here,so your daily cares may flee,here's a book of mine for you:read it nicely, through and through,give your hearts some gladness too.May your lives be happy always,and may you achieve straight A's.

U čitanju nek' vam knjigapruži radost i veselje,stvarajući ovu knjigu,to su bile moje želje.Bili sretni i veselii snivali divne snove,od sveg srca vama želi:mali Zeko knjige ove!Ne bilo vam nikad krivo,to vam želi pjesnik Ivo.

As you read, may this book bring youhappiness and merry cheer;as I made it, this was alwayswhat I wished, my wish sincere.May you all be glad and merry,may your dreams be lovely too —from his heart the little Bunnyof this book so wishes you!May you never feel put out:that's the poet Ivo's wish, no doubt!

U Valpovu, jednog sata,tridesetoga listopada,eto to je bilo - znajte,dvije tisuće i dvanaeste!

In Valpovo, at an hourof October's thirtieth day,that is when it was — take note —two thousand and twelve, I say!

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck), whose hero is the little Bunny. A "petica" (a five) is the top mark in Croatian schools.
