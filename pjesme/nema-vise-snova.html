Love poems

No More Dreams

Bili smo blizui tako sretni,onih prvih dana,kada smo se sreli.

We were so closeand so happyin those early dayswhen we first met.

Sada smo zajedno,a ipak daleko.Čitava vječnostnas dvoje dijeli.

Now we're together,but far apart;a whole eternitydivides us yet.

Pogledi našinekamo lete,u prazna prostranstvau daljine plave.

Our gazesfly off somewhere,into empty spaces,into the distant blue.

Ti pogledi suznipuni gorke jave.Nema više snova,samo bolne jave.

Those tearful gazes,full of bitter waking;there are no more dreams,only painful waking.

Valpovo, 14. 08. 1982.

Valpovo, August 14, 1982
