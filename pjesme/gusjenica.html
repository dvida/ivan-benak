The Caterpillar

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Ja sam malagusjenica.Jedem lišće,istina je cijela,no, list knjigeđaka Markanikad nisam jela.

I'm a littlecaterpillar.I eat leaves,that's the whole truth, it's true,but the leafof schoolboy Marko's bookI never chew.

Translator's note. In Croatian, as in English, list is both a leaf of a tree and a leaf of a book.
