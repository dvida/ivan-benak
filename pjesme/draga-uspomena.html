Love poems

A Cherished Memory

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nikad više neću ljubit mile oči.I nikada više mrsit tvoju kosu,niti tijelo tvoje milovat u noći,ni s usana tvojih piti mednu rosu.

Never again will I kiss those dear eyes of yours,and never again will I tousle your hair,nor caress your body in the night-time hours,nor drink the honeyed dew your lips would share.

Ti nikada više nećeš meni doći.Možda ti je drugi sad od mene draži.Da tu bol izdržim, da l' ću snage smoći?U tu ljubav svoju utkala si laži.

Never again will you come back to me.Perhaps another's dearer to you now than I.Will I find the strength to bear this agony?Into your love for me you wove a lie.

I tu patnju moju, tko će da opiše?Kao da po vatri gaze noge bose.Oh, ljubavi mila, ja te nemam više!Još jedino snovi mene tebi nose.

And this suffering of mine — who could tell it true?It is as if bare feet were treading on a fire.Oh, my dearest love, I have no more of you!Only my dreams still carry me to you.

I svu bol i suze, što mi srce rani.Kada tebe sretnem, bit ćeš tuđa žena.I sva ljubav naša i svi sretni dani,za mene će biti draga uspomena.

And all the pain and tears with which my heart is torn —when next I meet you, you will be another's wife.And all our love, and all the happy days we've known,will be, for me, a dear memory all my life.

Valpovo, 14. 04. 1975.

Valpovo, April 14, 1975
