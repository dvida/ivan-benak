The Sleeping Kitten

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Mala maca umiljataoštre šape ima.Zato, seko, ne ljuti je,jako grebe njima.

Little kitty, sweet and cuddly,has sharp paws, you know.So, dear sister, don't you tease her —she will scratch you so!

Naša maca, kada spava,miče šapom, brkom,valjda miša tada sanjai hvata ga trkom.

When our kitty is asleep,paw and whisker flick;she must dream she's chasing mice,running, oh so quick!

I kad spava, motor radi,ona tiho prede.Oh, te mačke baš ne znajugorivo da štede!

Even sleeping, motor running,purring soft and deep.Oh, these cats just never learnfuel isn't cheap!
