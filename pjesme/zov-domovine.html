Domoljubne i zavičajne

Zov domovine

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

The Call of the Homeland

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Rođena ti gruda sreću, ljubav daje,svako drvo, kamen, dječje radovanje.To ti tuđa zemlja nikad neće dati.Maćeha se majkom nikad neće zvati.

The soil where you were born gives love and joy,each tree, each stone, the gladness of a boy;no foreign land will give you this, no other:a stepmother will never be called mother.

Kada si daleko, nostalgija zove.Iz dna duše čut ćeš poziv zemlje ove.Na njedarca svoja k'o voljena žena,radosno te prima, to je dužnost njena.

When far from home, nostalgia calls you here;from deep within your soul this land's call rings clear.To her dear breast, just like a well-loved wife,she takes you gladly in: that is her part in life.

U tuđoj se zemlji gorka sjeta krije.Jednom ćeš se vratit, ako nikad prije,ono u starost, kad mladost uvene,da posiješ kosti u utrobe njene.

In foreign lands a bitter sadness lies in wait;one day you will return, if not before, then late —in your old age, when youth has had its day,to sow your bones within her womb, to stay.

Zato svoju zemlju i ljubi i voli,najdražom je zovi, vrijeđat ne dozvoli.Ona ti sve prašta, k'o rođena mati.Tu rođenu grudu treba čuvat znati.

So love your land, and cherish her, and hold her dear;call her the dearest, let no insult come near.She forgives all, just like the mother of your birth;you must know how to keep and guard your native earth.

Valpovo, 1981.
