Occasional verse and dedications

Esteemed Friends

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

Warm greetings I extend to all of you,and I rejoice at this assembly too.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,ovog časa što predsjednik kaže,ja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear the splendid results you have won,your good success, your plans for work ahead,and I congratulate you, every one,on all your president just now has said.

Sve čestitke vašem predsjednikui ostalim ovdje prisutnima,plodonosni rad skupštini vašoj,ja iskreno želim sada svima.

Congratulations to your presidentand all the others who are present here;I wish your whole assembly fruitful work,for all of you my wishes are sincere.

Za sva djela što ste učinili,svojim radom u godini ovoj,ja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the deeds that you have brought aboutby all your work throughout the year now done,I wish you still more good success, and more,fulfilled within the year that's just begun.

A skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ona sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And may this whole assembly, here and now,stand by the present Government in place,for, with élan in plenty, it will leadthe club in future work, and set the pace.

Da bi zdravo tijelo održali,redovito na Dravi pecali.Na godinu više se družili,s udrugama sportski takmičili.

To keep your bodies healthy, sound and hale,go fishing on the Drava without fail.Next year may you more often come to meet,and with the other clubs in sport compete.

Podružnice što se s vama druže,Petrijevci, Šag, Nard i Valpovo,Gat, Bizovac i Društvo Satnicaneka bude divna utakmica.

The branches that keep company with you,Petrijevci, Šag, Nard and Valpovo,Gat, Bizovac, the Satnica club too:let there be a splendid match between you!

Svi u zdravlju i veselju bili.Uživajte veselo bez rada.Mnogo ljeta na vodi pecali,ne tražite svetog Petra vrata.

May health and joy be with you all, and cheer;enjoy yourselves, and let the labour wait!For many summers fish the waters here,and don't go looking for Saint Peter's gate.

Ostvarenje vaših divnih snova,želi: Pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova!

May all your splendid dreams come true! Just sowishes the Poet Ivo from Valpovo!

Valpovo, 07. 02. 2013.

Valpovo, February 7, 2013

Translator's note. Written for the annual assembly of an angling society and its branches along the Drava. St Peter, a fisherman, is the patron of anglers and the keeper of heaven's gate.
