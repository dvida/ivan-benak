Radio burza

Ništa ne prodajem, već samo dajem

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

I'm Not Selling, Only Giving

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Poklanjam štence, dva mjeseca stare,od oca terijera i majke Tare.

Two-month-old puppies I'm giving for free:a terrier's their dad, their mum is Tara, see.

Majka je labrador pravi po rasi,divna ih dlaka sve skupa krasi.

Their mother's a labrador, a true pure breed;a lovely coat adorns them all, indeed.

Tako su lijepi, slatki i mali,slični su majci, vrh repa im fali.

They're pretty, sweet, and little, every one;they're like their mum — except the tail-tip's gone.

Namršte se i ponekad laju,pseći jezik već pomalo znaju.

They frown, and now and then they bark, and — say! —they know a bit of dog-speak, in their way.

Tko ih poželi, neka se javi,na moj telefon jedini pravi.

Whoever wants them, please get in touch — callmy phone, the one true number, that's all.

Šesto šezdeset dva dvjesto jedanaest,može dopodne il' poslije dvanaest.

Six hundred sixty-two two hundred eleven:call in the morning, or after twelve, by heaven!

Ako ih netko osobno traži,ulica je Hrvatske Mladeži.

If someone wants to fetch them, face to face,the street is Hrvatske Mladeži — that's the place.

Na broj osamnaest Kitišanci,to su adresi osobni znaci.

Number eighteen, Kitišanci, that will bethe address — its particulars, you see.

Opet kažem, nije za pare,već darujem štence naše Tare.

Again I say it: it is not for pay —I'm giving our Tara's puppies away.

Šest komada razne spoli,birajte sami, kakve tko voli.

Six little ones, of both sexes, too;choose for yourselves whichever pleases you.

Ponavljam telefon: 6 6 2 2 1 1,tko ih udomi nek' bude sretan.

The phone again: 6 6 2 2 1 1,may those who give them homes be happy, every one!

Valpovo, 22. 02. 2007.

Valpovo, February 22, 2007

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad giving away the puppies of "our Tara", a labrador. Kitišanci is a village near Valpovo.
