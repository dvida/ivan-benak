Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovane „Perunike“

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear "Perunike"

Diljem naše Slavonije,pjesmom ste ljepotu dalei još mnogo, mnogo ljeta,moje pjesme Vi pjevale.

All across our Slavoniayou've brought beauty with your song,and for many, many summerssing my songs, and sing them long!

Vaše društvo „PERUNIKE“,posebna ste meni dika.Taj fascikl nek' Vam bude,uspomena od pjesnika.

You, the group called Perunike,are my special pride, you see;let this folder be for youa keepsake from the poet: me.

Svojim glasom u pjevanju,osvojili zlatna slova.Iskreno Vam ovo želi:Benak Ivo iz Valpova!

With your voices, as you sing,may your names be writ in gold.Sincerely wishing this to you:Benak Ivo, from Valpovo!

Valpovo, 06. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 6, 2012

Translator's note. He gave the women's choir Perunike ("Irises") of Ivankovo a folder of his poems, and they sang his songs. "Written in golden letters" is the Croatian phrase for lasting fame.
