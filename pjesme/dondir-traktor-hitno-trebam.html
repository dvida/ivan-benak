Humorous poems

I Need a John Deere Tractor, Urgently

Ja subotom rado slušamslavonsko veselje,Cecilija divno vodi,te narodne želje.

On Saturdays I gladly hear"Slavonian Merriment";Cecilija presents so wellthe requests the people sent.

Toga časa što poželiš,svirat će svirači,samo reci svoje želje,Cecika će naći.

Whatever you might wish for then,the players play it through;just tell her what you'd like to hear —Cecika finds it for you.

Al' velika želja sadana srcu mi stoji,da dobijem traktor ĐONDIR,dragi, ljudi moji.

But now there is a great big wishthat weighs upon my breast:to get myself a JOHN DEERE tractor,dear people, I confess.

One njive od mog djede,orati bi moro'šta je djeda u davnini,volovima or'o.

Those fields my grandfather had,I'd need to plough them now,that Grandpa, in the olden days,with oxen used to plough.

Zato molim Cecilijunek' uzme na znanje,da mi traktor ĐONDIR dadedjedu za oranje.

And so I beg Cecilijato please take note of this:to give this grandpa a JOHN DEEREfor ploughing — that's my wish.

Adresa je moja ista,kao nekad znajte:pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova,samo traktor dajte.

My address is the same one still,as you knew it before:the poet Ivo, Valpovo;just give the tractor — nothing more!

Kada traktor ja dobijem,bit će k'o u raju.Provozat ću Ceciliju,po zelenom gaju.

And once I've got my tractor, itwill be like paradise:I'll drive Cecilija aroundthe green grove, once or twice!

Valpovo, 01. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 1, 2013

Translator's note. "Đondir" is his phonetic spelling of John Deere. Cecilija presented "Slavonsko veselje" (Slavonian Merriment), a Saturday radio request show.
