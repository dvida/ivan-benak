I Need a John Deere Tractor, Urgently

Ja subotom rado slušamslavonsko veselje,Cecilija divno vodi,te narodne želje.

"Slavonian Merriment" I loveto hear each Saturday;Cecilija presents so wellthe wishes folk convey.

Toga časa što poželiš,svirat će svirači,samo reci svoje želje,Cecika će naći.

Whatever you might wish for then,the players play for you;just tell her what your wishes are,Cecika sees it through.

Al' velika želja sadana srcu mi stoji,da dobijem traktor ĐONDIR,dragi, ljudi moji.

But now I've got a great big wishthat's weighing on my mind:to get myself a JOHN DEERE tractor,dear people, good and kind.

One njive od mog djede,orati bi moro'šta je djeda u davnini,volovima or'o.

Those fields that were my grandad's onceI'd need to plough right now,the ones that Grandpa, long ago,with oxen used to plough.

Zato molim Cecilijunek' uzme na znanje,da mi traktor ĐONDIR dadedjedu za oranje.

And so I beg Cecilijato note it, if she will:to give this grandpa a JOHN DEEREthe fields to plough and till.

Adresa je moja ista,kao nekad znajte:pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova,samo traktor dajte.

My address is the same one still,as you have known before:the poet Ivo, Valpovo;the tractor, nothing more!

Kada traktor ja dobijem,bit će k'o u raju.Provozat ću Ceciliju,po zelenom gaju.

Once I have got my tractor, lifewill be like paradise:I'll drive Cecilija aroundthe green grove, once or twice!

Valpovo, 01. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 1, 2013

Translator's note. "Đondir" is his phonetic spelling of John Deere. Cecilija presented "Slavonsko veselje" (Slavonian Merriment), a Saturday radio request show.
