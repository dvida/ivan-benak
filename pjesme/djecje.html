Pjesme

Pjesme za djecu

U dječjem pjesništvu bio je najbolji, kako su se složili i kritičari. Pjesme iz Malih snova, Zekine sreće i Žabljeg sastanka djeca i danas uče napamet u vrtićima i školama.

Poems

Poems for children

Critics agreed that children's poetry was where he was at his best. Children still learn the poems from Little Dreams, Bunny's Luck and The Frogs' Meeting by heart in kindergartens and schools.
