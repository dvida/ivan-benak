To My Friend Željko, on a Bottle of Rakija

Željko Bračun

Puno cijenim tvoju promociju,zato dajem ovu rakijicu,da Ti srce aktivnije bude,pa obraduj svoje drage ljude.Ti ne čekaj cijelu godinicu,već iznjedri sad novu knjižicu.Učini si ugodno veselje:to ti Tvoji prijatelji žele!I napiši to ugodno štivo:od srca ti želi Benak Ivo!

I prize this launch of yours so much, and soI give this little rakija, you know,so that your heart may be more active, too,and then go gladden those most dear to you.Don't wait a whole long year — there's no need to —bring out a little booklet now, brand new!Give yourself pleasant cheer, and have some fun:your friends all wish you this, yes, every one!And write that pleasant reading — have a go!So wishes, from the heart, Benak Ivo!

Valpovo, 24. 09. 2014.

Valpovo, September 24, 2014

Translator's note. Written on a bottle of rakija he gave at the launch of Željko Bračun's book Jesmo li prokleti ("Are We Cursed?").
