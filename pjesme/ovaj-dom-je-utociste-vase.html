Umirovljenički jadi

Ovaj dom je utočište vaše

Pensioners' woes

This Home Is Your Refuge

Svima vama što ste u tom Domuusamljeni u životu svomu,tuga vas je morila u tami,noć je tiha, vi kod kuće sami.

To all of you who live here in the Home,who in your lives were lonely, on your own:sorrow would wear you down when night came on,the night so still, at home you sat, alone.

Kuća prazna, sve iz kuće ode,nikog nema da ti pruži vode.U toj tuzi samoće su krive,zato ovdje štićenici žive.

The house is empty; everyone has gone,there's none to hand you water, not a one.That sorrow's cause is loneliness, I fear,and that is why the residents live here.

Ovaj Dom je za starije ljude,tu mnogima utočište nude,za sve one znane i neznane,da ugodno tu provedu dane.

This Home is meant for people old in years;it offers refuge to so many here,for all of those, the known and the unknown,to spend their days here pleasantly, at home.

I starački dovukli se jadi,kao nekad niste više mladi,vi potrebe imate po noći,ovdje će vam osoblje pomoći.

The woes of age have crept in, too, at last;you are not young, as in the days now past;at night you may have needs of every kind,and here the staff will help you, you will find.

Sjetite se kad ste mladi bilii ljubavne snove dok ste snili.I starost je ljepša kad se druži,to druženje Dom će da vam pruži.

Remember when you once were young, and whenyou dreamed the dreams of love you dreamed back then.Old age is sweeter, too, in company,and that the Home will give you, you will see.

Nakon snova kad otvoriš oči,sve što trebaš Dom će ti pomoći.Dobar ručak i šetnja te čeka,prije snova posteljica meka.

When dreams are done and you open your eyes,the Home will help, whatever needs arise.A good lunch and a walk await you, thena soft, soft bed before you dream again.

Pa zar može nešto bolje biti,u noćima divne snove sniti.Vaša mladost u snove se vrati,u tom Domu sve ćete imati.

Now tell me, could there be a better thingthan dreaming lovely dreams the nights will bring?In dreams your youth comes back to you, and soin this Home you'll have everything, you know.

Kad vas jutro iz toplog sna budii kad danak na ljepotu sluti,a prijatelj kraj tebe nasmije,eto, to su životne premije.

When morning wakes you from a cosy sleep,the day foretelling beauty on its way,and when a friend beside you gives a smile,why, those are bonuses that life will pay.

Valpovo, 04. 12. 2013.

Valpovo, December 4, 2013

Translator's note. Written for the residents of a home for the elderly.
