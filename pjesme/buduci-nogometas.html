Poems for children

The Future Footballer

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja unuka imam,dobar je u školi,al' najviše ipak,on nogomet voli.

I've a grandson whodoes well at his school,but most of all he lovesfootball, as a rule.

Kad iz škole dođe,u kut baci torbu,na nogomet hrli,na junačku borbu.

Home from school he comes,in the corner flings his case,off to football, whereheroic battles take place.

Pred sumrak se vratisa svoga turnira,u sobu uletida si nađe mira.

When dusk is near he's backfrom his tournament;into his room he bursts,on peace and quiet bent.

No, istoga trenamira nema više,djedica ga zove,da zadaću piše.

But that very momentthere's no peace in store:Granddad calls him into do his homework chore.

U mislima svojimjoš golove niže,nikakve zadatkene može da piše.

In his thoughts he's stillscoring goal on goal;not a single sumcan he write at all.

Djeda dalje viče:„Daj zadaću sredi!“Unuk knjigu zgrabimrsko u nju gledi.

Granddad keeps on shouting:"Get that homework done!"Grandson grabs his bookand scowls at it, glum.

Žurno knjigu lista,prsti brzo lete,podero se papir,kod stranice pete.

He flicks the pages fast,his fingers fly and dive,and then the paper ripsright there on page five.

Da je neka frkamilo mu je jasno,to se slova tuku,stigao je kasno.

That there's been a ruckushe can clearly tell:the letters have been fighting,and he's come late as well.

Sad ljutito gledarupu na papiru,a slova su tihai stoje u miru.

Now he glares in angerat the paper's hole,but the letters, silent,stand as good as gold.

Translator's note. Dedicated "to my grandson Denis Vida, who is an excellent student, but not a footballer".
