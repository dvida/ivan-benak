Radio burza

Sijeno za krave

Radio market

Hay for Cows

Preko burze prodana mi koza,ostalo mi sijena za tri voza.

The Burza sold my goat for me, and soI've hay left over — three whole loads to go.

Ne znam tona koliko to važe,ali znadem da ga ljudi traže.

I don't know how many tons it'd weigh,but I know people want it, anyway.

U trendu su opet suhe trave,ne daj Bože da polude krave.

Dry grasses are in fashion once again —God grant the cows don't go mad, amen!

Mljevene kosti i riblje brašno,na tov junadi djeluje snažno.

Ground bones and fishmeal, mixed into the feed,work strongly on the fattening steers, indeed.

Ljudi su kravama tu smjesu dali,da sebe truju to nisu znali.

People gave cows that mixture, unawarethat they were poisoning themselves right there.

I malim telcima već je jasno,da meso kravama nije slasno.

Why, even little calves know that it's true:that meat's not tasty to a cow to chew.

Tako od postanka znaju krave,da ne jedu meso, već samo trave.

Since time began, the cows have always knownthey don't eat meat, but grass, and grass alone.

A kod mene takve trave ima,pa i sada, makar je zima.

And I have grass like that right here with me,even now in winter, as you see.

Sve u suhom štaglju pohranjeno,miriši fino to suho sijeno.

It's stored away inside a barn that's dry —that dry hay smells so fine, I can't deny.

Ne tražim profita, ni zarade prave,već da vam krave ostanu zdrave.

I'm not after profit, nor real gain, myself —I only want your cows to keep their health.

Tko poželi sijena dati kravi,neka se na moj telefon javi:

Whoever wants to feed his cow some hay,just call me on my telephone, I say:

652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,za zdravlje krava bit ću sretan.

652 – eight hundred twenty-one;I'll be glad for healthy cows, when all is done.

Valpovo, 20.01. 2001.

Valpovo, January 20, 2001

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza, written during the "mad cow disease" (BSE) scare of 2000–2001, which was traced to cattle feed made from meat-and-bone meal.
