Protestne i društvene

Oj, dodo, oj, dodole

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

Oj, Dodo, Oj, Dodole

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nekada se pjevat znalo:Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

In the old days people sang:Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

Daj nam, Bože, kiše malo,da natopi žedno polje,da se malo živi bolje.

Give us, Lord, a little rain,soak the thirsty field again,so we'll live a little better.

Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

Kome krivo, kome milo,poreza je uvijek bilo,nameta i raznih daća,to je bilo od vajkada.Al' za kišu da se plaća,nije bilo baš nikada.

Some are sorry, some are glad,taxes we have always had,levies, dues of every kind —so it's been since times of yore.But a fee for rain, you'll find,never was, not once before.

Svakog dana kiše liju,nastala je vražja klima.Kiša pala, žetva stala,primanja su vrlo mala,al' nameta zato ima.

Every day the rains pour down,such a devil of a clime.Rain has fallen, harvest's stalled,and our earnings are so small,levies, though, come all the time.

Kraj poreza, raznih daća,PDV-a i nedaća,"KIŠARINA" sad se plaća.

Besides taxes, dues and fees,VAT and woes galore,"RAIN TAX" now is paid, what's more.

Sada narod od ljepote,pjesme pjeva sve na notei hrvatske plaća vode.

Now the folk, for sheer delight,sing their songs by note, just right,and pay Croatian Waters, too.

Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

Oj, dodo, oj, dodole.

Valpovo, 26. 04. 1998.

Valpovo, April 26, 1998

Translator's note. The dodole were village girls who, in times of drought, went from house to house in a folk rain-making rite, singing "Oj, dodo, oj, dodole". The "rain tax" (kišarina) is his joke name for the water charge paid to Hrvatske vode (Croatian Waters); VAT came in 1998.
