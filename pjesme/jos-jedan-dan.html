One More Day

Još jedan je prošao dan,tihi su večernji sati.Molim te, dođi, voljenašto prije do mene svrati.

One more day has passed away,and the evening hours are still.Please, beloved, come to me,come as soon as ever you will.

Ne gubi vrijeme uzalud,dođi mi noći ove,da vodimo slatku ljubav,da snivamo nježne snove.

Do not waste the time in vain,come to me this night, I pray,so that we may make sweet loveand in tender dreams may stay.

A kada zora svane,sunce kada grane,ljubit ću te, voljenai živjeti sretne dane.

And when dawn begins to break,when the sun comes shining through,I will kiss you, my beloved,and live happy days with you.

Valpovo, 15. 07. 1984.

Valpovo, July 15, 1984
