Protestne i društvene

Hrvatski ponos

Protest and society

Croatian Pride

Ja sam Hrvat, to morate znati,jer hrvatska rodila me matii sa svojim mlijekom nadajala,u hrvatskom duhu odgajala.

I am a Croat — this you ought to know,for a Croatian mother bore me so,and it was with her milk that I was fed,and in the Croatian spirit I was bred.

Ja hrvatsku trobojnicu nosim,što sam Hrvat time se ponosimtaj sam ponos od malena znao,svoj bi život za Hrvatsku dao.

I carry the Croatian tricolour;I'm proud to be a Croat, to the core.I've known this pride since I was very small;I'd give my life for Croatia — my all.

Hrvatsku su mnogi svojatali:Turci, Srbi, Nijemci i Mađari.Hrvati su ginuli u boju,braneć' hrabro domovinu svoju.

Many have claimed Croatia as their own:the Turks, Serbs, Germans, Hungarians, each one.Croatians fell in battle, fought and died,defending bravely all their homeland wide.

Za oružje svoj novac sam dao,trebalo je i nije mi žao.Sa gorčinom srce mi se puni,jer su novac uzeli tajkuni.

I gave my money for the arms we'd need;it had to be — I don't regret the deed.But now my heart is filled with bitterness,because the tycoons took that cash, no less.

Pati narod u Hrvatskoj sada,svuda kriza, sve manje je rada.Za boljitak nestala je nada,čelična bi tu trebala Vlada.

Now in Croatia the people suffer so,there's crisis everywhere, and work runs low;all hope of better days has gone, I fear;a Government of steel is needed here.

Valpovo, 24. 11. 2010.

Valpovo, November 24, 2010

Translator's note. In 1991 many Croatians gave money to arm the country's defence in the Homeland War; the "tycoons" are the profiteers of the privatisation that followed.
