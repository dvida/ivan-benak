Love poems

Echo

Ja te stvarno volim,ako ti sada kažem,vjerovati nećeš,reći ćeš da lažem.

It's true, I really love you;if I should tell you so,you never would believe me —you'd call it lies, I know.

Volim te, volim te, volim!Taj odjek divne jeke,u srcu mi snažno bruji,k'o žubor gorske rijeke.

I love you, love you, love you!That lovely echo's ringhums strongly in my heartlike mountain waters murmuring.

I zato što te volimmeni uvijek sude,zbog ljubavi mojei zbog pjesme lude.

And just because I love youthey're always judging me,for all this love of mineand for my foolish poetry.

Valpovo, 02. 04. 1979.

Valpovo, April 2, 1979
