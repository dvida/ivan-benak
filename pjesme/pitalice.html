Poems for children

Questions

from the books Bunny's Luck (1990) and Through Life in Verse (2010)

Pokaži, mi tata,kako trava rastei, molim te, reci,kud odlaze laste?

Show me, Daddy, please,how the grass can grow,and tell me, won't you,where the swallows go?

Po dalekom jugulaste se skitaju.I tamo su djecašto isto pitaju.

Far down in the southis where the swallows roam,and children there ask toothe same as you at home.

Reci, gdje se zimapo ljetu smrzavai pokaži krevetu kojem snijeg spava.

Tell me, in the summer,where does winter freeze?Show me, too, the bedwhere the snow sleeps, please.

Preko ljeta zimana sjeveru spava.Tamo snijega imai studen je prava.

In summer, winter sleepsfar north, where cold is real;there snow lies deep in heaps,and frost is all you feel.

Molim te, tata,objasni mi sve toi gdje se zimiskriva toplo ljeto.

Daddy, please explainall this, if you know,and where warm summer hidesin the winter snow.

Ljeto zimi odeu krajeve druge,ugrijati djecunakon zime duge.

Summer, in the winter,travels far awayto warm the children aftera long winter's stay.

I trava se žurišto prije narasti,kada dođe kravada ima što pasti.

And grass is in a hurryto grow up quick and high,so when the cow comes grazingshe'll find her meal nearby.

A krava će djecislatkog mlijeka datida brže narastu,da mogu sve znati.

The cow will give the childrensweet milk, and every onewill grow up all the fasterand know all under the sun.
