Poems for children

Questions

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Pokaži, mi tata,kako trava rastei, molim te, reci,kud odlaze laste?

Show me, Daddy, please,how the grass can grow,and tell me, won't you,where do swallows go?

Po dalekom jugulaste se skitaju.I tamo su djecašto isto pitaju.

Far away down souththe swallows roam, it's true.And there are children therewho ask the same as you.

Reci, gdje se zimapo ljetu smrzavai pokaži krevetu kojem snijeg spava.

Tell me, in the summerwhere does winter freeze?And show me the bedwhere the snow sleeps, please.

Preko ljeta zimana sjeveru spava.Tamo snijega imai studen je prava.

Winter, in the summer,sleeps up north, you know.There the cold is real,and there's lots of snow.

Molim te, tata,objasni mi sve toi gdje se zimiskriva toplo ljeto.

Daddy, please, explainall of this, I say,and where warm summer hideson a winter's day.

Ljeto zimi odeu krajeve druge,ugrijati djecunakon zime duge.

Summer, in the winter,goes to lands elsewhere,to warm the children afterthe long winter there.

I trava se žurišto prije narasti,kada dođe kravada ima što pasti.

And the grass is hurryingto grow up with speed,so that when the cow comesshe'll have what she needs.

A krava će djecislatkog mlijeka datida brže narastu,da mogu sve znati.

And the cow will give the childrensweet milk, so that theycan grow up the fasterand know it all one day.
