Homeland and native region

Šokica

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Nema ljepše cure do šokice:tankog stasa, umiljatog glasa,usta medna i obraza jedra,obla bedra i prkosna njedra.

There's no girl as lovely as a Šokica:slender figure, voice of tenderness,honeyed lips, and cheeks so full and fresh,rounded thighs, and bosom bold, no less.

Kad zaigra, vrti se k'o čigra,Šokce grije kad skutima vije,okom sijeva, milozvučno pjeva,u grudima nježno srce bije.

When she dances, like a top she'll whirl,warms the Šokci as her skirts unfurl,eyes a-flashing, sweetly she will sing,and her tender heart is fluttering.

U šljiviku ljubi svoga diku,dok on kosi, poljupce mu nosi,anđelima ljepotom prkosi,šokačkim se porijeklom ponosi.

In the plum grove she will kiss her darling,while he mows, she brings him kisses too;angels with her beauty she'll outdo,she is proud of her Šokac blood through and through.

Poljupcima prekrivam joj lice,najljepše je u moje Šokice.Ej Šokice, milo moje janje,na jesen te vodim na vjenčanje.

I will kiss her face all over, so —loveliest is my Šokica's, I know.Hey, Šokica, my little lamb, you'll see:come the autumn, you'll be wed to me.

Na polju je kao heroina,kada srpom zlatno klasje žanje.U posijelu uz kap rujnog vina,svom je diki umiljato janje.

In the field she's like a heroinewhen her sickle reaps the golden grain;at the sijelo, with a drop of ruby wine,to her darling she's a gentle lamb again.

Valpovo, 20.01.1995.

Valpovo, January 20, 1995

Translator's note. This is the version he revised for the 2014 festival in Požega, set to music by Antun Nikolić – Tuca and sung by Željko Witovsky.
