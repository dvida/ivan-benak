Pjesme za djecu

Sretan Božić

Poems for children

Merry Christmas

Sretan Božić vamaJa iskreno želim.Poklonima vašimI ja se veselim,Zato što ste dobriBožićnjak će znatiI pod bor će poklonaVama puno dati.

Merry Christmas to you all,I sincerely wish today;and I too will share your joyin the presents come your way.Since you've all been good, you know,Father Christmas knows it too,and beneath the tree he'll leavelots of presents just for you.

U igri i veseljuNek' vam bude živoTo vam djeco želiPjesnik Benak Ivo

May your games and merrimentall be lively, full of go —this, dear children, is the wishof the poet Benak Ivo.

Valpovo, 21. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 21, 2012

Translator's note. Written for the Bambi kindergarten's Christmas visit to the pensioners' home in Valpovo in December 2012. He signs himself surname first, Benak Ivo, in the old village way.
