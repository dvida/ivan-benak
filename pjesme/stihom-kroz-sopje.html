Domoljubne i zavičajne

Stihom kroz Sopje

Homeland and native region

Through Sopje in Verse

Dan općine četvrti je svibnja,Florijanu ljudi zavjet daju,da ne bude velikih požara,kao Patoš što Sopjani znaju.

The fourth of May's the Municipality Day,when people make their vow to Florian:may no great fire break out, is what they pray,like Patoš, known to every Sopje man.

Dan općine u Višnjici slave,svi vijećnici uz domjenak malii nagrade kad dodjele prave,nek' se znade što su Sopju dali.

In Višnjica they celebrate the day,the councillors, with a small reception laid;the awards are handed out, and that's the wayall come to know the gifts to Sopje made.

U proljeće MAJIPANI se sade,to mladići znak curama daju,jer ujesen svatovi se prave,neka budu spremne za udaju.

In spring the maypoles all are set up high:the young men give the girls a sign this way,for come the autumn, weddings will be nigh —let them be ready for their wedding day.

IVANJE se slavi još od prije,u ljepoti dobro odigrano:od Udruge "Sportske rekreacije",sa veseljem Sopju darovano.

Ivanje has been kept since long ago,in beauty, and it's played out well and true;the "Sports Recreation" club puts on the show,a gift to Sopje, given gladly too.

Kad Marija Magdalena dođe,drugi kirvaj što u Sopju traje,pa kroz Sopje sva ljepota prođe,to crkvene slave običaje.

When Mary Magdalene's day comes round again,the second church fair Sopje holds each year,and all the beauty passes through it then —these are the customs that church feasts hold dear.

Nogometni klub "Podravac" slavni,od davnina svoja ljeta broji.U sopjanskom kraju on je glavni,to službeno u zapisu stoji.

The football club "Podravac", famed and grand,has counted up its years since long ago;it is the leading club in Sopje's land:the official record tells us it is so.

Dobrovoljno vatrogasno društvo,već stotinjak godinica niže,sve požare u Sopju i bliže,svojim radom ugasiti stiže.

The Volunteer Fire Brigade has been therea hundred little years, strung one by one;to every fire in Sopje, and elsewhereclose by, it comes, and puts out every one.

Ribolovno društvo "Šaran" zvani,sa vršama često ribu hvata,kad uhvati deset kila soma,tu se onda pravi fišijada.

The fishing club that's called "Šaran" by namegoes often catching fish with traps, and whenit lands a catfish, ten kilos in weight,they hold a fish-stew feast right there and then.

Valpovo, 18. 09. 2012.

Valpovo, September 18, 2012

Translator's note. A year of Sopje's feasts: St Florian's Day (4 May) is the municipality's day; the maypoles (majipani) are set up by the young men; Ivanje is St John's Day at midsummer; the church fair (kirvaj) of Mary Magdalene falls in July. Patoš is the street once destroyed by fire, and Šaran means "carp".
