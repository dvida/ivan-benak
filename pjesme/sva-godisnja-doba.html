Sva godišnja doba

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Love poems

All the Seasons

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

U ljubavi našoj sve do ovih dana.Sva godišnja doba prošla su kroz mene.Od najljepše sreće pa do bolnih rana.Od žarkoga ljeta do zime studene.

In our love, right up until these days,all the seasons of the year have passed through me:from the loveliest of joys to aching wounds,from the burning summer to the winter's freeze.

Kada sam te sreo uz mirisno cvijeće,zadrhtaše strune od osmijeha tvoga.Za mene je bilo zeleno proljeće.S tobom mi je sreća došla dana toga.

When I met you where the fragrant flowers grew,at your smile the strings began to tremble so;for me it was the springtime, green and new;with you came happiness that day, I know.

Poljupcima vrelim ljeto si mi dala.Sa suncem u oku znala si mi doći.U srce mi zašla, a nisi ni znala,da te željno čekam svake tople noći.

With burning kisses, you gave summer to me;with the sun within your eyes, you'd come in sight;you came into my heart, and did not even seeI waited for you, longing, every warm night.

Za mene je bilo i suviše slatko.Želio sam tebe, tvoje zagrljaje.A naše je ljeto bilo vrlo kratko.Oh, kako sam htio da vječito traje!

For me it was all even far too sweet;I wanted you, I wanted your embrace.And yet our summer was so short and fleet —oh, how I wanted it to last forever!

Ali nakon sreće i bol mora doći.U moj život tiho jesen se uvukla.Ovi tmurni dani nikad neće proći.Kao list sa stabla srce mi je svukla.

But after happiness, the pain must also come;and into my life, so quietly, the autumn crept.These gloomy days will never pass, not one;it stripped my heart, as from a tree a leaf is swept.

I na kraju ipak i zima je došla.Srce mi je bolno puca od studeni.Kao da je sreća pokraj mene prošla.Kao santa leda srce je u meni.

And in the end, the winter came to me at last;my aching heart is cracking from the chill.As if my happiness had simply walked on past,the heart in me is like an iceberg, cold and still.

A gdje ti je ljubav kojom si se klela?Da me vjerno ljubiš svake tihe noći!Boli meni nikad ne bi dati htjela,da me nikad nećeš ostaviti moći!

And where's the love, then, that you swore by — where?That you would love me true each quiet night!That you would never wish to give me pain or care,that you could never bring yourself to leave me!

Gdje su slatke usne da mi cjelov pruže?Da mi led otope, gdje su tople ruke?Gdje su mile oči? Po kome sad kruže?Zašto me ostavljaš i mećeš na muke?

Where are the lips so sweet, to give to me a kiss?Where are the hands so warm, to thaw the ice in me?Where are those dear eyes now? On whom do they now rove?Why do you leave me so, and cast me into agony?

Valpovo, 19. 10. 1974.

Valpovo, October 19, 1974
