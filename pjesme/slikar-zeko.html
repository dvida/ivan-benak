Poems for children

Bunny the Painter

Jutros se u dvorištudigla strašna graja,jer netko je kokicamaofarbao jaja.

In the yard this very morningsuch a racket rose — and why?Someone went and painted allof the hens' eggs, on the sly.

Jedne žute, druge plave,a treće na točke.Obojena su i jajaispod stare kvočke.

Some were yellow, some were blue,some were polka-dotted, too.Even eggs beneath the oldbroody hen were painted through.

Ljutile se koke glasnopijetlu kvocat stale,pa su u toj silnoj grajii optužbe pale.

Loud and angry were the hens,to the rooster clucking so,and in all that mighty racketcharges flew both to and fro.

A pijetao prepun jadadvorištem se šeta.Da bi lakše naš'o krivcasa ograde gleda.

And the rooster, full of sorrow,paces up and down the yard;so he'll find the culprit sooner,from the fence he's looking hard.

Pogledom je sve motrio,čak i malog žuću:guske, patke, kravu bjelku,pa i našu kuću.

He kept watch on everything,even little Žućo there:geese and ducks, and Bjelka the cow,even our house, I declare!

I na kraju on opazikraj ograde uši,a to zeko svoje šapeobojene suši.

And at last the rooster spottedby the fence a pair of ears:it was bunny, drying offhis painted paws, it appears.

Mislim da je svima jasnotko je majstor bio.Eto, djeco, to je zekojaja obojio.

I think it's clear to everyonewho the master was that day.There, dear children: it was bunnywho had painted them that way.

Valpovo, 02. 2012.

Valpovo, February 2012

Translator's note. Žućo ("Yellowy") is a typical name for a farm dog, and Bjelka ("Whitey") for a cow.
