Poems for children

Bunny the Painter

Jutros se u dvorištudigla strašna graja,jer netko je kokicamaofarbao jaja.

In the yard this very morningsuch a racket rose:someone painted all the hens' eggs,who, nobody knows!

Jedne žute, druge plave,a treće na točke.Obojena su i jajaispod stare kvočke.

Some were yellow, some were blue,some with dots, and theneven eggs were painted underthe old broody hen!

Ljutile se koke glasnopijetlu kvocat stale,pa su u toj silnoj grajii optužbe pale.

Loud and angry, all the hensclucking to the rooster came,and in all that mighty racketthey began to lay the blame.

A pijetao prepun jadadvorištem se šeta.Da bi lakše naš'o krivcasa ograde gleda.

And the rooster, full of sorrow,paces round the yard;so he'll find the culprit sooner,from the fence he's looking hard.

Pogledom je sve motrio,čak i malog žuću:guske, patke, kravu bjelku,pa i našu kuću.

He kept watch on everything,little Žućo too:geese and ducks and Bjelka the cow,and our house in view.

I na kraju on opazikraj ograde uši,a to zeko svoje šapeobojene suši.

And at last he spied some earsby the fence nearby:it was bunny, holding outhis painted paws to dry.

Mislim da je svima jasnotko je majstor bio.Eto, djeco, to je zekojaja obojio.

I think everyone can seewho the master was today:there, dear children, it was bunnywho had painted them that way!

Valpovo, 02. 2012.

Valpovo, February 2012

Translator's note. Žućo ("Yellowy") is a typical name for a farm dog, and Bjelka ("Whitey") for a cow.
