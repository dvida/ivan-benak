Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Friends!

Želim da kroz knjigu ovu,naše prijateljstvo traje,jer stihovi ove knjigedaju nježne osjećaje.

It's my wish that through this bookmay our friendship last and stay,for the verses in this booktender feelings do convey.

Kad radosno srce kuca,toj ljepoti nema kraja,neka bude ova knjiga,dio naših osjećaja!

When the heart beats glad and fast,to such beauty there's no end;may this book become a partof our feelings, friend to friend!

To drugarstvo koje traje,da se nikad ne ugasi,neka ova pjesma bude,plamen da ju vječno spasi!

This companionship that lasts,so that it may never fade,let this poem be a flameby which it is ever saved!

Ožujak je danas prvidv'je tisuće i trina'jste.Stvarajući ovu pjesmu,u mom srcu sreća raste.

It's the first of March today,in two thousand and thirteen;as I make this poem, I feelhappiness grow deep within.

Valpovo, 01. 03. 2013.

Valpovo, March 1, 2013

Translator's note. A book dedication to Marija (Mica) and Željko Cavor of Ivankovo.
