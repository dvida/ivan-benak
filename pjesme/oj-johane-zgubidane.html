Humoristične pjesme

Oj, Johane - zgubidane!

Humorous poems

Oh, Johan, You Idle Man!

Ej, Jasmina baš Ti hvala!"KOMADA" si meni dala.Nisu snovi već je java,ta se lijepo umiljava.Osmijeh ima Mona Lize,kada grli nema krize.

Hey, Jasmina, thank you, dear,for the "DISH" you gave me here!It's no dream, it's real, you see —how she snuggles up to me!With a smile like Mona Lisa's,when she hugs, there are no crises.

Ej, Jasmina baš Ti hvala!Prijatelja si nazvala:„Oj Johane - ZGUBIDANE,Pane - bane i Cromane!“

Hey, Jasmina, thank you, dear!You called your friend, loud and clear:"Oh, Johan, you IDLE MAN,Pane — bane and Cromane!"

Dobar sam u školi bio,Romski nisam ja učio.Zato ne znam, tko Ti Pane,pa još bane i Cromane!!!

I was good at school, but see,Romani was not taught to me.So I don't know who's your Pane,let alone your bane and Cromane!!!

Promocija moja mala,ugodu je Tebi dala!Zgubidanom me nazvala,zar je ovo zato HVALA?!

My little book launch, it's true,gave some pleasure, then, to you!IDLE MAN is what you said —so is this my THANKS instead?!

Još na karti dalje piše:Moraju biti u skladu,vlastitim zapažanjima,uoči Velike Gospe.

On the card it further reads:They must be in keepingwith one's own observations,on the eve of the Assumption.

Što si htjela time reći,ja ne mogu to doseći.Da li da se molim Bogu?Ništa pojmit ja ne mogu.

What you meant to say by thatis beyond me, and that's flat.Should I pray to God above?Nothing can I make sense of.

Iskreno Ti sada velim,takva pisma ja ne želim.Prijatelj me, moraš znati:ZGUBIDANOM neće zvati.

Honestly, I tell you plain:I don't want such letters again.You should know, a friend won't evercall me IDLE MAN — no, never.

Pisat više ja ne mogu,Ti se lijepo moli Bogu.I za mene, sad izvoli,litaniju bar izmoli.

I can write no more, and soyou just pray to God, you know;and for me, too, if you please,say a litany, at least.

Da l' Ti pravo ili krivo,to Ti veli Benak Ivo.

Whether you like it or no,so says to you Benak Ivo.

Valpovo, 16. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 16, 2010

Translator's note. A reply to a card from Jasmina Nemet after his book launch. "Komad", "a piece", is slang for a good-looking woman, and "zgubidan" means a time-waster; "Pane, bane i Cromane" are nonsense rhymes, which he pretends are Romani.
