A Glass Brimming with Love

U mom srcu i u duši,ljubavi je puna čaša,ako me još, draga, volišvrati mi se ovog časa.

In my heart and in my soullove has filled a glass for two;if you love me still, my darling,come back now, this hour, please do!

Da zajedno ispijamo,kap po kap te slatke sreće.I nikad nas, nitko više,rastaviti, mila, neće.

So that we may drink together,drop by drop, that sweetest bliss,and that no one, darling, evercomes between us after this.

U mom srcu i u duši,ljubavi je puna čaša.Dođi, mila, neka budeu ljubavi sreća naša.

In my heart and in my soullove has filled a glass for two.Come, my darling, let it bethat our joy in love comes true.

Valpovo, 12. 12. 1975.

Valpovo, December 12, 1975
