HRV-a subotnje veselje

Occasional verse and dedications

HRV's Saturday Merriment

Kada vikend u Valpovu svanei subotnje dopodne pojavi,narod čeka te ugodne dane:HRV-a ljepota se slavi!

When weekend dawns in Valpovo, and thenthe Saturday forenoon comes into sight,the people wait for those fine days again:HRV's beauty is feted: what delight!

Umiljatim glasom Tomislava,na radiju zabrujale strune.Vjerujte mi, ljepota je prava,čestitka se sad sa pjesmom čuje.

With Tomislav's endearing voice, the stringsstrike up and hum upon the radio.Believe me, it's real beauty that it brings:with every song a greeting now will flow.

Deset sati, emisija kreće,prvi sat je KUD-ovaca slava.Slušatelji sad imaju sreće,to je za njih emisija prava.

At ten o'clock the programme's on its way;the first hour is the KUD folk's hour of fame.The listeners are lucky now, I'd say:for them, the programme that deserves the name!

Emisija to je tamburaša,slušatelje ugodno veseli,odavno je bila želja naša,da si može čestitati tko želi.

It is the tamburica players' show,it gladdens all who listen, fine and true;and it has been our wish since long agothat all who wished could send their greetings, too.

Ali kratko emisija traje,jedan sat su slušatelju dali,jer KUD-ova izvještaj se dajei taj satić slušaocu fali.

But all too short the programme's time on air:one hour's all the listener was allowed,for KUD reports are also broadcast there,and that small hour is missed by all the crowd.

„Daj narodu kruha i igara.“Pusti narod neka se veseli.Zato Šokci svi imaju dara:pjesnik Ivo to iskreno veli!

"Give people bread and circuses!" And solet the people have their fun and cheer.That's why the Šokci all have gifts, you know:the poet Ivo tells you, quite sincere!

To je naša emisija želja,bar subotom nek' se ona daje.Ta ljepota naših slušatelja:neka ona što duže potraje!

This is our request show, the show of wishes;at least on Saturdays let it go out.This beauty that our listeners all cherish:may it last on and on, without a doubt!

Valpovo, 21. 02. 2014.

Valpovo, February 21, 2014

Translator's note. Subotnje veselje ("Saturday Merriment") is the Saturday tamburica request show on the local radio station HRV, hosted by Tomislav. The first hour is given over to the reports of the KUDs, the village folk-arts societies, and the poet pleads for a longer show.
