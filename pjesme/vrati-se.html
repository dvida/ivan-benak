Love poems

Come Back

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Vrati se!Pruži mi ponovo ruku.Obnovimo ljubav našui počnimo život novi.Zaboravi,za patnju, bol i tugu.

Come back!Give me your hand again.Let us renew our loveand start a new life.Forgetthe suffering, the pain and the sorrow.

Vrati se!Pruži mi usne slatke.Probudi srce moje.Pruži mi ljubav svoju.Za tebe, za mene,za dane kratke.

Come back!Give me your sweet lips.Wake this heart of mine.Give me your love.For you, for me,for the days so brief.

Vrati se!Budimo sretni k'o prije.Ujutro kada se budiš,pričaj mi svoje snove.Spasi ljubav našu,dok kasno nije.

Come back!Let us be happy as before.In the morning, when you wake,tell me your dreams.Save our lovebefore it is too late.

Vrati se!U zagrljaj ponovo meni.Na mojim se grudima veseli,na njima sretna budi,na njima uživaji snivaj san sneni.

Come back!Into my arms again.Rejoice upon my breast,be happy upon it,take your delight there,and dream your drowsy dream.

Valpovo, 25. 11. 1974.

Valpovo, November 25, 1974
