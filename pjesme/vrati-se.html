Love poems

Come Back

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Vrati se!Pruži mi ponovo ruku.Obnovimo ljubav našui počnimo život novi.Zaboravi,za patnju, bol i tugu.

Come back!Hold out your hand to me again.Let us renew this love of oursand start a brand-new life.Forgetthe suffering, grief and pain.

Vrati se!Pruži mi usne slatke.Probudi srce moje.Pruži mi ljubav svoju.Za tebe, za mene,za dane kratke.

Come back!Give me your lips so sweet.Wake up this heart of mine.Give me that love of yours.For you, for me,for our days so fleet.

Vrati se!Budimo sretni k'o prije.Ujutro kada se budiš,pričaj mi svoje snove.Spasi ljubav našu,dok kasno nije.

Come back!Let us be happy as before.Each morning, when you wake,tell me what you dreamed.Save this love of oursbefore it is no more.

Vrati se!U zagrljaj ponovo meni.Na mojim se grudima veseli,na njima sretna budi,na njima uživaji snivaj san sneni.

Come back!Into my arms once more to stay.Upon my breast, my love, rejoice and rest,be happy lying there,take your delight there,and dream your drowsy dreams away.

Valpovo, 25. 11. 1974.

Valpovo, November 25, 1974
