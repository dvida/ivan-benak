Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Jadranka!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Jadranka!

Kad budete prije snova,u krevetu mekom bili.Iz te knjige pročitajtepjesmu, da bi ljepše snili.

When, before your dreams begin,in your soft bed you are laid,read a poem from this book,so that sweeter dreams are made.

Noćca će Vam ova dati,svu ljepotu noćnih snova,voljeni će slatko znati,sa usana cjelov brati.

This sweet night will bring to youall the beauty night dreams keep;your beloved, sweetly, too,from your lips a kiss will reap.

Eto, to su slatke noći,sa ljubavlju izvedene.A kad svane danak novi,pratit će Vas čari njene.

There, such are the sweetest nights,brought about with love, and so,when a new day dawns, its charmswill attend you where you go.

Valpovo, 28. 09. 2013.

Valpovo, September 28, 2013

Translator's note. Written for Jadranka Bardek in a copy of his book Odjeci srca (Echoes of the Heart).
