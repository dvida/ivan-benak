Draga Mila

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Mila

Da bi divne snove snila,a ujutro vedra bila,nek' ti mama svako veče:iz ZEKINE čita SREĆE!

So you'll dream the sweetest dreams,and wake bright when day is here,let your mother every nightread you Bunny's Luck, my dear!

Pa ćeš slatko ti usnutii predivne snove sniti,a ujutro kad se digneš,vesela ćeš, Mila, biti.

Then you'll sweetly fall asleep,dream such wondrous dreams, and then,in the morning, when you rise,Mila, you'll be glad again.

Nek' ti bude u životu,ostvarena želja svaka,to ti žele od sveg srca:djeda tvoj i tvoja baka!

May each wish you ever makein your life come true for you:this with all their hearts they wish,your grandpa and your grandma too!

Dvadeset i petog MARTA,dv'je tisuće trina'jste zime:MILI SPOTH u Stuttgart sada,pjesnik šalje svoje rime.

On the twenty-fifth of March,two thousand thirteen, winter-time,now to Mila Spoth in Stuttgarthere the poet sends his rhyme.

Sve što baka i djed želii Briefsteller isto veli!

All that Gran and Grandpa wish for you,the Briefsteller says it too!

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck) on behalf of Mila's grandparents. Briefsteller, German for "letter-writer", is the poet's joking name for himself.
