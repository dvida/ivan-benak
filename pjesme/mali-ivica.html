Poems for children

Little Ivica

from the books Little Dreams (1988) and The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Ivica je malijeo čokoladu,pa si je umrljaousta, nos i bradu.

Little Ivicaate some chocolate,and got his mouth and noseand chin in a state.

Samo da ga viditekakve mrlje nosi!Nešto čokoladenašlo se u kosi.

If you could only seethe smudges he wears!A bit of chocolateended up in his hair.

Pa mu sada mamanos i usta miva,njemu mrlja žaoi plače za njima!

So now his mum is washinghis nose and mouth for him,but he's sorry for the smudges,and he cries for them!

Translator's note. Ivica is the pet form of Ivan, like "Johnny".
