Protestne i društvene

Invalid

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

The Invalid

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Prođe neka žena,bolno se oslanja o štaku.Kao da meso para,štaka zacvili.Kroz kičmuprođu mi srsi.Ostaje u sjećanjutaj susret nemili.Netko nešto promrsi,da l' molbuili kletvu?Tko zna!Tek vidim,neke granate,krvavu žetvu.I vidim,tužnu prazninu,što mjesto noge zja.Osjetih,bolnu morunjenog sna.Oh, Bože!Uzdah se ote,nehotice.A suza mi klizneniz lice.

A woman passes by,leaning painfully on a crutch.As if tearing flesh,the crutch squeals.Down my spinea shiver runs.There stays in my memorythat grievous meeting.Someone mutters something,is it a pleaor a curse?Who knows!I only seesome shell'sbloody harvest.And I seethe sad emptinessgaping where a leg should be.I feltthe painful nightmareof her dream.Oh, God!A sigh escapes me,unbidden.And a tear slidesdown my face.

Valpovo, 1997.

Valpovo, 1997

Translator's note. The woman lost her leg to shelling in the Homeland War of 1991–95.
