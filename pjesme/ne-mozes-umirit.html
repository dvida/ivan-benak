Ne možeš umirit

Love poems

You Cannot Calm It

Umirit ne možeš uzburkano more,nit' ugasit lavu živoga vulkana.Ljubio bih tebe od mraka do zore.Poljupci su tvoji mome srcu hrana.

You cannot calm the sea when it is churning,nor quench a live volcano's lava, glowing red;I'd kiss you from the dusk till dawn's returning.Your kisses are the food on which my heart is fed.

Pa ni moje srce što za tebe kucai pritišće snažno moje vrele grudi,umirit ne možeš bez svoga poljupcai pogleda nježnog što mi snove budi.

Nor can you calm this heart of mine that beats for youand presses hard against my hot and burning breast;without your kiss you cannot bring it any ease,nor without the tender glance that wakes my dreams from rest.

Kada sretnem tebe, tvoje mile očii te slatke usne, koje meni nudiš.Ja ovako više živjet neću moći,jer u srcu mome žarku lavu budiš.

When I meet you and look into your darling eyes,and those sweet lips that you hold out for me to take,I cannot live like this much longer, I realise,for in my heart the burning lava you awake.

Po noći nas snivam zagrljene, sretne,k'o od istog debla da smo jedna grana.A kada se probudim, oči mi sjetne,jer te neću vidjet čitavoga dana.

At night I dream of us, embraced and blissful,as if we were one branch grown from a single tree;and when I wake, my eyes are sad and wistful,for all the livelong day your face I shall not see.

Valpovo, 20. 07. 1974.

Valpovo, July 20, 1974
