Ljubavne pjesme

Ne možeš umirit

Love poems

You Cannot Calm It

Umirit ne možeš uzburkano more,nit' ugasit lavu živoga vulkana.Ljubio bih tebe od mraka do zore.Poljupci su tvoji mome srcu hrana.

You cannot calm the sea when it is churning,nor quench the lava of a live volcano's fire.I'd kiss you from the dusk till dawn's returning.Your kisses are the food my heart requires.

Pa ni moje srce što za tebe kucai pritišće snažno moje vrele grudi,umirit ne možeš bez svoga poljupcai pogleda nježnog što mi snove budi.

Nor can you calm this heart that beats for youand presses hard against my burning breast,without your kiss, and one soft glance from you,the gentle glance that wakes my dreams from rest.

Kada sretnem tebe, tvoje mile očii te slatke usne, koje meni nudiš.Ja ovako više živjet neću moći,jer u srcu mome žarku lavu budiš.

When I meet you, and see your darling eyes,and those sweet lips you offer me to take,I can't live on like this, I realise,for burning lava in my heart you wake.

Po noći nas snivam zagrljene, sretne,k'o od istog debla da smo jedna grana.A kada se probudim, oči mi sjetne,jer te neću vidjet čitavoga dana.

At night I dream of us, embraced and blissful,as if we were one branch grown from one tree;and when I wake, my eyes are sad and wistful,for all day long your face I shall not see.

Valpovo, 20. 07. 1974.

Valpovo, July 20, 1974
