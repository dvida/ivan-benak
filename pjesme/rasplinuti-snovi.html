Love poems

Dreams Dissolved

Ljubljah ti usnetople i mekei obraze nježne,k'o ruža rumene.

I kissed your lips,so warm, so soft,and your tender cheeks,as rosy as a rose.

Ljubljah ti grudiod snijega bjeljei oči tvoježarke i snene.

I kissed your breasts,whiter than snow,and those eyes of yours,aflame and half-closed.

Rekoh ti noćassve skrite želje.

Tonight I told youall my secret longings.

Kad zora svanei sunce sine,te snove mojeu maglu raspline.

When dawn is breakingand the sun shines bright,it melts these dreams of mineto mist in morning light.

Valpovo, 1981.

Valpovo, 1981
