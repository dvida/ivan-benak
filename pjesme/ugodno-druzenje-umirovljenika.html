iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Pensioners' woes

A Pleasant Pensioners' Get-Together

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Sedmog srpnja dv'je tisuće sedme,sastali se da turnir održe,u Miholjcu, u predivnom gradui PENZIĆI da se malo druže.

On seventh July, two thousand and seven,they gathered for a tournament to play,in Miholjac, that town so wonderful,so oldies could have company that day.

Šest udruga skupilo se tada:iz Belišća, Valpova, Bizovca,Petr'jevaca, Satnice Miholjca,da si srcu pruže sportskog slada.

Six clubs had come together on that occasion:from Belišće, from Valpovo, Bizovac,from Petrijevci, from Satnica, Miholjac,to give their hearts a sporting celebration.

Bili su to sve vrsni igrači:u kuglanju, belotu, i šahu,a posebno žene u pikadu,rekord ruše svi u jednom dahu.

They all were players of the finest kindat bowling, at belote, and at chess,and in the darts the women most of all:they all broke records, all in one quick breath.

Ribići su pecali na Dravi,šah se igr'o potiho u sali,na kuglani meč je bio pravi,a kartaši sve su nadvikali.

The anglers fished away upon the Drava,the chess was quietly played inside the hall,the bowling alley saw a proper match,and card players out-shouted one and all.

I ženice svoj su obol dale,u veselju pikado igrale.Hotel BORIK domaćin je bio,sve igrače divno pogostio.

The ladies, too, made their contribution,and played at darts in merry competition.The Hotel Borik was the host, and therethe players all were treated to fine fare.

Po zasluzi svi su nagrađeni,pehar daje i čestita Mira,predsjednica Udruge Miholjca,a dvoranom tiho glazba svira.

By merit everyone received a prize;Mira hands out the cups and gives her praise,the president of the Miholjac club,and through the hall the music softly plays.

I na kraju ovoga turnira,igrači su za stolove sjeli.Dvoranom je nastala tišina,dok su u slast svi domjenak jeli.

And then, when this whole tournament was done,the players at the tables took their seat;a silence settled on the hall, while allwith gusto helped themselves to food to eat.

Ali ovo nije kraj toj priči,druženje je nastalo tek sada,još ne misle da će kući ići,jer ugodna atmosfera vlada.

But that was not the ending of the tale:the real get-together had just begun;of going home they did not think at all,for such good spirits reigned in everyone.

Veselo je, hvala dragom Bogu,zaigraše kolo, tango, polku,nek se vidi što PENZIĆI mogu,sutra ćemo misliti na boljku.

It's merry, thanks be to the Lord above;they danced the kolo, tango, polka too;let all now see what oldies still can do —tomorrow we'll give aches a thought or two.

Glazba svira, u srce me dira.Ej, ne svići skoro zlatna zoro.Bar da mi je svaki dan ovako,makar sutra ja sa kozom or'o.

The music plays, and how it moves my heart!Hey, golden dawn, don't break so soon, I pray!If only every day could be like this —though I should plough with a goat the next day!

Valpovo, 19.07.2007.

Valpovo, July 19, 2007

Translator's note. A pensioners' sports meet in Donji Miholjac in July 2007. "Ploughing with a goat" is a folk image of dire poverty: whatever tomorrow costs, tonight was worth it.
