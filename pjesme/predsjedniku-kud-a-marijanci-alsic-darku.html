Predsjedniku KUD-a Marijanci Alšić Darku

Occasional verse and dedications

To Darko Alšić, President of KUD Marijanci

POŠTOVANI GOSPODINE DARKO!

MY RESPECTED MISTER DARKO!

Neka igra kolo na Ćimenu,ta ljepota održat se mora,nagrada je to Vašem imenu,to veselje narodnog folklora.

On the Ćimen let the kolo spin,for such beauty must be kept alive;it's the prize your name has come to win,the gladness of our folk: long may it thrive!

Sve što bude Vaša ruka dalai budućnost reći će Vam HVALA!

For all your hand will give, and give anew,the future too will tell you: THANK YOU!

Poštovanom Darku Alšiću,Autor

To the esteemed Darko Alšić,the Author

Valpovo, 29. 05. 2012.

Valpovo, May 29, 2012

Translator's note. A KUD (kulturno-umjetničko društvo) is a village cultural and folklore society. The Ćimen is the village green of Marijanci, where its folklore festival is held.
