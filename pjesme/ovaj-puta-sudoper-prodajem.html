Radio burza

Ovaj puta sudoper prodajem

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

This Time I'm Selling a Sink

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ja sudoper od rostfraja imam,bez ormara, samo gornji dio,sa dv'je kade bez cijeđenja suda,a nekad je sa ormarom bio.

I've got a sink of stainless steel to sell,no cabinet, just the upper part alone,with basins two, but with no draining board —it used to have a cabinet of its own.

Kupio sam drugačiji sada,pa mi stari stoji u garaži.Preko burze sada oglas dajem,možda netko baš ovakav traži.

I've bought myself a different one instead,so in the garage now the old one stays.Through the Burza now I place my ad —perhaps someone wants one just like it these days.

Pedesetak eura on vrijedi,k'o brat bratu to morate znati,kako meni on više ne treba,za dvjesto ću kunića ga dati.

It's worth some fifty euros, give or take —as brother unto brother, you should know —but since I have no further need of it,for just two hundred kunas it will go.

Čekam poziv ja svakoga dana.Moj brzoglas već pripravan stoji:652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,baš toliko on znamenki broji.

I'm waiting for your call, day after day.My trusty phone stands ready, set to run:652 – eight hundred twenty-one,that's just how many digits it has, each one.

Valpovo, 16. 12. 2002.

Valpovo, December 16, 2002

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza. For the telephone he uses brzoglas ("swift-voice"), an old purist Croatian word, in jest.
