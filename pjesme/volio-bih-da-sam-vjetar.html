Love poems

I Wish I Were the Wind

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Volio bih da sam vjetar!Nježni lahor ja bih bio.Bijelo lice, medna usta,svojoj dragoj bih ljubio.

How I wish I were the wind!I would be a gentle breeze.My love's white face, honeyed mouth,I would kiss them as I please.

Volio bih da sam vjetar!Tražio bih skrite sjene.Voljenoj bih kose pleo,kose njene raspletene.

How I wish I were the wind!I would seek the hidden shade.I would plait my loved one's hair,all her loosened hair I'd braid.

Volio bih da sam vjetar!Pa da ljubim dragoj grudii milujem tijelo njeno,svako jutro kad se budi.

How I wish I were the wind!So I'd kiss my darling's breastand caress her body, too,as she rises from her rest.

Oh, da mogu biti vjetar!Koliko li za me sreće?Poljupcima prije snova,sklapao bih dragoj vjeđe.

Oh, if I could be the wind!Oh, the happiness I'd know!With my kisses, ere she dreams,I would close her eyelids so.

Valpovo, 25. 07. 1989.

Valpovo, July 25, 1989

Translator's note. Set to music by Marija Cavor and sung by the women's choir Perunike of Ivankovo.
