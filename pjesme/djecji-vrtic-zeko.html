Poems for children

Zeko Kindergarten

U Slatini jedandobar zeko ima,svu malenu djecurado k sebi prima.

In Slatina livesa bunny, good and kind;he gladly takes in allthe little ones he'll find.

Tako vam se zoveu Slatini vrtić,tu se djeca igrajuil' gledaju crtić.

That's the very nameof Slatina's nursery school;here the children playor watch cartoons, as a rule.

I taj dobri „ZEKO“,neka je na znanje,na hranu djecu primai na stanovanje.

And good old "ZEKO",let it be known, I say,takes children in for boardand lodging through the day.

Sve su tete drage,one na njih paze,sa njima se igraju,pomalo ih maze.

All the aunties are sweet,they watch them with great care,they play along with themand cuddle them here and there.

Kada se naigraju,onda slatko papaju,pa u krevet pođudok po njih ne dođu.

When they've played enough,they eat their yummy fare,then off to bed they gotill they're fetched from there.

Translator's note. Zeko ("Bunny") is a kindergarten in Slatina; the children's teachers are the tete, "aunties".
