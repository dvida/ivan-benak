A Face in the Empty Glass

Mjesto lica tvogaljubio sam čašu.Pio rujno vino,a ne tvoje usne.Misleći na tebei na ljubav našu,dok mi se na licutopla suza gusne.

Instead of your faceI kissed only glass;I drank the red wineand not your sweet lips,thinking of you onlyand our love, alas,while down my cheek slowlya warm teardrop slips.

Gledao sam čašudo dna ispijenu.U njoj meni dragotvoje lice milo.I na tome licutanku, tužnu sjenu.Bila je to java,ne, ja nisam snio.

I gazed at the glass,drained down to the last,and in it, so dear,your sweet face would gleam,and over that facea thin, sad shadow cast.I was wide awake:no, I did not dream.

I u tome časuviše nego ikad,želio sam tebevinuti na grudi.Ljubeći te vječnone ostavit' nikad.Zagrljene, sretne,da nas zora budi.

And in that moment,more than ever before,I longed to draw youclose against my breast,to kiss you foreverand leave you no more,so dawn would awake usembraced and so blest.

Valpovo, 14. 01. 1975.

Valpovo, January 14, 1975
