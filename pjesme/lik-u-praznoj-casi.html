A Face in the Empty Glass

Mjesto lica tvogaljubio sam čašu.Pio rujno vino,a ne tvoje usne.Misleći na tebei na ljubav našu,dok mi se na licutopla suza gusne.

In place of your faceI kissed the glass;I drank the red wineand not your lips,thinking of youand our love, alas,while down my facea warm teardrop slips.

Gledao sam čašudo dna ispijenu.U njoj meni dragotvoje lice milo.I na tome licutanku, tužnu sjenu.Bila je to java,ne, ja nisam snio.

I gazed at the glass,drained down to the last;and in it, so dear,your sweet face was seen,and over that facea thin, sad shade cast.It was real, awake —it was not a dream.

I u tome časuviše nego ikad,želio sam tebevinuti na grudi.Ljubeći te vječnone ostavit' nikad.Zagrljene, sretne,da nas zora budi.

And in that moment,more than ever before,I longed to draw youclose to my breast,to kiss you foreverand leave you no more,so that dawn would wake usembraced and so blest.

Valpovo, 14. 01. 1975.

Valpovo, January 14, 1975
