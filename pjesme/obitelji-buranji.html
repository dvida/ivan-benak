Obitelji Buranji

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Buranji Family

Nek Vam ova knjiga moja,pruži ugodno veselje,stvarajući ovu knjigu,to su bile moje želje.

May this book of mine for youbring a pleasant merriment;as I made it, that was justwhat I wished for, what I meant.

Kao ovaj zeko maliimali baš uvijek sreće.Po životnom Vašem putu,cvjetalo Vam divno cvijeće.

Like this little bunny here,may you have good luck each day;and along your path of lifemay sweet flowers bloom all the way.

Obitelji BuranjiMariji i Marku,ugodno veselje,bez tuge i brige,iskreno im želi:autor ove knjige!

To the Buranji family,to Marija and Marko,a pleasant, merry time,with no sorrow, no care,sincerely wishesthe author of this book!

Valpovo, 23. 12. 2013.

Valpovo, December 23, 2013

Translator's note. The Buranjis run the Ivanovi dvori restaurant in Ivankovo. The "little bunny" is the hero of his children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck).
