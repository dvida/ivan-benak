Occasional verse and dedications

The Television Show "Država i Grad i Selo"

Jednog dana prođe dnevnik,na TV-u to je bilo.U Sopjanskoj nošnji ljudi,meni oko srca milo.

One day, when the news was through,there it was upon TV:people dressed in Sopje costume;how my heart warmed, just to see!

A razlog je tomu bio:jer tu divnu emisijuvodio je Škoro smjelo.

And the reason for it wasthat this lovely programme, see,Škoro hosted, confidently.

Granjaša sam toga časa,na TV-u ugledao,želja mi je tada bilada bi Sopje opisao.

At that moment I saw Granjašon the TV; there he stood,and my wish right then becameto describe Sopje, if I could.

I, zato sam sa zamolbomNačelnika zamolioza podatke koje trebamda bi pjesmu napisao.

And so, with this plea, I've cometo request the mayor's aidfor the facts that I requireso the poem can be made.

Valpovo, 20. 11. 2010.

Valpovo, November 20, 2010

Translator's note. The show's name means "The State, the Town and the Village". Josip Granjaš was the head of Sopje municipality; the poet asks him for facts so he can write a poem about Sopje.
