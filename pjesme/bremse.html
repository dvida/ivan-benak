Bremse

Occasional verse and dedications

Bremse

Kao pjesnik mnogo ljeta pišem.Izdao sam nekoliko knjiga,došla mi je predivna ideja,nemam para, a to mi je briga.

A poet, I've been writing many a year,and several books of mine are out, you see;a lovely notion came into my head —no money, though, and that's what worries me.

Ja posjetih trgovinu "BREMSE",gospon' Slavko na kočnicu stao.Rijetko naći tu ljudsku vrlinu,bez problema, donaciju dao.

I paid a visit to the Bremse shop,and Mr Slavko put his foot on the brake;such human kindness is a rarity:and, with no fuss, he gave for my book's sake.

Gospon' Slavko, kako da zahvalim?Riječ je mala, da Vam kažem hvala.To jedino što ja mogu znati,uz zahvalu Vama knjigu dati.

Mr Slavko, how can I thank you now?"Thank you" is but a little word to say.The only thing that I know how to dois, with my thanks, to give a book to you.

Što bi moja duša sada znala,gospon' Slavko, od srca Vam hvala.

What more is there my soul could know to do?Mr Slavko, my heartfelt thanks to you!

Valpovo, 16. 06. 2010.

Valpovo, June 16, 2010

Translator's note. Slavko Oršolić's shop is called Bremse, German for "brake", so the poet jokes that its owner "put his foot on the brake": he stopped to help.
