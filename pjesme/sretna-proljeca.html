Happy Springtimes

Jednog dana ozari me sunce,ozari me i sunce i sreća.Poljupcima koje si mi dala,sretna su mi svanula proljeća.

One day I was lit up by the sun,lit up by the sun and by delight;with the kisses that you gave to me,happy springtimes dawned for me, so bright.

Ne znam gdje si do sad bila, draga.Da l' pod snijegom k'o ljubica mala?Zbog ljepote te čarobne noći,ti si meni svoju ljubav dala.

I don't know where you have been, my dear —under snow, a violet, could it be?For the beauty of that magic nightyou have given all your love to me.

Poklanjam ti moje srce sada,svojom dragom ja te želim zvati.Za vjenčanje neka ti je nada,pred oltarom prsten ću ti dati!

Now I give my heart to you, my dear,and I want you as my sweetheart true.Let your hope be for a wedding near:at the altar I'll give a ring to you!

Neka bude tako dovijeka,u zagrljaj ja te rado želim.Srce moje tebe željno čekai dolasku tvome se veselim.

May it be like this for evermore:in my arms I'd gladly hold you tight.And my heart awaits you, longing sore,and your coming fills me with delight.

Sad mi pružaš radosti proljeća,pogledima griješ moje grudi,poljupcima razigravaš srce,što se za te svako jutro budi.

Now you give me all the joys of spring,with your glances warm my breast anew,with your kisses set my heart to dance,which each morning wakes again for you.

Valpovo, 20. 08. 1986.

Valpovo, August 20, 1986
