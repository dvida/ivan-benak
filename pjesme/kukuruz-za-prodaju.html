Radio burza

Kukuruz za prodaju

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Maize for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

I do sad mi pomagala BURZA,za prodaju imam kukuruza.Osam tona stoje mi od lani,u vrećama dobro spakovani.Lijep kukuruz, ko dukat se žuti,od njeg' stoka neće da poludi.Njegova je kvaliteta snažna,on je dobar bez koštanog brašna.U zrnu ga stoka jako voli,šrotovanog jedu ga bez soli.Samljevenog sa dodatkom trave,duplo mlijeka dati će vam krave.Pilićima izvrsna je hrana,pet kila su za šezdeset dana.Taj kukuruz kad koke kljucaju,po dva jaja one dnevno daju.A praščići kada bi ga jeli,kao lopte bili bi debeli.Pa i guska kad bi ga probala,svoje perce za njega bi dala.Da je dobar, morate priznati,ovaj puta, jeftino ću dati.Kukuruz je u zrnu, bez klipa,kilu dajem samo za sto lipa.I još nešto ovdje valja znati,za istu ću cijenu šrotovati.Molim kupce ozbiljne i prave,neka mi se na telefon jave:6 5 2 osamsto dvadeset jedan,robu nudim cijeli ovaj tjedan.

Till now the Burza's always helped me through;I've maize for sale, and here it is for you.Eight tonnes of it I've had since last year's crop,all packed in sacks, and well packed, to the top.Fine maize, as yellow as a ducat, bright;your stock won't go mad from it — that's right.Its quality is sturdy, strong, and whole;it's good without bone meal — upon my soul.The stock adore it whole, in grain, and how!Ground up, they'll eat it without salt, I vow.Mixed with some grass, once it's been milled right through,your cows will give twice as much milk for you.For chicks it's excellent, the finest feed:in sixty days, five kilos each — indeed!When hens peck at this maize, you'll find they laytwo eggs apiece, each one of them, a day.And piglets, if they ate it, I'd be bound,would all grow fat as balls, and just as round.And if a goose should taste it, then, I swear,she'd give her feathers for it, then and there.That it is good, you'll have to own it's true;and this time I will sell it cheap to you.It's all in grain, there's not a cob in sight;a hundred lipa buys a kilo — right!And one more thing that's worth your knowing, too:I'll grind it at the same price, just for you.I ask for buyers serious and fairto ring me on the telephone, right there:6 5 2 eight hundred twenty-one;I'm offering the goods all week, till it's done.

Valpovo, 15. 08. 2001.

Valpovo, August 15, 2001

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A verse ad for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. It was written during the mad-cow (BSE) scare of 2000–01, when bone meal in cattle feed was blamed for the disease. A lipa is a hundredth of a kuna, so a hundred lipa is one kuna.
