Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Marici iz Čađavice

Occasional verse and dedications

To Marica from Čađavica

U toplini ovog ljeta,poštom došla mi koverta,koverta iz Čađavice,u njoj pjesma od Marice.Na toj pjesmi puno hvalakoju ste mi Vi poslala!Divna mala pjesmičica,ustvari je zahvalnica.Priroda Vam sreće dala,pjesnika ste upoznala,a srce Vam kuca živo;pred kapijom Benak Ivo.O svemu smo mi pričalii lično se upoznali,kavicu smo pijuckali,o pjesmama raspravljali.Pišimo što srce želi,nek' književnost svoje veli.Neke pjesme sam čitao,i kritiku za njih dao,pa neka nam bude želja,da pišemo iz veselja.

In this summer's warmth, one day,post brought an envelope my way,envelope from Čađavica,with a poem by Marica.For the poem you sent me here,many thanks, and most sincere!Such a lovely little poem —in fact, a thank-you note you wrote.Nature gave you luck, you see:you have met a poet — me!And your heart is beating lively:at the gate stands Benak Ivo.We talked over everything,met each other face to face,sipped our little cups of coffee,talked of poems in that place.Let us write what hearts would say,let literature have its way.Several poems I read through,and I gave my critique too;so let's make this our intent:that we write from merriment.

Upozno' sam divnu ženu,veselu i skromnost njenu.Lijepa kuća usred selai obitelj tu joj cijela.Pa zar može biti veće,u životu ljepše sreće?Kućica je uređena,okolina isto njena,a dvorište divno, bajno,dotjerano, čisto, sjajno.Pa unučad, divna djeca,a bobika čuva zeca,igraju se u živici,k'o da nisu protivnici.U tom raju sve joj štimai ljepote ona ima.Zadovoljni mi smo biliuto smo se i rastali.

I have met a lovely woman,cheerful, modest as can be;a fine house in the village centre,there with all her family.Could there be a greater thing,lovelier joy that life can bring?Her small house is well arranged,and her grounds are just as fine;and the yard's a wonder, magic,trim and clean, and all a-shine.And the grandchildren, lovely children,Bobika, who guards the bunny,in the hedge they play together,just as if they were not foes.In that paradise, all's well,and has beauty, truth to tell.We were happy, satisfied,and just then we said goodbye.

Bilo mi je vrlo drago,blaženo mi bilo lice,upoznati ovo blago:Marice iz Čađavice!

I was glad as I could be,and my face shone, blissfully,to have come to know this treasure:Marica from Čađavica!

Valpovo, 02. 07. 2013.

Valpovo, July 2, 2013

Translator's note. Marica, a fellow poet from the village of Čađavica, had sent him a poem of thanks; this is his reply and his account of visiting her.
