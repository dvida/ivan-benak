Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovana Blaženka!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Blaženka!

U druženju našem časnom,neke sam Ti knjige dao.Koliko je mojih bilo?Vjeruj, Blažo, nisam znao!

In our friendship, good and true,I have given books to you.How many of mine were there?Trust me, Blažo, I'd no clue!

Slučajno sam ovih dana,u arhivu pogledao.Knjigu: "ŽABLJI SASTANAK" sam,Tebi, Blažo, darovao.

By chance, in these last few days,I have looked my archive through:"The Frogs' Meeting" was the bookthat, dear Blažo, went to you.

Kad pogledam malo bolje:neka mi ljepota sine.Knjiga meni poklonjena:"NA LJUBIČASTOM PLASTU MJESEČINE".

When I looked a little closer,something lovely came to light:and the book you gifted me:"On the Violet Haystack of Moonlight".

Poznamo se već odavno.Poštujem Te, trebaš znati.Zato moju šestu knjigu,rado ću Ti darovati.

We have known each other long.I respect you, know it's true;that is why my sixth book, now,I will gladly give to you.

Valpovo, 31. 05. 2014.

Valpovo, May 31, 2014

Translator's note. Written in a copy of Stihom kroz život, his sixth book, for Blaženka Maglaić of Valenovac, who had given him her own book of poems.
