Luka Jokić and His Works

Ljubavne pjesme pisao Luka,tu vrlinu, Muze su mu dale.Od djetinjstva bila vrijedna ruka,izdat će knjigu, one su znale.

Our Luka wrote his poems about love;the Muses gave that virtue to his art.His hand was hard at work since he was young;they knew he'd bring a book out, from the start.

Ljubav mu je okitila snove,pero vješto držala mu ruka."SVE JE TO ŽIVOT", knjiga se zove.Prvu knjigu iznjedrio Luka.

Love came and decorated all his dreams;his hand could hold the pen with skill, and write.“It's All Life” — that is what the book is called:the first book Luka brought into the light.

Prva mu je knjiga snage dala,probudila Luki divne snove,obitelj se isto radovala."SUNČANI OSMJEH", druga se zove.

His first book gave him strength, and it awokein Luka dreams most wonderful to see;his family rejoiced as well, and then“A Sunny Smile” the second came to be.

Skupili se ljudi, čestitali,promocija bila, radost cijela.Prođu dva ljeta, nismo ni znali,procvale "RUŽE TREĆEG ANĐELA".

The people gathered, wished him joy, and thenthere was a launch, a joy complete and whole;two summers passed before we knew it, whenthe “Roses of the Third Angel” came to bloom.

Neumorno Luka dalje piše.Život prođe k'o da je na slici.Iz Đurdana veseli glas stiže,nastali su „SREBRNI VIDICI“.

Unwearied, Luka goes on with his writing;life passes by, as in a picture framed.From Đurdan comes a joyful voice to tell us:“Silver Horizons” — so the new book's named.

Piši, Luka, dok si dobre voljei razigraj te pjesničke snove.Pa zar može nešto biti bolje?"PROPLAMSAJ SRCA", peta se zove.

Write, Luka, while your spirits still are high,and set your poet's dreams to dance and play!Now could there be a better thing than this?“A Glimmer of the Heart”, the fifth, I say.

Nakon snova, kad zora zarudi,kada prođu ove divne noći.Uspomena kad razigra grudi:"STOPAMA VJETRA", knjiga će doći.

When dreams are done, and dawn begins to glow,when these most wondrous nights have all gone by,when memories set hearts dancing, there will comethe book, “In the Wind's Footsteps”, by and by.

"SOFIJA OZLOJAĐENA ŽENA".Preporuku za nju dajem svima,sudbina je opisana njena,u toj knjizi divnih stvari ima.

“Sofija, an Embittered Woman” — well,I recommend it warmly to you all;her destiny is told within its pages,and lovely things are in it, great and small.

Osma knjiga, to su: "KRATKE PRIČE",sami naslov svoj sadržaj kaže.Kad ju čitaš, divno radost klice,sve se divno u toj knjizi slaže.

The eighth book is the one called “Short Stories”;the title by itself tells what's inside;as you read on, joy sprouts up wondrously,and all that's in the book fits side by side.

I dođosmo opet do romana:„U POTRAZI“, to je knjiga znana.Deveta je, od Luke izdana,sa ljepotom divno ispričana.

And once again we've come to a novel:“In Search”, a book that's widely known and read;it is the ninth that Luka has brought out,and beautifully told, it must be said.

Obišli smo, Lukin knjižni svijet,deseta je knjiga napisana,to je „PRAŠINA I PONEKI CVIJET“.Ta ljepota od Boga mu dana.

We've toured through Luka's world of books, and see,the tenth one has been written, it is here:it's “Dust and the Occasional Flower”.That beauty was God's gift to him, that's clear.

Ja se nadam, prijatelju mili,da desetom knjigom nema kraja.Oduvijek smo o ljepoti snili,jedanaesta donijet će ti Raja.

I hope, my friend, my dear and cherished friend,that with the tenth book there will be no end;we've always dreamed of beauty, you and I:the eleventh book will bring you Paradise.

Valpovo, 11. 06. 2014.

Valpovo, June 11, 2014

Translator's note. A tribute to his fellow writer Luka Jokić, naming his first ten books. The titles are given in English here; the Croatian originals are in the facing text.
