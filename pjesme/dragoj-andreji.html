Obiteljski kutak

Dragoj Andreji!

Family corner

To Dear Andreja!

Ovu knjigu ja Andreji dajem,unučici koju volim jako,nek' joj knjiga bude uspomena,jer to moje srce želi tako.

This book I give to Andreja, my deargranddaughter, whom I love in every way;may this book be a keepsake for her here,because my heart would have it just that way.

Ova knjiga bit će joj milina,jer u njoj je i njezino ime.Za proslavu bilo koju njenu,često sam joj ja pisao rime.

This book will be a joy to her, for therewithin its pages she will find her name;I often wrote her verses, it is true,for every celebration as it came.

A rođendan kad je njezin bio,uz kolače i kavu smo pili,svirka nam je svu ljepotu dala,svaki puta veseli smo bili.

And when it was her birthday, we would eatour cakes and drink our coffee, all as one;the music gave us all its loveliness,and every time we all had merry fun.

Prohujalo djetinjstvo i mladost.Udala se, sretna je uz muža,u naručju svoje čedo ljubi,sad joj život cvjeta kao ruža.

Her childhood and her youth have rushed away.She married, and is happy with her spouse;she kisses her dear baby in her arms,and now her life is blooming like a rose.

S najdražima divne snove sniva,pratila ju sreća i veselje.Nek' joj život u ljepoti cvijeta:to su bake i djedine želje.

With her dear ones she dreams her lovely dreams;may happiness go with her, and good cheer.May her life blossom in its loveliness:so wish her grandma and her grandpa dear.

Ovo pišem jedanaestog dana,u ožujku kad stiže proljeće,dv'je tisuće i trinaestog ljeta:nek' joj život cvjeta kao cvijeće!

I'm writing this on the eleventh dayof March, the month when spring is on its way,in the year two thousand and thirteen:may her life bloom as flowers bloom, I pray!

Valpovo, 11. 03. 2013.

Valpovo, March 11, 2013

Translator's note. Written in a copy of one of his books for his granddaughter.
