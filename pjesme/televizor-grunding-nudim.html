Radio burza

Televizor „Grunding“ nudim

Radio market

Grunding TV on Offer

Televizor prodajem u boji,GRUNDING marke, čuveno je ime.Još antenu kupite za njega,pa da lijepo uživate s njime.

I'm selling a colour television set,a GRUNDING make — a famous name, you know.Just buy an aerial for it as well,and you'll enjoy it, watching every show.

Televizor radi kao novi.Pedeset pet je ekranu širina.Sve jezike ovog svijeta znade,mili, Bože, gledat ga milina.

The television works as good as new,its screen is fifty-five in width, no less.It knows each language of the world, and oh,dear God, to watch it is pure happiness.

Lijep je ekran, još ljepša mu slika,tko ga kupi, to sve mora znati.Televizor ovaj u koloru,za pedeset eura ja ću ga dati.

Its screen is fine, its picture finer still —whoever buys it has to know all that.This television, in full colour, too,I'll let you have for fifty euros flat.

Na burzi me već odavno poznaju,trgovac sam preko burze stari.Eto, ljudi, toliko da znate,ja prodajem uvijek dobre stvari.

On Radio burza I've been known for years,a trader there, an old one, through and through.So there, good people, that's for you to know:the things I sell are always good, it's true.

Pa i ovaj televizor fini,za tu cijenu, koja puno znači.Na 6 5 2, osamsto dvadeset jedan,na tome broju možeš ga naći.

And so this fine TV, too, can be hadat such a price — and that means quite a lot.At 6 5 2, eight hundred twenty-one,that is the number where it can be got.

Valpovo, 30. 01. 2007.

Valpovo, January 30, 2007

Translator's note. A verse ad for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio, where he was a regular. "Grunding" is his spelling of Grundig; the phone number was his own.
