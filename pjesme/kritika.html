Criticism

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Jednog dana mi reče,kritičar strog i ljut,da pjesme ne pišem više,da pero bacim u kut.

One day, with an angry look,a stern critic said to meto write no poems any moreand fling my pen in a nook.

Meni kad želje se jave,k'o jesenje guste kiše,u ruci pero se nađei samo pjesme piše.

But when my longings gatherlike autumn's heavy rain,a pen turns up in my handand writes poems once again.

Valpovo, 1982.

Valpovo, 1982
