Domoljubne i zavičajne

Napredno je mjesto Čađavica

Homeland and native region

Čađavica Is a Thriving Place

U ljepoti naše Slavonijetu vijuga cesta od davnine,povezuje sela i gradove:MAGISTRALA to je Podravine.

Amid the beauty of our Slavoniaa road has wound its way since days of old,connecting all the villages and towns:the Podravina Highway, so we're told.

Na tom dijelu naše Panonije,pružila se Podravska ravnica.U ljepoti na domaku Drave,smjestilo se mjesto Čađavica.

Upon this stretch of our Pannonian landthe Podravina plain spreads far and wide.In beauty, within reach of the Drava,there Čađavica settled to abide.

Spominje se u šestom stoljeću,na tom mjestu ZAGOCHA se znala.Povijest kaže u stoljeću sedmom:ZAGOCHA se ČAĐAVICOM zvala.

It's mentioned back in the sixth century:upon this spot, Zagocha was well known.And in the seventh, history reports,Zagocha took Čađavica's name as its own.

Tisuću tristo trideset četvrte,BOGOMOLJU ljudi sagradili.Svetom Petru dičnog apostola:oltar crkve časno su nazvali!

In thirteen hundred thirty-four, the peoplebuilt up a house of prayer, and with due grace,unto Saint Peter, glorious apostle,they named the altar of that holy place!

Dva stoljeća već postoji škola,na tisuće đaka izučila,a Davorin Trstenjak se zove:mnogo mudrih glava iznjedrila!

The school has stood two centuries by now,and thousands of its pupils it has taught;Davorin Trstenjak is what it's called:how many clever heads to light it's brought!

Od davnina općina postoji,deset sela sada joj pripada,strukturu je infra izgradila,tu napredna politika vlada.

The municipality has long been here,ten villages today are in its hand;it has built up its infrastructure here,and here progressive policy rules the land.

Još i poštu Čađavica ima,čak i banku gdje se novac prima.Tu je doktor pa i apoteka,za svu boljku tu se nađe lijeka.

And Čađavica has its own post office, too,and even a bank, where money's paid in — true!There is a doctor here, and a pharmacy:for every ailment there's a remedy.

Već je prošlo mnogo desetljeća,Čađavicom Ćiro prolazio,od Slatine do grada Osijeka,putnike je mnoge prevozio.

Now many decades have gone by since then,when Ćiro passed through Čađavica here;from Slatina right through to Osijekit carried many passengers each year.

Tu je tada i stanica bila,putnici su karte kupovali.Stanica je tu ostala ista,samo su je birtijom prozvali!

A station stood here, too, back in those days;the passengers would buy their tickets there.The station still remains, the very same —except that now it's called "the tavern" there!

Nekada se tu i žito mljelo,i pilana kod Lazičke bila.Svega bilo u tadašnje vrijeme,a krčma je Čarugu častila.

Once, here, the grain was milled in days gone by,and by Lazička a sawmill could be found.There was a bit of everything back then,and the inn would stand Čaruga a round.

Sada ima trgovine dvijei semafor Čađavicu krasi,ona ima benzinsku crpku,da se motor vozila ne gasi.

Now there are two shops here, and what is more,a traffic light adorns Čađavica, too;it has a petrol pump now of its own,so no car's engine sputters out on you.

Petrovo je kirvaj Čađavice.Dan općine toga dana traje.Dolazim u goste kod Marice,alaj volim stare običaje!

St Peter's Day is Čađavica's fair,and Municipal Day is that same day, too.I come to Marica's to be her guest —how I love the old customs, through and through!

KUD „Podravina“: folklor-tamburaši,kad nastupe čuje se veselo.Svu ljepotu Čađavici daju:napredno je to Šokačko selo!

KUD "Podravina": dancers, tambura players —when they perform, how merrily they play!They give Čađavica all her loveliness:a thriving Šokac village, I must say!

Valpovo, 29. 03. 2014.

Valpovo, March 29, 2014

Translator's note. Ćiro is the affectionate Croatian nickname for the old local steam train; Čaruga (Jovo Stanisavljević, 1897–1925) was a notorious Slavonian outlaw. St Peter's Day is the village kirvaj, the church fair held on the parish saint's feast. A KUD is a village folk-arts society.
