Hello, Little One

Rekoh ti zdravo, malenaprvi put kad smo se sreli.Tvoj osmijeh sve mi rečei oka pogled vreli.

"Hello, little one," I said,when we first met, you and I;your smile told me everything,and the burning glance of your eye.

Uz poljupce slatke tvoje,u tvoj sam se zagrljaj svio,a vrijeme je brzo prošlo,kanda ga vjetar odnio.

With your sweet kisses, darling,in your arms I curled and lay,and the time went by so quickly,as if the wind had blown it away.

Na usnama šapat lebdi:Dođi mi, ja te molim.U tvoj ću zagrljaj doći,jer ja te, malena, volim!

On the lips a whisper hovers:come to me, I beg you, do.Into your embrace I'll come,for, my little one, I love you!

Valpovo, 15. 08. 1982.

Valpovo, August 15, 1982
