Obiteljski kutak

Dobro došli prijatelji dragi!

Family corner

Welcome, My Dear Friends!

Ja sam rođen u blizini Drave,u dolini polja i livada,okoline Podravske Slatine:Šokica me porodila mlada.

Near the Drava river I was born,down in a vale of fields and meadows wide,in the country round Podravska Slatina:a young Šokica mother gave me life.

To je bilo trideset i treće,u mjesecu kad se žito želo,dvajs'devetog, a bio je petak,pa sam svakoj radosti početak.

It was the year of nineteen thirty-three,the month when all the wheat was being reaped,the twenty-ninth, and Friday was the day,so I'm the start of every joy, I'd say.

Mnogo ljeta iza mene stojina plećima: sedamdeset devet.Koliko ću? Dragi Bogo znade,sve je dobro dok ne nađem krevet.

Many a summer lies behind me now,upon my shoulders: seventy and nine.How many more? Dear God alone knows how;as long as I'm not bedridden, all is fine.

Svima vama, dragi moji hvalašto ste danas posjetili mene,nema puno, vrijeme mi je kratko,da se sklope moje oči snene.

To all of you, my dear ones, thanks I give,that you have come to visit me today;not much is left, the time I have is brief,before my drowsy eyes are closed to stay.

Kad se sjetim svojega djetinjstva,bolnih dana u životu momei mladosti da stvorim budućnost,robovanja u životu svome.

When I remember childhood, long ago,the painful days I went through in my life,my youth, to build a future, toiling so,my slaving years of labour and of strife.

U Belišću pedeset i prve,dva pupoljka bila su na radu.Jednog dana tu se upoznali,jedno drugom svoje srce dali.

In Belišće, in nineteen fifty-one,two buds were working there, side by side.And there they met one day, and from the starteach one gave to the other one their heart.

Godine su prolazile burno.Djeca su nam kuću popunila.Za budućnost mi smo snove snili,uz svu patnju i sretni smo bili.

The years went by, and they were turbulent.Our children came along and filled the house.We dreamed our dreams of what the years would bring,and we were happy, too, through suffering.

Da bi sretni u starosti bili,obiteljski dom smo sagradili.Mirovinu svoju uživamo,sreću našu nikome ne damo!

So in our old age happy we might be,we built our home, a home for family.We now enjoy our pension, as we may,and no one takes our happiness away!

Recite mi, ima li što ljepše:kada svane osamdeset ljeta,kad se skupi i rodbina naša,prijatelji, da nam sreća cvjeta.

Now tell me, is there anything more sweet:when one's eightieth summer dawns, and thenour relatives and friends all come to meet,so that our happiness may bloom again?

Draga moja djeco i rodbino:još jedanput ja Vam velim hvalašto ste mene danas posjetili,skupa sa mnom i vi sretni bili!

My dearest children, and my kinfolk too,once more I say it: thank you, all of you,for coming here to visit me today;be happy, all of you, with me, I pray!

Valpovo, 27. 07. 2012.

Valpovo, July 27, 2012

Translator's note. His toast at the gathering for his 79th birthday. Podravska Slatina is a town near the Drava; he met his wife while both were working in Belišće.
