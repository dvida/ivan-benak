Poštovana Cecilija

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Cecilija

Topli pozdrav vama želi sada:pjesnik Ivo iz Valpova grada.Svu ljepotu Đondiru sam dao,nemam Đondir, pa mi jako žao.I Color sam napisao Vama.Žao mi je što stojite sama.Da je Đondir u Coloru javnoi svirači da sviraju slavnoi telefon da zvoni k'o sada,pune ruke imali bi radai svi kupci bili bi sa Vama,vjerujte mi, ne bi bila sama.Eto, to je što Vam Ivo veli:sve najbolje od srca Vam želi!

Warm greetings he now wishes you, you see:the poet Ivo, from Valpovo town (that's me).To Đondir I gave all the beauty that I had;now I've no Đondir, and it makes me very sad.And Color I have written, too, for you;I'm sorry that you stand alone, I do.If Đondir came to Color, for all to see,with players playing there most gloriously,and if the phone kept ringing as it does today,your hands would be so full of work, I'd say;and all the customers would be with you:believe me, you'd not be alone, it's true.Well, that is all that Ivo says, for his part:he wishes you the very best, with all his heart!

Valpovo, 15. 06. 2013.

Valpovo, June 15, 2013

Translator's note. Đondir and Color appear to be local businesses for which he had written verses; the exact story is not known.
