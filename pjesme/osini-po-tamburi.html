Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Ošini po tamburi

Occasional verse and dedications

Strike Up the Tambura!

Sad ošini po tamburi!Gledatelj bih rado bio,svakog petka na TV-u,emisiju tu bih htio.

Now go strike up the tambura!I'd be glad to watch it too,every Friday on the TV:that's the show I'd love to view.

To što vode divne cureSTV-a to je dio.Josipu i Doris sada,ja bih rado pozdravio!

Hosted by two lovely girls,it's a part of STV.Josipa and Doris, both,I would greet most happily!

Emisija ta uživo,neka petkom ide dalje.Iz Valpova Benak Ivoi režiji pozdrav šalje.

Friday after Friday, live,may this show go on and on;Benak Ivo from Valpovogreets the production team, each one.

Sad ošini po tamburi!Tu se svatko javit smije,pozdrav dati prijatelju:nema ljepše emisije.

Now go strike up the tambura!Anyone may call in here,send a greeting to a friend:there's no lovelier show, that's clear!

Kad sviraju tamburaši,divnu glazbu slušam i ja,to veselje Šokadije,čuje cijela Slavonija.

When the tambura players play,I too hear the lovely sound;Šokadija's merrimentis heard all Slavonia round.

Sad ošini po tamburi!Tu se živi u veselju,a bendovi tamburašaispunjaju svaku želju.

Now go strike up the tambura!Here one lives in merriment,and the tambura bands play on,granting every wish that's sent.

Da dosadan ne bi bio,ovaj put sam dosta dao,za drugi put ja bih opetnešto lijepo napisao.

So I won't become a bore,this time I've given quite a lot;next time I would write once moresomething lovely, like as not.

Da bi znali gdje stanujem,nek' vam kaže pjesma ova.Podatke vam svoje daje:Ivan Benak iz Valpova.

So you'd know where I reside,let this poem tell you so;here it gives you my details:Ivan Benak from Valpovo.

Kada bude CD bio,adresu mi sada znate.Da bi došao do mene,na poštu ga vi predajte.

When the CD comes about,now you know my address, too;so that it can get to me,hand it to the post, will you?

Svakog petka uz tambure,ugodno je kod vas, živo.Topli pozdrav iz Valpova:vama šalje Benak Ivo!

Every Friday, with tamburas,it's so lively, nice with you;warmest greetings from ValpovoBenak Ivo sends you, too!

Valpovo, 12.11.2014.

Valpovo, November 12, 2014

Translator's note. "Ošini po tamburi" (roughly "give the tambura a good strum") was a live Friday tamburica show on STV, Slavonian Television, in Osijek.
