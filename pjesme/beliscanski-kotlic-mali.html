Domoljubne i zavičajne

Belišćanski kotlić mali

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

The Little Cauldron of Belišće

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Belišće je divan gradićsmjestio se pokraj Drave,industrijsko to je mjesto,proizvode razne prave.

Belišće's a lovely town,nestled by the Drava's flow;it's a place of industry,making all sorts, as you know.

A radnička klasa, zna se,oduvijek je složna bila,pogotovo kad bi kakvazabava se dogodila.

And the working class, of course,always stood as one, it's true —all the more when some occasionfor a party came in view.

Tisuću i nešto ljudida bi fešta nahranila,napraviti takav kotlićvruća im je želja bila.

So a fête could feed the people,some one thousand, give or take,it became their burning wish:such a cauldron they must make!

Ideju je Delač dao,Borić sredstva odobrio,a pogon je za popravketakav kotlić izradio.

Delač offered the idea,Borić saw the funding through,and the works' repair departmentbuilt a cauldron, all brand-new.

Dimenzije kotlića sukakve nećeš naći često:Metar visok, metar širok,a litara ima šesto!

And the cauldron's measurementsyou won't meet with every day:metre high and metre wide,and six hundred litres — hey!

Kako bi ga iznad vatresa varivom postavili,od pet coli jakih cijevinogare su napravili.

So that they could set it, fullof its stew, above the fire,out of sturdy five-inch pipingthey built legs to hold it higher.

I taj ponos belišćanskišto litara ima šesto,paprikaše, grah, čobanacSlavonijom kuha često.

And this pride of all Belišće,all six hundred litres, too,often cooks across Slavoniapaprikaš, čobanac, bean stew.

A kuhar je Josip Delačšto čobanac kuha vješto,tek onda je prava slogakada kotlić skuha nešto.

And the cook is Josip Delač,who makes čobanac with skill;only then is true accord,when the cauldron cooks its fill.

Iz kotlića tog čuvenogdobar grah je s nama jeona Dan grada u Belišćui predsjednik Mesić Stevo.

From that cauldron of renown,on Belišće's own Town Day,President Mesić, Stevo, too,ate good beans with us, I say!

Poželimo tom kotlićuda mu grana mira cvjeta,nek' čobanac dobar kuhai nahrani pola svijeta!

Let us wish the cauldron well:may its branch of peace bloom forth;may it cook a good čobanac,feeding half the world on earth!

Valpovo, 04. 06. 2005.

Valpovo, June 4, 2005

Translator's note. Čobanac is the spicy Slavonian "shepherd's stew" of mixed meats, cooked outdoors in a kotlić, a cauldron over an open fire; Stjepan (Stevo) Mesić was President of Croatia from 2000 to 2010.
