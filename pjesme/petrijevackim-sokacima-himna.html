Domoljubne i zavičajne

Petrijevačkim sokacima – himna

Homeland and native region

Through the Lanes of Petrijevci (Anthem)

Ej, da su mi konji vraniI fijaker novi,tada bi se ostvarili,moji davni snovi.

Hey, if I had raven horsesand a carriage new,then my dreams of long agowould at last come true.

Vozo' bih se kroz Petrijevcei po danu i po mrakui pozdravljo cure, snašeu svakom sokaku.

I would drive through Petrijevciboth by day and in the night,greeting all the girls and young wivesin each lane in sight.

Još Rimljani Petrijevce su znalii Mursela ime su mu dali.I još nešto tu se dogodilo,naše selo republika bilo.

Petrijevci the Romans knew of old,and gave the name Mursela to it, we're told.And something else took place here, you should know:our village was a republic — it was so!

Krenuo bih Bregovitom,sve do Vetamice,a onda bih Drenovitomošo' dragoj ljubit lice.Prošao bih Kolođvarom,čak u Novo Selo,tamo bi me dočekalo,moje janje bijelo.

Down Bregovita I'd set offto Vetamica's place,and then along DrenovitaI'd go to kiss my darling's face.Through Kolođvar I'd be drivingright to Novo Selo, whereI'd be greeted, waiting for me,by my own white lamb, right there.

Još Rimljani Petrijevce su znalii Mursela ime su mu dali.I još nešto tu se dogodilo,naše selo republika bilo.

Petrijevci the Romans knew of old,and gave the name Mursela to it, we're told.And something else took place here, you should know:our village was a republic — it was so!

Iz mlinske bih ja polakoi u Bikov Sokak,da svečeri stignem,ja dragoj na konak.Republiku kada prođemi u Fenek dođem.Kada duši nađem mira,nek' se pjeva, nek' se svira.

From Mlinska, slow and easy,down the Bikov Sokak way,so by evening I'd have cometo my darling, there to stay.When I've gone through the Republicand to Fenek I have come,when my soul has found its peace, thenlet them sing, and let them strum!

Još Rimljani Petrijevce su znalii Mursela ime su mu dali.I još nešto tu se dogodilo,naše selo republika bilo.

Petrijevci the Romans knew of old,and gave the name Mursela to it, we're told.And something else took place here, you should know:our village was a republic — it was so!

Translator's note. The song version of the poem above, with a new refrain: music by Goran Ivanović – Lac and Goran Đurković – Maš, arranged by Goran Rusan, performed by Boris Šili. Mursela was the Roman settlement near Petrijevci, which locals proudly call "the Republic".
