Woman

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Što je žena,Kakva je uloga njena?Žena je san,Lijepa k'o sunčan dan.BožanstvenaU majčinoj ulozi,Jer nju su stvarali BoziHerkul i Titan.Žena je BoginjaLjepote,MudrostiI kreposti.Ili još višeOna je skup svegaŠto božanstvom odiše.Žena je najljepša bajka,Kao sestra,Kao supruga,Kao majka.Žena je nježno ljubila,Mnoge je bogove rodila.Zvijezde trepereU njenoj kosi,U bujnim njedrimaMjesec skriva.Na krhkim plećimaPlanetu nosi.I bit će voljenaSve dok je živa.

What is a woman,what is her role?A woman is a dream,lovely as a sunny day.Divinein the mother's role,for she was made by the gods,Hercules and Titan.Woman is the Goddessof Beauty,of Wisdomand of Virtue.Or even more,she is the sum of allthat breathes of the divine.Woman is the loveliest fairy tale,as a sister,as a wife,as a mother.Woman loved tenderly,and many gods she bore.Stars are twinklingin her hair;in her ample bosomshe hides the moon.On her fragile shouldersshe carries the planet.And she will be lovedas long as she's alive.

Valpovo, 1980.

Valpovo, 1980
