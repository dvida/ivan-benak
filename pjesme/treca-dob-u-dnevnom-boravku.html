Umirovljenički jadi

Treća dob u „Dnevnom boravku“

Pensioners' woes

The Third Age at the "Day Centre"

U Valpovu usred parkaima jedna divna zgrada,sastaje se ovdje društvou BORAVKU DNEVNOM sada.

In the park in Valpovostands a lovely building, wherefriends now meet in companyin the Day Centre, gathered there.

Tu je društvo svakog dana,ženice se zabavljajui prošlost je lijepa bila,ali za nju ne pitaju.

Here the group is every day,ladies having fun, at play;and the past was lovely, true,but they pay it no mind today.

Tu igraju razne igre,dosadno im nikad nije,a kad grlo se osuši,kavica se slatko pije.

Here they play at many games,and they're never bored, not ever;and when throats begin to dry,coffee's sipped, and sweet as ever.

Svakog dana ovdje dođu,mnogo dama našeg kraja,preko dana da si nađu,zemaljskoga malo raja.

Every day they come here, manyladies from around our way,just to find a little heavenhere on earth, throughout the day.

Petrović je tu Vanesakoja ovo društvo vodi.Kad se ovdje žene skupe,tu se druže u ljepoti.

Vanesa Petrović is here,and the group is led by her;when the women gather round,lovely hours they share together.

Oh, divote, mili Bože,kad se skupe ovdje dame,pa kad priče raspredaju,društvo prave sebi same.

Oh, what joy, dear God, to seewhen the ladies gather round;spinning stories, one and all,their own company they've found.

Ponekada nešto rade,vezenjem se neke bave,al' godine brzo lete,pa rođendan često slave.

Now and then they do some work;some embroider, it is true;but the years go flying fast,birthdays come round often, too.

A posao važan nijeza njeg' nitko i ne hajetaj BORAVAK DNEVNI želebar što duže da potraje.

Work is not what matters here,no one gives a thought to it;what they want is for the Day Centreto last as long as time permits.

Valpovo, 24. 11. 2013.

Valpovo, November 24, 2013

Translator's note. About the day centre for elderly people in the park in Valpovo.
