Medika je dom Balikić Ane

Occasional verse and dedications

Medika Is Ana Balikić's Home

Onaj tko je ovom Domu ime dao,što je MED I DIKA, on je dobro znao,jer od meda znajte, ništa slađe nema,a dika sa dikom slatku ljubav sprema.

The one who gave this Home its name knew right:it's MED I DIKA, honey and delight.For nothing's sweeter, mark you, than honey is,and dear with dear prepares sweet love's own bliss.

I onda kad spojiš dva imena ova,u ovom je DOMU sva ljepota snova.Zato ime DOMA to je vrlo krasno,što MEDIKA znači, to je svima jasno.

And when you put these two names side by side,then in this HOME the beauty of dreams resides.That's why the HOME's name is a lovely one:what MEDIKA means is plain to everyone.

U tom DOMU sada štićenici žive,za njihovu prošlost samoće su krive.Kuća prazna, pusta, sve iz kuće ode,pa nikoga nema da ti pruži vode.

Within this HOME its residents now stay;for what is past, blame loneliness, I say.The house stands empty, desolate, all gone,with no one left to hand you water, none.

I vaš život sada u snove se vrati,tu ljepotu može MEDIKA vam dati.A mjesto je Doma, Osjek blizu Drave,pa ujutro sviću divne zore plave.

And now in dreams your life returns anew;that beauty MEDIKA can give to you.The Home's in Osijek, near the Drava's side,where lovely blue dawns break at morningtide.

A vlasnik je DOMA sad Balikić Ana,kao dobra žena u DOMU je znana.Tu normalan život u domu postoji,tridesetak svojih štićenika broji.

The owner of the HOME is Ana Balikić now,and in the HOME she's known as a good woman.A normal life goes on here in this home;some thirty residents it counts its own.

Za vaše probleme što imate noćiosoblje MEDIKE vama će pomoći.Nakon snova jutrom, to morate znati:osvježenje tijela MEDIKA će dati.

Whatever troubles you may have at night,the MEDIKA staff will help you set them right.And after dreams, at morning, you should know:the body's refreshment MEDIKA will bestow.

I starost je ljepša kad se s nekim druži,tu ljepotu MEDIKA može da vam pruži.Nakon dnevne šetnje, dobar ručak čeka,a nakon večere, posteljica meka.

Old age is lovelier when shared with friends;that beauty MEDIKA to you extends.After the daily walk, a good lunch is spread,and after supper comes a soft, snug bed.

U postelji toploj divne snove sniti,pa zar može nešto od tog ljepše biti.Kada se u snove vaša mladost vrati,da bi mogli veselo društvu ispričati.

To dream such lovely dreams in a warm bed —what could be lovelier than that, it's said?When youth comes back to you in dreams at night,so you can tell your friends of it with delight.

Pa kada vas jutro iz toplog sna budi,a jutarnji danak na ljepotu sluti.I gospođa Ana kad vam se nasmije,vjerujte mi to su životne premije.

And when the morning wakes you from warm sleep,and the new day's a promise beauty will keep,and when Mrs Ana gives a smile your way,believe me, these are life's own prizes, I say.

Valpovo, 17. 01. 2013.

Valpovo, January 17, 2013

Translator's note. A care home for the elderly near Osijek. The name MEDIKA splits into MED I DIKA, "honey and delight" (dika is pride, joy, a darling).
