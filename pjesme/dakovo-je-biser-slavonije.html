Domoljubne i zavičajne

Đakovo je biser Slavonije

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Đakovo Is the Pearl of Slavonia

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Slavonijo, domovino moja,volim tebe k'o majčino krilo.Od davnina naselja te krase,u središtu Đakovo se svilo.

Slavonia, my homeland and my own,I love you like my mother's lap, so dear.Since ancient times your settlements have graced you,and at your centre Đakovo nestles here.

Đakovštino, budi ponosita,priroda te ljepotom nadaja,tebe krase bregovi i ravni,ti si dio zemaljskoga raja.

O Đakovština, stand proud and tall,with beauty nature has endowed you fair,the hills and plains adorn you, one and all;of paradise on earth you have a share.

Ravnica te plodovima hrani,a bregovi natapaju vinom.Sretan onaj tko u tebi živi,tko se može zvati tvojim sinom.

The plain feeds you with all the fruits it yields,the hills keep you well watered with their wine.How happy he who lives within your bounds,who can be counted as a son of thine.

Đakovo je biser tvoga kraja,pun znamenja i ljudskih vrlina,njega krasi park i katedrala,sjemenište znano od starina.

Đakovo is the pearl of all your land,rich in its sights and virtues of mankind;its park adorns it, and its great cathedral,its seminary, famed since olden times.

Na diku ti ergela i konji,plemenite rase lipicanci,što su ponos naše Slavonije,u svijetu su čuveni i znani.

The stud farm and its horses are your pride,the Lipizzaners, of a noble breed,the glory of our own Slavonia,renowned throughout the world, and known indeed.

"ĐAKOVAČKI VEZOVI" su slavni,poznati su u čitavom svijetu.Tu se skuplja biser iz Hrvatskemladež naša u najboljem cvijetu.

The "Đakovački vezovi" are famed,and known throughout the world, both far and near.The pearl of all Croatia gathers here:our youth, in the full flower of their prime.

Jubilarni "VEZOVI" su stigli,koji traju već trideset ljeta.Za "VEZOVE ĐAKOVAČKE" znajuna tisuće ljudi širom svijeta.

The jubilee "Vezovi" have arrived,already thirty summers they have run;the "Đakovački vezovi" are knownto thousands, everywhere beneath the sun.

Na tom zboru ne znam šta je ljepše:Da l' rubina svilom izvezena,il' zaprega skladno išarana,il' dva vrana konja lipicanca?

At that great gathering, I can't tell what's fairest:the rubina with silk embroidered through,or the horse-drawn carriage, finely patterned,or two raven-black Lipizzaners, too?

Možda sajam vina i kulina,da l' izložba glazbala i slika?Ili možda revija rubineil' kad kolo zaigra moj dika?

Perhaps the fair of wine and of kulen,or the display of instruments and art?Or maybe the parade of the rubina,or when my darling in the kolo takes part?

Najljepše je kad folklor zaigrai razvije kolo vilovito,na tamburi kada žica ciknei šokica vrisne tankovito.

The loveliest is when the dancers startand spread the kolo, fairy-like and light,when on the tambura a string will squealand a Šokica whoops out, high and bright.

Još je ljepše kad se bira snašasva u nošnji kao lutka živa.Na njoj svila i dukati žuti,tu ljepotu svaki šokac sniva.

Still lovelier, when they choose the fairest wife,all in her costume, like a living doll;on her, the silk and yellow ducats shine:such beauty is the dream of Šokci all.

Ej šokice, naša ljepotice,plemenita roda Hrvatice,ti si ponos naše Šokadije,sretan onaj tko ti ljubi lice.

Hey, Šokica, you beauty of our own,of noble birth, Croatian, full of grace,you are the pride of our Šokadija;and happy he who gets to kiss your face.

"ĐAKOVAČKI VEZOVI" su znani,već trideset ljeta održani,svaki puta bolji nego lani,ti veseli šokački divani.

The "Đakovački vezovi" are known,held thirty summers now, and every yeareach time still better than the year before —these merry Šokac get-togethers here.

Godinama bit će dalje tako,igrat će se lijepo i junački,birat će se ljepotice mladei "VEZOVI" bit će Đakovački.

For years to come it will continue so:they'll dance so beautifully, with gallant skill,and young beauties will be chosen there,and Đakovo will keep its "Vezovi" still.

Sve dok bude naše Slavonije,bit će: ića, pića lijepih snaša.Na skupove pjesme i veselja,skupljat će se šokadija naša.

As long as our Slavonia shall last,there'll be food, drink, and lovely wives in store;to gatherings of song and merrimentour Šokadija will gather evermore.

Valpovo, 29.07.1997.

Valpovo, July 29, 1997

Translator's note. Written for the 30th Đakovački vezovi ("Đakovo Embroideries"), the summer folklore festival held in Đakovo since 1967. Đakovo is also famous for its cathedral and its stud farm of Lipizzaner horses; rubina is the white linen dress of Šokac women's costume, and kulen is Slavonia's paprika sausage.
