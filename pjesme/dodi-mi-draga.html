Dođi mi, draga

Love poems

Come to Me, Darling

Dođi, draga, bar još jednom,na grudi mi lezi moje.Ko' putniku vode žednompruži meni usne svoje.

Come, my darling, just once more,lie upon my breast, come near;as a thirsty traveller water,offer me your lips, my dear.

Dođi mi na bolne grudisa poljupcem vidaj rane.Na grudima mojim budiobasjaj mi tmurne dane.

Come to my poor aching breast,kiss my wounds and make them well;stay upon my breast, and rest,light my gloomy days as well.

U zagrljaj dođi meni,kad zvjezdane budu noći.Probudi moj život sneni,a poljupcem sklopi oči.

Come into my arms, embrace me,when the nights have starry skies;wake this dreamy life of mine,with a kiss then close my eyes.

Da ja snivam slatke snoveu krilašcu, mila, tvome,da osjetim slasti noveu bolnome srcu svome.

So that I may dream sweet dreamsin your little lap, sweetheart,and may feel new sweetness startin my own poor aching heart.

Valpovo, 14. 11. 1974.

Valpovo, November 14, 1974
