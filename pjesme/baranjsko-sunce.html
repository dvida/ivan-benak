Umirovljenički jadi

Baranjsko sunce

Pensioners' woes

The Baranja Sun

U Belome BARANJSKO JE SUNCE,dom za stare i nemoćne ljude,mnogima je utočište dalo,bolna srca mnoga obasjalo.

In Beli there's the Baranja Sun, a homefor aged people and for those grown frail;to many it has given refuge, too,and many aching hearts it has shone through.

Ovaj DOM se časno sad ponosi,što BARANJSKO SUNCE ime nosi.Oni što su ime DOMU dali,snagu sunca oduvijek su znali.

This Home may now with honour proudly claimthat it is called the Baranja Sun by name.And those who named the Home have always knownthe strength the sun possesses as its own.

Svima vama što ste u tom DOMU,usamljeni u životu svomu,nek' vam teku u ljepoti dani,od BARANJSKOG SUNCA obasjani.

To all of you who live within this Home,lonely in life, and living it alone,may all your days in beauty flow and run,lit by the Baranja Sun, every one.

Tuga vas je morila u tami,noć je tiha, vi kod kuće sami,ali ovdje blažene su noći,jer vi znate SUNCE će pomoći.

In darkness, sorrow wore away at you;the night was still, at home alone, just you;but here the nights are blessed; you know it's true:the Sun is here, and it will help you through.

I starački dovukli se jadi,kao nekad niste više mladi,često puta bolno kuca srce,tko će pomoć ako neće SUNCE.

And now the woes of age have crept your way;you are not young, as you were in your day;your heart so often beats in pain and grief:who'll help, if not the Sun, and bring relief?

Nakon snova kad otvoriš oči,sve što trebaš SUNCE će pomoći,dobar ručak i društvo te čeka,prije snova posteljica meka.

When, after dreams, you open up your eyes,whatever you may need, the Sun supplies;a good lunch and good company await,and, before dreams, a soft bed, neatly made.

Pa zar može nešto bolje biti,u noćima divne snove sniti.Vaša mladost u snove se vrati,to vam može samo SUNCE dati.

So could there be a better thing than this:at night to dream such dreams of loveliness?Your youth returns in dreams to visit you,and that the Sun alone can give to you.

Valpovo, 14. 12. 2011.

Valpovo, December 14, 2011

Translator's note. Written for Baranjsko sunce ("the Baranja Sun"), a home for the elderly and infirm in Beli Manastir, which he calls simply Beli. The poem plays on the home's name throughout.
