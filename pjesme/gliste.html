The Worms

Došle male glistekod šustera Risteda cipele naruče –je li je moguće?

Some little worms cameto Risto, cobbler by name,to order shoes. Dear me,now how can that be?

Majstor veli njima:„Sada kad je zima,za hod oko kućeuzmite papuče.Za noge velikeuzmite patike,kada snijeg zavijeza napolje nije.

The master says, "My dears,now winter time is here,for pottering indoorstake slippers for the floors.For feet of bigger sizetake trainers; that is wise.But when the snowstorms blow,they're not for outdoors, though.

A kad zima minei kad sunce sine,kad nestanu rose –mogu noge bose!“

And once the winter's pastand sunshine comes at last,and the dew is gone, and so,then barefoot you can go!"

Translator's note. The joke, of course, is that worms have no feet.
