Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Davoru prijatelju!

Occasional verse and dedications

To Davor, My Friend!

Imam jednog prijatelja,sa muzikom on se bavi,naći ćeš ga uvijek tamo:gdje se svira, gdje se slavi.

I have got a friend, and hespends his time with melody;where there's music, where there's cheer,you will always find him near.

Davor Borić mu je ime.Svojom pjesmom srca dira.VJEČITI je OSMIJEH benda,sa kojima Davor svira.

Davor Borić is his name;with his songs he touches hearts;in the band "Eternal Smile"is where Davor plays his part.

Pa uz glazbu kad zapjeva,zrak treperi od miline,slušaoce razveseli,eto, to su mu vrline.

When he sings along to music,the air quivers with delight;and he cheers his listeners up —there, his virtues come to light!

Kao dragom prijatelju,poklanjam mu knjigu moju.Sa radošću ja mu želim,da uglazbi pjesmu koju.

As a friend so dear to me,I present my book to him;and I gladly hope that hesets a poem to melody.

Valpovo, 22. 11. 2013.

Valpovo, November 22, 2013

Translator's note. Vječiti osmijeh ("Eternal Smile") is the name of Davor Borić's band.
