Obiteljski kutak

Majka

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Family corner

Mother

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kada dijete noću plače,iz snova kada se budi,da čedu svome utjehu nađe,majka ga svija na grudi.

When a child cries in the night,waking from its dreams in fright,mother, to console her little one,holds it to her bosom tight.

Kad čuje bilo srca njenog,toplinu osjeti majke,ona je njemu božica sreće,ljepša od svake bajke.

When it hears her heart's soft beatingand it feels its mother's warmth,she's its goddess of all gladness,lovelier than any fairy tale.

Kad padne i bolno plače,tad majci brže hita.Ona mu nježno suze brišei poljupcem rane vida.

When it falls and cries in pain,to its mother fast it goes;tenderly she wipes its tears,kisses better all its woes.

Za svaku bolest čeda svoga,majčino srce pati.Svoje bi oči za njega dala.To može samo mati.

For each illness of her childa mother's heart aches through and through.She would give her eyes for it —that, a mother alone can do.

Valpovo, 04.03.1980.

Valpovo, March 4, 1980
