Poems for children

The Shovel and the Pick

Lopata i stari kramp,svakog dana složno rade:zajednički cestu grade,često puta trnje vade,a ponekad ruže sade.

The shovel and the old pickwork in concord every day:together they build the road,often clear the thorns away,and plant roses along the way.

A sve rade bez nagrade!

And all of this without pay!
