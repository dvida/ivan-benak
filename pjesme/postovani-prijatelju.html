Poštovani prijatelju!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Friend!

U proljeće mnoge žabe,u bari su kreketale.Da se riješi žablji problem,sastanak su održale.

In the springtime, many frogscroaked there in the pond all day;to resolve the frogs' dilemmathey held a meeting right away.

Sastale se jednog dana,usred ove barske vode,a tema je, žaba, bila,da se bore protiv rode.

One day they came together, so,in the middle of the bog,and the topic for each frogwas to fight the stork, their foe.

Šta je dalje tamo bilo?Ako želiš to saznati?Najdražima ovu knjigu,trebao bi pročitati.

What went on there after that?If you'd like to find it out,to your dearest ones, this bookyou should read aloud, no doubt.

Čitaj djeci prije snova,divne pjesme knjige ove,da bi lijepe snove snili,a ujutro vedri bili.

Read the children, before bed,all the lovely poems here,so sweet dreams will fill each headand they'll wake up bright and clear.

Dragi Pleša Danijelu,ova knjiga Tebi želi,najmlađima pružit radost:to Ti autor knjige veli!

Dear Danijel Pleša, here'swhat this book wishes for you:that you bring the young ones joy —so the author tells you true!

Valpovo, 12.02.2014.

Valpovo, February 12, 2014

Translator's note. A dedication in his children's book The Frogs' Meeting (Žablji sastanak).
