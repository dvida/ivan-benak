Sjećanje na Pavicu Kusturić

Occasional verse and dedications

Remembering Pavica Kusturić

Pavica se sa nama družila,svu ljepotu prijateljstva dala,stihove je društvu podarila,Slavonijom pjesmice čitala.Toplog srca kao pjesnik bila,domovinu svoju je ljubila,ljepotu je Slavoniji dala.Tri je divne knjige napisala:PJEVAM TEBI SLAVONIJO,DUŠA MOJA OTVORILA VRATA,SLAVONIJA U ZJENICI OKA!Oduvijek si dio srca moga.To književno blago ostavila,bez pozdrava nas je napustila.Svu ljubav je dala zavičaju,nek' joj duša počiva u raju.Zemljica joj ova laka bila,Slavonije koje je ljubila.Uživala sva blaženstva raja:to joj žele pisci zavičaja!

Pavica came and spent her time with us,gave all of friendship's beauty, through and through,bestowed her verses on our company,and read her poems all Slavonia through.Warm-hearted, as a poet she was known,and loved the homeland that she called her own,and to Slavonia she gave beauty's grace.Three lovely books she wrote, and here they are:I SING TO YOU, SLAVONIA,MY SOUL HAS OPENED UP ITS DOOR,SLAVONIA IN THE PUPIL OF THE EYE!You always were a part of my own heart.She left this literary wealth behind,and left us without farewell of any kind.She gave her native region all her love;may her soul rest in paradise above.May this dear soil lie lightly on her breast,Slavonia's soil, the land she loved the best.May she know all the blessedness of heaven:so wish the writers of her native region!

Valpovo, 22. 09. 2013.

Valpovo, September 22, 2013

Translator's note. In memory of the poet Pavica Kusturić; the lines in capitals are the titles of her books.
