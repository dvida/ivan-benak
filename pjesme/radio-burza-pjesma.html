Radio burza

Radio burza

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Radio Burza

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dok jutarnju svoju kavu pijem,slušam burzu, pa se i nasmijem.To je program što se rado sluša,svega ima što ti želi duša.Financijsko da poprave stanje,sve se mijenja, daje il' prodaje.Svega ima što ti srce želi,razne robe, da te Bog veseli.Svatko svoje za prodaju sprema,vjerujte mi, tu nema šta nema.Emisija to je za nas prava:ima svinja, teladi i krava,kukuruza, kupusa, krumpira,rakijice i sprava što svira.Automobil jeftino se nudi,vikendice, njive nude ljudi,ciglu, crijepa i drvene građe,tko što treba, tu može da nađe.I piliće tu nude bez mane,bilo žive ili očupane.Velku' gicu ili malo prase,svaki puta možeš kupit za se.Jedna žena komodu prodaje,uz komodu piramidu daje.Mladi rastu, hormoni se bude,ružmarine za svatove nude.Toplu bundu od pravoga nerca,da joj nema nadaleko premca.Sijena, slama sva je balirana,pa i drva nude izrezana.Za rakiju kazan se prodaje,komplet alat za kolinje daje.Prvoklasne orahe bez mane,svega ima da ti pamet stane.Na radiju sve se ovo nudi,to je prava trgovina, ljudi.Da nabrojim sve što se prodaje,to bi moglo poduže da traje.Svi podaci pišu se korektno,na radiju vode burzu spretno.

While sipping at my morning coffee cup,I hear the Burza, and it cheers me up.It is a programme people gladly heed;it offers all that any soul could need.So as to put their finances aright,they swap things, give things, sell things, left and right.There's everything your heart desires, you'll find,all sorts of goods, God bless you, every kind.Each person gets his wares for sale set out;believe me, there is nothing it's without.This show is just the thing for us, and how!There's pigs on offer, calves, and many a cow,there's corn and cabbages, and spuds they bring,some rakija, and things that play and sing.Cheap motor cars are offered on the air,and weekend cottages and fields are there,bricks, roofing tiles, and timber, beams and boards;whatever someone needs, the show affords.They offer chickens, too, without a speck,alive, or plucked right down from tail to neck.A great big pig, or else a piglet, too:each time, you'll find one there to buy for you.One woman sells a chest of drawers, and shethrows in a pyramid with it, for free.The young grow up, and hormones stir and wake;there's rosemary for weddings, yours to take.A cosy mink coat, genuine, so grandthere's none to match it far across the land.There's hay and straw, all baled and neatly tied,and firewood, sawn already, is supplied.A still for making rakija's on sale,and a kolinje toolkit, knife and pail,and first-class walnuts, not a single fault;so much, your mind would grind down to a halt.All this is offered to us on the air:that's proper trading, people, I declare!If I should count up all that's being sold,it would take long before it all was told.All details are correctly written down;they run the Burza deftly, best in town.

Valpovo, 16. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 16, 2000

Translator's note. Radio burza ("Radio Exchange") was the swap-and-sell programme on the local radio in Valpovo, for which he wrote his many verse ads. At Slavonian weddings, guests wear sprigs of rosemary.
