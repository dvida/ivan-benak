Saint Lucy

Lucija je svjetlonoša bila,mnoge bolne vidom obdarila,svojim darom pomoći je znala,mnogima je danju svjetlost dala.

Saint Lucy was a bearer of the light,and many in their pain she gave their sight;she knew to help, with gifts that were her own,and gave the light of day to many a one.

Svatko onaj časno se ponosi,što Lucije svete ime nosi.Oni što su djeci ime dalinjene moći sigurno su znali.

With honour, anyone may proudly claimto carry as their own Saint Lucy's name.And those who gave her name to children knewfull surely all the good her powers could do.

Ima onih što su svoj vid dali,ili mnogi nisu ga ni znali.Samoća ih mučila u tami,bolni život živjeli su sami.

There are some people who have lost their sight,and many never even knew the light;their loneliness tormented them in the dark;they lived their painful lives alone, apart.

Svima vama što ste u toj tamiusamljeni u životu sami,nek' vam teku u ljepoti dani,od LUCIJE SVETE OBASJANI.

To all of you who in that darkness stay,alone and lonely all along life's way,may all your days in beauty flow and run,lit up by Holy Lucy, every one.

Valpovo, 11. 12. 2012.

Valpovo, December 11, 2012

Translator's note. St Lucy (feast day 13 December) is the patron saint of eyesight and of the blind.
