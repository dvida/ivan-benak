Umirovljenički jadi

Draga generacijo

Pensioners' woes

My Dear Generation

Da biste me javno upoznalii o meni pravo stanje znali,reći ću vam sada ime mojei podatke stvarne dati svoje.

So that in public you may know me welland know the true state of affairs, I'll tellyou now my name, and give to all of youmy own particulars — and they are true.

Ivan Benak to je moje ime,ja sam pjesnik koji piše rime,ulica se moja pisat mora:Pejačević naša slavna Dora.

My name is Ivan Benak, and yes, I'ma poet, one who writes his lines in rhyme;and then my street, which must be written down:our Dora Pejačević of renown.

Ako netko hoće da me traži,moje kuće broj pet za sad važi,još dodajte ovdje mjesto moje:eto, znajte, slavno Valpovo je.

If anyone should wish to seek me out,my house is number five, for now, no doubt;and add my town here too, so you will know —well, there you have it: famous Valpovo.

Sve najbolje sada vama želim,jer sa vama i ja ljeta dijelim,mladost moja više mi ne cvijetai ja imam osamdeset ljeta.

I wish you all the very best today,for I share years with you along the way;my youth no longer blossoms, it is true,and I have eighty summers, just like you.

Pratila vas i radost i sreća,mirovina bila vam što veća,zdrava ljeta nek' vam sretno služe,mirovinu primali što duže.

May joy and happiness attend you, too,the biggest pension possible for you;may healthy summers serve you happily,and draw your pension long as long can be.

Ne bih više, toliko za sada,ne tražite svetog Petra vrata.Eto, to je sve što vama želim,vašoj sreći i ja se veselim!

That's all I'll say for now, I'll say no more;don't go out looking for Saint Peter's door.There, that is all I wish for all of you:in all your happiness I'm joyful too!

Valpovo, 16. 11. 2011.

Valpovo, November 16, 2011

Translator's note. Dora Pejačević (1885–1923), the celebrated Croatian composer, gave her name to his street. He was actually 78 when he wrote this. "Saint Peter's door" is the gate of heaven: the wish is that they not hurry there.
