Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Obitelji Nikolić!

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Nikolić Family!

Ovu knjigu dajemNikolić Antunu,da sa njome sredimomi suradnju punu.Kako gospon' Antununučadi ima,ova će mu knjigabiti kao prima.Neka čita djeci,bajke prije snovai to će mu bitijedna igra nova.I baka će malovremena da nađeda uspava onounuče najmlađe.Čitat će mu pjesmaiz ZEKINE SREĆE.Bakino sunašcekad se probudi:pa zar ima neštoljepše, dragi ljudi.I kada se igrai po sobi skače,baki srce udaraod veselja jače.Tu ljepotu snova,njima autor želi,njihovoj idilii on se veseli.

This book I giveto Nikolić Antun,so that between usour work's in tune.As Mister Antunhas grandchildren, too,this book will befirst-rate, through and through.Let him read to the childrenfairy tales at night,and that will be for hima new game, a delight.And Grandma will finda little time as wellto rock to sleepthe youngest grandchild of all.A poem will be read himfrom BUNNY'S LUCK.Grandma's little sunshine,when he wakes from sleep:is there anything lovelier,dear people, you could seek?And when he playsand hops about the floor,Grandma's heart beatswith joy all the more.That beauty of dreamsthe author wishes them,and in their idyllhe rejoices as well.

Valpovo, 31.01.2014.

Valpovo, January 31, 2014

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck), for reading to the grandchildren.
