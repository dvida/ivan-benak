Domoljubne i zavičajne

Slavonija

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Homeland and native region

Slavonia

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Od Krndije do Papuka,Psunja pa do Bilogore,na večernjim posijelima,slavonske se pjesme ore.

From Krndija to Papuk,Psunj, and Bilogora's height,at the evening village gatheringsSlavonian songs ring through the night.

Sa sjevera i zapadaravna polja Slavonije,kukuruzom zasađena,sežu čak do Semberije.

From the north and from the westlie the plains of Slavonia,sown with maize, and reaching outall the way to Semberija.

Opet tamo na istokusrijemskom se ravnicom vežetalasasto more žita,sve dokle ti oko seže.

There again, off to the east,joining Srijem's level plain,far as ever eye can seerolls a billowing sea of grain.

To predivno zelenilo,rijekama je prepleteno,brežuljcima okrunjenoi šumama okićeno.

All this wondrous green expanseis with rivers interlaced,by the rolling hills is crowned,and with forests richly graced.

Kad navečer sunce zađe,bezbroj zvijezda nebom sija.Nigdje nema ljepšeg krajanego što je Slavonija.

When the sun has set at evening,countless stars across the sky —nowhere is a land more lovelythan our own Slavonia.

U edenu tom zemaljskom,eto, tu sam rođen i ja.Tu življahu moji preci:to je moja Slavonija!

In this earthly Eden, here,there, you see, I too was born.Here my forefathers once lived:this is my Slavonia!

Valpovo, 1980.

Valpovo, 1980

Translator's note. Krndija, Papuk, Psunj and Bilogora are the hills that ring Slavonia to the west; Semberija lies across the Sava in Bosnia, and Srijem is the plain to the east.
