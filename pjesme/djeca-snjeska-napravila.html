Pjesme za djecu

Djeca snješka napravila

Poems for children

The Children Made a Snowman

Došla zima, sniježak prštii dječici štipa uši.Bijeli sniježak tiho pada,to idila zimska vlada.

Winter's come, and snow is sprinkling,children's ears it's nipping, tingling.White the snow falls, soft and slow:winter's idyll reigns below.

U dvorištu našem sada,neka gužva opća vlada.Dječica se sakupilai snješka su napravila.

Now within our yard, what's more,reigns a general uproar.All the children gathered round,built a snowman on the ground.

Od ugljana oči ima,nos od mrkve snješku štima,dugmad crna po kaputu,usta ima traku žutu.

Two coal eyes the snowman shows,nicely fits his carrot nose;down his coat black buttons shine,and his mouth's a yellow line.

Šešir mu je lonac stari,za brkove baš ne marii još nešto oko vrata,snješku fali baš kravata.

An old pot's his hat up there;for moustaches he's no care,and there's one more thing, I fear:round his neck, no tie is here!

Ispod ruke metlu ima,svidio se snješko svima.Oko njega cika, vika,snješko bio djeci dika.

With a broom beneath his arm,he won all of us with charm.Round him squeals and yells and noise;he's the pride of girls and boys.

Desetak je bio dana,djeci radost razigrana.Ta je radost kratka bila,djeca snješka napustila.

For ten days or so he stayed,joy for children as they played.But the joy did not last long:children left him and were gone.

Pa je snješko tužan bio,od tuge se rastopio.Mrkva, ugljan, metla stoje:što od snješka ostalo je.

Then the snowman grew so sad,melted from the grief he had.Carrot, coal and broom stay on:all that's left, now he is gone.

Valpovo, 09.02.2014.

Valpovo, February 9, 2014
