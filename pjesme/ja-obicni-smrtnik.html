I, an Ordinary Mortal!

Zar imam pravo,da te nježno ljubim?Zar imam pravo,da ti snove kidam,da ti se u srcuk'o skitnica skitam?Oh, božice moja!Kakav sam ja drznik.Kada te sa svetogpijedestala skidam,u naručje svoje.Ja obični smrtnik.

Have I the rightto kiss you tenderly?Have I the rightto tear your dreams apart,to roam like a vagabondinside your heart?Oh, my goddess!What a daring man I am,when I lift you downfrom your holy pedestalinto my arms —I, an ordinary mortal.

Valpovo, 15. 07. 1983.

Valpovo, July 15, 1983
