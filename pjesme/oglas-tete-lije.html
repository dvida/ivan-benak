Poems for children

Auntie Fox's Notice

from the book Bunny's Luck (1990)

Noćas na ogradiostao mi brkzbog jedne lude,pseće sile.Molim nalaznikada brk zadrži,a meni donesekoku ili pile.

Last night on a fenceI lost a whisker,all thanks to a maddog's brute force.I ask the finderto keep the whiskerand bring me a henor a chick, of course.

Teta Lija

Teta Lija

Translator's note. A mock lost-and-found notice. Teta Lija ("Auntie Fox") is the fox of Croatian children's tales.
