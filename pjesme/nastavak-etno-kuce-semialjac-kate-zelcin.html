Domoljubne i zavičajne

Nastavak etno kuće Semialjac Kate Zelčin

Homeland and native region

Kata Zelčin's Semialjac Ethno House, Continued

U kola se sad ne prežu konji,ni rzanje više se ne čuje,pa i štagalj miris sijena sanja,po dvorištu sad traktori bruje.

No horses now are harnessed to the cart,nor is their neighing heard here any more;the very barn now dreams the scent of hay,and in the yard the tractors hum and roar.

Iza štaglja predivna ljepota,zrak miriše, procvalo je voće,noć je topla, divna mjesečina,a zvijezde su okitile veče'.

Behind the barn, a beauty to behold:the air is fragrant, fruit trees are in flower,the night is warm, the moonlight is divine,and stars have come to deck the evening hour.

Još se nađu dobre, zlatne ruke,i posao uredno se kreće,kad u vrtu sve radove srede,kupa šljive i rakiju peče.

There still are good and golden hands to find,and all the work moves neatly, with a will;when all the garden's chores are put in order,they gather plums and fire the rakija still.

A tek što bi u tom vrtu bilo,da ga brižno mlade ruke paze,da se ljube i beru orahe,usamljene krevete da maze.

And what that garden then would get to see,if young hands tended it with loving care,to kiss and gather walnuts — and caressthe lonely beds that stand forsaken there.

U tom raju rode gnijezdo svile,cijelog dana klepeću od sreće,i onima što krevete maze,poklonit će bebu kao cvijeće.

In that paradise the storks have built their nest,and clatter for their happiness all day;and those caressing beds will get from thema baby, as a gift, like a bouquet.

Valpovo, 07. 03. 2013.

Valpovo, March 7, 2013

Translator's note. A sequel to his poem about the ethno house of Kata Zelčin of the Semialjac family: an old farmstead kept as a small museum of village life.
