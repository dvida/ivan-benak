Obiteljski kutak

Andreji za 22. rođendan

Family corner

To Andreja on Her 22nd Birthday

Andrejice unučice,draga naša golubice.I još jedno ljeto prođe,tvoj rođendan dragi dođe.Došao je danak pravi,tvoj rođendan da se slavi.Sad godinu imaš više,dva'est druga već se piše.Budi uvijek vedra, mila,želja ti se ostvarila.Stazama ti cvalo cvijeće,u životu puno sreće.To je naša želja stara,evo tebi malo para.Da se malo proveseliši da kupiš što si želiš.Mnogo sreće, puno ljeta,tebi žele baka i djeda!

Little Andreja, grandchild dear,our beloved dove, now hear:one more summer's passed away,and your dear birthday's come your way.Now the very day is heretime to celebrate and cheer.Now you have one more year too:now the count is twenty-two.May you be bright and sweet, and mayall your wishes come true one day.May flowers bloom along your ways,lots of luck through all your days.This, our wish, is old and true;here's a little money, too,so that you can have some funand buy what you wish, dear one.Lots of luck and many a yearwish you Grandma and Grandpa, dear!

Valpovo, 09. 09. 2006.

Valpovo, September 9, 2006
