Radio burza

Auto snova, a malena lova

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Dream Car for a Little Cash

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Renol 4 T L, smeđe boje,prod'o bih ga, radosti mi moje.Sedamdeset i osme je rođen,samo od dva vozača je vožen.Da vam kažem iskreno po duši,od njih dvoje ni jedan ne puši.Ne zna što su oluje, nevere,u garaži bio u to vrijeme.Tome autu ništa i ne fali,treba samo jedan pregled mali.Bateriju još dodajte njemui ostalu koju treba spremu.Pa bi mogli na tehnički ićii tablice za njega podići.Pet stotina eura se traži,po vrijednosti on još više važi.Njegov motor kao mačor prede,od te cijene, više gume vrijede.Tu se traži neka sitna lova,da imate svoj auto iz snova.Eto ljudi, to je zgoda prava,da imate auto zabadava.Nema love, a dijelovi skupi,možda netko za rezervu kupi.Nije meni samo da se proda,već da drugi pješice ne hoda.

A Renol 4 T L, and brown in hue —I'd sell it, oh, my joy, I'm telling you.It came into the world in seventy-eight,and only two have driven it, to date.I'll tell you honestly, upon my soul:of those two drivers, neither smokes at all.It doesn't know what storms and tempests are —it sat them out inside the garage, this car.There's nothing wrong with this car, none at all;it only needs a check-up, something small.Just add a battery to it, by the way,and any other kit it needs, I'd say,and then you could take it for its roadworthy testand get its number plates, and all the rest.Five hundred euro is the price I seek;in value, it's worth more still, so to speak.Its engine purrs just like a tomcat, too;the tyres are worth more than that price, it's true.It's just small change I'm asking for, you see,so you can have your dream car, finally.There, people, now's a real chance, and how:to get a car for nothing, here and now.Now cash is short, and spare parts cost a lot;someone might buy it just for spares — why not?It isn't just a sale I'm hoping for,but that some other man need walk no more.

Valpovo, 29. 07. 2001.

Valpovo, July 29, 2001

Translator's note. One of his verse ads for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio. "Renol" is his spelling of Renault, as it is pronounced in Croatian; the Renault 4 was a common family car in the old Yugoslavia.
