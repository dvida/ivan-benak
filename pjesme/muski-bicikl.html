Radio burza

Muški bicikl

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Men's Bicycle

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Potpuno novi, ispravan, pravi,prodajem muški bicikl plavi.

Brand new, in working order, sound and true,I'm selling a men's bicycle in blue.

Bicikl dobar, bez svake mane,ima branik lanca i blatobrane.

A fine bike, with no fault in it to find,it's got a chain guard, mudguards front and behind.

I za noćni promet on vam štima,jer prednje svjetlo i zadnje ima.

For night-time traffic, too, it's just right,because it's got a front and a rear light.

Sve što mu treba na sebi nosi,kada poželiš sjedi i vozi.

It's got all that it needs on it, and so,whenever you please, just sit on it and go.

Molim vas kupci, dobro je znati,za petsto kuna njega ću dati.

Dear buyers, please, it's good you understand:five hundred kuna puts it in your hand.

Adresa moja, kao za PONI,zato nek' isti telefon zvoni.

My address is as for the PONI bike,so ring the same phone number, if you like.

652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,biciklom nek' kupac bude sretan.

652 - eight hundred twenty-one,may this bike bring its buyer joy and fun.

Valpovo, 14. 05. 2005.

Valpovo, May 14, 2005

Telefonski broj iz pjesme više nije u upotrebi.

The phone number in this poem is no longer in use.

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad; he had advertised a Poni bicycle the week before.
