Radio burza

Prodah motor, kaciga ostala

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

I Sold the Motorbike, the Helmet Stayed

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

JAMAHU sam prod'o svoju juče',sad mi stoji kaciga kod kuće.Bez kacige vozali se mnogi,došao je sada zakon strogi.Sa svih strana policija vrebaza bicikl sad kaciga treba.Kao moja, ta se sada traži,ta za motor i bicikl važi.Kaciga je nova, sačuvana,korištena samo mjesec dana.Ni jedanput još pokisla nije,glavu čuva, kada kiša lije.Okrugla je, žuto-plave boje,a kroz vizir gledaš zlato svoje.Kad je hladno, ona glavu grije,oči štiti, kada sunce sije.Dva ventila još sa strane ima,to mu dođe, kao autu klima.Kada padaš, i ugledaš zvijezdu,siguran si, kao ptić u gnijezdu.Kacigu ću jeftino prodati,ako netko želi je imati.Dovoljno je da se meni javi,na telefon moj jedini pravi.Na ovaj broj šaljite mi vijest:šest, šest, dva, devetsto, devedeset, šest.

I sold my Yamaha just yesterday,and now at home my helmet's stored away.Without a helmet many used to ride,but now a strict new law has been applied.The police lie in wait on every side;you need a helmet now for a bike ride.A helmet like my own is sought today,for motorbike and bike, it works either way.The helmet's new, it's been kept well and clean,just one month's use is all it's ever seen.Not once so far has it got wet with rain;it keeps your head safe when it pours — that's plain.It's round, in yellow and in blue, and throughthe visor you can see your darling, too.When it is cold, it keeps your head warm, andit shields your eyes from sun, you understand.Two vents it also has along the side —it's like the air-con in a car you ride.And when you fall and see a star or two,you're safe as a chick in its nest — it's true.The helmet I will sell you cheap, you see,if anyone would like to have it from me.It is enough to give me just a callon my one proper telephone, that's all.Send me your news on this number, and be quick:six, six, two, nine hundred, ninety, six.

Valpovo, 21.08.2004.

Valpovo, August 21, 2004

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza, written when a new law made helmets compulsory.
