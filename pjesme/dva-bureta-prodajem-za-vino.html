Radio burza

Dva bureta prodajem za vino

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

Two Wine Barrels for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Prodao sam moj vinograd stari,pa sad imam za prodaju stvari.

I've sold my old vineyard, and so, you see,I've got some things for sale now, here with me.

Zato želim da upoznam ljude,dva bureta za vino se nude.

And so I want to let folks know, I say:two barrels for wine are on offer today.

Svako bure od tristo litara,prodao bih, da dođem do para.

Each barrel holds three hundred litres, andI'd sell them both to get some cash in hand.

Lijepe bačve, zaobljene fino,u njima sam ja držao vino.

Fine barrels, rounded nicely, through and through;I used to keep my wine in them, it's true.

Izrađene od hrasta lužnjaka,a stežu ih šest obruča jaka.

Made out of lowland oak, and clamped aroundby six strong hoops that hold them good and sound.

Svako bure dva otvora ima,jedno pušta, drugo vino prima.

Each barrel has two holes, and they combine:one lets it out, the other takes in wine.

Kad u jesen napunim bačvicu,svakog dana uzimam litricu.

When in the autumn I have filled the cask,a litre every day is all I ask.

Godinama ja već tako radim,pijem vino, pomalo se sladim.

For years and years I've done it just this way:I drink my wine and savour it each day.

Ali više vinograda nemam,zato bačve za prodaju spremam.

But I've no vineyard any more, and soI'm getting both these barrels set to go.

Malo prije čuo sam, baš fino,jedna žena nudi crno vino.

A while ago I heard — now that is fine —a certain woman's offering red wine.

Baš divota i to valja znati,a ja bačve imam za prodati.

How splendid — that is something to be known,for I have barrels for sale, of my own.

Dogovorom svaka cijena važi,tri kunića po litri se traži.

The price can be agreed on — here's the bit:three little kuna a litre, that is it.

Preko burze šaljem oglas vrijedan,nazovite: 652 osamsto dvadeset jedan.

Through the Burza I send this ad — a worthy one:call 652 eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 26. 07. 2001.

Valpovo, July 26, 2001

Translator's note. A verse ad for "Radio burza", the swap-and-sell show on the local radio; the phone number was his own.
