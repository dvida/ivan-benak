A Date at the End of the Street

Prođoh ulicom našomu prvi sumrak, jučer.Vidjeh te, na trenutak,na prozoru tvoje kuće.

I walked along our streetat early dusk, yesterday.I saw you, just for a moment,at the window of your house.

Rekla si, dragana, doćimeni, da ljubim ti lice.Ja sretan pođoh tada,do kraja naše ulice.

You told me, darling, you'd cometo me, so I could kiss your face.And happy, I set off thento the end of our street.

Čekam, a studen me bije.Magla se spušta plava.Na nebu zvijezde se palei grad polako već spava.

I wait, and the cold bites hard,the blue mist is coming down;the stars in the sky are kindling,and slowly sleeps the town.

Već ponoćna kuca ura,bol mi je stegla srce.Ti nisi došla, draga,na spoj, na kraju ulice.

Already midnight is striking,pain has gripped my heart.You didn't come, my darling,to our date at the end of the street.

Valpovo, 1981.

Valpovo, 1981
