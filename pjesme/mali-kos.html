The Little Blackbird

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Jutros ranocrni kosobojiosmeđe nos,pa onako- sebe pun -pokazujesvima kljun,i po rosistalno ideda ga drugeptice vide.

Early todaythe blackbird roseand painted brownhis own nose,and then, like that,— full of pride —shows off his beakon every side,and he walks and walksthrough the dew,so other birdscan get a view.

Rosa ga jeprala,prala,prala,i prljavu bojus nosamaloga kosaizaprala.Pa sad opetmali kosima lijepižuti nos!

The dew washed him,washed,washed,washed,and the dirty paintfrom the noseof the little blackbirdwashed away.So now once morethe blackbird showsa lovely yellowlittle nose!
