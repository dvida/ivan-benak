Pjesme za djecu

Zbrkani snovi

iz knjige Žablji sastanak (2005.)

Poems for children

Muddled Dreams

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Slušajte me, djeco, sada:probudih se jednog jutra,mjesto zore suton pada –mislim da je bilo sutra.

Listen, children, what I say:I woke up one morning, whendusk instead of dawn held sway;I think it was tomorrow then.

U dvorištu koke sjede,puše lulu, kavu piju,neka plete, neka predei košulju pijetlu šiju.

In the yard the hens are sitting,puffing pipes and coffee sipping;some are spinning, some are knitting,and a shirt for Rooster stitching.

Iza kuće na livadi,razvezana i bez lanca,posjedala četir vrancai kartaju preferansa.

On the meadow past the house,all untied and free of chains,four black horses sat down there,playing cards: preferans.

Naša guska čudno gače,k'o magarac viče „IJA!“U dvorištu s kokošimaispod ruke šeta lija.

Our goose honks a curious honk,cries "Hee-haw!" like donkeys do.In the yard, arm in arm with hens,a fox goes strolling through.

A kudronju mog Šarova,upregao mačor Jozau velika kola novapa miševe selom voza.

Joza, the tomcat, has harnessedmy curly Šarov to a newgreat big wagon, and he's drivingmice around the village too.

Malo dalje kraj vrbika,vidio sam u cik zorekako neki stari djedakrticama zemlju ore.

A bit further, by the willows,at the crack of dawn I foundan old granddad using molesas his team to plough the ground.

Slušajte me, prijatelji,gledam čudne stvari mnoge,kad u onoj mutnoj barigliste peru svoje noge.

Listen to me now, my friends,many a wondrous sight I meet:there, within that murky pond,earthworms washing their own feet!

Priđoh bliže ja do vode,a bara se zatalasa.Po obali ribe hodei pjevaju iz sveg glasa.

To the water I drew nigh,and the pond rippled around;on the bank the fish walked by,singing with a mighty sound.

U šumi su čudne stvari:leptiri se s vukom tuku,šumu ruši dabar stari,gusjenice klade vuku.

In the woods are wondrous sights:butterflies and the wolf have fights,the old beaver fells the trees,caterpillars haul logs with ease.

Kad navečer zora sviće,snovi mi na javu sliče.Da se nisam probudio,još bih i sad čuda snio!

When at nightfall dawn is breaking,dreams to me seem just like waking.If I hadn't woken, Iwould be dreaming wonders still!

Translator's note. Preferans is a card game popular in the Balkans and Eastern Europe. Šarov is a common Croatian dog's name.
