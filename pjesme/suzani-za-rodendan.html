Obiteljski kutak

Suzani za rođendan

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Family corner

For Suzana's Birthday

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Za tren oka nestalo djetinjstvo,u nepovrat otišli su snovi.Kćeri mila, od srca ti želim,da ti budu sretni dani novi.

Your childhood vanished in the blink of an eye,your dreams have gone away, not to return.My darling daughter, from my heart I wishthat happy new days now be yours in turn.

Ono što si sama ti poželiš,barem da ti podariti mogu.Al' za sreću tvojemu životuskrušeno ću moliti se Bogu.

Whatever you yourself might wish to have,I wish at least I had the means to give;but for the happiness of your whole lifeI'll pray to God most humbly while I live.

Prohujao život kao vjetar.Sada slaviš trideset i neku.Od srca ti roditelji žele,da ti sretni novi dani teku!

Life has blown past, just as the wind goes by;you're celebrating thirty-odd today.Your parents wish you, from their hearts, that nowyour happy new days flow along their way!

Valpovo, 12. 11. 2003.

Valpovo, November 12, 2003

Translator's note. For his daughter Suzana.
