Tamburaški bend „Vječiti osmijeh“

Occasional verse and dedications

The Tambura Band "Vječiti Osmijeh"

Milica mi ovo ispričala!

Milica told me this herself!

U Valpovo tambura me zvalaSvoju ljubav Milanu sam dalaBračno gnijezdo u Valpovu svilaI sa njime tu se udomilaObitelj sam divnu osnovalaDvoje djece s mužem odgajalaNajsretnija u životu bilaKao cura te sam snove snilaTo vam veli iskreno MilicaBivša cura Čamagajevaca

To Valpovo the tambura called me, soI gave my love to Milan, long ago.I wove our marriage nest in Valpovo town,and made my home with him, and settled down.I founded a fine family, and with careI raised two children with my husband there.My life has been as happy as can be;as a girl I dreamt these very dreams, you see.This, honestly, is Milica's word to you,a former girl of Čamagajevci, too.

Ovu knjigu ja Milici dajemNeka bude prijateljstvo našePovezano suprugom MilanomKoji vodi slavne tamburaše

This book I give to Milica with care;may it be for the friendship that we share,linked by her husband, Milan, who's the manwho leads the famous tamburica band.

Valpovo, 17. 08. 2013.

Valpovo, August 17, 2013

Translator's note. The band's name means "Eternal Smile"; it is led by Milan Orešković of Valpovo. Written in a copy of his book Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting) for Milan's wife Milica; the first part is in her voice.
