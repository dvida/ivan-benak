The Anthill

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Gledao sam jednomneko čudno zdanje:na livadi stojiveliko imanje.Ovo nije šala,već istina prava:bila je to kućapravih, vrijednih mrava.

Once I looked upona strange building there:out in a meadow stooda great estate, I swear.I'm not joking, no —this is true, I say:it was the house of realants, who work all day.

U toj kući imašto ti srce želi:raznovrsne hrane,svakojakih roba,hodnika, kuhinjai spavaćih soba.

In that house there's allyour heart could wish for:food of every kind,goods of every sort,hallways, kitchens, andbedrooms by the score.

Svi marljivo radesve do kasne noći –čak i mali mravimajstori su pravi.Kada spavat pođu- eh, kakve li sloge! -Svi zubiće perui malene noge.

All of them work hardtill late in the night;even the little antsare master hands, all right.When it's time for bed— how in step they go! —they all brush their little teethand wash each tiny toe.
