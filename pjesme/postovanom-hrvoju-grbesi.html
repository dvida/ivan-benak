Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovanom Hrvoju Grbeši!

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Esteemed Hrvoje Grbeša!

Ova knjiga: "ZEKINA JE SREĆA",za djecu je ona meni treća.Tu se zeko sad od sreće smije,jer ga ŠARKO uhvatio nije.

This book of mine, called "BUNNY'S LUCK", you see,is the third one for children made by me.Here Bunny's laughing now for sheer delight,since ŠARKO hasn't caught him — he's all right!

Da dječica ne bi bila sama,ovu knjigu nek im čita mama.Kad naštima tata tamburicu,on će djeci svirati pjesmicu.

So that the little ones are not alone,let Mummy read this book to them at home.And when Dad tunes his tamburica, hewill play a little song for them, you see.

Kada djeca legnu prije snova,nek' čitana bude knjiga ova.Djeca će vam divne snove sniti,a ujutro vesela će biti.

When children go to bed, before they rest,let this book here be read to them — that's best.The children will dream lovely dreams for you,and in the morning they'll be merry too.

Pa snivajte i vi divne snove,to Vam želi autor pjesme ove!

So you, too, dream sweet dreams the whole night through:this poem's author wishes it for you!

ŠESNAESTI JE SVIBANJ SADA ZNAJTEDVIJE TISUĆE I JOŠ ČETRNAESTE.

NOW KNOW THAT IT'S THE SIXTEENTH DAY OF MAY,THE YEAR TWO THOUSAND AND FOURTEEN, TODAY.

Translator's note. Bunny's Luck (Zekina sreća) is one of his children's books; Šarko is the dog who chases the bunny.
