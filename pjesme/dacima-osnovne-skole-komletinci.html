Đacima Osnovne škole Komletinci

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Pupils of Komletinci Primary School

Jednog dana usred baresastale se mnoge žabe,da se bore protiv rodeusred ove barske vode.Ja vas molim pročitajte,što u bari žabe rade!?Sastanak su održale,da se riješe rode mlade.Pa ako vam knjiga mojazadovoljstva pruži malo,i meni bi, vjerujte mi,toga časa sreće dalo!

One day, in the middle of a bog,there assembled many a frogto stand up against the stork,right amid the marshy murk.Please, I beg you, read it through:what the marsh frogs get up to!?And they held a meeting, sothat the young stork had to go!So, if this book of mine should bringyou some pleasure, just a touch,then, believe me, at that momentit would gladden me so much!

Poštovani, dragi đaci,divne škole Komletinci,dobar uspjeh sve petice,vama želim, dragi klinci.Više znanja i igranjai autora to veseli.Za vas kuca moje srce,pa dođite u Prkovce!

Honoured pupils, children dear,of Komletinci's lovely school,good results, straight A's all year,I wish you, dear kids, in full!More to know and more to play:that will cheer the author's day;for you all my heart beats true,so come to Prkovci, do!

Valpovo, 30. 10. 2012.

Valpovo, October 30, 2012

Translator's note. Written in a copy of his children's book Žablji sastanak ("The Frogs' Meeting"), given to the Komletinci pupils after they performed at a folk-poetry evening in Prkovci in October 2012.
