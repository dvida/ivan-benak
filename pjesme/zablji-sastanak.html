The Frogs' Meeting

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Sastale se žabeusred barske vode,a tema je bilaborba protiv rode.

Out in the marsh waterthe frogs met to talk,and the topic wasthe fight against the stork.

Travanjskoga danadesilo se ovo,na veliki praznik,na žablje kvakovo.

One April daythis came to pass,on the great feast,the frogs' Croakmas.

Na listu lokvanjaneki žabac stojii kreštavim glasomsve prisutne broji.

On a lily padthere stands a frog,and in a shrill voicecounts all in the bog.

Za sastanak ovajza taj važan forum,utvrdit se moraimaju li kvorum.

For this meeting, then,this important forum,it must be establishedif they have a quorum.

„Otvaram sastanakusred ove vode,a naš je zadatakuništiti rode.“

"Here on the waterI open our talk,and the task before us:to destroy the stork."

Kvak, rege, kvak,sve žabe krekeću.„Mir, tišinu molim,ja galamu neću.“

Croak, ribbit, croak,every frog croaks loud."Order, silence, please!No racket allowed!"

Tada iza trskeneki žabac skoči.„Rodi, rodi hoćupogledat u oči!“

Then from behind the reedsa frog jumps up high:"I want to look the stork,the stork, right in the eye!"

Onako naduvenžablje snage punviče: „Hoću rodida savijem kljun!“

All puffed up like that,full of froggy might,he yells: "I will bendthat stork's beak outright!"

Još je nešto htioponosno da doda,ali u tom časupojavi se roda.

One more thing he wantedproudly to say,but at that momentthe stork came their way.

Oh, kakve li strke,kakvoga dar-mara,k'o u vrelom kotluzavrila je bara!

Oh, what a scramble,what a to-do!Like a boiling cauldronthe pond boiled through!

Bježe, skaču žabe,puca suha trska,žuti list se svija,svuda voda prska.

Frogs flee and leap,the dry reeds snap,the yellow leaf curls,the water splashes, slap!

Odtad više žabljihsastanaka nema.Samo stari žabacna lokvanju drijema.

Since that day, frog meetingsare nowhere to be seen;only the old frog dozeson his lily pad green.

Translator's note. Kvakovo, the frogs' "great holiday", is a made-up feast named after the frog's croak (kvak), in the style of Croatian feast names such as Petrovo; "Croakmas" imitates the joke.
