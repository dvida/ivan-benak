Promocija knjige «Duge noći bez sna»

Occasional verse and dedications

The Launch of the Book «Long Nights Without Sleep»

Poštovanoj gospodi Paljetak:slavnom Luku i Anamariji.Ovaj pozdrav časni je početak,na sadašnjoj divnoj promociji.

To you, esteemed Paljetaks, I say,to famous Luko and to Anamarija:this greeting is an honoured start todayat this, the lovely launch we're holding here.

Promocija Belišću je dika:«Duge noći bez sna» divna knjiga.Za taj prikaz čast nam je velikašto je doš'o i Pater Rudika.

This launch is Belišće's great pride, and look:«Long Nights Without Sleep», a lovely book!We're greatly honoured at this launch, it's true,that Pater Rudika has come here too.

A posebno Frani Budić hvala,za tu knjigu što mu sudba dala.I onima što su pomoć dalida bi ovu knjigu pročitali.

And special thanks to Frano Budić, then,for this book destiny gave to his pen;and all who helped — the thanks are theirs indeed —so that this book was here for us to read.

Valpovo, 10. 4. 2013.

Valpovo, April 10, 2013

Translator's note. A greeting at the Belišće launch of Frano Budić's book Duge noći bez sna; Luko Paljetak is one of Croatia's best-known poets.
