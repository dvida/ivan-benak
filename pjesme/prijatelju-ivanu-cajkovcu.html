Prijatelju Ivanu Čajkovcu

Occasional verse and dedications

To My Friend Ivan Čajkovac

Poštovani prijatelju,hvala što si knjigu dao!Zbog ljepote sadržaja,sve sam pjesme pročitao.

My respected friend, thank youfor the book you gave to me!For the beauty of its contentsI've read all its poems, you see.

Za dobroga pjesnika sam,ja za Tebe davno znao,zato sam Ti dragi, Ivo,sa ponosom knjige dao.

I have known for long that youare a poet, good and true;that is why, dear Ivo, proudlyI have given my books to you.

"ZEKINU ti SREĆU" davno,mali zeko je donio,a "BEĆARAC VALPOVAČKI",rado sam Ti poklonio.

Long ago a little bunnybrought "BUNNY'S LUCK" along to you,and "THE VALPOVO BEĆARAC"I was glad to give you too.

"ODJEK SRCA", jednog dana,do Tebe je odjeknuo.A "STIHOM KROZ ŽIVOT", Ivo,od mene si sam odnio.

"ECHO OF THE HEART", one day,echoed all the way to you,and "THROUGH LIFE IN VERSE", dear Ivo,you yourself took home, it's true.

Jedanput sam usred bare,na "SASTANKU ŽABA" bio,a zapisnik sa tog skupa,još Ti nisam dostavio.

Once I sat amid a marshat "THE FROGS' MEETING" there,but the minutes of that sessionI've not sent you yet, I swear.

Na skupu je utvrđeno:"SASTANAK ti ŽABA" falii najdraže meni knjige,moj prvijenac: "SNOVI MALI".

At the meeting it was notedyou lack "THE FROGS' MEETING", it seems,and the book I love the dearest,my first-born: "LITTLE DREAMS".

"MALIH SNOVA" više nemam.Da bih propust ispravio,a "SASTANAK mojih ŽABA",sada bih Ti poklonio.

"LITTLE DREAMS" I have no more.So, to set the slip aright,"THE MEETING of my FROGS"I would give you now outright.

Valpovo, 10. 01. 2011.

Valpovo, January 10, 2011

Translator's note. Ivan Čajkovac was a poet from Prkovci. The titles in capitals are Benak's own books, which he plays on: Zekina sreća (Bunny's Luck), Valpovački bećarac, Odjeci srca (Echoes of the Heart), Stihom kroz život (Through Life in Verse), Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting) and his first, Mali snovi (Little Dreams).
