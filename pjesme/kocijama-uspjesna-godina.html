Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Kočijama uspješna godina

Occasional verse and dedications

A Successful Year to Kočije

Sretan Božić i Novu Godinu!U svom radu uspjeha imalitamburicom tu divnu milinuslušateljstvu svu ljepotu dali!

A Happy Christmas and a glad New Year!May all your work be crowned with good success,and with the tamburica's sweet delightmay you give listeners all its loveliness!

Za sva djela što ste učinili,tamburicom u godini ovojja vam želim uspjeha još više,ostvarili u godini novoj!

For all the things that you've accomplished herewith tamburica in this passing year,I wish you even more success to come,achieved in the new year that's drawing near!

Nastupima ovjenčali slavujer slavonci svi KOČIJE znaju.Ja vam želim pjesmu onu pravu:„ŠOKADIJO MOJ VOLJENI KRAJU!“

With your performances you've crowned your fame,for all Slavonians know Kočije's name.I wish you that true song, the one so fine:“Šokadija, Beloved Land of Mine!”

Pratili vas svuda FESTIVALIi na njima uspjehe nizali.Pa ARANŽMAN ŠOKADIJI dalisa tom pjesmom dično nastupali.

May festivals go with you everywhere,and may you string successes one by one;you gave “Šokadija” its arrangement,and proudly played that song, as you have done.

Svaku krizu sretno savladalii KOČIJE časno nastupali.Na tamburi žice naštimali,Slavoniji svu ljepotu dali.

May every crisis happily be passed,may Kočije perform with honour, too;you've tuned the strings upon the tamburaand given Slavonia beauty through and through.

Ona pjesma što sam napisao,izvedena od Oreškovića,da će biti divna, to sam znao,sa tamburom izvedena priča.

That song of mine, the one that I had penned,which Orešković has now performed and played,I knew that it would be a lovely one:a story on the tambura conveyed.

Sve najbolje vama sada želim,vašem radu i ja se veselim.Uspješna vam bila godinica:od srca vam to želi Ivica!

I wish you all the very best today,and in your work I too rejoice, I say.May this dear little year go well for you:so wishes Ivica, with heart that's true.

Valpovo, 19.11.2014.

Valpovo, November 19, 2014

Translator's note. Christmas and New Year wishes to Kočije (“Carriages”), a tamburica band from Nard, near Valpovo. Ivica is a familiar form of Ivan, the poet's own name.
