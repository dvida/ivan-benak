Radio burza

Bemix bicikl za noge male

Radio market

A Bemix Bike for Little Legs

Za male klince i klinceze,nudim bicikl, a ne friteze.

For little boys and little girls, I say,I'm selling a bike, not a deep-fryer, today.

Bicikl dajem sada bez šalevozilo pravo ga klince male.

No joke: I'm letting this bicycle go —a proper ride for little kids, you know.

BEMIX bicikl prodajem sada,za male sportaše, prava nada.

A Bemix bicycle I'm selling now —for little sportsmen, a real hope, and how!

To je bicikl čuvene marke,do deset ljeta za noge jake.

It is a bike of a famous make, it's true,for strong young legs up to age ten, it'll do.

Prodajna cijena njemu je puna,samo za sto pedeset kuna.

I'll name the asking price in full outright:it's just a hundred and fifty kuna — right!

I ta vam je cijena bez problema,jeftinije vjeruj, ni PEVEC nema.Javite se molim, u čas sretan,na telefon: 6 5 2 8 2 1

And that price, too, will pose you no concern —not even Pevec's cheaper, you will learn.Please call me — may the hour be a lucky one —on the phone: 6 5 2 8 2 1

Valpovo, 23. 04. 2005.

Valpovo, April 23, 2005

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. Pevec was a Croatian chain of household and DIY stores known for low prices; the phone number was his own.
