Occasional verse and dedications

Valpovački Dvori

U restoranu lijepom"VALPOVAČKI DVORI",jednog ljetnog danaplavušu sam sreo,dok sam toplu, crnuekspres kavu pio.Bila je to java, ne,ja nisam snio.

In the lovely restaurant,"Valpovački Dvori",one summer's dayI met a blonde girl there,while I drank my coffee,espresso, black and hot.It was real, and no,a dream it was not.

Ne zadugo potom,svatovi su bili.U restoranu krasnom,"VALPOVAČKI DVORI."Vjenčalo se dvoje,zaljubljeni, mili.Za nagradu oni,traku su dobili.

Not so long after,wedding guests came throughto the splendid restaurant,"Valpovački Dvori".Two were wed, a pairof sweethearts, dear and true,and for a rewardthey got a tape, too.

Sada sretno plove,svojoj zlatnoj zorii video trakugledati će moći,što su njima dali,"VALPOVAČKI DVORI",kao uspomenu,u svadbenoj noći.

Now they sail, so happy,to their golden dawn,and the video tapethey'll be free to view,the one that was givenby "Valpovački Dvori"as a keepsake oftheir wedding night, for two.

Valpovo, 06. 05. 2001.

Valpovo, May 6, 2001

Translator's note. Valpovački Dvori ("Valpovo Courts") is a restaurant in Valpovo; the couple met there, held their wedding there, and were given the wedding video as a present.
