Don't Say You Love Me

Ne reci da me voliš,to govore oči tvoje,dok srce tvoje meni,ljubavnu pjesmu poje.

Do not say you love me,for your eyes say it, dear,while your heart is singinglove songs in my ear.

Ne reci da me voliš,to lebdi zrakom ozona,to srce tvoje odzvanja,s tisuću čarobnih zvona.

Do not say you love me,it floats on the ozone air,it's your heart resoundingwith a thousand magic bells there.

Valpovo, 11. 02. 1990.

Valpovo, February 11, 1990

Translator's note. "Ozone" is the old popular word for fresh, clean air.
