Obiteljski kutak

Andrejice unučice

Family corner

Little Andreja, Granddaughter Dear

Donijela ti ova Nova,radno mjesto tvojih snova.Da ti bude tuge manjei novčano bolje stanje.Vesela ti bila nova,u prilogu mala lova.Sa voljenim sretna bilai najljepše snove snila.Pratila te sreća svaka,to ti žele djed i baka!

May this New Year bring your waythe job you've dreamt of night and day,so your sorrows may be fewand your finances better too.Merry New Year, be it so;here, enclosed, a little dough.Happy with the one you love,dreaming dreams you're fondest of.May all happiness attend you:that's the wish your grandparents send you!

Valpovo, 13. 12. 2005.

Valpovo, December 13, 2005

Translator's note. A New Year's verse sent with a money gift.
