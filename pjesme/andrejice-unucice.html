Obiteljski kutak

Andrejice unučice

Family corner

Little Andreja, Granddaughter Dear

Donijela ti ova Nova,radno mjesto tvojih snova.Da ti bude tuge manjei novčano bolje stanje.Vesela ti bila nova,u prilogu mala lova.Sa voljenim sretna bilai najljepše snove snila.Pratila te sreća svaka,to ti žele djed i baka!

May this New Year bring your waythe job you dream of night and day.May your sorrows be but few,and your purse grow fuller too.May your New Year merry stay;here's some cash enclosed today.Happy with the one you love,dreaming dreams you're fondest of.May all luck be yours each year:so wish Grandpa and Grandma, dear!

Valpovo, 13. 12. 2005.

Valpovo, December 13, 2005

Translator's note. A New Year's verse sent with a money gift.
