Obiteljski kutak

Najdražima ja darujem dušu

Family corner

To My Dearest I Give My Soul

Prošao je život kao jekai odnio dio naših snova.Najdražima, obitelji mojoj,nek' im radost pruži knjiga ova.

My life has passed, as echoes pass away,and carried off a part of all our dreams.For those I love the most, my family,may this, my book, bring joy to all of them.

I stihovi ove divne knjige,od mene su sretno iznjedreni,za široku javnost namijenjeni,obitelji Benak poklonjeni.

And all the verses of this lovely book,which I have happily brought forth to light,intended for the public, far and wide,are gifted to the Benak family.

Da bi mogli sreću uživati,ova knjiga najdraža Vam bila.Daruje Vam onaj tko Vas voli.Vaša srca sretne snove snila.

So that you may enjoy your happiness,may this book be the dearest one to you;it's given by the one who loves you all.May happy dreams be dreamt by your hearts too.

Mome sinu, snahi i unuci,kada žele dati radost duši,ovu knjigu držati u rucinjena djela da im radost pruži.

For my son, his wife, and granddaughter, whenthey wish to bring their souls some joy: to holdthis book of mine within their hands, and thento let the works in it give them delight.

Uživali uvijek divne snove,želi autor drage knjige ove.

May lovely dreams be yours through every year:so wishes the author of this book so dear.

Valpovo, 15. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 15, 2010

Translator's note. A dedication to his son's family in Kitišanci, written in a copy of one of his books.
