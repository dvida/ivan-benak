The Little Saver

Kupio tatagipsano praseza maloga Mišu,pravog štedišu.

Dad went out and boughta plaster piggy bankfor little Mišo,a saver of first rank.

S rupom na leđima,na četiri točkada novac skupljak'o piliće kvočka.

With a slot upon its back,on four wheels it stood,to gather money uplike a hen her brood.

Po sobi Mišoprase kotura,kovane kuneu njega tura.

Round and round the roomMišo rolls the pig,and pushes kuna coinsin, both small and big.

Ne skiči praseveć zvecka muklo,od silne težinebrzo je puklo!

The piggy doesn't squeal,it gives a muffled clink,and from the mighty weightit burst, quick as a wink!

U sobi zablista,k'o srebro pravo,uzvikne Mišo,od sreće: „BRAVO!“

The room was all a-sparkle,like real silver, all aglow,and Mišo, full of joy,cried out loud: "Bravo!"

Za čas proradiMišina mašta,pred njim se stvorisvega i svašta.

In a flash young Mišo'sfancy came alive,and all sorts of things before himsuddenly arrive.

„Veliki sladoledkupit ću sebiod deset kugli –pa zašto ne bih?

"I'll buy myself an ice cream,a great big one, oh my,with ten scoops on it —and why shouldn't I?

Čokoladu, bombonarazne vrste –uh, što ću mljackati lizat prste!“

"Chocolate, and candyof every kind I've seen —oh, how I'll munch and smackand lick my fingers clean!"

Translator's note. The kuna was Croatia's currency until 2023.
