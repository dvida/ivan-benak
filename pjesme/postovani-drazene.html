Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani Dražene!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Dražen!

Prijatelju i pjesniče!Ja za dobro,uvijek dobrim plaćam,za Tvoju knjigu,svojom dug Ti vraćam!

My friend and poet!For good, you'll find,I always pay in kind:so for your book,with mine I pay my debt!

Ova knjiga Ti pripada,dva'jst devetog listopada,u Soljani nakon fešte,dv'je tisuće i trinaeste.

On October twenty-ninethis book's yours, no longer mine;in Soljani, after the fête,two thousand and thirteen's the date.

Translator's note. Written in a copy of Žablji sastanak (The Frogs' Meeting) for Dražen Strukar, a poet from Cerna, in return for a book of his.
