Humorous poems

AIDS

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Vječna si mi želja bila,od djetinjstva sve do sada.Oj, djevojko, moja mila,hoću li te imat' kada?

You've been all I've wished for, dear,since my childhood, to this day;oh, my darling girl, now hear:will you ever come my way?

Nešto ću ti, mala, reći,otvoreno nema stida.Sa tobom sam htio leći,sad se bojim, vlada sida.

Something, lass, I'll tell you true,openly, with no charades:I have longed to lie with you;now I fear the reign of AIDS.

Dok u ono doba đačko,dao bih ti svoga vida.Gdje si do sad bila mačko?Sada neću, kad je sida.

Back in school, when I was smitten,you'd have had my eyes, as love persuades.Where've you been till now, my kitten?Now I won't, for now there's AIDS.

Za grudima tvojim, draga,meni želja srce kida.Ti sad hoćeš, al' ja neću,jer u svijetu vlada sida.

For your bosom, oh my darling,longing tears my heart and never fades.Now you'd like to, but I won't,for the world is ruled by AIDS.

I na kraju ostale susamo puste želje moje,jer zbog ove strašne side,ne uživah tijelo tvoje.

So, when all is said, what's leftis my empty longing, dear;all because of dreadful AIDS,I've not held your body near.

Valpovo, 14. 06. 1987.

Valpovo, June 14, 1987

Translator's note. Sida is the Croatian word for AIDS; the poem dates from 1987.
