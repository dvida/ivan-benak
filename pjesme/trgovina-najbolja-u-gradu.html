Occasional verse and dedications

The Best Shop in Town

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

„MEGASAN“ je u Valpovu gradu,u ulici iduć prema Nardu.

"Megasan" is in the town of Valpovo,along the street that leads to Nard, you know.

Braće Radić ulica se zove,lijepe zgrade, izgrađene nove.

The street is named Braće Radić, you see,with lovely buildings, new as new can be.

„MEGASAN“ je na usluzi svima,što poželiš, svega u njem' ima.

"Megasan" is at everybody's call;whatever you might wish, they have it all.

Da bi mogli kupci kupovati,radnja radi do dvadeset sati.

So shoppers can come shopping, as they might,the shop stays open until eight at night.

Trgovci su uslužni, veseli,tu ćeš naći što ti srce želi.

The staff are helpful, cheerful, and you'll findhere all the things your heart may have in mind.

Sva je roba jeftina i fina,još na popust, pa to je milina.

All goods are cheap and fine, and then, what's more,they're on discount: a sheer delight in store!

Valpovo, 30. 11. 2009.

Valpovo, November 30, 2009

Translator's note. A verse advertisement for the Megasan store in Valpovo. Nard is the neighbouring village.
