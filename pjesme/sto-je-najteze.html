What Is Heaviest?

Najteže na svijetu što je?Imali u školi temu.Pa sad djeca raspravljaju,baš o svemu i svačemu.

What's the heaviest thing on earth?That was set for class one day,and the children argue itevery which and every way.

"Željezo je", veli Sanja."Ne blebeći mala sovo!",važnim glasom veli Denis:"Najteže je baš olovo!"

"It is iron," Sanja says."Stop your babbling, little owl!"Denis says, all self-important,"Lead's the heaviest of all!"

Zatim reče bucko Stevo,krupnim glasom on zareži:"Molim fino nastavnice,kažem da je žar najteži."

Chubby Stevo then speaks up,growling in a gruff, deep tone:"Please, Miss, if I may, I sayembers are the heaviest known."

Tad Ivica mali reče:"Nema ništa u toj priči,najteže je valjda ono,što ni Bog ne može dići."

Little Ivica then said:"None of that is right, you see:heaviest, I'd guess, must bewhat not even God can lift."

"Prosudite molim sami,o čemu se ovdje radi.""Ma ne petljaj", veli mama,"daj već jednom ga usadi!"

"Judge yourselves now, if you please,what it is that's going on.""Oh, stop fumbling," says my mum,"stick it in and get it done!"

Škripi krevet, dahće tata,al' sve čuti nisam stig'o.A onda je rekla mama:"Ma ni Bog ga ne bi digo!"

The bed creaks, Dad huffs and puffs,but I didn't catch enough;then my mum said, loud and clear:"Not even God could get it up!"

Valpovo, 02. 06. 2004.

Valpovo, June 2, 2004

Translator's note. The last line plays on Ivica's answer: the heaviest thing is what not even God can lift.
