My Prija

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Noć je tiha. Mjesec sija.U goste mi došla prija.Ej radosti, što da radim?Moram brzi da izvadimRakijicu, šljivovicuda počastim priju Micu.

Night is quiet. The moon is bright.My prija's come to call tonight.Oh, what joy! What shall I do?Quick, I've got to fetch the brew:a little rakija of plumto treat Prija Mica, who's come.

Nakon druge, treće čaše,jezici se razvezaše.Sve smo lijepo ispričali,o rodbini što smo znalii novosti koje ima,što se priča u gostima.

By the second glass, the third,tongues were loosened, word by word.All of it we talked right through:all about our kin we knew,and whatever news was out,what the neighbours talk about.

Bilo je to baš veselo,pretresli smo cijelo selo.

It was merry, what a treat:we picked the village, street by street.

Negdje oko dva, tri sata,gledam mjesec zvijezdu hvata.Sve razmišlja tako i ja,možda želi to i prija.

Round two or three o'clock, I spythe moon catch a star in the sky,and so I keep thinking, it's true:maybe my prija wants it too.

Voli prija, volim i ja,ova noć je fantazija.

My prija loves it, and I do too:this night's a fantasy come true.

Otišla kući draga prijaZaš'o mjesec zasp'o i ja.

My dear prija went home at last;the moon went down, I fell asleep fast.

Ej mjeseče, zvijezdo sjajna,nek to bude naša tajna!

Hey there, moon, and star so bright,let this be our secret, right?

Valpovo, 17.07.1997.

Valpovo, July 17, 1997

Translator's note. A prija is the mother of one's child's spouse, a relationship Croatian names with its own word.
