What Makes You Sleep Sweetly

ŠKOLSKA NASTAVA

In the Classroom

Imat ćemo danas temu,to će biti radost prava:"Recite mi sada, djeco,od čega se slatko spava?"

Here's the topic for today —it will be a real delight:"Tell me now, my children, whatmakes you sleep so sweet at night?"

"Reći ću vam nastavnice,meni kad sam bio mali",kaže Stevo očalinko,"čaj su mi od maka dali."

"I'll tell you, Miss," says four-eyed Stevo,"when I was a little lad,poppy tea is what they gave me —that's the tea I always had."

Baš su sinoć o toj temi,raspravljale moje tete,pa su onda jasno rekle,da ne mogu bez tablete.

Just last night my aunties arguedthis same topic, and they saidvery plainly that they can'tdo without a pill in bed.

"Od bibera, nastavnice,to vam mogu dokazati.Sinoć moji kad su legli,počeli su se maziti."

"It's the pepper, Miss, that does it —I can prove it to you, too:last night, when my folks lay down,they began to kiss and coo."

Onda reče tata mami,to morate sada znati:"Da ti, srce, zabiberim,pa ćeš slatko ti spavati."

Then my dad said to my mum —this is something you should hear —"Let me spice you up with pepper,then you'll sleep so sweetly, dear."

Valpovo, 25. 09. 2004.

Valpovo, September 25, 2004

Translator's note. Poppy-seed tea was a folk remedy to put children to sleep. "Zabiberiti", to pepper something, is folk slang for making love.
