Protestne i društvene

Vojnikova majka

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Protest and society

The Soldier's Mother

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Muško sam čedo rodila,njegovala ga i mazila.Nad njim bdila i strepila,u igri njegovoj,uz osmijeh njegovsretna sam bila.

I gave birth to a baby boy,nursed him and cuddled him.I watched over him and trembled,in his games,in his smile,I was happy.

Tisuću devetstodevedeset druge,U selo je došlagrupa vojnika.Gordo je stao uz Satnika.Pogledom sam ga ispratilai suze lila,suze tuge i radosniceza mladog vojnika.

In nineteen hundredand ninety-twothere came to the villagea group of soldiers.He stood proudly beside the Captain.I followed him with my eyesand shed tears,tears of sorrow and of joyfor the young soldier.

Jednog dana dođe,tužna vijest iz daljina,o smrti moga sina.

Then one day there camesad news from far awayof my son's death.

Dok sam ga u mislima,na posljednji počinak ispraćala.Rane me njegove pekle,u bolnim grudimaćutala sam metke.

As in my thoughtsI walked him to his final rest,his wounds were burning me;in my aching breastI felt the bullets.

Ubiše mi sina,od osamnaest godina.Život je izgubio u boju,braneći domovinu svoju.

They killed my son,eighteen years old.He lost his life in battle,defending his homeland.

Da odnijeti mogustručak cvijeća svježi.Barem da znademgdje mu tijelo leži.

If only I could bringa posy of fresh flowers.If only I knewwhere his body lies.

Iskreno te, Gospo,ja za pomoć molim,pomozi da nađemsina koga volim.

Our Lady, with all my heartI pray for help from above:help me, that I may findthe son I love.

Valpovo, 20. 05. 1998.

Valpovo, May 20, 1998

Translator's note. Written in 1998 about the Croatian War of Independence (1991–95), which Croatians call the Homeland War. Many families never learned where their sons were buried.
