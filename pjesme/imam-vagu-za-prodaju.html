Radio burza

Imam vagu za prodaju

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

I Have a Scale for Sale

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Sinoć meni moja žena kaže:"imaš vagu dvjesto kila važe.Dobra vaga, ne škripi, ne lupai utege podaj sa njom skupa."Prodaj vagu makar za šest stoja,tako meni veli žena moja.Kad sam čuo što mi žena kaže,tu mi drugi zakon ne pomaže,već da oglas dajem na sva zvonai napišem broj mog telefona.Da ne budem žalostan ni sjetan:652 - osamsto dvadeset jedan,to brojevi mog su brzoglasa,nek' se oglas do kupca talasa.

Last night my wife, she says to me, she says:"You've got a scale — two hundred kilos it weighs.A good scale, and it won't creak or clatter, true,and give the weights along with it, won't you?""Just sell the scale, though six hundred's low" —that's what my wife keeps telling me, you know.When I had heard the words my wife had said,no other law would help me out; insteadI had to ring the ad out on every belland write my telephone number down as well.And so that I won't be sad or woebegone:652 - eight hundred twenty-one,those are the numbers that my phone goes by —let this ad ripple out to someone who'll buy.

Valpovo, 09. 09. 2000.

Valpovo, September 9, 2000

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad. "Six hundred" is presumably kuna.
