Love poems

My Girl

Moja je djevojka divna,vedra k'o sunčan dan.Za njom mi srce tučei sniva ljubavni san.

My girl is so delightful,bright as the sun above;for her my heart is poundingand dreams a dream of love.

Usne joj crvena trešnja,a lice trešnjin cvijet.Kad ljubim usne njene,kao da pijem med.

Her lips are a red cherry,her face, a cherry flower;and when I kiss her lips,it's like drinking honey.

Kao badem oči njene,sa njom su mi vedri dani.A umiljat pogled njezin,mora svakog da rani.

Her eyes are like almonds,with her my days are clear;and that sweet gaze of hersmust wound all who come near.

Tek nosić njezin mali,licu je divan broš,kada me s njime takne,ja sretan želim „još“.

And her little nose, so lovely,is her face's brooch; and whenwith it she just touches me,happy, I wish: "Again!"

Kosa joj mirisna, plava,a vlasi duge i čvrste,meni je najljepše onda,kad stavim u njih prste.

Her hair is fragrant, golden,its strands long, strong and fine;and for me it's loveliestwhen my fingers in it twine.

Slavuja pjesme pojumiljat ima glas.Kao vile bajnevitak je njezin stas.

Like the nightingale's warbling,sweet and winning is her tone;and her figure is as slenderas a fabled fairy's own.

Valpovo, 1975.
