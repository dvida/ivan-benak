Violets

Kao dio neba,kao oči tvoje,ljubičice plavekraj puteljka stoje.

Like a piece of the skyand like your eyes, too,by the little path standthe violets of blue.

Sjećaš se, mila,kada sam ih brao,uz poljupce slatke,tebi darovao?

Do you still remember,dear, when I picked a fewand with sweet kissesmade a gift of them to you?

Valpovo, 1982.

Valpovo, 1982
