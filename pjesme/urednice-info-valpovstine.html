Protestne i društvene

Uredniče Info Valpovštine

Protest and society

To the Editor of INFO Valpovštine

Dvadeset i šestog svibnjasedamdeseti broj INFA.

On the twenty-sixth of MayINFO's seventieth came our way.

Pjesmu mi je objavioi potpuno uništio.

It printed a poem of mine,and it wrecked it, line by line.

Ja Vas pitam Uredniče,što se moje pjesme tiče.

Editor, I ask you — pray,as concerns my poem, say:

Što ste s time Vi dobili,da ste pjesmu uništili.

What was it you gained thereby,wrecking my poem — tell me why?

Za nečasnu tu rabotu,to je Vama na sramotu.

Such dishonest work, I claim,brings on you nothing but shame.

Niste mene povrijedili,već svoj INFO ponizili.

It's not me that you've brought low:you have shamed your own INFO.

Ja ću dalje stih pisatii ljepotu puku dati.

I'll keep writing verse, it's true,bringing beauty to the people, too.

Valpovo, 27. 05. 2011.

Valpovo, May 27, 2011

Translator's note. A verse complaint to the editor of the local paper INFO Valpovštine, whose issue no. 70 (May 26, 2011) printed one of his poems in mangled form.
