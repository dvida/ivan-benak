The Wasp

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Kad proljeće svanei kad sunce grane,ne samo pčela,već i osa slijećena mirisno cvijeće.

When spring's day is breaking,when the sun is waking,not only the beebut the wasp, too, settleson the fragrant flowers.

Jednog ljetnog danapojavi se ona.

One summer's dayshe came my way.

Oh, koliko sreće!

Oh, such happiness!

Kao zumbul plavimiriše joj kosa.Ja sletoh na cvjetaki postadoh osa.

Her hair has the scentof a hyacinth blue;I lit on the blossom,and a wasp I grew.

U naručju njenom,sada snivam snove.Uz poljupce slatkemrsim njenu kosu.Tu predivnu ženu,žarko ljubit smijem.

In her embracenow I dream my dreams,with sweet kissesI tousle her hair;that wonderful womanI may kiss with ardour.

Sa usana njenih,sa tog slatkog cvijeta,sada nektar pijem.

From her lips,from that sweet flower,now I drink the nectar.

Oh, sretnog li mene,što sam osa bio?

Oh, how lucky am Ithat I was a wasp?

Valpovo, 1979.

Valpovo, 1979
