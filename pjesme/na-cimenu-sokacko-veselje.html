Domoljubne i zavičajne

Na Čimenu Šokačko veselje

Homeland and native region

Šokac Merriment on the Čimen

U Mar'jance dođi meni lolona šesnaesto druženje folklorakada igra na Čimenu kolo,ta ljepota doživjet se mora.

Come to me in Mar'janci, my lolo,to the sixteenth folklore gathering here,when upon the Čimen spins the kolo:such a beauty must be lived, my dear.

Tu se skuplja Šokadija mladabiser naše Slavonije ravneda prikažu svu ljepotu sadauspomene iz prošlosti davne.

Here the young Šokadija comes today,pearl of our Slavonia's level plain,setting all its beauty on display,bringing distant memories back again.

Kad povorka kroz Mar'jance prođekolo pletu mladi folkloraši,pa na Čimen kada glazba dođe,svu ljepotu daju tamburaši.

When the procession through Mar'janci goes,the young folk dancers weave the kolo's ring,and when to the Čimen the music flows,tambura players all their beauty bring.

Na Čimenu kad tambure ječeto se zove Šokačko veseljekoje pruža radosti i srećemarijanačkog KUD-a to su želje.

When on the Čimen the tamburas sound,that is Šokac merriment by name,giving joy and happiness all round:that's the Marijanci KUD's wish and aim.

To je dio zemaljskoga raja,tako kažu svi gosti i znanci.To što čine Šokci ovoga krajaKunišinci i selo Mar'janci.

This is part of earthly paradise;all our guests and friends say this is true:it's what Šokci of this land devisein Kunišinci and Mar'janci too.

Ljepše smotre nadaleko nemakao što je na Čimenu kolood zapada pa do ravnoga Srijema,zato dođi u Mar'jance lolo.

Far and wide no finer show is foundthan the Čimen, where they dance the kolo,from the west to Srijem's level ground:so come to Mar'janci, my lolo.

Valpovo, 17. 05. 2012.

Valpovo, May 17, 2012

Translator's note. Written for the sixteenth folklore festival on the Čimen, the village green at Marijanci, organised by its KUD, the village folklore society. He calls his listener "lolo", an old word for a sweetheart.
