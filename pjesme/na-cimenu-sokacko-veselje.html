Domoljubne i zavičajne

Na Čimenu Šokačko veselje

Homeland and native region

Šokac Merriment on the Čimen

U Mar'jance dođi meni lolona šesnaesto druženje folklorakada igra na Čimenu kolo,ta ljepota doživjet se mora.

My darling, come to Mar'janci, to me,to where the sixteenth folklore gathering's held,when on the Čimen the kolo dances free:such beauty must be lived through, must be felt.

Tu se skuplja Šokadija mladabiser naše Slavonije ravneda prikažu svu ljepotu sadauspomene iz prošlosti davne.

Here gathers all the young Šokadija,the pearl of our Slavonia's level plain,to show us all its beauty now, and bringthe memories of distant times again.

Kad povorka kroz Mar'jance prođekolo pletu mladi folkloraši,pa na Čimen kada glazba dođe,svu ljepotu daju tamburaši.

When through Mar'janci the procession goes,the young folk dancers weave the kolo's ring,and when the music to the Čimen flows,the tambura players all their beauty bring.

Na Čimenu kad tambure ječeto se zove Šokačko veseljekoje pruža radosti i srećemarijanačkog KUD-a to su želje.

When on the Čimen the tamburas resound,that's Šokac merriment, that's what it's called,which gives out joy and happiness all round:such is the Marijanci KUD's wish for all.

To je dio zemaljskoga raja,tako kažu svi gosti i znanci.To što čine Šokci ovoga krajaKunišinci i selo Mar'janci.

This is a corner of earth's paradise,so all the guests and all our friends agree:the work the Šokci of this land devise,Kunišinci and the village Mar'janci.

Ljepše smotre nadaleko nemakao što je na Čimenu kolood zapada pa do ravnoga Srijema,zato dođi u Mar'jance lolo.

There is no finer festival aroundto match the kolo on the Čimen here,from the far west to Srijem's level ground —so come to Mar'janci, my darling dear.

Valpovo, 17. 05. 2012.

Valpovo, May 17, 2012

Translator's note. Written for the sixteenth folklore festival on the Čimen, the village green at Marijanci, organised by its KUD, the village folklore society. He calls his listener "lolo", an old word for a sweetheart.
