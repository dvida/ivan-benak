Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Matici umirovljenika Satnica

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Occasional verse and dedications

To the Satnica Pensioners' Association

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

POŠTOVANO PREDSJEDNIŠTVOI POŠTOVANA GENERACIJO

Esteemed Presidencyand Esteemed Generation

Vama žele topli pozdrav,ostvarenje vaših snovai uspješni rad skupštine:Delegati iz Valpova!

Warmest greetings they wish you,and that all your dreams come true,and a fruitful session, too:Delegates from Valpovo!

Za desetu obljetnicu,uspješnoga vašeg rada,sa ponosom sve čestitke:Valpovačkih delegata!

For your tenth anniversary,which your fine work celebrates,come with pride congratulationsfrom the Valpovo delegates!

U daljnjemu vašem radu,nek' vas vodi nova Vlada,sa uspjehom, to je želja:Valpovačkih delegata!

In the work that now awaits,may a new Government guide youto success — that is the wishof the Valpovo delegates!

S udrugama međusobno,u veselju sportskog rada,družili se, to je želja:Valpovačkih delegata!

With the other clubs, as mates,in the joy of sporting play,may you mingle — that's the wishof the Valpovo delegates!

Mirovina dosadašnja,nek' vam bude veća, nova.Dobro zdravlje žele vama:Delegati iz Valpova!

May the pension you have nowgrow into a bigger one.Good health they wish all of you:Delegates from Valpovo!

Mnogo ljeta poživjeli,ne tražite RAJSKA VRATA!Iskrene su ovo želje:Valpovačkih delegata!

Many years may you live on —don't go seeking heaven's gates!These are the sincere good wishesof the Valpovo delegates!

Valpovo, 08. 05. 2009.

Valpovo, May 8, 2009

Translator's note. A greeting from the Valpovo delegates to the tenth-anniversary assembly of the pensioners' association in Satnica. He calls its committee a “Government”.
