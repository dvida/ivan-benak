Prigodne pjesme i posvete

Poštovani Josipe!

Occasional verse and dedications

Dear Josip!

Život bez povratkapolako teče,po stazama trnjetek poneko cvijeće.Na Vašemu putu,koji se kreće,imali ljubavi,zdravlja i sreće.Ostvarili svoježivotne snove,želi Vam autorknjižice ove!

Life without returnflows along so slow;thorns along the paths,and few flowers grow.On your own pathway,as onward you go,may love and good healthand luck to you flow.May your dreams of lifebe fulfilled and come true:this booklet's authorwishes this for you!

Valpovo, 23. 08. 2010.

Valpovo, August 23, 2010
