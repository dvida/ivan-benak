Umirovljenički jadi

Čepinskoj udruzi umirovljenika

Pensioners' woes

To the Čepin Pensioners' Association

Topli pozdrav vama svima želimi skupštini vašoj se veselim.

To all of you warm greetings I extend,and your assembly gladdens me no end.

Slušam divne rezultate vaše,dobar uspjeh i smjernice rada,što Ljubica, predsjednica kaže,ja čestitam svima vama sada.

I hear of all the fine results you've won,your good success, the course your work will take,and what Ljubica, your president, has said;to each of you my compliments I make.

Za sva djela što ste učinilisvojim radom u godini ovoj.Ja vam želim uspjeha još višeostvarili u godini novoj.

For all the deeds that you have carried outthrough all your work within this passing year,I wish you still more triumphs, and that youachieve them in the new year drawing near.

Sve čestitke vašoj predsjednicii ostalim ovdje prisutnima,plodonosni rad skupštini vašoj,ja iskreno želim sada svima.

Congratulations to your presidentand all the others who are present here;and fruitful work to your assembly, too:to all of you I wish it, most sincere.

Osječka ste podružnica bilii po želji njihovoj radili.Odvažna je gospa Tomić bilai nepravdu njihovu odbila.

You were a branch of Osijek's before,and worked the way they wished, no less, no more.But Mrs Tomić showed that she was braveand spurned the unjust treatment that they gave.

Ako treba da se bira Vladagospa Tomić najbolja je sada,svojim radom uvijek revna bila,od Osijeka društvo izbavila.

If there's a Government to choose today,then Mrs Tomić is the best, I say;she always worked with zeal, as all can see,and got the club from Osijek set free.

A skupština u ovome času,nek' podrži tu sadašnju Vladu,jer će ona sa puno elana,vodit društvo u budućem radu.

And let the assembly, at this very hour,stand firmly by the Government in place,for it will lead, with plenty of élan,the club in all its work in coming days.

I pjevački zbor što vam je slavan,jer milinu svojim glasom sije,on je ponos kod udruge vašei nastupa diljem Slavonije.

And then your choir, so famous far and wide,that with its voices sows delight and cheer:of your association it's the pride,and all across Slavonia they appear.

Svojim glasom divne pjesme poje,u matici ima mjesto svoje,a za Čepin posebna je dikai udruzi umirovljenika.

With lovely voices lovely songs they raise,and in the parent union have their place;for Čepin they're a special pride and prize,and for the pensioners' own club likewise.

Svi u zdravlju i veselju bili,pratila vas i radost i sreća,mnogo ljeta penziju primali,makar bila i ponešto veća.

May all of you in health and gladness stay,with joy and happiness to see you through;and draw your pension many a year, I pray —and may it be a little bigger, too!

Nek' vam bude veselo i živo,pozdravlja vas pjesnik Benak Ivo!

So may your days be lively, full of cheer:greetings from Benak Ivo, poet, here!

Valpovo, 04. 05. 2013.

Valpovo, May 4, 2013

Translator's note. A rhymed greeting to the association's annual assembly, after it broke away from the Osijek branch. He playfully calls the association's leadership the "Government" (Vlada).
