The Poni Bike Is All the Rage

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Ovim putem najavljujem svima,bicikl PONI kod mene ima.

I hereby let it be known, far and wide:I've got a Poni bicycle to ride.

Neka naši slušaoci znaju,da je PONI sada za prodaju.

So let our listeners all know this, today:the Poni is for sale now, right away.

Niklovane, sjajne žbice ima,stabilan je, ništa se ne klima.

The spokes are nickel-plated, shining bright;it's steady, nothing wobbles — all is tight.

Bicikl je narančaste boje,sve što treba PONI ima svoje.

The bicycle is orange in its hue;the Poni's got whatever it needs, too.

Paket-treger, blatobrane ima,pa i zvonce da se javi svima.

It has a rack and mudguards, and as well,to greet all passers-by, a little bell.

Dobre gume, napunjene zrakom,ugodan je, svidjet će se svakom.

Good tyres, pumped up with air, and nicely so;it's comfy — you'll all like it, that I know.

Ako sklona bude mi FORTUNA,prodat ću ga ja za dvjesto kuna.

If Fortuna's kind to me, this bike will gofor two hundred kuna — now you know.

Dobar kupac, samo onaj pravi,na telefon neka mi se javi.

A good buyer, just the right one, please —give me a telephone call, at your ease.

Na telefon moj valjan i vrijedan:šest, pet, dva, osamsto dvadeset jedan.

My telephone, reliable and second to none:six, five, two, eight hundred twenty-one.

Valpovo, 07. 05. 2005.

Valpovo, May 7, 2005

Translator's note. A Radio burza verse ad, addressed to the show's listeners. Poni was a popular small-wheeled Yugoslav bicycle.
