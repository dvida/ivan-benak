Radio burza

Bocanjevčanka

Radio market

Bocanjevčanka

„BOCANJEVČANKA“ vam jebiljna apoteka.Za sve bilje i raslinjeona ima lijeka.

"Bocanjevčanka" is for youa pharmacy for plants:for every herb and growing thing,a cure for their complaints.

Trgovine dvije ima,važno je da znate.U Belišću „KOD JABLANA“,u Valpovu „KOD MATE“.

It has two shops, and it's importantthat you all should know:"Kod Jablana" in Belišće,and "Kod Mate" in Valpovo.

Tu se može nabaviti,što ti srce želi.Sva je roba prvoklasna,da te Bog veseli!

Here you can get anythingyour heart may want, I'd say;all the goods are of the best —may God make glad your day!

Fungicida, herbicidai sjemenja, zna se,pa pilića za tovljenje,možeš kupit za se.

Fungicides and herbicides,and seeds, that's understood;and chicks for fattening, which youcan buy for your own good.

Gnojiva su razne vrstei sadnice voća,i trsova sortnog grožđa.Odlična kakvoća!

Fertilisers of all kinds,and saplings of fruit trees,and grapevines, choice varieties.Top quality, all these!

Sve za agrar tu ćeš naći,nećeš požaliti,samo reci svoje želje,poslužen ćeš biti.

Everything for farms is here,and you'll have no regret;just say what you would like to have,and that is what you'll get.

Kupi puno, plati malo,čuvaj novac za se.A to će ti ostvariti:„BOCANJEVČANKA“, zna se!

Buy a lot, and pay a little,keep your money in your purse;and who'll make that come true for you?"Bocanjevčanka", of course!

Valpovo, 08. 04. 2005.

Valpovo, April 8, 2005

Translator's note. A verse advertisement for an agricultural supply shop; its two stores are "Kod Jablana" (At the Poplar) in Belišće and "Kod Mate" (At Mate's) in Valpovo.
