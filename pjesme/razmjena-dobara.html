Radio burza

Razmjena dobara

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Radio market

A Swap of Goods

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Na burzi sam jučer oglas daoi sav krumpir odmah sam prodao.

I placed an ad on the Burza yesterday,and sold all my potatoes straight away.

Prođe jesen ide ljuta zima,a kod mene dobrog zelja ima.

Autumn is passing, bitter winter's near,and I have some good cabbage over here.

Kupus mijenjam za polu od giceil' za suhu šunku od prasice.

I'll swap my cabbage for half a pig, you see,or for a dry-cured ham from a sow, maybe.

Il' za pile, može i zaklano,samo nek' je dobro očupano.

Or for a chicken — slaughtered, that will do,as long as it is plucked well, through and through.

Kad bih kakvog dobrog kupca znao,ja bih kupus i za prase dao.

If I knew of a buyer who was good,I'd give my cabbage for a piglet — yes, I would.

Može pura ili janjetina,to bi bila razmjena baš fina.

A turkey, or some lamb, would do as well:that's a fine swap, as anyone can tell.

Ja bih dao nešto para pridejerbo meso sa kupusom ide.

I'd give a little cash on top, what's more,for meat goes with the cabbage, that's for sure.

Javite se odmah dobri ljudi,jer se kupus za razmjenu nudi.

So call me right away, good people, do:the cabbage is for swapping, here, with you.

Moj brzoglas bit će vrlo vrijedan,kupus nudim samo ovaj tjedan.

My telephone will work hard, so to speak;the cabbage is on offer just this week.

Valpovo, 30.10. 2000.

Valpovo, October 30, 2000

Translator's note. A verse ad for Radio burza: he offers his cabbage in exchange for meat. For the telephone he uses brzoglas ("swift-voice"), an old purist Croatian word, in jest.
