Beneath the Old Linden

Kad bi stara lipaispričati znala,sve poljupce slatkekoje si mi dala.

If the old lindencould tell, if it knew,of all the sweet kissesthat I had from you.

I dok je kroz lišćemjesec zrake sij'o,ljubio sam tebei usne ti pio.

And while through the leavesthe moon sowed its light,I was kissing you,drinking your lips' delight.

A zvijezda je sjajnakroz oblak treptala,dok si meni tihoi sneno šaptala.

A shining star was twinklingthrough a cloud above,while you were whisperingso dreamily, my love.

Volim te, volim,oh, ljubavi mila,s mirisom lipe,sreća se razlila.

I love you, I love you,oh, darling, my dear;with the scent of lindenjoy spread far and near.

Sreća dvaju bićašto su skupa bili,u proljetnoj noćiljubav provodili.

The joy of two beingswho were together there,spending a spring nightin love, a happy pair.

Valpovo, 12. 07. 1974.

Valpovo, July 12, 1974
