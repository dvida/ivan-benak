Ljubavne pjesme

Od pogleda tvoga!

iz knjige Stihom kroz život (2010.)

Love poems

From That Glance of Yours!

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Jednom sam ćutao u grudima boliod pogleda tvoga što bezazlen bješe.Do tad nisam znao da te srce volii dva bića naša sa ljubavlju veže.

Once I felt an aching deep within my breastfrom a glance of yours, so innocent to see;till then I did not know, I never even guessed,my heart loved you and bound in love both you and me.

Jedne tamne noći tužno sam te snio,u oblaku sivom kanda drugom letiš.Od tih bolnih snova u strahu sam bio.Zar se tog pogleda ne možeš da sjetiš?

One dark night I dreamt of you, and it was sad:in a cloud of grey, it seemed, with someone else you flew;I lay in fear from all the painful dreams I had.Can you not recall that glance, or is it lost to you?

Bog ljubavi nama naklonjen je biou zagrljaj meni što te opet vrati.Kada tebe, draga, ne bih ljubit smio,ni sunce na nebu ne bi htjelo sjati.

The god of love looked kindly on us two,and back into my arms he brought you once again;if I, my darling, were not allowed to love you,not even the sun on high would want to shine then.

Tvoja ljubav meni vedre snove vraća,poljupcima vrelim kad mi sklopiš oči.Kada si kraj mene, sjeta mi je kraća,jer znam da me nježno ti ljubiš u noći.

Your love brings back to me my dreams, so bright and clear,when with your burning kisses you close my eyes;my sadness does not last so long when you are near,for I know you kiss me tenderly at night.

Valpovo, 05. 08. 1974.

Valpovo, August 5, 1974
