Memories and reflections

From Childhood to Old Age

Proš'o Božić i godina Nova,odjurio i Valentin sveti,a godine na pleća se nižu,jer nam život kao strijela leti.

Christmas gone, the New Year gone as well,and Saint Valentine has hurried by;years keep piling up upon our shoulders,for our life, just like an arrow, flies.

Kad se sjetim svojega djetinjstvai mladosti što je sretno došla.U životu Amorovih strijela,kroz srce mi na stotine prošla.

When I think back on my childhood daysand my youth, so happy from the start —in my life, Amor's arrows by the hundredhave gone flying straight on through my heart.

Mili Bože, ima li što ljepše?Kad se dvoje u ljubavi spoje,pa kad krvca talasa k'o more,a ti ne znaš gdje ti misli stoje.

Dear Lord, is there anything more lovelythan when two in love are joined as one,and the blood goes surging like the sea,and you don't know where your thoughts have gone?

Što je lijepo uvijek kratko trajei mladost je nestala u trenu.Djeca su mi kuću popunila,pa mi snaga na posao krenu.

What is lovely never lasts for long,and in a moment youth had passed away;children came and filled my house, and soall my strength went off to work each day.

I starački dovukli se dani,ni Amora ne sretnem k'o prije.Srce mi je kao nekad gordo,još ga starost ukrotila nije.

Now the days of old age have dragged in,and I meet Amor less — it's not the same;yet my heart is proud, as once it was —that's a thing old age has yet to tame.

Valpovo, 19.02.2010.

Valpovo, February 19, 2010

Translator's note. Amor is Cupid, the god of love.
