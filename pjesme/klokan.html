The Kangaroo

from the book The Frogs' Meeting (2005)

Klokana nježnog, malog,kad rujna zora zarudi,dok u toploj torbi spava,majčino srce budi.

The tender little kangaroo,when dawn is flushing red,is woken by his mother's heartwhile in his warm pouch-bed.

Polako proviri njušku,za rub se torbe hvatai gleda u polje zelenonosi li doručak tata.

He slowly pokes his snout out,grips the pouch's edge with care,and looks across the meadow green:is Daddy bringing breakfast there?

Kad gricne malo hrane,u tobolac on se vrati,znade da će mu majkatoploga mlijeka dati.

When he's nibbled a little,back in the pouch he goes,for his mother will give himwarm milk — that much he knows.

Kad s doručkom gotov bude,tada se igra, skače.To životnu uči školuk'o svako dobro đače.

When he's finished with his breakfast,then he plays and hops with glee;so he learns in the school of life,as good pupils ought to be.

A kada je igre dostai noć se spusti plava,on majčin tobolac tražida opet u njemu spava.

And when he's played enoughand blue night settles deep,he looks for his mother's pouchso there again he'll sleep.
