Snježana

Da li si od snježnih pahuljicaili od perja rajske ptice?Ili leptir krilima je nježnomilovao tvoje divno lice?

Are you made of flakes of snow,or of plumes from a bird of paradise?Or did a butterfly, with gentle wings,tenderly caress your lovely face?

Oči tvoje dva su ognja vrela,iz dubine tvoje duše grije.Na usnama tvojim osmijeh lebdiljepotu i radost svuda sije.

Your eyes are two fires, burning hot,warming from the depths of your soul inside;on your lips there hovers a smilethat sows beauty and joy far and wide.

Da mi spleteš ruke oko vrata,kad bi svoja ustašca mi dala,meni, dušo, istoga bi časa,ukrašena zvijezda opet sjala.

If you'd twine your arms around my neck,if you'd give me your little lips, and then,at that very moment, darling,an adorned star would shine for me again.

Valpovo, 20. 02. 1989.

Valpovo, February 20, 1989

Translator's note. The name Snježana comes from snijeg, "snow", hence the snowflakes.
