Protest and society

The Chimney Sweeps' Tragedy

from the book Through Life in Verse (2010)

Dimnjačar je simbol nade, srećetako narod od davnina veli.U Valpovu mladi dimnjačarisebi sreću nisu donijeli.

A chimney sweep's a sign of hope and luck,so people have been saying from of old.But in Valpovo the young chimney sweepsbrought no such luck upon themselves, we're told.

Toga jutra oko sedam satižurili su na posao ljudi.Među njima i dva dimnjačarane sluteći što im sudba sudi.

That morning, round about seven o'clock,the people hurried to their work with speed;among them were two chimney sweeps as well,not guessing what their fate had now decreed.

Bila su to dva pupoljka mlada,dva pupoljka još nerascvjetana.Veselo su raspravljali momcidogađaje iz prošloga dana.

They were two buds, still young, the two of them,two buds that had not yet come into bloom.The lads were chatting cheerfully awayabout the happenings of yesterday.

Odjednom se začu grmljavinako' iz vedrog neba puče grom.Na pločnik su popadali momciu nesretnom, bolnom grču svom.

Then suddenly a thundering was heard,as from a clear blue sky a thunderbolt;the lads fell down onto the pavement stonesin their unhappy, painful, writhing throes.

Oko njih je tama, košmar strašni,granata im nježna tijela smrvi.Ležali su na pločniku bolni,u potoku svoje tople krvi.

Around them darkness and a nightmare dread;the shell had crushed their tender flesh and bone.They lay upon the pavement in their pain,in a warm stream of blood that was their own.

Jednom momku nikad više nećezlatno sunce ni zora da svane,a drugom su za cijelog života,polomljene nježne mlade grane.

For one young lad the golden sun no more,nor any dawn, will ever rise again;and for the other, all his life to come,the tender, youthful branches broke in twain.

Tko da majci mrtvog sina vrati,jedan mladi život ugašeni?Tko da drugom opet nogu plati,drugi mladi život uništeni?

Who will give back the mother her dead son,a young life that's been snuffed out and is gone?Who will repay the other for his leg,a second young life, ruined and undone?

Ne može se nikad ničim platittužnoj majci boli nanesene,a niti se nikad mogu vratitmladom biću snove raznesene.

Nothing can ever pay, in any way,the grieving mother for the pain she bore,nor can the young man's dreams, blown all apart,be given back to him again, restored.

Hrvatska je puna invalida,što praznina po tijelu im zjapi.Nepravda se naplatiti mora,svaka rana za tom željom vapi!

Croatia is so full of invalids,on whose bodies empty hollows gape.Injustice must be paid for in the end —for that, each single wound cries out aloud!

Valpovo, 04. 04. 1992.

Valpovo, April 4, 1992

Translator's note. On November 23, 1991, during the Homeland War, a shell fell on a Valpovo street, killing one young chimney sweep and maiming another. In folk belief a chimney sweep brings good luck.
