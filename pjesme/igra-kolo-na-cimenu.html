Domoljubne i zavičajne

Igra kolo na Ćimenu

Homeland and native region

The Kolo Dances on the Ćimen

Skupili se folkloraši,lijepe naše Šokadije,u Mar'jance na Ćimenu,Slavonsko se kolo vije.

Folklore dancers have assembledfrom our lovely Šokadija;in Mar'janci, on the Ćimen,whirls the kolo of Slavonia.

Dv'je tisuće i trinaeste,sedamnaesto bit će ovo,naše KOLO NA ĆIMENUigrat će se na Petrovo.

Two thousand and thirteen — thiswill be the seventeenth, and soour own "Kolo on the Ćimen"will be danced on Petrovo.

Gledat ćemo sa ponosom,divnu smotru ovog ljeta,slavno KOLO NA ĆIMENU,tu ljepotu kako cvjeta.

We shall watch with pride this summersuch a splendid festival,famous "Kolo on the Ćimen",how its beauty blooms for all.

Kad povorka selom prođezasviraju tamburaši,na Ćimenu kolo plešu,mnogobrojni folkloraši.

When the march has passed the village,tambura players start to play;on the Ćimen, in the kolo,countless dancers dance away.

Na godinu bit će isto,tako žele ljudi naši,na Ćimenu nek' se opetosamnaesto kolo praši.

Next year it will be the same,so our people hope and pray:on the Ćimen, let the eighteenthkolo raise the dust that day.

Sve dok bude Slavonijei Šokačkih tamburaša,igrat će se na Ćimenu,bit će smotra folkloraša.

While Slavonia remainsand our Šokac tambura men,they will dance upon the Ćimen,and the dancers meet again.

Valpovo, 25. 05. 2013.

Valpovo, May 25, 2013

Translator's note. Kolo na Ćimenu is the folklore festival held every year on the village green at Marijanci; Petrovo is St Peter's Day, 29 June.
