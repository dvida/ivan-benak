Thought of the day

A thought from his poems for every day of the year, chosen to suit the season and the feast days.

Misao dana · Thought of the day

Ja sam mala
gusjenica.
Jedem lišće,
istina je cijela,
no, list knjige
đaka Marka
nikad nisam jela.

— iz pjesme Gusjenica

I'm a little
caterpillar.
I eat leaves,
that's the whole truth, it's true,
but the leaf
of schoolboy Marko's book
I never chew.

— from The Caterpillar
