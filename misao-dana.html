A thought from his poems for every day of the year, chosen to suit the season and the feast days.

Misao dana · Thought of the day

Žena je nježno ljubila,
Mnoge je bogove rodila.
Zvijezde trepere
U njenoj kosi,
U bujnim njedrima
Mjesec skriva.
Na krhkim plećima
Planetu nosi.
I bit će voljena
Sve dok je živa.

— iz pjesme Žena

Woman's love was tender, mild;
many a god has been her child.
Stars are twinkling
in her hair,
in her full bosom's fold
she hides the moon from view.
Her fragile shoulders hold
the planet in her care.
And loved she'll be
her whole life through.

— from Woman
