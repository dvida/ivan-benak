Dobro došli

Welcome

Ivan Benak (Sopje, 29. srpnja 1933. – Valpovo, 11. svibnja 2015.) bio je pučki pjesnik iz Slavonije. Trideset i šest godina vozio je hitnu pomoć u Valpovu, a cijeli je život pisao pjesme: ljubavne i domoljubne, šaljive i protestne, a najviše i najljepše za djecu.

Ivan Benak (Sopje, July 29, 1933 – Valpovo, May 11, 2015) was a folk poet from Slavonia, in eastern Croatia. For thirty-six years he drove an ambulance in the town of Valpovo, and all his life he wrote poems: love poems and poems of homeland, funny poems and protest poems, and, most of all and best of all, poems for children.

Objavio je šest samostalnih zbirki, pjesme su mu uglazbljene i pjevane na festivalima, a stotine puta čitao ih je na sijelima, u vrtićima, školama i domovima za starije diljem Slavonije. Ove stranice čuvaju sve njegove pjesme koje je obitelj sačuvala, na hrvatskom izvorniku i u engleskom prijevodu, da ih mogu čitati i njegovi unuci i praunuci, gdje god u svijetu bili.

He published six collections of his own. Several of his poems became songs performed at festivals, and he read his verses hundreds of times at village poetry evenings, in kindergartens, schools and old people's homes all over Slavonia. These pages keep every poem his family has preserved, in the Croatian original with an English translation beside it, so that his grandchildren and great-grandchildren can read them wherever in the world they live.

„Ivan Benak mi je ime, / pjesnik koji piše rime.“

“Ivan Benak is my name, / poet: rhyming is my aim.”

Misao dana · Thought of the day

Draga naša unučice!
Sve najbolje što sad znamo,
rođendan ti čestitamo!
Pa makar si sada snaša,
unuka si ipak naša.

— iz pjesme Draga naša unučice!

Our dear little granddaughter!
All the best that we can say:
happy birthday to you today!
Even though you're now a wife,
you're our granddaughter for life.

On these pages

Životopis: pjesnikov život, od Sopja do Valpova, i njegov stihovani „osvrt“ na pjesnički put.

Biography: the poet's life, from Sopje to Valpovo, and his own rhymed “look back” on his path as a poet.

Životna priča: njegov život, od siromašnog djetinjstva u Ritskoj Adi do Pečata Grada Valpova, kako ga je zapisala kći Suzana.

Life story: his life, from a childhood of poverty in Ritska Ada to the Seal of the Town of Valpovo, as told by his daughter Suzana.

Pjesme: šest stotina pjesama, razvrstanih po temama, s engleskim prijevodom.

Poems: six hundred poems, arranged by theme, with English translations.

Knjige: šest samostalnih zbirki, od Malih snova (1988.) do Stihom kroz život (2010.).

Books: six collections of his own, from Little Dreams (1988) to Through Life in Verse (2010).

Uglazbljene pjesme: Ne budi me, majko, rano, Zelene su oči tvoje i Pod granama jorgovana.

Songs: Don't Wake Me Early, Mother, Green Are Those Eyes of Yours and Beneath the Lilac Boughs.

Galerija: fotografije, naslovnice, ilustracije i zahvalnice.

Gallery: photographs, book covers, illustrations and certificates of thanks.

Riječi prijatelja: što su o njemu napisali prijatelji pjesnici.

Words of friends: what his fellow poets wrote about him.

Dođi na moje grudi

loving

Come and Lie Upon My Breast

Kada se jutrom ranom budiši zlatna zora zarudi,da ljepši nam bude dan,dođi na moje grudi!

When you wake in early morningand the dawn in gold is dressed,so our day may be more lovely,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada umoran dođem kućii duša za domom žudi,da nađem radost srcu svome,dođi na moje grudi!

When I come home tired and wearyand my soul yearns for its nest,so my heart may find its gladness,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada prođe cvijet mladostii starost polako se nudi,da nađeš ljubav kao žena,dođi na moje grudi!

When the flower of youth has fadedand old age steals in, a guest,so you'll find love as a woman,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada djeca odrastu i odui život te izmuči kruti,da svome bolu utjehu nađeš,dođi na moje grudi!

When the children grow and leave usand harsh life has you distressed,so your pain may find its comfort,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada dođu starački danii mara te u snu budi,da odmoriš staru, umornu dušu,dođi na moje grudi!

When the days of age are comingand a nightmare breaks your rest,so your old and weary soul may find repose,come and lie upon my breast!

Kada dođe ono vrijemei sudba za prošlost sudi,da toplu suzu iz oka pustiš,dođi na moje grudi!

When at last that time is comingand fate puts our past to the test,so you may shed a warm tear from your eye,come and lie upon my breast!

Why Is Man Not Like a Child

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteu čistoći svoje duše?Zašto neki kad odrastusve vrline ljudske ruše?

Why is man not like a childin the pureness of his heart?Why do some, when they are grown,tear man's virtues all apart?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto koru kruha među sobom dijeli?Zašto mnogi zemaljsko blagosamo za sebe želi?

Why is man not like a childthat will share its crust of bread with all its friends?Why do many want earth's richesonly for their selfish ends?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteu poštenju, marljivosti?Zašto su pojedinci zvijeriu otimanju i gramzljivosti?

Why is man not like a childin his honesty and toil?Why are some like beasts of prey,grasping, snatching, greedy for the spoil?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto mu se pogled kupa u rosi?Zašto svojim postupcimabol, bijedu i jad nanosi?

Why is man not like a childwhose gaze bathes in dewdrops, fresh and clear?Why, by all he does, must hebring pain, want and sorrow here?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijeteŠto osmjehom proljeće nosi?Zašto prljavim ratovimatuđe živote gnječi i kosi?

Why is man not like a childthat brings springtime with its smile?Why does he crush the lives of other men,scything them in dirty wars so vile?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto se mora držati bontona?Zašto čovjek želi prestižu oružju od atoma?

Why is man not like a childthat must mind its manners, be polite?Why does man seek his prestigein atomic weapons' might?

Zašto čovjek nije kao dijetešto ne gleda porijeklo ni rasuZašto mnogi kad odrastumeđu ljudstvom prave klasu?

Why is man not like a childthat pays no heed to someone's birth or race?Why do many, once they're grown,rank mankind by class and place?

Protiv svoje volje

poignant

Against My Will

Kad sam bio u trbuhu majke,nikada mi nije bilo bolje.Mene ništa ni pitali nisu,rodiše me protiv moje volje.

When I was lying in my mother's womb,I never had it better there. Until,without so much as asking what I wished,they bore me into life against my will.

Hranili me po vlastitoj želji,bacali me i gore i dolje,igrali se kao mačka s mišem,opet kažem: protiv moje volje.

They fed me as and when it pleased them best,they bounced me up and down with all their skill,they played with me as cats play with a mouse:and once again, I say, against my will.

Kad sam stao na vlastite noge,mislio sam da će biti bolje.No, do škole opet svakog danatjerali me, protiv moje volje.

When I could stand at last on my own feet,I thought that things would now improve — until,to school again, and every single day,they chased me off, and all against my will.

Došla mladost i sa njome ljubav,pa zar može nešto biti bolje?Tek što krenuh bećarskim životom,ja u vojsku, protiv svoje volje.

Then youth arrived, and love along with it:could anything be better? What a thrill!I'd barely started on the bećar's life:the army called me up against my will.

Lijepu mladost armiji sam dao,vratili me kad se želo polje.Još se nisam naljubio cura,ožene me, i bez moje volje.

I gave my lovely youth to army life;they sent me home at harvest time; and stillI hadn't had my fill of courting girls:they married me, and that without my will.

Prši sniježak u noćima hladnim,žena grije od furune bolje.Ljubljah strasno u noćima dugim,došla djeca, i bez moje volje.

On chilly nights the snow would flurry down;a wife beats any stove against the chill.I loved with passion through the long, long nights:the children came, and that without my will.

Da bi kuća bila puna svega,mojoj djeci da bi bilo bolje,morao sam k'o magare vući,vjerujte mi - protiv svoje volje.

So that our house be full of everything,and that my children's lot be better still,just like a donkey I would pull and drag:believe me, friends, against my very will.

Često žena, pa i majka njena,jezicima bodu kao zolje.Ja bih rado podviknuo glasno,ali šutim, protiv svoje volje.

My wife, her mother too, will often stingme with their tongues, like wasps, so sharp and shrill;I'd gladly give a good loud shout at them,but hold my tongue, although against my will.

Godine su učinile svoje,kičma mi se savila na dolje.Od života osta uspomena,sve je prošla, protiv moje volje.

The years have done their work, as years must do;my spine is bent, and bends down lower still.Of life there's nothing left but memory:it all went by, and quite against my will.

I starost je polako već stigla,barem da je zdravlje malo bolje.Sada čekam na rajska blaženstva,vjerujte mi, protiv svoje volje.

Old age has slowly come to me as well;if only I were not so often ill!Now I await the blessed joys of heaven —believe me, friends, against my very will.

Usliši me, Bože

questioning

Hear Me, O God

Kako ja puno već ljeta brojim,u fazama raznim životom svojim.Zato se Bogu obraćam čestoda meni u raju nađe mjesto.

Of summers I have counted many a score,through all the stages life has held in store;and so I often turn to God in prayerthat He may find a place for me up there.

Hoću li, Bože, i ja u raj?To iskreno želim, ali se bojim.U raju će biti slava i sjaj,a ja Ti na lošem glasu stojim.

Shall I too, O God, to heaven go?I truly wish it, yet I live in fear.In heaven there'll be glory, splendour's glow,and my good name with You is poor down here.

Možda ima kakav skromni kutići to je, Bože, dovoljno za me.Stisnut ću se kao suhi prutić,usko je moje staračko rame.

Perhaps there is some corner, not too big,and that, O God, would be enough for me.I'll squeeze in there, as thin as a dry twig:my aged shoulder's narrow as can be.

Nikome neću pravit probleme,sve bi to, Bože, bez pompe bilo.Ja Te molim, ne zaboravi me.Biti u raju svakom je milo.

I'll make no trouble, cause no one distress,and all, O God, would pass without a show.I beg You, don't forget me, nonetheless,for heaven's dear to everyone below.

Ako Ti nije do mene stalo,tako starog, bijednog odrpanca.Pusti barem da pričekam malo,onog jadnog moga starog znanca.

If You don't care for me, if that's the case,so old a ragged wretch, so poor as I,then let me wait at least a little spacefor my poor old acquaintance to come by,

Da mu kažem nek' se za me moli,da bi u raj bio primljen boljei dalje ću čekati bez boli,makar bilo i bez moje volje.

to tell him that for me he ought to pray,so heaven might receive me better still,and I'll wait on without a pang, and stay,though it should even be against my will.

Pa kad uđu kojima je dato,znam da ću i ja imati srećei meni ćeš mjesta dati zato,jer srce je Tvoje od neba veće!

And when they've entered, those You've granted grace,I know that I'll be lucky by and by,and You will give me, too, a little place,because Your heart is bigger than the sky!

s ljubavlju

Pjesma sinu!

loving

A Poem for My Son!

Živio sam s tobom sretno svoju mladost.Spavao si, sine, na mojim rukama.U igri sa tobom dijelio sam radost.Za bol tvoju ja sam bio na mukama.

I lived my youth with you, in happiness, my boy.You slept, my son, so often in my arms at rest;in playing with you, I would share in all your joy;and when you were in pain, I suffered, sore distressed.

Ti još i ne znaš, kako bi i znao,koliko te moje biće silno voli,kada budeš svoje čedo sinom zvaotek ćeš onda znati za strepnje i boli.

You do not know it yet (and how could you have known)how mightily my whole being loves you so;but when you call a son of yours your very own,then only will you know the dread, the pain, the woe.

Sa tobom je bilo mnogo sretnih dana,a još više strepnjom probdivenih noći.Zato nikad, sine, nikad ne dozvoli,da ranjenom dušom u grob moram poći.

With you, my son, there have been many happy days,and more, the nights I lay awake in dread and fear.So never, son, oh never, never let it bethat with a wounded soul I'm carried to my bier.

Zov domovine

homeland

The Call of the Homeland

Rođena ti gruda sreću, ljubav daje,svako drvo, kamen, dječje radovanje.To ti tuđa zemlja nikad neće dati.Maćeha se majkom nikad neće zvati.

The soil where you were born gives you both love and joy,each tree, each stone, and all the gladness of a boy;no foreign land will ever give you this, no other:a stepmother will never bear the name of mother.

Kada si daleko, nostalgija zove.Iz dna duše čut ćeš poziv zemlje ove.Na njedarca svoja k'o voljena žena,radosno te prima, to je dužnost njena.

Whenever you're far off, nostalgia calls you near;from deep within your soul, this land's own call rings clear.Upon her own dear breast, just like a well-loved wife,she gladly takes you in: that is her part in life.

U tuđoj se zemlji gorka sjeta krije.Jednom ćeš se vratit, ako nikad prije,ono u starost, kad mladost uvene,da posiješ kosti u utrobe njene.

In foreign lands a bitter sorrow lies in wait;one day you will come back, if not before, then late:in old age, when your youth has withered all away,to sow your bones within her womb, and there to stay.

Zato svoju zemlju i ljubi i voli,najdražom je zovi, vrijeđat ne dozvoli.Ona ti sve prašta, k'o rođena mati.Tu rođenu grudu treba čuvat znati.

So love your land, and cherish her, and hold her dear;call her the dearest one, let no insult come near.She forgives all, just like the mother of your birth;you must know how to keep and guard your native earth.

San o sreći

poignant

A Dream of Happiness

Budila me moja majka,svako jutro zorimicei suzama svojim toplim,umivala moje lice.

My own mother used to wake meevery morning, at dawn's first trace,and with her own tears, so warm,she would wash my little face.

Od nepravde i od bola,cijepala joj tuga grudi,dok očuhov sinak spava,mene majka u mrak budi.

From injustice and from paingrief would cause her heart to break;while my stepfather's son slept,in the dark she'd make me wake.

Da bi moje noge bosetresle hladnu rosu s trave,oko suzom zamagljeno,gledalo gdje pasu krave.

So that my bare feet would shakethe cold dew from grassy ground,and my eye, all dimmed with tears,watch where cows grazed all around.

Dotle veći i stariji,od očuha sinak mio,u postelji toploj, mekoj,blažene je snove snio.

Meanwhile he, the older, bigger,my stepfather's darling boy,in a bed so warm and soft,dreamed his dreams of bliss and joy.

A ja malen još nejačak,daleki sam snio sanak.U mašti sam sretan bio,a na javi plaćo danak.

And I, little, weak, each daydreamed a dream of far away.In my fancies I was happy,but awake, I had to pay.

Majka

loving

Mother

Kada dijete noću plače,iz snova kada se budi,da čedu svome utjehu nađe,majka ga svija na grudi.

When a child cries in the night,waking from its dreams in fear,to find comfort for her little one,mother holds it to her bosom near.

Kad čuje bilo srca njenog,toplinu osjeti majke,ona je njemu božica sreće,ljepša od svake bajke.

When it hears her heart's soft beatingand its mother's warmth is there,she's the goddess of its joy and gladness,than all fairy tales more fair.

Kad padne i bolno plače,tad majci brže hita.Ona mu nježno suze brišei poljupcem rane vida.

When it falls and cries in pain,to its mother fast it goes;tenderly she wipes away its tearsand with kisses heals its woes.

Za svaku bolest čeda svoga,majčino srce pati.Svoje bi oči za njega dala.To može samo mati.

For each illness of her little onea mother's heart aches through and through.She would give her very eyes for it:that, a mother alone can do.

Love and Pain

Ljubav je davanje.Ljubav je primanje.I što više daješ,to duže traje.No, teško je trpjetinepravedne boli,a još su teže,kad ih zadajekoga srce voli.

Love is giving.Love is receiving.And the more you give,the longer it will stay.But it's hard to sufferpains that are not due,and harder stillwhen they come your wayfrom one your heart loves true.

poignant

The Old Man

Koliki je jad i bijeda,preko mojih prošlo leđa?Kosa mi je sada sijeda,a bila je crno-smeđa.

How much misery and carehave these shoulders come to know?Grey now, faded, is my hair,once dark brown, so long ago.

Udovi mi bolno hodei to krhko nose tijelo.Slabe oči što me vode,a još bi se živjet htjelo.

Painfully my limbs still go,bearing this frail frame along;weak the eyes that guide me so,yet the wish to live is strong.

Vrati bože, osmjeh, radost,svakom stvoru da se divim.Slatku ljubav, sretnu mladostjoš jedanput da proživim.

Give me back, O God, my smileand the joy to marvel still;sweetest love and youth, a whilelet me live them, if you will.

Suha kora prove

wisdom

The Dry Crust of Cornbread

Što da pričam siromaštvo,tugu svoju, jad i bijedu.Dok sam koru prove griz'o,gled'o sam gdje meso jedu.

What's to tell of poverty,sorrow, grief, and woe complete?While I gnawed the cornbread crust,I would watch them eating meat.

Bijelog kruha svježi miris,kod drugih se širit stao,dok sam koru prove griz'ood bola sam zaplakao.

Fresh white bread, its fragrant smell,from the others drifted near;while I gnawed the cornbread crust,in my pain I shed a tear.

U snovima bijelog kruha,svježu krišku ja sam snio,a na javi koru provesuzama sam natopio.

In my dreams I used to dreamof a fresh white slice of bread,but awake, the cornbread crustI would soak with tears instead.

Kao dijete ja ne shvatihmeđu ljudstvom što se stvara,da ne grize koru proveonaj koji ima para.

As a child I did not seehow the ways of men unfold:that the cornbread crust is nevergnawed by those who have the gold.

Kad ugledah nepoštenje,kod bogatih strvinara,i ta suha kora provevrijedila je meni para.

When I saw the crookednessof the rich, the vulture kind,even that dry cornbread crustwas worth money, to my mind.

A kad shvatih tu razlikumeđu ljudstvom što se rađa.I ta suha kora provebila mi je tada slađa.

And when I grasped the difference,grown between the rich and poor,even that dry cornbread crusttasted sweeter than before.

loving

Don't Wake Me Early, Mother

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Još se nisam naspavao.Kad mi draga u san dođe,za te snove sve bih dao.

Mother, do not wake me early,I am not yet done with sleeping.When my love comes in my dreaming,all I'd give those dreams for keeping.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Ne kidaj mi divne snove,kada zoveš moje ime,mislim da me draga zove.

Mother, do not wake me early,leave my lovely dreams unbroken:when you call my name, I fancyit's my darling who has spoken.

Ne budi me, majko, rano.Najljepše je snivati u zoru.U snovima na rastanku,ljubim dragu na prozoru.

Mother, do not wake me early,sweetest are the dreams when day is starting;in my dreams I kiss my true loveat her window, at our parting.

Dođi voljena

loving

Come, My Beloved

Dođi, voljena,kada sam sretan,da radost svojus tobom podijelim.

Come, my beloved,when I am happy,so that my gladnessI may share with you.

Dođi, draga,u naručaj meni,osmijehu tvomeda se veselim.

Come, my darling,come into my arms,so that your sweet smilemay gladden me too.

Dođi i ondakada sam tužan,srce te mojei onda želi.

Come to me alsowhen I am grieving,for this heart of minelongs to have you near.

I tugu je lakšenositi udvoje,kad ju se s nekimiskreno dijeli.

Sorrow, too, is lighterwhen carried by two,if someone shares itwith a heart sincere.

Vojnikova majka

poignant

The Soldier's Mother

Muško sam čedo rodila,njegovala ga i mazila.Nad njim bdila i strepila,u igri njegovoj,uz osmijeh njegovsretna sam bila.

I bore a son and held him tight,I nursed and cuddled my delight,kept watch and trembled through the night;in his every game,in his every smile,my heart was light.

Tisuću devetstodevedeset druge,U selo je došlagrupa vojnika.Gordo je stao uz Satnika.Pogledom sam ga ispratilai suze lila,suze tuge i radosniceza mladog vojnika.

In nineteen hundredand ninety-twothere came to the villagesoldiers in a line.Proud, by the Captain, stood that son of mine.With my eyes I saw him goand let my tears flow,tears of sorrow and of joyfor that young soldier of mine.

Jednog dana dođe,tužna vijest iz daljina,o smrti moga sina.

Then one day there camesad news from far away, which saidthat my son was dead.

Dok sam ga u mislima,na posljednji počinak ispraćala.Rane me njegove pekle,u bolnim grudimaćutala sam metke.

As I, within my thoughts,was walking him to his last resting place,his wounds were burning me;in my aching breastI felt the bullets.

Ubiše mi sina,od osamnaest godina.Život je izgubio u boju,braneći domovinu svoju.

They killed my son,eighteen years beneath the sun.He fell in battle, making a stand,defending his own native land.

Da odnijeti mogustručak cvijeća svježi.Barem da znademgdje mu tijelo leži.

If only I could bringfresh flowers, a small bouquet.If only I knewwhere his body lay.

Iskreno te, Gospo,ja za pomoć molim,pomozi da nađemsina koga volim.

Our Lady, with all my heartI pray for help from above:help me, that I may findthe son I love.

Od djetinjstva do staračkih dana

wisdom

From Childhood to Old Age

Proš'o Božić i godina Nova,odjurio i Valentin sveti,a godine na pleća se nižu,jer nam život kao strijela leti.

Christmas gone, and gone the New Year, too,and Saint Valentine has hurried past;years keep piling up upon our shoulders,for, just like an arrow, life flies fast.

Kad se sjetim svojega djetinjstvai mladosti što je sretno došla.U životu Amorovih strijela,kroz srce mi na stotine prošla.

When I think back on my childhood daysand my youth, so happy from the start,all my life, by hundreds, Amor's arrowshave gone flying straight on through my heart.

Mili Bože, ima li što ljepše?Kad se dvoje u ljubavi spoje,pa kad krvca talasa k'o more,a ti ne znaš gdje ti misli stoje.

Dear Lord, is there anything more lovelythan when two in love are joined as one,and the blood goes surging like the sea,and you don't know where your thoughts have run?

Što je lijepo uvijek kratko trajei mladost je nestala u trenu.Djeca su mi kuću popunila,pa mi snaga na posao krenu.

What is lovely never lasts for long,and in a moment youth had slipped away;children came and filled my house, and soall my strength went off to work each day.

I starački dovukli se dani,ni Amora ne sretnem k'o prije.Srce mi je kao nekad gordo,još ga starost ukrotila nije.

Now the days of old age have dragged in,and I meet Amor less, it's not the same;yet my heart is proud, as once it was:that's a thing old age has yet to tame.

Slavonac na Jadranu

homeland

A Slavonian on the Adriatic

Sa sjevera donio me vjetar,u Karlobag do plavoga mora,da se divim toj velikoj barii da gledam kako sviće zora.

The wind has carried me down from the northto Karlobag, beside the sea so blue,to marvel there at that enormous pondand watch the way the dawn comes breaking through.

Skitao sam po Jadranu plavom,od Rovinja do čuvenog Splita,ali nigdje ja vidio nisam,ljepšeg mora do slavonskog žita.

Along the blue Adriatic I've roamed,from Rovinj down to famous Split, my feethave carried me, but nowhere have I seena fairer sea than our Slavonian wheat.

Poj sirena još odzvanja zrakom,more šumi, vjetar ga talasa,ljepše pjesme nisam nikad čuo,do šuštanja zrelog žitnog klasa.

The sirens' song still echoes on the air,the sea is stirred by wind, the waves resound;a lovelier song I never yet have heardthan ripened wheat-ears rustling all around.

Ja mrzim!

questioning

I Hate!

Ja mrzim dvorcei ne volim kule,tvrđave i vilei sva zdanjabez ljudske topline.Mrzim kazamate,glad i nasilje,ratove i krvoproliće.Mrzim gramzljivostsvu bez pardonai slatku fasadupokvarene duše.Ja mrzim sjaj,ljudskog licemjerstva,uvijeno u fino ruho bontona.

I hate castlesand do not love towers,fortresses and villasand all the buildingswithout human warmth.I hate dungeons,hunger and violence,wars and the shedding of blood.I hate greed,all of it, without regret,and the sweet façadeof a rotten soul.I hate the glitterof human hypocrisy,wrapped in the fine garb of etiquette.

Lastavica

tender

The Swallow

Lasto, ptico milaproljeća vjesniče!Jesi li umornaod dugoga leta?

Swallow, darling bird,herald of the spring!Are you tired outfrom your lengthy flight?

Volim tebe, lasto!Ti mi nosiš srećei toplo proljeće.Puno divno cvijeta.

How I love you, swallow!You bring luck and cheerand warm spring each year,flowers fair and bright.

Vraćaš mi se, lasto,sa svojim cvrkutom.Tvoje toplo gnijezdogledajuć' uživam.

Swallow, back you comewith your chirping sound;watching your warm nestfills my heart anew.

Miris cvijeta širišrascvjetanim vrtom.U tvoj stari domja te rado primam.

Blossom scent you spreadthrough the garden all around;to your old home groundI gladly welcome you.

Kada dođe jesen,srce me zazebe.S odlaskom svojimstvaraš meni sjetu.

When the autumn comes,my heart starts to freeze;as you fly away,you leave me sad and blue.

Išao bih s tobomu tople krajeve,ali krila nemamda letim po svijetu.

I'd go with you, swallow,to warm lands overseas,but I have no wingsto fly the whole world through.

s ljubavlju

Kroz Valpovo teče Karašica

loving

The Karašica Flows Through Valpovo

Noć Mjesecom obasjana cijelai zvijezdama okićeno veče',ja i moja Valpovčanka Jela,Karašicu gledamo gdje teče.

The whole night's bathed in moonlight, far and wide,the evening's decked with stars across the sky;my Valpovo girl, my Jela, at my side,we watch the Karašica flowing by.

Kad je lipa šorom mirisala,ljubav mi je Valpovčanka dala,mjesečina nas je obasjala,Karašica tiho proticala.

When linden scent along the lane would blow,her love my Valpovo girl would then bestow;the moon shone down on us with gentle glow,and quietly the Karašica would flow.

Kada noćca na počinak zove,poljupcima sklopim njene očii ugodne poželim joj snove,jer uskoro i zora će doći.

When night comes softly, calling us to rest,with kisses I close gently both her eyes,and wish her pleasant dreams upon my breast,for soon enough the dawn, too, will arise.

Kad jutarnji zapjevaju pijetli,zora nam je odnosila snovezagrljeni kad danak zasvijetli,poljupci nam daju snage nove.

When morning roosters start their crowing cries,the dawn would carry all our dreams away;embraced, as daylight glimmers in the skies,our kisses give new strength to face the day.

wisdom

It's Sad to Be Alone

U smiraj noći,u praznoj sobi,tužan je svaki dan.Teško je moći,u takvoj noći,naći ljubavni san.

When the night draws in,in an empty room,sad is every day.Hard it is to spin,when such nights begin,dreams where love holds sway.

Zato si nađi,životnog druga,nikad ne budi sam.Život je slađi,manja je tuga,vjeruj mi,ja to znam.

Go, then, seek and finda companion true;never be alone.Life's more sweet, more kind,and less sorrow, too;trust me,this I've known.

Šokački običaji

homeland

Šokac Customs

Volim tamburicu kad joj žica cikne,u dušu me takne kada vodim kolo.Srce zatreperi kad mi dika vikne:u kijer mi dođi moj voljeni lolo!

I love the tamburica when its string rings high,it touches my soul when I'm leading the kolo;my heart starts to flutter to hear my dear cry:"Come into my chamber, my beloved lolo!"

Volim kad se ljubav sokacima vodi.Volim škripu đerma i seosku viku.Volim kada mjesec sa zvijezdama hodi,na toj mjesečini volim svoju diku.

I love when the lanes see the lovers go by,I love the well-sweep's creaking, the village's call,I love when the moon strolls with stars through the sky,in that moonlight I love my sweetheart best of all.

Volim prela, sijela, šokačke divane,i maškare volim, kada se opreme,svu ljepotu volim u svatovske dane,divne običaje što žive kroz vrijeme.

I love prela, sijela, the Šokac yarns they spin,I love the maškare, when dressed up they appear,I love all the beauty when wedding days begin,the wonderful customs still living year on year.

Nema više konja da dvorištem njište,mjesto njih traktori sokacima bruje.Nema mladih snaša da u kolu vrište,sve je utihnulo, ništa se ne čuje.

There are no more horses in farmyards to neigh,in their place, only tractors go droning down lanes;no young wives in the kolo now whooping away,all has fallen silent, and no sound remains.

Sada televizor zamjenjuje prela.Sokaci su pusti, ne igra se kolo,ni bećara nama da obiđu sijela.Ničeg više nema, moj voljeni lolo!

The television has now replaced the prela,the lanes are deserted, none dances the kolo,no bećars are left now to go round the sijela.Nothing's left any more, my beloved lolo!

Što je drvo

tender

What Is a Tree?

Pitala me kćerka mala:„Reci, tata, što je drvo?“Da ti kažem, dijete moje,kao deblo dođe prvo.

Once my little daughter asked me:"Tell me, Daddy, what's a tree?"Let me tell you, child of mine:first, the trunk is what you see.

To je njemu kao tijeloiz kojega žilje klije.Još na njemu rastu grane,što ih snažni vjetar vije.

That's its body, you might say,from which all its roots will sprout;and on it the branches growthat strong winds toss all about.

Na granama lista lišće,što ga nježni lahor njiše,a to su mu, dijete, pluća,ono kao i ti diše.

On the branches leaves come out,which the gentle breeze blows through,and, my child, these are its lungs:it breathes just the way you do.

Kad proljetno sunce grane,svako drvo tad procvjetai opojni miris širi,što je ukras ovog svijeta.

When the sun of spring shines down,every tree blooms, full of grace,spreading heady scent aroundto adorn this earthly place.

Tada pčele cvijet oplode,iza cvijeta plod urodi,a s jeseni, kad sazori –najljepše je u prirodi.

Bees then pollinate the blossom,after blossom, fruit will grow,and in autumn, when it's ripened,nature has no finer show.

Sjećanje na djetinjstvo

poignant

Remembering My Childhood

Djetinjstvo moje bolno mi prisjelo.Svakoga sam dana bos po trnju hodo.Na meni je bilo skrpljeno odijelo,dok sam tuđe krave po strnjaku vodo.

My childhood weighs on me, a bitter pain I've known:barefoot I walked on thorns, and did so every day;the clothes upon my back were patched and roughly sewn,while I led other people's cows where stubble lay.

I sad kad se sjetim srce me zazebe.Na svakom koraku, bijedu sam susreto.Nikada se nisam stidio sam sebe,jer poštenje meni bilo je presveto.

Remembering it now, a chill goes through my frame.At every step I met with poverty and need;yet I have never had to hang my head in shame,for honesty to me was sacred, as a creed.

Koru suhe prove svaki dan sam grizo.Da se može bolje, nisam mog'o znati,ipak nisam nikom u životu gmiz'o.Tako me je moja naučila mati.

A crust of dry cornbread I gnawed on every day;that life could be much better, I could never know,yet all my life I've crawled to no one on my way:and that is how my mother taught me, long ago.

Domovino moja

homeland

My Homeland

Slavonijo, zemljo ponosita,malo ravna, malo bregovita,zlatnim klasjem ti si okićenai sa rujnim vinom zalivena.

O Slavonia, land so proud and fair,somewhat flat, and hilly here and there,decked in golden ears of wheat, you shine,watered well with ruby-coloured wine.

Slavonijo, ljepotice moja,nikada te neću nikom dati,svaki dio te ponosne zemlje,ja ću voljet i čuvati znati!

O Slavonia, my beauty dear,I will give you up to no one, never;every corner of this land so proudI will love and guard it well forever!

Zemljo moja, srcu najmilija,ja sam tebi dao zavjet sveti:Ako treba, život ću ti dati,tu sam rođen, tu ću i umrijeti!

O my land, the dearest to my heart,I have sworn you a sacred vow, that I,if need be, will give my life for you:here I was born, and here I'll also die!

Zato, mila, iskreno te molim,za svu ljubav koju sam ti dao,podari mi jedan kutak sebe,da bi vječno s tobom drugovao.

So, my dear, I earnestly implore,for all the love I've given, deep and true,grant to me a corner of yourself,that forever I may stay with you.

I kad umrem ti ćeš dalje cvastii majčinski širit krila svoja.Dok je tebe, Šokadijo, mila,bit će dičnih ljudi i heroja!

And when I die, you'll blossom on and on,spreading wide your wings as mothers do;while you live, my dear Šokadija,there'll be worthy folk and heroes too!

Gospo sveta, čuvaj mi Hrvatsku,tu najdražu djedovinu moju,zato sam ti dao zavjet sveti,da sačuvam Šokadiju svoju.

Holy Lady, keep Croatia safe,dearest heritage my forebears gave;that is why I swore a vow to you:that my Šokadija I would save.

Most po kome gaziš

loving

The Bridge You Walk Across

Sada nosim života breme.Most sam po kome gaziš.A kada prođe naše vrijeme,kasno će biti da me maziš.

Now I carry all of life's great weight,I'm the bridge on which you tread;and when our time has passed, it will be late,too late for you to stroke my head.

Mladost naša prolazi,za podmladak živiš sad.Starost brzo dolazi,a s njome bol i jad.

Our youth is slipping past,you live for the young today;old age is coming fast,with pain and grief to stay.

Zato me miluj, draga,poljupce daj mi sada.Liječi mi srce bolnoi dušu punu jada.

So caress me, darling,give me kisses while you may;heal this aching heart of mineand my soul in its dismay.

Ne zaboravi da vrijeme letii za sobom ostavlja trag.Mladosti naše tada se sjetii bit ću ti ponovno drag.

Don't forget how swiftly time will fly,leaving all its trace behind;then remember how our youth went by,and once more I'll be dear, you'll find.

Opijelo ljubavi

questioning

Requiem for Love

Zar je bilo dosta sretnoga života?Zar me nikad više poljubiti neće?Ona koju volim, što mi nosi sreće.

Was that enough, then, of a life of happiness?Will she never, never kiss me any more,she whom I love, the joy that I am living for?

Zar je ljubav uvijek pomiješana plačem?Kao koktel sreće, a najviše bola.Zar se u ljubavi uvijek patit mora?

Is love forever mingled with our weeping,like a cocktail mixed of joy, but mostly pain?Must one who loves be made to suffer time and again?

Za nagradu meni, što sam suze briso',procvjetat će rupčić, k'o mirisna ruža,jer je u njem sjeme, njena topla suza.

As my reward for all the tears that I have dried,my handkerchief will blossom like a fragrant rose,for there's a seed in it, her own warm tear, that grows.

Zašto uvijek pišem na kraju života?A preskačem sreću onih prvih dana,dok još srce nije bilo puno rana?

Why do I always write when life is at its end,and skip the joy of those first days, right at the start,when there were not yet many wounds upon my heart?

Zato moja pjesma svaka tugu lije,kao ova sada, kojoj suze brišem.Dok u agoniji osmijeh tugu krije,ja ljubavi svojoj sad opijelo pišem.

That's why in each poem of mine the sorrow's flowing,like this one, whose tears I'm wiping dry tonight;while in its agony a smile hides grief from showing,I write my love's own requiem, its final rite.

Jučer digla baka

tender

Yesterday Grandma Held Her Head High

Jučer digla bakačelo do oblaka,u srcu joj buktistalna radost jaka.

Yesterday, oh my,Gran held her head sky-high!In her heart, a blazingjoy that will not die.

S unukom se bakaprošetala malo,do te divne šetnjebaki puno stalo.

With her grandchild, Grannywent out for a stroll,and that lovely outingpleased her heart and soul.

Što bi sada onada unuka nema?Ostala bi bakau kući da drijema.

What would Granny dowith no grandchild there?She'd just stay at home,dozing in her chair.

Oj, unuče moje,bakino veselje,samo reci, dušo,baki svoje želje.

Oh, my little grandchild,Granny's joy and cheer,tell your wishes, darling,into Granny's ear.

Sve što si poželišbaka će ti dati,sve na ovom svijetuk'o rođena mati.

Everything you wish forGranny'll give to you,all in this wide world,as your mum would do.

Unuče je bakidio njene sreće,unuče je bakibogatstvo najveće.

A grandchild, to Granny,is part of her pleasure;a grandchild, to Granny,is her greatest treasure.
